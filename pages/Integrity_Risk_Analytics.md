# Integrity Risk Analytics

# Integrity Risk Analytics

Integrity Risk Analytics is proposed to use auditable analytics to identify potential integrity risks for human review.

## Principle

**Analytics identifies signals; humans investigate.**

## Safeguards

- auditability;
- defined purpose;
- proportionality;
- human review;
- appropriate access controls;
- protection against automated conclusions.
