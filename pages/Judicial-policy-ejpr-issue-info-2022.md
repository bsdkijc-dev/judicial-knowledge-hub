# Judicial-policy-ejpr-issue-info-2022

**European Journal of Political Research (Vol. 61, No. 4, November 2022)** is the official publication information document, editorial governance structure, contributor guidelines, and table of contents for Volume 61, Issue 4 (November 2022) of the *European Journal of Political Research* (EJPR). The journal is published four times a year on behalf of the European Consortium for Political Research (ECPR) by John Wiley & Sons Ltd.

## Summary

This document sets out EJPR's editorial leadership structure, led by three editors (Isabelle Engeli, Emiliano Grossman, and Sofia Vasilopoulou), the technical guidelines for article submission ("Notes for Contributors"), and the full table of contents for Volume 61, Issue 4, comprising 16 scholarly works (13 full research articles and 3 research notes). This issue covers major topics in comparative European politics, including the legitimacy-conferring capacity of constitutional courts, the effect of repeated electoral defeat on political trust, party manifesto responses to defections, and populism's relationship with intra-party democracy.

## Background

EJPR was established as ECPR's official scholarly journal to publish comparative empirical and theoretical research in political science. It is published on a regular schedule in February, May, August, and November.

## Objectives

1\. Publish original, substantive contributions to comparative European political studies. 2. Provide rigorous editorial standards and methodological criteria for the global political science community. 3. Facilitate Open Access publishing through a hybrid Creative Commons licensing scheme.

## Scope

The journal's coverage spans all subfields of comparative political science, encompassing quantitative and qualitative approaches, review articles, research notes, and forum sections.

## Key Provisions & Contributor Guidelines

- **Maximum Word Limit:** Manuscripts may not exceed **10,000 words** (including main text, footnotes, tables, figures, and references).
- **Abstract Limit:** Maximum of **500 words**.
- **File Format:** Must be compatible with Microsoft Office; PDF and LaTeX submissions are **not accepted**.
- **Referencing Style:** Harvard style of referencing.
- **Submission Portal:** All submissions are made online via Manuscript Central (\`http://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/ejpr\`).
