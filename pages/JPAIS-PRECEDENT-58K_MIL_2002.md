## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 58 K/MIL/2002 dated April 28, 2003, establishes an important legal principle distinguishing between the criminal offense of fraud under Article 378 of the KUHP and a civil legal relationship structured as a sales and purchase agreement. The Supreme Court affirmed that the receipt of a down payment (\*uang panjar / DP\*) amounting to Rp 2,500,000 in connection with a car purchase transaction does not fulfill the element of "causing loss to another person" nor the element of "fraud" under Article 378 of the KUHP, but rather constitutes a dispute within the realm of civil law. Consequently, the Defendant must be acquitted of all charges brought by the Military Prosecutor.

## Case Background

The Defendant (\[Defendant\], Serka Nrp. 58010074, Sabhara Non-Commissioned Officer at Takalar Resort Police) was charged with the criminal offense of fraud (Article 378 of the KUHP) in connection with the sale and purchase of 1 (one) unit of a Kijang XS car (Plate No. DD 341 Z) in Takalar in November 1998. Witness-1 (\[Party A\]) acted as an intermediary in the sale between the Defendant and Witness-2 (\[Party C\]). Witness-2 claimed a loss of Rp 66,500,000 allegedly handed over to the Defendant through Witness-1. However, the vehicle ownership document (\*BPKB\*) was never handed over, and the car was subsequently repossessed by the Defendant.

The Makassar Military Court III-16 (Decision No. PUT/18-K/MM.III-16/POL/II/2001 jo. No. PUT/143-K/MM.III-16/POL/IX/2001 dated September 13, 2001) sentenced the Defendant to 2 (two) months of imprisonment. The Surabaya Military High Court III (Decision No. PUT/39-K/MMT.III/BDG/POL/VII/2002 dated July 8, 2002) upheld the conviction. Dissatisfied with these rulings, the Defendant filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The element of "causing loss to another person" (Witness-2 \[Party C\]) is unproven, because the Defendant never received funds from Witness-1 (\[Party A\]) amounting to Rp 66,500,000 (vide Statement Letter of \[Party A\]).
2.  The funds actually received by the Defendant from Witness-1 amounted to Rp 2,500,000, which represented a down payment (\*uang panjar / DP\*) for a sale and purchase agreement concerning one unit of a car.
3.  The legal relationship occurring between the Defendant, Witness-1 (intermediary), and Witness-2 (prospective buyer) is a car sale and purchase agreement categorized as a civil matter. Therefore, the Defendant is declared not proven to have committed the criminal offense of "Fraud" as charged by the Military Prosecutor and must be acquitted of the charges.

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation / Defendant \[Defendant\], Serka Nrp. 58010074; Annulling the Surabaya Military High Court III decision dated July 8, 2002, No. PUT/39-K/MMT.III/BDG/POL/VII/2002 and the Makassar Military Court III-16 decision dated February 13, 2001 / September 13, 2001, No. PUT/18-K/MM.III-16/POL/II/2001; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring that Defendant \[Defendant\], Serka Nrp. 58010074 is not proven to have committed the criminal offense of "Fraud"; (2) Acquitting the Defendant of the Military Prosecutor's charges; (3) Restoring the Defendant's rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity; (4) Charging procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State.
