# RIGHT-0003

# Fair Trial

**Fair trial** refers to procedural guarantees intended to ensure that judicial proceedings are conducted fairly and according to applicable legal standards.

The precise content of the right varies by jurisdiction and applicable legal instruments.

**\[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## Potential Dimensions

Depending on the applicable legal framework, fair trial may involve:

- Independent adjudication
- Impartiality
- Equality of arms
- Notice
- Opportunity to be heard
- Reasoned decisions
- Access to evidence
- Timeliness
- Ability to challenge decisions

This list is not exhaustive.

## Technology

Digital judicial systems may affect fair trial through:

- Remote hearings
- Electronic filing
- Digital evidence
- Automated systems
- AI-assisted processes

## AI and Fair Trial

AI systems may create risks involving:

- Incorrect outputs
- Automation bias
- Lack of transparency
- Inability to challenge outputs
- Discrimination
- Inadequate human review

## Human Decision-Making

Where AI supports judicial processes, the institutional design should clarify:

- What AI does
- What humans do
- Who decides
- Who reviews
- How errors are corrected
