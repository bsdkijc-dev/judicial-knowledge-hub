## Policy Analysis

- **Background & Core Facts**:
  - The *Homu Shiryo* (法務資料) series comprises official foreign legal translations published periodically by the Ministry of Justice of Japan. Index catalog covers Nos. 421 (Feb 1975) through 469 (Dec 2023).
  - **Publication Objective**: Provides authoritative Japanese translations of foreign statutes accompanied by expert introductory overviews (概説/解説) to support comparative law research, domestic legislative reform, and judicial practice.
  - **Indexed Jurisdictions & Subject Coverage**:
    - **Civil & Procedural Law**: German BGB Book 4 Family Law (No. 468), German FamFG (No. 469), UK Property & Succession Law (No. 467), French Civil Execution Code (No. 466), German HGB (No. 465), EU Civil Procedure / Brussels Ia (No. 464), German ZPO (Nos. 462, 450, 440), Austrian ZPO (No. 456), French Civil Code (Nos. 441, 433).
    - **Criminal Law & Procedure**: PRC Criminal Procedure Law (No. 463), German StGB (Nos. 461, 439, 424), German StPO (Nos. 460, 437), French Code of Criminal Procedure (Nos. 459, 435, 431), French Penal Code (Nos. 452, 451, 448), Italian Code of Criminal Procedure (No. 457), NY Criminal Procedure Law (No. 428), Austrian Penal Code (No. 423).
    - **Specialized Frameworks**: Adult Guardianship in 8 Nations (No. 458), Legal Aid Systems in 5 Nations (No. 454), EC & Foreign Product Liability (No. 449), ABA Judicial Administration Standards (No. 443).
- **Interpretations & Structural Inferences**:
  - Reflects half a century (1975–2023) of continuous institutional commitment by Japan's Ministry of Justice to assimilate comparative legal developments.
  - Scholar-authored introductory essays transform literal translations into doctrinal tools used directly by the Legislative Council of the MOJ (法制審議会).
