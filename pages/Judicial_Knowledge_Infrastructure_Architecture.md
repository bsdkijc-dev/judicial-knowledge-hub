# Judicial Knowledge Infrastructure Architecture

# Judicial Knowledge Infrastructure Architecture

The proposed architecture connects governance, trusted data, judicial knowledge, AI and users.

## Architecture

Supreme Court and judicial governance ↓ Judicial data governance layer ↓ National judicial data and interoperability layer ↓ Judicial knowledge repository and knowledge graph ↓ Secure AI and analytics layer ↓ Judicial Copilot services ↓ Court administration services ↓ Public and professional access services ↓ Monitoring, audit and cybersecurity layer

## Core Rule

**AI recommends. Human verifies. Judge decides.**
