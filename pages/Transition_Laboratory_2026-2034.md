# Transition Laboratory 2026–2034

The period 2026–2034 should function as a transition laboratory.

The purpose is to develop institutional capabilities before the post-2035 target state becomes operational.

## Capabilities to Test

- AI governance;
- judicial data governance;
- judicial knowledge architecture;
- AI competency;
- interoperability;
- digital accessibility;
- cybersecurity;
- public trust measurement;
- innovation governance.

## Strategic Principle

Do not wait until 2035 to begin building the post-2035 judiciary.

**Experiment early → Learn → Evaluate → Institutionalise**
