# International Judicial Benchmarking

International benchmarking identifies practices that may inform Indonesian judicial transformation.

## Priority Jurisdictions

- Estonia
- Singapore
- European Union / CEPEJ
- United Kingdom
- Australia
- Korea

## Method

Benchmarking should distinguish:

- transferable practice;
- context-specific practice;
- institutional prerequisites;
- implementation risks.

## Principle

**Learn internationally; design for Indonesia.**
