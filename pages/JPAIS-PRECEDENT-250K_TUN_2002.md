## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 250 K/TUN/2002 dated October 23, 2002, establishes fundamental labor and employment jurisprudence:

- \*\*Serious Misconduct and Termination Sanctions (Minister of Manpower Decree No. Kep-150/Men/2000):\*\* Workers/laborers who commit unlawful acts categorized as serious misconduct under Article 18 paragraph (1) letters h and k of Minister of Manpower Decree No. Kep-150/Men/2000 (such as occupying hotel lobbies, conducting unprocedural demonstrations/without police notification ex Law No. 9 of 1998 & Law No. 22 of 1957, damaging facilities, and leaving the employer's property in a dangerous condition) may be subjected to the sanction of Termination of Employment (\*PHK\*).
- \*\*Awarding Severance Pay for the Sake of Justice (Casuistic / Ex Aequo et Bono):\*\* Although formally under statutory law workers who commit serious misconduct are not entitled to severance pay (Article 18 paragraph (4) of Minister of Manpower Decree No. Kep-150/Men/2000), the Supreme Court acting as \*Judex Juris\* deemed it necessary to uphold human rights and humanitarian considerations for workers during an economic crisis. Based on case-by-case justice (\*casuistic\*), terminated workers are granted severance pay amounting to 3 (three) times the provisions of Article 22 of Minister of Manpower Decree No. Kep-150/Men/2000.

## Case Background

The object of the dispute was the Decree of Defendant I / Central Committee for Labor Dispute Settlement (\*P4P\*) No. 602/358/63-5/IX/PHK/5-2001 dated May 1, 2001, concerning the authorization of termination of employment (\*PHK\*) between PT Swadharma Kerry Satya (Shangri-La Hotel Jakarta) and its workers (Abd. Rochim et al. / 414 persons). The dispute arose from demands raised by the Shangri-La Independent Labor Union (\*SPMS\*) regarding the distribution of service charges, pension funds, and human resource development. Following the suspension of the SPMS Chairman (Halilintar Nurdin), the workers staged a demonstration in the hotel lobby, occupied the hotel premises, refused to resume work, which resulted in a hotel lock-out and property damage.

The Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 227/G/2001/PT.TUN.Jkt dated March 26, 2002) annulled the P4P decree and ordered the employer to reinstate the workers. Dissatisfied with the appellate ruling, P4P (Petitioner for Cassation I) and PT Swadharma Kerry Satya / Shangri-La Hotel (Petitioner for Cassation II) filed cassation petitions to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- \*\*Ratio Decidendi:\*\*

1.  The \*Judex Facti\* (Jakarta State Administrative High Court) committed an error in applying the law by ignoring authentic evidence—namely the South Jakarta District Court Decision No. 22/Pdt.G/2001/PN.Jkt.Sel. dated November 1, 2001 (jo. Jakarta High Court No. 195/Pdt/2002/PT.DKI), which had acquired permanent legal force—qualifying the workers' demonstration and actions at Shangri-La Hotel as an unlawful act (\*onrechtmatige daad\*).
2.  The actions of the workers failing to follow proper procedures (violating Article 6 paragraph (1) of Law No. 22 of 1957 and Law No. 9 of 1998 on freedom of expression in public without police notification), leaving employer assets in danger, and disregarding the Shangri-La Hotel Collective Labor Agreement (\*KKB\*) fall within the category of serious misconduct ex Article 18 paragraph (1) letters h and k of Minister of Manpower Decree No. Kep-150/Men/2000, thereby justifying the authorization of termination.
3.  Labor union advocacy pursued with good intentions does not justify using rough, impolite methods, hotel lobby occupation, or destructive actions causing harm to other parties.
4.  Although formally workers committing serious misconduct are not entitled to severance pay, the Supreme Court, based on considerations of justice (\*ex aequo et bono\*) upholding human rights and worker welfare during an economic crisis, established an award of severance pay equivalent to 3 (three) times the standard stipulated in Article 22 of Minister of Manpower Decree No. Kep-150/Men/2000.

- \*\*Ruling:\*\* Granting the cassation petitions from Petitioner for Cassation I (CENTRAL COMMITTEE FOR LABOR DISPUTE SETTLEMENT / P4P) and Petitioner for Cassation II (PT SWADHARMA KERRY SATYA / Shangri-La Hotel); Annulling the Jakarta State Administrative High Court decision dated March 26, 2002, No. 227/G/2001/PT.TUN.Jkt; and Independently Adjudicating: Declaring null and void the P4P Decree No. 602/358/63-5/IX/PHK/5-2001 dated May 1, 2001, insofar as it concerns severance pay, long-service awards, housing/medical compensation, and the names of the Plaintiffs alone; Ordering P4P to issue a new decree providing: (I) Authorizing the Employer PT Swadharma Kerry Satya / Shangri-La Hotel to terminate the employment (\*PHK\*) of the workers (60 persons) effective as of the end of January 2001; (II) Mandating the Employer PT Swadharma Kerry Satya to pay in cash severance pay amounting to 3 (three) times the provisions of Article 22 of Minister of Manpower Decree No. Kep-150/Men/2000 to workers who have not yet or are unwilling to accept termination; (III) Requiring the Employer to submit data of terminated workers to the Central Jakarta Municipal Manpower Office; (IV) Execution to be conducted under the supervision of Labor Inspectors; and ordering the Respondents for Cassation / Plaintiffs to pay cassation court costs amounting to Rp 200,000. \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Wednesday, October 23, 2002, by Prof. Dr. Paulus Effendi Lotulung, S.H. \[Presiding Judge\], Dr. H.M. Laica Marzuki, S.H., and Ny. Chairani A. Wani, S.H.).\*
