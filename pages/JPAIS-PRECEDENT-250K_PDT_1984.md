## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 250 K/Pdt/1984 dated February 27, 1986, establishes fundamental Civil Procedure jurisprudence regarding the criteria and limitations for executing settlement decisions (\*Acte van Vergelijk\*): A settlement decision or deed that fails to clearly state the definite obligations of the parties (in casu stating only that a debt of US\$ 500,000 owed to a third party shall be discussed further until a proper resolution satisfying both parties is reached) cannot be forcibly executed (\*non-executable\*), and an execution confiscation (\*sita eksekusi\*) carried out based on such a settlement deed must be lifted or annulled.

## Case Background

The Petitioner for Cassation / formerly Objector / Appellee (PT Limasatu Sankyo Industri Pangan, presently PT Supermi Indonesia) filed an objection (\*verzet\*) at the East Jakarta District Court against the Respondent for Cassation / formerly Defendant-Objected / Appellant (\[Party A\]). The objection was filed against the execution of a confiscation on June 23, 1982, based on the East Jakarta District Court Determination No. 26/1982/Eks jo. No. 49/Anm/1981 jo. No. 1/T/1975 G dated June 9, 1982, targeting the Objector's factory assets and noodle-making machinery.

The execution confiscation was requested by the Defendant-Objected on the grounds that the Objector had not fulfilled Article 8 of Settlement Deed No. 1/1975 G dated June 30, 1975, concerning the management of a debt amounting to US\$ 500,000 owed to a third party (Chori America Inc.). The Objector rejected the execution confiscation, arguing that all obligations under Settlement Deed No. 1/1975 G jo. Settlement Deed No. 240 a/76 P dated July 27, 1976, had been completely fulfilled and that there was no promise to pay the debt directly to the Defendant-Objected.

The East Jakarta District Court (Decision No. 130/JT/1982 G dated December 22, 1982) granted the Objector's objection in part, declared the Objector to be a rightful objector, and ordered the lifting of the execution confiscation over the Objector's factory assets and machinery. However, the Jakarta High Court (Decision No. 102/1983 PT. Perdata dated June 27, 1983) annulled the East Jakarta District Court judgment and rejected the Objector's opposition. Dissatisfied with the Jakarta High Court ruling, PT Limasatu Sankyo Industri Pangan (PT Supermi Indonesia) filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The Jakarta High Court erred in applying the law in evaluating the dispositive provisions of Settlement Deed No. 1/1975 G dated June 30, 1975, particularly Article 8.
2.  Although pursuant to guarantee deed No. 29 dated June 9, 1973, the Defendant-Objected's debt to Chori Company Ltd. amounting to US\$ 500,000 was guaranteed by the Objector, the dispute between the Objector and the Defendant-Objected had been terminated through a settlement (Settlement Deed No. 1/1975 G dated June 30, 1975), wherein Article 8 stipulated that the settlement method regarding the Defendant-Objected's debt to Chori Company Ltd. amounting to US\$ 500,000 would be discussed further until a resolution satisfying both parties was achieved.
3.  Because the matter concerning the debt to Chori Company Ltd. has not yet reached a settlement to date, under procedural law, the provisions of Article 8 are non-executable through Settlement Deed No. 1/1975 G because it remains unclear what is to be executed, as it requires further discussion; thus, the appropriate legal avenue for the Defendant-Objected is to file a lawsuit against the Objector in a new proceeding.
4.  Furthermore, the claim of Chori America Inc. was never assigned to the Defendant-Objected via a valid assignment (\*cessie\* pursuant to Article 613 of the Civil Code) that would give rise to the Defendant-Objected's right to directly claim the sum of US\$ 500,000 from the Objector.
5.  Legal Principle: "A settlement decision/deed that fails to clearly state the obligations of the parties cannot be executed, and execution confiscation carried out based on such settlement deed must be lifted."

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation PT LIMASATU SANKYO INDUSTRI PANGAN (presently PT SUPERMI INDONESIA); Annulling the Jakarta High Court decision dated June 27, 1983, No. 102/1983 PT. Perdata; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Granting the Objector's opposition in part; (2) Declaring that the Objector is a rightful objector (\*Pembantah yang benar\*); (3) Lifting the execution confiscation levied upon the movable and immovable properties of the Objector (consisting of noodle-making machinery, noodle-packaging machinery and accessories, diesel engine, office inventory, office and factory buildings, and the land underneath) based on East Jakarta District Court Determination No. 26/1982/Eks jo. No. 49/Anm/1981 jo. No. 1/T/1975 G dated June 9, 1982, executed on June 23, 1982; (4) Rejecting the remainder of the Objector's opposition; (5) Ordering the Respondent for Cassation (\[Party A\]) to pay court costs across all levels of jurisdiction (cassation assessed at Rp 20,000). \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Wednesday, February 5, 1986, and pronounced on Thursday, February 27, 1986, by H.R. Poerwoto S. Gandasoebrata, S.H. \[Vice Chairman of the Supreme Court / Session Chairman\], Yahya, S.H., and Samsoeddin Aboebakar, S.H. \[Associate Justices\]).\*
