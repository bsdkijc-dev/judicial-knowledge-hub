# Disaster-Resilient Digital Justice

# Disaster-Resilient Digital Justice

Disaster-Resilient Digital Justice aims to maintain essential court services during natural disasters and other major disruptions.

## Capabilities

- backup;
- redundancy;
- recovery;
- continuity procedures;
- alternative service channels;
- resilient infrastructure.
