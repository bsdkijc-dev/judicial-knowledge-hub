# External Legislative and Executive Regulatory Agenda

# External Legislative and Executive Regulatory Agenda

The Renstra identifies regulatory matters falling within legislative and executive authority that are relevant to judicial reform.

## Identified Instruments

1.  Draft Criminal Procedure Code
2.  Draft Civil Procedure Code
3.  Draft Judicial Power Law
4.  Draft Supreme Court and Courts Law
5.  Draft Judges Law
6.  Draft Tax Court Law
7.  Draft Bankruptcy and PKPU Law
8.  Government Regulation on Functional Court Clerks
9.  Presidential Regulation on the Supreme Court Secretariat
10. Presidential Regulation on the Supreme Court Registrar's Office

## Strategic Relevance

These instruments are identified as part of the wider legal framework needed to support judicial independence, procedural modernization, institutional integration, professionalization and access to justice.
