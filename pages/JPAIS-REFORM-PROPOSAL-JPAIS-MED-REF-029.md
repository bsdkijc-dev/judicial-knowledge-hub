## Description / Deskripsi

Provides an operational manual for designing, executing, and evaluating courtroom simulations, integrating spatial zoning and sightline geometry for judicial benches, bar tables, and witness stands, role-specific operational mandates, standardized time governance and evidentiary frameworks, media access protocols, and multi-factor performance evaluation architectures.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-031](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-031): Immersive legal simulations require balancing realistic spatial design, procedural rigor, precise role definitions, and structured pedagogical evaluation to bridge theoretical jurisprudence with trial practice.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-032](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-032): Without standardized time management and clear evidentiary boundaries, courtroom simulations risk devolving into unstructured debates.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-033](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-033): Uncontrolled media access or improper participant decorum compromises trial fairness, witness integrity, and institutional simulation realism.

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-029](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-029): Indonesian judicial and advocacy training programs incorporate structured courtroom simulations and practical trial exercises to test evidentiary rules and procedural compliance.

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-029](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-029): Leading international judicial colleges and training centers enforce strict spatial hierarchies, technological integration, and multi-factor grading rubrics during trial simulations.

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-010](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-010): Informal or ad-hoc simulation planning often lacks standardized courtroom spatial zoning, rigorous time governance, and multi-dimensional evaluation rubrics.

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Adopt a standardized courtroom simulation operations manual establishing precise architectural layouts, role mandates, time allocations, simplified evidence rules, media standing orders, and a four-phase debriefing framework.

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-031](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-031): Configure simulated courtrooms with strict spatial segregation of judicial benches, bar tables, witness stands, and jury boxes.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-032](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-032): Enforce explicit time allocations and countdown timers across opening statements, cases-in-chief, and closing arguments.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-033](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-033): Execute a four-phase pedagogical debriefing immediately following trials to provide recursive feedback and systemic reflection.

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-029](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-029): 90-day institutional trial simulation pilot utilizing the operational manual's spatial zoning, countdown time governance, and 100-point multi-factor evaluation matrix.

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-029](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-029): 100% adherence to trial time allotments and media standing orders; zero spatial or decorum breaches; comprehensive completion of multi-factor evaluation rubrics.

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Standardization of immersive simulation protocols across judicial training academies and advocacy clinical programs.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Adoption of formal media standing orders, administrative contempt enforcement rules, and expert qualification \*voir dire\* protocols within simulated court environments.

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Structured courtroom simulations bridge theoretical jurisprudence and practical trial advocacy, enabling participants to master statutory application and evidentiary mechanics.
- Multi-factor evaluation rubrics and four-phase debriefing sessions transform experiential exercises into measurable competency-based learning.
