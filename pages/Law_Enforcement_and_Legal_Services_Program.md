# Law Enforcement and Legal Services Program

The Renstra identifies the **Program Penegakan dan Pelayanan Hukum** as a major program supporting judicial case resolution and legal services.

## Program Outcomes

The program covers:

- Supreme Court case resolution;
- General Courts;
- Religious Courts;
- Military Courts;
- Administrative Courts;
- services for vulnerable groups;
- technical judicial support.
