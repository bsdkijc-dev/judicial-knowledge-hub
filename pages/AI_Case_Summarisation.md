# AI Case Summarisation

AI Case Summarisation is proposed to generate structured summaries of long judicial records for authorised users.

## Requirements

- source links;
- human validation;
- controlled deployment;
- defined risk tier;
- measurable outcome.

## Dependencies

- data quality;
- legal authority;
- cybersecurity;
- user training;
- interoperability.

## Governance

The system should have an explicit retirement criterion so that obsolete technology does not become a permanent institutional liability.
