# AI Case Triage

AI Case Triage is proposed to support administrative identification and routing of cases.

## Intended Uses

- administrative categorisation;
- workload analysis;
- identification of processing requirements.

## Boundary

Triage must not determine judicial outcome or substitute for judicial authority.

## Controls

- human oversight;
- risk classification;
- auditability;
- performance monitoring;
- exception handling.
