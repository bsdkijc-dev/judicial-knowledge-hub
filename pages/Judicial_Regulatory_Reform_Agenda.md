# Judicial Regulatory Reform Agenda

# Judicial Regulatory Reform Agenda

The Renstra's Regulatory Framework Matrix identifies regulatory needs within the Supreme Court's authority.

## Priority Areas

1.  Civil judgment execution
2.  Court mediation
3.  Simple claims
4.  Women in conflict with the law
5.  Restitution and compensation
6.  Sentencing under the Anti-Corruption Law
7.  Narcotics sentencing
8.  Personal-data protection in judicial proceedings
9.  Judicial promotion and transfer
10. Human-resource governance
11. Internal judicial supervision
12. Digital judicial administration and case management

## Strategic Logic

**Regulatory Gap → Regulatory Reform → Institutional Capability** → Better Judicial Services
