# Judicial Knowledge Wiki–AI Ecosystem

The Wiki should become one layer in an integrated judicial knowledge ecosystem.

## Architecture

**Official Sources ↓ Knowledge Repository ↓ Judicial Knowledge Wiki ↓ Ontology + Knowledge Graph ↓ Semantic Search ↓ AI-Assisted Research ↓ Human Verification ↓ Judicial Use**

## Strategic Outcome

A future judiciary in which knowledge is connected, searchable, traceable and institutionally preserved.
