# Judicial Education Reform

The Blueprint identifies judicial education and training as a strategic support function.

## Core Objectives

Education and training should support:

- competency;
- professionalism;
- integrity;
- career development;
- institutional needs.

## Modern Extension

A contemporary judicial competency framework may additionally include:

- digital literacy;
- data literacy;
- AI literacy;
- research literacy;
- knowledge-management literacy.

**Note:**

These modern competencies are architectural extensions of the Wiki, not claims that the 2010 Blueprint explicitly established them.
