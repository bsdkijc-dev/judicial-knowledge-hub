# Judicial-policy-supervisory-control-court-management-2009

**The Supervisory Control and Court Management Policy Framework** (*Oberaufsicht und Justizmanagement*) is a constitutional policy and judicial management framework formulated by Prof. Dr. iur. Andreas Lienhard (University of Bern). It analyzes the reciprocal relationship between parliamentary high-level oversight (*parliamentary supervisory control / Oberaufsicht*), judicial self-government, and the ten elements of good court management, ensuring that these mechanisms do not violate the principle of judicial independence under the Swiss Federal Constitution (*Bundesverfassung - BV 1999*) \[1, 3, 7, 25, 28, 31–34, 83–85\].

## Summary

Published by Prof. Dr. iur. Andreas Lienhard (University of Bern) in *Die Schweizer Richterzeitung* and the *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA), this policy framework responds to a new era of Swiss judicial accountability triggered by political demands for a 20% budget cut to the Federal Supreme Court in 2005 \[1–2\]. Addressing the tension between judicial independence and political oversight, this study asserts that parliamentary oversight (*Oberaufsicht*) is strictly limited to administrative and budgetary efficiency, explicitly prohibiting the evaluation of the substantive content of judicial decisions (*Art. 26 para. 4 ParlA*) \[9–10, 13–15\]. To guarantee judicial quality, this framework formulates the ten elements of good court management, integrating the right of judicial self-government (*Art. 188 para. 3 BV*) with modern management instruments \[28, 33–35, 83–85\].

## Legal Basis

- **Swiss Federal Constitution (Bundesverfassung - BV 1999):**
  - *Article 168(1) & Article 169(1) BV:* The authority of the Parliament to elect judges and exercise high-level supervisory control (*Oberaufsicht*) over federal courts.
  - *Article 188(3) BV:* The right of judicial self-government/self-administration of the Federal Supreme Court.
  - *Article 191c & Article 30(1) BV:* The constitutional guarantee of institutional and procedural judicial independence.
  - *Article 126(1) & Article 170 BV:* The principles of cost efficiency in the use of budgetary resources and the effectiveness requirement for public duties.
- **Federal Act on the Federal Assembly (Parliament Act / ParlA, SR 171.10):** *Article 26* establishes oversight criteria (legality, appropriateness, efficiency, effectiveness) and strictly forbids the evaluation of the substance of judicial rulings.
- **Federal Supreme Court Act (FSCA / BGG, SR 173.110):** Establishes the structure and self-governance of the Federal Supreme Court.
- **Ordinance on Appointments to the Federal Supreme Court (June 23, 2006, SR 173.110.1):** Mandates the establishment of judicial controlling procedures.

## Background

Political statements demanding a 20% budget cut for the judiciary in 2005 ended the era where courts existed in a "sheltered realm of public respect" \[1–2\]. The courts were suddenly required to prove that they managed state resources efficiently without sacrificing judicial independence. This sparked intensive discussions and court management reforms at the federal and cantonal levels through the adoption of *New Public Management* (NPM) instruments.

## Objectives

1\. Firmly establish the constitutional boundaries of parliamentary oversight (*Oberaufsicht*), focusing on administration and budget efficiency without interfering with adjudicative independence \[7–10, 15, 23, 83–84\]. 2. Operationalize the right of judicial self-government based on Article 188(3) of the BV 1999. 3. Implement the ten elements of good court management to guarantee prompt and timely adjudication \[30, 33–35, 83–85\]. 4. Establish a system of differentiated information dissemination within judicial controlling procedures.

## Scope

This policy applies to the Swiss Federal Supreme Court, the Federal Administrative Court, the Federal Criminal Court, and cantonal supreme courts throughout the Swiss Confederation (e.g., Bern, Zug, Solothurn, Lucerne, Zurich, Aargau, Fribourg, Geneva, Ticino, Jura, Schaffhausen).

## Key Provisions

- **Limits of Parliamentary Oversight (Art. 26 ParlA):** Oversight focuses on legality, appropriateness, efficiency, and effectiveness. Evaluation of the content of judicial decisions is legally prohibited.
- **Ten Elements of Good Court Management:**

1.  *Strategic Basis:* Operationalizing statutory mandates through strategic planning (e.g., "Restruct" and "Reorg" projects).
2.  *Client-Friendly:* Services oriented towards the needs of court users (e.g., comprehensive surveys in Canton Bern) \[37–38\].
3.  *Job Satisfaction:* Managing the intrinsic/extrinsic motivation of staff (e.g., "Stawa-flow" project in Solothurn) \[39–40\].
4.  *Management Structures:* Separation of powers between the Plenary Assembly (*Plenum*) and the Management Board.
5.  *Management Support:* Appointment of a Secretary General / Justice Manager to handle administration.
6.  *Steering Instruments:* Output-oriented resource agreements and performance mandates.
7.  *Caseload Management:* Measuring workload using 5 variant methodologies \[61–63\].
8.  *Court Controlling:* Periodic ICT-based oversight with differentiated information dissemination.
9.  *Quality Management:* Adoption of standards such as CAF, ISO 9001, TCPS, and CEPEJ indicators \[71–76\].
10. *External Certification:* Testing court administration quality by independent accredited bodies (e.g., SQS/DEKRA certification of the Bolzano Prosecution Office) \[80–82\].

## Implementation

- **Differentiated Information Dissemination:** Individual judge performance data is exclusively accessed internally by court leadership; parliamentary commissions receive condensed reports; and the public receives aggregate annual reports.
- **Implementation of the 2007 Controlling System:** The ratification of the *Konzept Controlling Bundesgericht zuhanden der GPK* as a cooperative agreement between the parliament and the supreme court.

## Policy Problems

- **Danger of Disclosing Individual Judge Data:** The risk of politicizing judicial re-selection if daily individual performance data is handed over raw to the parliament \[65–68\].
- **Overlapping Oversight Committees:** The complexity of coordinating among the Control Committees (GPK), Finance Committees, Court Commission, and the Federal Audit Office (EFK) \[20–21\].

## Reform / Change

- Institutionalization of the *Council of Justice (Conseil Supérieur de la Magistrature)* in Swiss cantons as an independent body for overseeing and nominating judges.
- Application of external quality certification (ISO 9001) to strengthen public and parliamentary trust without inviting intervention.

## Multilingual Summaries (Swiss National Languages)

### Deutsch (Schweizer Hochdeutsch)

**Rahmenwerk für die Oberaufsicht und das Justizmanagement** Dieses von Prof. Dr. iur. Andreas Lienhard (Universität Bern) im *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA, 2009/2013) und der *Schweizer Richterzeitung* veröffentlichte Rahmenwerk analysiert die Beziehung zwischen der parlamentarischen Oberaufsicht, der richterlichen Selbstverwaltung und dem Gerichtsmanagement. Um die Spannung zwischen richterlicher Unabhängigkeit und politischer Kontrolle zu überwinden, stellt die Studie klar, dass sich die Oberaufsicht strikt auf administrative und budgetäre Effizienz beschränkt (Art. 26 Abs. 4 ParlG). Zur Sicherung der Justizqualität formuliert das Konzept "Zehn Elemente eines guten Gerichtsmanagements", die Selbstverwaltung mit modernen Managementinstrumenten kombinieren.

- **Rechtsgrundlage:** Bundesverfassung (BV Art. 168, 169, 170, 188(3), 191c, 126); Parlamentsgesetz (ParlG Art. 26); Bundesgerichtsgesetz (BGG).

### Français

**Cadre de la haute surveillance et de la gestion des tribunaux** Publié par le Prof. Dr. iur. Andreas Lienhard (Université de Berne) dans la *Schweizer Richterzeitung* et l' *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA, 2009/2013), ce cadre politique analyse la relation entre la haute surveillance parlementaire, l'autonomie judiciaire et la gestion des tribunaux. Pour surmonter la tension entre l'indépendance judiciaire et le contrôle politique, l'étude affirme que la haute surveillance se limite strictement à l'efficacité administrative et budgétaire (Art. 26 al. 4 LParl). Pour garantir la qualité de la justice, le cadre formule les « dix éléments d'une bonne gestion des tribunaux », intégrant l'autonomie judiciaire aux instruments de gestion modernes.

- **Base Légale:** Constitution fédérale (Cst. art. 168, 169, 170, 188(3), 191c, 126) ; Loi sur le Parlement (LParl Art. 26) ; Loi sur le Tribunal fédéral (LTF).

### Italiano

**Quadro per l'alta vigilanza e la gestione dei tribunali** Pubblicato dal Prof. Dr. iur. Andreas Lienhard (Università di Berna) nella *Schweizer Richterzeitung* e nell' *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA, 2009/2013), questo quadro politico analizza la relazione tra l'alta vigilanza parlamentare, l'autogoverno giudiziario e la gestione dei tribunali. Per superare la tensione tra indipendenza giudiziaria e controllo politico, lo studio afferma che l'alta vigilanza è strettamente limitata all'efficienza amministrativa e di bilancio (Art. 26 cpv. 4 LParl). Per garantire la qualità della giustizia, il quadro formula i "dieci elementi di una buona gestione dei tribunali", integrando l'autogoverno giudiziario con i moderni strumenti di gestione.

- **Base Legale:** Costituzione federale (Cost. art. 168, 169, 170, 188(3), 191c, 126); Legge sul Parlamento (LParl Art. 26); Legge sul Tribunale federale (LTF).

### Rumantsch

**Cadro per la controlla da surveglianza e la gestiun da las dretgiras** Publitgà dal Prof. Dr. iur. Andreas Lienhard (Universitad da Berna) en la *Schweizer Richterzeitung* ed en l' *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA, 2009/2013), analisescha quest cadro politic la relaziun tranter la controlla da surveglianza parlamentara, l'administraziun autonoma giudiziala e la gestiun da las dretgiras. Per surmuntar la tensiun tranter l'independenza giudiziala ed il controlla politic, affirma il studi che la controlla da surveglianza è limitada strictamain a l'effizienza administrativa e budgetara (Art. 26 al. 4 LParl). Per garantir la qualitad da la giustia, formulescha il cadro ils "diesch elements d'ina buna gestiun da dretgiras", ch'integreschan l'administraziun autonoma giudiziala cun instruments da gestiun moderns.

- **Basa Legala:** Constituziun federala (BV art. 168, 169, 170, 188(3), 191c, 126); Lescha davart il Parlament (LParl Art. 26); Lescha davart il Tribunal federal (LTF).
