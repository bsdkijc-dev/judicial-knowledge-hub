## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 17 K/MIL/2004 dated June 29, 2004, establishes an important legal principle regarding the limits of criminal (military) court competence in disputes originating from civil debt-receivable relations. The Supreme Court affirmed that if the core issue in a case stems from a debt-receivable obligation involving collateral and mutual agreements to substitute collateral items between parties, the matter falls within the scope of civil jurisdiction. Consequently, the criminal court lacks the authority to adjudicate the case; thus, lower court rulings were annulled, and the Military Prosecutor's indictment was declared inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*).

## Case Background

The Defendant, \[Defendant\] (Pelda/Peltu Nrp. 523361, Ba Tilat Alin Den Pusdiktop, Pusdiktop Kodiklat TNI AD), was charged with the criminal offense of embezzlement violating Article 372 of the KUHP. The case originated from a debt-receivable transaction in September 2001, where Witness I (\[Witness B\]) and \[Witness D\] borrowed Rp 4,000,000 from the Defendant, providing 1 Daihatsu Zebra car (License Plate B-2269-AB) belonging to \[Witness D\] as collateral. Because \[Witness D\] failed to fulfill his repayment promise, the collateral was replaced with a Suzuki Jimny vehicle belonging to Witness II (Kopka \[Witness A\]), and later substituted again with 1 Nissan sedan (License Plate AD-8157-PA) belonging to Witness III (Mr. \[Witness C\]), accompanied by a written collateral exchange agreement dated October 1, 2001. Subsequently, the Defendant pledged the Nissan sedan to Witness IV (Mr. \[Witness E\]) for Rp 6,000,000 without the consent of Witness III.

The Yogyakarta Military Court II-11 (Decision No. PUT/40-K/MM.II-11/AD/IV/2003) sentenced the Defendant to 3 months of imprisonment under Article 372 of the KUHP. On appeal, the Jakarta High Military Court II (Decision No. PUT/47-K/BDG/MMT-II/AD/VIII/2003) annulled the Yogyakarta Military Court ruling, declared the Defendant not legally proven guilty, and acquitted him of all charges. The Military Prosecutor subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** Because the core issue in this case is a debt-receivable matter involving collateral (namely vehicles between the Defendant and Witness \[Witness D\], followed by a substitution of collateral belonging to Witness III pursuant to the written collateral agreement dated October 1, 2001), this matter falls within the scope of civil jurisprudence; hence, the criminal court lacks competence to adjudicate it.
- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from the Petitioner for Cassation MILITARY PROSECUTOR AT MILITARY PROSECUTOR'S OFFICE II-11 YOGYAKARTA; Annulling the Jakarta High Military Court II decision dated August 7, 2003, No. PUT/47-K/BDG/MMT-II/AD/VIII/2003 and the Yogyakarta Military Court II-11 decision dated April 29, 2003, No. PUT/40-K/MM.II-11/AD/IV/2003; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring the indictment of the Military Prosecutor at Military Prosecutor's Office II-11 Yogyakarta inadmissible; (2) Charging all procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State.
