# Timely Transmission of Appellate Judgments

# Timely Transmission of Appellate Judgment Copies

Indicator 1.2 measures the percentage of judgment copies transmitted on time by appellate courts to the referring courts.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Copies sent to referring courts on time}} {\text{Cases decided}}\times100\\ \</math\>

## Strategic Function

The indicator measures the timeliness of post-judgment administrative service at the appellate level.
