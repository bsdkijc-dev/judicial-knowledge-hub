# Wiki Maintenance System

The Judicial Knowledge Wiki requires continuous maintenance.

## Weekly

- review new pages;
- check verification queue;
- identify missing citations;
- monitor vandalism/errors.

## Monthly

- review high-risk content;
- review new strategic initiatives;
- inspect category consistency;
- review AI-generated content.

## Quarterly

- ontology review;
- source registry review;
- KPI review;
- technology review.

## Annually

- comprehensive knowledge audit;
- international benchmark update;
- strategic alignment review;
- archive/supersession review;
- publish [Annual Future Judiciary Report](Annual_Future_Judiciary_Report.md).

## Core Principle

**A knowledge system is maintained, not merely built.**
