# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:1992K PDT 2000

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 1992 K/Pdt/2000** (dated October 23, 2002) is an Indonesian civil procedure law jurisprudence precedent establishing two vital legal rules regarding the legal consequences of an appellate court's failure to consider defendant exceptions (*Onvoldoende Gemotiveerd*) and the formal validity requirements of a Special Power of Attorney (*Surat Kuasa Khusus*).

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established two primary legal principles:

1.  **Imperfect Judgment (*Onvoldoende Gemotiveerd*) Resulting from Ignored Exceptions:** If the *Judex Facti* (High Court) annuls a District Court judgment without reviewing and considering the exceptions raised by the Defendant, such High Court judgment must be declared incomplete (*onvoldoende gemotiveerd*) and null and void.
2.  **Validity of a Special Power of Attorney Without Detailed Listing of All Defendant Names:** The failure to exhaustively list every defendant's name in detail within a special power of attorney does not render the power of attorney void or invalid, provided the power of attorney clearly specifies the plaintiff's name, the basis of the lawsuit, the object of the lawsuit, and the name of Defendant I.

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioners for Cassation (\[Party A\] et al.), annulled Bandung High Court Decision No. 556/Pdt/1998/PT.Bdg dated December 23, 1998, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by ordering the Bandung High Court to examine and adjudicate the merits of the case (*pokok perkara*), while ordering the Respondents for Cassation / Original Defendants to pay cassation court costs amounting to Rp 100,000.

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Legal Consequences of High Court Omission of Exception Considerations (Imperfection / *Onvoldoende Gemotiveerd*)

*Is a High Court justified in annulling a District Court judgment without considering exceptions raised by the Defendant?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Unjustified / Procedural Defect.** A High Court judgment annulling a District Court judgment without examining and reviewing exceptions rejected or considered by the District Court constitutes an incomplete judgment (*onvoldoende gemotiveerd*).

### 2. Formal Validity Parameters of Special Civil Powers of Attorney (SEMA No. 1 of 1971 / SEMA No. 6 of 1994)

*Does a Special Power of Attorney submitted by a Plaintiff become void or invalid solely because it fails to itemize the names and addresses of all Defendants (specifying only Defendant I et al.)?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Remains Valid.** A special power of attorney containing the Plaintiff's name, the legal basis of the lawsuit, the object of the lawsuit, and specifically naming Defendant I satisfies the requirement of specificity (*substansi spesifik*). The omission of detailed listings for every individual co-defendant does not render the special power of attorney void or invalid.

### 3. Supreme Court Independent Adjudication Authority Remanding Cases for Adjudication on Merits

When a High Court erroneously renders an inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO) judgment based on power of attorney formalities, the Supreme Court possesses authority to annul such judgment and remand the case to the High Court to examine and adjudicate the merits (*remand for judgment on merits*).

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (*Judex Juris* / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* Bandung High Court (Decision No. 556/Pdt/1998/PT.Bdg) err in applying civil procedure law (*foutieve toepassing van het procesrecht*) by declaring the Plaintiff's Special Power of Attorney void and dismissing the lawsuit as inadmissible (NO) without considering exceptions?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. Bandung High Court misapplied civil procedure law. The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioners for Cassation (\[Party A\] et al.), annulled Bandung High Court Decision No. 556/Pdt/1998/PT.Bdg, and independently adjudicated by ordering the Bandung High Court to examine and adjudicate the merits of the case.

### Question of Fact (*Judex Facti* / Bandung District Court & Bandung High Court)

Examination of facts regarding family relationships among heirs of the late Ny. Idjah and late Dede Sukamaja; validity of Inheritance Rights Certificate Notarial Deed No. 1/KWI-I/1994 by Notary Siti Munigar Temmy Suhandy, S.H.; execution of the Deliberation Minutes Statement dated November 6, 1995, concerning inheritance distribution obligations of Rp 75,000,000 per person; gold conversion calculations for damages; physical possession of assets including the Kopo 333 Bandung residence, Cikutra/Bojongkoneng property, and shares in PT Saudara Tunggal Kemajuan; and conservatory attachment rulings (*conservatoir beslag*) No. 88/Pdt/G/1998/PN.Bdg.

### Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- Doctrine of *Onvoldoende Gemotiveerd* Judgments: Judgments omitting exception considerations or failing to supply adequate legal reasoning for annulments are null and void as incomplete (*onvoldoende gemotiveerd*).
- Doctrine of Power of Attorney Formal Flexibility: Specificity in powers of attorney is governed by the clarity of the dispute object and primary parties, rather than a rigid requirement to list every co-defendant.

## What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

### Established by Evidence

- The Petitioners for Cassation's Special Power of Attorney dated April 7, 1999 / May 21, 1997, is **VALID** and satisfies civil procedure formalities.
- Bandung High Court Decision No. 556/Pdt/1998/PT.Bdg dated December 23, 1998, is legally defective (*onvoldoende gemotiveerd*) and annulled by the Supreme Court.
- Bandung High Court is legally ordered to examine and adjudicate the substantive merits of the inheritance dispute.
- Respondents for Cassation / Original Defendants ordered to pay cassation court costs amounting to Rp 100,000.

### Unproven / Reserved (Awaiting High Court Merits Review)

- The Supreme Court has not adjudicated the substantive material merits of the inheritance dispute in this cassation instance, but has restored jurisdiction to the Bandung High Court to adjudicate the merits.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Special Power of Attorney Formalities vs. Access to Justice:** This landmark ruling provides a critical procedural precedent in Indonesia: *Judex Facti* must avoid hyper-formalistic refusals of public lawsuits merely because a special power of attorney omits exhaustive typed listings of dozens of co-defendant names, provided the lawsuit identity, dispute object, and primary Defendants are clear. Rigid formalism ignoring exceptions produces legally defective (*onvoldoende gemotiveerd*) judgments.

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Is a special power of attorney considered invalid if it fails to comprehensively list all defendant names, and what are the consequences if a court fails to consider exceptions?  
**The power of attorney remains valid, and judgments omitting exception considerations are legally defective.** Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence No. 1992 K/Pdt/2000:

- The omission of comprehensive listings for all co-defendant names within a special power of attorney does not render it void or invalid, provided it specifies the plaintiff, legal basis, dispute object, and Defendant I.
- If a High Court annuls a District Court judgment without considering the Defendant's exceptions, such judgment is classified as incomplete (*onvoldoende gemotiveerd*) and is annulled by the Supreme Court.
