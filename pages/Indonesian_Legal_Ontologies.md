# Indonesian Legal Ontologies

# Indonesian Legal Ontology

The Indonesian Legal Ontology provides a common conceptual model for legal and judicial knowledge.

## Core Concepts

- legislation;
- regulation;
- article;
- case;
- judgment;
- legal issue;
- doctrine;
- institution;
- court;
- process stage;
- date.

## Purpose

The ontology enables consistent classification and supports future semantic search and knowledge-graph applications.
