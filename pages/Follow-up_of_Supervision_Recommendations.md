# Follow-up of Supervision Recommendations

# Follow-up of Supervision Recommendations

Indicator 2.3 measures the percentage of recommendations resulting from supervision or examination that are followed up by the relevant work unit.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Recommendations followed up}} {\text{Total examination recommendations}}\times100\\ \</math\>

## Targets

| 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| 85%  | 85%  | 87%  | 90%  | 90%  |
