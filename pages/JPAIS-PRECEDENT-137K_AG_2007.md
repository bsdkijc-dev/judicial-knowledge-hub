## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 137 K/AG/2007 dated February 6, 2008, establishes pioneering jurisprudence and legal rules in Islamic Civil Law: A wife who files for divorce from her husband (\*cerai gugat\*) is not automatically deemed to be in a state of disobedience (\*nusyuz\*). Even though the divorce suit is initiated by the wife, as long as her \*nusyuz\* is not proven, the husband can be ordered \*ex officio\* to provide \*iddah\* maintenance to his ex-wife, on the grounds that the ex-wife must undergo the \*iddah\* waiting period, the purpose of which includes \*istibra'\* (clearing of the womb) which also concerns the husband's interests. In addition, the Supreme Court affirmed that discrepancies in age notation between the marriage certificate and the national identity card (\*KTP\*) that do not obscure the identity of the parties do not constitute a formal defect (\*obscuur libel\*).

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Petitioner for Cassation (\[Plaintiff\]) filed a divorce suit against her husband, the Original Defendant / Respondent for Cassation (\[Party B\], a teacher), at the Bekasi Religious Court. Both were married on November 20, 1984 (Marriage Certificate Extract No. 324/25/XII/1984) and were blessed with 3 children (\[Party A\], M. Husni Thamrin, and Handi Muamar Khadafi). Their household became discordant starting in 2001 because the Defendant frequently insulted her with foul language, threatened the Plaintiff with a sharp weapon, and failed to provide maintenance for approximately 2 years. The Plaintiff worked to support the household economy, but the Defendant grew jealous and threatened the Plaintiff's colleagues.

The Bekasi Religious Court (Decision No. 688/Pdt.G/2005/PA.Bks. dated August 25, 2005 AD) granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit and pronounced a single \*ba'in sughra\* divorce. However, the Bandung High Religious Court (Decision No. 112/Pdt.G/2006/PTA.Bdg. dated November 28, 2006 AD) annulled the Bekasi Religious Court judgment and declared the lawsuit inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*) on the grounds of \*obscuur libel\* due to age discrepancies between the marriage certificate and the \*KTP\*. The Plaintiff filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The Bandung High Religious Court erred in interpreting the discrepancy in age notation between the marriage certificate and the \*KTP\* as well as the unclear duration of non-maintenance as an \*obscuur libel\*, whereas there was no doubt regarding the actual existence of the marriage and the identity of the parties. The Bandung High Religious Court decision must be annulled and the Bekasi Religious Court judgment reinstated.
2.  Pursuant to Article 41 letter (c) of Law No. 1 of 1974 jo. Article 149 letter (b) of the KHI, although the lawsuit is filed by the wife (\*cerai gugat\*), as long as her \*nusyuz\* is not proven, the judge has the authority \*ex officio\* to order the husband to provide \*iddah\* maintenance, because the wife must undergo the \*iddah\* period for \*istibra'\*, which concerns the husband's interests.
3.  Pursuant to Article 41 letter (b) of Law No. 1 of 1974 jo. Articles 105(c) and 156(d) of the KHI, the Respondent for Cassation is burdened with providing maintenance for his three biological children until they reach maturity (\[age removed\]).

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation \[Plaintiff\]; Annulling the Bandung High Religious Court decision dated November 28, 2006 AD, No. 112/Pdt.G/2006/PTA.Bdg; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit; (2) Pronouncing a single \*ba'in sughra\* divorce of Defendant (\[Party B\]) against Plaintiff (\[Plaintiff\]); (3) Ordering the Defendant to provide \*iddah\* maintenance to the Plaintiff amounting to Rp 1,000,000 (one million rupiahs); (4) Ordering the Defendant to provide maintenance for his three children (\[Party A\], M. Husni Thamrin, and Handi Muamar Khadafi) of at least Rp 500,000 per month starting from the rendering of this decision until the three children reach maturity (\[age removed\]); (5) Directing the Registrar of the Bekasi Religious Court to send a copy of the decision to the local Marriage Registration Official (\*PPN\*); (6) Ordering the parties to pay court costs in accordance with the judicial level. \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Cassation Panel of Justices on September 19, 2007, and pronounced on February 6, 2008, by H. Andi Syamsu Alam, S.H., M.H. \[Presiding Judge\], Drs. H. Hamdan, S.H., M.H., and Dr. Rifyal Ka'bah, M.A.).\*
