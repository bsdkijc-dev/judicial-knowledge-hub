# AI Governance Architecture

# Judicial AI Governance Architecture

AI adoption in the judiciary requires governance before large-scale deployment.

## Governance Components

- AI inventory;
- risk classification;
- legal assessment;
- fundamental-rights assessment;
- data governance;
- procurement controls;
- vendor-risk management;
- cybersecurity;
- auditability;
- human oversight;
- incident reporting;
- independent evaluation.

## Decision Rule

Every significant AI deployment should have a clear:

**Pilot → Evaluate → Scale or Stop**

gate.
