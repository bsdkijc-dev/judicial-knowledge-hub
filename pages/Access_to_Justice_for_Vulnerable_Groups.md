# Access to Justice for Vulnerable Groups

The Renstra establishes dedicated service outcomes for vulnerable groups.

## General Courts

Targets include:

- 100% processing of fee-waiver applications;
- hearings outside court buildings increasing from 80% to 100%;
- judges with competence to adjudicate vulnerable-group cases

` increasing from 14% to 34%.`

## Religious Courts

Targets include:

- fee-waiver services: 97% → 99%;
- hearings outside court buildings: 97% → 99%;
- competent judges: 20% → 50%.

## Military Courts

Hearings outside court buildings:

**90% → 95%**

## Administrative Courts

Hearings outside court buildings:

**100% → 100%**

Fee-waiver applications resolved:

**60% → 60%**
