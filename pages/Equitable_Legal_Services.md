# Equitable Legal Services

# Equitable Legal Services

The second mission is to provide equitable legal services to justice seekers.

## Strategic Orientation

The mission emphasizes:

- equality;
- fairness;
- accessibility;
- legal certainty;
- responsiveness to justice seekers.

## Related Reform Areas

- access to justice;
- vulnerable groups;
- electronic court services;
- alternative dispute resolution.
