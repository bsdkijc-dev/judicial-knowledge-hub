# JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:JPAIS-MED-REF-008

## Summary / Ringkasan

## Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

- Entity ID: JPAIS-MED-REF-008
- Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
- Ingestion Status: Confirmed

## Description / Deskripsi

Evaluates judicial performance measurement and key performance indicator (KPI) architectures across the Indonesian and Japanese judiciaries, analyzing the operational distortions caused by uniform disposition targets (SEMA No. 2/2014) and proposing a balanced reform framework incorporating Differentiated Case Management (DCM), a Weighted Caseload System (CWM), an Appellate Durability Index, and independent blind survey platforms (ICUX)\[cite: 8\].

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-001): Uniform disposition deadlines (5-month SEMA No. 2/2014) and real-time EIS rankings create transactional compliance pressure, incentivizing metric gaming such as pre-registration delays and premature procedural dismissals (putusan NO)\[cite: 8\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-002): Indonesian court workloads and backlogs are evaluated through raw numerical volume rather than complexity-weighted metrics, producing workload imbalances across jurisdictions\[cite: 8\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-003): Current performance indicators treat all dispositions homogeneously, failing to evaluate substantive legal reasoning quality, while counter-side in-person customer satisfaction surveys (SKM/SPAK) suffer from social pressure and metric inflation\[cite: 8\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-001): Centralized digital monitoring framework administered by Badilum via SIPP and EIS v3, reinforced by court quality certification (AMPUH), enforcing uniform 5-month disposition ceilings\[cite: 8\].

## Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-001): Decentralized qualitative oversight centered in the Supreme Court General Secretariat, operating under the Act on Expediting Trials (Act No. 107 of 2003) with flexible 2-year macro-targets and biannual empirical verification reviews\[cite: 8\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-001): Absence of case complexity calibration, weighted caseload models, and substantive legal quality metrics within the executive monitoring framework\[cite: 8\].
- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-002): Lack of independent, blind sampling for public integrity perception surveys and structural integration of appellate durability into first-instance evaluations\[cite: 8\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Establish Differentiated Case Management (DCM) tracks (A, B, C) in SIPP, deploy a Weighted Caseload Allocation Module (CWM), integrate an Appellate Durability Module into AMPUH/EIS, transition customer satisfaction collection to an independent blind survey engine (ICUX) managed by Bawas, and execute a 90-day multi-court balanced pilot\[cite: 8\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-001): Deploy DCM and CWM sub-routines in staging databases, decouple local PTSP survey collection, and establish automated WhatsApp/SMS blind survey delivery via Bawas (ICUX)\[cite: 8\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-002): Process incoming civil and criminal dockets through active SIPP-DCM/CWM modules, enforce weighted caseload ceilings (40 active points/judge), and conduct bi-weekly exception tracking\[cite: 8\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-003): Perform appellate quality audits on procedural dismissals (putusan NO), analyze econometric pilot data against baseline controls, and compile administrative reform reports for Supreme Court leadership\[cite: 8\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-008](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-008): 90-day multi-court pilot program across Pengadilan Negeri Jakarta Pusat, Pengadilan Negeri Surabaya, and Pengadilan Negeri Sleman\[cite: 8\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-008](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-008): Milestone Timeliness Index \$\ge 95\\\$; adjournment interval \$\le 10\$ calendar days; caseload variance coefficient \$\le 0.15\$; weighted clearance ratio \$1.0\$ to \$1.1\$; appellate affirmance rate \$\ge 85\\\$; procedural dismissals \$\le 5\\\$ of civil dispositions; externalized integrity index \$\ge 85\\\$\[cite: 8\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Promulgate a revised Supreme Court Regulation superseding SEMA No. 2 of 2014 to institutionalize DCM tracks, weighted caseload allocations, and independent integrity evaluations\[cite: 8\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Upgrade EIS platform to version 4, revise AMPUH accreditation checklists (Areas II and VI), and enact Supreme Court Decrees governing ICUX blind surveys and the Judicial Decision Quality Framework\[cite: 8\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Indonesian Supreme Court productivity ratios remain high (99.47% in 2023, 99.26% in 2024, 99.54% in 2025) under administrative speed enforcement, while first-instance courts resolved over 2.7 to 2.9 million cases annually\[cite: 8\].
- Japanese civil first-instance proceedings average 12.3 to 12.4 months under flexible 2-year statutory targets backed by intensive preparatory proceedings\[cite: 8\].
