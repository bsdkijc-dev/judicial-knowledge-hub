# Court User Satisfaction Index

# Court User Satisfaction Index

The Court User Satisfaction Index measures the satisfaction of justice seekers with court services against established service standards.

## Assessment Dimensions

- requirements;
- systems, mechanisms and procedures;
- completion time;
- cost/tariff;
- service product/specification;
- competence of personnel;
- personnel conduct;
- complaint, suggestion and feedback handling;
- facilities and infrastructure.

## Targets

| 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| 3.78 | 3.80 | 3.82 | 3.84 | 3.85 |
