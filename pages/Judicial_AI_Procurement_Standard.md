# Judicial AI Procurement Standard

# Judicial AI Procurement Standard

AI procurement should begin with the judicial problem rather than a vendor product.

## Required Assessment

- problem;
- risk;
- expected outcome;
- data handling;
- security;
- performance evidence;
- limitations;
- human oversight;
- incident procedures;
- interoperability;
- migration;
- termination.

## Evaluation

Vendor claims should be tested using realistic Indonesian legal and administrative scenarios.

Generic benchmark performance alone is insufficient evidence for judicial deployment.
