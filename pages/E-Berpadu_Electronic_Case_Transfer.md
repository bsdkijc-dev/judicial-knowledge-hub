# e-Berpadu Electronic Case Transfer

Indicator 1.12 measures the percentage of criminal, jinayat, and military-criminal cases transferred electronically through e-Berpadu.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Cases transferred electronically}} {\text{Total transferred criminal cases}}\times100\\ \</math\>
