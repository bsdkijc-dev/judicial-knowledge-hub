# Initiative Legal Basis

# Initiative Legal Basis

Each initiative should identify its legal and regulatory basis before deployment.

## Mapping

- statutory authority;
- regulation;
- institutional policy;
- procedural authority;
- data authority;
- procurement authority;
- relevant rights protections.

## Rule

**Strategic approval does not automatically constitute legal authority for operational deployment.**
