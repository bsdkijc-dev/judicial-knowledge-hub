## Policy Analysis

- **Background & Core Theme**:
  - Published by the Ministry of Justice, Japan in December 2024, focusing on "Life Restart Told by Persons Concerned and Supporters" (当事者と支援者が語る人生のリスタート).
  - Analyzes recovery/desistance narratives from former offenders and supporters (Cooperative Employers, Half-way House staff, NPOs).
  - Integrates 5 Primary Desistance Factors into the Phase Two Action Framework (Schema No. 93):
    - Motivation for recovery.
    - Basic life needs (food, housing, clothing) alongside employment/educational stability.
    - Healthy interpersonal relationships.
    - Self-esteem and feelings of usefulness.
    - Aligning/sharing needs between affected individuals and supporters.
- **Key Statistical Metrics**:
  - Recidivist Ratio: Among 183,269 total cleared penal code offenders, 86,099 were recidivists (47.0% ratio).
  - 2-Year Re-imprisonment Target: Surpassed the 2012 ministerial target ("16.0% or lower by 2021"), reaching 13.0% for 2022 released inmates.
  - Full-Term Release vs. Parole: 2-year re-imprisonment rate for full-term released inmates stood at 20.2%, whereas parolees recorded a significantly lower 8.6%.
  - Crime-Type Variations: Theft (19.4%), Injury/Assault (11.5%), Stimulants Control Act violations (10.6%), and Sex offenses (6.2%).
  - Demographics: Elderly (65+) re-imprisonment rate stood at 18.3%; female rate stood at 10.8%.
  - Volunteer Probation Officers (VPOs / 保護司): Active VPOs totaled 46,584 (88.7% fulfillment rate against 52,500 target).
  - Local Plans: 100% of Prefectures (47/47) and 19 out of 20 designated cities established Local Recidivism Prevention Promotion Plans.
- **Specific Policy Initiatives**:
  - Employment Support: Enhanced subsidies for Cooperative Employers mentoring youth under 20 (from FY2023).
  - Health & Elderly Care: Expanded dementia screening tests across penal institutions nationwide.
  - Early Intervention: Entrance support adjustments initiated during detention phase at District Prosecutor Offices and Probation Offices.
  - Education & Disability: Implemented Educational Support Packages alongside "Juvenile Training School Transferred Treatment" at Ichihara Youth Correctional Center for young inmates with intellectual disabilities.
  - Local Support Desks: Established "Ristapo" (りすたぽ) community consultation desks under Probation Offices.
- **Interpretations & Structural Inferences**:
  - The 2024 White Paper indicates a paradigm shift from administrative supervision toward a narrative-driven, cross-sectoral social safety network (welfare, medical, educational, labor).
  - The gap in 2-year re-imprisonment between parolees (8.6%) and full-term releases (20.2%) underscores that structured supervision and community reintegration mechanisms serve as primary determinants in preventing re-offending.
