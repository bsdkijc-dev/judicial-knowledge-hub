# AI Readiness Level

# AI Readiness Level

| Level | Meaning                    |
|-------|----------------------------|
| 1     | Unstructured               |
| 2     | Digitised                  |
| 3     | Structured                 |
| 4     | Semantically connected     |
| 5     | Machine-ready and governed |

## Rule

AI readiness measures the usability of knowledge for responsible machine processing; it does not measure legal authority.
