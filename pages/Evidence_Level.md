# Evidence Level

# Evidence Level

**Evidence Level** classifies the strength and verification status of information contained within a knowledge object.

## Proposed Scale

| Level | Meaning                                             |
|-------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| E0    | Unverified claim                                    |
| E1    | Source identified                                   |
| E2    | Source independently located                        |
| E3    | Claim checked against source                        |
| E4    | Expert/human verification                           |
| E5    | Institutionally authoritative or formally validated |

This scale is a proposed Wiki governance mechanism, not a universal evidentiary standard.

## Important Distinction

**Evidence Level ≠ Legal Authority**

A highly authoritative source may provide strong legal authority, while a scholarly source may provide analytical evidence without being legally binding.

## Application

Every substantive claim should ideally have an evidence level.

## AI Use

AI systems should preferentially retrieve high-evidence-level content when answering high-stakes questions.

## Updating

Evidence levels may change when:

- New sources appear
- Law changes
- A decision is overturned
- Verification is completed
- An error is discovered
