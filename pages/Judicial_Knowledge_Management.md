# Judicial Knowledge Management

# Judicial Knowledge Management

**Judicial Knowledge Management** is the systematic creation, capture, organization, preservation, sharing, application, and renewal of knowledge within the judiciary.

## Knowledge Cycle

**CREATE** → **CAPTURE** → **STRUCTURE** → **VERIFY** → **PUBLISH** → **USE** → **LEARN** → **UPDATE**

## Knowledge Sources

Judicial knowledge may originate from:

- Laws and regulations
- Court decisions
- Judicial practices
- Institutional policies
- Research
- Statistics
- Training
- Lessons learned
- Comparative judiciary
- International cooperation

## Knowledge Risks

Potential risks include:

- Knowledge loss
- Fragmentation
- Outdated information
- Duplicate documents
- Unsupported claims
- Institutional silos

## AI Role

AI may assist with:

- Classification
- Summarization
- Search
- Translation
- Entity extraction
- Relationship discovery

AI-generated content requires appropriate verification.

## Strategic Principle

**KNOWLEDGE SHOULD BE TREATED AS INSTITUTIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE.**
