# International Judicial Innovation Observatory

The Observatory tracks selected jurisdictions and relevant judicial innovations.

## Priority Benchmarks

- Estonia;
- Singapore;
- European institutions;
- Australia;
- United Kingdom;
- Korea.

## Comparative Method

The Observatory should distinguish transferable practices from context-specific practices.

Foreign models should generate Indonesian design questions rather than simply being copied.
