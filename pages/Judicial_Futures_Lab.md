# Judicial Futures Lab

# Judicial Futures Lab

The Judicial Futures Lab is proposed as an institutional horizon-scanning and experimentation capability.

## Scope

- emerging technology;
- demographic change;
- social change;
- legal developments;
- international judicial practice;
- future workforce needs.

## Core Discipline

The Lab should distinguish:

- known;
- emerging;
- plausible;
- speculative.

It should not become a technology advocacy unit.
