# Judicial Copilot

# Judicial Copilot

A Judicial Copilot is proposed as a controlled institutional AI service rather than an unrestricted chatbot.

## Requirements

- retrieve from approved sources;
- provide citations;
- preserve appropriate audit logs;
- clearly identify uncertainty;
- prevent autonomous exercise of judicial authority.

## Core Rule

**AI recommends → Human verifies → Judge decides**
