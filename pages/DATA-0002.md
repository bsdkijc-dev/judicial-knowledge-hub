# DATA-0002

# Judicial Data

**Judicial data** refers to structured or unstructured information generated, collected, maintained, or used in connection with judicial institutions and processes.

## Data Categories

Potential categories include:

- Case data
- Decision data
- Court statistics
- Administrative data
- Personnel data
- Financial data
- Training data
- Digital service data
- User data
- Research data

## Case Data

Case-related information may include:

- Case identification
- Filing
- Procedural events
- Parties
- Claims
- Decisions
- Appeals
- Disposition

The availability and publication of specific fields depends on applicable legal and privacy requirements.

## Judicial Statistics

Potential indicators include:

- Incoming cases
- Disposed cases
- Pending cases
- Case duration
- Backlog
- Clearance rate

Indicators must be defined consistently before comparison.

## Data Quality

Before using judicial data for research or AI, evaluate:

- Completeness
- Accuracy
- Consistency
- Timeliness
- Provenance
- Missingness
- Bias

## Data for AI

Judicial AI systems may require datasets for:

- Training
- Validation
- Testing
- Monitoring

Data used for one purpose should not automatically be assumed appropriate for another.

## Privacy

Judicial data may contain personal or sensitive information.

Data governance should therefore address applicable privacy, security, and access requirements.

## Open Judicial Data

Where legally permissible, public judicial data can support:

- Research
- Transparency
- Public accountability
- Policy analysis
- Education
- Innovation

Publication decisions must comply with applicable law and policy.
