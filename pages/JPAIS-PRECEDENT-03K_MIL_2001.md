## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 03 K/MIL/2001 establishes a crucial legal precedent regarding the evidentiary standards of official authority in bribery offenses. The Supreme Court affirmed that the statutory element of acting "contrary to authority or obligations concerning public interest" was unproven, as the authority and obligation to select and nominate the candidate for Regent of Bantul rested with the Bantul Regency Regional House of Representatives (D DPRD Tk. II Bantul), rather than Soeharto in his capacity as Chairman of the Dharmais Foundation. Consequently, the bribery charges were legally unestablished, resulting in the complete acquittal of the Defendant and the restoration of his honor and dignity.

## Case Background

The Defendant, \[Defendant\] (Colonel Art. NRP 22083 / 22093, former Regent of Bantul for the 1991–1996 term), was charged with giving bribes or inducing an official (Article 209 paragraph 1 item 1 of the KUHP / Article 2 of Law No. 11 of 1980) in connection with his efforts to be nominated and reappointed as Regent of Bantul for the 1996–2001 term. The Defendant was accused of issuing a letter of commitment to donate Rp 1,000,000,000 (one billion rupiahs) to the Dharmais Foundation, chaired by President Soeharto, to secure approval for his reappointment.

The Jakarta High Military Court II (Decision No. PUT.07-05/K-AD/MMT.II/VII/1999) sentenced the Defendant to 9 months of imprisonment. On appeal, the Supreme Military Court in Jakarta (Decision No. PUT/03/MMA/BDG/II/2000) modified the ruling, sentencing the Defendant to 6 months of imprisonment and a fine of Rp 10,000,000. The Defendant subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** Because the element contrary to the authority or obligation to appoint the Defendant as Regent fell outside the jurisdiction and responsibility of Soeharto as Chairman of the Dharmais Foundation—resting instead within the authority and obligation of the Bantul Regency Regional House of Representatives (DPRD) to conduct elections and nominate candidates—the elements of the bribery offense were not legally proven, rendering the Supreme Military Court's decision subject to annulment.
- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition of the Petitioner / Defendant \[Defendant\]; annulling the decisions of the Supreme Military Court Jakarta No. PUT/03/MMA/BDG/II/2000 and the High Military Court II Jakarta No. PUT.07-05/K-AD/MMT.II/VII/1999; and adjudicating independently: Declaring the Defendant not legally and convincingly proven guilty of the charges under the Primary, Subsidiary, or Secondary indictments; acquitting the Defendant of all charges; and restoring the Defendant's capacity, position, honor, and dignity (rehabilitation).
