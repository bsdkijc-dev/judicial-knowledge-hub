# Judicial Integrity Observatory

# Judicial Integrity Observatory

The Judicial Integrity Observatory is proposed as a strategic institutional mechanism for monitoring integrity risks and trends.

## Potential Functions

- aggregate integrity indicators;
- monitor emerging risks;
- support research;
- produce institutional insights;
- support preventive interventions.

## Principle

**Observe → Analyse → Verify → Respond**
