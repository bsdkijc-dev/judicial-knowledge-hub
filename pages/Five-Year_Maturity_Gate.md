# Five-Year Maturity Gate

# Five-Year Maturity Gate

Judicial maturity should be reassessed at major strategic gates.

## Review

- institutional readiness;
- process maturity;
- data;
- technology;
- people;
- outcomes.

## Purpose

The five-year review creates a learning loop rather than treating baseline assessment as a one-time exercise.
