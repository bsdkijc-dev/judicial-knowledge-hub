# Indonesian Legal Ontology

# Indonesian Legal Ontology

The Indonesian Legal Ontology is proposed as a formal conceptual structure for representing judicial and legal knowledge.

## Potential Classes

- Legal Rule
- Regulation
- Court
- Judge
- Case
- Judgment
- Legal Issue
- Doctrine
- Procedure
- Evidence
- Party
- Remedy
- Legal Principle
- Strategic Indicator

## Relationship Examples

**Case → applies → Legal Rule**

**Judgment → interprets → Legal Rule**

**Judgment → resolves → Legal Issue**

**Institution → publishes → Judgment**

**Indicator → measures → Strategic Target**
