# Human-in-the-Loop

**Human-in-the-loop** refers to an AI system design in which human actors retain defined roles in reviewing, approving, correcting, or controlling AI outputs or actions.

## Basic Model

**AI GENERATES** → **HUMAN REVIEWS** → **HUMAN DECIDES** → **SYSTEM RECORDS**

The appropriate model depends on the risk and purpose of the system.

## Meaningful Oversight

Human oversight should ideally include:

- Sufficient information
- Adequate competence
- Real authority
- Time to review
- Ability to reject output
- Ability to correct errors

A human merely clicking "approve" does not necessarily constitute meaningful oversight.

## Judicial Context

Human oversight is particularly important where AI may affect:

- Rights
- Legal status
- Access to justice
- Case outcomes
- Judicial reasoning
- Confidential information

## Automation Bias

Human reviewers may nevertheless over-rely on AI outputs.

Training and system design should address this risk.
