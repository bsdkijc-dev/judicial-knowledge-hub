# Judicial Data Ownership

# Judicial Data Ownership

Judicial data governance must identify institutional responsibility for data.

## Questions

- Who creates the data?
- Who maintains it?
- Who may access it?
- Who may modify it?
- Who may disclose it?
- Who accepts data risk?

## Principle

**Data responsibility must be explicit.**
