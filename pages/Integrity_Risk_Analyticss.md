# Integrity Risk Analytics

Analytics may identify patterns requiring human integrity review.

## Controls

- purpose limitation;
- privacy;
- false-positive management;
- audit;
- human investigation;
- due process.

## Boundary

An analytical flag is not evidence of misconduct by itself.
