## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 39 K/Pid/1984 dated August 28, 1984, establishes a vital legal principle drawing a sharp distinction between the criminal offense of fraud (Article 378 of the KUHP) and civil legal relationships. The Supreme Court affirmed that the legal relationship arising between the Defendant and the witness/victim constitutes a civil relationship in the form of a sale and purchase agreement subject to a 1 (one) month deferred payment term, which cannot be interpreted or criminalized as the crime of fraud under Article 378 of the KUHP. Payment delays or defaults (\*nakoming\*) in a commercial transaction cannot serve as a justification or be manipulated into constituting the element of trickery required for fraud.

## Case Background

The Defendant, \[Defendant\] (\[age removed\], trader/farmer, residing in \[address removed\]), was brought before the Kendal District Court under two alternative/cumulative charges:

- **Primair (Article 378 of the KUHP):** With intent to unlawfully benefit oneself, purchasing 10 kg of dried cloves belonging to witness \[Witness\] at a price of Rp 15,000/kg (totaling Rp 150,000) under the promise that payment would be remitted the following month (August 1981). However, after selling the cloves to a third party, the proceeds were not paid to witness \[Witness\] but instead utilized by the Defendant to purchase stink beans (\*jengkol\*).
- **Subsidair (Article 372 of the KUHP):** Embezzlement of the clove property belonging to witness \[Witness\].

The Kendal District Court (Decision No. 28/1981/Kts.Kdl. dated August 23, 1982) declared the Defendant legally proven guilty of the criminal offense of "fraud" and sentenced him to 5 (five) months of imprisonment. The Semarang High Court (Decision No. 32/1983/Pid/PT.Smg. dated July 29, 1983) upheld the Kendal District Court decision in its entirety. The Defendant subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The \*judex facti\* misapplied the law by interpreting the legal relationship established between the Defendant and witness \[Witness\] as the criminal offense of fraud under Article 378 of the KUHP.
2.  The legal relationship occurring between the Defendant and witness \[Witness\] is a civil relationship structured as a sale and purchase agreement with a payment term of 1 (one) month.
3.  The Defendant's willingness to purchase at a price higher than the market rate and his subsequent default on payment (\*nakoming\*) cannot serve as grounds or be manipulated to constitute the element of trickery formulated under Article 378 of the KUHP.
4.  Although the acts charged against the Defendant are factually proven, such acts do not constitute a crime or a petty criminal offense (rather representing a civil dispute/relationship); hence, the Defendant must be released from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*).

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation \[Defendant\]; Annulling the Semarang High Court decision dated July 29, 1983, No. 32/1983/Pid/PT.Smg. and the Kendal District Court decision dated August 23, 1982, No. 28/1981/Kts.Kdl.; and Independently Re-Adjudicating: (1) Declaring that the acts charged against Defendant \[Defendant\] are proven, but that such acts do not constitute a crime or a misdemeanor; (2) Releasing the Defendant from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*); (3) Charging procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State.
