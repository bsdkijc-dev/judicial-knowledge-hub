# Evidence-Based Judicial Strategy

# Evidence-Based Judicial Policy

Major judicial policy proposals should explicitly state their evidence, baseline and expected outcomes.

## Minimum Policy Logic

**Problem → Evidence → Baseline → Intervention → Outcome**

## Strategic Objective

Policy development should become measurable, testable and capable of learning from implementation.
