# Annual Future Judiciary Cycle

# Annual Future Judiciary Cycle

Each year should follow a repeatable strategic learning cycle.

1.  Baseline
2.  Priority selection
3.  Pilot
4.  Evaluation
5.  Independent assurance
6.  Scale or stop
7.  Competency update
8.  Public reporting

## Principle

The Blueprint should operate as a living strategy rather than a document that is archived after approval.
