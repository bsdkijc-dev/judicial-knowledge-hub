# Initiative Timeline

Every initiative should have an implementation timeline linked to dependencies and decision gates.

## Standard Stages

1.  Discovery
2.  Design
3.  Preparation
4.  Pilot
5.  Evaluation
6.  Scale
7.  Institutionalisation
8.  Review
9.  Retirement
