# JPW-EVENT-003

**Legal Philosophy Workshop at Amsterdam Law School (2025)** (*Legal Philosophy Workshop di Amsterdam Law School (2025)*) is an international academic workshop held at the Amsterdam Law School, University of Amsterdam.

## Summary

This workshop was co-hosted by Hadassa Noorda, a member of the Amsterdam Center for Criminal Justice (ACCJ) and the organizing committee. The event brought together leading international scholars to discuss developments in legal theory and the philosophy of criminal law.

- \*\*Not found in the available sources.\*\* \[1–2\]

## Background

This event forms part of the annual Legal Philosophy Workshop series, enhancing international discourse in legal philosophy.

## Objectives

- Bring together international scholars in the fields of legal theory and the philosophy of criminal law.
- Foster in-depth discussions and research collaboration at the forefront of legal scholarship.

## Key Provisions

- Hosting international scholarly discussion sessions on current legal theory research.
- Active involvement of researchers from the Amsterdam Center for Criminal Justice (ACCJ).

## Implementation

Held at the Amsterdam Law School with the support of the international organizing committee in July 2025.
