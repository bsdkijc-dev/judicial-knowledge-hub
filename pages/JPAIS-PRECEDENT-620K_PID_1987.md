## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 620 K/Pid/1987, dated October 13, 1987, established crucial criminal corruption and criminal procedural rules concerning the limitation of additional restitution payments (\*uang pengganti\*) ex Article 34 letter c of Law No. 3 of 1971 and cassation time limits.

First, Limitation of Corruption Restitution: The High Court (Central Sulawesi High Court in Palu) was declared to have misapplied the law by sentencing the Defendant to pay restitution exceeding the provisions of Article 34 letter c of Law No. 3 of 1971. The Supreme Court affirmed that restitution obligations imposed on a Defendant cannot exceed the value of property or gains actually acquired by the Defendant from the corruption offense (in this case, the Defendant's net acquisition was Rp 3,572,000.—, meaning the restitution amount of Rp 8,000,000.— ordered by the High Court was annulled and modified by the Supreme Court to Rp 3,000,000.—).

Second, Limit of Subsidiary Confinement for Fines (Article 30 paragraph 6 of the Criminal Code): Subsidiary confinement for fines cumulatively cannot exceed 8 (eight) months.

Third, Late Prosecutor's Cassation Memorandum (Article 248 paragraph 1 of the KUHAP): The cassation petition of the Public Prosecutor at the Donggala State Prosecutor's Office was declared inadmissible because the cassation memorandum was submitted past the 14-day time limit from the registration of the cassation petition.

## Executive Summary

The central legal principle established in this decision is that additional restitution (\*uang pengganti\*) in corruption cases must strictly reflect the actual unlawful financial gains received by the perpetrator, and procedural deadlines for filing cassation memorandums are absolute.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Restitution penalties cannot legally exceed the proven net proceeds acquired by the defendant from corrupt activities.
- Cumulative subsidiary confinement terms for unpaid fines are legally capped under the Criminal Code.
- Public prosecutors missing statutory 14-day filing windows for cassation memorandums suffer dismissal of their appeals as inadmissible.

## Case Background

The Defendant, **\[Defendant\]** (\[age removed\], Donggala Public Works employee / Technical Supervisor for Inpres Elementary School Project Unit I No. 5/1981 in Damsol District), was charged with continuous corruption and bribery (Article 1 paragraph 1 sub b / Subsidiary & sub c of Law No. 3 of 1971 jo. Articles 55, 64 of the Criminal Code) in the construction project of SD Inpres Unit I Sampaga and Ogoamas Teacher's Official Residence for Fiscal Year 1981/1982 in Damsol District. The Defendant acted as technical supervisor while executing the project unilaterally under company names CV Sinjai Putra and CV Setia Abadi, and fabricated physical progress reports for 100% disbursement when physical construction was only 53.39% and 0% complete.

The Palu District Court (Decision No. 42/Pid/B/1984/PN. Dgl. dated May 13, 1985) sentenced the Defendant to 5 months' imprisonment and a Rp 6,000.— fine. The Central Sulawesi High Court in Palu (Decision No. 10/PID/B/1986/PT. Palu dated August 20, 1986) modified the sentence to 2 years' imprisonment, a Rp 3,000,000.— fine (subsidiary 6 months' confinement), and Restitution (\*Uang Pengganti\*) of Rp 8,000,000.— (subsidiary 6 months' confinement). Both the Public Prosecutor at the Donggala State Prosecutor's Office and the Defendant filed cassation petitions with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Maximum Limit of Additional Restitution Penalties ex Article 34 letter c of Law No. 3 of 1971

The primary issue was the maximum amount of additional Restitution (\*Uang Pengganti\*) penalties that may be imposed upon a convicted corruption offender.

The Supreme Court held that the Central Sulawesi High Court in Palu misapplied the law. Pursuant to Article 34 letter c of Law No. 3 of 1971, additional restitution obligations imposed on a Defendant cannot exceed the value of property or gains actually acquired by the Defendant from the corruption offense. Imposing Rp 8,000,000.— in restitution when the Defendant's actual gain was only Rp 3,572,000.— constituted a legal defect. The Supreme Court corrected the restitution amount to Rp 3,000,000.— subsidiary to 3 months' confinement.

### Maximum Limit of Subsidiary Confinement for Fines ex Article 30 Paragraph (6) of the Criminal Code

The \*Judex Facti\* of Palu High Court violated substantive law by imposing cumulative subsidiary fine confinements totaling 12 months (6 months + 6 months). Article 30 paragraph (6) of the Criminal Code strictly caps subsidiary fine confinement at a maximum of 8 (eight) months.

### Lapses in Cassation Rights Due to Late Submission of Cassation Memorandums (Article 248 Paragraph 1 KUHAP)

When a Petitioner for Cassation (Public Prosecutor) submits a cassation memorandum beyond the 14-day statutory limit from the date the cassation petition is registered, pursuant to Article 248 paragraph (4) of the KUHAP, the right to appeal via cassation lapses and the petition must be declared inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\*).

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Did the Palu High Court misapply the law (\*foutieve kepassing van het recht\*) by imposing Rp 8,000,000.— in restitution exceeding the Defendant's corruption proceeds (Rp 3,572,000.—) in violation of Article 34c of Law No. 3/1971, and imposing 12 months' cumulative subsidiary fine confinement in violation of Article 30(6) of the Criminal Code?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes, the Palu High Court misapplied the law. The Supreme Court annulled Palu High Court Decision No. 10/PID/B/1986/PT. Palu and adjudicated afresh, sentencing the Defendant to 1 year's imprisonment, a Rp 2,000,000.— fine (subsidiary 2 months' confinement), and Restitution of Rp 3,000,000.— (subsidiary 3 months' confinement).

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Palu District Court & Palu High Court)

Examination of facts concerning the appointment of \[Defendant\] as technical supervisor for the SD Inpres No. 5/1981 project in Damsol District, unilateral execution of projects utilizing CV Sinjai Putra and CV Setia Abadi, creation of fictitious physical progress reports, fund disbursement from BRI Palu Branch, and receipt of gratuities/gifts from contractors.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The Public Prosecutor's cassation petition is inadmissible because the memorandum was filed exceeding the 14-day time limit ex Article 248 paragraph (1) of the KUHAP.
2.  Regarding the Defendant's cassation appeal, the Palu High Court misapplied the law by sentencing the Defendant to pay Rp 8,000,000.— in restitution, exceeding the actual financial gains acquired from corruption (Rp 3,572,000.—). Pursuant to Article 34 letter c of Law No. 3 of 1971, restitution cannot exceed property acquired from corruption. Furthermore, cumulative subsidiary fine confinement violated Article 30(6) KUHP.
3.  The Supreme Court annulled the Palu High Court decision and adjudicated afresh, sentencing the Defendant to 1 year's imprisonment, a Rp 2,000,000.— fine (subsidiary 2 months), and Restitution of Rp 3,000,000.— (subsidiary 3 months).

## Ruling

The Supreme Court ruled: 1. Declaring the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation / Public Prosecutor at Donggala State Prosecutor's Office inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\*); 2. Annulling Central Sulawesi High Court in Palu Decision No. 10/PID/B/1986/PT. PALU dated August 20, 1986; 3. Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation / Defendant (**\[Defendant\]**); and adjudicating afresh: Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] proven legally and convincingly guilty of committing "Continuous Corruption"; Sentencing the Defendant to a prison term of 1 (one) year; Sentencing the Defendant to a fine of Rp 2,000,000.— subsidiary to 2 (two) months' confinement; Sentencing the Defendant to pay Restitution (\*Uang Pengganti\*) of Rp 3,000,000.— subsidiary to 3 (three) months' confinement; Deducting the full period of detention already served; and charging court costs.

## Evidence Status and Gaps

### Established by Evidence

- Defendant \[Defendant\] was proven legally and convincingly guilty of committing corruption repeatedly as a continuous offense.
- Actual material gains acquired by the Defendant from corrupt activities amounted to Rp 3,572,000.—.
- The Public Prosecutor's cassation memorandum was submitted past the 14-day time limit, rendering the Prosecutor's appeal inadmissible.

### Not Established / Modified on Cassation

- The prosecution failed to prove that the Defendant acquired Rp 8,000,000.— in proceeds from corruption, leading to the judicial reduction of the restitution penalty by the Supreme Court.

### Uncertainty and Gaps

- Restorative-Reparative Nature of Corruption Restitution: This landmark ruling establishes the fundamental special criminal law principle that Restitution (\*Uang Pengganti\*) is a state financial loss recovery instrument (\*restitutive\*) matching the perpetrator's actual material gain, rather than an arbitrary punitive fine or penalization exceeding proven financial gains.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: What is the maximum limit of additional Restitution (\*Uang Pengganti\*) penalties that a Judge may impose on a Convicted Corruption Offender under Law No. 3 of 1971?**

**A:** A maximum equivalent to the value of property or financial gains actually acquired by the Convicted Offender from corruption. Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 620 K/Pid/1987, additional restitution penalties ex Article 34 letter c of Law No. 3 of 1971 must not exceed the sum of money or property value genuinely acquired by the Offender from corrupt acts. Imposing restitution exceeding a defendant's net proceeds constitutes an error of law subject to reversal on cassation.
