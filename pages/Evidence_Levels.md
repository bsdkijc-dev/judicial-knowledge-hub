# Evidence Levels

# Evidence Level

Evidence Level indicates the strength and authority of support for a Wiki claim.

## Proposed Levels

| Level | Meaning                                    |
|-------|--------------------------------------------|
| E1    | Primary authoritative source               |
| E2    | Official institutional or strategic source |
| E3    | Peer-reviewed or recognised research       |
| E4    | Comparative/international evidence         |
| E5    | Working hypothesis or emerging evidence    |

## Rule

Evidence level must never be interpreted as a legal hierarchy unless the applicable legal framework expressly establishes such hierarchy.
