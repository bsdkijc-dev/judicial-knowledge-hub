## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 98 K/TUN/1998 dated February 8, 2000, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal rules in Land Law and State Administrative Law: Land originating from Western property rights (\*Eigendom\*) that has reverted to the State cannot be subject to ownership status certification issued by Village Heads (\*Lurah\*) and Sub-District Heads (\*Camat\*), as they lack the authority to issue statements of ownership for such lands. The issuance of land titles based on Lurah/Camat statements regarding former \*Eigendom\* land violates Government Regulation No. 10 of 1961 and breaches the Principles of Good Governance (\*Algemene Beginselen van Behoorlijk Bestuur\* / AAUPB).

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Petitioner for Cassation (\[Plaintiff\], SH, retired Principal Senior Prosecutor IV/e / former Deputy Attorney General for General Crimes) acquired a plot of land measuring approximately 1,000 m² on Jalan Sultan Agung, Semarang, pursuant to the Decree of the Minister of Agriculture/Agrarian Affairs dated July 15, 1963, No. SK.I/376/Ka/63 under Freehold status (\*Hak Milik\*). However, when the Plaintiff sought to register the land, obstacles arose. Meanwhile, the Defendant (Head of the Land Office of Semarang Municipality), pursuant to the Central Java Governor's Decree dated August 18, 1987, No. SK.DA.II/HGB/662/I/1929/87, issued Building Use Rights (\*HGB\*) No. 280 in the name of Agung Bunakur Partowidjojo, which was subsequently transferred and upgraded into Freehold Title (\*SHM\*) No. 224 (in the name of Adi Suharno) and SHM No. 225 (in the name of Reddy Susyanto) on June 19, 1992.

The Plaintiff filed a lawsuit seeking the cancellation of SHM No. 224 and SHM No. 225 at the Semarang State Administrative Court. The Semarang State Administrative Court (Decision No. 11/G/TUN/TN/1997/PTUN.SMG dated October 2, 1997) rejected the Plaintiff's lawsuit. The Surabaya State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 91/B/TUN/1997/PT.TUN.SBY dated January 13, 1998) affirmed the Semarang State Administrative Court judgment. Dissatisfied with the ruling, the Plaintiff filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The disputed land originated from Western property rights (\*Eigendom\*) that had reverted to the State. Consequently, Village Heads (\*Lurah\*) and Sub-District Heads (\*Camat\*) lack the authority to issue Statements of Ownership regarding such land, rendering the issuance of Freehold Titles No. 224 and No. 225 based on such Lurah/Camat statements contrary to Government Regulation No. 10 of 1961.
2.  The \*Judex Facti\* erred in interpreting the Ministerial Decree of Agriculture/Agrarian Affairs dated July 15, 1963 (exhibit P.1A), which was incorrectly deemed void on the grounds that the Plaintiff had not performed land registration. Exhibit P.1A contained no registration time-limit clause, and the Plaintiff had already been designated as the lawful owner entitled to erect buildings thereon (exhibit P.2).
3.  The issuance of the Central Java Governor's Decree No. SK.DA.II/HGB/662/I/1929/87 dated August 18, 1987, serving as the basis for the disputed certificates, failed to apply the Principles of Good Governance (\*AAUPB\*); thus, the issuance of SHM No. 224 and SHM No. 225 is contrary to law and null and void (\*batal demi hukum\*).

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation \[Plaintiff\], SH.; Annulling the Surabaya State Administrative High Court decision dated January 13, 1998, No. 91/B/TUN/1997/PT.TUN.SBY jo. the Semarang State Administrative Court decision dated October 2, 1997, No. 11/G/TUN/TN/1997/PTUN.SMG; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit; (2) Declaring null and void (\*batal demi hukum\*) the Defendant's Decree dated June 19, 1992, concerning Freehold Title (\*SHM\*) No. 224 and SHM No. 225, both located in Gajahmungkur Village (formerly Wonotingal), Semarang Municipality; (3) Ordering the Defendant to issue a certificate of freehold title in the name of the Plaintiff (\[Plaintiff\], SH.) covering the disputed land object measuring approximately 1,000 m² (40 m x 25 m); (4) Ordering the Respondent for Cassation to pay all court costs across all levels of jurisdiction (assessed at Rp 100,000 at the cassation level). \*(Decided by Cassation Panel of Justices: H. Soeharto, SH. \[Presiding\], H. Achmad Kowi AS., SH., and Iskandar Kamil, SH.)\*.
