# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:5096K PDT 1998

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 5096 K/Pdt/1998** (dated April 28, 2000) is a fundamental civil law jurisprudence precedent establishing two vital rules concerning the legal standing of submitting Bilyet Giro instruments as debt acknowledgments and the standard criteria and percentage limits for damages regarding loss of expected profit (*loss of expected profit* / *kerugian kelayakan*).

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established two primary legal principles:

1.  **Submission of Bilyet Giro as Debt Acknowledgment:** "The issuance/payment made via bilyet giro to an individual can be equated with a debt acknowledgment; hence, it is proven that the issuer acknowledges having a debt."
2.  **Determination of Expected Profit Damages / Interest:** "Damages for the loss of expected profit aligned with a sense of justice amount to 10% per year calculated from the date the lawsuit is registered at the District Court until the debt is fully paid off."

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioner for Cassation (\[Party B\]), annulled East Kalimantan High Court in Samarinda Decision No. 81/Pdt/1998/PT.Smda. dated July 15, 1998, *jo.* Balikpapan District Court Decision No. 63/Pdt.G/1996/PN.Bpp. dated May 24, 1997, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by partially granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit, declaring that the Defendant (\[Party A\]) owes an amount of Rp 56,996,000, declaring the Defendant's conduct a default (*wanprestasi*), ordering the Defendant to pay the debt of Rp 56,996,000, ordering the Defendant to pay expected profit damages of 10% per year from the date the lawsuit was registered until fully paid, and ordering the Defendant to pay a coercive daily penalty (*dwangsom*) of Rp 100,000 for each day of delay.

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Legal Status of Bilyet Giro Submission as Documentary Evidence of Debt Acknowledgment (Articles 1867, 1881 Civil Code / Article 164 HIR)

*Can the submission of a bilyet giro by a debtor to a creditor be qualified as a valid debt acknowledgment proving a debt-obligation relationship, even in the absence of a specialized written agreement?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Equated with Debt Acknowledgment.** The submission of a Bilyet Giro instrument by a debtor to a creditor is legally equated with a written debt acknowledgment (*erkentenis van schuld*). The existence of a bilyet giro signed by the debtor provides formal and material evidentiary certainty regarding a valid debt obligation, requiring courts to grant a creditor's default lawsuit.

### 2. Standards of Propriety and Determination of Expected Profit Damages (Articles 1246, 1250 Civil Code)

*What is the fair and equitable rate of interest/damages for loss of expected profit under the law when a creditor demands exorbitant interest (such as 10% per month)?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Capped at 10% Per Year from Lawsuit Registration Date.** Demands for 10% monthly interest/profit are irrational and violate principles of propriety. In accordance with equity and justice (*ex aequo et bono*), damages for loss of expected profit are set at 10% per year, calculated from the date the lawsuit is registered at the District Court registry until the debt is fully paid off.

### 3. Imposition of Coercive Penalty Fines (*Dwangsom*) Over Monetary Payment Obligations

The Supreme Court affirmed the propriety of imposing a coercive penalty fine (*dwangsom*) of Rp 100,000 per day for delays by a debtor in executing a final and binding judicial ruling.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* East Kalimantan High Court (Decision No. 81/Pdt/1998/PT.Smda.) and Balikpapan District Court (Decision No. 63/Pdt.G/1996/PN.Bpp.) err in applying the law of evidence (*foutieve toepassing van het bewijsrecht* *ex* Article 164 HIR) by dismissing the Plaintiff's lawsuit on the pretext of lacking a debt proof, whereas the Plaintiff held 2 valid Bilyet Giro instruments from the Defendant?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. The *Judex Facti* misapplied the law. Bilyet giros are equated with debt acknowledgments. The Supreme Court granted \[Party B\]'s cassation petition, annulled the lower court decisions, and independently adjudicated by declaring the Defendant in default, ordering payment of Rp 56,996,000, awarding 10% annual interest from the lawsuit registration date, and imposing a daily *dwangsom* of Rp 100,000.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Balikpapan District Court & East Kalimantan High Court)

Verification of facts concerning the issuance of Bank Pembangunan Daerah (BPD) Balikpapan Branch Bilyet Giro Nos. GA 150805 for Rp 50,000,000 (due May 25, 1995) and GA 150809 for Rp 6,996,000 from \[Party A\] to \[Party B\]; verification of non-payment / empty account status; verification of the Statement Letter dated August 22, 1995, between Drs. H. Andi Syarifuddin, MBA (PT Handai Tolan) and Abdul Kadir (CV Sinar Jaya Kayan Abadi); and review of the wrong-address exception.

## Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

### Statutory Regulations

- **Civil Code (KUHPerdata / BW):** Articles 1243, 1246, 1250 (Default Damages & Interest) & Articles 1867, 1881 (Debt Acknowledgment).
- **HIR (Herziene Inlandsch Reglement):** Article 164 (Civil Modes of Proof).
- **Bank Indonesia Board of Directors Decree on Bilyet Giro:** Legal Rules of Bilyet Giro Instruments as Giral Payment Media.
- **Law No. 14 of 1970 *jo.* Law No. 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court:** Cassation and Independent Adjudication Authority.

### Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- **Doctrine of Bilyet Giro Evidentiary Force:** Submitting a bilyet giro that cannot be cashed due to insufficient funds does not extinguish the principal debt obligation; rather, it functions as written debt acknowledgment evidence in court.
- **Standardization of Moratory Interest / Loss of Profit:** The Supreme Court consistently curtails exorbitant monthly interest claims down to a reasonable 10% per year calculated from lawsuit registration to uphold propriety and commutative justice.

## What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

### Established by Evidence

- BPD Balikpapan Branch Bilyet Giro Nos. GA 150805 and GA 150809 are **PROVEN VALID AS THE DEFENDANT'S DEBT ACKNOWLEDGMENT** amounting to Rp 56,996,000.
- The Defendant (\[Party A\]) is **PROVEN TO HAVE COMMITTED A DEFAULT (*WANPRESTASI*)**.
- The Defendant is ordered to pay the principal debt of Rp 56,996,000 to the Plaintiff.
- The Defendant is ordered to pay interest/profit damages of 10% per year calculated from the date the lawsuit was registered at Balikpapan District Court until fully paid.
- The Defendant is ordered to pay a coercive daily penalty (*dwangsom*) of Rp 100,000 per day of delay.
- East Kalimantan High Court Decision No. 81/Pdt/1998/PT.Smda. and Balikpapan District Court Decision No. 63/Pdt.G/1996/PN.Bpp. are **NULL AND VOID**.

### Dismissed Claims

- The Plaintiff's claim demanding interest at 10% per month (amounting to Rp 91,213,600) was **REJECTED AND ADJUSTED BY THE SUPREME COURT TO 10% PER YEAR**.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Legal Certainty Protection for Creditors Holding Insufficient-Fund Bilyet Giros:** This landmark ruling establishes robust legal protection for business operators and creditors in Indonesia: debtors issuing empty bilyet giros cannot escape debt obligations by claiming the absence of a written contract. Courts firmly qualify the physical bilyet giro instrument as a valid debt acknowledgment to order debt settlement, fair 10% p.a. interest, and *dwangsom*.

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

**Q: Can the submission of a bilyet giro be equated with a written debt acknowledgment, and what is the proper standard interest rate for damages pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence?**

**A: Equated with Debt Acknowledgment & Interest Capped at 10% Per Year.** Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence Reg. No. 5096 K/Pdt/1998:

- The issuance/payment made via a bilyet giro to an individual **can be equated with a debt acknowledgment**, proving that the debtor acknowledges having a valid debt.
- Damages for loss of expected profit aligned with justice amount to **10% per year**, calculated from the date the lawsuit is registered at the District Court until the debt is fully paid off.
- The Supreme Court annulled appellate rulings and independently adjudicated by ordering the debtor to pay the principal debt, 10% p.a. interest, and a daily *dwangsom* of Rp 100,000.
