# Court Innovation Funds

# Court Innovation Fund

The Court Innovation Fund supports controlled experimentation and evidence-based court innovation.

## Funding Criteria

- clear problem;
- measurable outcome;
- responsible governance;
- defined risk;
- evaluation plan;
- potential scalability.
