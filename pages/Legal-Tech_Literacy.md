# Legal-Tech Literacy

**Legal-tech literacy** is the ability to understand and critically evaluate technologies used in legal and judicial environments.

## Technology Domains

Potential domains include:

- Electronic filing
- Case management systems
- Legal databases
- Digital evidence
- Document automation
- Analytics
- AI
- Knowledge graphs
- Digital identity

## Professional Skills

Users should understand:

- System purpose
- Data flows
- Limitations
- Security risks
- Privacy implications
- Reliability
- Human responsibilities

## AI Connection

Legal-tech literacy helps prevent the mistaken assumption that:

**TECHNOLOGY = NEUTRAL**

Technology choices can influence workflows, incentives, accessibility, and institutional power.
