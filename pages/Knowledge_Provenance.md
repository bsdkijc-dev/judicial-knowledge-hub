# Knowledge Provenance

# Knowledge Provenance

**Knowledge provenance** records the origin, transformation, verification, and history of a knowledge object.

## Provenance Chain

**SOURCE** → **EXTRACTION** → **TRANSFORMATION** → **VERIFICATION** → **PUBLICATION** → **UPDATE**

## Provenance Metadata

Potential fields include:

- Original source
- Source date
- Author
- Extractor
- Transformation method
- AI involvement
- Reviewer
- Verification date
- Version
- Change history

## AI-Generated Content

Where AI materially contributes to a knowledge object, the workflow may record:

- AI system
- Task
- Input source
- Output
- Human verification
- Date

## Legal Knowledge

Provenance is particularly important where a claim depends on:

- A specific provision
- A specific decision
- A historical version
- A statistical dataset

## Reproducibility

A researcher should ideally be able to reconstruct how a claim entered the Wiki.
