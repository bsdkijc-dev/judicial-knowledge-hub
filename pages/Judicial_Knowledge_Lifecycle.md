# Judicial Knowledge Lifecycle

The **Judicial Knowledge Lifecycle** describes how institutional knowledge is created, maintained, used, evaluated, and retired.

## Lifecycle

**1. CREATE**

Knowledge is generated through research, decisions, policy, practice, or experience.

**2. CAPTURE**

The knowledge is documented.

**3. STRUCTURE**

Metadata, entities, claims, and relationships are identified.

**4. VERIFY**

Sources and claims are checked.

**5. PUBLISH**

The appropriate version is made available.

**6. USE**

Knowledge supports research, education, administration, or policy.

**7. REVIEW**

Knowledge is periodically evaluated.

**8. UPDATE**

New evidence is incorporated.

**9. ARCHIVE**

Superseded knowledge is preserved appropriately.

## Lifecycle Principle

**PUBLISHED DOES NOT MEAN PERMANENTLY CURRENT.**

Legal and institutional knowledge can change.

## AI Role

AI can assist in identifying potentially outdated pages, but final validation should remain subject to appropriate human review.
