# Indonesian Judicial Knowledge Graph

The Judicial Knowledge Graph connects judicial knowledge objects and their relationships.

## Candidate Knowledge Objects

- legislation;
- regulations;
- judgments;
- legal issues;
- doctrines;
- institutions;
- metadata;
- judicial policies;
- training materials.

## Strategic Function

**Connect → Retrieve → Explain → Reuse**

The graph provides a foundation for source-grounded AI and semantic legal search.
