# JPW-REPORT-018

## ICC Annual Report to UNGA 2015/16 — Wiki Entry

**Report of the International Criminal Court on its Activities in 2015/16** is the ICC's official annual report to the UN General Assembly for the period 1 August 2015 to 31 July 2016, submitted pursuant to Article 6 of the Relationship Agreement between the United Nations and the ICC.

## Summary

The report summarizes the ICC's judicial, prosecutorial, and institutional activities over the reporting period, recording four simultaneous trials, the conviction of Jean-Pierre Bemba, the inauguration of the Court's new permanent premises, El Salvador's accession, and continued obstacles to executing 13 outstanding arrest warrants.

## Legal Basis

- [Relationship Agreement between the United Nations and the International Criminal Court](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Relationship_Agreement_between_the_United_Nations_and_the_International_Criminal_Court): Article 6, on the obligation to submit annual reports.
- [UN General Assembly Resolution 70/264](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/UN_General_Assembly_Resolution_70/264): Paragraph 28.
- [Rome Statute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rome_Statute): The judicial framework, and Article 115(b) on financing arising from UN Security Council referrals.

## Background

Submission of this 71st annual report to the UN General Assembly reflects the accountability relationship and principal communication channel between the ICC and the UN, aimed at strengthening international law-enforcement cooperation.

## Objectives

- Report on judicial and prosecutorial achievements across all active situations.
- Report on operational efficiency and the inauguration of the new permanent premises in The Hague.
- Strengthen the integration of international criminal justice into the UN's 2030 Agenda (SDG 16).

## Key Provisions

- **Prosecutorial Activity**: Management of 11 preliminary examinations and the opening of a full investigation in Georgia.
- **Judicial Decisions**: Conviction of Jean-Pierre Bemba (18 years' imprisonment); opening of the Ntaganda and Gbagbo/Blé Goudé trials; and confirmation of charges against Al Mahdi and Ongwen.
- **Institutional Development**: Relocation to new permanent premises (cost: €204 million); El Salvador's accession as the 124th State Party; and the establishment of the ICCBA.
- **Victim Assistance**: The Trust Fund for Victims (TFV) assisted over 300,000 victims in Uganda and the DRC.

## Implementation

Carried out through close coordination among the Court's organs (Presidency, OTP, Registry), the UN, peacekeeping missions (such as MINUSCA), and civil society organizations.

## Evidence

- Registration of 410 Article 15 communications.
- Participation of 2,571 victims in proceedings, alongside TFV assistance to over 300,000 victims in Uganda and the DRC.
- Submission of 724 visa requests and 224 cooperation requests by the Registry, and 380+ requests for assistance by the OTP.
- El Salvador's accession as the 124th State Party on 3 March 2016.

## Policy Issues

- Slow response by the UN Security Council to 14 findings of non-cooperation.
- Challenges in executing 13 outstanding arrest warrants.
- The need for UN financing under Article 115(b) of the Rome Statute for costs arising from Security Council referrals.

## Relationships with Other Entities

- **[International Criminal Court](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/International_Criminal_Court)**: Reporting institution.
- **[United Nations General Assembly](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/United_Nations_General_Assembly)**: Recipient of the report.
- **[Jean-Pierre Bemba Gombo](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Jean-Pierre_Bemba_Gombo)**: Defendant in the Central African Republic I situation, convicted of crimes against humanity and war crimes and sentenced to 18 years' imprisonment on 21 June 2016.
- **[Bosco Ntaganda](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Bosco_Ntaganda)**: Defendant in the Democratic Republic of the Congo situation, on trial before Trial Chamber VI since 2 September 2015.
- **[Laurent Gbagbo](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Laurent_Gbagbo)** and **[Charles Blé Goudé](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Charles_Bl%C3%A9_Goud%C3%A9)**: Defendants in the Côte d'Ivoire situation, whose joint trial opened before Trial Chamber I on 28 January 2016.
- **[Ahmad Al Faqi Al Mahdi](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Ahmad_Al_Faqi_Al_Mahdi)**: Defendant in the Mali situation, surrendered to the Court on 26 September 2015 on war crimes charges for directing attacks on historic and religious buildings in Timbuktu.
- **[Dominic Ongwen](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Dominic_Ongwen)**: Defendant in the Uganda situation, whose charges were confirmed by Pre-Trial Chamber II on 23 March 2016, with trial set to begin 6 December 2016.
- **[Omar Hassan Ahmad Al Bashir](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Omar_Hassan_Ahmad_Al_Bashir)**: President of Sudan, subject of two outstanding arrest warrants; findings of non-cooperation over the failure to arrest him were referred to the Assembly of States Parties and the UN Security Council after Djibouti and Uganda failed to execute the warrants.
- **[Simone Gbagbo](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Simone_Gbagbo)**, **[Sylvestre Mudacumura](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Sylvestre_Mudacumura)**, **[Walter Barasa](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Walter_Barasa)**, **[Paul Gicheru](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Paul_Gicheru)**, **[Philip Kipkoech Bett](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Philip_Kipkoech_Bett)**, **[Saif Al-Islam Gaddafi](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Saif_Al-Islam_Gaddafi)**, **[Ahmad Harun](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Ahmad_Harun)**, **[Ali Kushayb](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Ali_Kushayb)**, **[Abdel Raheem Muhammad Hussein](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Abdel_Raheem_Muhammad_Hussein)**, **[Abdallah Banda](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Abdallah_Banda)**, **[Joseph Kony](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Joseph_Kony)**, **[Vincent Otti](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Vincent_Otti)**: Twelve other individuals with outstanding public arrest warrants during the reporting period.
