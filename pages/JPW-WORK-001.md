# JPW-WORK-001

## Access Portals for Citizens and Small Businesses — Wiki Entry

**Access Portals for Citizens and Small Businesses** is a 2024 legal research book by Rogier Hartendorp, Priscilla Haricharan, and Tim Lubbers, published by Stichting Onderzoek Rechtspleging (Foundation Research on the Dutch Judicial System) together with Eleven, examining the various forms of legal assistance and the complexity of choosing the right referral route to resolve legal problems for citizens and small entrepreneurs in the Netherlands.

## Summary

The publication addresses the various forms of legal assistance available in the Netherlands and the challenges faced by citizens and small businesses in selecting the most appropriate route for resolving their legal problems.

## Legal Basis

- **Not found in the available sources.**

## Background

In the Netherlands, there are many forms of legal assistance and numerous options for resolving legal problems. This wide availability of options creates particular complexity for laypeople and small entrepreneurs.

## Objectives

- Analyze access portals and legal-assistance schemes for citizens and small entrepreneurs.
- Support understanding of legal-problem resolution routes in the Netherlands.

## Key Provisions

- Identification of the navigational complexity of the Dutch legal-assistance system for citizens and small entrepreneurs.
- Documentation of legal-problem resolution options available at the national level.

## Implementation

The publication was released in 2024 as a first edition of 280 pages, available in paperback (ISBN 9789047302049) and e-book (ISBN 9789400113916) formats through Eleven and Stichting Onderzoek Rechtspleging.

## Policy Issues

- The high complexity of legal-assistance referral choices for non-expert citizens and small entrepreneurs.
