# Mediation Case Resolution

Indicator 1.9 measures the percentage of cases successfully resolved through mediation.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Cases successfully resolved through mediation}} {\text{Cases required to undergo mediation}}\times100\\ \</math\>

## Strategic Purpose

The indicator measures implementation of mediation within the judicial process.

## Policy Context

The Renstra identifies mediation as part of efforts to promote faster, simpler and lower-cost dispute resolution.
