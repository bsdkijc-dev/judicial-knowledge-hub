# Judicial Integrity

# Judicial Integrity

**Judicial integrity** concerns institutional and individual conditions supporting trustworthy, impartial, ethical, and accountable judicial administration and adjudication.

**\[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## Integrity Dimensions

Potential dimensions include:

- Independence
- Impartiality
- Ethics
- Transparency
- Accountability
- Anti-corruption
- Conflict-of-interest management
- Professional competence

## Institutional Integrity

Institutional integrity may involve:

- Governance
- Procurement
- Human resources
- Financial management
- Information systems
- Complaint mechanisms
- Internal controls

## Technology

Digital systems can strengthen integrity through:

- Audit trails
- Access controls
- Transparent workflows
- Data integrity
- Monitoring

They can also introduce new risks.

## AI Governance

AI systems should be governed to address:

- Manipulation
- Bias
- Unauthorized access
- Hidden decision criteria
- Vendor dependency
- Accountability gaps

## Integrity and Trust

Public trust may be affected by perceptions and evidence of:

- Fairness
- Independence
- Transparency
- Competence
- Accountability

Trust should be measured empirically rather than inferred solely from institutional activity.
