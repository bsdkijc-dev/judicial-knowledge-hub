## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 253 K/AG/2002 dated March 17, 2004, establishes important Islamic Civil Procedure jurisprudence regarding claim joinder (\*kumulasi tuntutan\*) and the qualification limits of vague lawsuits (\*obscuur libel\*): The joinder of multiple claims by a plaintiff is justified as long as it combines a divorce claim with its legal consequences as stipulated under Article 86 of Law No. 7 of 1989, whereas other claims not regulated under said article should simply be declared inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*). The entire lawsuit of the Plaintiff should not be dismissed as inadmissible on the grounds of \*obscuur libel\* solely due to such joinder.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Petitioner for Cassation (\[Party A\]) and the Original Defendant were lawfully married in 1978 and blessed with 4 children. During the marriage, joint properties consisting of movable and immovable assets were acquired. Disputes arose because the Original Defendant contracted another marriage without permission with another woman (\[Party B\]) and traveled to Mecca in 1995. The peak of the dispute occurred in November 2000 when the Original Defendant accused the Original Plaintiff of adultery, causing them to separate households, and the Defendant ceased providing maintenance.

The Original Plaintiff filed for divorce (\*cerai gugat\*), division of joint property, security attachment (\*conservatoir beslag\*), and annulment of the Original Defendant's marriage to \[Party B\]. The Mataram High Religious Court (Decision No. 50/Pdt.G/2001/PTA.Mtr dated August 20, 2001 AD / 1 Jumada al-Akhirah 1422 AH) declared the Plaintiff's lawsuit inadmissible in its entirety on the grounds of a vague lawsuit (\*obscuur libel\*) due to the joinder of various claims. Dissatisfied with the ruling, the Petitioner for Cassation filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The joinder of multiple claims by a plaintiff is justified as long as it represents a combination of a divorce claim with its legal consequences regulated under Article 86 of Law No. 7 of 1989.
2.  Regarding other claims not governed by Article 86 of Law No. 7 of 1989, they should merely be declared inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*), and it does not follow that the Plaintiff's entire lawsuit must be declared inadmissible under the consideration of being a vague lawsuit (\*obscuur libel\*).
3.  In accordance with civil procedural principles under the HIR and RBg, a lawsuit is deemed to meet requirements if it states the parties, the factual basis (\*posita\*), and the petitory claims (\*petitum\*) supported by the factual basis.

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation \[Party A\]; Annulling the Mataram High Religious Court decision dated August 20, 2001 AD, No. 50/Pdt.G/2001/PTA.Mtr; and Independently Adjudicating: Rejecting the Plaintiff's lawsuit, and ordering the Plaintiff to pay court costs across all levels of jurisdiction, assessed at the cassation level at Rp 200,000 (two hundred thousand rupiahs). \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Wednesday, March 17, 2004, by Drs. H. Syamsuhadi Irsyad, S.H., M.H. \[Presiding Judge\], Drs. H. Habiburrahman, M.H., and Dr. H. Hamdan, S.H., M.H. \[Associate Justices\]).\*
