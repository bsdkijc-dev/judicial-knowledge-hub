## Description / Deskripsi

This page records the empirical analysis of official judicial statistics and the 7-point Problem Inventory List (Daftar Inventaris Masalah / DIM) issued by the Central Jakarta Commercial Court on 23 July 2026, in response to Official Supreme Court Request Letter No. \`23/KM.BIN/HK1.2/VII/2026\` dated 9 July 2026 (signed by Chairman of the Development Chamber, Syamsul Ma'arif, S.H., LL.M., Ph.D.). Formally signed by the Chief of Central Jakarta Commercial Court, Dr. Husnul Khotimah, S.H., M.H., the dataset establishes Central Jakarta as the primary judicial hub for competition disputes in Indonesia, logging 78 incoming objection filings between 2021 and 2026 (peaking at 46 cases in 2026 alone). The report identifies critical procedural defects under PERMA No. 3/2021, including witness summoning disparities, SIPP software limitations in registering multi-petitioner actions, time-limit contradictions (3–12 months vs SEMA No. 2/2012), lack of court mediation, and the absence of asset-tracing mechanisms for fine execution. All structural entities, yearly caseload matrices, judicial rosters, and problem matrices have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher **Cecep Mustafa**.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-046](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-046): Extreme Filing Surge vs Active Bench Allocation, where the sudden surge from 7 cases in 2025 to 46 cases in mid-2026 represents a 557% increase in caseload, while only 2 judges (Anton Rizal Setiawan, S.H., M.H., and Muhammad Firman Akbar, S.H., M.H.) actively preside over competition panels out of 15 listed judges, creating severe panel burnout and scheduling bottlenecks.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-047](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-047): SIPP Software Architecture Lockout & Witness Disparities, where the SIPP case tracking system cannot log more than one petitioner under a single case register number (blocking secondary co-respondents in multi-party cartel cases), while judicial panels exhibit inconsistent witness summoning practices due to ambiguous language in Article 13 of PERMA No. 3/2021.

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-041](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-041): Under current practice at Central Jakarta, competition objections bypass mediation entirely pursuant to Article 12 PERMA No. 3/2021, relying on administrative dossier review, with over 83% of resolved cases appealed to the Supreme Court via Cassation while trial panels face inconsistent rules regarding new witness hearings.

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-041](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-041): In Japan (Tokyo High Court / JFTC), orders are reviewed under strict administrative record review (\*substantial evidence rule\*), prohibiting new witness testimony unless the JFTC arbitrarily refused to hear the witness during administrative proceedings, providing a model for restricting de novo evidentiary re-openings.

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-041](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-041): The primary structural gap is the absence of explicit statutory authority for Commercial Court judges to order asset discovery during trial or mandate that KPPU submit certified asset schedules of respondents to enable effective civil execution, compounded by SIPP architectural rigidity.

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Amend Article 13 of PERMA No. 3/2021 to explicitly restrict new witnesses/experts not presented at KPPU; abolish the minimum 3-month trial timeframe in Article 14(1) while retaining a 60-working-day cap; re-introduce court mediation under PERMA No. 1/2016; mandate in court judgment amars that KPPU attach certified asset schedules; and upgrade SIPP database schemas to support multi-petitioner master case registration.

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-042](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-042): Issue a Supreme Court Circular (SEMA) standardizing witness summoning procedures and waiving minimum trial duration requirements.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-043](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-043): Deploy the SIPP Multi-Petitioner Update and promulgate the PERMA Amandemen 3/2021 incorporating Central Jakarta's 7-point DIM recommendations.

## Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-041](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-041): Central Jakarta Commercial Court (High-Density Hub) implementation of SIPP multi-petitioner updates and standardized hearing protocols.

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-041](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-041): 100% SIPP recording compliance for multi-petitioner objection filings; reduction of average case trial duration to \$\le 60\$ working days; zero execution failures caused by missing asset inventories in final judgments.

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Expanding judicial panel authority under PERMA No. 3/2021 to allow judges to order prosecution/KPPU evidence production or asset tracing reports during trial, alongside re-balancing judicial roster assignments at Central Jakarta District Court.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Updating SIPP/e-Court database architecture and revising PERMA No. 3/2021 provisions concerning evidentiary limits, trial timelines, and execution remedies.

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Official report document (\*Laporan Data Statistik Perkara KPPU dan DIM Pengadilan Niaga Pada Pengadilan Negeri Jakarta Pusat.pdf\*) signed by Dr. Husnul Khotimah, S.H., M.H., Chief of Central Jakarta Commercial Court, issued on 23 July 2026 pursuant to Supreme Court Letter No. \`23/KM.BIN/HK1.2/VII/2026\`.
