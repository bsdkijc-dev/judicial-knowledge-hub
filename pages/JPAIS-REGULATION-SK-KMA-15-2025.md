# JPAIS:REGULATION:SK-KMA-15-2025

## Summary / Ringkasan

### Bahasa Indonesia

Keputusan Ketua Mahkamah Agung Republik Indonesia Nomor 15/KMA/SK.HK2/II/2025 ditetapkan pada tanggal 10 Februari 2025 oleh Ketua Mahkamah Agung Sunarto guna menjamin kekuasaan kehakiman yang mandiri dan akuntabel serta bebas dari konflik kepentingan dalam penyelesaian perkara di Mahkamah Agung. Keputusan ini memberlakukan pedoman yang mewajibkan para Hakim (Hakim Agung, Hakim Ad Hoc, dan Hakim Yustisial) mengungkapkan dan melaporkan hubungan keluarga (sedarah dan semenda hingga derajat ketiga), afiliasi sosial, serta afiliasi finansial melalui Sistem Informasi Pengadilan (SIP). Laporan tersebut bersifat rahasia dan menjadi salah satu parameter pimpinan dalam penunjukan majelis hakim. Selain itu, pedoman ini mengatur tata cara pengunduran diri wajib bagi hakim dan panitera pengganti apabila terdapat potensi atau terjadinya konflik kepentingan, yang penetapannya dilakukan oleh Pimpinan Mahkamah Agung secara final dan mengikat.

### English

Decree of the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court Number 15/KMA/SK.HK2/II/2025 was stipulated on 10 February 2025 by Chief Justice Sunarto to guarantee an independent and accountable judiciary free from conflicts of interest during case resolution at the Supreme Court. The decree establishes operational guidelines that mandate judges (Supreme Court Justices, Ad Hoc Judges, and Judicial Judges) to report and disclose family relationships (consanguinity and affinity up to the third degree), social affiliations, and financial affiliations into the Court Information System (SIP). These disclosures are classified as confidential and utilized as an objective parameter in judicial panel assignments. Furthermore, it regulates mandatory recusal mechanisms whenever a conflict of interest arises, requiring written approval from the Supreme Court Leadership to reassign case panels and substitute registrars.

## Definition / Definisi

### Bahasa Indonesia

Konflik Kepentingan (Conflict of Interest)  
Kondisi Hakim dalam menangani perkara memiliki atau patut diduga memiliki kepentingan pribadi untuk menguntungkan diri sendiri dan/atau orang lain, hubungan keluarga, afiliasi sosial, afiliasi finansial dengan pihak yang beperkara, atau pernah menangani perkara yang sama pada tingkat peradilan yang berbeda atau dalam profesi lain telah menangani perkara tersebut.

Hakim (Judge)  
Hakim Agung, Hakim Ad Hoc pada Mahkamah Agung, dan Hakim Yustisial pada Kepaniteraan Mahkamah Agung yang bertugas dalam penanganan perkara di Mahkamah Agung.

Pimpinan Mahkamah Agung (Supreme Court Leadership)  
Ketua, Wakil Ketua Bidang Yudisial, Wakil Ketua Bidang Non Yudisial, dan para Ketua Muda pada Mahkamah Agung.

Hubungan Keluarga (Family Relationship)  
Hubungan sedarah atau semenda sampai dengan derajat ketiga atau memiliki hubungan suami atau istri meskipun sudah bercerai dengan hakim, panitera, penuntut umum, advokat, terdakwa, atau para pihak dalam perkara yang sedang atau pernah ditangani.

Afiliasi Sosial (Social Affiliation)  
Keanggotaan atau keterkaitan Hakim dengan lembaga, organisasi, yayasan, atau perkumpulan sosial non-profit.

Afiliasi Finansial (Financial Affiliation)  
Keterkaitan antara Hakim dengan entitas bisnis dan keuangan yang memungkinkan Hakim tersebut mendapatkan keuntungan selain tabungan (seperti saham, aset kripto, reksadana, obligasi, deposito, dan surat berharga lainnya).

Pengunduran Diri (Recusal)  
Tindakan Hakim mengajukan permohonan pengunduran diri disertai dengan alasan kepada Pimpinan Mahkamah Agung karena ada konflik kepentingan dalam menangani perkara.

Sistem Informasi Pengadilan (SIP)  
Sistem informasi yang disediakan oleh Mahkamah Agung untuk memberi pelayanan terhadap pencari keadilan yang meliputi administrasi, pelayanan perkara, dan persidangan secara elektronik.

### English

Conflict of Interest (Konflik Kepentingan)  
A condition where a Judge handling a case has or is reasonably suspected of having personal interests to benefit themselves and/or others, family ties, social affiliations, or financial affiliations with the litigating parties, or has previously handled the same case at a different judicial level or in another professional capacity.

Judge (Hakim)  
Supreme Court Justices (Hakim Agung), Ad Hoc Judges at the Supreme Court, and Judicial Judges (Hakim Yustisial) at the Supreme Court Registry assigned to case processing at the Supreme Court.

Supreme Court Leadership (Pimpinan Mahkamah Agung)  
The Chief Justice, Vice Chief Justice for Judicial Affairs, Vice Chief Justice for Non-Judicial Affairs, and Chamber Chairpersons (Ketua Muda) of the Supreme Court.

Family Relationship (Hubungan Keluarga)  
Lineal or collateral blood relations (sedarah) or relations by marriage (semenda) up to the third degree, or marital ties (husband/wife) even if divorced, with judges, registrars, public prosecutors, advocates, defendants, or litigating parties.

Social Affiliation (Afiliasi Sosial)  
Membership or involvement of a Judge in non-profit institutions, organizations, foundations, or social associations.

Financial Affiliation (Afiliasi Finansial)  
Connections between a Judge and business/financial entities permitting benefits beyond personal bank savings (e.g., stocks, crypto assets, mutual funds, bonds, deposits, or other securities).

Recusal / Resignation from Case (Pengunduran Diri)  
The formal submission by a Judge to the Supreme Court Leadership seeking withdrawal from handling a case, accompanied by concrete reasons concerning a conflict of interest.

Court Information System (Sistem Informasi Pengadilan / SIP)  
The electronic information platform provided by the Supreme Court encompassing case administration, public judicial services, and electronic court proceedings.

## Legal Basis / Landasan Hukum

### Bahasa Indonesia

- Undang-Undang Nomor 14 Tahun 1985 tentang Mahkamah Agung sebagaimana telah beberapa kali diubah terakhir dengan Undang-Undang Nomor 3 Tahun 2009 tentang Perubahan Kedua atas Undang-Undang Nomor 14 Tahun 1985 tentang Mahkamah Agung.
- Undang-Undang Nomor 28 Tahun 1999 tentang Penyelenggaraan Negara yang Bersih dan Bebas dari Korupsi, Kolusi dan Nepotisme.
- Undang-Undang Nomor 31 Tahun 1999 tentang Pemberantasan Tindak Pidana Korupsi sebagaimana telah diubah dengan Undang-Undang Nomor 20 Tahun 2001 tentang Perubahan atas Undang-Undang Nomor 31 Tahun 1999 tentang Pemberantasan Tindak Pidana Korupsi.
- Undang-Undang Nomor 48 Tahun 2009 tentang Kekuasaan Kehakiman.

### English

- Law Number 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court, as last amended by Law Number 3 of 2009.
- Law Number 28 of 1999 on State Administration that is Clean and Free from Corruption, Collusion, and Nepotism.
- Law Number 31 of 1999 on the Eradication of Criminal Acts of Corruption, as amended by Law Number 20 of 2001.
- Law Number 48 of 2009 on Judicial Power.

<td><p><strong>Preventive Disclosure &amp; Reporting</strong></p></td>
<td><ul>
<li>Reporting to detect conflicts of interest is performed initially and must be updated whenever personal circumstances change.</li>
<li>Every Judge must complete the disclosure declaration (Annex II) covering:
<ul>
<li>Personal biodata and previous occupations/institutions;</li>
<li>Lineal blood relations (children, grandchildren, great-grandchildren, parents, grandparents, great-grandparents);</li>
<li>Collateral blood relations (siblings, aunts/uncles, nephews/nieces, cousins, first cousins once removed);</li>
<li>Affinity by marriage (spouse, parents-in-law, children-in-law, siblings-in-law, grandparents-in-law, great-grandchildren-in-law);</li>
<li>Social affiliations (foundations, non-profit institutions, associations, organizational roles);</li>
<li>Financial affiliations (stocks, crypto assets, mutual funds, bonds, deposits, negotiable instruments).</li>
</ul></li>
<li>Completed declarations must be uploaded to the SIP, where they are maintained strictly confidential.</li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><p><strong>Case Assignment Parameter in SIP</strong></p></td>
<td><p>Case distribution and judge assignment through the SIP must integrate recorded conflict of interest data as an objective parameter used by the Supreme Court Leadership.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td><p><strong>Mandatory Recusal Procedure</strong></p></td>
<td><ul>
<li>A Judge must submit a formal written recusal request (Annex III) to the Supreme Court Leadership upon identifying any conflict of interest.</li>
<li>Upon approval, the Chamber Chairperson (Ketua Muda) issues an official order (Annex IV) appointing replacement panel members (Ketua Majelis / Anggota Majelis).</li>
<li>When a Substitute Registrar (Panitera Pengganti) recuses themselves, the Chamber Registrar (Panitera Muda Kamar) issues a replacement decree (Annex V).</li>
<li>Determinations by the Supreme Court Leadership regarding recusal requests are final and binding.</li>
</ul></td>
</tr>

<td><p><strong>Application to Subordinate Courts</strong></p></td>
