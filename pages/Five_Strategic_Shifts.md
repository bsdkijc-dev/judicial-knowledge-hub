# Five Strategic Shifts

# Five Strategic Shifts

The proposed framework identifies five fundamental shifts.

1.  Technology-centred → Human-centred judiciary
2.  AI tool → Institutional AI capability
3.  Fragmented knowledge → Structured judicial knowledge
4.  Raw data → Governed strategic asset
5.  Short-course training → Continuous national capability system

## Strategic Logic

The transformation is therefore institutional rather than merely technological.

**Technology + Knowledge + Data + People + Governance → Better Justice**
