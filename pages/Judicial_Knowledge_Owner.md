# Judicial Knowledge Owner

# Judicial Knowledge Owner

Each major knowledge domain should have an accountable owner.

## Responsibilities

- define scope;
- approve taxonomy;
- coordinate subject-matter review;
- monitor quality;
- manage review cycles;
- resolve ownership questions;
- coordinate updates.

## Principle

**No owner → no sustainable knowledge system.**
