# AI Incident Reporting

# AI Incident Reporting

Judicial AI incidents should be recorded and managed through a controlled institutional process.

## Incident Examples

- fabricated legal authority;
- material erroneous output;
- privacy breach;
- security incident;
- discriminatory performance;
- unauthorized use;
- failure of human oversight.

## Response

**Detect → Contain → Assess → Notify → Remediate → Learn**

## Institutional Learning

Incident records should contribute to future training, system improvement and governance review.
