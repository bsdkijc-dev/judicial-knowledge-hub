# Judicial AI Verification Protocol

AI-generated judicial content should undergo verification before being relied upon for high-impact work.

## Verification

1.  Verify the source.
2.  Verify the legal authority.
3.  Verify the quotation or proposition.
4.  Verify the context.
5.  Check for omissions.
6.  Check uncertainty.
7.  Record the verification where required.

## Principle

**AI output is a research lead until verified.**
