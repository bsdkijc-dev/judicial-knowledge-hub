# Accessible Justice by Design

# Accessible Justice by Design

Accessibility should be built into judicial services rather than added after deployment.

## Dimensions

- physical;
- digital;
- linguistic;
- cognitive;
- informational;
- assisted access.

## Principle

**Digital by default does not mean digital only.**
