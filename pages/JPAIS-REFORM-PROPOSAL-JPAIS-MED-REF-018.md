## Description / Deskripsi

Provides an exhaustive assessment of global maritime security and crime trends from 2015 to the present, examining structural evolutions such as the post-2024 piracy resurgence (137 global incidents in 2025), the exponential expansion of the opaquely owned "Shadow Fleet" (1,300–1,481 active vessels financing state-backed conflict), Red Sea and Bab el-Mandeb chokepoint kinetic disruptions, containerized drug trafficking, and marine environmental vulnerabilities, structured under the Japan-Indonesia ADR Integration System (JPAIS) knowledge framework and styled using the \*\*Mahkamah Agung Corporate University (BSDK)\*\* institutional color hierarchy.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-001): Global maritime crime incidents rebounded significantly between 2024 and 2025, reaching 137 reported incidents characterized by low-level opportunistic boardings and increased firearm carriage in strategic chokepoints like the Singapore Straits\[cite: 33\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-002): The proliferation of a parallel "Shadow Fleet" (1,300–1,481 aging, unrated tankers) operating outside international regulations enables systemic sanctions evasion, generating billions in state revenues while introducing severe marine pollution and environmental disaster risks\[cite: 33\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-003): Kinetic non-state attacks in the Red Sea and Bab el-Mandeb Strait have forced commercial shipping diversions around the Cape of Good Hope, expanding global ton-mile demand by 6%, driving freight rate volatility, and threatening global supply chain inflation\[cite: 33\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-001): Regional maritime security in Southeast Asian chokepoints (SOMS) is maintained through coordinated law enforcement operations, such as Indonesian Marine Police interventions that dismantled active pirate syndicates in mid-2025\[cite: 33\].

## Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-001): Advanced maritime nations collaborate through international naval deployments, maritime domain awareness (MDA) sharing, and rigorous compliance verification mechanisms to secure vital sea lines of communication\[cite: 33\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-001): Limitations in coastal state maritime domain awareness (MDA), unflagged or fraudulently flagged vessel registrations (Flags of Convenience), and unrated P&I insurance gaps that complicate high-seas enforcement against shadow fleets\[cite: 33\].
- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-002): Absence of mandatory multi-agency verification frameworks for vessel insurance and ownership transparency across primary international trade corridors\[cite: 33\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Establish mandatory insurance verification frameworks for commercial tankers transiting strategic straits, enforce stateless and fraudulent vessel interdiction protocols under UNCLOS, expand multilateral naval escorts and maritime domain awareness in high-risk chokepoints, integrate AI-driven container inspection at major container ports, and establish regional coastal state environmental liability funds\[cite: 33\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-001): Implement mandatory P&I insurance verification for vessels transiting strategic regional chokepoints (e.g., Malacca and Singapore Straits)\[cite: 33\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-002): Deploy AI-driven container inspection and cargo anomaly detection systems across primary domestic and regional container terminals\[cite: 33\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-003): Expand public-private satellite AIS and synthetic aperture radar (SAR) monitoring to detect IUU fishing and dark shadow fleet transfers within national EEZs\[cite: 33\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-018](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-018): 90-day regional maritime security and shadow fleet monitoring pilot across the Straits of Malacca and Singapore (SOMS) approach corridors\[cite: 33\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-018](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-018): 100% verification compliance of transiting commercial tanker insurance credentials; \$\ge 90\\\$ reduction in undetected dark vessel transfers within pilot EEZ sectors; zero uninspected high-risk container imports in pilot ports\[cite: 33\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Promulgate regional and national maritime security directives enhancing cooperative domain awareness, port state controls, and high-seas interdiction protocols\[cite: 33\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Strengthen national maritime laws and port state control regulations to enforce compliance with IMO Assembly Resolution A.1192(33) regarding deceptive shadow fleet operations and unrated marine insurance\[cite: 33\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Global maritime crime incidents rose to 137 in 2025 (up from 116 in 2024), with the Singapore Straits accounting for 80 incidents (58% of global totals)\[cite: 33\].
- The active shadow fleet comprises 1,300 to 1,481 aging vessels, delivering 2.6 million barrels per day of sanctioned crude to China and financing major state defense budgets\[cite: 33\].
