# JPW-REGULATION-002

**Regulations of the Court** is the official internal operational regulation adopted by the judges of the International Criminal Court (ICC) pursuant to Article 52 of the Rome Statute to govern judicial administration procedures, trial proceedings, document page and time limits, defense rights, legal assistance, victim participation and reparations, and detention center management \[\].

## Summary

This document establishes the binding internal framework regulating the functions of the Presidency, the Chambers (Pre-Trial, Trial, and Appeals), the Office of the Prosecutor, and the Registry, ensuring the expeditious and fair conduct of proceedings before the ICC \[\].

## Legal Basis

- [Rome Statute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rome_Statute): Article 52 (Regulations of the Court), Article 9, Article 34, Article 36, Article 38, Article 50, and Article 51 \[\].
- [Rules of Procedure and Evidence](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rules_of_Procedure_and_Evidence) \[\].

## Background

The Regulations of the Court were initially adopted by the Plenary of Judges on 26 May 2004. To adapt to evolving international criminal justice practices, the regulations have undergone periodic amendments, with the latest amendments adopted on 19 September 2024, entering into effect on 21 October 2024 \[\].

## Objectives

- Ensure transparency, legal certainty, and administrative smoothness in judicial proceedings \[\].
- Promote expeditiousness and fairness in trials \[\].
- Standardize document management, deadlines, and translation protocols \[\].
- Regulate counsel qualification requirements and detained persons' rights \[\].

## Key Provisions

The Regulations are structured into 9 main Chapters \[\]:

- \*\*Chapter 1: General Provisions\*\*: Establishment of the Coordination Council (Reg 3) and the Advisory Committee on Legal Texts (Reg 4) \[\].
- \*\*Chapter 2: Composition and Administration\*\*: Seniority order of judges (Reg 10), functions of the Presidency (Reg 11), and designation of Duty Judges (Reg 17) \[\].
- \*\*Chapter 3: Conduct of Proceedings\*\*:
  - \*\*Broadcast Delay (Reg 21)\*\*: Minimum 30-minute delay for audio-video public broadcasts.
  - \*\*Electronic Systems (Reg 26)\*\*: Mandatory electronic management of court records and non-oral evidence.
  - \*\*Page Limits (Reg 37 & 38)\*\*: General document limit of 20 pages; trial briefs and closing briefs capped at 120 pages.
  - \*\*Confirmation of Charges Decision (Reg 53)\*\*: Must be delivered within 60 days of the conclusion of the confirmation hearing.
- \*\*Chapter 4: Counsel and Legal Assistance\*\*: Minimum 10 years of relevant experience required for lead counsel and duty counsel (Reg 67 & 73); establishment of the Office of Public Counsel for the Defence (OPCD) (Reg 77).
- \*\*Chapter 5: Victim Participation and Reparations\*\*: Standardized victim application forms (Reg 86) and reparation requests (Reg 88); establishment of the Office of Public Counsel for Victims (OPCV) (Reg 81).
- \*\*Chapter 6: Detention Matters\*\*: Management of the detention center, independent inspections (Reg 94), and general rights of detainees (Reg 99) \[\].
- \*\*Chapter 7: Co-operation and Enforcement\*\*: Referral of cooperation disputes (Reg 108) and the Presidency's sentence enforcement unit (Reg 113) \[\].
- \*\*Chapter 8: Removal from Office and Disciplinary Measures\*\*: Procedures for handling complaints against judges, the Prosecutor, and the Registrar (Reg 119–125) \[\].
- \*\*Chapter 9: Code of Judicial Ethics\*\*: Adoption of the Code of Judicial Ethics (Reg 126).

## Implementation

Mandatorily applied across all three judicial divisions (Pre-Trial, Trial, and Appeals), the Presidency, the Office of the Prosecutor, and the Registry \[\].

## Policy Issues

- Managing legal consequences of participants' non-compliance with the Regulations or Chamber orders (Reg 29) \[\].
- Handling confidential, *ex parte*, and *under seal* documents under Regulation 23 bis \[\].
- Oversight of independent counsel qualifications and disciplinary enforcement \[\].
