## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 353 K/AG/2005 dated July 7, 2006, establishes fundamental Islamic civil jurisprudence regarding out-of-court inheritance distribution determinations (\*P3HP\*): An out-of-court inheritance distribution deed (P3HP Deed) ex Article 107 of Law No. 7 of 1989 (as amended by Law No. 3 of 2006) must list all legal heirs. If it fails to do so, such a deed can be challenged and declared legally void (\*batal demi hukum\*) on the grounds of manifest error.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiffs (Franciscus \[Party\] / \[Child\], \[Child\] \[Party\] / \[Child\], and \[Child\] Br \[Party\] / \[Child\]) are the children born from the lawful marriage of \[Deceased father\] \[Party\] (SGT \[Party\]) alias \[Party\] Gun Tjun and Angela alias Lie Wan Njong, solemnized on September 26, 1951, in Medan. On June 7, 2003, SGT \[Party\] passed away. When seeking to administer the late father's estate, the Plaintiffs discovered that the Medan Religious Court had issued an Inheritance Distribution Deed Determination No. 11/PPPHP/2003/PA.Mdn. dated June 16, 2003, designating Defendant I (Hj. \[Party C\]), Defendant II (\[Party A\]), and Defendant III (\[Party B\]) as the sole heirs of SGT \[Party\] based on Marriage Certificate Extract No. 115/IV/1987 from the Office of Religious Affairs (\*KUA\*) of Sunggal Sub-District.

Upon investigation at KUA Sunggal (Letter No. K211PW/01/106/2003), it was revealed that Marriage Certificate Extract No. 115/IV/1987 did not belong to SGT \[Party\] and Hj. \[Party C\], but rather to Budy Caroko and Sridarmi. The Plaintiffs subsequently filed a lawsuit seeking the annulment of the P3HP Deed at the Medan Religious Court. The Medan Religious Court (Decision No. 557/Pdt.G/2004/PA.Mdn. dated November 10, 2004) granted the Plaintiffs' lawsuit and declared Determination No. 11/PPPHP/2003/PA.Mdn. to be without legal force. However, the Medan High Religious Court (Decision No. 19/Pdt.G/2005/PTA.Mdn. dated March 7, 2005) annulled the Medan Religious Court judgment and ruled that the Religious Court lacked jurisdiction. The Plaintiffs filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The Medan High Religious Court erred in applying the law, because determinations concerning out-of-court inheritance property distribution fall within the judicial product of the Religious Courts pursuant to Article 107 of Law No. 7 of 1989.
2.  In the event of a manifest error within such a determination, it can be declared devoid of legal force. Therefore, the Medan Religious Court possessed the jurisdiction to examine and adjudicate the a quo case.
3.  Medan Religious Court Determination No. 11/PPPHP/2003/PA.Mdn. dated June 16, 2003, determined that the sole heirs of SGT Agus \[Party\] were the Defendants. However, trial evidence under Exhibits P.5, P.6, and P.7 established that SGT \[Party\] also had children from his marriage with Angela, namely the Plaintiffs.
4.  Based on these facts, Inheritance Distribution Deed No. 11/PPPHP/2003/PA.Mdn. is legally flawed because it failed to include all legal heirs of SGT \[Party\].
5.  Legal Principle: "An out-of-court inheritance distribution deed (P3HP Deed) ex Article 107 of Law No. 7 of 1989 as amended by Law No. 3 of 2006 must include all heirs. If it fails to do so, such a deed can be challenged and declared devoid of legal force on the grounds of manifest error."

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioners for Cassation (1. FRANCISCUS \[Party\] / \[Child\], 2. \[Child\] \[Party\] / \[Child\], 3. \[Child\] Br \[Party\] / \[Child\]); Annulling the Medan High Religious Court decision dated March 7, 2005 AD / 26 Muharram 1426 AH, No. 19/Pdt.G/2005/PTA.Mdn.; and Independently Adjudicating: I. IN EXCEPTION: Rejecting the Defendants' exceptions; II. IN THE PRINCIPAL CASE: (1) Granting the Plaintiffs' lawsuit; (2) Declaring Determination No. 11/PPPHP/2003/PA.Mdn. to be without legal force; (3) Ordering the Respondents for Cassation / Defendants to pay court costs across all levels of jurisdiction, with cassation costs assessed at Rp 500,000 (five hundred thousand rupiahs). \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Wednesday, April 27, 2006, and pronounced in an open session on Friday, July 7, 2006, by H. Andi Syamsu Alam, S.H., M.H. \[Junior Chairman of the Supreme Court / Presiding Judge\], Dr. Rifyal Ka'bah, M.A., and Drs. H. Hamdan, S.H., M.H. \[Associate Justices\]).\*
