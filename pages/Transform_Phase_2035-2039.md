# Transform Phase 2035–2039

The Transform phase embeds intelligent, interoperable and knowledge- driven judicial operations.

## Priorities

- intelligent court;
- knowledge graph;
- data-driven management;
- responsible AI;
- continuous learning;
- institutional innovation.
