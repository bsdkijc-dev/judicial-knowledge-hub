## Description / Deskripsi

This page records the empirical analysis of official judicial statistics and the Problem Inventory List (Daftar Inventaris Masalah / DIM) for business competition objection cases (Komisi Pengawas Persaingan Usaha / KPPU) at the Surabaya Commercial Court at the Surabaya District Court, covering the reporting period from 2021 through 2026. The primary dataset demonstrates an active judicial docket, recording three major objection cases (Case No. 1/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2021/PN Sby, Case No. 1/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2022/PN Sby, and Case No. 1/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2025/PN Sby) with all three resulting in judgments favoring the petitioners in whole or in part (\*dikabulkan / dikabulkan sebagian\*). The accompanying DIM focuses on jurisdictional conflicts between KPPU and public procurement oversight bodies (LKPP, APIP, BPKP, PTUN) regarding specialized high-tech infrastructure procurement, taking the Gili Trawangan seawater desalination project as a central case study. All statistical entries, case records, judicial panel members, and issue inventories have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher **Cecep Mustafa**.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-036](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-036): High Petitioner Success Rate, where in 100% of recorded objection cases (3 out of 3 cases between 2021 and 2026), the Surabaya Commercial Court overturned or modified KPPU's administrative decisions, underscoring intense judicial scrutiny of KPPU's evidentiary findings and market definitions.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-037](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-037): Overlapping Jurisdictional Friction in Specialized Procurement, highlighting a severe regulatory clash between KPPU's anti-monopoly mandates under Article 22 & Article 50 of Law No. 5/1999 and the authority of procurement officials (PPK, PA/KPA) and auditors (APIP, BPKP, LKPP) in high-tech, high-investment infrastructure projects.

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-036](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-036): The Surabaya Commercial Court actively reviews KPPU decisions across diverse economic sectors, maintaining specialized judicial panels composed of senior commercial judges who evaluate public utility concessions under civil procedural rules to determine whether technical specifications constitute legitimate efficiency choices or unlawful competition barriers.

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-036](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-036): In Japan (Tokyo High Court / JFTC), public utility and high-tech infrastructure concessions subject to JFTC review are evaluated under formal inter-agency consultation guidelines between JFTC and government procurement agencies, utilizing a broad "rule of reason" test that explicitly factors in technical necessity and capital investment risks.

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-036](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-036): The primary gap lies in the absence of a legal framework for KPPU to coordinate with LKPP/APIP before declaring a specialized direct-appointment procurement illegal, alongside overly narrow geographic market definitions (e.g., restricting the market definition to Gili Trawangan island rather than national specialized desalination capability).

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Establish a formal administrative gateway requiring KPPU to consult APIP/LKPP technical audits before issuing findings of bid-rigging on specialized direct-appointment procurements, and draft clear Supreme Court guidelines defining statutory exemption standards under Article 50 of Law No. 5/1999.

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-036](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-036): Issue a Joint Decree/Circular between Mahkamah Agung, KPPU, and LKPP regulating evidentiary standards for technical procurement objections.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-037](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-037): Incorporate specialized market definition standards (national vs. localized geographic markets) into the upcoming PERMA Amendment.

## Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-036](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-036): Surabaya Commercial Court (East Java & NTB Jurisdiction) pilot integration for specialized infrastructure procurement review.

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-036](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-036): 100% alignment between judicial market definition assessments and economic efficiency standards in infrastructure cases; resolution of objection cases within \$\le 60\$ working days per statutory standard.

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Amending Law No. 5/1999 to provide explicit remedies for upstream procurement adjustments and clarify KPPU's jurisdiction relative to PTUN and APIP, alongside enhancing judicial training for Surabaya Commercial Court judges on complex public utility concessions and econometrics.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgating inter-agency coordination rules between competition authorities and procurement oversight bodies to resolve administrative and judicial conflicts.

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Official court record and Problem Inventory List (DIM) document from Surabaya Commercial Court (\*DIM KPPU PN SURABAYA.pdf\*) covering cases No. 1/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2021/PN Sby, No. 1/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2022/PN Sby, and No. 1/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2025/PN Sby.
