## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 96 K/MIL/2006 dated May 31, 2007, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal rules regarding the application of excusing conditions (\*alasan pemaaf\*) in Military Criminal Law. The Supreme Court affirmed that even though the Defendant (a subordinate soldier) was proven to have committed an act violating Article 105 paragraph (1) of the KUHPM by uttering inappropriate threats against his superior, such an act is unpunishable because it arose from an uncontrollable emotional outburst triggered by a heinous act—the rape of the Defendant's wife committed by that same superior. This outburst constitutes a legal excusing condition, requiring the Defendant to be released from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*).

## Case Background

The Defendant (\[Defendant\], Serda Nrp. 634800, Personnel Administration Clerk at Ajen Rem 131/Stg) was brought before the Manado Military Court III-17 under the charge of violating Article 105 paragraph (1) of the KUHPM ("A military personnel who intentionally and with overt acts threatens a superior with violence"). On March 16, 2004, in front of the Sandi Room at Ma Korem 131/Stg, the Defendant approached the victim witness, First Lieutenant (\*Lettu Inf\*) \[Witness\], while pointing fingers and threatening: "You are the one who tied my wife's hands together with Sergeant Rustam; I will kill you, I have many family members."

The threats were triggered by the reality that the victim witness, Lettu Inf \[Witness\], had raped the Defendant's wife (\[Victim\] / Merry Ulaan) under firearm threat while binding her hands and covering her eyes and mouth with black duct tape. The victim witness Lettu Inf \[Witness\] was himself sentenced to 8 months of imprisonment and dishonorably discharged from the Indonesian Army (TNI AD) pursuant to a final and binding military court judgment.

The Manado Military Court III-17 (Decision No. PUT/35-K/PM.III-17/AD/V/2005 dated May 25, 2005) sentenced the Defendant to 7 (seven) months of imprisonment. The Surabaya Military High Court III (Decision No. PUT/52-K/PMT.III/BDG/AD/VI/2006 dated June 1, 2006) upheld the conviction. The Defendant subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  Although the Defendant is proven to have committed the acts described in the Military Prosecutor's indictment (uttering threats against a superior), the underlying causes of such acts must be taken into account.
2.  The Defendant's actions were caused by an uncontrollable emotional outburst resulting from the rape committed by his superior (Lettu Inf \[Witness\]) against the Defendant's wife. A similar reaction could happen to anyone experiencing the same traumatic event, impairing their ability to maintain self-control in accordance with their emotional surge.
3.  The emotional outburst resulting from the superior's heinous act constitutes an excusing condition (\*alasan pemaaf\*) under the law. Therefore, the \*Judex Facti\* erred in its considerations, and it is legally justified for the Supreme Court to annul the \*Judex Facti\* rulings and release the Defendant from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*).

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation / Defendant \[Defendant\], Serda Nrp. 634800; Annulling the Surabaya Military High Court III decision dated June 1, 2006, No. PUT/52-K/PMT.III/BDG/AD/VI/2006, which upheld the Manado Military Court III-17 decision dated May 25, 2005, No. PUT/35-K/PM.III-17/AD/V/2005; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring that Defendant \[Defendant\], Serda Nrp. 634800 is proven to have committed the acts formulated in the Military Prosecutor's indictment, but that such acts are unpunishable; (2) Releasing the Defendant accordingly from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*); (3) Restoring the Defendant's rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity; (4) Charging procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State. \*(Decided in the Supreme Court deliberation meeting on May 31, 2007, by the Cassation Panel of Justices: German Hoediarto, S.H. \[Presiding\], M. Imron Anwari, S.H., SpN., M.H., and Timur P. Manurung, S.H.)\*.
