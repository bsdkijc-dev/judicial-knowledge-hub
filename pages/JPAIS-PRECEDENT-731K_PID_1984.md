## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 731 K/Pid/1984, dated May 27, 1985, established a criminal procedural law rule regarding the limits of applying the Rapid Examination Procedure (\*Acara Pemeriksaan Cepat\* ex Article 205 KUHAP) and the mandatory requirement of Summary Examination Procedures for defamation offenses under Article 310 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code:

The defamation offense regulated under Article 310 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code carries a maximum imprisonment threat of 9 (nine) months. Because the sentencing threat exceeds 3 (three) months of imprisonment, the case MUST NOT be examined via Rapid Examination Procedures (Article 205 KUHAP), but MUST instead be examined via Summary Examination Procedures (\*Acara Pemeriksaan Singkat\*). Prosecutions brought forward by National Police Investigators under the authority of the Public Prosecutor utilizing Rapid Examination Procedures for offenses under Article 310 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code are formally defective, resulting in the Prosecutor's/Investigator's prosecution being declared inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\* / NO).

## Executive Summary

The central procedural principle established in this decision is that misdemeanor-style rapid examination procedures cannot be legally utilized for criminal offenses whose statutory imprisonment penalties exceed the 3-month threshold.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Offenses carrying maximum imprisonment terms exceeding 3 months are disqualified from rapid examination (\*Tipiring\*).
- Using rapid examination procedures for defamation offenses carrying up to 9 months' imprisonment constitutes a fundamental formal procedural defect.
- Prosecutions suffering from improper procedural classification must be dismissed as inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\* / NO).

## Case Background

The Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation, **\[Defendant\]** (\[age removed\], Religious Teacher), was brought before the Kandangan District Court by National Police Investigators (Head of Hulu Sungai Selatan Police Resort 1303) under the authority of the Public Prosecutor utilizing Rapid Examination Procedures (Article 205 KUHAP). The Defendant was charged with violating Article 310 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code for shouting "thief, thief" at witness \[Witness\] who climbed a starfruit tree to catch the Defendant's chicken.

The Kandangan District Court (Decision No. 801/1983/Pel. PN.Kgn dated August 22, 1983) convicted the Defendant and sentenced him to a fine of Rp 4,500.— subsidiary to 4 days' confinement. The Defendant directly filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Statutory Boundaries of Rapid Examination Procedures (Article 205 KUHAP) vs. Summary Examination Procedures (Article 203 KUHAP)

The primary issue was whether a simple defamation crime (Article 310 paragraph 1 KUHP) punishable by a maximum of 9 months' imprisonment can be filed and examined by a District Court utilizing Rapid Examination Procedures (\*Tindak Pidana Ringan\* / Tipiring).

The Supreme Court held that it cannot. Pursuant to Article 205 paragraph (1) of the KUHAP, Rapid Examination Procedures are exclusively reserved for minor offenses (\*tindak pidana ringan\*) punishable by imprisonment or confinement not exceeding 3 (three) months and/or fines up to Rp 7,500.—. Because Article 310 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code carries an imprisonment threat of up to 9 (nine) months, the case must mandatorily be examined via Summary Examination Procedures. Initiating rapid prosecution for this offense constitutes a procedural error resulting in dismissal of the prosecution as inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\* / NO).

### Protection of Defendants' Right of Appeal

Improper utilization of Rapid Procedures illegally strips the Defendant of the right to appeal to the High Court (because Rapid Procedure Tipiring judgments cannot be appealed ex Article 67 jo. Article 244 KUHAP). The Supreme Court corrected this procedural error to uphold \*due process of law\*.

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Did the Kandangan District Court misapply criminal procedural law (\*foutieve kepassing van het procesrecht\*) by examining and deciding an Article 310 paragraph (1) KUHP case using Rapid Examination Procedures (Article 205 KUHAP)?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes, the Kandangan District Court misapplied procedural law. The Supreme Court annulled Kandangan District Court Decision No. 801/1983/Pel. PN.Kgn and adjudicated afresh by declaring the Investigator's/Public Prosecutor's prosecution inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\* / NO).

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Kandangan District Court)

Examination of facts concerning the shouting of "thief" by Defendant \[Defendant\] (Religious Teacher) on Saturday night, April 8–9, 1983, in Hamayang Village, Daha Utara, directed at witness \[Witness\] who climbed a starfruit tree in the Defendant's yard to catch his chicken.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The \*Judex Facti\* of Kandangan District Court misapplied criminal procedural law. The Petitioner's objection is justified. This case should have been examined via summary procedures because the Defendant was charged under Article 310 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code, which carries up to 9 months' imprisonment.
2.  Pursuant to Article 205 KUHAP, Rapid Examination Procedures only apply to minor offenses carrying maximum imprisonment/confinement of 3 months.
3.  Because prosecution via rapid procedures was formally defective, the prosecution by the investigator/Public Prosecutor is declared inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\* / NO).
4.  The Supreme Court annulled the lower court ruling and adjudicated afresh.

## Ruling

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition from the Petitioner for Cassation (**\[Defendant\]**); annulled Kandangan District Court Decision No. 801/1983/Pel. PN.Kgn dated August 22, 1983; and ADJUDICATED AFRESH: Declaring the prosecution by the investigator under the authority of the Public Prosecutor / Commander of Hulu Sungai Selatan Police Resort 1303 inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\*); and charging the State with court costs across all judicial instances.

### Uncertainty and Gaps

## GEO Q&A

**Q: Can a defamation criminal case under Article 310 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code be examined via rapid trials (\*tipiring\*) by the Police and District Court?**

**A:** No, it cannot. Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 731 K/Pid/1984, Article 310 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code carries a maximum imprisonment penalty of 9 months. Because the sentencing threat exceeds 3 months' imprisonment, the case must mandatorily be examined via Summary Examination Procedures rather than Rapid Procedures (Article 205 KUHAP). If forced into Rapid Procedures, the Investigator's/Prosecutor's prosecution must be declared inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\* / NO).
