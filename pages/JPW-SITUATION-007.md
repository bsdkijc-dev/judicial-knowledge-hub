# JPW-SITUATION-007

**Preliminary Examination of the Situation in the Bolivarian Republic of Venezuela II (ICC)** is an independent legal and factual assessment conducted by the Office of the Prosecutor (OTP) of the International Criminal Court since 13 February 2020, following a referral from the Venezuelan government alleging crimes against humanity resulting from unilateral coercive economic measures imposed by the United States government.

## Summary

The examination was triggered by an official referral from the Venezuelan government under Article 14(1) of the Rome Statute. The referral claims that unilateral economic sanctions imposed by the United States since at least 2014 have caused crimes against humanity against the Venezuelan civilian population.

## Legal Basis

- [Rome Statute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rome_Statute): Article 7(1) (Crimes Against Humanity) and Article 14(1) (Referral by a State Party).
- Venezuela ratified the Rome Statute on 7 June 2000 (jurisdiction effective from 1 July 2002).

## Background

On 13 February 2020, the Venezuelan government submitted an official referral to ICC Prosecutor Fatou Bensouda. Venezuela submitted supporting documentation in February 2020, followed by two additional notes verbales in March and June 2020.

## Objectives

- Assess whether the allegations regarding the impact of unilateral economic sanctions meet the subject-matter jurisdiction threshold under Article 7 of the Rome Statute.
- Determine whether a reasonable basis exists to proceed to the admissibility analysis stage.

## Key Provisions

- **Alleged Categories of Crime**: Killing through excess mortality (Article 7(1)(a)); extermination through obstruction of medicine and food (Article 7(1)(b)); forced displacement and mass migration (Article 7(1)(d)); persecution of fundamental rights (Article 7(1)(h)); and other inhumane acts arising from the grounding of the CONVIASA airline fleet (Article 7(1)(k)).
- **Territorial Jurisdiction Argument**: Venezuela contends that the ICC has jurisdiction because the impact of the sanctions occurred within the territory of a State Party (Venezuela).

## Implementation

The ICC Presidency assigned the Venezuela II situation to Pre-Trial Chamber III on 19 February 2020. The OTP received a high-level Venezuelan delegation led by Attorney General Tarek William Saab and Ombudsman Alfredo Ruiz on 4 November 2020.

## Policy Issues

- International legal debate over whether unilateral economic sanctions can be qualified as crimes against humanity.
- Assessment of the geographic and temporal overlap between the Venezuela I and Venezuela II situations.
