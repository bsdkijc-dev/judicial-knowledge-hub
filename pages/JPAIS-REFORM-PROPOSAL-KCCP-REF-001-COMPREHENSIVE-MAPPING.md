# JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:KCCP-REF-001-COMPREHENSIVE-MAPPING

## Core Reform Summary: KCCP-REF-001 / Ringkasan Reformasi Inti: KCCP-REF-001

- Structural Problem (KCCP-PROB-001): Mandatory court-connected mediation under PERMA No. 1 of 2016 relies on a solo mediator model, resulting in stagnant settlement rates (~4.0%) in general civil courts (Pengadilan Negeri).
- Japanese Benchmark (KCCP-PRACT-JP-001 & 003): Under Articles 5, 6, and 17 of Japan's Civil Conciliation Act, 3-member committees (Chōtei Iinkai) issue evaluative Orders in Lieu of Conciliation (Chōtei ni kawaru kettei), achieving \>50% settlement in civil disputes and 71.8% in inheritance divisions.
- Proposed Adaptation (KCCP-REF-001):

` * Tripartite Panels (Majelis Konsiliasi Tripartit): Pairs 1 Certified Judge Mediator with 2 Accredited Non-Judge Expert Commissioners for commercial, property, and inheritance disputes exceeding Rp 500 million.`  
` * Orders in Lieu of Settlement (Penetapan Pengganti Perdamaian): Empowers panels to issue a provisional evaluative order upon impasse, converting into an enforceable Settlement Deed (Akta Perdamaian) if no party objects within 14 calendar days.`

- Pilot & KPI Targets (KCCP-PILOT-001 / KPI-001): A 90-day pilot across 6 target courts (PN Jakarta Pusat, PN Surabaya, PN Medan, PA Jakarta Selatan, PA Surabaya, PA Malang) targeting $\ge 25\%$ District Court settlement success and duration $\le 21$ calendar days.

## Integrated Operational Modules / Modul Operasional Terintegrasi

1\. Regulatory Draft: Supreme Court Leadership Decree (SK KMA) authorizing the 90-day pilot sandbox. 2. Technical Workflow: SIPP auto-triaging, expert matching, and 14-day objection timer automation. 3. Training Architecture: BSDK Kumdil 20-hour clinical co-mediation and Yōken-Jijitsu claim deconstruction curriculum for 120 expert commissioners. 4. Legislative Policy: Statutory draft articles submitted to Commission III DPR RI for the Draft Civil Procedure Code (RUU Hukum Acara Perdata).
