## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 592 K/Pid/1984, dated March 30, 1985, established a criminal law rule concerning the absence of the element "with intent to unlawfully possess" (\*wederrechtelijkheid\*) in the offense of theft (Article 362 of the Criminal Code / KUHP) and unpleasant acts (Article 335 of the Criminal Code / KUHP).

The Supreme Court held that a person who taps and collects rubber latex from trees they genuinely believe to belong to themselves (because they planted them on family land and the auction boundaries of the reporting party remain unclear/unmeasured) is not proven to meet the element of "with intent to unlawfully possess". Because the element of \*wederrechtelijkheid\* was unproven, the Defendant was acquitted of all charges (\*vrijspraak\*) and his rights, standing, and dignity were restored.

## Executive Summary

The central criminal law principle established in this decision is that harvesting produce from land reasonably believed to be one's own due to administrative boundary ambiguity negates the subjective element of unlawful intent (\*mens rea\* / \*wederrechtelijkheid\*).

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Tapping rubber trees under a good-faith belief of ownership rooted in unclear auction boundaries eliminates the intent to unlawfully possess.
- Lower court judgments failing to meticulously examine the element of unlawfulness in property dispute prosecutions are subject to reversal (\*onvoldoende gemotiveerd\*).
- Defendants lacking subjective culpability in property boundary disputes must be acquitted (\*vrijspraak\*).

## Case Background

The Original Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation, **\[Defendant\]** (\[age removed\], farmer), was charged with rubber theft (Article 362 jo. Articles 64, 65 KUHP / Charge I) and unpleasant acts (Article 335 paragraph 1 KUHP / Charge II) in Kampung Simpang Maju Tanjung Siram, Labuhan Batu. The reporting witness (\[Witness\]) claimed to have purchased a 7-hectare rubber plantation belonging to the Defendant's parents through a public auction held by the Rantau Prapat District Court on January 10, 1981, whereas the Defendant continued to tap rubber and forbid the witness's workers.

The Rantau Prapat District Court (Decision No. 200/KTS/1981/PN-Rap. dated June 14, 1982) convicted the Defendant and sentenced him to 10 months' imprisonment. The Medan High Court (Decision No. 1/Pid./1984/PT.Mdn. dated January 28, 1984) modified the sentence to 6 months' probation for 1 year and restitution of Rp 50,000.—. The Petitioner for Cassation subsequently filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### The Element of "With Intent to Unlawfully Possess" (Wederrechtelijke Toe-eigening) in Article 362 KUHP

The primary issue was whether an individual who taps and collects rubber latex from land they genuinely believe to be their family's property (having planted it themselves, while the auction boundaries of a court auction purchaser remain unmeasured and ambiguous) can be qualified as committing theft.

The Supreme Court held that they cannot be penalized. The subjective element of "intent to unlawfully possess" requires awareness (\*mens rea\*) that the property taken belongs to another without right. When a Defendant acts upon a reasonable assumption (claim of right / \*sangkaan hak\* / good faith) that the rubber trees are situated within their own enclosure due to administrative boundary ambiguity, the element of \*wederrechtelijkheid\* is extinguished by operation of law.

### Defect in Legal Reasoning (Onvoldoende Gemotiveerd) under Article 335 Paragraph (1) KUHP

The \*Judex Facti\* misapplied the law by imposing criminal liability for unpleasant acts without thoroughly examining and weighing whether the Defendant's warnings or prohibitions directed at external workers were unlawful, given that the Defendant acted to protect physical possession over land with unsettled auction boundaries.

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Did the \*Judex Facti\* (Medan High Court & Rantau Prapat District Court) misapply the law (\*foutieve kepassing van het recht\*) by ignoring the absence of unlawful intent (\*wederrechtelijkheid\*) and failing to carefully weigh (\*onvoldoende gemotiveerd\*) the Defendant's good-faith belief regarding his land boundaries?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes, the \*Judex Facti\* misapplied the law. The Supreme Court annulled the Medan High Court and Rantau Prapat District Court decisions and adjudicated afresh, acquitting the Defendant of all charges (\*vrijspraak\*) and restoring his rights and dignity.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Rantau Prapat District Court & Medan High Court)

Examination of the facts surrounding rubber-tapping activities in Kampung Simpang Maju Tanjung Siram in January 1981, Court Auction Minutes No. 10 dated January 10, 1981, concerning the 7-hectare rubber plantation belonging to Iskandar Marpaung (the Defendant's father) purchased by witness \[Witness\], the absence of precise boundary measurements by the Agrarian Office/Bailiff during the auction, and the Defendant's warnings issued to workers Subandi and Bani.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The appellate and district \*Judex Facti\* misapplied the law. Regarding Charge I (Article 362 KUHP), at the time the Defendant tapped the rubber, he assumed the trees were within his own garden (planted by himself, with boundary markers of witness \[Witness\] never precisely measured and remaining ambiguous). Consequently, the element of "with intent to unlawfully possess" was unproven.
2.  Regarding Charge II (Article 335 KUHP), the \*Judex Facti\* failed to meticulously weigh (\*onvoldoende gemotiveerd\*) the element of unlawfulness concerning the Defendant's prohibitions on land believed to be his own.
3.  The Supreme Court annulled the lower court rulings and adjudicated afresh, acquitting the Defendant of all charges (\*vrijspraak\*).

## Ruling

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition from the Petitioner for Cassation (**\[Defendant\]**); annulled Medan High Court Decision No. 1/Pid./1984/PT.Mdn. dated January 28, 1984, in conjunction with Rantau Prapat District Court Decision No. 200/KTS/1981/PN-Rap. dated June 14, 1982; and adjudicated afresh: Declaring that Defendant \[Defendant\] is unproven legally and convincingly to have committed the acts charged under Charge I and Charge II; Acquitting him therefore from the charges (\*vrijspraak\*); Restoring the Defendant's rights regarding capacity, standing, and dignity; Ordering the return of evidence; and charging the state with court costs across all judicial instances.

## Evidence Status and Gaps

### Established by Evidence

- The Defendant (\[Defendant\]) tapped rubber trees and collected latex on a 9-hectare plot in Simpang Maju Tanjung Siram planted by himself since 1971.
- A public auction took place over a 7-hectare rubber plantation belonging to Iskandar Marpaung (the Defendant's father) based on Auction Minutes No. 10 dated January 10, 1981, won by witness \[Witness\].
- The boundaries of the seized and auctioned rubber plantation land were neither precisely known nor mapped in detail at the time of the auction.

### Not Established / Pure Acquittal

- It was unproven that the Defendant possessed criminal intent (\*mens rea\*) to unlawfully appropriate another's property, as the Defendant tapped with the belief that the trees belonged to him.
- The element of unlawfulness under Article 335 paragraph (1) KUHP was unproven.

### Uncertainty and Gaps

- Importance of Land Execution Boundary Certainty: This decision highlights the dangerous ramifications of administrative land uncertainties (the absence of boundary stakes/precise measurements in judicial auction minutes) which risk triggering the criminalization of original owners or their families occupying land in good faith.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: Can a person who harvests produce on land believed to be their own (because another buyer's auction boundaries are unclear/ambiguous) be prosecuted for theft?**

**A:** They cannot be penalized and must be fully acquitted (\*Vrijspraak\*). Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 592 K/Pid/1984, if an individual harvests crops with the reasonable belief that such crops are situated on their own land—owing to the unmeasured and ambiguous boundaries of another purchaser's auction—the element of "with intent to unlawfully possess" (\*wederrechtelijkheid\*) is unproven. The Defendant must be acquitted of all charges of theft and unpleasant acts.
