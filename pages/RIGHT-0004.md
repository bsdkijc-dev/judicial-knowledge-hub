# RIGHT-0004

# Human Autonomy

**Human autonomy** concerns the capacity of individuals to make meaningful decisions concerning matters affecting their lives.

The precise legal status and scope of autonomy vary among legal and philosophical frameworks.

**\[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## AI Context

AI systems may influence autonomy through:

- Recommendation
- Profiling
- Prediction
- Persuasion
- Automated decision-making
- Personalized information
- Behavioral optimization

## Automation Bias

People may give excessive weight to automated recommendations.

This may create risks where users accept AI outputs without sufficient independent evaluation.

## Judicial Context

Judicial AI should be designed so that appropriate human decision-makers retain meaningful responsibility for consequential decisions.

## Cognitive Dimensions

Emerging debates may concern:

- Cognitive liberty
- Mental privacy
- Neurotechnology
- AI-mediated persuasion
- Human agency

These concepts require separate legal and scholarly analysis.
