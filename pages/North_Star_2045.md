# North Star 2045

# North Star 2045

By 2045, the Indonesian judiciary should be recognised not simply as a digital judiciary, but as a **trusted intelligent judiciary**.

## Desired Characteristics

The judiciary should be:

- independent;
- trusted;
- human-centred;
- knowledge-driven;
- technologically advanced;
- accessible;
- timely;
- consistent;
- high-quality.

## Core Proposition

**Digital Judiciary → Intelligent Judiciary → Trusted Judiciary**
