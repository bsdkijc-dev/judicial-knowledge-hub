# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:2981K PDT 1984

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 2981 K/Pdt/1984** (dated July 22, 1985 / deliberation on June 5, 1985) is a fundamental trademark law and civil procedure jurisprudence precedent establishing vital rules regarding the formal ripeness of trademark registration cancellation lawsuits pursuant to Law No. 21 of 1961 on Trademarks.

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established a primary legal principle:

- **Unpublished Trademarks in the State Gazette Supplement Do Not Constitute Trademark Actions:** A lawsuit seeking the cancellation of a trademark registration filed before a District Court regarding a trademark registered with the Directorate of Patents and Copyrights, but not yet published in the Supplement to the Official Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia (*Tambahan Berita Negara Republik Indonesia* / TBN RI), does **not constitute a trademark action** pursuant to Article 10 paragraph (1) of Law No. 21 of 1961.

Because the trademark "Ratu Ayu" had been registered under No. 167258 but lacked official publication in the State Gazette Supplement, the lawsuit was premature and formally defective, mandating dismissal as **Inadmissible** (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioner for Cassation (Ny. Dra. Martha Tilaar), annulled Central Jakarta District Court Decision No. 048/1984 G. dated July 25, 1984, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by declaring the Plaintiff's (PT Rama Pharmaceutical Industry) lawsuit **Inadmissible** (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Formal Ripeness Requirements for Trademark Cancellation Lawsuits (*Ripeness of Action* *ex* Article 10 paragraph 1 of Law No. 21 of 1961)

*Does a district court possess jurisdiction to receive and adjudicate third-party trademark registration cancellation lawsuits concerning marks registered at the Patent Directorate but not yet published in the Supplement to the Official Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia (TBN RI)?*

**Supreme Court Rule: No Jurisdiction / Premature Lawsuit.**

Pursuant to consistent Supreme Court jurisprudence concerning Article 10 paragraph (1) of Law No. 21 of 1961, official publication in the Supplement to the Official Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia (TBN RI) constitutes a mandatory formal prerequisite (*conditio sine qua non*) for a trademark registration to be validly classified as a "trademark action" subject to judicial cancellation. Prior to publication in the State Gazette Supplement, registration remains publicly unperfected, rendering cancellation lawsuits premature and mandating dismissal as **Inadmissible** (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).

### 2. Scope of Cassation Admissibility Review *Ex Officio*

The Supreme Court possesses authority *ex officio* to annul district court decisions based on independent legal grounds (regardless of formal cassation objections raised by the Petitioner) upon discovering that the district court entertained a legally defective or premature lawsuit.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (*Judex Juris* / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* Central Jakarta District Court (Decision No. 048/1984 G.) err in applying procedural and trademark law (*error in iudicando*) by accepting, examining the merits of, and granting the cancellation lawsuit against trademark "Ratu Ayu" No. 167258, whereas said trademark registration had not been published in the State Gazette Supplement?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. Central Jakarta District Court misapplied the law. Trademark cancellation lawsuits concerning marks unpublished in the State Gazette Supplement are immature (premature) and do not constitute trademark actions *ex* Article 10(1) of Law No. 21 of 1961. The Supreme Court granted Dra. Martha Tilaar's cassation petition, annulled the District Court decision, and independently adjudicated by declaring the Plaintiff's (PT Rama Pharmaceutical Industry) lawsuit Inadmissible (NO).

### Question of Fact (*Judex Facti* / Central Jakarta District Court)

Examination of facts regarding registration of trademark "Ratu Ayu" No. 167258 on October 13, 1982, in the name of Ny. Dra. Martha Tilaar; cosmetics production permits from the Minister of Health No. 220/Menkes/Per/IX/76 dated June 5, 1981, and Directorate General of POM registration in 1982 in the name of PT Rama Pharmaceutical Industry; examination of trademark label evidence ("Ratu Ayu" *vs.* "Ratu Ayu Solo"); and verification of the non-issuance of publication for registration No. 167258 in the State Gazette Supplement.

## Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

### Statutory Regulations

- **Law No. 21 of 1961 on Trademarks:**
  - Article 2 paragraph (2) (Declaratory System).
  - Article 10 paragraph (1) (Registered Trademark Cancellation Lawsuits).
  - Article 18 paragraph (1) letter b (Extinction of Trademark Registration Force).
- **Law No. 14 of 1970 *jo.* Law No. 13 of 1965 *jo.* Law No. 1 of 1950 on the Supreme Court:** Cassation and Independent Adjudication Authority.

### Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- Doctrine of Trademark Ripeness (*Ripeness Doctrine in Trademark Law*): Trademark registrations become binding and litigable for cancellation before civil courts only when all administrative registration stages—including public publication in the Official Gazette / State Gazette Supplement—have been executed by the trademark registration authority.
- Established Jurisprudence on State Gazette Supplement Requirements: Supreme Court Decision No. 2981 K/Pdt/1984 serves as a landmark precedent under the regime of Law No. 21/1961 establishing that State Gazette Supplement publication is mandatory for trademark cancellation actions.

## What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

### Established by Evidence

- Trademark Registration No. 167258 ("Ratu Ayu") in the name of Ny. Dra. Martha Tilaar **HAS NOT BEEN PUBLISHED** in the Supplement to the Official Gazette of the Republic of Indonesia (TBN RI).
- The trademark registration cancellation lawsuit filed by the Plaintiff is **PREMATURE AND FORMALLY DEFECTIVE** *ex* Article 10 paragraph (1) of Law No. 21 of 1961.
- Central Jakarta District Court Decision No. 048/1984 G. dated July 25, 1984, is **NULL AND VOID**.
- The Plaintiff's (PT Rama Pharmaceutical Industry) lawsuit is declared **INADMISSIBLE** (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).

### Dismissed / Unconsidered Arguments

- The substantive question of who qualifies as the first user (*first user*) entitled to the "Ratu Ayu" trademark and whether confusing similarity existed **WAS NOT CONSIDERED BY THE SUPREME COURT**, as the lawsuit lapsed upon formal admissibility testing.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Importance of Rigorous Admissibility Checks Prior to Substantive IP Examination:** This landmark ruling emphasizes the necessity of judicial discipline in testing formal lawsuit conditions (*admissibility check*). In intellectual property disputes, judges must not prematurely rush into investigating substantive unfair competition or confusing similarity before verifying that the disputed object has satisfied all statutory publication formalities.

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Can a registered trademark cancellation lawsuit be entertained by a court if the mark has not been published in the State Gazette Supplement (TBN RI)?  
**Inadmissible (NO).** Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence Reg. No. 2981 K/Pdt/1984:

- Trademarks registered at the Directorate of Patents and Copyrights that remain unpublished in the State Gazette Supplement (TBN RI) do not qualify as "trademark actions" pursuant to Article 10 paragraph (1) of Law No. 21 of 1961.
- Trademark cancellation lawsuits filed prior to official State Gazette Supplement publication are categorized as premature and formally defective.
- The Supreme Court annulled the Central Jakarta District Court decision and independently adjudicated by declaring the Plaintiff's lawsuit Inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).
