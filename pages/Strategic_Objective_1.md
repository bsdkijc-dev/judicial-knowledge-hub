# Strategic Objective 1

# Strategic Objective 1

**To realize an independent judiciary in the provision of legal** services and enforcement of law that is certain, equal, and just.

## Measurement

The objective is measured through the:

**Judicial Service Effectiveness Index**

The index incorporates the indicators associated with Strategic Targets 1 and 2.

## Strategic Dimensions

- independence;
- certainty;
- equality;
- justice;
- service effectiveness;
- accessibility;
- responsiveness.
