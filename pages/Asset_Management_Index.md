# Asset Management Index

# Asset Management Index

Indicator 3.4 measures the quality of management of state-owned assets (BMN), based on the relevant Ministry of Finance framework.

## Parameters

1.  Accountable and productive BMN management
2.  Compliance with applicable laws and regulations
3.  Effective supervision and control
4.  Reliable BMN administration

## Targets

| 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| 3.75 | 3.76 | 3.77 | 3.78 | 3.80 |
