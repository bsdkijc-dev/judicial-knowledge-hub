# Strategic Objective 2

# Strategic Objective 2

**To realize a transparent, accountable, modern, and professional** judiciary.

## Strategic Dimensions

- transparency;
- accountability;
- modernization;
- professionalism;
- public trust;
- digital transformation.

## Measurement

The objective is measured through the:

**Judicial Governance and Management Effectiveness Index**

The index incorporates the indicators under Strategic Target 3.
