## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 55 K/TUN/1992 dated November 25, 1993, establishes an important legal principle regarding the basis for assessing the validity of State Administrative Decrees and the authority of Administrative Officials in issuing Building Permits (\*Izin Mendirikan Bangunan / IMB\*):

- A building that was originally erected without a Building Permit (\*IMB\*), notwithstanding subsequent sales of the land and structure to a third party who subsequently applies for an \*IMB\* over that structure, remains an unlawful structure in its origin.
- The action of the Mayor in refusing the Plaintiff's application for an \*IMB\* does not constitute an arbitrary or unlawful act. The granting or refusal of an \*IMB\* falls entirely within the authority of the State Administrative Official, and no arbitrariness or abuse of authority for extraneous purposes (\*detournement de pouvoir\*) occurred in this matter.

## Case Background

The Respondent for Cassation / Original Plaintiff (\[Plaintiff\]) purchased a 335 m² plot of land at Lorong Kuningan Lama No. 187/B/2, 17 Ilir Subdistrict, Palembang, from Koesmanto Wirahadikusuma pursuant to 5 Sale and Purchase Deeds (subsequently split into Ownership Land Certificates No. 1684 through 1688 / 17 Ilir). The Plaintiff subsequently filed an \*IMB\* application on October 14, 1991 (Agenda No. 352/0510/1991) to continue construction on an existing 5-unit shop structure located on the premises. The application was refused by the Petitioner for Cassation / Original Defendant (Mayor of the Level II Municipality of Palembang) via Letter No. 640/0030/44/II dated December 9, 1991, on the grounds that an unpermitted building stood upon the land and that Supreme Court Decision No. 966/K/Pdt/1985 remained unexecuted.

The Plaintiff challenged the refusal decree at the Palembang State Administrative Court (\*PTUN\*). The Palembang \*PTUN\* (Decision No. 01/PTUN/G/PLG/1992 dated April 25, 1992) granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in its entirety and annulled the \*IMB\* refusal letter. The Medan High State Administrative Court (\*PTTUN\*) (Decision No. 26/BDG.G.PL/PT.TUN/1992 dated August 18, 1992) upheld the Palembang \*PTUN\* ruling. Dissatisfied with these decisions, the Mayor of Palembang filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  A structure originally erected without a Building Permit (\*IMB\*), even if the land and structure are subsequently sold and transferred to a third party who applies for an \*IMB\*, remains an unpermitted and rule-violating structure.
2.  The Mayor's action in refusing the Plaintiff's application to issue an \*IMB\* does not constitute arbitrary or unlawful conduct.
3.  The issuance or refusal of an \*IMB\* is entirely within the discretion and authority of the State Administrative Official, and the refusal in this case was sufficiently reasoned; the Supreme Court finds no arbitrariness or misuse of authority for improper purposes (\*detournement de pouvoir\*).
4.  The fact that the Plaintiff purchased the land following a final and binding civil judgment altered the status of land ownership, but this remains distinct from the Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation's authority regarding an \*IMB\* for a structure that was unlawful from its inception.

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation MAYOR OF LEVEL II MUNICIPALITY OF PALEMBANG; Annulling the Medan High State Administrative Court decision dated August 18, 1992, No. 26/BDG/PLG/PT.TUN/1992 jo. Palembang State Administrative Court decision dated April 25, 1992, No. 01/PTUN/G/PLG/1992; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Rejecting the Plaintiff's lawsuit; (2) Ordering Respondent for Cassation (\[Plaintiff\]) to pay all court costs across all levels of jurisdiction (assessed at Rp 50,000 at the cassation level).
