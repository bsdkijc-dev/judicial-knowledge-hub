# Benchbook: Standard Timelines

# Chapter 2: Standard Timeline Regulations

*Procedural Management Criteria*

## Case-Tier Classification

Cases are routed into specific management tracks based on complexity:

- **Tier 1 (Fast-Track):** Straightforward contractual or tort disputes. Concludes within 6 months.
- **Tier 2 (Standard):** Multi-party or intermediate commercial cases. Concludes within 12 months.
- **Tier 3 (Complex):** Class actions, antitrust, or severe intellectual property disputes. Managed via individualized scheduling orders.
