# Retirement Criterion

# Retirement Criterion

Every technology initiative should have a defined retirement criterion.

## Possible Triggers

- failure to produce expected outcomes;
- unacceptable risk;
- obsolete technology;
- excessive cost;
- replacement by a superior system;
- legal incompatibility;
- security concerns.

## Principle

**Not every pilot should become permanent infrastructure.**
