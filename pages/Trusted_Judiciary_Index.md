# Trusted Judiciary Index

# Trusted Judiciary Index

The Trusted Judiciary Index is proposed as a strategic measure of public confidence in the future judiciary.

## Potential Dimensions

- fairness;
- accessibility;
- transparency;
- integrity;
- reliability;
- digital trust;
- perceived accountability.

## Strategic Outcome

Technology should ultimately contribute to a judiciary that is more trusted, not merely more digital.
