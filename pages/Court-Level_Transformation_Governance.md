# Court-Level Transformation Governance

# Court-Level Transformation Governance

Transformation must be implemented through courts rather than only through central institutions.

## Court Responsibilities

- implementation;
- user feedback;
- local process improvement;
- risk identification;
- training;
- incident reporting;
- outcome measurement.

## Principle

National standards + local learning.
