# AI Assessment

**AI assessment** evaluates whether a judicial professional has demonstrated the knowledge and skills required for responsible AI use.

## Assessment Dimensions

Potential dimensions include:

- AI knowledge
- Prompting
- Source verification
- Data interpretation
- Privacy awareness
- Security awareness
- Ethical reasoning
- Risk assessment

## Assessment Methods

Possible methods include:

- Knowledge tests
- Practical exercises
- Case simulations
- Prompt evaluation
- Source verification exercises
- Scenario-based assessment

## Example Exercise

A participant receives an AI-generated legal memorandum containing three fabricated citations.

The participant must:

1.  Identify the fabricated citations.
2.  Locate the authoritative sources.
3.  Explain the errors.
4.  Correct the memorandum.
5.  Document the verification process.

## Competency Evidence

Assessment should measure demonstrated ability rather than simple familiarity with AI terminology.
