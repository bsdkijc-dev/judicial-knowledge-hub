# Renstra Vision

# Vision of the Supreme Court

The vision of the Supreme Court remains:

**“The Realization of an Excellent Indonesian Judiciary.”**

The vision is derived from the long-term Judicial Reform Blueprint 2010–2035.

## Strategic Meaning

The vision provides the long-term institutional destination toward which the 2025–2029 strategic plan is directed.
