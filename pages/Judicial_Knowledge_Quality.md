# Judicial Knowledge Quality

# Judicial Knowledge Quality

Knowledge quality should be evaluated through:

- accuracy;
- completeness;
- consistency;
- timeliness;
- provenance;
- authority;
- accessibility.

## Quality Rule

A highly searchable but poorly sourced page is not high-quality judicial knowledge.
