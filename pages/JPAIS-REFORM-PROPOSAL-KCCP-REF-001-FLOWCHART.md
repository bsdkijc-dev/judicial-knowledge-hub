## Executive Summary & Framework Context / Ringkasan Eksekutif & Konteks Kerangka

The KCCP-REF-001 proposal (Tripartite Conciliation Panels and Orders in Lieu of Settlement) integrates directly with the Evidence-Based Judicial Policy Framework developed under Pustrajak Kumdil BSDK. Rather than launching nationwide court-annexed mediation reforms through immediate statutory directives, the implementation model is designed around empirical testing, data validation, and policy feedback loops.

## Integration with Evidence-Based Policy Domains / Integrasi dengan Domain Kebijakan Berbasis Bukti

1\. Judicial Policy Laboratory & Controlled Sandboxes (JPAIS-LAB): Controlled Pilot Testing via Chief Justice pilot decree (SK KMA) authorizing temporary procedural adaptations in designated testing courts (Pengadilan Percontohan). The 90-day pilot across six target courts (PN Jakarta Pusat, PN Surabaya, PN Medan, PA Jakarta Selatan, PA Surabaya, PA Malang) acts as a controlled sandbox for pre- and post-intervention empirical evaluations before proposing permanent amendments to PERMA No. 1 of 2016 or RUU Hukum Acara Perdata. 2. Standardized Micro-Data Capture (JPAIS-STAT): Automated SIPP Schema capturing Tripartite Pra-Sidang transactions (panel auto-triaging, expert matching, 14-day objection clock) as structured JSON micro-data objects. Privacy compliance maintained through automated anonymization under SK KMA No. 2-144/KMA/SK/VIII/2022. 3. Empirical Verification & Multi-Dimensional Dashboards (JPAIS-EVAL & JPAIS-DASH): Biennial Performance Audits utilizing empirical verification metrics rather than mechanical clearance rates. The recalibrated Multi-Dimensional Judicial Performance Dashboard (MD-JPD) assigns 30% weight to Adjudication Quality and Reasoning Stability (including Substantive Settlement Rates and settlement durability upon appeal).
