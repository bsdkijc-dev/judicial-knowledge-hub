# AI Audit

**AI audit** is a structured examination of an AI system, its data, processes, controls, performance, and governance.

## Audit Dimensions

An audit may examine:

- Accuracy
- Reliability
- Bias
- Security
- Privacy
- Data quality
- Documentation
- Human oversight
- Compliance
- Performance

## Audit Lifecycle

**SCOPE** → **COLLECT EVIDENCE** → **TEST** → **ANALYZE** → **REPORT** → **REMEDIATE** → **FOLLOW-UP**

## Evidence

Audit evidence may include:

- System documentation
- Test results
- Logs
- Data samples
- Model documentation
- User reports
- Incident records

## Independence

The appropriate degree of auditor independence depends on the risk and institutional context.

## Continuous Assurance

For important systems, audit should not necessarily be a one-time activity.
