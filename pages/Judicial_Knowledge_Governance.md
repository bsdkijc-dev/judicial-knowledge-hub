# Judicial Knowledge Governance

# Judicial Knowledge Governance

Judicial Knowledge Governance establishes rules for creating, validating, maintaining, accessing, and retiring institutional knowledge.

## Governance Principles

- Accuracy
- Authenticity
- Traceability
- Version control
- Access control
- Accountability
- Human verification

## AI Principle

AI may assist knowledge processing but does not replace authoritative human validation.
