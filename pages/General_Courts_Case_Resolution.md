# General Courts Case Resolution

The second program outcome concerns certain and equitable case resolution within the General Courts.

## Selected Indicators

- timely resolution of criminal and corruption cases at appellate level;
- timely resolution of qualifying criminal, corruption and PHI cases

` at first instance;`

- timely transmission of judgment copies;
- judgment publication;
- civil judgment execution;
- restorative justice;
- mediation;
- juvenile diversion;
- e-Court;
- e-Berpadu.
