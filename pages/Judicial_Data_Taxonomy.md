# Judicial Data Taxonomy

# Judicial Data Taxonomy

A national judicial data taxonomy should provide common conceptual categories and metadata.

## Potential Domains

- cases;
- judgments;
- parties;
- courts;
- proceedings;
- legislation;
- evidence;
- performance;
- workforce;
- services.

## Requirement

The taxonomy should respect legal mandates, privacy and institutional authority.
