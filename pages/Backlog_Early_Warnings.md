# Backlog Early Warnings

# Backlog Early Warning

Backlog Early Warning identifies emerging workload pressures before they become systemic bottlenecks.

## Inputs

- pending cases;
- duration;
- workload;
- staffing;
- procedural bottlenecks.

## Output

An alert for human management intervention.
