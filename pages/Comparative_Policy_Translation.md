# Comparative Policy Translation

International research should be converted into Indonesian design questions.

## Translation Process

1.  Identify foreign practice.
2.  Identify evidence.
3.  Identify institutional conditions.
4.  Identify legal conditions.
5.  Identify differences with Indonesia.
6.  Assess transferability.
7.  Design Indonesian adaptation.
8.  Pilot.
9.  Evaluate.

## Principle

**Comparative research informs design; it does not determine policy.**
