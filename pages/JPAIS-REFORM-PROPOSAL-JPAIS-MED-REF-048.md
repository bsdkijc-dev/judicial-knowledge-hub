## Description / Deskripsi

This page records the comprehensive synthesis of the Master Problem Inventory List (Daftar Inventarisasi Masalah / DIM) prepared for the amendment of Supreme Court Regulation No. 3 of 2021 (PERMA No. 3/2021) concerning the Procedures for Filing and Examining Objections against KPPU Decisions in Commercial Courts. Compiled in August 2026 under the auspices of the Agency for Legal and Judicial Strategy Development (BSDK Mahkamah Agung RI), this master synthesis integrates empirical and normative inputs from eight distinct primary sources: (1) PN Jakarta Pusat DIM & Statistics Report (23 July 2026); (2) PN Medan Internal DIM; (3) PN Semarang DIM & Statistics Report (2021–2026); (4) PN Surabaya DIM & Statistics Report; (5) PN Makassar Case Presentation (2024–2026); (6) Economic Analysis & Evidence Standards Paper by Ine Ruky (2 August 2026); (7) Practice Issues Submission by the Indonesian Competition Lawyers Association (ICLA, 3 August 2026); and (8) Comparative PERMA Trajectory Analysis by Prof. Dr. Ningrum Natasya Sirait (3 August 2026). The consolidated dataset identifies 35 specific problem points structured across 9 issue clusters, targeting 14 existing PERMA articles for amendment and identifying 12 novel legal issues currently lacking statutory coverage in PERMA No. 3/2021. All structural entities, issue distributions, and core reform proposals have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher **Cecep Mustafa**.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-059](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-059): Administrative Overload in Groups C and G, where Groups C (Formal Requirements & Administration) and G (Witness & Expert Examination) represent the highest workload concentration, each containing 5 distinct problem points, causing litigation stalls due to administrative logjams (SIPP multi-petitioner lockouts, bank guarantee disputes) and procedural uncertainty surrounding expert/witness testimony.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-060](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-060): Information Asymmetry & Evidentiary Lock, where petitioners face severe information asymmetry due to restricted access to investigation dossiers (\*BAP/inzage\*) and missing protective \*Data Rooms\*, while Article 13(5) imposes a strict evidence lock prohibiting new documentary evidence even when KPPU introduces novel factual findings in final written decisions.

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-048](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-048): Objections are processed without pre-trial court mediation (Article 12) or formal \*inzage\* rules, forcing commercial judges to conduct dossier-bound reviews without economic expert evaluation tools, while SIPP and e-Court software architectures reject secondary objection filings against the same KPPU ruling, and bank guarantees are demanded indiscriminately.

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-048](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-048): International comparative practices embodied in the 8 Core Reform Proposals include Germany's specialized \*Kartellsenat\* review, full \*inzage\* rights, and secure \*Data Rooms\* (GWB); Australia's \*\*Hot Tubbing (Concurrent Expert Evidence)\*\* for simultaneous expert economic testimony under court supervision (ACT); and Japan's structured judicial review over JFTC economic market definitions.

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-048](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-048): The primary structural gap is that 12 out of 35 problem items (34.3% of all DIM items) currently have zero statutory coverage in PERMA No. 3/2021, leaving judges without procedural guidance on critical matters like MSME partnership appeals, economic analysis review standards, \*Data Rooms\*, and \*Hot Tubbing\*.

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Implement the 8 Core Reform Proposals (\*Usulan Pokok yang Mengemuka\*): (1) Uniform Deadlines harmonized with PP No. 44/2021 (working days) and bifurcated memory submission; (2) Proportional Bank Guarantee restricted strictly to monetary fines; (3) Clear Review Scope affirming \*judex facti\* and relevant market re-examination; (4) Economic Testing Standards for methodology, data, assumptions, and alternatives; (5) Reciprocal Evidence Lock Relaxation; (6) File Access & Confidentiality (\*inzage\*, two-version documents, \*Data Rooms\*); (7) Trial Modernization (online video expert examination, e-Court master case consolidation); and (8) Corrective Mechanisms & Execution (strict Judicial Review / PK and bailiff asset tracing).

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-050](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-050): Draft and enact the comprehensive PERMA Amandemen 3/2021 incorporating the 8 Core Reform Proposals during Phase 1 (Short-Term / 2026).
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-051](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-051): Upgrade SIPP/e-Court architecture for Master Register Consolidation and promulgate KMA Adjudication Guidelines for Commercial Judges during Phase 2 (Medium-Term / 2027).

## Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-048](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-048): Implementation across all 5 Commercial Courts (Central Jakarta, Medan, Semarang, Surabaya, Makassar) under the Master DIM Reform Package.

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-048](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-048): 100% resolution of the 12 unregulated novel issues in the amended PERMA text; 100% elimination of e-Court registration lockouts for multi-petitioner cartel objections; adjudication efficiency maintained strictly at \$\le 60\$ working days per case.

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Comprehensive alignment of Supreme Court competition rules with PP No. 44/2021, Law No. 6/2023, and Law No. 1/2023 (New KUHP), alongside strengthening institutional capacity building for Commercial Court judges through specialized BSDK MA RI economic certification programs.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgating an updated Supreme Court Regulation (PERMA) that systematically resolves all 35 operational and legal issues identified across the 9 DIM clusters.

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Master Problem Inventory List presentation document (\*PPT Amandemen PERMA 3_2021\*) synthesized by the Supreme Court Working Group and BSDK MA RI in August 2026, integrating inputs from 8 primary regional and expert sources.
