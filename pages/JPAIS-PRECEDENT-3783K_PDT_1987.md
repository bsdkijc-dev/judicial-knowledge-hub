# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:3783K PDT 1987

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 3783 K/Pdt/1987** (Interlocutory Decision dated June 14, 1989 / Final Deliberation Decision dated November 21, 1989, pronounced on February 14, 1990) is a fundamental civil procedure and national land law jurisprudence precedent establishing vital rules concerning the Supreme Court's authority to issue Interlocutory Decisions (*Putusan Sela*) to conduct supplementary examinations (*aanvullend onderzoek*) at the cassation level, the impossibility of customary rights/girik titles arising over State Land originating from Former Private Eigendom under Law No. 1 of 1958, and the dismissal of land ownership claims based on forged inheritance decrees and fictitious girik titles.

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established three primary legal principles:

1.  **Authority of Supplementary Examination via Cassation Interlocutory Decisions:** Before rendering a final decision, the Supreme Court is authorized to issue an Interlocutory Decision to conduct a supplementary examination (*aanvullend onderzoek*) executed directly by the Supreme Court to summon expert witnesses (Agrarian Service, Land Tax/Ipeda, Sub-district Head, Village Head, Notary, and Court Registrar) to ascertain the precise status/location of the disputed land and examine the material truth of evidence where the *Judex Facti* misapplied the law of evidence.
2.  **Incompatibility of Customary/Girik Rights Over State Land Originating from Former Private Eigendom (Law No. 1 of 1958):** Over State Land originating from Former Eigendom Verponding Nos. 3861 and 1959, which was affirmed as land directly controlled by the State pursuant to Law No. 1 of 1958, **customary rights (such as girik titles / Ipeda tax assessments) cannot possibly attach**.
3.  **Lawsuits Based on Forged Inheritance Decrees and Fictitious Giriks Must Be Dismissed:** Land ownership lawsuits grounded on rights-transfer deeds supported by Judicial Inheritance Decrees proven to be fictitious/forged (testimony of Special District Court Registrar of Jakarta \[Party A\]) and girik titles unrecorded in Ipeda land tax registers **must be declared unproven and dismissed entirely**.

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioner for Cassation (State of the Republic of Indonesia *qq.* Government of the Republic of Indonesia *qq.* Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Indonesia), annulled Jakarta High Court Decision No. 375/Pdt/1987/PT.DKI dated August 31, 1987, *jo.* South Jakarta District Court Decision No. 148/Pdt/G/1986/PN.Jkt.Sel. dated June 16, 1987, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by dismissing the original Plaintiff's lawsuit (Yayasan Pendidikan Wiraswasta Indonesia) entirely, declaring the conservatory attachment invalid, and ordering the lifting of the conservatory attachment.

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Authority of the Supreme Court to Conduct Supplementary Examinations (*Aanvullend Onderzoek*) via Cassation Interlocutory Decisions (Law No. 14 of 1985 *jo.* Law No. 14 of 1970)

*Does the Supreme Court at the cassation level possess the authority to issue an interlocutory decision to summon and examine witnesses, land administration officials, and review evidence directly during cassation hearings?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Authorized / Constitutional.** Before rendering a final decision, the Supreme Court is authorized to issue an Interlocutory Decision to conduct a supplementary examination (*aanvullend onderzoek*) carried out by the Supreme Court itself. This step is undertaken to clearly ascertain the status and location of disputed land and uncover material truths where the *Judex Facti* is proven to have misapplied evidentiary law.

### 2. Juridical Status of Customary/Girik Rights Over State Land Originating from Former Private Eigendom *ex* Law No. 1 of 1958

*Can customary rights/giriks arise, be recognized, or be maintained over parcels of land designated as State Land originating from Former Private Eigendom pursuant to Law No. 1 of 1958?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Impossible / Incompatible.** Over State Land originating from Eigendom Verponding/Private Eigendom which, pursuant to Law No. 1 of 1958 *jo.* Agrarian Minister Decrees, was affirmed as land directly controlled by the State, customary rights such as girik/Ipeda land titles cannot possibly attach.

### 3. Legal Consequences of Land Ownership Lawsuits Based on Forged Inheritance Decrees and Fictitious Giriks

Notarial rights-transfer deeds grounded on judicial inheritance decrees proven to be fictitious/forged (pursuant to testimony from court registrars) and giriks unrecorded in PBB Ipeda registers are qualified as legally defective transactions conferring no ownership rights whatsoever. Land ownership lawsuits must be dismissed entirely, and conservatory attachments improperly placed over State assets must be lifted.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* Jakarta High Court (Decision No. 375/Pdt/1987/PT.DKI) and South Jakarta District Court (Decision No. 148/Pdt/G/1986/PN.Jkt.Sel.) err in applying agrarian evidentiary law (*foutieve toepassing van het bewijsrecht*) by granting land ownership lawsuits grounded on girik titles over State Land originating from Former Private Eigendom under Law No. 1 of 1958?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. The *Judex Facti* misapplied the law. Through an Interlocutory Decision and Supplementary Examination, the Supreme Court proved that the Plaintiff's inheritance documents were forged and giriks fictitious. The Supreme Court granted the Ministry of Finance's cassation petition, annulled Jakarta High Court's decision, and independently adjudicated by dismissing the Plaintiff's lawsuit entirely and ordering the lifting of the conservatory attachment.
