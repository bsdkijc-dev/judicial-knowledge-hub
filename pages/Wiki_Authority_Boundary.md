# Wiki Authority Boundary

The Judicial Knowledge Wiki is an institutional knowledge system, not a substitute for official legal publication.

## Distinction

**Official authoritative text** ≠ **Institutional explanatory knowledge**

## Rule

The Wiki must clearly identify whether content is:

- authoritative;
- explanatory;
- analytical;
- comparative;
- proposed;
- historical.

## Purpose

This boundary protects legal reliability and prevents users from mistaking an explanatory Wiki page for an official legal instrument.
