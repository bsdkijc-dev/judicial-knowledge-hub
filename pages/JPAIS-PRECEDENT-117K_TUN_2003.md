## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 117 K/TUN/2003 dated October 26, 2004, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal rules regarding the legal protection of permanent workers and termination procedures: Termination of Employment (\*PHK\*) executed by an employer (Bank Internasional Indonesia / BII) against a worker (Samuel M.L. Tobing) without the authorization of the Central Committee for Labor Dispute Settlement (\*P4P\*), despite the worker legally holding the status of a worker for an indefinite period (permanent worker), is null and void under the law. Furthermore, the grounds for Termination of Employment (\*PHK\*) based on worker incapacity or indiscipline must be proven beforehand by the employer before the court.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Petitioner for Cassation (Samuel M.L. Tobing, Security Guard at BII Sub-Branch Jatinegara) had been employed since August 6, 1996. Although his employment contract had been extended consecutively beyond statutory limits (Minister of Manpower Regulation No. 2 of 1993), changing his employment status by operation of law into a permanent worker (\*PKWTT\*), the Original Defendant / Respondent for Cassation (Central Committee for Labor Dispute Settlement / P4P) issued Decree No. 1110/1385/196-3/IX/PHK/7-2000 dated July 24, 2000, terminating the employment relationship between BII and the Plaintiff accompanied by severance pay amounting to 2 times the standard multiple (\*PMTK\*).

The Plaintiff filed a lawsuit against the P4P decree at the Jakarta State Administrative High Court. The Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 307/G/2001/PT.TUN.JKT dated August 6, 2001) rejected the Plaintiff's lawsuit in its entirety. Dissatisfied with the ruling, the Plaintiff filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  Termination of Employment (\*PHK\*) carried out by the employer Bank Internasional Indonesia (BII) against worker Samuel M.L. Tobing without authorization from the P4P, even though the worker had already attained the status of a worker for an indefinite period (permanent worker), is contrary to the law.
2.  The grounds for Termination of Employment (\*PHK\*) based on incapacity or indiscipline must be proven in advance at trial by the employer.
3.  The \*Judex Facti\* (Jakarta State Administrative High Court) committed an error in applying the law by upholding the P4P decree that legitimized termination without authorization and without proof of the worker's faults or indiscipline. Consequently, the appellate administrative ruling must be annulled, and the Plaintiff must be reinstated as a permanent employee.

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation SAMUEL M.L. TOBING; Annulling the Jakarta State Administrative High Court decision dated August 6, 2001, No. 307/G/2001/PT.TUN.JKT; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit in its entirety; (2) Declaring null and void under the law the Defendant's (P4P) Decree No. 1110/1385/196-3/IX/PHK/7-2000 dated July 24, 2000, concerning the Termination of Employment between Bank Internasional Indonesia (BII) Jatinegara Branch, Jakarta, and Samuel M.L. Tobing; (3) Ordering the Defendant to issue a new decree obligating the employer Bank Internasional Indonesia to reinstate the Plaintiff while granting all employment rights effective from August 6, 1999, until the decision acquires permanent legal force, complete with delay interest on wage payments pursuant to Government Regulation No. 8 of 1981, and obligating BII management to issue an official appointment decree elevating the Plaintiff's status from a contract worker to a permanent worker; (4) Ordering the Respondent for Cassation to pay cassation-level court costs amounting to Rp 200,000. \*(Decided on Tuesday, October 26, 2004, by the Cassation Panel of Justices: Ny. Chairani A. Wani, SH. \[Presiding\], Ny. Titi Nurmala Siagian, SH., and Prof. DR. H. Ahmad Sukardja, SH.)\*.
