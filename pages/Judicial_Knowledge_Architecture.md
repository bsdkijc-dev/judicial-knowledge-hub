# Judicial Knowledge Architecture

The Judicial Knowledge Architecture defines how judicial knowledge is organized, described, connected, governed, and made discoverable.

## Core Layers

1.  **Source Layer** — laws, regulations, judgments, official reports,

` research, policies, and institutional documents.`

1.  **Evidence Layer** — verified claims and their provenance.
2.  **Knowledge Object Layer** — structured judicial concepts,

` doctrines, institutions, processes, and reforms.`

1.  **Relationship Layer** — semantic relationships between knowledge

` objects.`

1.  **Application Layer** — research, education, policy analysis,

` judicial learning, and institutional decision support.`

## AI Readiness

A structured knowledge architecture enables:

- semantic search;
- retrieval-augmented generation;
- knowledge graph construction;
- evidence-based question answering;
- institutional knowledge preservation.
