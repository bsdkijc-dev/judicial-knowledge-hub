# Judgment Publication Indicator

# Judgment Publication Indicator

The indicator measures compliance by first-instance courts, appellate courts and the Supreme Court in uploading judgments to the Decision Directory.

## Timing Requirement

The Renstra states that publication should occur no later than the time the case is minuted.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Judgments uploaded to Decision Directory}} {\text{Judgments minuted}}\times100\\ \</math\>
