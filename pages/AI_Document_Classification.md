# AI Document Classification

# AI Document Classification

AI Document Classification is proposed to classify documents and support administrative routing.

## Control Mechanisms

- confidence thresholds;
- human exception handling;
- controlled pilot;
- quality evaluation;
- auditability.

## Boundary

The initiative is administrative.

It should not be interpreted as delegating judicial authority to an algorithm.
