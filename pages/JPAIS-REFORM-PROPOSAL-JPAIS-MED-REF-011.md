# JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:JPAIS-MED-REF-011

## Summary / Ringkasan

## Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

- Entity ID: JPAIS-MED-REF-011
- Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
- Ingestion Status: Confirmed

## Description / Deskripsi

Provides an exhaustive comparative assessment of access to justice across eight core domains between Indonesia and Japan, addressing structural deficiencies such as Indonesia's fragmented legal aid reimbursement ceilings and archipelagic transit bottlenecks versus Japan's consolidated legal support corporation (Hōterasu), summary court network, and digital civil procedure transformation, and proposes an integrated reform framework\[cite: 11\].

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-001): Indonesian state-funded legal aid is structurally fragmented, geographically maldistributed, and fiscally constrained by retrospective reimbursement ceilings, forcing indigent litigants to rely on under-resourced non-governmental OBHs\[cite: 11\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-002): Archipelagic geography, marine transit costs, and rugged terrain impose severe physical and financial barriers on rural populations reaching distant courts, while electronic litigation (\*e-Court\* and \*e-Litigasi\*) creates a digital divide that excludes self-represented and marginalized litigants\[cite: 11\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-003): Vulnerable users face secondary trauma and gender bias, disabled and linguistic minorities encounter inaccessibility and lack of certified sign interpreters, small claims face procedural bottlenecks, mediation suffers from formalism, and public information transparency experiences inconsistencies under data privacy mandates (UU PDP)\[cite: 11\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-001): Governed by Law No. 16 of 2011 (BPHN/OBH), Perma No. 1 of 2014 (Posbakum), Perma No. 1/2019 and Perma No. 7/2022 (e-Court), Perma No. 3/2017 (Women/Children), PP No. 39/2020 (Disabilities), Perma No. 4/2019 (Small Claims), Perma No. 1/2016 (Mediation), and SK KMA No. 2-144/2022 (Public Information Standards)\[cite: 11\].

## Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-001): Governed by the Comprehensive Legal Support Act (Hōterasu), Summary Court network (Kan'i Saibansho), CCP digital amendments taking full effect May 21, 2026, Crime Victims and DV Protection Acts, Act on Elimination of Discrimination against Persons with Disabilities, Small Claims track (Shōgaku Soshō), Civil Conciliation Act (Minji Chōtei), and APPI data privacy frameworks\[cite: 11\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-001): Separation of courthouse advice from defense, episodic mobile court hearings, mandatory e-court portals without unrepresented paper support or open-court video displays, and lack of dedicated operational budgets for disability and trauma accommodations\[cite: 11\].
- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-002): Absence of intermediate legal professions (\*shihō shoshi\* equivalents), solo mediator capacity constraints, and manual clerk redactions leading to publication errors under data privacy statutes\[cite: 11\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Establish permanent Public Defender Stations (Sentra Advokat Publik) with salaried public advocates and DTKS automated fee waivers, deploy Tele-Justice Access Pods in island sub-districts, institutionalize assisted digital filing at PTSP desks with mandatory paper backup, implement automated vulnerability screening and UPTD PPA referrals, fund certified sign-language interpreters, introduce interactive small-claims form kiosks with active judicial guidance, form tripartite expert conciliation panels, and deploy AI-assisted automated pseudonymization and redaction pipelines\[cite: 11\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-001): Promulgate joint ministerial and Supreme Court decrees (SKB Tiga Lembaga) establishing the National Public Defender Roster, remote archipelagic hearing SOPs, and disability/linguistic accommodation budgets\[cite: 11\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-002): Deploy the 90-day multi-domain pilot across six targeted regional jurisdictions (Wamena, Saumlaki, Tahuna, Natuna, Talaud, Surabaya, Bandung, Padang, Denpasar, Mataram, Sleman, Kupang, Ambon)\[cite: 11\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-003): Audit pilot performance metrics, evaluate representation rates and digital access, finalize permanent PERMA regulations, and submit statutory reform proposals to DPR\[cite: 11\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-011](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-011): 90-day multi-domain pilot program across 14 designated first-instance jurisdictions spanning remote islands, urban centers, and regional trial courts\[cite: 11\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-011](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-011): Indigent civil representation \$\ge 45\\\$; mean prodeo processing duration \$\< 24\$ hours; rural travel cost reduction \$\ge 75\\\$; self-represented digital initiations increased by \$50\\\$; vulnerability pre-trial assessment \$100\\\$; disability assessment completion \$100\\\$ with zero linguistic hearing postponements; small claims 25-day compliance \$\ge 95\\\$; mediation success \$\ge 40\\\$; automated PII masking precision \$99.8\\\$\[cite: 11\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Promulgate joint ministerial regulations, Supreme Court circulars, and executive-legislative framework codifications to establish permanent national budget appropriations for salaried public defenders, remote satellite terminals, and algorithmic privacy compliance\[cite: 11\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgate comprehensive Supreme Court Regulations (PERMA) amending Perma No. 7 of 2022 (E-Court), Perma No. 4 of 2019 (Small Claims), Perma No. 1 of 2016 (Mediation), alongside unified regulations on Disability/Linguistic Accommodations, Trauma-Informed Courtrooms, and automated SK KMA 2-144/2022 pseudonymization\[cite: 11\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Indonesia's e-Court registered 410,754 first-instance cases in 2024, but unrepresented individual litigants (\*Pengguna Lain\*) account for a negligible fraction due to strict onboarding barriers\[cite: 11\].
- Japan's Hōterasu operates with a central budget of JPY 32.1 billion, supporting 50 branches and salaried staff attorneys, while its amended CCP digital transformation culminates on May 21, 2026\[cite: 11\].
