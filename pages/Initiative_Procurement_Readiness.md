# Initiative Procurement Readiness

# Initiative Procurement Readiness

Technology procurement should occur only after the institution has defined the problem, requirements, governance and evaluation criteria.

## Readiness Checklist

- problem validated;
- requirements defined;
- legal basis checked;
- data requirements defined;
- interoperability assessed;
- security assessed;
- vendor risk assessed;
- total cost estimated;
- exit strategy defined;
- evaluation criteria established.
