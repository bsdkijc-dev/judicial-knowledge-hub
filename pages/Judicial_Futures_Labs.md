# Judicial Futures Lab

The Judicial Futures Lab develops evidence-based scenarios and strategic options for the future judiciary.

## Activities

- horizon scanning;
- scenario development;
- policy experiments;
- technology assessment;
- stakeholder research;
- strategic foresight.
