# Legal-Tech Fellowship

The Legal-Tech Fellowship develops professionals capable of bridging law, technology, data and judicial practice.

## Potential Activities

- applied research;
- court technology projects;
- knowledge engineering;
- AI governance;
- policy development;
- international benchmarking.
