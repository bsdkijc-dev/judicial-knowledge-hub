# Estonia Transferability Framework

Estonian practices should be assessed before adaptation.

## Transferability Questions

1.  Is the institutional context comparable?
2.  Is the legal basis compatible?
3.  Is the infrastructure available?
4.  Is the workforce capability sufficient?
5.  Can the practice operate at Indonesian scale?
6.  Does it preserve judicial independence?
7.  Does it improve justice outcomes?

## Principle

**Transfer the principle, not automatically the institution.**
