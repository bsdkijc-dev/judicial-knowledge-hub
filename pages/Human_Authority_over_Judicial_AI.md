# Human Authority over Judicial AI

# Human Authority over Judicial AI

Judicial authority remains human, legal and constitutionally grounded.

AI may assist judicial work but should not replace judicial authority.

## Operating Principle

**AI assists → Humans verify → Judges decide**

## Implications

AI systems used in courts should not independently exercise judicial authority.

Human accountability must remain identifiable throughout the decision-support process.
