# JPW-POL-2018-COMP

## Summary

**FACT**: The [Comprehensive Measures for Acceptance and Coexistence of Foreign Nationals](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Comprehensive_Measures_for_Acceptance_and_Coexistence_of_Foreign_Nationals) (外国人の受入れ・共生のための総合的対応策) is Japan's national policy framework enacted on December 25, 2018, by the Ministerial Conference on Acceptance and Coexistence of Foreign Nationals. The policy outlines an inter-ministerial strategy to build an inclusive society where Japanese citizens and foreign residents can coexist safely and comfortably (\*society of harmonious coexistence\* / 共生社会). The FY2021 revised version includes **197 concrete policy measures** coordinated by the [Immigration Services Agency of Japan](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Immigration_Services_Agency_of_Japan) (ISA) acting as the administrative command center.

## Legal Basis

- **Cabinet Decision Base**: Basic Policy for Economic and Fiscal Management and Reform 2018 (Cabinet Decision, June 15, 2018).
- **General Coordination Mandate**: Cabinet Decision on July 24, 2018, designating general coordination authority to the Ministry of Justice.
- **Adoption & Revisions**: Decision of the 3rd Ministerial Conference (Dec 25, 2018), FY2020 Revision (July 14, 2020), and FY2021 Revision (June 15, 2021).

## Background

**FACT**: Severe labor shortages across small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) led to the creation of the *Specified Skilled Worker* status in 2018. The government recognized that expanding foreign workforce intake required establishing social infrastructure, translation services, and equal public service access to prevent foreign resident isolation.

## Objectives

1\. Establish a harmonious coexistence society (\*共生社会\*) where Japanese and foreign residents live together safely. 2. Provide seamless life-stage support including living consultations, language education, housing, healthcare, and employment security. 3. Ensure the fair, transparent intake of skilled foreign labor while eliminating unauthorized brokers and illegal intermediaries.

## Key Provisions

1\. **Foreign Residents Support Center (FRESC)**: Established a centralized multi-agency facility in Yotsuya, Tokyo in July 2020 housing units from 4 ministries and 8 support organizations. 2. **One-Stop Consultation Counters**: Central government funding to municipal consultation centers (214 local governments subsidized in FY2021). 3. **Plain Japanese Guidelines**: Published the \*Plain Japanese Guidelines for Foreign Residents Support\* (August 2020) and the \*Guidebook on Living and Working\* in 14 languages. 4. **Employment & Residency Frameworks**: Expanded the \*Specified Skilled Worker\* system across 14 industries and instituted strict measures against training program abscondment. 5. **Digital Systems Integration**: Initiated plans to merge the Residence Card and My Number Card by FY2025 alongside expanding online administrative applications.
