## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 02 K/AG/2001 establishes the legal principle that any marriage entered into by an individual who already has a wife must be accompanied by authorization from the Religious Courts and the consent of the first wife, as stipulated under Articles 3, 9, 24, and 25 of Law No. 1 of 1974. A polygamous marriage conducted using false information regarding marital status and domicile, and executed without the first wife's consent or Religious Court authorization, is null and void, rendering any marriage certificate issued by the Office of Religious Affairs (KUA) legally ineffective.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff (\[Party A\]) filed a marriage annulment lawsuit at the Sungai Penuh Religious Court against her husband, Defendant I (Muhammad Nasir bin H. Abdurrahman / H. Ciduk), and Defendant II (\[Party B\]). The Plaintiff and Defendant I had been legally married since 1963 and had four children. However, on June 14, 1998, Defendant I contracted a marriage with Defendant II at the KUA of Kayu Aro Subdistrict, Kerinci Regency, without the Plaintiff's consent, without polygamy authorization from the Religious Courts, and using falsified personal data. The Sungai Penuh Religious Court (Decision No. 112/Pdt.G/1998/PA.Spn) granted the Plaintiff's claim and annulled the marriage. However, the Jambi High Religious Court (Decision No. 10/Pdt.G/1999/PTA.Jb) overturned the Religious Court's decision and declared the Plaintiff's claim inadmissible. Consequently, the Plaintiff filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** A marriage contracted by an individual who already has a wife should be accompanied by authorization from the Religious Courts as mandated by Articles 3, 9, 24, and 25 of Law No. 1 of 1974. Because the marriage between Defendant I and Defendant II was carried out using false information concerning the status of the first wife and the defendants' domiciles, and lacked the first wife's consent and Religious Court authorization, the marriage must be annulled and the Kayu Aro KUA marriage certificate declared legally void and non-binding.
- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition of Petitioner \[Party A\]; annulling the Jambi High Religious Court decision No. 10/Pdt.G/1999/PTA.Jb dated December 6, 1999; and independently adjudicating: (1) Granting the Plaintiff's claim; (2) Annulling the marriage between Defendant I (Muhammad Nasir bin H. Abdurrahman / H. Ciduk) and Defendant II (\[Party B\]) solemnized on June 14, 1998; (3) Declaring Marriage Certificate No. 043/14/VI/1998 dated June 14, 1998, recorded by the Kayu Aro KUA, to be legally ineffective and non-binding; (4) Imposing procedural costs.
