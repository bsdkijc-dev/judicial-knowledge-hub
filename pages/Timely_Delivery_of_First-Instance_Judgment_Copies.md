# Timely Delivery of First-Instance Judgment Copies

Indicator 1.3 measures the percentage of judgment copies provided or sent by first-instance courts to the parties within the prescribed time.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Copies provided/sent on time}} {\text{Cases decided}}\times100\\ \</math\>

## Strategic Function

The indicator measures the timeliness of administrative service after judgment delivery.
