## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 216/K/TUN/2010 dated September 28, 2010, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal rules in State Administrative Law (Civil Service): Only the President of the Republic of Indonesia has the right and authority to dismiss and/or temporarily release a Civil Servant (\*PNS\*) holding a Principal Functional Position / Principal Widyaiswara (Rank IV/e) from and in their office. A Decree of the Minister of Finance temporarily releasing a Principal Widyaiswara of Rank IV/e contains a legal defect because it was issued outside of official authority (\*onbevoegd\* / \*ultra vires\*).

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Respondent for Cassation (Drs. \[Plaintiff\], Ak., MA., a Civil Servant holding the Functional Position of Principal Widyaiswara, Main Supervisor rank, Rank IV/e at Financial Training Center II Palembang, BPPK) received a copy of the Decree of the Minister of Finance of the Republic of Indonesia No. 113/KMK.01/UP.11/2009 dated April 1, 2009, concerning the Temporary Release of the Plaintiff from the Functional Position of Principal Widyaiswara effective from October 1, 2008. The Plaintiff filed a lawsuit seeking the annulment of the decree at the Jakarta State Administrative Court.

The Jakarta State Administrative Court (Decision No. 81/G/2009/PTUN-JKT dated September 2, 2009) granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in its entirety, declared the Minister of Finance's Decree No. 113/KMK.01/UP.11/2009 null and void, ordered the Defendant to revoke the decree, and ordered the Defendant to pay compensation of Rp 5,000,000 plus court costs. The Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 251/B/2009/PT.TUN.JKT dated February 2, 2010) upheld the Jakarta State Administrative Court judgment. Dissatisfied with the ruling, the Defendant (Minister of Finance of the Republic of Indonesia) filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The \*Judex Facti\* (Jakarta State Administrative Court and Jakarta State Administrative High Court) were accurate and committed no error in applying the law, as the disputed object decree contained a legal defect.
2.  The Defendant (Minister of Finance of the Republic of Indonesia) lacked the authority to issue the disputed State Administrative Decree.
3.  Pursuant to Article 12 of the Joint Regulation of the Head of LAN and the Head of BKN No. 7 of 2005 and No. 17 of 2005, as well as Article 26 of the Regulation of the Minister of State Apparatus Utilization No. PER/66/M.PAN/6/2005, the official authorized to determine the temporary release from and in the position of Principal Widyaiswara is identical to the official authorized to determine temporary dismissal from and in the position of Principal Widyaiswara, namely the President of the Republic of Indonesia.
4.  Legal Principle: "Only the President has the right to dismiss and/or temporarily release Civil Servants holding Principal Functional Positions / Principal Widyaiswara (rank IV/e) from and in their office."

- **Ruling:** Rejecting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation THE MINISTER OF FINANCE OF THE REPUBLIC OF INDONESIA; Ordering the Petitioner for Cassation to pay cassation-level court costs amounting to Rp 500,000 (five hundred thousand rupiahs). \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Tuesday, September 28, 2010, by Dr. H. Imam Soebechi, S.H., M.H. \[Presiding Judge\], Dr. H. Supandi, S.H., M.Hum., and Prof. Dr. Ahmad Sukardja, S.H., M.A. \[Associate Justices\]).\*
