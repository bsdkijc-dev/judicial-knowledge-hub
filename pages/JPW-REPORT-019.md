# JPW-REPORT-019

## ICC Annual Report to UNGA 2020/21 — Wiki Entry (English)

**Report of the International Criminal Court on its Activities in 2020/21** is the ICC's official annual report to the UN General Assembly for the period 1 August 2020 to 31 July 2021, submitted pursuant to Article 6 of the Relationship Agreement between the United Nations and the ICC and UN General Assembly Resolution 75/3.

## Summary

The report summarizes the ICC's judicial, prosecutorial, and institutional activities during the period, recording the conviction of Dominic Ongwen, confirmation of Bosco Ntaganda's sentence, the final acquittals of Gbagbo and Blé Goudé, the revocation of U.S. sanctions, a major change in ICC leadership, and progress on victim reparations.

## Legal Basis

- [Relationship Agreement between the United Nations and the International Criminal Court](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Relationship_Agreement_between_the_United_Nations_and_the_International_Criminal_Court): Article 6, on submission of annual reports.
- [UN General Assembly Resolution 75/3](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/UN_General_Assembly_Resolution_75/3): Paragraph 28.
- [Rome Statute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rome_Statute): The judicial framework, including Articles 15, 18, 58, 70, and 75.

## Background

Submission of this annual report to the 76th session of the UN General Assembly reflects the accountability relationship and principal communication channel between the ICC and the UN, aimed at strengthening the international criminal justice system.

## Objectives

- Report on judicial and prosecutorial achievements across 14 active situations.
- Address the operational challenges of the COVID-19 pandemic through hybrid trial mechanisms.
- Report progress on implementing the Independent Expert Review (IER) process.

## Key Provisions

- **Prosecutorial Activity**: Opening of a new investigation in Palestine (March 2021); a request for authorization to investigate in the Philippines (May 2021); completion of 4 preliminary examinations (Iraq/UK, Nigeria, Ukraine, Philippines); and receipt of a new referral from Bolivia.
- **Judicial Decisions**: Conviction of Dominic Ongwen (25 years); confirmation of Bosco Ntaganda's conviction (30 years, plus a \$30 million reparations order); the final acquittals of Gbagbo and Blé Goudé; vacating of the arrest warrant against Simone Gbagbo; and confirmation of charges against Ali Kushayb (31 counts) and Paul Gicheru (8 counts).
- **Institutional Development**: Election of Karim Khan (Prosecutor), Piotr Hofmański (ICC President), and Silvia Fernández de Gurmendi (ASP President).
- **Diplomatic Development**: U.S. revocation of Executive Order 13928 on 2 April 2021.

## Implementation

Carried out through close coordination among the Court's organs (Presidency, OTP, Registry), the UN, the Trust Fund for Victims (managing 27 projects), and civil society organizations. The Court adopted hybrid proceedings and distributed work arrangements to maintain its judicial calendar despite the COVID-19 pandemic.

## Evidence

- Participation of approximately 11,000 victims in ICC proceedings, with 2,300+ new victim applications registered.
- Registration of 612 Article 15 communications (438 outside jurisdiction).
- Transmission of 472 requests for assistance by the OTP and 222 principal cooperation requests by the Registry.
- Ratification/accession to the Article 8 Amendments, the Crime of Aggression Amendments, and the 2019 Starvation Amendment by a number of States Parties.

## Policy Issues

- Lack of UN Security Council response to 16 findings of non-cooperation.
- Challenges in executing 12 outstanding arrest warrants.

## Relationships with Other Entities

- **[International Criminal Court](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/International_Criminal_Court)**: Reporting institution.
- **[United Nations General Assembly](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/United_Nations_General_Assembly)**: Recipient of the report.
- **[Dominic Ongwen](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Dominic_Ongwen)**: Defendant in the Northern Uganda situation, convicted of 61 crimes against humanity and war crimes and sentenced to 25 years' imprisonment in May 2021 (on appeal).
- **[Bosco Ntaganda](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Bosco_Ntaganda)**: Defendant in the DRC situation, whose conviction and 30-year sentence were confirmed by the Appeals Chamber on 30 March 2021, alongside a \$30 million reparations order.
- **[Laurent Gbagbo](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Laurent_Gbagbo)** and **[Charles Blé Goudé](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Charles_Bl%C3%A9_Goud%C3%A9)**: Defendants in the Côte d'Ivoire situation, whose acquittals were finally confirmed by the Appeals Chamber on 31 March 2021.
- **[Simone Gbagbo](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Simone_Gbagbo)**: Former First Lady of Côte d'Ivoire, whose arrest warrant was vacated by Pre-Trial Chamber II on 19 July 2021, upon the Prosecutor's request, for failing to meet the Article 58 evidentiary threshold.
- **[Alfred Yekatom](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Alfred_Yekatom)** and **[Patrice-Edouard Ngaïssona](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Patrice-Edouard_Nga%C3%AFssona)**: Anti-Balaka defendants (CAR II situation), whose trial began 16 February 2021 before Trial Chamber V.
- **[Mahamat Said Abdel Kani](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Mahamat_Said_Abdel_Kani)**: Séléka defendant (CAR II situation), who surrendered and was transferred to ICC custody on 24 January 2021.
- **[Al Hassan Ag Abdoul Aziz Ag Mohamed Ag Mahmoud](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Al_Hassan_Ag_Abdoul_Aziz_Ag_Mohamed_Ag_Mahmoud)**: Defendant in the Mali situation, whose trial continued before Trial Chamber X over crimes in Timbuktu.
- **[Ali Muhammad Ali Abd-Al-Rahman](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Ali_Muhammad_Ali_Abd-Al-Rahman)** ("Ali Kushayb"): Defendant in the Darfur, Sudan situation, whose 31 counts of war crimes and crimes against humanity were confirmed by Pre-Trial Chamber II on 9 July 2021.
- **[Paul Gicheru](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Paul_Gicheru)**: Defendant in the Kenya situation, who surrendered in November 2020, with 8 counts of offences against the administration of justice confirmed on 15 July 2021.
- **[Karim Asad Ahmad Khan](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Karim_Asad_Ahmad_Khan)**: ICC Prosecutor elected by the Assembly of States Parties for a nine-year term, taking office 16 June 2021.
- **[Piotr Hofmański](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Piotr_Hofma%C5%84ski)**: Judge elected President of the International Criminal Court on 11 March 2021 for a three-year term.
- **[Silvia Fernández de Gurmendi](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Silvia_Fern%C3%A1ndez_de_Gurmendi)**: President of the Assembly of States Parties, elected by acclamation for the 20th to 22nd sessions.
- **[Joe Biden](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Joe_Biden)** / US Administration: U.S. President who revoked Executive Order 13928 on 2 April 2021, ending economic sanctions and visa restrictions against ICC officials.

- **[Ferdi Raditya Akbar](Ferdi_Raditya_Akbar.md)**: **Not found in the available sources.**
