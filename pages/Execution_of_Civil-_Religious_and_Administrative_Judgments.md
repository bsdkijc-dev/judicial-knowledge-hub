# Execution of Civil, Religious and Administrative Judgments

Indicator 1.7 measures the percentage of execution applications for civil, religious-court and administrative-court judgments that are resolved.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Execution applications resolved}} {\text{Judgments for which execution is requested}}\times100\\ \</math\>

## Strategic Importance

The indicator focuses on the implementation of judgments after they have been issued.
