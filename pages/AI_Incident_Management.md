# AI Incident Management

**AI incident management** is the process for identifying, reporting, containing, investigating, correcting, and learning from harmful or unexpected AI-related events.

## Potential Incidents

Examples may include:

- Fabricated legal authorities
- Serious incorrect outputs
- Data leakage
- Unauthorized access
- Security compromise
- System outage
- Biased outputs
- Misuse of AI
- Failure of human oversight

## Incident Lifecycle

**DETECT** → **REPORT** → **CONTAIN** → **ASSESS** → **REMEDIATE** → **DOCUMENT** → **LEARN**

## Incident Record

A record may contain:

- Incident ID
- Date
- System
- Description
- Impact
- Data affected
- Immediate action
- Root cause
- Corrective action
- Responsible unit
- Closure date

## Severity

A conceptual model may classify incidents according to:

- Impact
- Likelihood
- Rights affected
- Number of people affected
- Institutional consequences

## Learning Loop

Incident information should feed back into:

- Training
- System design
- Risk assessment
- Policies
- Procurement
- Testing
