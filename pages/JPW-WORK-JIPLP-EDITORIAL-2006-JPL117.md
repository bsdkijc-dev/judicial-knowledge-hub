# JPW-WORK-JIPLP-EDITORIAL-2006-JPL117

**Editorial: Of form and format** is a legal editorial authored by [Jeremy Phillips](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Jeremy_Phillips) and published in the [Journal of Intellectual Property Law & Practice](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Journal_of_Intellectual_Property_Law_%26_Practice) (Vol. 1, No. 10) by [Oxford University Press](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) on August 2, 2006 [Editorial Publication](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Editorial_Publication) \[1–9\].

## Executive Summary

This editorial analyzes the legal and commercial frameworks governing the protection of television and radio programme formats internationally [TV Format Protection](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/TV_Format_Protection) \[1–9\]. The author highlights the absence of a standalone "format right" and outlines how format creators leverage a combination of existing intellectual property (IP) laws across multiple jurisdictions [IP Combination](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/IP_Combination) \[2–5\].

## Legal Framework & Jurisdictions

- **Ordinary Copyright Law (Netherlands & Brazil)**: Programme formats can be classified as copyrightable works under standard copyright legislation [Format Copyright](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Format_Copyright) \[2–5\].
- **Unfair Competition Law (Denmark)**: Protection relies on principles preventing unfair marketing practices [Unfair Competition](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Unfair_Competition) \[2–5\].
- **Patent Law (United States)**: Potential protection of format elements under patent law mechanisms [Format Patents](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Format_Patents) \[2–5\].
- **Trade Secrets & Trade Marks**: Brand titles are secured through trade mark registrations, while technical production manuals are protected via trade secrecy and copyright [Trade Secrets](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Trade_Secrets) \[2–5\].

## Background

During a conference of media lawyers and producers held in Moscow in June 2006, discussions centered on the legal ambivalence surrounding formats: formats are claimed as valuable property when licensed, but alleged to be unprotected when copied by competitors [Moscow Conference](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Moscow_Conference) \[1–9\].

## Core Objectives

1.  Clarify existing format protection mechanisms across different legal jurisdictions [IP Mechanisms](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/IP_Mechanisms) \[2–5\].
2.  Identify commercial strategies used by developers to protect high-value format assets [Commercial Strategies](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Commercial_Strategies) \[2–5\].
3.  Propose three global principles to serve as a baseline for an international WIPO standard [WIPO Standards](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/WIPO_Standards) \[1–9\].

## Key Provisions & Principles

- **Title Trade Marks**: Securing trade mark registrations for distinctive titles such as *Survivor*, *Big Brother*, and *Who Wants to Be a Millionaire?* [Title Trade Marks](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Title_Trade_Marks) \[2–5\].
- **Production Manuals**: Packaging technical pre-production and production know-how into confidential manuals accessible only to licensees [Production Manuals](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Production_Manuals) \[2–5\].
- **Three Global Principles for Format Adoption**:

1.  Wholesale unauthorized adoption of a format is prohibited (\*unauthorized wholesale adoption forbidden\*) [Wholesale Adoption Principle](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Wholesale_Adoption_Principle) \[1–9\].
2.  Unauthorized adoption is permitted if sufficient original elements are added so that audiences perceive it as a new derivative work [Derivative Work Principle](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Derivative_Work_Principle) \[1–9\].
3.  The adoption of bare ideas is not prohibited unless communicated within a confidential relationship [Bare Idea Principle](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Bare_Idea_Principle) \[1–9\].

## Policy & Unresolved Issues

- **Global Legal Uncertainty**: The absence of a unified international standard creates ambiguity for format developers and investors [Global Uncertainty](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Global_Uncertainty) \[1–9\].
- **Demand for Predictability**: The media industry requires predictable legal boundaries regarding acceptable and unacceptable format adoption [Legal Predictability](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Legal_Predictability) \[1–9\].
