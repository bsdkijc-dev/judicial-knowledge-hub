## Description / Deskripsi

This page records the empirical multi-jurisdictional statistical compilation and Problem Inventory List (Daftar Inventaris Masalah / DIM) prepared for the inaugural Kick-Off Meeting of the Business Competition Law Working Group (Kelompok Kerja Hukum Persaingan Usaha) of the Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia, held in Jakarta on 3–4 August 2026. The comprehensive dataset synthesizes official caseload figures and judicial resource allocations across three core Commercial Court seats (Central Jakarta, Makassar, and Semarang) for the 2021–2026 reporting period. The statistics reveal extreme geographic centralization: Central Jakarta accounts for \*\*98.7% of all incoming objection filings\*\* (77 out of 78 total cases) and \*\*96.7% of resolved decisions\*\* (29 out of 30 total cases). Across all three jurisdictions, \*\*63.3% of resolved objection cases resulted in judgments rejecting the objection\*\* (19 cases), while \*\*36.7% resulted in judgments granting or partially granting the objection\*\* (11 cases). All structural entities, numerical matrices, and issue categories have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher **Cecep Mustafa**.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-039](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-039): Extreme Geographic Asymmetry & Docket Concentration, where Central Jakarta handles over 98% of national competition objections, straining its 2 active presiding competition judges while regional courts in Semarang and Makassar maintain 14 to 6 certified judges with near-zero dockets.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-040](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-040): E-Court Technical Blockade for Multi-Petitioner Cases, where the compilation's DIM highlights that SIPP/e-Court architecture locks master case registration after the first petitioner registers, rejecting subsequent co-respondents in joint conduct cases and forcing separate, fragmented trials across different courts.

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-039](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-039): Competition objections are processed without formal court mediation (Article 12 PERMA No. 3/2021) relying on administrative dossier review, while SIPP/e-Court systems lack multi-petitioner consolidation features and suffer from norm conflicts between calendar days (PERMA 3/2021) and working days (PP 44/2021).

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-039](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-039): In Japan (Tokyo High Court / JFTC), centralized review at the Tokyo High Court is paired with a unified digital case management system that automatically consolidates multi-party appeals against Japan Fair Trade Commission orders, preventing fragmented trials.

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-039](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-039): The primary gap is the structural disconnect between PERMA No. 3/2021 procedural rules and SIPP/e-Court technical capabilities, alongside the rigid "evidence lock" (Article 13(5) PERMA No. 3/2021) that prevents judges from evaluating new economic counter-evidence.

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Deploy an automated e-Court feature allowing multiple co-respondents filing objections against the same KPPU decision to link their petitions to a single Master Case Number, and re-allocate certified competition judges from low-density courts (Semarang and Makassar) to participate in virtual e-Court panels supporting Central Jakarta.

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-040](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-040): Issue a Supreme Court Circular (SEMA) standardizing filing deadlines to "working days" and establishing guidelines for multi-petitioner e-Court registration.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-041](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-041): Promulgate PERMA Amandemen 3/2021 establishing limited evidence lock relaxation, specialized economic evidence procedures (\*Hot Tubbing\*), and explicit Judicial Review (\*Peninjauan Kembali\*) rules.

## Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-039](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-039): Working Group on Competition Law (Pokja MA RI) pilot coordination across Central Jakarta, Makassar, and Semarang Commercial Courts.

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-039](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-039): 100% elimination of parallel trials arising from multi-petitioner e-Court registration errors; reduction of average objection trial duration to \$\le 60\$ working days per statutory standard.

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Harmonizing PP No. 44/2021 and PERMA No. 3/2021 regarding working days, bank guarantee requirements, and execution of PNBP fines, alongside establishing formal inter-agency protocols between Mahkamah Agung, KPPU, and the Ministry of Finance (DJKN) for effective asset tracing and fine recovery.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Updating SIPP/e-Court system architecture to incorporate multi-petitioner master case linking and cross-jurisdictional virtual judicial panel assignments.

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Official Working Group Kick-Off Meeting compilation document (\*Kompilasi Data dari P Niaga dan Draft DIM.pdf\*) prepared for the Supreme Court of RI on 3–4 August 2026 in Jakarta.
