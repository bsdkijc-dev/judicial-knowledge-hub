## Policy Analysis

- **Background & Core Facts**:
  - Covers MOJ operations (internal units, councils, local branches, external bodies) from Jan 1 – Dec 31, 2022. Total budget post-revision: ¥839.51 billion; staff quota: 55,033.
  - **Civil & Family Law Reform (Act No. 102 of 2022)**: Removed child disciplinary rights (懲戒権) to prevent abuse and revised legitimate child presumption (嫡出推定) to resolve unregistered child cases.
  - **Civil Litigation Digitalization (Act No. 48 of 2022)**: Enacted online filing, e-dockets, and remote trial proceedings.
  - **Penal & Correctional Reform (Act No. 67 & 68 of 2022)**: Merged imprisonment types into a unified confinement sentence (拘禁刑) focusing on rehabilitation; increased penalties for insult crimes (侮辱罪).
  - **Multicultural Coexistence Roadmap**: Approved June 14, 2022, establishing 218 actionable items for foreign national integration through FY2026.
  - **Legal Aid & Spiritual Fraud Support**: Houterasu launched the "Spiritual Sales Response Dial" in Nov 2022 to assist victims of religious/spiritual exploitation.
  - **Specified Juveniles**: Fully implemented revised Juvenile Act (Apr 1, 2022) defining "Specified Juveniles" (特定少年) for ages 18–19.
- **Interpretations & Structural Inferences**:
  - Reflects a strategic pivot toward digital court infrastructure, human-rights-focused family law reforms, and structured multicultural social planning.
