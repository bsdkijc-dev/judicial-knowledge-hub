## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 02 K/MIL/2002 establishes an essential legal precedent within the military judiciary concerning proportionality and sentencing objectives. The Supreme Court affirmed that the sentence handed down by the Judex Facti (High Military Court), which included an additional penalty of dismissal from service, was excessively harsh and failed to properly accommodate the core objectives of sentencing. The Defendant's expression of remorse and 15 years of dedicated service in the Indonesian National Police (Polri) served as mitigating factors. Consequently, elements prioritizing public order, public safety, and rehabilitation must take precedence, resulting in the annulment of the additional dismissal penalty and the reinstatement of a standard imprisonment term.

## Case Background

The Defendant, \[Defendant\] (Brigadier Police Officer NRP 67040328, Member of Dalmas, Directorate of Sabhara, Bali Regional Police), was charged with assault violating Article 351 paragraph (1) of the KUHP. The incident occurred on June 26, 2000, in Denpasar, where the Defendant took offense at remarks made by Witness I (Mr. \[Witness\]) at a gambling site and struck Witness I in the face once, resulting in an inner lower lip laceration and chin abrasion as confirmed by the Visum et Repertum.

The Denpasar Military Court III-14 (Decision No. PUT/01-K/MM.III-14/POL/I/2001) sentenced the Defendant to 10 months of imprisonment. On appeal, the High Military Court III Surabaya (Decision No. PUT/11-K/MMT.III/BDG/POL/VII/2001) modified the verdict, sentencing the Defendant to 7 months of imprisonment alongside an additional penalty of dismissal from military/police service. The Defendant subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** The sentencing imposed by the Judex Facti (High Military Court) disregarded sentencing objectives by being overly punitive, failing to account for the Defendant's remorse as a mitigating circumstance. Elements encompassing public order, public safety, and rehabilitation must be prioritized as weighed by the Judex Facti, rendering the High Military Court's decision subject to annulment.
- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition of the Petitioner / Defendant \[Defendant\]; annulling the decision of the High Military Court III Surabaya dated July 2, 2001, No. PUT/11-K/MMT.III/BDG/POL/VII/2001; and adjudicating independently: Declaring the Defendant legally and convincingly proven guilty of the criminal act of "Assault," and sentencing the Defendant to 10 (ten) months of imprisonment.
