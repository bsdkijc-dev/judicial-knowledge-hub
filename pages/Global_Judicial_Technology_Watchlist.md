# Global Judicial Technology Watchlist

# Global Judicial Technology Watchlist

The Watchlist tracks emerging technologies relevant to courts.

## Technology Domains

- generative AI;
- knowledge graphs;
- semantic search;
- digital evidence;
- judicial analytics;
- translation;
- accessibility;
- cybersecurity;
- digital identity;
- interoperability.

## Assessment

Technology should be assessed according to judicial relevance, risk and maturity.
