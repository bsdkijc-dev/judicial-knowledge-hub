## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 277 K/Pdt/1984 dated June 15, 1985, establishes important civil jurisprudence regarding immovable property lease disputes (house/shop): In lease agreements, the provisions of Article 1579 of the Civil Code (BW) apply, whereby the lessor cannot terminate the lease by stating an intention to personally use the leased property, unless otherwise agreed upon beforehand. Furthermore, late payment or non-payment of rent by the lessee does not constitute grounds for the automatic termination or cancellation of the lease agreement by operation of law (\*demi hukum\*), but rather serves as grounds to demand fulfillment of rent payment obligations.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiffs / Respondents for Cassation (\[Party A\], Theodorus Tolen Kerans, Baltasar Leopold Kerans, Maria Dolorosa Kerans, and Hendrikus Kelelleng Kerans — the heirs of the late Sinyo Kerans) filed a lawsuit against the Original Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation (\[Party B\]) at the Larantuka District Court. The object of the dispute was a plot of house/shop (measuring 6 x 18 meters) situated on inherited yard land belonging to the late Sinyo Kerans in Postoh Village, Larantuka. The lease agreement had been ongoing since 1952 with the Plaintiffs' parents and continued from 1963 until May 1977 with the Plaintiffs. On January 1, 1977, the Plaintiffs requested the Defendant to vacate the house/shop because the Plaintiffs intended to use it themselves, but the Defendant ignored the request. Since June 1, 1977, the Defendant failed to pay rent, prompting the Plaintiffs to demand lease cancellation, eviction, and payment of rent arrears.

The Larantuka District Court (Decision No. 9/PN.Ltk/Pdt.G/1982/III dated February 14, 1983) granted the Plaintiffs' lawsuit in part: declaring the lease terminated by operation of law, ordering the Defendant to vacate the house/shop, and ordering rent payment. The Kupang High Court (Decision No. 60/PTK/1983/PDT dated October 12, 1983) amended the Larantuka District Court judgment: declaring the lease agreement to remain valid, rejecting the claims for lease termination and eviction because under Article 1579 of the BW the lessor cannot terminate the lease on the grounds of personal use. The Defendant (\[Party B\]) filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The \*Judex Facti\* of the Kupang High Court committed no error in applying the law and correctly considered that the lease relationship between the Plaintiffs and the Defendant was valid.
2.  Pursuant to Article 1579 of the Civil Code (BW), the lessor cannot terminate the lease by stating an intention to personally use the leased property unless such a right has been explicitly stipulated beforehand.
3.  The delay or failure of the lessee to pay rent constitutes grounds to demand the fulfillment of rent payment, but does not constitute grounds for the cancellation or termination of the lease agreement by operation of law.
4.  The Petitioner for Cassation's cassation objections regarding the evaluation of evidentiary facts cannot be considered at the cassation level (ex Law No. 1 of 1950 jo. Law No. 14 of 1970). Consequently, the Petitioner for Cassation's petition must be rejected.

- **Ruling:** Rejecting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation: \[Party B\]; Ordering the Petitioner for Cassation to pay cassation-level court costs amounting to Rp 20,000 (twenty thousand rupiahs). \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Wednesday, May 29, 1985, and pronounced in an open session on Saturday, June 15, 1985, by R. Harsadi Darsokusumo, S.H. \[Presiding Judge\], H. Amiroeddin Noer, S.H., and H. Masrani Basran, S.H. \[Associate Justices\]).\*
