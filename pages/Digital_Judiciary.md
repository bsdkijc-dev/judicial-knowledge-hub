# Digital Judiciary

# Digital Judiciary

**Digital judiciary** refers to the use of digital technologies to support judicial administration, proceedings, services, knowledge, communication, and institutional operations.

## Digital Transformation Layers

A useful analytical model is:

**DIGITIZE** → **DIGITALIZE** → **INTEGRATE** → **INTELLIGENT** → **ADAPTIVE**

These stages are conceptual rather than a universal maturity standard.

## Digital Components

Potential components include:

- Electronic filing
- Digital case management
- Electronic documents
- Online services
- Digital hearings
- Digital evidence
- Judicial databases
- Analytics
- Knowledge management
- AI systems

## Benefits

Potential benefits include:

- Accessibility
- Efficiency
- Reduced administrative burden
- Faster information exchange
- Better data availability

## Risks

Potential risks include:

- Digital exclusion
- Cybersecurity
- Privacy
- System outages
- Vendor dependency
- Data quality
- Automation bias

## Digital Inclusion

Digital transformation should consider people who:

- Lack connectivity
- Lack digital skills
- Have disabilities
- Require language assistance
- Need alternative access channels

## AI Readiness

Digitalization can create infrastructure for AI, but:

**DIGITAL DATA ≠ AI-READY DATA**

Data quality, metadata, governance, and interoperability remain necessary.
