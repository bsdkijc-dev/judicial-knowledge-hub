# Judicial AI Governance

# Judicial AI Governance

**Judicial AI Governance** is the institutional framework for managing the development, procurement, deployment, use, monitoring, and retirement of artificial intelligence systems within the judiciary.

## Objectives

The framework should seek to ensure:

- Legality
- Accountability
- Human oversight
- Reliability
- Security
- Privacy
- Fairness
- Transparency
- Judicial independence
- Public trust

## Governance Lifecycle

**IDENTIFY** → **ASSESS** → **APPROVE** → **PROCURE/DEVELOP** → **TEST** → **DEPLOY** → **MONITOR** → **AUDIT** → **RETIRE**

## Governance Actors

Potential actors include:

- Judicial leadership
- Judges
- Court administrators
- IT units
- Legal researchers
- Data protection/privacy officers
- Cybersecurity personnel
- Procurement officials
- External experts

Actual institutional responsibilities require jurisdiction-specific verification.

## Core Principle

**AI SUPPORTS JUDICIAL INSTITUTIONS; IT DOES NOT REPLACE** JUDICIAL ACCOUNTABILITY.
