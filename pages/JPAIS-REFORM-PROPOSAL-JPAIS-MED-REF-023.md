## Description / Deskripsi

Provides an exhaustive manual for prosecutors establishing a two-step analytical model for charging discretion, plea bargaining governance, corporate liability principles, constitutional indictment drafting standards, digital evidence management under ISO/IEC 27037, constitutional disclosure obligations (\*Brady\*, \*Giglio\*, Jencks Act), ethical witness preparation bounds, amended FRE 702 expert gatekeeping standards, and transnational evidence-gathering mechanisms via MLATs and Letters Rogatory.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-013](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-013): Indictment drafting defects such as duplicity and multiplicity compromise jury unanimity and violate Fifth Amendment Double Jeopardy protections.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-014](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-014): Failure to adhere to ISO/IEC 27037 methodologies during digital evidence handling exposes electronic records to suppression based on spoliation or lack of authentication.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-015](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-015): Historical judicial misapplications of expert testimony standards allowed expert overstatement and improper delegation of gatekeeping duties to juries.

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-023](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-023): Public prosecutors evaluate case files (berkas perkara) from investigators to ensure formal and material completeness before initiating judicial proceedings.

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-023](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-023): Advanced jurisdictions implement strict prosecutorial discretion guidelines, rigorous pre-trial discovery mandates, and active judicial gatekeeping for expert evidence.

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-006](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-006): Inadequate vetting of expert methodologies and unverified digital chain-of-custody protocols undermine conviction integrity in complex transnational trials.

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Implement a standardized prosecutorial execution framework spanning charging discretion, duplicity/multiplicity checks, continuous discovery compliance, active FRE 702 gatekeeping, and structured OIA/MLAT international cooperation.

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-013](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-013): Apply the two-step charging discretion model to verify probable cause and substantial federal interest before filing charges.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-014](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-014): Audit all digital evidence workflows against ISO/IEC 27037 identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation standards.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-015](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-015): Enforce amended FRE 702 preponderance standards, requiring proponents to establish expert reliability across all four statutory criteria.

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-023](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-023): 180-day prosecutor training initiative focusing on advanced digital evidence handling, FRE 702 methodological vetting, and complex indictment review workflows.

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-023](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-023): 100% compliance with \*Brady\*/\*Giglio\* continuous disclosure mandates; zero indictments dismissed for duplicity or multiplicity; 100% expert witness compliance with reliable application bounds under FRE 702(d).

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Institutionalization of rigorous internal review protocols (such as OCRS for RICO) and comprehensive departmental disclosure policies exceeding constitutional minimums.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Adoption of standardized statutory pleading rules and mandatory pre-trial evidentiary gatekeeping mechanisms for specialized scientific and technical testimony.

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Plea bargaining resolves 90 to 95 percent of federal criminal cases, requiring strict supervisory approval frameworks for specialized plea structures like \*Alford\* or \*nolo contendere\* pleas\[cite: 4, 5\].
- Amended FRE 702 successfully eliminates expert overstatement by enforcing an active judicial gatekeeping role under a preponderance of the evidence standard.
