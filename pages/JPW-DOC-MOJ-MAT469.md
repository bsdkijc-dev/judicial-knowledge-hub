## Policy Analysis

- **Background & Core Facts**:
  - Published in December 2023 by MOJ Judicial System Dept, offering an authoritative Japanese translation of the German FamFG as amended through January 1, 2023.
  - **Origins of German FamFG**: Promulgated in 2008 and effective September 1, 2009, unifying the 1898 Voluntary Jurisdiction Act (FGG) and Book 6 of the Civil Procedure Code (ZPO) into a consolidated procedural statute.
  - **Structural Layout (9 Books / 9編)**:
    - **Book 1 (General Provisions)**: §§ 1–85 (General procedural framework for court jurisdiction).
    - **Book 2 (Family Matters)**: §§ 111–270 (Marriage, divorce, parent-child relations, adoption, domestic violence, maintenance, and marital property).
    - **Book 3 (Custodianship & Placement Matters)**: §§ 271–341 (Managed by the Custodianship Court / *Betreuungsgericht*).
    - **Book 4 (Inheritance & Division Matters)**: §§ 342–373.
    - **Books 5–6**: Registry, Corporate, and Other Voluntary Jurisdiction Matters.
    - **Book 7 (Deprivation of Liberty Matters)**: §§ 415–432.
    - **Books 8–9**: Public Citation Procedures and Concluding Provisions.
  - **Key Statutory Principles**:
    - **Large Family Court System (大家庭裁判所)**: Consolidates all family and domestic matters under unified Family Court jurisdiction.
    - **Ex-Officio Investigation Principle (§ 26)**: Mandates active judicial investigation of facts alongside statutory duties of disclosure (§ 28).
    - **Formal vs. Informal Evidence (§ 29, § 30)**: Requires strict ZPO-standard formal evidence in parent-child relations (§ 177(2)), custodianship (§ 280(1)), and placement proceedings (§ 321(1)).
    - **Child Procedural Assistant (§ 158)**: Mandates appointment of a legal procedural assistant (*Verfahrensbeistand*) to safeguard child participation rights.
    - **Accelerated Access & Custody Proceedings (§ 155)**: Adapts the Cochem Model, requiring initial hearings within 1 month of filing.
    - **Independent Temporary Orders (§ 49)**: Authorizes interim injunctive relief independent of main proceedings.
- **Interpretations & Structural Inferences**:
  - Demonstrates Japan MOJ's intensive study of foreign comparative procedural models to inform future domestic reforms in family and voluntary jurisdiction proceedings.
