# AI Procurement

**AI procurement** concerns the acquisition of AI systems, services, models, datasets, infrastructure, or related technology by judicial institutions.

## Procurement Lifecycle

**NEED** → **MARKET ASSESSMENT** → **REQUIREMENTS** → **RISK ASSESSMENT** → **TENDER/SELECTION** → **CONTRACT** → **TESTING** → **DEPLOYMENT** → **MONITORING**

## AI-Specific Requirements

Procurement documents may consider:

- Data ownership
- Data location
- Confidentiality
- Security
- Model performance
- Audit rights
- Logging
- Incident notification
- Vendor support
- Exit strategy

Actual contractual requirements require legal review.

## Vendor Dependency

Institutions should consider risks arising from:

- Vendor lock-in
- Proprietary formats
- Closed systems
- Migration costs
- Provider discontinuation

## Accountability

Procurement should not transfer institutional accountability to the vendor.
