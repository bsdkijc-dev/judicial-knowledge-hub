# Judicial Lessons Learned

**Judicial Lessons Learned** records knowledge derived from experience, implementation, evaluation, successes, failures, and unexpected outcomes.

## Lesson Structure

A lesson may contain:

- Context
- Objective
- What happened
- Evidence
- What worked
- What did not work
- Why
- Recommendation
- Applicability
- Verification

## Example Structure

**CONTEXT**

A digital transformation project.

**OBSERVATION**

An implementation challenge occurred.

**CAUSE**

The documented evidence identifies contributing factors.

**LESSON**

A specific organizational or technical insight.

**ACTION**

A future project should consider the identified lesson.

## Important Distinction

A lesson learned is not automatically a universal best practice.

It should be evaluated before being generalized.

## AI Role

AI can assist with discovering recurring patterns across lesson records.
