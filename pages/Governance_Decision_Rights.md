# Governance Decision Rights

# Governance Decision Rights

The governance architecture should explicitly assign decision rights.

## Questions

- Who approves a use case?
- Who owns the data?
- Who accepts risk?
- Who can suspend a system?
- Who reports incidents?
- Who evaluates outcomes?
- Who approves scaling?

## Principle

**Unassigned responsibility is institutional risk.**
