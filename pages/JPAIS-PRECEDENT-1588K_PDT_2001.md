# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:1588K PDT 2001

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 1588 K/Pdt/2001** (dated June 30, 2004; deliberated May 18, 2004) is a fundamental Indonesian land and civil law jurisprudence precedent establishing vital legal rules regarding the nullity by operation of law of land rights certificates issued prior to the date of the underlying deed of sale and purchase (*antedated certificates*) as well as the nullity of certificates issued without an application from the lawful owner.

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established two primary legal principles:

1.  **Nullity of Certificates Issued Prior to the Deed of Sale and Purchase (AJB):** A land certificate issued prior to the deed of sale and purchase serving as its foundational legal title is contrary to the law and declared null and void. The issuance of SHM No. 93 on December 23, 1980, based on Deed of Sale and Purchase No. 479/JS/VII/1981 dated July 7, 1981, contradicts actual legal reality and bears a fatal legal defect.
2.  **Nullity of Land Certificate Issuance Without the Lawful Owner's Application:** The issuance of a land rights certificate without an official application filed by the respective lawful owner is illegal and possesses no binding legal force.

The Supreme Court granted the Plaintiff's (\[Party A\]) cassation petition, annulled the Bandung High Court Decision No. 214/Pdt/1999/PT.Bdg *jo.* Bale Bandung District Court Decision No. 47/Pdt.G/1998/PN.BB, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part, declaring the Defendants' actions unlawful, declaring that Hak Milik Certificate (SHM) No. 91 in the name of \[Party B\] — subsequently subdivided into SHM No. 92 (in the name of Ny. Siti Aminah) and SHM No. 93 (in the name of Sutisna and later transferred to Teddy Suhardjo) — contains legal defects and possesses no legal force, and declaring the disputed land to be the lawful property of the Plaintiff (\[Party A\]).

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Absolute Legal Defect in Land Certificate Issuance Due to Chronological Anomalies (Antedated Certificate)

*Is a land rights certificate (SHM No. 93 issued on December 23, 1980) valid under the law when trial evidence proves that the deed of sale and purchase (AJB No. 479/JS/VII/1981) serving as its foundational title was executed months/years later (on July 7, 1981)?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Unlawful and Void by Operation of Law.**

The Supreme Court established that land certificates issued prior to the date of execution of the Deed of Sale and Purchase (AJB) represent a chronological anomaly contradicting the logic of land registration and the principle of publicity. Certificates issued without a valid foundational title at the time of issuance contain an absolute legal defect and must be declared null and void (*nemo plus iuris transferre potest quam ipse habet*).

### 2. Nullity of Land Rights Certificate Issuance Without the Lawful Owner's Application

*Does the Land Office (BPN) possess the authority to issue land certificates in the name of third parties without an official application filed by the lawful holder of the foundational title/land?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Void and Legally Defective.**

The issuance of land rights certificates (SHM Nos. 91, 92, 93) executed without an application filed by the lawful owner (\[Party B\]) stems from administrative manipulation/procedural defects, rendering all derivative certificates born therefrom devoid of binding legal force.

### 3. Error in Judicando by Bandung High Court in Assessing Ownership Disputes

The Bandung High Court misapplied the law by intertwining the disputed land ownership dispute with the ownership matters of a weaving enterprise ("Munding Laya" / "Pergaruda"), whereas the core dispute focused purely on the validity of certification and land ownership in Blok Toblong, Majalaya.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* (Bandung High Court Decision No. 214/Pdt/1999/PT.Bdg) err in applying the law (*foutieve toepassing van het recht*) by setting aside the Bale Bandung District Court judgment and validating SHM No. 93, despite SHM No. 93 being proven to have been issued on December 23, 1980, preceding AJB No. 479/JS/VII/1981 dated July 7, 1981, and being issued without the lawful owner's application?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. The Bandung High Court erred in applying the law. The Supreme Court granted Plaintiff \[Party A\]'s cassation petition, annulled Bandung High Court Decision No. 214/Pdt/1999/PT.Bdg, and independently adjudicated by partially granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit, declaring SHM Nos. 91, 92, and 93 legally defective and without binding legal force, and declaring the disputed land to be the lawful property of the Plaintiff (\[Party A\]).

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Bale Bandung District Court & Bandung High Court)

Examination of facts regarding the 1960 land exchange between \[Party A\] and \[Party B\] (Letter C No. 1777 Parcel 103 S IV measuring 468 Tumbak exchanged for Blok Toblong Kohir 2315 Parcel 209 DI measuring 80 tumbak & 70 tumbak); facts of land possession and textile factory leasing by the Plaintiff since 1976; issuance of SHM No. 91 covering 1,995 m² in \[Party B\]'s name without \[Party B\]'s application; subdivision of SHM No. 91 into SHM No. 92 covering 1,120 m² in the name of Ny. St. Aminah and SHM No. 93 covering 875 m² in the name of Sutisna (Defendant I) then transferred to Teddy Suhardjo (Defendant II); mortgaging of SHM No. 92 at Bank Exim by Eddy Tuhfah; and examination of written exhibits and witnesses (including witness \[Party B\] confirming he never sold the land nor applied for certification).

## Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

### Statutory Regulations

- Law No. 5 of 1960 on Basic Agrarian Regulations (UUPA).
- Government Regulation No. 10 of 1961 *jo.* Government Regulation No. 24 of 1997 on Land Registration: Material prerequisites for valid land rights certificate issuance (existence of valid foundational title/AJB and applications from right-holders).
- Civil Code (KUHPerdata): Articles 1320 & 1365 (Requirements for Valid Agreements & Tort / *Perbuatan Melawan Hukum*).
- Law No. 14 of 1985 *jo.* Law No. 4 of 2004 *jo.* Law No. 5 of 2004 on the Supreme Court: Cassation authority & independent adjudication powers.

### Doctrines & Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- Principle of *Nemo Plus Iuris Transferre Potest Quam Ipse Habet*: No person can transfer rights exceeding what they possess. A defect in the foundational root title vitiates the validity of all subsequent certificate issuance chains.
- Land Registration Legal Certainty: Certificates stemming from manipulative procedures / without owner applications enjoy no legal protection.

## What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

### Established by Evidence

- The Plaintiff (\[Party A\]) is the lawful owner of the disputed land in Blok Toblong, Parcel No. 209 D.I Kohir No. 2315 (currently Pelangi St., Rt.02/Rw.03, Majalaya Sub-District, Bandung Regency) based on a 1960 land exchange with \[Party B\].
- SHM No. 93 was proven to have been issued earlier (December 23, 1980) than Deed of Sale and Purchase No. 479/JS/VII/1981 (July 7, 1981), and is thus declared void and legally defective.
- SHM Nos. 91, 92, and 93 were issued without any application from the lawful owner (\[Party B\]), rendering them legally defective and devoid of binding legal force.
- The Defendants were proven to have committed Tort (*Perbuatan Melawan Hukum*).
- The occupation of approximately 875 m² of land utilizing SHM No. 93 by Defendants I and II constitutes an unlawful act.
- The protective attachment (*sita jaminan*) dated November 14, 1998, is declared valid and lawful.

### Rejected / Dismissed Claims

- The Plaintiff's Provisionary claim was rejected by the Supreme Court.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Procedural Oversight of Land Certification by BPN:** This landmark ruling provides an exceptionally robust legal precedent in Indonesia: the Land Office (BPN) is strictly prohibited from issuing land certificates without direct applications from lawful foundational titleholders or relying on antedated deeds of sale and purchase. Certificates born from reversed chronology and procedural defects are absolutely declared null and void by the courts.

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Is a land certificate (SHM) valid if its issuance date precedes the date of the Deed of Sale and Purchase (AJB) serving as its foundational title, or if issued without the owner's application?  
**Such certificates are invalid and void by operation of law.** Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence No. 1588 K/Pdt/2001:

- Land certificates issued prior to the underlying deed of sale and purchase contradict the law and are declared null and void.
- The issuance of land rights certificates without official applications filed by lawful owners constitutes legally defective issuance possessing no binding legal force.
