# AI Transparency Standard

Judicial AI use should be sufficiently transparent to permit meaningful institutional accountability.

## Transparency Areas

- system purpose;
- responsible institution;
- risk classification;
- human oversight;
- approved use;
- limitations;
- evaluation;
- incident history where appropriate.

## Principle

Transparency should support accountability without exposing protected information or creating security vulnerabilities.
