## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 13 K/MIL/2001 establishes an important legal principle regarding the mandatory imposition of criminal fines in psychotropic offenses. The Supreme Court affirmed that sentencing for violations of Article 62 of Law No. 5 of 1997 on Psychotropics is cumulative in nature (consisting of both imprisonment and fines). Consequently, the ruling of the Medan High Military Court I omitting the criminal fine must be annulled as it contradicts substantive criminal law.

## Case Background

The Defendant, \[Defendant\] (Sergeant Major / Serka Nrp. 636400, Ba Jasrem-023/KS Korem-023/KS), was charged with the criminal offense of "Unlawfully possessing, storing, and/or carrying Psychotropics" violating Article 62 of Law No. 5 of 1997. On July 16–17, 1999, at the Puri Desa Kalangan Motel, Central Tapanuli Regency, the Defendant entrusted 30 ecstasy pills to Witness I (Sertu \[Witness\]) to be sold for Rp 35,000 per pill. During a Military Police raid on July 17, 1999, at 03:00 AM, ecstasy pills belonging to the Defendant and Witness I were discovered.

The Medan Military Court I-02 (Decision No. PUT/044-K/MM I-02/AD/III/2000) sentenced the Defendant to 1 year of imprisonment, dismissal from the military service of the Indonesian Army (TNI-AD), and a fine of Rp 100,000. On appeal, the Medan High Military Court I (Decision No. PUT/B-31/K/AD/MMT-I/IX/2000) modified the ruling by increasing the imprisonment term to 1 year and 6 months and upholding the dismissal, but omitted the criminal fine. The Defendant subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** Because the Medan High Military Court I failed to impose a criminal fine against the Defendant, whereas sentencing in psychotropic cases (Article 62 of Law No. 5 of 1997) is cumulative, the decision of the Medan High Military Court I must be annulled.
- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from the Petitioner for Cassation / Defendant \[Defendant\], Serka Nrp. 636400; Annulling the Medan High Military Court I decision dated September 29, 2000, No. PUT/B-31/K/AD/MMT-I/IX/2000; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] legally and convincingly proven guilty of the criminal offense of "Unlawfully possessing Psychotropics"; (2) Sentencing the Defendant to Principal Penalty: Imprisonment for 1 (one) year; Additional Penalty: Dismissal from Military Service Cq. TNI-AD; Fine: Rp 100,000 (one hundred thousand rupiahs) with a subsidiary confinement of 3 (three) months; (3) Charging cassation-level court costs amounting to Rp 2,500.
