## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 15 K/AG/1980 dated November 25, 1981, establishes two essential legal principles: 1. **Continuous Quarreling as Grounds for Fasakh:** Proven facts sufficiently demonstrating continuous quarreling that can no longer be reconciled require the Plaintiff's lawsuit to have her marriage to the Defendant annulled (\*fasakh\*) to be granted. 2. **Cassation Procedure in Religious Courts:** Pursuant to Article 40 of Law No. 14 of 1970 jo. Law No. 1 of 1950, final judgments rendered by the Islamic High Court / Provincial Mahkamah Syar'iyah may be brought via direct cassation before the Supreme Court.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff (\[Party A\] / Siti Hawa) filed a lawsuit for marriage annulment (\*fasakh\*) against her husband, the Original Defendant (A. Rahman), at the Kualasimpang Religious Court / Mahkamah Syar'iyah. The couple married in 1960 and had six children. However, starting in 1976, frequent quarrels broke out, with the Defendant failing to provide financial support, accusing the Plaintiff of theft/robbery, and committing physical abuse (beating) on February 6, 1979. The Kualasimpang Religious Court / Mahkamah Syar'iyah (Decision No. 48/1979) granted the lawsuit and annulled the marriage. However, the Provincial Religious Court / Mahkamah Syar'iyah of Aceh in Banda Aceh (Decision No. 77/1979) overturned the first-instance ruling and declared the marriage to remain valid. The Plaintiff subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** Proven facts sufficiently demonstrating "continuous quarreling that can no longer be reconciled" warrant the granting of the plaintiff's lawsuit to annul (\*fasakh\*) her marriage to the defendant. The Provincial Mahkamah Syar'iyah of Aceh in Banda Aceh erred in concluding that insufficient grounds existed for a divorce.
- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition filed by the Plaintiff for Cassation (\[Party A\]); Annulling the decision of the Provincial Mahkamah Syar'iyah of Aceh in Banda Aceh dated November 17, 1979, No. 77/1979; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Accepting the Plaintiff's lawsuit; (2) Annulling (\*fasakh\*) the marriage between Defendant (A. Rahman) and Plaintiff (\[Party A\]); (3) Ordering the Defendant in cassation / original defendant to pay all court costs across all levels of jurisdiction.
