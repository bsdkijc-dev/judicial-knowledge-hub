# Ontology Category System

# Judicial Knowledge Ontology

The Wiki should use a controlled category hierarchy.

## Level 1: Entity

- Courts
- Cases
- Judgments
- Laws
- Regulations
- Institutions
- People/Roles

## Level 2: Knowledge

- Legal Rules
- Legal Issues
- Doctrines
- Principles
- Procedures
- Evidence

## Level 3: Strategy

- Policies
- Strategic Objectives
- Initiatives
- KPIs
- Programs

## Level 4: Capability

- AI
- Data
- Workforce
- Cybersecurity
- Infrastructure
- Innovation

## Level 5: Evidence

- Sources
- Claims
- Research
- Benchmarks
- Evaluations
