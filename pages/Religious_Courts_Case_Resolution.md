# Religious Courts Case Resolution

# Religious Courts Case Resolution

The Religious Courts program outcome concerns certain and equitable resolution of cases.

## Indicators

- timely resolution of jinayat cases;
- resolution of overseas Indonesian-citizen marriage-isbat cases;
- resolution of integrated marriage-isbat cases;
- timely civil-case resolution at appellate level;
- timely civil-case resolution at first instance;
- timely transmission of judgment copies;
- timely delivery of judgment copies to parties;
- timely notification of appellate, cassation and judicial-review

` judgment contents;`

- timely delivery of jinayat judgment copies;
- judgment publication;
- civil execution;
- mediation;
- e-Court;
- e-Berpadu.
