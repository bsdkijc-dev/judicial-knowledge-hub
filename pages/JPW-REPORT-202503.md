## 4. Policy Analysis

Background  
Criminal cases involving persons with mental disabilities (encompassing intellectual disabilities, autism spectrum disorders, and mental illnesses such as schizophrenia and bipolar disorder) present acute procedural challenges. In South Korea, crimes committed by persons with mental disabilities accounted for 1.1% of total crimes in 2023 (13,915 individuals out of 1,250,885). However, individuals with mental disabilities face double vulnerability: high public fear/stigma following unprovoked acts of violence, combined with systemic procedural exclusion due to an inability to comprehend rights warnings, interrogation questions, or court proceedings.

<!-- -->

Policy Problem  
**Procedural Invisible Barriers & Ineffective Defense Rights**: Unlike physical disabilities, mental disabilities are often hidden or unobserved by police, prosecutors, and judges. Investigative agencies frequently process cases formally relying on family members without understanding the suspect's cognitive limits, leading to coerced or unreliable statements.

**High Detention Rates**: Arrest/detention rates for suspects with mental disabilities (4.5% in 2023) are nearly double the rate for general suspects (2.3%) due to lack of stable community support or family supervision during investigation.

**Defunct Trial Suspension Rules (CPC Art. 306)**: Article 306(1) of the Criminal Procedure Code mandates trial suspension when a defendant lacks litigation capacity (사물의 변별 또는 의사의 결정을 할 능력이 없는 상태). However, because Korean law lacks statutory mechanisms for competency restoration treatment (unlike US *Dusky*/*Sell* procedures), Article 306 is virtually dead-letter in judicial practice; judges rarely suspend trials to avoid indefinite case stalling.

**Monopolistic Custody Petitions**: Under the Act on Medical Treatment and Custody, the authority to petition for medical treatment and custody (치료감호) rests exclusively with prosecutors (Art. 4(1)). When courts determine that an acquitted or mentally incapacitated defendant urgently requires institutional medical custody, they cannot issue custody orders if the prosecutor failed to petition.

**Statement Intermediary Restriction**: The current Statement Intermediary (진술조력인) system under the Sexual Violence Punishment Act and Disability Welfare Act (Art. 59-16) is legally restricted to crime victims. Suspects and defendants with communication difficulties receive no official statement intermediary support.

<!-- -->

Legal/Policy Gap  
Korean criminal procedure lacks a unified, cross-stage framework designed specifically to identify mental disabilities at arrest, convey disability data to courts at indictment, provide neutral communication intermediaries during hearings, and deliver state-funded medical treatment orders.

<!-- -->

Objectives  
Ensure substantive constitutional equality and procedural due process under UN CRPD Articles 12 and 13; establish early disability identification protocols; expand statement intermediaries to suspects and defendants; revitalize litigation capacity assessments; and reform medical custody and treatment orders.

<!-- -->

Implementation & Proposed Statutory Reforms  
**Harmonized Terminology (Criminal Act Art. 10 Amendment)**: Amend Criminal Act Article 10 to replace the outdated term "심신장애인" with "정신적 장애인," aligning criminal law with the Disability Welfare Act and Developmental Disabilities Act.

**Simplified Pre-Indictment Psychiatric Evaluation (기소전 간이감정)**: Utilize Professional Investigative Advisers (전문수사자문위원 under CPC Art. 245-2) or Sexual Violence Act expert opinion queries to conduct brief 2-hour pre-indictment evaluations (modeled after Japanese prosecution practice) to verify communication barriers and necessity for procedural support.

**Mandatory Interrogation Recording & Indictment Attachment**: Mandate audio-visual recording (영상녹화) and stenographic transcripts (속기록) for interrogating suspects with mental disabilities. Require prosecutors to attach a "Suspect Disability Verification Report" (피의자 장애확인 보고서) to the indictment, informing courts of the defendant's disability from the very first hearing without violating the single-document indictment rule (공소장일본주의).

**Universal Statement Intermediary System (확대된 진술조력인 제도)**: Amend the Criminal Procedure Code to enact proposed Article 244-6 (Intermediary in Investigation) and Article 276-3 (Intermediary in Trial), extending statement intermediaries to all suspects, defendants, and witnesses with communication difficulties. Promulgate matching Supreme Court Rules (진술조력인의 재판절차 참여에 관한 규칙(안)).

**Clarification of Procedural Support Roles**: Establish clear operational boundaries between Assistants (보조인 — family/guardians exercising legal rights), Persons with Reliable Relationship (신뢰관계인 — emotional support), Communication Assistants (의사소통 보조인력 — ad-hoc sign/device support), and Statement Intermediaries (진술조력인 — certified neutral communication translators).

**Specialized Disability Trial Divisions (장애인 전문재판부)**: Institutionalize and expand specialized criminal trial divisions for defendants with disabilities (pioneered at Seoul Central District Court in Feb 2024), applying a case weighting factor (가중치) of 1.5 to 2.0x to reflect extended hearing times.

**Medical Treatment and Custody Act Amendments**:

- Amend Article 4(7) to mandate that prosecutors must comply with a court's request to file a medical treatment and custody petition (법원의 치료감호 청구 요구에 응하여야 한다).
- Amend Article 2-3 to allow Medical Treatment Orders (치료명령) for defendants acquitted due to mental incapacity (심신상실 무죄) or sentenced to fines (벌금형).
- Transition medical treatment order costs to full state funding.

<!-- -->

Evidence & Empirical Data  
**National Disability Crime Statistics (2023)**: Total registered persons with disabilities in Korea stood at 2,641,896 (including 104,197 with mental illness, 239,833 with intellectual disability, and 42,744 with autism).

**Prosecutorial Disposition Data (2023)**: Out of 13,445 suspect dispositions involving mental disabilities, 54.8% were indicted (4.1% bound trial, 18.0% unbound trial, 32.7% summary order), 36.7% unindicted (32.6% prosecution suspension / 기소유예), and 2.2% transferred to juvenile/family protection.

**2023 Full Audit of Custody & Treatment Rulings**: Analysis of 168 first-instance Medical Treatment and Custody cases in 2023 showed 133 granted (79.2%) and 35 rejected (20.8%). Out of 35 rejections, 19 defendants were instead sentenced to probation with Medical Treatment Orders (치료명령), demonstrating courts' active preference for community-based medical orders over full institutional custody when appropriate.

## 6. MediaWiki Output

**Study on Improvement in Criminal Procedure for Persons with Mental Disabilities** is an official judicial policy research report published by the Judicial Policy Research Institute (JPRI) of South Korea's Supreme Court.

## Summary

This document is an official judicial policy research report published by the Judicial Policy Research Institute (JPRI) of South Korea's Supreme Court. It comprehensively, empirically, and comparatively analyzes every stage of criminal procedure (형사소송절차) for suspects and defendants (피의자·피고인) with mental disabilities (정신적 장애, including intellectual disability, autism, and mental illness). It diagnoses communication barriers, high detention rates, and the non-functioning of litigation capacity assessments (소송능력); analyzes the law of 5 countries (US, UK, Germany, France, Japan) and UN CRPD standards; and formulates reforms including simplified pre-indictment psychiatric evaluation (기소전 간이감정), mandatory audio-visual recording (영상녹화), a Suspect Disability Verification Report (피의자 장애확인 보고서) attached to indictments, expansion of statement intermediaries (진술조력인), specialized disability trial divisions (장애인 전문재판부), and amendments to the Medical Treatment and Custody Act (치료감호법).

## Legal Basis

The legal frameworks analyzed include:

- **Constitution of the Republic of Korea**: Article 11(1) (Equality Principle), Article 12 (Bodily Freedom & Due Process), and Article 27(1) (Right to a Fair Trial).
- **Criminal Act of the Republic of Korea (형법)**: Article 10 (심신장애 / Mental and Physical Disorder — proposed amendment of the term to 정신적 장애인).
- **Criminal Procedure Code of the Republic of Korea (형사소송법)**: Article 29 (보조인 / Assistant), Article 33 (국선변호인 / Public Defender), Article 244-5 (Special Interrogation Protection), Article 245-2 (Professional Investigative Adviser), Article 276-2 (Special Trial Examination Protection), Article 279-2 (Expert Examiner), Article 306 (공판절차 정지 / Trial Suspension), and proposed Articles 244-6 & 276-3.
- **Act on Welfare of Persons with Disabilities (장애인복지법)**: Article 2, Article 32 (Disability Registration), and Article 59-16.
- **Act on Guarantee of Rights of Persons with Developmental Disabilities (발달장애인법)**: Article 2, Article 8, Article 12 (Procedural Safeguards), and Article 13 (Dedicated Investigators).
- **Disability Anti-Discrimination Act (장애인차별금지법)**: Article 4 and Article 26 (Reasonable Accommodation in Judicial Proceedings).
- **Act on Medical Treatment and Custody (치료감호 등에 관한 법률 / 치료감호법)**: Article 2, Article 2-3, Article 4, Article 7, Article 12, Article 44-3, Article 44-4, Article 44-9.
- **International Conventions and Standards**: UN Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD Articles 5, 12, 13, 14, 21; General Comments No. 1 & No. 6; Concluding Observations for Korea 2014 & 2022); the UN International Principles and Guidelines on Access to Justice for Persons with Disabilities (2020).

## Background

2023 statistics recorded 13,915 crimes committed by persons with mental disabilities (1.1% of all crime in South Korea). Although this proportion is small, persons with mental disabilities face a detention rate twice as high (4.5% vs. 2.3%), communication isolation during interrogation, and the absence of legal statement intermediaries. Meanwhile, Article 306 of the Criminal Procedure Code, concerning trial suspension (공판절차 정지) due to lack of litigation capacity, has become dead-letter law due to the absence of a capacity restoration program, while judges cannot order medical treatment and custody (치료감호) without a monopolized petition from the prosecutor.

## Objectives

1.  Guarantee due process (적법절차) and substantive equality for suspects/defendants with mental disabilities in accordance with UN CRPD Articles 12 & 13.
2.  Enact a simplified pre-indictment evaluation system (기소전 간이감정) and attach a Suspect Disability Verification Report to indictments.
3.  Expand the Statement Intermediary (진술조력인) system to suspects, defendants, and witnesses under an amended Criminal Procedure Code.
4.  Amend the Medical Treatment and Custody Act to require prosecutors to comply with court requests for treatment orders.

## Scope

- **Empirical & Statistical Study**: Registration data on 2.64 million persons with disabilities, analysis of 13,445 prosecutorial dispositions in 2023, and a full audit of 168 Medical Treatment and Custody rulings in 2023 (133 granted, 35 rejected).
- **Five-Country Comparative Study**:
  - **United States**: Competency to Stand Trial (CTS / *Dusky* & *Sell* Criteria), the Insanity Defense, and Mental Health Courts.
  - **United Kingdom**: Appropriate Adult (AA / PACE Code C) and Registered Intermediary (*WIS*).
  - **Germany**: *Verhandlungsfähigkeit* (StPO) and *Therapieweisung* (StGB § 68b).
  - **France**: Exemption from criminal responsibility (Code pénal Art. 122-1) and the judicial representative (*MJPM*).
  - **Japan**: Audio-visual interrogation recording and the 2-hour pre-indictment psychiatric evaluation (기소전 간이감정).

## Key Provisions

- **Simplified Pre-Indictment Evaluation (기소전 간이감정)**: Uses Professional Investigative Advisers (전문수사자문위원, CPC Article 245-2) to evaluate a suspect's communication barriers before indictment.
- **Disability Verification Report (피의자 장애확인 보고서)**: Attached to the indictment (공소장) to inform the trial panel of the defendant's disability from the first day of trial.
- **Statement Intermediary Rules (Proposed CPC Articles 244-6 & 276-3)**: Establishes the right to use a statement intermediary (진술조력인) for suspects and defendants with communication difficulties.
- **Separation of Support Team Roles**: Clarifies the distinction between Assistants (보조인 / legal assistants), Persons with Reliable Relationship (신뢰관계인 / emotional companions), Communication Assistants (의사소통 보조인력 / ad-hoc communication aides), and Statement Intermediaries (진술조력인 / defined neutral communication interpreters).

## Implementation

Courts route disability cases to the Specialized Disability Trial Division (장애인 전문재판부), hold pre-trial preparation hearings (공판준비절차) to determine communication needs, and assign dedicated sentencing investigation officers (양형조사관).

## Policy Issues

- Absence of a litigation capacity restoration program under Article 306 of the Criminal Procedure Code.
- The prosecutor's monopoly on the authority to petition for medical treatment and custody (치료감호), which limits judicial discretion.

## Reform / Amendments

1.  Amendment of Article 10 of the Criminal Act (changing '심신장애인' to '정신적 장애인').
2.  Enactment of Articles 244-6 & 276-3 of the Criminal Procedure Code (statement intermediaries for suspects/defendants).
3.  Enactment of Supreme Court Rules on statement intermediary participation in trial proceedings.
4.  Amendment of Article 4(7) of the Medical Treatment and Custody Act (requiring prosecutors to comply with court treatment order requests).
5.  Amendment of Article 2-3 of the Medical Treatment and Custody Act (enabling treatment orders for defendants acquitted due to mental incapacity or sentenced to fines).
