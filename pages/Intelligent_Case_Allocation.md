# Intelligent Case Allocation

# Intelligent Case Allocation

Intelligent Case Allocation is proposed as a data-supported approach to administrative workload allocation.

## Potential Inputs

- workload;
- case characteristics;
- court capacity;
- administrative constraints.

## Governance Boundary

Allocation mechanisms must not compromise judicial independence or predetermine judicial outcomes.
