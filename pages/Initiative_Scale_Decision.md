# Initiative Scale Decision

# Initiative Scale Decision

Scaling should depend on evidence rather than pilot completion.

## Scale Gate

- outcome achieved;
- risk acceptable;
- legal basis confirmed;
- cost sustainable;
- workforce ready;
- technical architecture scalable;
- cybersecurity acceptable;
- users demonstrate value.

## Decisions

**Scale / Redesign / Extend Pilot / Stop**
