# BSDK as Judicial Futures Engines

# BSDK as Judicial Futures Engine

BSDK can function as a capability and knowledge engine for the future judiciary.

## Potential Roles

- futures research;
- judicial AI academy;
- policy laboratory;
- knowledge curation;
- innovation observation;
- strategic capability measurement.

## Principle

BSDK should create standards, curricula, research and learning systems without unnecessarily centralising every technical activity.
