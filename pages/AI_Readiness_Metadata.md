# AI Readiness Metadata

AI Readiness indicates whether a knowledge object is suitable for machine-assisted retrieval, analysis or generation.

## Proposed Scale

| Level | Meaning                                 |
|-------|-----------------------------------------|
| 1     | Unstructured / unsuitable               |
| 2     | Basic digitisation                      |
| 3     | Structured                              |
| 4     | Verified and machine-readable           |
| 5     | AI-ready with provenance and governance |

## Level 5 Requirements

- structured metadata;
- reliable source;
- provenance;
- verification status;
- temporal validity;
- relationships;
- access classification.
