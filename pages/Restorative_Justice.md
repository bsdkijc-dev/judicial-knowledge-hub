# Restorative Justice

# Restorative Justice Case Resolution

Indicator 1.8 measures the percentage of cases successfully resolved through a restorative justice approach.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Cases successfully resolved through restorative justice}} {\text{Cases meeting restorative justice eligibility criteria}} \times100\\ \</math\>

## Strategic Purpose

The indicator is intended to measure judicial compliance in applying restorative justice in accordance with applicable legislation.

## Data Principle

The denominator is limited to cases meeting the criteria for application of the restorative justice approach.
