# E-Berpadu Indicator Dictionary

# e-Berpadu Performance Indicators

Two strategic indicators measure electronic criminal-process integration.

## 1.12 Electronic Case Transfer

Measures criminal, jinayat and military-criminal cases transferred electronically through e-Berpadu.

\<math\> \frac{\text{Cases transferred electronically}} {\text{Cases transferred}}\times100\\ \</math\>

## 1.13 Electronic Criminal Services

Measures criminal services submitted through e-Berpadu.

Services include:

- seizure authorization;
- search authorization;
- detention extension;
- detention suspension;
- diversion determination;
- online detainee visitation;
- permission to leave detention;
- evidence loan/use;
- detention transfer;
- pretrial registration;
- other services determined by Supreme Court regulation.

\<math\> \frac{\text{Electronic criminal services}} {\text{Total criminal services}}\times100\\ \</math\>
