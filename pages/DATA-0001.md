# DATA-0001

# Data Governance

**Data governance** concerns the policies, responsibilities, standards, processes, and controls governing the creation, collection, management, use, sharing, protection, retention, and disposal of data.

## Judicial Data Governance

Judicial data may include:

- Case data
- Decision data
- Court workload data
- Personnel data
- Financial data
- Training data
- Administrative data
- Public service data
- Research data

## Governance Dimensions

Important dimensions include:

- Data ownership
- Data stewardship
- Data quality
- Data provenance
- Access
- Security
- Privacy
- Retention
- Interoperability
- Metadata
- Auditability

## Data Quality

Important characteristics may include:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Consistency
- Timeliness
- Validity
- Uniqueness

Poor data quality can affect analytics and AI systems.

## Data Provenance

Data provenance should help answer:

- Where did the data originate?
- When was it created?
- Who manages it?
- How was it transformed?
- What version is being used?

## Data and AI

AI systems may depend heavily on data quality.

Potential risks include:

- Bias
- Missing data
- Historical distortions
- Labeling errors
- Data leakage
- Privacy violations

## Access Control

Judicial data should be classified according to applicable legal and institutional requirements.

Potential classifications may include:

- Public
- Internal
- Restricted
- Confidential

Actual classifications must be established from applicable rules.
