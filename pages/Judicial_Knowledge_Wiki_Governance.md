# Judicial Knowledge Wiki Governance

# Judicial Knowledge Wiki Governance

The Judicial Knowledge Wiki should operate as a governed institutional knowledge system rather than an unrestricted public encyclopedia.

## Governance Layers

- editorial;
- source verification;
- legal review;
- access control;
- technical administration;
- AI ingestion;
- audit.

## Principle

**Open structure does not mean uncontrolled authority.**
