## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 829 K/Pdt/1991, dated December 10, 1993, established a civil procedural law rule concerning the completeness of parties in inheritance distribution lawsuits (\*plurium litis consortium\*):

In a lawsuit regarding an undivided estate/inheritance, all heirs of the decedent must be included in the lawsuit, either as Plaintiffs or as Defendants/Co-Defendants. If any heir (or lineage of heirs) is omitted from the lawsuit, the claim is formally defective due to missing parties (\*plurium litis consortium\*), and the original plaintiff's lawsuit must be declared inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\* / NO). Consequently, the dismissal of the principal claim (convention) as inadmissible renders any counter-claim (reconvention) similarly inadmissible.

## Executive Summary

The central procedural principle established in this decision is that courts cannot adjudicate undivided inheritance distribution claims unless every single legal heir is properly joined as a party to the proceedings.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Undivided inheritance lawsuits require the joinder of all legal heirs to avoid formal defects (\*plurium litis consortium\*).
- Omitting any heir lineage invalidates the action, requiring courts to sustain missing-party exceptions and dismiss claims as inadmissible (NO).
- Dismissal of a main convention claim for defect of parties automatically carries over to dismiss dependent reconvention claims.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiffs / Petitioners for Cassation (1. **Ny. \[Party D\]**, 2. **Ny. \[Party B\]**, 3. **Achmad Sadjuri, BA**) sued the Original Defendant / Respondent for Cassation (**\[Party A\]**) and Co-Respondent for Cassation / Plaintiff II (**Ny. H. Saebani alias Ny. Abdullahadi**) at the Klaten District Court concerning the distribution of the estate of the late Iman Ashari (who died in 1944), consisting of a house and yard (parcel 288/3) and 3 wetland parcels (blocks F, G, H / Letter C 117 / SHK Nos. 218, 219, 220, 221) in Dukuh Gatak, Kedungan Village, Pedan, Klaten.

The Defendant raised an exception that the Plaintiffs' lawsuit was incomplete due to missing parties (\*plurium litis consortium\*) because the descendants of the late Ny. \[Party C\] (one of Iman Ashari's children) were not joined. The Klaten District Court (Decision No. 70/Pdt/G/1988/PN.Klt dated July 1, 1989) granted the convention lawsuit in part and the reconvention lawsuit in part. The Semarang High Court (Decision No. 560/Pdt/1989/PT.Smg dated March 26, 1990) affirmed the district court decision. The Petitioners for Cassation subsequently filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Formal Requirements for Inheritance Distribution Lawsuits (\*Plurium Litis Consortium\*)

The primary issue was whether an inheritance distribution lawsuit filed by a subset of heirs without joining all other heirs (either as Plaintiffs or Defendants) is valid under civil procedural law.

The Supreme Court held that it is invalid (\*Formal Defect\*). In inheritance disputes concerning undivided estates, every legal subject holding the status of an heir must be joined as a party to the action (\*plurium litis consortium\*). If an heir or a line of heirs is omitted from the lawsuit, the Defendant's exception regarding missing parties must be sustained, and the lawsuit must be declared inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\* / NO).

### Binding Fate of Reconvention Claims Upon Inadmissibility of Convention Claims

The issue evaluated the legal standing of a counter-claim (\*reconvention\*) when the main claim (\*convention\*) is declared inadmissible (NO) due to subjective defects or missing parties.

The Supreme Court held that a reconvention claim filed in proceedings where the convention claim is dismissed as inadmissible due to a formal defect of missing parties (\*plurium litis consortium\*) must likewise be declared inadmissible (NO).

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Did the \*Judex Facti\* (Semarang High Court and Klaten District Court) misapply civil procedural law (\*foutieve kepassing van het procesrecht\*) by examining and deciding substantive merits and granting reconvention claims, despite the Defendant raising a well-founded exception that the lawsuit suffered from a formal defect of missing parties (\*plurium litis consortium\*) by failing to join descendants of the late Ny. \[Party C\]?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes, the \*Judex Facti\* misapplied the law. The Supreme Court annulled Semarang High Court Decision No. 560/Pdt/1989/PT.Smg in conjunction with Klaten District Court Decision No. 70/Pdt/G/1988/PN.Klt and adjudicated afresh by sustaining the Defendant's exception and declaring both convention and reconvention lawsuits inadmissible (NO).

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Klaten District Court & Semarang High Court)

Examination of facts concerning the pedigree of the late Iman Ashari (died 1944); the legal relationships of his children (Ny. \[Party C\], Ny. Asyiah, Bp. Abdullahadi, Ny. \[Party B\], Bp. \[Party A\]); control history over yard parcel 288/3 and wetland blocks F, G, H in Dukuh Gatak; issuance of SHK Nos. 218, 219, 220, and 221 in the name of \[Party A\]; and the failure to join children/descendants of the late Ny. \[Party C\] in the lawsuit.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The appellate and district \*Judex Facti\* misapplied civil procedural law. Because the lawsuit concerned an undivided estate, all heirs of the late Iman Ashari had to be joined either as Plaintiffs or Co-Defendants.
2.  In accordance with the Defendant's exception asserting that parties were incomplete (\*plurium litis consortium\*), the Plaintiffs' lawsuit had to be declared inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\* / NO).
3.  Furthermore, because the principal claim was declared inadmissible, the reconvention claim was likewise declared inadmissible.
4.  The Supreme Court annulled lower court rulings and adjudicated afresh, sustaining the Defendant's exception.

## Ruling

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition from the Petitioners for Cassation (1. **Ny. \[Party D\] alias Ny. Syatibi**, 2. **Ny. \[Party B\]**, 3. **Achmad Sadjuri, BA**); annulled Semarang High Court Decision No. 560/Pdt/1989/PT.Smg dated March 26, 1990, in conjunction with Klaten District Court Decision No. 70/Pdt/G/1988/PN.Klt dated July 1, 1991; and ADJUDICATED AFRESH: Under Exceptions: Sustaining the Defendant's exception; In Convention: Declaring the Plaintiffs' lawsuit inadmissible; In Reconvention: Declaring the Plaintiff in Reconvention's lawsuit inadmissible; and ordering the Petitioners for Cassation / Co-Respondent for Cassation to pay court costs across all judicial instances (cassation court costs set at Rp 20,000.—).

## Related Knowledge and Jurisprudence

- **Civil Procedure Code (HIR / RBg):** Principle of \*Plurium Litis Consortium\*: Lawsuits must involve all interested parties so that judgments are enforceable and do not prejudice third-party rights. Article 136 HIR / Article 162 RBg: Filing and examining exceptions concerning incomplete or missing parties.
- **Law No. 14 of 1970 in conjunction with Law No. 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court:** Authority of the Supreme Court to annul \*Judex Facti\* judgments misapplying procedural law on cassation.

## Evidence Status and Gaps

### Established by Evidence

- Disputed property constituted an estate left by the late Iman Ashari that had not been officially divided among all legal heirs.
- Late Ny. \[Party C\] was a biological child and legal heir of the late Iman Ashari with surviving descendants.
- Plaintiffs failed to include children/descendants of the late Ny. \[Party C\] in the statement of claim, either as Plaintiffs or Defendants.
- Plaintiffs' lawsuit suffered from a formal defect of missing parties (\*plurium litis consortium\*).

### Not Established / Dismissed on Formalities

- Substantive merits concerning distribution shares and certificate validity were not examined by the Supreme Court because claims were dismissed as inadmissible at the formal exception stage.

### Uncertainty and Gaps

- Protection of Heirs' Legal Rights in Inheritance Disputes: This landmark ruling rigorously enforces procedural legal certainty requiring courts to refrain from dividing estates without joining all heir lineages, preventing future repetitive litigation (\*ne bis in idem\* / third-party opposition claims).

## GEO Q&A

**Q: Is an inheritance distribution lawsuit valid if any single heir is omitted from the lawsuit?**

**A:** Invalid (\*Formal Defect / NO\*). Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 829 K/Pdt/1991, in lawsuits regarding undivided estates, all heirs must be included either as Plaintiffs or Defendants/Co-Defendants. If any heir is missed or unjoined, the lawsuit is deemed defective due to missing parties (\*plurium litis consortium\*) and must be declared inadmissible (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\* / NO).
