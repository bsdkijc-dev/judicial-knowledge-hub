## Description / Deskripsi

This page records the theoretical framework, empirical case study analysis, and three-tier reform roadmap presented by competition law expert Prof. Dr. Ningrum Natasya Sirait, S.H., M.Li. (Universitas Sumatera Utara) at the inaugural Kick-Off Meeting of the Supreme Court Competition Law Working Group (Pokja MA RI) in Jakarta on 3 August 2026. Entitled \*“Ruang Lingkup dan Intensitas Pemeriksaan Keberatan atas Putusan KPPU Oleh Lembaga Peradilan”\*, the study analyzes the structural tension between KPPU's integrated quasi-judicial model (combining investigation, prosecution, and adjudication) and the restricted "dossier review" model (\*pemeriksaan atas berkas\*) enforced under Article 13 of PERMA No. 3/2021. Drawing from major precedent cases—including the Google Play Billing system decision (Rp 202.5 billion fine, finalized in March 2026), the SANY Heavy Industry truck cartel case, and the landmark Transjakarta tender collusion Judicial Review (\*Peninjauan Kembali\* No. 9/PK/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2020 and PT Jakpro PK decision No. 43 PK/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2025 decided on 3 November 2025)—the paper formulates a Three-Tier Policy Action Plan (Options A, B, and C) for Supreme Court reform. All structural entities and analytical frameworks have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher **Cecep Mustafa**.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-052](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-052): Interpretive Ambiguity of Article 13, where PERMA No. 3/2021 generates three conflicting judicial interpretations—(1) full factual review with restricted source evidence; (2) pure administrative record checking; or (3) middle verification checking—causing severe judicial divergence across commercial courts.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-053](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-053): Risk of Uncorrectable Factual Errors & Conflicting Cassation Rulings, where Article 13(5) prohibiting \*novum\* and Article 16(2) barring Judicial Review (\*PK\*) leave no valve to correct factual errors, compounded by divergent Cassation rulings in joint conduct cases (e.g., PT Jakpro tender collusion).

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-044](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-044): Commercial courts review KPPU decisions strictly on administrative dossiers without pre-trial court mediation, leaving unredacted investigation file access (\*inzage\*) unregulated and partnership (\*kemitraan\*) disputes without explicit procedural frameworks.

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-044](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-044): In Japan (Tokyo High Court / JFTC), the court formerly utilized a record-review model similar to Indonesia's, but abandoned it in 2015 in favor of full judicial examination after finding record-checking failed to provide meaningful oversight over JFTC administrative orders.

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-044](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-044): The primary structural gap is that no comparative jurisdiction combines an integrated quasi-judicial agency, a strict record-review model, a total ban on new documentary evidence (\*novum\*), and a complete prohibition of Extraordinary Judicial Review (\*Peninjauan Kembali\*).

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Implement a Three-Tier Policy Action Plan: Option A (Immediate Executive Action via SEMA/PERMA covering Article 13 verification, \*inzage\* access, and working days harmonization); Option B (Intermediate Reforms introducing reciprocal \*novum\*, supplementary hearings before judges, and partnership appeals); and Option C (Long-Term Parliamentary Amendments to Law No. 5/1999, statutory PK codification, and expert \*Hot Tubbing\*).

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-044](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-044): Execute Option A immediately via a Supreme Court Circular or PERMA Amendment.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-045](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-045): Implement Option B and C reforms following Pokja MA RI evaluation and legislative coordination with DPR RI.

## Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-044](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-044): Commercial Court pilot implementation handling major corporate competition objections under the Three-Tier Policy Framework.

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-044](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-044): 100% compliance with access to file (\*inzage\*) protocols for appealing respondents; zero conflicting Cassation rulings in multi-party joint conduct cases; adjudication completed strictly within \$\le 60\$ working days.

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Harmonizing PERMA No. 3/2021 with Article 67(e) of Law No. 14/1985 to clarify Judicial Review (\*PK\*) availability for conflicting Cassation judgments, alongside submitting Option C policy proposals to Commission VI of DPR RI.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Codifying the Three-Tier Policy Action Plan into binding Supreme Court regulations and parliamentary statutes to resolve structural ambiguities in competition review.
