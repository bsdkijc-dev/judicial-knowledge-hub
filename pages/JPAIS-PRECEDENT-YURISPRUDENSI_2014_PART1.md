# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:YURISPRUDENSI 2014 PART1

**Supreme Court Landmark Decisions Compendium (2014 Part 1)** (encompassing Supreme Court Decisions Reg. Nos. 154 K/Pdt/2012, 805 K/Pdt/2013, 165 PK/Pdt.Sus/2012, and 505 K/Pdt.Sus-BPSK/2013) represents a pivotal compilation of Supreme Court jurisprudence established under Chief Justice Decree No. 45/KMA/SK/III/2014. It consolidates strategic landmark rulings across General Civil Law, Special Intellectual Property Law (Trademarks), and Special Consumer Dispute Law:

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established four primary legal principles across distinct chambers:

1.  **Irrevocability of Marital Property Gifts to Children:** "Children can sue their parents over joint marital property (*harta gono-gini*) that has been gifted prior to divorce (*ex* Supreme Court Decision No. 154 K/Pdt/2012)." Such gifts cannot be unilaterally withdrawn or revoked except for restrictive reasons under Article 1688 of the Civil Code (BW), and the sale of gifted property by parents to third parties without the children's consent constitutes an Unlawful Act (Tort / PMH) that is null and void (*nietig*).
2.  **Mandatory Registration of Overseas Mixed Marriages:** "A marriage between an Indonesian citizen and a foreign citizen (mixed marriage) solemnized abroad (e.g., Hong Kong) must be reported to the local Republic of Indonesia Representative Office (*ex* Article 37 paragraphs (1) and (2) of Law No. 23 of 2006 on Population Administration); if unreported, an application filed at a District Court directing the Civil Registry Office to record such marriage is inadmissible (*niet ontvankelijk verklaard*)."
3.  **Distinct Class and Non-Confusing Trademark Comparison (IKEA vs. IKEMA):** "The IKEMA Trademark (owned by PT Angsa Daya for Class 19 - ceramics/tiles) does not bear a principal resemblance (*persamaan pada pokoknya*) to the Well-Known IKEA Trademark (owned by Inter Ikea Systems B.V. for Classes 11, 21, 24, 35, and 42 - household appliances/furniture) due to differences in goods classification, product types, phonetics/pronunciation, and consumer target markets."
4.  **BPSK Lacks Competence Over Insurance Policy Claims:** "The legal relationship between an insured party and an insurer based on an Insurance Policy constitutes a dispute within the scope of general civil law; therefore, the Consumer Dispute Resolution Board (*BPSK*) lacks competence (*incompetence ratione materiae*) to examine and adjudicate the case in question."

The Supreme Court granted the cassation and reconsideration petitions of the Petitioners in these respective matters, annulled erroneous lower court rulings (*judex facti*), and reinforced legal certainty and substantive justice.

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Absolutism of Marital Property Gift Irrevocability to Children (Article 1688 Civil Code / BW)

*Can joint marital property gifts agreed upon by both parents prior to divorce be annulled or unilaterally withdrawn via a Notarial Deed?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Cannot Be Unilaterally Revoked.** Pursuant to Article 1688 of the Civil Code, gift revocation is valid only if it fulfills 3 restrictive reasons: (1) non-fulfillment of gift conditions, (2) recipient committing crimes against the donor's life, or (3) recipient refusing maintenance when the donor falls into poverty. Unilateral gift revocation deeds are null and void (*nietig*), and parental sales of gifted property without children's consent constitute an Unlawful Act (Tort / PMH).

### 2. Formal Reporting Obligations for Overseas Mixed Marriages (Article 37 Law No. 23 of 2006 on Population Administration *jo.* Article 56 Law No. 1 of 1974)

*Can a marriage between an Indonesian citizen and a foreign citizen solemnized abroad without reporting to the KBRI/Indonesian Representative Office be recognized/recorded by a District Court in Indonesia?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard*).** Pursuant to Article 37 paragraphs (1) and (2) of Law No. 23 of 2006, overseas marriages of Indonesian citizens must be reported to the RI Representative Office in that country. Failure to report constitutes a strict procedural defect rendering applications for recognition/recording at Civil Registry Offices via District Courts inadmissible (*NO*).

### 3. Testing the *Neely Resemble* Doctrine and Goods Class Distinction Against Well-Known Trademarks (Article 6 Law No. 15 of 2001 on Trademarks)

The existence of a well-known mark does not automatically prohibit the registration of another mark bearing phonetic similarities in completely distinct goods classes (i.e., IKEMA Class 19 ceramics vs. IKEA Class 11/21/24/35/42 furniture), provided no bad-faith intent to free-ride or mislead consumers (*misleading/confusing*) is proven.

### 4. Limits of BPSK Absolute Competence (Article 1 point 11 & Article 45 Law No. 8 of 1999 on Consumer Protection)

Disputes involving the interpretation of loss/earthquake insurance policy clauses between bank debtors/insured parties and insurance companies constitute pure civil contract disputes falling under the absolute competence of General Courts (District Courts) / Arbitration; hence, BPSK lacks authority to adjudicate such matters.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

- *Children's Gift Case:* Did Surabaya High Court (Decision No. 646/PDT/2010/PT.SBY) correctly affirm the Banyuwangi District Court ruling declaring a unilateral notarial gift revocation deed legally invalid?

  
**Supreme Court Answer:** Yes. The *Judex Facti* reasoning was correct. Marital property gifts cannot be unilaterally revoked *ex* Article 1688 Civil Code. The Supreme Court rejected H. Suyit Abdillah's cassation petition.

- *BPSK Insurance Case:* Did Padang District Court (Decision No. 101/Pdt.G/BPSK/2013/PN.PDG) and Padang BPSK exceed their absolute jurisdiction by adjudicating a property insurance policy dispute?

  
**Supreme Court Answer:** Yes. BPSK and Padang District Court misapplied procedural law. BPSK lacks competence over insurance policy claims. The Supreme Court annulled Padang District Court and BPSK Padang rulings and independently adjudicated by declaring BPSK incompetent.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Banyuwangi District Court, Surakarta District Court, Central Jakarta District Court, & Padang District Court)

Verification of the Statement Letter of Marital Property Handover dated September 17, 1996; Hong Kong Marriage Certificate No. A 222440 dated January 27, 1993; IKEMA Trademark Certificate No. IDM000247161 Class 19 vs. IKEA; and Loss Adjuster PT Satria Dharma Pusaka Crawford THD reports concerning physical damage to showroom buildings from the Padang earthquake on September 30, 2009.

### Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- **Doctrine of *Irrevocability of Inter Vivos Gifts*:** Completed gifts to children prior to divorce instantly transfer ownership and close the door on unilateral parental annulment.
- **Doctrine of BPSK Jurisdictional Limits (Absolute Jurisdiction Boundaries):** BPSK is established to resolve simple consumer disputes; complex insurance policy claim disputes requiring loss adjuster verification and complex contract interpretation fall outside BPSK jurisdiction.

- The Statement Letter of Marital Property Handover dated September 17, 1996, is **PROVEN VALID, AND GIFTS TO CHILDREN CANNOT BE UNILATERALLY REVOKED**.
- The mixed marriage of A.S.M. Maniruzzaman and Fransisca Sri Luyatni WR in Hong Kong is **PROVEN NEVER REPORTED TO THE KBRI/RI REPRESENTATIVE, RENDERING REGISTRATION PETITIONS INADMISSIBLE (*NO*)**.
- IKEMA Trademark owned by PT Angsa Daya (Class 19) is **PROVEN NOT TO BEAR A PRINCIPAL RESEMBLANCE TO THE IKEA TRADEMARK**.
- Padang BPSK is **PROVEN TO LACK COMPETENCE TO ADJUDICATE PT ACA INSURANCE POLICY CLAIM DISPUTES**.
- Cassation/Reconsideration petitions filed by H. Suyit Abdillah, A.S.M. Maniruzzaman, and Inter Ikea Systems B.V. are **REJECTED BY THE SUPREME COURT**.

### Dismissed Findings (Overturned Rulings)

- Padang BPSK Ruling No. 49/BPSK-PDG/ARBT/V/2013 ordering PT ACA to pay Rp 1 billion plus 5%/day fines was **ANNULLED ENTIRELY BECAUSE BPSK LACKED JURISDICTION**.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Population Administration Certainty, Children's Rights Protection, & Consumer Jurisdiction:** Landmark rulings in this compendium deliver four pillars of legal certainty in Indonesia: (1) Marital property gifts to children are final and immune to parental divorce manipulations; (2) Legality of overseas marriages must comply with strict reporting discipline at RI Diplomatic Representatives; (3) Protection of well-known marks must not stifle local, different-class trademark registrations filed in good faith; and (4) BPSK is strictly barred from usurping District Court jurisdiction over civil insurance policy disputes.

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Does BPSK possess authority to adjudicate insurance policy claim disputes, and can overseas marriages without KBRI reports be recorded pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence?  
**BPSK Lacks Competence & Marriage Petitions Inadmissible (*NO*).** Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence Reg. Nos. 505 K/Pdt.Sus-BPSK/2013 and 805 K/Pdt/2013:

- The Consumer Dispute Resolution Board (*BPSK*) lacks competence to adjudicate insurance policy claim disputes, as insurance policy legal relationships constitute general civil disputes falling under the competence of District Courts / Arbitration.
- Mixed marriages solemnized abroad must be reported to the local RI Representative Office (KBRI/Consulate) pursuant to Article 37 of Law No. 23 of 2006 on Population Administration. If unreported, applications for recognition/recording at Civil Registry Offices are inadmissible (*niet ontvankelijk verklaard*).
- The Supreme Court annulled Padang BPSK rulings and rejected cassation petitions concerning unreported mixed marriages.
