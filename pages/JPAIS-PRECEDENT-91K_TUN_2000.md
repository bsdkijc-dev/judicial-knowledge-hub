## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 91 K/TUN/2000 dated November 13, 2000, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal principles regarding the absolute competence of tax dispute adjudication. The Supreme Court outlined that pursuant to Article 37 of Law No. 19 of 1997 on Tax Collection via Warrant of Execution (\*Surat Paksa\*), a tax debtor's lawsuit against the implementation of a Warrant of Execution \*\*may only be submitted to the Tax Dispute Resolution Board (BPSP)\*\*. Consequently, the State Administrative Court (PTUN) \*\*lacks absolute competence\*\* to examine and adjudicate lawsuits concerning the implementation of tax collection Warrants of Execution.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Respondent for Cassation (PT Timor Putra Nasional, represented by President Director Drs. Suharto) filed a lawsuit at the Jakarta State Administrative Court against the Defendants (Head of Jakarta Type A Customs and Service Office, Tanjung Priok III, Tanjung Priok II, and Head of Jakarta Tanah Abang Tax Service Office). The lawsuit challenged the issuance of more than a dozen Warrants of Execution for the collection of import duties, VAT, and luxury goods sales tax arrears related to the Timor national sedan car program for the 1996 through 1998 tax years.

The Jakarta State Administrative Court (Decision No. 025/G.TUN/1999/PTUN.JKT dated July 19, 1999) granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in entirety and declared the Warrants of Execution null and void. The Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 157/B/1999/PT.TUN.JKT dated December 9, 1999 / November 22, 1999) upheld the Jakarta State Administrative Court judgment. Dissatisfied with the ruling, the Defendants filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court raising an exception of absolute judicial competence.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The cassation objections raised by the Petitioners for Cassation are well-founded because the Jakarta State Administrative High Court committed an error in applying the law.
2.  Pursuant to Article 37 of Law No. 19 of 1997 on Tax Collection via Warrant of Execution, a tax debtor's lawsuit against the implementation of a Warrant of Execution "may only be submitted to the BPSP (Tax Dispute Resolution Board)" and therefore falls outside the jurisdiction of the State Administrative Court to examine and adjudicate.
3.  Under prevailing positive law, absolute competence to examine and adjudicate matters concerning tax collection via warrants of execution rests with the Tax Dispute Resolution Board (BPSP), as explicitly underscored in Article 37 of Law No. 19 of 1997 by the phrasing \*"may only be submitted to the BPSP..."\*.
4.  Such statutory formulation dictates that matters of tax collection via warrants of execution—regardless of whether viewed from tax administration or general tax law perspectives—cannot be handled by any judicial body other than the BPSP. Therefore, the Defendants' exception of absolute competence is sustained, and the Plaintiff's lawsuit is declared inadmissible.

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from the Petitioners for Cassation: I. HEAD OF JAKARTA TYPE A CUSTOMS AND SERVICE OFFICE; II. HEAD OF TANJUNG PRIOK III TYPE A CUSTOMS AND SERVICE OFFICE; III. HEAD OF TANJUNG PRIOK II CUSTOMS AND SERVICE OFFICE; IV. HEAD OF JAKARTA TANAH ABANG TAX SERVICE OFFICE; Annulling the Jakarta State Administrative High Court decision dated December 9, 1999, No. 157/B/1999/PT.TUN.JKT jo. the Jakarta State Administrative Court decision dated July 19, 1999, No. 025/G.TUN/1999/PTUN.JKT; and Independently Adjudicating: In Exception: Sustaining the Exceptions of Defendants I, II, III, and IV; In the Principal Case: Declaring the Plaintiff's lawsuit (PT Timor Putra Nasional) inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*); Ordering the Respondent for Cassation to pay all court costs across all levels of jurisdiction (assessed at Rp 100,000 at the cassation level). \*(Decided by Cassation Panel of Justices: Prof. Dr. Paulus E. Lotulung, SH. \[Presiding\], Ny. Hj. Emin Aminah Achadiat, SH., and Ny. Hj. Asma Samik Ibrahim, SH.)\*.
