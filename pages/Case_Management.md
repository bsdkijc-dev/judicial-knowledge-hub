# Case Management

# Case Management

**Case management** concerns the organization and management of a judicial case from initiation through disposition.

## Objectives

Potential objectives include:

- Timeliness
- Efficient allocation of resources
- Procedural fairness
- Reduction of unnecessary delay
- Transparency
- Predictability
- Effective use of judicial resources

## Case Management Lifecycle

**REGISTRATION** → **CLASSIFICATION** → **ASSIGNMENT** → **SCHEDULING** → **MONITORING** → **DISPOSITION** → **CLOSURE**

Actual procedures vary by jurisdiction.

## Data

Case management systems may generate:

- Filing data
- Procedural events
- Scheduling data
- Duration data
- Disposition data
- Workload data

## AI Applications

Potential applications include:

- Case classification
- Scheduling assistance
- Workload forecasting
- Duplicate detection
- Document routing
- Deadline monitoring

AI recommendations should remain subject to appropriate human oversight.

## Performance

Case management can be evaluated through:

- Time to disposition
- Pending cases
- Clearance rate
- Backlog
- Workload distribution

Metrics must be carefully defined.
