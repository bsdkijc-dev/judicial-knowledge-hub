# Indonesian Judiciary

The **Indonesian Judiciary** refers to the judicial system and judicial institutions operating within the constitutional and legal framework of Indonesia.

This page provides a high-level knowledge map rather than a substitute for official institutional or legal sources.

The Indonesian judicial system operates within a constitutional and statutory framework governing:
