# Indonesian Legal Ontologys

# Indonesian Legal Ontology

The ontology defines common concepts and relationships used to represent Indonesian judicial knowledge.

## Potential Entities

- law;
- regulation;
- court;
- case;
- judgment;
- legal issue;
- doctrine;
- procedure;
- institution.

## Purpose

Create consistent semantic representation for humans and machines.
