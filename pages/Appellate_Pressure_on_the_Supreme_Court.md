# Appellate Pressure on the Supreme Court

The Blueprint identifies the high number of cassation applications as one factor contributing to Supreme Court workload.

The document reports that, in the historical context examined during its preparation, approximately 80% of cases at the appellate level were likely to proceed to the Supreme Court.

## Historical Qualification

The figure represents the context of the period surrounding the preparation of the Blueprint.

It must not be interpreted as a current statistic.
