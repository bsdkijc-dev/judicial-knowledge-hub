# Judicial AI Certifications

# Judicial AI Certification

AI systems and/or responsible personnel may require certification before use in designated judicial contexts.

## Potential Certification Areas

- system risk;
- data;
- security;
- evaluation;
- human oversight;
- competency;
- governance.

## Principle

Certification should be evidence-based and periodically renewed.
