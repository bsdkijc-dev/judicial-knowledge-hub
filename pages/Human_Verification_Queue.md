# Human Verification Queue

AI-generated or machine-assisted knowledge requiring human review should enter a controlled verification queue.

## Priority

High priority should be given to:

- legal propositions;
- case summaries;
- evidence-related content;
- high-impact AI outputs;
- rights-sensitive content.

## Workflow

**Generate → Flag → Review → Verify → Publish**

## Principle

AI output must not silently become institutional knowledge.
