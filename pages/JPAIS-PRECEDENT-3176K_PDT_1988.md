# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:3176K PDT 1988

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 3176 K/Pdt/1988** (dated April 19, 1990 / deliberation on April 3, 1990) is a fundamental land law and civil evidentiary law jurisprudence precedent establishing vital rules concerning the legal standing of Ownership Rights Certificates (*Sertifikat Hak Milik* / SHM) as absolute authentic proof of land ownership compared to Tax Receipts / *Girik* (*Letter C*):

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established three primary legal principles:

1.  **Certificates as Authentic and Absolute Proof of Land Ownership:** A plot of land that clearly possesses a land certificate (i.e., SHM No. 626/Pusaka Rakyat Village) **cannot be freely transacted or acquired solely on the basis of a *Girik* letter**, but must be based on the relevant land certificate, which serves as authentic and absolute proof of ownership.
2.  **Legal Function of *Girik* Letters:** A *Girik* or Letter C serves **solely as a sign for paying taxes** (*Petuk Pajak*), and does not constitute proof of ownership rights over land. The bearer or holder of a *Girik* cannot trade or clear land for which a certificate has already been issued in the name of another party.
3.  **Unlawful Acts by Developers / Purchasers Based on *Girik*:** A developer (i.e., PT Green Garden / Defendant I) that clears certified land solely based on a *Girik* letter from local residents or *Girik* holders who are not the certificate owners is classified as acting without rights / unlawfully (*perbuatan melawan hukum*).

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioner for Cassation (PT Astra International Inc.), annulled Jakarta High Court Decision No. 527/Pdt/1987/PT.DKI dated October 22, 1987, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by partially granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit, declaring the Plaintiff's rights over land SHM No. 626 based on Power of Attorney Deed No. 92 and Appointment Deed No. 93 valid under the law, declaring Defendant I without rights in building a road over the disputed land, and ordering Defendant I to surrender the disputed land to the Plaintiff.

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Evidentiary Weight of Ownership Rights Certificates (SHM) vs. Girik / Letter C (Article 19 of Government Regulation No. 10 of 1961 / UUPA No. 5 of 1960)

*Can a sale-and-purchase transaction or land clearance over certified land (SHM) executed by a developer solely based on a third party's*Girik*be considered valid and override the rights of the certificate holder?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Invalid / Null and Void.**  
A land certificate constitutes authentic and absolute proof of land ownership rights. Land for which a certificate has clearly been issued cannot be transacted or cleared simply on the basis of a *Girik* letter. A *Girik* / Letter C / tax receipt is not proof of ownership, but merely proof of tax payment.

### 2. Legal Validity and Protection of Absolute Power of Attorney Transfers and Appointments Over Certified Land

A purchaser who has received a transfer of rights and authority from a lawful certificate holder (via a Power of Attorney Transfer Deed and Appointment Deed executed before a Notary/PPAT) is legally protected against usurpation or unauthorized construction committed by a developer.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* Jakarta High Court (Decision No. 527/Pdt/1987/PT.DKI) err in applying the law (*foutieve toepassing van het recht*) by dismissing PT Astra International Inc.'s lawsuit, whereas the Plaintiff held rights originating from SHM No. 626, while PT Green Garden (Defendant I) based its legal basis solely on *Girik* / Letter C documents?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. Jakarta High Court misapplied the law. The Supreme Court granted PT Astra International Inc.'s cassation petition, annulled the Jakarta High Court decision, and independently adjudicated by declaring the Plaintiff's rights over SHM No. 626 valid, declaring Defendant I's road construction on the disputed land unauthorized, and ordering Defendant I to surrender the land.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Central Jakarta District Court & Jakarta High Court)

Examination of facts regarding the issuance of SHM No. 626/Pusaka Rakyat Village measuring 19,850 m<sup>2</sup> in the name of \[Party A\] (Defendant VI) on December 19, 1975; verification of the power of attorney transfer from Defendant VI to Ir. \[Party B\] (Defendant VIII) and subsequently to PT Astra International Inc. via Notary Kartini Mulyadi, S.H. (Deeds No. 92 & 93 dated January 21, 1980); verification of asphalt road construction by PT Green Garden pursuant to Jakarta Governor Decree No. 0310/BKD.Wk.VIII/83; and testimonies from Bekasi Agrarian Office officials (Djuhana, S.H.) and North Jakarta Agrarian Measurement Section Head (Nursyirwan Ramli, S.H.) confirming the lawful issuance of SHM No. 626.

## Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

### Statutory Regulations

- **Law No. 5 of 1960 on Basic Agrarian Principles (UUPA):** Articles 19, 23, 32 (Land Registration & Evidentiary Force of Certificates).
- **Government Regulation No. 10 of 1961 on Land Registration:** Article 19 (Deeds of Land Rights Transfers).
- **Civil Code (KUHPerdata / BW):** Article 1365 (Unlawful Acts / Tort) & Article 1868 (Authentic Deeds).
- **Law No. 14 of 1970 *jo.* Law No. 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court:** Cassation and Independent Adjudication Authority.

### Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- **Doctrine of Certificate Supremacy Over *Girik*:** Land certificates represent the highest authentic proof of rights, overriding land claims based solely on *Girik*, Letter C, or tax receipts.
- **Doctrine of the Legal Function of *Girik*:** A *Girik* or Letter C serves strictly as fiscal evidence of land tax payment obligations (PBB/STTP), and can never function as a title deed capable of defeating a land certificate issued by the Land Agency (*Badan Pertanahan*).

## What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

### Established by Evidence

- SHM No. 626/Pusaka Rakyat Village in the name of \[Party A\] is **PROVEN LAWFULLY VALID AS ABSOLUTE AUTHENTIC PROOF OF LAND OWNERSHIP**.
- Rights acquired by PT Astra International Inc. pursuant to Power of Attorney Transfer Deed No. 92 and Appointment Deed No. 93 dated January 21, 1980, are **PROVEN LAWFULLY VALID**.
- PT Green Garden's (Defendant I) actions in constructing an asphalt road and parceling housing lots over SHM No. 626 land based solely on local residents' *Girik* documents are **PROVEN UNAUTHORIZED / UNLAWFUL**.
- Defendant I is ordered to surrender the disputed land and buildings thereon to the Plaintiff.
- Jakarta High Court Decision No. 527/Pdt/1987/PT.DKI is **NULL AND VOID**.

### Dismissed Arguments

- Defendant I's defense that clearing *Girik* land pursuant to a Jakarta Governor Decree could override the existence of SHM No. 626 was **REJECTED ENTIRELY BY THE SUPREME COURT**.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Protecting Legal Certainty for Land Certificate Holders Against Developers and *Girik* Clearance:** This landmark ruling establishes an exceptionally robust foundation for land law in Indonesia: developers, private entities, and government agencies cannot conduct land clearance or acquisition merely by purchasing *Girik* / Letter C documents without verifying land registry records at the Land Office. Land certificates are absolute authentic instruments immune to private transactions centered on *Girik* documents.
