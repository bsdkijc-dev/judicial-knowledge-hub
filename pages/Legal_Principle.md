# Legal Principle

# Legal Principle

A **legal principle** is a general normative proposition used within legal reasoning, interpretation, adjudication, or legal analysis.

Its legal authority depends on its source and the applicable legal system.

**\[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## Principle Structure

A structured principle record may include:

- Name
- Definition
- Legal source
- Jurisdiction
- Scope
- Exceptions
- Related provisions
- Relevant decisions
- Evidence level

## Principle and Rule

A legal principle may differ from a specific legal rule in generality, abstraction, or mode of application.

The distinction should be maintained rather than assumed.

## Principle and Judicial Decision

A court decision may:

- Apply a principle
- Interpret a principle
- Balance principles
- Distinguish a principle
- Develop jurisprudence concerning a principle

## AI and Legal Principles

AI systems may identify candidate principles from legal texts.

However, identification by AI does not establish that the proposition is legally authoritative.

The source must be verified.
