# Benchbook: AI Directives

# Chapter 3: SEMA Speech-To-Text AI Guidelines

*Administrative Directive on Automated Transcription Protocols*

## 1. Verification Requirement

Draft transcripts generated by automated speech-to-text engines inside localized pilot courtrooms are strictly considered **draft materials**. They hold no official evidentiary weight until they have been cross-checked manually against the master courtroom backup audio files.

## 2. Data Security Restrictions

To preserve privacy and legal privilege, no draft audio tracks or rough transcript arrays may be processed on unauthorized public cloud networks. All automated processing must occur within offline district server backups.
