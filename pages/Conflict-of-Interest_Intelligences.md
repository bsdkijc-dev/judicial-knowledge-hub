# Conflict-of-Interest Intelligences

# Conflict-of-Interest Intelligence

Data-assisted tools may help identify potential conflicts requiring human verification.

## Requirements

- reliable data;
- defined conflict rules;
- privacy protection;
- human verification;
- auditability.

## Boundary

System-generated matches require verification before any institutional action.
