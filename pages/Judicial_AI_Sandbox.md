# Judicial AI Sandbox

A Judicial AI Sandbox provides a controlled environment for testing AI-enabled judicial processes.

## Required Boundaries

- legal authority;
- scope;
- duration;
- participating court;
- users;
- data boundary;
- security;
- safeguards;
- evaluation criteria;
- exit condition.

## Rule

Sandbox participation does not constitute authorization for institution-wide deployment.
