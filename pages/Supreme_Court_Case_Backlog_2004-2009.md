# Supreme Court Case Backlog 2004–2009

The Blueprint records a reduction in the Supreme Court case backlog.

| Year | Cases  |
|------|--------|
| 2004 | 20,314 |
| 2009 | 11,479 |

## Interpretation

The Blueprint presents the reduction as one of the achievements of the 2005–2009 reform period.

## Historical Limitation

These figures represent a historical baseline and must not be used as current statistics without updated evidence.
