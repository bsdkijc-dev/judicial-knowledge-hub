# Strategic Retirement Principle

# Strategic Retirement Principle

Not every initiative should continue indefinitely.

## Retirement Reasons

- obsolete;
- ineffective;
- too risky;
- too costly;
- legally incompatible;
- replaced by a better capability.

## Principle

**A mature institution knows when to stop.**
