# e-Court Utilization

Indicator 1.11 measures the percentage of first-instance and appellate civil, religious-court, and administrative cases submitted through e-Court.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Eligible cases submitted through e-Court}} {\text{Total eligible cases submitted}}\times100\\ \</math\>

## Scope

The Renstra defines electronic case administration as covering electronic receipt of claims/applications, payment, summons and notifications, pleadings, appeals, and management and storage of case documents.
