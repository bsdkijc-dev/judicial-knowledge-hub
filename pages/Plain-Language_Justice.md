# Plain-Language Justice

# Plain-Language Justice

Judicial communication should be understandable to its intended users while preserving legal accuracy.

## Applications

- public information;
- service instructions;
- procedural explanations;
- accessibility materials;
- approved summaries.

## Boundary

Plain-language explanations must not alter the legal meaning of authoritative documents.
