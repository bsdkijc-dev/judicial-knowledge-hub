# Open Judicial Datas

# Open Judicial Data

Open Judicial Data seeks to increase transparency and evidence-based research while protecting privacy, security and due process.

## Principles

- openness by design where lawful;
- privacy protection;
- security;
- data quality;
- traceability;
- responsible reuse.
