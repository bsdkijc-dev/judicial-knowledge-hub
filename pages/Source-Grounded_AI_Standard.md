# Source-Grounded AI Standard

# Source-Grounded AI Standard

Judicial AI systems should be grounded in authorised and traceable sources.

## Minimum Requirements

- approved source repository;
- source citation;
- provenance;
- retrieval traceability;
- uncertainty disclosure;
- human verification.

## Core Rule

**No source → no authoritative claim.**
