## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 808 K/Pid/1984 established a fundamental criminal procedural law rule concerning the material requirements for the validity of a Public Prosecutor's indictment:

A Public Prosecutor's indictment that is imprecise, unclear, and incomplete regarding the description of criminal events, dates, locations, and the qualification of the charged offenses (\*obscuur libel\*) MUST BE DECLARED NULL AND VOID (\*BATAL DEMI HUKUM\*). The Public Prosecutor may not obscure civil debt-dispute matters involving collateral security (\*borg\*) into criminal embezzlement offenses without formulating factual elements of unlawful acts precisely, clearly, and completely.

## Executive Summary

The central procedural principle established in this decision is that blurring the lines between civil debt disputes and criminal offenses through defective, vague indictments renders the entire prosecution legally null and void (\*batal demi hukum\*).

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Indictments lacking precision, clarity, and completeness regarding dates, places, and legal qualifications are null and void under criminal procedure code provisions.
- Creditor possession of collateral stemming from loan agreements constitutes a civil matter rather than criminal embezzlement.
- Appellate and district judgments upholding deficient indictments are subject to complete reversal on cassation.

The Defendants subsequently filed cassation petitions with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Material Requirements of Indictments and Consequences of Nullity (\*Obscuur Libel\* ex Article 143 KUHAP / Article 282 HIR)

The primary issue was whether a Public Prosecutor's indictment that obscures boundaries between civil loan-collateral disputes and criminal embezzlement—failing to describe the unlawful acts of each Defendant with precision, clarity, and completeness—is valid under the law.

The Supreme Court held that it is null and void (\*Batal Demi Hukum\*). A Public Prosecutor's indictment failing to precisely formulate crime elements, times, places, and specific roles of Defendants constitutes a substantially defective indictment (\*obscuur libel\*). Prosecutors are prohibited from framing civil loan contract disputes as criminal embezzlement without precise factual descriptions, requiring the indictment to be declared null and void.

### Distinguishing Civil Default in Pledges/Collateral from Criminal Embezzlement (Article 372 KUHP)

Creditor possession of collateral (\*borg\*) arising from loan agreements constitutes possession based strictly on a pure civil relationship. Disputes regarding debt repayment, interest calculations, and collateral return must be resolved within the civil domain and cannot be criminalized as embezzlement.

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Does a Public Prosecutor's indictment formulating criminal embezzlement imprecisely, unclearly, and incompletely over a secured loan dispute satisfy statutory criteria for nullity (\*batal demi hukum\*) ex Article 143 KUHAP / HIR?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes. The Supreme Court annulled the Semarang High Court and Pati District Court decisions, declaring the Public Prosecutor's indictment null and void (\*batal demi hukum\*).

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Pati District Court & Semarang High Court)

Examination of facts concerning the Rp 1,000,000.— loan by Ny. \[Witness B\] / Pak \[Witness B\] to Defendant I (\[Witness\]) secured by diamond jewelry belonging to Ny. Mitro which was subsequently substituted; delivery of ransom/deposit funds of Rp 1,400,000.— before Defendant II (\[Defendant\]); and the background of social unrest/racial issues in Tayu/Pati in 1980.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The \*Judex Facti\* of Semarang High Court and Pati District Court misapplied the law.
2.  The indictment formulated by the Public Prosecutor failed to formulate criminal facts with precision, clarity, and completeness regarding crime qualifications and the roles of individual Defendants, while obscuring a secured debt dispute into a criminal embezzlement charge.
3.  Pursuant to criminal procedural provisions (Article 143 KUHAP / Article 282 HIR), an imprecise, unclear, and incomplete indictment is Null and Void (\*Batal Demi Hukum\*).
4.  The Supreme Court annulled lower court rulings and declared the Public Prosecutor's indictment null and void.

## Ruling

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition from the Petitioners for Cassation (Defendant I: **\[Defendant I\] alias \[Witness\] alias NY. \[Defendant\]** and Defendant II: **\[Defendant II\]**); annulled Semarang High Court Decision No. 225/1982/Pid/P.T.Smg dated January 16, 1984, in conjunction with Pati District Court Decision No. 491/1981/Pid.S dated April 13, 1982; and ADJUDICATED AFRESH: Declaring the Public Prosecutor's Indictment Null and Void (\*Batal Demi Hukum\*); Ordering the return of evidence to those entitled; and charging the State with court costs across all judicial instances.

### Uncertainty and Gaps

- Prevention of Criminalizing Secured Debt Disputes: This landmark ruling establishes strict boundaries preventing Courts and Public Prosecutors from allowing civil pledge/debt disputes to be dragged into criminal courts via imprecisely drafted indictments.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: What are the legal consequences if a Public Prosecutor's indictment fails to describe criminal acts with precision, clarity, and completeness?**

**A:** Null and Void (\*Batal Demi Hukum\* / \*Obscuur Libel\*). Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 808 K/Pid/1984, a Public Prosecutor's indictment failing to state factual actions, times, locations, and crime qualifications with precision, clarity, and completeness is declared null and void. Prosecutors are prohibited from obscuring civil legal relationships (such as loans secured by collateral goods) into criminal embezzlement offenses without precise factual crime formulations.
