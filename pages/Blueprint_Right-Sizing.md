# Blueprint Right-Sizing

# Blueprint Right-Sizing

The Blueprint associates organizational reform with the principle of creating an organization with appropriate functions and size.

## Core Principle

**Right Function + Right Size**

## Enabler

Business process re-engineering is identified as an important prerequisite.

## Modern Interpretation

Institutional process redesign should precede automation when the objective is genuine organizational transformation.
