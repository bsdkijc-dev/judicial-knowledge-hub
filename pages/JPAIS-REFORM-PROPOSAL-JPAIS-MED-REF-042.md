## Description / Deskripsi

This page records the standardized adjudication framework and policy guidelines for KPPU objection proceedings, formulated pursuant to Supreme Court Directives Letter No. \`23/KM.BIN/HK1.2/VII/2026\` issued on 9 July 2026 by Chairman of the Development Chamber, Syamsul Ma'arif, S.H., LL.M., Ph.D., and the official technical submission delivered on 23 July 2026 by Chief of the Central Jakarta Commercial Court, Dr. Husnul Khotimah, S.H., M.H. The reform proposals focus on resolving three critical procedural bottlenecks in PERMA No. 3/2021: (1) eliminating the normative contradiction between the 3-to-12-month trial timeframe in Article 14(1) PERMA 3/2021 and the 5-month maximum case disposal limit under SEMA No. 2/2012; (2) re-integrating court-annexed mediation under PERMA No. 1/2016 into competition disputes to foster win-win commercial solutions; and (3) empowering Commercial Court judges to order asset discovery and mandate bailiff seizure (\*sita jaminan\*) for unpaid Non-Tax State Revenue (PNBP) administrative fines. All structural entities, legal analyses, and procedural guidelines have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher **Cecep Mustafa**.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-048](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-048): Normative Timeframe Contradiction, where trapping judicial panels between the minimum 3-month rule in Article 14(1) PERMA No. 3/2021 and the 5-month maximum limit under SEMA No. 2/2012 causes unnecessary delays and administrative non-compliance.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-049](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-049): Adversarial Rigidity & Execution Impasse, where excluding mediation under Article 12 PERMA No. 3/2021 forces all objections into protracted litigation, while the absence of asset discovery powers and attached asset schedules renders court judgments unenforceable when non-compliant respondents conceal corporate assets.

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-042](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-042): Competition objection trials proceed strictly on administrative dossiers without a pre-trial mediation phase, resulting in an adversarial posture where over 83% of cases escalate to Supreme Court Cassation, while trial durations vary significantly across commercial courts due to conflicting timeline interpretations, and fine collection relies on District Court execution clerks lacking compulsory asset disclosure powers.

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-042](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-042): In Japan (Tokyo High Court / JFTC), the court combines specialized judicial review with administrative enforcement mechanisms that mandate public financial disclosures, enabling swift asset attachment for unpaid administrative penalties, providing a benchmark for empowering Indonesian commercial courts with asset discovery and mediation tools.

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-042](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-042): The primary gap is the lack of statutory provisions in PERMA No. 3/2021 authorizing Commercial Court judges to order asset discovery, mandate pre-trial mediation, and complete trials within a streamlined 60-working-day window.

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Amend Article 14(1) of PERMA No. 3/2021 to delete the 3-month minimum trial timeframe, establishing a strict maximum disposal cap of 60 working days; revise Article 12 to permit court-annexed mediation under PERMA No. 1/2016; amend execution provisions to require KPPU to submit certified respondent asset schedules, empowering judges to include bailiff seizure orders (\*sita jaminan\*) in final amars; and issue binding guidelines clarifying witness summoning restrictions.

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-042](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-042): Issue a Supreme Court Circular (SEMA) waiving minimum trial duration requirements and encouraging voluntary judicial mediation.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-043](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-043): Promulgate PERMA Amandemen 3/2021 incorporating Central Jakarta's 7-point DIM recommendations regarding trial timelines, mediation, and asset seizure.

## Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-042](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-042): Central Jakarta Commercial Court (High-Density Hub) implementation of standardized adjudication guidelines.

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-042](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-042): 100% case disposal completed strictly within \$\le 60\$ working days; zero execution failures caused by missing corporate asset inventories in final judgments; successful implementation of court mediation for eligible commercial competition disputes.

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Harmonizing SEMA No. 2/2012 and PERMA No. 3/2021 to ensure uniform trial management across all 5 Commercial Courts, alongside strengthening inter-agency cooperation between Mahkamah Agung, KPPU, and Ministry of Finance (DJKN) for PNBP fine execution and asset tracing.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgating standardized adjudication guidelines that reconcile trial timelines, introduce court mediation, and establish enforceable execution remedies.

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Supreme Court Request Letter No. \`23/KM.BIN/HK1.2/VII/2026\` by Syamsul Ma'arif, S.H., LL.M., Ph.D. (9 July 2026) and Official Report by Dr. Husnul Khotimah, S.H., M.H. (23 July 2026).
