## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 830 K/Pid/2003, dated July 23, 2003, established a special criminal corruption law rule concerning the principle of sentencing proportionality and preventive/educative functions in corruption offenses involving trillions of rupiahs of Bank Indonesia Liquidity Assistance (\*BLBI\*) funds:

A 4-year imprisonment sentence imposed by the Jakarta High Court (\*Judex Facti\*) for joint corruption involving the misappropriation of BLBI funds amounting to Rp 1,291,530,307,776.84 (approximately Rp 1.291 Trillion) committed by a bank President Director (\[Defendant\]) was declared UNBALANCED and UNPROPORTIONAL given the gravity of corruption offenses punishable by maximum penalties of capital punishment, life imprisonment, or 20 years' imprisonment. The Supreme Court annulled the Jakarta High Court decision and enhanced the Defendant's sentence to 8 (eight) years' imprisonment, a fine of Rp 30,000,000.— subsidiary to 6 months' confinement, and an order to pay Restitution (\*Uang Pengganti\*) of Rp 1,291,530,307,776.84 to deliver educative, preventive, and equitable retribution.

## Executive Summary

The central legal principle established in this decision is that grand corruption involving trillions in state liquidity assistance requires severe, proportional sentences reflecting extraordinary crimes, rejecting lenient judicial discounts.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Light sentences (e.g., 4 years) for trillion-rupiah systemic banking corruption are unproportional and must be enhanced on cassation.
- Misappropriation of liquidity assistance funds for interbank debt settlements and fictitious instruments constitutes severe abuse of authority.
- Trillion-rupiah restitution orders must accompany prison enhancements to ensure state financial recovery.

## Case Background

The Original Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation, **\[Defendant\]** (\[age removed\], former President Director of PT Bank Umum Servitia Tbk), was charged with joint corruption (Law No. 3 of 1971 jo. Law No. 31 of 1999) involving the misappropriation of Bank Indonesia Liquidity Assistance (\*BLBI\*) funds amounting to Rp 988.26 Billion via 34 Credit Notes to Bank Sanho, issuance of fictitious Negotiable Certificates of Deposit (\*NCD\*) to Bank Dagang Bali (Rp 155 Billion) and Bank Eksekutif (Rp 127.1 Billion), and demand loan financing for PT Mitra Rona Wana Sejahtera (Rp 25.6 Billion), resulting in total state financial losses of Rp 1,291,530,307,776.84.

The West Jakarta District Court (Decision No. 504/Pid.B/2001/PN.Jkt.Bar. dated March 11, 2002) sentenced the Defendant to 1 year's imprisonment, a Rp 30,000,000.— fine, and Restitution of Rp 1.291 Trillion. The Jakarta High Court (Decision No. 67/Pid/2002/PT.DKI dated August 12, 2002) modified the prison sentence to 4 years. Both the Defendant and the Public Prosecutor filed cassation petitions with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Proportionality Testing and Educative/Preventive Effects of Banking Mega-Scandal Corruption Penalties (BLBI)

The primary issue was whether a 4-year imprisonment sentence imposed by the Jakarta High Court upon a Defendant involved in BLBI fund misappropriation valued at Rp 1.291 Trillion satisfies principles of justice and sentencing objectives.

The Supreme Court held that it does not. Joint corruption involving Bank Indonesia Liquidity Assistance facilities causing trillion-rupiah state losses constitutes an extraordinary crime (\*extraordinary crime\*) punishable by maximum penalties of capital punishment, life imprisonment, or 20 years' imprisonment. Imposing overly lenient sentences (4 years) by the \*Judex Facti\* offends public sense of justice and violates sentencing objectives requiring educative, preventive, and equitable (\*retributive & deterrent\*) sanctions proportional to the economic damage inflicted upon the State.

### Elements of Abuse of Authority and Unlawful Acts by Banking Directors in Disbursing BLBI Funds ex Article 1 paragraph 1 sub b of Law No. 3 of 1971

Actions by a Bank President Director diverting Bank Indonesia liquidity facility funds under their supervision to settle related-party debt obligations (Bank Sanho), unlawfully issuing certificates of deposit (\*NCD\*) while the bank's current account was in debit balance, and disbursing fictitious loans are qualified as abuse of authority and pure unlawful acts harming State finances.

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Did the \*Judex Facti\* (Jakarta High Court) misapply the law (\*foutieve kepassing van het recht\*) in determining sentencing quantum (\*strafmaat\*) by imposing a 4-year prison sentence unproportional to state losses of Rp 1.291 Trillion and maximum statutory corruption penalties?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes, the \*Judex Facti\* misapplied sentencing law. The Supreme Court annulled Jakarta High Court Decision No. 67/Pid/2002/PT.DKI in conjunction with West Jakarta District Court Decision No. 504/Pid.B/2001/PN.Jkt.Bar and adjudicated afresh by enhancing the Defendant's prison sentence from 4 years to 8 (eight) years and ordering Restitution of Rp 1,291,530,307,776.84.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / West Jakarta District Court & Jakarta High Court)

Examination of facts concerning Defendant \[Defendant\]'s position as President Director of PT Bank Umum Servitia Tbk; issuance of 34 Credit Notes valued at Rp 988,265,055,555.56 to Bank Sanho; NCD sales transactions to PT Bank Dagang Bali (Rp 155 Billion) and PT Bank Eksekutif (Rp 127.1 Billion); demand loan disbursements for PT Mitra Rona Wana Sejahtera (Rp 25.6 Billion); and total actual State financial losses of Rp 1,291,530,307,776.84.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The appellate \*Judex Facti\* of Jakarta High Court misapplied the law in determining sentencing quantum (\*strafmaat\*). A 4-year prison sentence imposed for trillion-rupiah BLBI corruption was unbalanced and unproportional to maximum statutory corruption penalties.
2.  \*Judex Facti\* should have imposed sentences possessing educative, preventive, and equitable effects proportionate to proven acts.
3.  The Supreme Court annulled lower court rulings and adjudicated afresh, enhancing prison terms to 8 years, imposing a fine, and ordering full restitution of Rp 1,291,530,307,776.84.

## Ruling

The Supreme Court ruled: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation (Defendant **\[Defendant\]**); Annulling Jakarta High Court Decision No. 67/Pid/2002/PT.DKI dated August 12, 2002, in conjunction with West Jakarta District Court Decision No. 504/Pid.B/2001/PN.Jkt.Bar. dated March 11, 2002; and ADJUDICATING AFRESH: 1. Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] proven legally and convincingly guilty of committing the crime of "Joint Corruption"; 2. Sentencing the Defendant to a prison term of 8 (eight) years; 3. Deducting the full period of detention served; 4. Sentencing the Defendant to a fine of Rp 30,000,000.— subsidiary to 6 months' confinement; 5. Sentencing the Defendant to pay Restitution to the State amounting to Rp 1,291,530,307,776.84 (one trillion two hundred ninety-one billion five hundred thirty million three hundred seven thousand seven hundred seventy-six rupiahs and eighty-four cents); 6. Forfeiting evidence land/buildings to the State; and charging cassation court costs amounting to Rp 2,500.—.

## Evidence Status and Gaps

### Established by Evidence

- Defendant \[Defendant\] was proven legally and convincingly guilty of committing joint corruption.
- Total State financial losses caused by the Defendant's misuse of BLBI funds, NCDs, and fictitious loans amounted to Rp 1,291,530,307,776.84.
- The Defendant was sentenced to 8 years' imprisonment, a Rp 30,000,000.— fine (subsidiary 6 months), and Restitution of Rp 1,291,530,307,776.84.

### Not Established / Rejected

- The Defendant's defense asserting that credit note withdrawals and NCD issuances were merely efforts to rescue the bank from depositor bank runs was dismissed and set aside by the Supreme Court because such acts were unlawfully executed while in debit balance.

### Uncertainty and Gaps

- Standardization of Banking Corruption Sentencing / BLBI: This landmark ruling serves as a vital precedent giving strict guidance to judges across Indonesia not to grant lenient discounts or overly light sentences for grand corruption, but to impose severe, proportional sentences to recover state funds and establish national deterrence.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: Does the Supreme Court possess authority to enhance prison sentences for BLBI corruption offenders given overly lenient sentences by High Courts?**

**A:** Yes, it possesses authority and the obligation. Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 830 K/Pid/2003, a 4-year prison sentence imposed by a High Court upon a BLBI corruption offender involving Rp 1.291 Trillion (\[Defendant\]) was declared unbalanced and unproportional to the gravity of the crime. The Supreme Court is authorized to annul such decisions and enhance prison sentences to 8 (eight) years alongside Restitution of Rp 1.291 Trillion to deliver educative, preventive, and equitable penal sanctions.
