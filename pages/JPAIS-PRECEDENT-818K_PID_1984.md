## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 818 K/Pid/1984, dated May 30, 1985, established a material criminal law rule concerning the application of offense qualifications for homogenous offenses carrying lighter statutory penalties (\*inclusief\* / \*generics-specifics\*):

Although the offense prosecuted by the Public Prosecutor in the indictment is Ordinary Defamation / Slander ex Article 310 of the Criminal Code (KUHP), the Defendant can be found guilty and punished under Article 315 of the Criminal Code (Minor Defamation) if the material factual acts proven at trial fail to satisfy the element of specific attribution of acts (Article 310 KUHP), but instead constitute minor defamation. Because the offense under Article 315 KUHP is a homogenous (sejenis) yet lesser offense carrying a lighter statutory penalty than Article 310 KUHP, such verdict qualification modifications are lawful and affirmed by the Supreme Court without violating the boundaries of prosecution.

## Executive Summary

The central legal principle established in this decision is that courts may modify conviction qualifications from ordinary defamation to minor defamation when trial evidence proves general insults rather than specific false imputations, provided both crimes are homogenous.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Indictments charging ordinary defamation under Article 310 KUHP can support convictions under Article 315 KUHP for minor defamation if specific imputations are unproven.
- Homogenous offenses with lighter penalty ceilings permit judicial re-qualification without exceeding prosecutorial bounds.
- Cassation appeals challenging such modifications without showing substantive prejudice are dismissed while formal qualifications are corrected.

## Case Background

The Original Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation, **\[Defendant\]** (\[age removed\], journalist for Harian Analisa), was charged with defamation (Article 310 KUHP) for stating on December 16, 1978, at the Central Market of Medan that Himpunan Pedagang Indonesia (H.P.I.) Rayon I and its board members "smacked of Komando Jihad".

The Medan District Court (Decision No. 978/KS/1979/PN.Mdn dated May 17, 1979) convicted the Defendant of defamation (Article 310 KUHP) and sentenced him to a fine of Rp 500.— subsidiary to 2 days' confinement. The Medan High Court (Decision No. 206/Pid/1983/PT.Mdn dated December 31, 1983) modified the sentence to 1 month's imprisonment with a 3-month probation period (Article 14a KUHP). The Defendant subsequently filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Application of the Homogenous Lesser Offense Doctrine (\*Inclusief\* / \*Generics-Specifics\*) in Criminal Sentencing

The primary issue was whether a Judge is justified in convicting a Defendant under Article 315 of the Criminal Code (Minor Defamation) when the Public Prosecutor's indictment formulates sole prosecution under Article 310 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code (Ordinary Defamation / Slander).

The Supreme Court held that it is justified. When trial evidence fails to prove the specific element of "imputing a specific act to be made public" under Article 310 KUHP, but establishes elements of Minor Defamation under Article 315 KUHP, the Trial Panel holds the authority to sentence the Defendant under Article 315 KUHP. This is grounded on the principle that Article 315 KUHP is a homogenous (homogen) offense sharing core characteristics with Article 310 KUHP while carrying a lighter statutory penalty ceiling, ensuring that qualification adjustments do not violate fair trial principles or exceed prosecution boundaries.

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Did the \*Judex Facti\* (Medan High Court & Medan District Court) commit a legal qualification error in their verdict dictums by declaring the Defendant guilty of "Defamation/Slander" (Article 310 KUHP), whereas utterances of "smacking of Komando Jihad" without specific act imputation constitute "Minor Defamation" (Article 315 KUHP)?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes, the lower courts' verdict qualifications required correction. The Supreme Court rejected the Defendant's cassation petition while modifying the judgment to declare the Defendant proven guilty of committing the crime of "Minor Defamation" ex Article 315 of the Criminal Code.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Medan District Court & Medan High Court)

Examination of facts concerning the incident on December 16, 1978, around 13:00 WIB at Wahab Merek Horas Kios, 2nd Floor, Mercu Buana Project, Medan Central Market; statements made by Defendant \[Defendant\] (journalist) regarding "H.P.I. Rayon I Central Market Medan and its board members smelling of Komando Jihad"; and resulting offense/grievance taken by H.P.I. board members (Sutan Lukman et al. and Wahab / Wong Peng Cun).

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The \*Judex Facti\* did not misapply the law regarding establishing guilt, but erred regarding verdict qualifications.
2.  The Defendant's acts did not satisfy the specific act imputation requirement ex Article 310 paragraph (1) KUHP, but constituted Minor Defamation ex Article 315 KUHP.
3.  Although Article 310 KUHP was charged, the Defendant could be found guilty under Article 315 KUHP because Article 315 KUHP constitutes a homogenous yet lesser offense carrying lighter statutory penalties than Article 310 KUHP.
4.  The Supreme Court rejected the Defendant's cassation petition while modifying the verdict qualification to "Minor Defamation".

## Ruling

The Supreme Court ruled: Rejecting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation (**\[Defendant\]**); Modifying the ruling of Medan High Court Decision No. 206/Pid/1983/PT.Mdn dated December 31, 1983, and Medan District Court Decision No. 978/KS/1979/PN.Mdn dated May 17, 1979, solely regarding the formulation of proven guilt and qualification dictums to read: Declaring the Defendant legally and convincingly proven guilty of committing the crime of "Minor Defamation"; Ordering the Petitioner for Cassation / Defendant to pay cassation court costs amounting to Rp 2,500.—.

## Evidence Status and Gaps

### Established by Evidence

- Defendant \[Defendant\] uttered public statements that H.P.I. and its board members "smacked of Komando Jihad" on December 16, 1978.
- These statements wounded the feelings and reputation of H.P.I. board members (Sutan Lukman and Wahab).
- The Defendant's acts legally satisfied elements of the crime of Minor Defamation under Article 315 of the Criminal Code.

### Not Established / Modified on Cassation

- The Defendant was unproven to have imputed specific acts as required under the Slander offense of Article 310 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code, leading to the judicial re-qualification of the offense by the Supreme Court.

### Uncertainty and Gaps

- Flexibility of Verdict Qualifications Without Subsidiary Indictments: This landmark ruling provides vital guidance showing that scaling homogenous lesser offenses (\*generics-specifics\*) enables judges to correct sentencing qualifications for justice without forcing outright acquittals merely due to the absence of subsidiary indictment formulations.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: May a Judge convict a Defendant under Article 315 of the Criminal Code (Minor Defamation) if the Prosecutor solely charged Article 310 of the Criminal Code (Slander)?**

**A:** Yes, it is permitted. Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 818 K/Pid/1984, although ordinary defamation under Article 310 KUHP is charged in the indictment, the Defendant can be found guilty and punished under Article 315 KUHP (Minor Defamation) if proven acts constitute minor defamation without specific act imputations. This is permissible because Article 315 KUHP is a homogenous (homogen) offense carrying a lighter statutory penalty than Article 310 KUHP.
