# JPW-BRIEFING-003

**Sixth Diplomatic Briefing Information Package of the International Criminal Court** (*Sixième réunion d’information de la Cour pénale internationale à l’intention du corps diplomatique - Document d’information*) is an official policy document and operational report published in March 2006 in The Hague.

## Summary

This document contains a summary of the ICC's activities and major developments since the Fourth Session of the Assembly of States Parties (ASP). Key developments include the surrender of the first suspect (Thomas Lubanga Dyilo), the adoption of the Regulations of the Registry, the constitution of Pre-Trial Chambers, as well as evaluation of investigations in the DRC, Uganda, Darfur, Iraq, and Venezuela \[1–2, 8, 10, 14–19\].

## Legal Basis

- [Rome Statute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rome_Statute): Article 8 (Prosecution of war crimes regarding the enlistment of child soldiers).
- [Rules of Procedure and Evidence](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rules_of_Procedure_and_Evidence): Rule 14(1) (Legal basis for adopting the Regulations of the Registry).

## Background

The Sixth Diplomatic Briefing was held on 23 March 2006 in The Hague to share periodic updates with the diplomatic corps regarding trial operational readiness and ICC budget management.

## Objectives

- Present operational readiness reports for the first trial and detention center.
- Report on investigation progress in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC), Uganda, and Darfur situational areas \[14–18\].
- Explain the adoption of the Regulations of the Registry and supporting organizational structures such as the OPCV.

## Key Provisions

- \*\*Thomas Lubanga Dyilo Case\*\*: Unsealing of the arrest warrant on 17 March 2006 and first appearance on 20 March 2006 under Article 8 war crimes charges \[1–2\].
- \*\*Regulations of the Registry\*\*: Approved by the Presidency on 6 March 2006 pursuant to Rule 14(1) of the RPE.
- \*\*Presidency Composition\*\*: Election of Philippe Kirsch (President), Akua Kuenyehia (1st VP), and René Blattmann (2nd VP) on 11 March 2006.
- \*\*Iraq & Venezuela Situation Analysis\*\*: The OTP announced that statutory criteria were not met to open investigations.

## Implementation

The Presidency constituted Pre-Trial Chambers I, II, and III. The Registry operationalized the detention facility in Scheveningen, launched a training program for Acholi, Swahili, and Lingala language paraprofessional interpreters, and managed a List of Counsel containing 136 names \[24–25\].

## Policy Issues

- Finalization of the Headquarters Agreement negotiations with the Host State.
- Management of remaining budget contribution arrears by certain States Parties.
