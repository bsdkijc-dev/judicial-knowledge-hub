## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 63 K/Pdt/1987 dated August 15, 1988, establishes settled jurisprudential legal rules regarding commercial transactions paid using empty \*Bilyet Giro\* instruments and the determination of moratory interest damages:

- **Breach of Contract via Empty Bilyet Giro:** In cases where a Defendant pays for purchased goods using a \*Bilyet Giro\* that turns out to have no funds (\*kosong\*), such conduct is classified as a breach of contract (\*wanprestasi\*), and the Defendant incurs a debt or loan obligation toward the Plaintiff equal to the purchase price of the goods.
- **Moratory Interest of 6% per Annum:** Regarding damages for payment delays that were not previously agreed upon, the amount of damages is calculated based on the statutory moratory interest rate of 6% (six percent) per year, calculated from the date the lawsuit is registered at the District Court until the debt is paid in full.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Respondent for Cassation (Susanto / Soesanto) sold goods in the form of cigarettes to the Original Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation (\[Party A\]) valued at Rp 5,352,500. For this transaction, the Defendant paid using 10 (ten) pieces of \*Bilyet Giro\* issued by Bank Surya Indonesia, Surabaya Central Office, totaling Rp 5,352,500. However, upon presentation for clearing, all of the \*Bilyet Giro\* instruments turned out to have no funds (\*kosong\*). Although the Plaintiff repeatedly issued verbal and written reminders and demands, the Defendant failed to settle the debt.

The Plaintiff filed a breach of contract lawsuit at the Surabaya District Court (Case No. 406/Pdt/G/1984/PN.Sby). The Surabaya District Court rendered a decision on February 18, 1985, granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part, declaring the Defendant in breach of contract, ordering the Defendant to pay the debt of Rp 5,352,500 in cash, and declaring the security confiscation valid and enforceable. Dissatisfied with the ruling, the Surabaya High Court (Decision No. 393/Pdt/1986/PT.Sby dated July 23, 1986) amended the appellate judgment by adding an order for interest amounting to 1.5% per month starting from May 1984. The Defendant (\[Party A\]) subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  When a Defendant pays for the purchase price of goods using a \*Bilyet Giro\* that proves to have no funds (\*kosong\*), it signifies that the Defendant has committed a breach of contract and holds a debt or loan obligation toward the Plaintiff equal to the price of such goods.
2.  Regarding damages for the buyer's delay in payment, such damages constitute compensation based on unagreed interest, namely 6% per annum in accordance with established settled jurisprudence of the Supreme Court.
3.  The \*judex facti\* ruling of the Surabaya High Court setting interest at 1.5% per month in the absence of a prior agreement lacks legal basis and contradicts settled Supreme Court jurisprudence, and must therefore be annulled.

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation \[Party A\]; Annulling the Surabaya High Court decision dated July 23, 1986, No. 393/Pdt/1986/PT.Sby and the Surabaya District Court decision dated February 18, 1985, No. 406/Pdt/G/1984; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part; (2) Declaring that the Defendant has committed a breach of contract (\*ingkar janji\*) toward the Plaintiff; (3) Ordering the Defendant to pay a debt to the Plaintiff amounting to Rp 5,352,500 (five million three hundred fifty-two thousand five hundred rupiahs) in cash and in a lump sum; (4) Declaring that the security confiscation executed by the Substitute Bailiff of the Surabaya District Court on September 14, 1984, pursuant to the determination dated September 12, 1984, is valid and enforceable; (5) Ordering the Defendant to pay damages of 6% per annum to the Plaintiff from the date the lawsuit was registered at the District Court until the debt is paid in full; (6) Rejecting the remainder of the Plaintiff's lawsuit; (7) Ordering the Petitioner for Cassation to pay procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction (assessed at Rp 20,000 at the cassation level).
