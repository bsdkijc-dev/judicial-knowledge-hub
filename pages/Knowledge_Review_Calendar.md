# Knowledge Review Calendar

Knowledge objects should have scheduled review dates appropriate to their volatility and risk.

## Suggested Review Priority

- high-risk legal content — frequent review;
- AI governance — periodic review;
- strategic initiatives — milestone review;
- historical material — review when new evidence appears;
- stable definitions — scheduled review.

## Metadata

Every page should record:

- last reviewed;
- next review;
- reviewer;
- status.
