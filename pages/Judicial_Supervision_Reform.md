# Judicial Supervision Reform

# Judicial Supervision Reform

The Blueprint identifies supervision as a central component of judicial accountability.

## Strategic Issues

- supervision organization;
- supervision workload;
- complaints;
- human resources;
- budget;
- expertise;
- interaction with external oversight.

## Desired Capability

Supervision requires credible institutions supported by competent and ethical personnel.
