# CONCEPT-0003

# Judicial Independence

**Judicial independence** concerns the ability of judges and judicial institutions to perform adjudicative functions without improper interference or influence.

Its precise meaning and institutional safeguards depend on the applicable constitutional and legal framework.

**\[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## Dimensions

Judicial independence may be analyzed at several levels:

- Institutional independence
- Individual judicial independence
- Administrative independence
- Financial independence
- Decision-making independence
- Structural independence

These dimensions may overlap but should not automatically be treated as identical.

## Institutional Independence

Institutional independence concerns the relationship between the judiciary and other institutions of government and society.

Relevant questions may include:

- Governance
- Appointment
- Tenure
- Budget
- Administration
- Accountability
- External influence

## Individual Independence

Individual judicial independence concerns the ability of judges to decide cases impartially and according to applicable law and evidence.

## Judicial Independence and Accountability

Independence does not necessarily eliminate accountability.

A judicial system may require mechanisms that preserve independence while addressing:

- Misconduct
- Conflicts of interest
- Administrative failures
- Performance
- Ethical obligations

The appropriate balance depends on the applicable legal framework.

## Judicial Independence and AI

AI systems used in judicial environments should not improperly interfere with judicial decision-making.

Relevant governance questions include:

- Who controls the system?
- Who supplies the data?
- Who determines system objectives?
- Can outputs be audited?
- Can judges understand relevant limitations?
- Who is accountable for errors?
- Can judges reject automated recommendations?
