# AI-Assisted Wiki Publishing

# AI-Assisted Wiki Publishing

AI may assist with extraction, structuring, summarisation and MediaWiki formatting.

## Workflow

**Source → Extract → Structure → Verify → Human Approve → Publish**

## Rule

AI-generated content must not automatically acquire authoritative status merely because it has been published.
