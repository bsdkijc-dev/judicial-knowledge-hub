# Judicial Knowledge Wiki as Strategic Initiative

# Judicial Knowledge Wiki

The Judicial Knowledge Wiki is one of the proposed strategic initiatives under Judicial Knowledge Infrastructure.

## Strategic Function

It provides a structured environment for:

- judicial knowledge curation;
- institutional memory;
- legal concepts;
- case-law knowledge;
- policy knowledge;
- training knowledge;
- machine-readable relationships.
