# Initiative Risk Tier Matrix

# Initiative Risk Tier Matrix

Initiatives should be classified according to potential institutional and societal harm.

| Tier     | General Characteristic                          | Governance               |
|----------|-------------------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| Low      | Limited consequence                             | Standard controls        |
| Moderate | Material operational impact                     | Enhanced review          |
| High     | Significant rights/institutional impact         | Formal oversight         |
| Critical | Potential systemic or fundamental-rights impact | Highest-level governance |

## Rule

The actual classification must be determined through a documented risk assessment rather than assumed from the technology label.
