# Initiative Costing

# Initiative Costing

Initiative costing estimates the resources required throughout the initiative lifecycle.

## Cost Categories

- design;
- development;
- infrastructure;
- licensing;
- implementation;
- training;
- cybersecurity;
- maintenance;
- evaluation;
- retirement.

## Principle

Total cost of ownership should be considered rather than only initial procurement cost.
