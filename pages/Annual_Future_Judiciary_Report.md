# Annual Future Judiciary Report

The Annual Future Judiciary Report is proposed as a recurring institutional assessment of emerging developments and transformation.

## Potential Contents

- strategic signals;
- innovation progress;
- technology developments;
- AI governance;
- competency;
- data maturity;
- risks;
- international benchmarking;
- recommendations.

## Function

The report supports an annual strategic learning cycle.
