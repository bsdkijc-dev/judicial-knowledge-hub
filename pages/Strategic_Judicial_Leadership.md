# Strategic Judicial Leadership

Strategic judicial leadership provides direction for long-term transformation while preserving judicial authority.

## Responsibilities

- strategic direction;
- institutional priorities;
- governance;
- accountability;
- resource alignment;
- protection of judicial independence.
