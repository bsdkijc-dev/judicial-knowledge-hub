## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 04 K/Pdt.Pen/2009 establishes the constitutional legal principle that the Supreme Court and the General Judiciary lack the legal authority to evaluate, correct, or review decisions rendered by other judicial bodies such as the Constitutional Court (MK). Although the Supreme Court acknowledged the Petitioner's plight regarding disqualification from regional elections resembling a form of "civil death," the Supreme Court, in exercising its jurisdiction, cannot exceed the powers conferred upon it by statute.

## Case Background

The Petitioner for Cassation (H. \[Party A\], S.H., paired with H. Hartawan, S.H.) won the South Bengkulu Regency regional election for the 2009–2014 term based on South Bengkulu KPUD Decree No. 59 of 2008 dated December 10, 2008, securing 51.7% of the vote. The losing candidates (H. Reskan Effendi and Drh. Rohidin Mersyah, M.M.A.) filed an objection petition at the Constitutional Court (Reg. No. 57/PHPU.D-VI/2008). On January 8, 2009, the Constitutional Court annulled the regional election results and ordered a revote without including the Petitioner. The Petitioner subsequently petitioned the Manna District Court to declare the Constitutional Court's decision null and void by law and treated as never having existed. The Manna District Court, via Determination No. 02/Pdt.P/2009/PN.Mn dated May 11, 2009, declared the Petitioner's request inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*). Dissatisfied with this determination, the Petitioner filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** The Supreme Court and the General Judiciary possess no authority to evaluate, correct, or review decisions of other judicial institutions like the Constitutional Court because statutory law grants no such power. While the Supreme Court empathizes with the Petitioner's predicament where disqualification effectively results in civil death, the Supreme Court, in executing its functions as a General Judiciary institution, cannot exceed its statutory limits. Regarding the enjoyment and loss of civic rights, the established legal maxim dictates that "No punishment shall result in Civil Death or the loss of all civil rights."
- **Ruling:** Rejecting the cassation petition filed by Petitioner for Cassation H. \[Party A\], S.H., and ordering the Petitioner for Cassation to bear the court costs at the cassation level.
