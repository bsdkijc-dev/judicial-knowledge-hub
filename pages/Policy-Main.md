# Policy:Main

The **Judicial Policy Portal** serves as the primary gateway for Supreme Court regulations, guidelines, and benchbooks. Every active researcher and presiding judge begins here.

## Supreme Court Benchbooks

Benchbooks represent authorized, standard operating criteria for procedural matters:

- **[Civil Case Management Benchbook (Latest 2026 Edition)](Policy-Civil_Case_Management.md)**: Authoritative directory managing pretrial timelines and remote deposition.
- **[Criminal Trial Protocol Benchbook](Policy-Criminal_Trial_Protocol.md)**: Guidelines for electronic evidence chain-of-custody, SHA-256 hash validation, and remote detention rules.

## SEMA Administrative Circulars

View and track administrative directives:

- **[SEMA No. 2 of 2026: AI Speech-to-Text Transcriptions](Policy-SEMA_02_2026.md)**: Instructions for validating draft transcripts from automated transcription arrays.
- **[SEMA No. 1 of 2026: Remote Signature Validation](Policy-SEMA_01_2026.md)**: Verification criteria for digital notary stamps on third-party cloud engines.
