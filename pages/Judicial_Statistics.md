# Judicial Statistics

# Judicial Statistics

**Judicial statistics** are quantitative information describing judicial cases, institutions, activities, resources, or outcomes.

## Common Indicators

| Indicator      | General Meaning                                             |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Incoming Cases | Cases received during a defined period                      |
| Disposed Cases | Cases resolved during a defined period                      |
| Pending Cases  | Cases remaining unresolved at a defined point               |
| Clearance Rate | Relationship between dispositions and incoming cases        |
| Backlog        | Accumulated unresolved workload under a defined methodology |
| Duration       | Time associated with case processing                        |

Definitions must be established before comparisons are made.

## Statistical Integrity

Reliable judicial statistics require:

- Clear definitions
- Consistent methodology
- Complete data
- Quality controls
- Reproducibility
- Versioning
- Provenance

## Comparative Statistics

Cross-court or cross-country comparison must account for:

- Different case definitions
- Different procedures
- Different reporting periods
- Different workloads
- Different institutional structures

## AI and Judicial Statistics

AI may support:

- Trend analysis
- Forecasting
- Anomaly detection
- Visualization
- Classification

Statistical outputs remain dependent on data quality.
