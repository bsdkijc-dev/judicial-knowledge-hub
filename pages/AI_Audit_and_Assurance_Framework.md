# AI Audit and Assurance Framework

AI assurance should combine multiple disciplines.

## Audit Domains

- technical testing;
- legal review;
- data-quality assessment;
- cybersecurity testing;
- user evaluation;
- human-rights assessment where applicable.

## Risk-Based Audit

Controls should correspond to system risk.

## Independent Review

High-impact systems should, where feasible, receive independent external scrutiny in addition to internal assurance.
