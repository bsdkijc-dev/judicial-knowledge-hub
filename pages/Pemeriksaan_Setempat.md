# Pemeriksaan Setempat

**Pemeriksaan setempat** refers to a judicial inspection or observation conducted at a location relevant to a dispute.

The legal basis, procedure, evidentiary status, authority, participants, documentation, and consequences must be determined from applicable Indonesian law and judicial practice.

**\[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## Potential Purpose

A site inspection may assist a court in understanding relevant physical circumstances of a dispute.

The inspection should be distinguished from the legal determination of the dispute itself.

## Knowledge Object

A structured record may include:

- Case
- Court
- Date
- Location
- Participants
- Object inspected
- Questions examined
- Observations
- Documentation
- Photographs
- Measurements
- Relevant evidence
- Procedural consequences

## Digital Documentation

Potential technologies include:

- Geotagged photographs
- Video
- Digital maps
- Measurements
- 3D representations
- GIS
- Digital inspection records

Use of these technologies must comply with applicable legal, privacy, evidentiary, and procedural requirements.

## Evidence Relationship

The knowledge model may be represented as:

**CASE** → **REQUIRES FACTUAL CLARIFICATION** → **SITE INSPECTION** → **OBSERVATION** → **DOCUMENTATION** → **JUDICIAL ASSESSMENT**

This model does not itself establish evidentiary effect.

## Mediation Context

The potential relationship between site inspection and mediation should be separately analyzed under applicable procedural rules.

**\[LEGAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## Pre-Trial Context

The potential use of site inspection in pre-trial or preliminary procedures should likewise be established from authoritative sources.

**\[LEGAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## AI and Site Inspection

AI or computer vision may potentially assist with:

- Image organization
- Object identification
- Change detection
- Mapping
- Document classification

AI output should remain subject to human verification.
