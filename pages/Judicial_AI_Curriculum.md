# Judicial AI Curriculum

# Judicial AI Curriculum

The **Judicial AI Curriculum** is a structured educational framework for developing AI competencies among judicial professionals.

## Core Modules

| Module | Focus                  |
|--------|------------------------|
| 1      | AI Fundamentals        |
| 2      | Generative AI          |
| 3      | Prompt Literacy        |
| 4      | Legal Research with AI |
| 5      | AI Verification        |
| 6      | Data Literacy          |
| 7      | Privacy and Security   |
| 8      | AI Ethics              |
| 9      | AI Risk Assessment     |
| 10     | Judicial AI Governance |

## Advanced Modules

Potential advanced subjects:

- Knowledge graphs
- Retrieval-augmented generation
- AI evaluation
- Algorithmic auditing
- AI procurement
- Model governance
- AI impact assessment

## Role-Based Tracks

**JUDGES**

Professional judgment + verification + ethics

**RESEARCHERS**

Research + prompting + retrieval + evidence

**ADMINISTRATORS**

Workflow + governance + procurement

**IT**

Architecture + security + monitoring

**LEADERS**

Strategy + governance + transformation
