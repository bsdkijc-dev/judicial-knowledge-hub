## Description / Deskripsi

Provides a comprehensive pedagogical curriculum design for admiralty and maritime advocacy, integrating doctrinal mastery of federal maritime statutes (Jones Act, COGSA, Limitation of Liability Act), digital forensic evidentiary mechanics (AIS telemetry, VDR audio logs, ARPA radar), multi-module instruction, and a capstone interscholastic simulation involving the M/V Pacific Dragon casualty\[cite: 9\].

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-025](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-025): Admiralty litigation differs fundamentally from general civil litigation due to complex jurisdictional mechanisms, historical absence of jury trials in \*in rem\* proceedings, and statutory exceptions like the Saving to Suitors clause\[cite: 9\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-026](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-026): Modern maritime disputes require advocates to master both complex legal doctrines and digital marine forensics (AIS, VDR, ARPA radar), creating a dual-literacy demand that traditional legal curricula often fail to address\[cite: 9\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-027](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-027): Procedural friction between federal admiralty limitation injunctions (Supplemental Rule F) and state court personal injury claims demands sophisticated multi-forum strategic agility\[cite: 9\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-027](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-027): Indonesian maritime legal education integrates statutory shipping regulations with practical navigation and casualty investigation standards to prepare practitioners for commercial admiralty disputes\[cite: 9\].

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-027](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-027): Advanced maritime legal training programs deploy experiential learning models combining technical navigation labs, expert witness management, and appellate moot court competitions\[cite: 9\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-008](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-008): Traditional law school curricula rarely provide structured, experiential training on laying evidentiary foundations for electronic marine telemetry and navigating technical expert testimonies under FRE 702\[cite: 9\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Implement a seven-module experiential curriculum spanning admiralty jurisdiction, personal injury, COGSA cargo carriage, Rule F limitation petitions, COLREGS collision forensics, expert witness management, and interscholastic appellate advocacy\[cite: 9\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-025](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-025): Integrate digital navigation and forensic labs into advocacy training, focusing on AIS telemetry, VDR audio authentication, and electronic chart plotting\[cite: 9\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-026](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-026): Execute simulated courtroom practicums and problem-based learning exercises modeled on complex historical maritime casualties\[cite: 9\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-027](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-027): Conclude training with a multi-stage capstone simulation (The M/V Pacific Dragon Casualty) combining trial advocacy and Supreme Court-formatted appellate argument\[cite: 9\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-027](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-027): 14-week intensive maritime advocacy clinic incorporating technical navigation data labs, bench mooting workshops, and interscholastic competition preparation\[cite: 9\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-027](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-027): 100% student proficiency in laying evidentiary foundations for digital marine telemetry; successful execution of complex limitation of liability and COGSA trial arguments\[cite: 9\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Advancement of specialized maritime legal education standards, aligning academic advocacy training with national competition requirements like the Judge John R. Brown Admiralty Moot Court Competition\[cite: 9\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Standardization of procedural rules governing multi-forum admiralty litigation, electronic evidence admissibility, and marine casualty investigations\[cite: 9\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Experiential problem-based learning combined with technical navigation labs bridges the gap between theoretical admiralty law and high-stakes courtroom execution\[cite: 9\].
- Standardized 100-point rubric scoring and statistical scaling successfully eliminate grading variance in national appellate brief evaluations\[cite: 9\].
