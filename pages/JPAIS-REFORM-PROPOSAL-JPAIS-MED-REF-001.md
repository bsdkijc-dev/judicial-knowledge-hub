# JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:JPAIS-MED-REF-001

## Summary / Ringkasan

## Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

- Entity ID: JPAIS-MED-REF-001
- Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
- Ingestion Status: Confirmed

## Description / Deskripsi

Transforms Indonesian court-connected mediation by replacing the solo mediator model with a **Hybrid Mediation Panel** (*Majelis Mediasi Hibrida*) for high-value civil claims, commercial disputes, construction cases, and agrarian conflicts. The panel pairs 1 internal Judge Mediator (responsible for formal legal compliance and homologation enforceability) with 1 or 2 accredited Non-Judge Expert Commissioners (possessing technical expertise in engineering, accounting, business law, or customary practice).

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-001): Court mediation in general civil district courts suffers from structural ritualization (*tick-box formalism*) with stagnant success rates hovering between 8% and 12%.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-002): Career judges suffer from severe role overload, simultaneously carrying adjudicative burdens and conducting superficial 15–20 minute mediation sessions\[cite: 1\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-003): External non-judge professional mediators experience systemic exclusion from court-annexed mediation due to the absence of public budget compensation (DIPA), resulting in a zero-cost bias toward free judge mediators\[cite: 1\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-001): Mandatory universal court mediation under PERMA No. 1 of 2016 relies on a solo mediator model operating within a rigid 30+30 day post-filing window\[cite: 1\].

## Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-001): Under the Civil Conciliation Act (1951), Japan utilizes tripartite Civil Conciliation Committees (*Chōtei Iinkai*—1 Conciliation Judge + 2 Professional Lay Commissioners) achieving success rates exceeding 45% to 50%\[cite: 1\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-001): Absence of a primary statutory act providing permanent fiscal appropriations for non-judge conciliation commissioners\[cite: 1\].
- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-002): Lack of an autonomous judicial ADR governance directorate within the Supreme Court\[cite: 1\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Institutionalize Hybrid Mediation Panels mandatorily for commercial and land disputes exceeding Rp 200 million in metropolitan and class IA courts, and facultatively in class IB and II courts\[cite: 1\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-001): Promulgate SK KMA pilot decree, establish steering committee, and configure SIPP workflow\[cite: 1\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-002): Deploy Hybrid Panels across designated pilot courts and implement preliminary assessment protocols\[cite: 1\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-003): Audit performance metrics and draft permanent PERMA\[cite: 1\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-001): 6-month pilot across PN Jakarta Pusat, PN Surabaya, PN Sleman, PN Makassar, PA Jakarta Selatan, and PA Bandung\[cite: 1\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-001): Substantive Settlement Rate (SSR) \$\ge 25\\\$ in general courts and \$\ge 60\\\$ in religious courts\[cite: 1\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Issue Supreme Court Leadership Decree (SK KMA) and integrate expert commissioner rosters into SIPP and court fee surcharges\[cite: 1\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgate revised PERMA on Integrated Mediation and Conciliation superseding PERMA No. 1 of 2016\[cite: 1\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- District court general civil mediation success rate ranges persistently between 8% and 12%\[cite: 1\].
- Japanese conciliation committees achieve \>45–50% settlement rates\[cite: 1\].
