# Judicial Knowledge Wiki

The Judicial Knowledge Wiki is a structured institutional repository for preserving, connecting, and discovering judicial knowledge.

## Strategic Functions

- institutional memory;
- legal research;
- judicial education;
- policy analysis;
- knowledge sharing;
- semantic retrieval;
- AI-assisted knowledge discovery.

## Architecture

**Sources**

→ **Knowledge Objects**

→ **Ontology**

→ **Knowledge Graph**

→ **Semantic Search**

→ **AI Applications**

## Governance

The Wiki does not replace authoritative legal sources.

It provides a structured layer for discovering and connecting them.
