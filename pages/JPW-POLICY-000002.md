# JPW-POLICY-000002

The **Sustainable Buddhist Cultural Community Tourism Framework** (Stable ID: `JPW-POLICY-000002`) is a community-based development initiative established for Mueang Chonburi District, Chonburi Province, Thailand. Authored by Phramaha Suchat Thammakamo of Mahachulalongkornrajavidyalaya University, the framework outlines strategies for developing tourist activities, building collaborative stakeholder networks, and upgrading physical amenities grounded in local Buddhist heritage. Note: Specific judicial policies and legal regulations are *Not found in the available sources*.

## Summary

The framework evaluates four core dimensions of Buddhist cultural tourism: overall potential (\$\bar{X} = 4.09\$), activity development (\$\bar{X} = 4.24\$), collaboration networks (\$\bar{X} = 3.48\$), and tourism elevation (\$\bar{X} = 3.67\$). It integrates the 3S Principles (Stories, Senses, Sophistication) and 6A's tourism components to create a sustainable model for local economic and spiritual development.

## Legal Basis

- **Statutory / Constitutional Basis:** *Not found in the available sources*.
- **Institutional Basis:** Research conducted under Mahachulalongkornrajavidyalaya University; approved and published in the *Journal of Multidisciplinary Academic Research and Development*, Vol. 7 No. 3 (2025).

## Background & Policy Problems

### Background

Community-based tourism serves as a tool for sustainable local development, cultural preservation, and income generation in Thailand. However, local initiatives often face obstacles due to shifting tourist expectations, insufficient network integration, and lack of structured management linking activities to Buddhist ways of life.

### Policy Problems

- Moderate network collaboration level (\$\bar{X} = 3.48\$) requiring improved communication and central data systems.
- Inadequate public transportation and signage for religious sites.
- Need for structured human resource training for local guides and monks.

## Objectives

1\. Assess the potential and routes of Buddhist community tourism in Mueang Chonburi District. 2. Develop tourist activities centered on local Buddhist culture. 3. Enhance collaboration networks among community members, temples, government, and educational sectors. 4. Elevate overall tourism quality through accessibility, amenities, and sustainable management.

## Scope & Implementation

### Scope

Applies strictly to Mueang Chonburi District, Chonburi Province, Thailand.

### Key Provisions

- **3S Activity Development:** Focus on local storytellers (Stories, \$\bar{X} = 4.37\$), Buddhist teachings and crafts (Sophistication, \$\bar{X} = 4.25\$), and unique local festivals such as the Buffalo Racing Festival (Senses, \$\bar{X} = 4.12\$).
- **Network & Information System:** Establishment of a central database, online platform, and local multi-stakeholder committee.
- **Eco-Tourism & Facilities:** Waste sorting, reduction of single-use plastics, bilingual signage (Thai/English), clean restrooms, and disabled access (ramps and elevators).

### Implementation

- **Quantitative Survey:** Data collected from 400 Mueang Chonburi residents between March and June 2024 using the Taro Yamane formula and cluster sampling.
- **Qualitative Interviews:** In-depth interviews with 25 key informants (tourism experts, network chairs, cultural influencers) conducted July–August 2024 via purposive sampling.

## Reform

- Formation of localized joint committees representing monks, community leaders, schools, and local businesses.
- Launch of central information portals and specialized ad-hoc learning groups.
