# Juvenile Diversion Indicator

# Juvenile Diversion Performance Indicator

The indicator measures successful resolution of juvenile cases through diversion.

## Eligibility

The Renstra identifies eligible cases as those carrying a maximum imprisonment threat below seven years and which are not repeat offences.

## Successful Diversion

Success requires:

- a determination that diversion was successful; and
- a judicial determination terminating examination of the case.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Juvenile cases successfully resolved through diversion}} {\text{Juvenile cases completing diversion}}\times100\\ \</math\>
