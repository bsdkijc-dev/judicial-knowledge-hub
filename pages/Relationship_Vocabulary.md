# Relationship Vocabulary

# Controlled Relationship Vocabulary

The knowledge graph should use a controlled set of relationships.

- applies to
- interprets
- amends
- repeals
- supersedes
- cites
- concerns
- decides
- implements
- supports
- depends on
- owned by
- measured by
- evaluated by
- derived from
- related to
- contradicts
- updates

## Rule

Relationships should be explicit rather than inferred merely from co-occurrence.
