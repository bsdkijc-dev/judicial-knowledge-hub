# Judicial Asset Management Reform

The Blueprint identifies asset management as an important support function.

## Strategic Issues

- court locations;
- accessibility;
- court buildings;
- asset management;
- procurement;
- state asset information.

## Knowledge Principle

Asset information should be:

**Structured → Updated → Traceable → Auditable**
