## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 972 K/Pid/2000, dated November 30, 2000, established fundamental material criminal law and criminal procedural rules concerning the proof of the element "unlawful ownership" (\*memiliki secara melawan hukum\*) in the crime of embezzlement (Article 372 KUHP) and the admissibility of cassation against impure acquittals (\*niet-zuivere vrijspraak\*):

When the element of unlawful ownership is proven, the Defendant is legally and convincingly proven guilty of committing the crime of "Embezzlement" as charged by the Public Prosecutor, and sentencing must be imposed. The Defendant's act of leasing out another party's warehouse without permission and utilizing the rental proceeds for personal use is qualified as realizing a decision to act as an owner (\*zich toe-eigenen\*). The existence of an ongoing civil dispute lacking final and binding legal force does not eliminate the unlawful nature of the Defendant's acts, because prior to any court ruling establishing the Defendant as lawful owner, the registered rights holder (SHGB) must remain regarded as the lawful owner. The Supreme Court annulled Southeast Sulawesi High Court Decision No. 04/Pid/2000/PT.Sultra and adjudicated afresh by sentencing the Defendant to 2 (two) months' imprisonment with a 4-month probation period.

## Executive Summary

The central legal principle established in this decision is that leasing another person's registered property without authorization and keeping the proceeds constitutes unlawful embezzlement (\*zich toe-eigenen\*), which cannot be excused by pending civil claims.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Unauthorized leasing of another party's registered property constitutes criminal embezzlement (\*zich toe-eigenen\*).
- Pending civil disputes lacking finality do not strip registered land rights holders of their lawful ownership status.
- Appellate judgments improperly acquitting defendants based on flawed legal interpretations of criminal elements constitute impure acquittals (\*niet-zuivere vrijspraak\*) reviewable on cassation under Article 244 KUHAP.

## Case Background

The Original Defendant / Respondent for Cassation, **\[Defendant\]** (\[age removed\], entrepreneur), was charged with embezzlement (Article 372 KUHP) for leasing a warehouse in Jembatan Batu, Bau-Bau, on April 9, 1998, to \[Lessee\] for Rp 8,500,000.— per year without permission from witness \[Party C\] (the Defendant's younger sibling / lawful owner holding SHGB No. 91/1989) and utilizing the rental proceeds for his child's medical expenses in Surabaya.

The Bau-Bau District Court (Decision No. 156/Pid.B/1999/PN.BB dated August 5, 1999) convicted the Defendant to 2 months' imprisonment with a 4-month probation period. The Southeast Sulawesi High Court in Kendari (Decision No. 04/Pid/2000/PT.Sultra dated March 4, 2000) annulled the district court decision and acquitted the Defendant (\*vrijspraak\*) on the grounds that the leasing act lacked unlawful intent because the warehouse involved a pending civil dispute regarding Irian Store. The Public Prosecutor at the Bau-Bau State Prosecutor's Office filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Construction of the Element "Unlawful Ownership" (\*Zich Toe-eigenen\*) in Embezzlement (Article 372 KUHP)

The primary issue was whether leasing a building/warehouse under a Building Land Rights Certificate (\*SHGB\*) registered in another person's name to a third party without the owner's permission, while using the rental proceeds for personal use, satisfies the element of "unlawful ownership" (\*zich toe-eigenen\*) under Article 372 of the Criminal Code.

The Supreme Court held that it satisfies the element. Pursuant to criminal law doctrine (Hoge Raad arrests of October 16, 1905, and March 26, 1906), the concept of "ownership" (\*toe-eigenen\*) refers to actions where a property holder controls or acts as if they are the owner of the property. When the Defendant leased another person's warehouse and received/enjoyed rental proceeds of Rp 8,500,000.— per year without the registered owner's consent, the Defendant manifested a concrete decision to exercise unlawful control over the property (\*zich toe-eigenen\*).

### Admissibility of Prosecutor's Cassation Petition Against Impure Acquittals (\*Niet-Zuivere Vrijspraak\* ex Article 244 KUHAP)

The issue evaluated whether a Public Prosecutor's cassation petition is admissible against a High Court judgment acquitting a Defendant on grounds that the Defendant's acts related to an ongoing civil dispute.

The Supreme Court held that it is Admissible. When a lower court's acquittal (\*vrijspraak\*) rests upon a flawed interpretation of the criminal elements contained in the indictment (rather than a failure to prove material acts), the acquittal is categorized as an impure acquittal (\*niet-zuivere vrijspraak\*). The Supreme Court possesses authority to examine and correct such legal misapplications pursuant to Article 244 of the KUHAP.

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Did the \*Judex Facti\* of Southeast Sulawesi High Court in Kendari misapply the law (\*foutieve kepassing van het recht\*) by narrowly interpreting the element of "unlawful ownership" under Article 372 KUHP and acquitting the Defendant on civil dispute grounds, despite SHGB No. 91 being validly registered in the victim witness's name?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes, the Southeast Sulawesi High Court misapplied the law. The Supreme Court annulled High Court Decision No. 04/Pid/2000/PT.Sultra and adjudicated afresh, declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] proven legally and convincingly guilty of committing the crime of "Embezzlement" ex Article 372 KUHP.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Bau-Bau District Court & Southeast Sulawesi High Court)

Examination of facts concerning the leasing of the Jembatan Batu warehouse in Wale Village, Buton Regency, on April 9, 1998, to witness \[Lessee\] (via Yanto Alexander) for Rp 8,500,000.— per year; documentary evidence of SHGB No. 91 of 1989 and original purchase receipts dated December 22, 1987, valued at Rp 27,500,000.— in the name of \[Party C\]; utilization of rental funds by the Defendant for his child's medical expenses in Surabaya; and civil reconvention lawsuit case files No. 29/Pdt.G/1997/PN.BB jo. No. 62/Pdt.G/99.PT.Sultra.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The appellate \*Judex Facti\* of Southeast Sulawesi High Court misapplied the law (constituting an impure acquittal reviewable via cassation ex Article 244 KUHAP).
2.  The element of unlawful ownership (\*zich toe-eigenen\*) was proven legally and convincingly because the Defendant realized a decision to act as owner by leasing out the warehouse without the lawful owner's consent and using rental proceeds for personal use.
3.  Prior to any court decision establishing the Defendant as owner, \[Party C\] remained legally regarded as the lawful owner pursuant to SHGB No. 91.
4.  The Supreme Court annulled the High Court decision and adjudicated afresh, convicting the Defendant of Embezzlement (Article 372 KUHP) and sentencing him to 2 months' imprisonment with a 4-month probation period.

## Ruling

The Supreme Court ruled: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation (**Public Prosecutor at the Bau-Bau State Prosecutor's Office**); Annulling Southeast Sulawesi High Court Decision No. 04/Pid/2000/PT.Sultra dated March 4, 2000; and ADJUDICATING AFRESH: 1. Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] proven legally and convincingly guilty of committing the crime of "Embezzlement"; 2. Sentencing the Defendant to imprisonment for 2 (two) months; 3. Stipulating that the sentence need not be served unless a final and binding court decision determines otherwise prior to the expiration of the 4-month probation period; 4. Ordering the return of evidence (SHGB Warehouse & rental receipts attached; original warehouse purchase receipt dated Dec 22, 1987, returned to \[Party C\]); and charging cassation court costs amounting to Rp 2,500.— to the Defendant.

## Evidence Status and Gaps

### Established by Evidence

- Defendant \[Defendant\] leased the warehouse in Jembatan Batu, Bau-Bau, on April 9, 1998, to \[Lessee\] for Rp 8,500,000.— per year.
- The disputed warehouse was proven to be the lawful property of witness \[Party C\] based on Building Land Rights Certificate No. 91 of 1989 and original purchase receipts dated December 22, 1987.
- Defendant leased the warehouse without consent from \[Party C\] and enjoyed rental proceeds for personal purposes.
- Defendant's acts satisfied all elements of the crime of "Embezzlement" under Article 372 KUHP.

### Not Established / Set Aside

- Defendant's defense asserting that the warehouse constituted joint assets of Irian Store or immune to criminal liability due to pending civil disputes was set aside and rejected by the Supreme Court because the Defendant lacked valid ownership proof while SHGB registration remained validly vested in \[Party C\].

### Uncertainty and Gaps

- Separation Between Civil Disputes and Criminal Liability: This landmark ruling establishes vital guidance ensuring that unadjudicated civil claims cannot serve as a "shield" to justify physical possession or unauthorized leasing of certified property without permission from registered owners.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: Can someone who leases out a building under an SHGB registered in another person's name without permission be convicted of embezzlement (Article 372 KUHP) even if claiming the building is subject to a pending civil dispute?**

**A:** Yes, they can be convicted. Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 972 K/Pid/2000, leasing out another party's building/warehouse without permission and utilizing rental proceeds for personal use satisfies the element of "unlawful ownership" (\*zich toe-eigenen\*) under Article 372 KUHP. Prior to any final court decision establishing the Defendant as owner, the registered rights holder (SHGB) must remain regarded as the lawful owner.
