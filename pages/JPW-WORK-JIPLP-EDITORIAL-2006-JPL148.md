# JPW-WORK-JIPLP-EDITORIAL-2006-JPL148

**Editorial: Should forum-shopping be a basket case?** is a legal editorial authored by [Jeremy Phillips](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Jeremy_Phillips) and published in the [Journal of Intellectual Property Law & Practice](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Journal_of_Intellectual_Property_Law_%26_Practice) (Vol. 1, No. 11) by [Oxford University Press](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) on September 6, 2006 [Editorial Publication](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Editorial_Publication) \[1–10\].

## Executive Summary

This editorial critically evaluates the stance of the [European Court of Justice](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/European_Court_of_Justice) (ECJ) in \*Roche Nederland v Primus and Goldenberg\*, which rejected patent plaintiffs' practice of *forum-shopping* under the [Brussels Convention](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Brussels_Convention) [Forum Shopping Critique](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Forum_Shopping_Critique) \[1–10\]. The author argues that forcing separate actions in each defendant's home jurisdiction hinders effective cross-border patent enforcement against coordinated global infringers [Patent Enforcement](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Patent_Enforcement) \[1–10\].

## Legal Framework & Doctrines

- **Brussels Convention on Jurisdiction and the Enforcement of Judgments**: The primary European treaty governing jurisdiction and enforcement in civil and commercial litigation [Brussels Convention](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Brussels_Convention) \[1–10\].
- **Abuse of Process Doctrine**: Procedural restrictions preventing duplicate litigation over identical issues, as applied in the trade mark dispute \*PepsiCo v Coca-Cola\* [Abuse of Process](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Abuse_of_Process) \[1–10\].

## Background

IP litigation within public court systems frequently imposes substantial financial burdens, extended delay, and commercial uncertainty [IP Litigation Cost](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/IP_Litigation_Cost) \[1–10\]. Consequently, alternative private dispute resolution systems, including arbitration, mediation, and domain name dispute mechanisms under [WIPO](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/WIPO) and [EurID](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/EurID), have grown rapidly [IP Arbitration](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/IP_Arbitration) \[1–10\].

## Core Objectives

1.  Analyze the legal and practical impact of ECJ Case C-539/03 on patent holders across the European Union [ECJ Impact](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/ECJ_Impact) \[1–10\].
2.  Examine whether forum selection (forum-shopping) serves as a constructive form of competition between court systems [Jurisdictional Competition](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Jurisdictional_Competition) \[1–10\].
3.  Assess the procedural efficiency of the Brussels Convention in multi-jurisdictional patent enforcement [Brussels Convention Efficiency](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Brussels_Convention_Efficiency) \[1–10\].

## Key Holdings & Observations

- **Roche Nederland v Primus and Goldenberg Holding**: The ECJ ruled that patent holders must file nine independent lawsuits across nine separate jurisdictions where affiliated group entities reside [Roche Holding](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Roche_Holding) \[1–10\].
- **Forum Selection Options**: IP litigants face choices between traditional courts, accelerated forums (such as US "rocket dockets" or the [US International Trade Commission](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/US_International_Trade_Commission)), and between national versus Community rights in Europe [Forum Selection](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Forum_Selection) \[1–10\].
- **Abuse of Process Limits**: \*PepsiCo v Coca-Cola\* demonstrated that losing a Community trade mark action prevents a subsequent Benelux national trade mark suit targeting the same product [PepsiCo Case](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/PepsiCo_Case) \[1–10\].

## Policy & Unresolved Issues

- **Enforcement Barrier**: Requiring fragmented litigation across multiple jurisdictions acts as a financial deterrent for researchers and investors seeking to protect IP [Enforcement Barrier](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Enforcement_Barrier) \[1–10\].
- **Single Market Contradiction**: A contradiction exists between encouraging market competition across the EU while rejecting competitive choices in judicial dispute resolution [Single Market Contradiction](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Single_Market_Contradiction) \[1–10\].
