# Ferdi Raditya Akbar

## Contributor Profile

**Ferdi Raditya Akbar** is a core research contributor at the Judicial Knowledge Hub, participating in technical infrastructure documentation, data synthesis, and research briefing indexing for the BSDK Mahkamah Agung RI corpus\[cite: 4\].

### Research Scope & Contributions

- \*\*Technical Documentation\*\*: Participating in infrastructure documentation and synthesis across briefing papers and research reports\[cite: 4\].
- \*\*Repository Workflow\*\*: Assisting in the organization and indexing of institutional data assets.
