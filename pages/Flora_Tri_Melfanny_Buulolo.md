# Flora Tri Melfanny Buulolo

## Contributor Profile

**Flora Tri Melfanny Buulolo** is a core research contributor at the Judicial Knowledge Hub, actively engaged in legal policy data analysis, framework document indexing, and repository management for the BSDK Mahkamah Agung RI corpus.

### Research Scope & Contributions

- \*\*Corpus Indexing\*\*: Structuring entity data and tracking regulatory references within the JPAIS architecture\[cite: 2\].
