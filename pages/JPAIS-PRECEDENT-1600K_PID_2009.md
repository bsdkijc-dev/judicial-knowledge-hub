# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:1600K PID 2009

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 1600 K/Pid/2009** (dated November 24, 2009) is a fundamental Indonesian criminal law and criminal procedure jurisprudence precedent establishing pivotal rules regarding the application of the **Restorative Justice** doctrine and the **Principle of Balance** in adjudicating family criminal matters (specifically between a mother-in-law and a daughter-in-law):

> "In resolving a criminal case, if a Judge finds an effective resolution based on the principle of balance, a sense of justice, forgiveness, and benefits that are far greater if the respective criminal case is terminated due to the withdrawal of the case by the Complainant rather than continuing the case examination merely to fulfill legal formalities, **THEN A JUDGE MAY MAKE A JUDICIAL BREAKTHROUGH BY DEVIATING FROM FORMAL LEGAL ASPECTS (CRIMINAL PROCEDURE LAW).**"

Although the withdrawal of the complaint/report by the Complainant (Witness Victim Ny. \[Complainant\] / mother-in-law) exceeded the 3-month statutory deadline stipulated under Article 75 of the Criminal Code (KUHP), the Supreme Court outlined that peace, reconciliation, and forgiveness between the mother-in-law and daughter-in-law restored the disrupted legal balance and family ties (*restorative justice*). Forcibly examining a family criminal case in a rigid, formalistic manner would instead destroy family bonds and the best interests of the children/grandchildren.

The Supreme Court granted the Defendant's (\[Defendant\]) cassation petition, annulled the Yogyakarta High Court Decision No. 01/PID/PLW/2009/PT.Y dated March 2, 2009, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by granting Ny. \[Complainant\]'s complaint withdrawal petition and declaring the prosecution of Case No. 317/Pid.B/2008 in the name of Defendant \[Defendant\] **Inadmissible** (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Application of the Restorative Justice Doctrine and Principle of Balance in Family Criminal Matters

*Is the Court justified in continuing criminal prosecution against a daughter-in-law based on her mother-in-law's report, even though the victim witness (mother-in-law) has explicitly withdrawn the complaint, forgiven the Defendant, and declared that family relations and legal balance have been restored?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Unjustified / Prosecution Must Be Declared Inadmissible (NO).**

The Supreme Court outlined that the primary objective of Criminal Law is to restore legal balance disrupted by a criminal offense (*restorative justice*). In an internal family conflict between a mother-in-law (complainant) and daughter-in-law (Defendant), acts of forgiveness and complaint withdrawal by the victim represent a high-value restoration of balance. Facilitating conflict resolution satisfying the parties is vastly more effective and just than enforcing formal criminal penalties.

### 2. Reach of Substantive Justice Over Complaint Withdrawal Time Limits (Article 75 KUHP)

*Does Article 75 KUHP restricting complaint withdrawals to a maximum of 3 (three) months operate so rigidly as to bar the Court from granting complaint withdrawals filed after 3 months?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Can Be Deviated From in Favor of Substantive Justice.**

The Yogyakarta High Court's ruling rejecting complaint withdrawals based on Article 75 KUHP was assessed by the Supreme Court as rigid and overly formalistic. Although complaint withdrawals occur beyond the 3-month deadline, Judges possess authority to execute a legal breakthrough by deviating from criminal procedure formalities if terminating the case delivers vastly greater benefits to justice and family harmony.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* (Yogyakarta High Court Decision No. 01/PID/PLW/2009/PT.Y) err in applying the law (*foutieve toepassing van het recht*) by annulling the Yogyakarta District Court Judgment and ordering trial proceedings to continue, despite the Victim Witness (Defendant's mother-in-law) having filed a complaint withdrawal and forgiven the Defendant based on Restorative Justice?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. Yogyakarta High Court erred in applying the law by exhibiting rigidity and excessive formalism. The Supreme Court granted Defendant \[Defendant\]'s cassation petition, annulled Yogyakarta High Court Decision No. 01/PID/PLW/2009/PT.Y dated March 2, 2009, and independently adjudicated by granting Ny. \[Complainant\]'s complaint withdrawal petition and declaring the prosecution of Case No. 317/Pid.B/2008 in the name of Defendant \[Defendant\] inadmissible (NO).

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Yogyakarta District Court & Yogyakarta High Court)

Examination of facts regarding the mother-in-law and daughter-in-law family relationship between Ny. \[Complainant\] and \[Defendant\]; facts of electronic business capital disbursement amounting to Rp 3,910,000,000 between April and October 2007; issuance of promissory notes/barter cheques unpayable at banks; submission of complaint withdrawal petition letter dated October 28, 2008, by Ny. \[Complainant\] read before the Yogyakarta District Court Panel of Judges on November 5, 2008; and the existence of the Defendant's 2 young children (complainant's grandchildren).

### Legal Doctrines & Landmark Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- Restorative Justice Doctrine: Criminal case resolution focusing on restoring harmony and balance in relations among perpetrators, victims, and society.
- Principle of Judicial Breakthrough: Judges possess rights to deviate from criminal procedure formalities if conflicting with substantive justice and forgiveness.

## What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

### Established by Evidence

- Victim Witness / Complainant (Ny. \[Complainant\]) was legally proven to have voluntarily withdrawn all legal complaints/charges against her daughter-in-law (Defendant \[Defendant\]) based on humanitarian grounds, family bonds, and grandchildren's futures.
- Legal balance and family relationship harmony between Victim Witness and Defendant were proven fully restored.
- Complaint withdrawal petition granted by the Supreme Court.
- Criminal Prosecution of Case No. 317/Pid.B/2008 in the name of Defendant \[Defendant\] declared INADMISSIBLE (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).
- Court costs across all levels of jurisdiction charged to the State.

### Annulled (Yogyakarta High Court Verdict)

- Yogyakarta High Court Decision No. 01/PID/PLW/2009/PT.Y annulling complaint withdrawals and ordering trial continuation was entirely annulled by the Supreme Court as rigid and overly formalistic.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Jurisprudential Discretion on Restorative Justice Implementation in Indonesian Criminal Procedure:** This landmark ruling represents a historical milestone decision in Indonesian Supreme Court Jurisprudence adopting the Restorative Justice doctrine. It provides legal certainty that criminal prosecution formalities must yield to social balance restoration and family forgiveness, affirming that Judges must not remain "legislative captives" (*bouche de la loi*) when confronted with substantive justice.

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Can a criminal case between a mother-in-law and daughter-in-law be terminated if the mother-in-law withdraws the complaint even if such withdrawal occurs after the 3-month deadline?  
**Can Be Terminated (Prosecution Declared Inadmissible).** Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence No. 1600 K/Pid/2009:

- Under the Restorative Justice doctrine and Principle of Balance, complaint withdrawals and forgiveness by a mother-in-law toward her daughter-in-law represent an invaluable restoration of legal balance.
- Although complaint withdrawals occur past the 3-month deadline (Article 75 KUHP), Judges possess authority to execute a legal breakthrough deviating from criminal procedure formalities in favor of substantive justice and family utility.
- The Supreme Court grants complaint withdrawals and declares criminal prosecutions inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).
