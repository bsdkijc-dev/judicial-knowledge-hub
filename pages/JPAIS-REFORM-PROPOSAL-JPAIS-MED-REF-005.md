# JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:JPAIS-MED-REF-005

## Summary / Ringkasan

## Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

- Entity ID: JPAIS-MED-REF-005
- Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
- Ingestion Status: Confirmed

## Description / Deskripsi

Provides an exhaustive comparative assessment of issue and evidence management across six core procedural domains between Indonesia and Japan, identifying systemic gaps in Indonesia's piecemeal hearing model and proposing the institutionalization of a comprehensive Pra-Sidang and Evidence Modernization System modeled after Japan's CCP and LTRI doctrine\[cite: 5\].

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-001): Indonesian civil litigation suffers from trial fragmentation and unstructured document dumping because courts lack a pre-trial mechanism to crystallize issues and map documentary exhibits to material facts\[cite: 5\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-002): Burden of proof allocation is unpredictable due to unguided judicial application of Roman maxims, and severe information asymmetry exists due to a lack of compulsory document disclosure mechanisms\[cite: 5\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-003): District courts handle electronic records inconsistently, treating digital files as mere paper printouts requiring physical stamps (nazegelen), while civil judgments remain formalistic and opaque\[cite: 5\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-001): Civil adjudication relies on colonial-era codes (HIR/RBg) featuring sequential written exchanges (gugatan, jawaban, replik, duplik) and unstructured evidence submission without front-indexed mapping\[cite: 5\].

## Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-001): Japan's Code of Civil Procedure of 1996 incorporates specialized preparatory proceedings (benron junbi tetsuzuki), evidence explanation schedules (shoko setsumeisho), document production orders (bunsho teishutsu meirei), and the Theory of Presupposed Ultimate Facts (Yoken-jijitsu-ron)\[cite: 5\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-001): Absence of an interlocutory pre-trial hearing (pra-sidang) to bind contested issues, structured evidence explanation matrices, and systematic claim deconstruction doctrines\[cite: 5\].
- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-002): Lack of enforceable document production orders, cryptographic hash authentication standards for digital evidence, and structured evidentiary reasoning protocols in judgment drafting\[cite: 5\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Institutionalize a mandatory Pra-Sidang conference post-mediation, a Structured Evidence Matrix via e-Court, the Material Requisite Facts doctrine (Yoken-jijitsu-ron), targeted Document Production Orders with adverse inferences, cryptographic digital evidence standards, and reformed judgment templates\[cite: 5\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-001): Promulgate Supreme Court Decree (SK KMA) authorizing the Pilot Pra-Sidang Program, finalize e-Court Evidence Matrix module, and publish the official Yoken-jijitsu Benchbook\[cite: 5\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-002): Deliver 5-day professional certification courses for judges and registrars, deploy e-Court matrix into pilot registries, and mandate Pra-Sidang for all contentious civil and commercial cases\[cite: 5\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-003): Monitor case progression via SIPP, submit bi-weekly operational reports to Ditjen Badilum, and publish the 90-day comprehensive pilot evaluation report\[cite: 5\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-005](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-005): 90-day pilot program across commercial and general civil divisions at Central Jakarta District Court (PN Jakarta Pusat) and Surabaya District Court (PN Surabaya)\[cite: 5\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-005](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-005): Post-mediation judgment duration \$\le 90\$ calendar days; evidentiary hearings \$\le 3\$ sessions; factual claims resolved via stipulation \$\ge 30\\\$; pre-trial settlement \$\ge 25\\\$; evidence matrix compliance \$100\\\$; document production compliance \$\ge 85\\\$; digital stipulation rate \$\ge 80\\\$; cassation overturn rate \$\le 15\\\$\[cite: 5\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Issue Supreme Court Circular Letters mandating Minutes of Disputed Issues, structured burden allocation reasoning, and integration of judgment metrics into EIS\[cite: 5\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgate comprehensive Supreme Court Regulations (PERMA) on Pre-Trial Case Management, Structured Evidence Explanation, Targeted Document Production, Digital Evidence Admissibility, and Judgment Reasoning, while codifying provisions into the Draft Civil Procedure Code\[cite: 5\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Japanese preparatory proceedings and Yoken-jijitsu training reduced first-instance disposition times from 12.4 months to 9.2 months and achieved over 50% conciliation settlement rates\[cite: 5\].
- Indonesian civil trials suffer from prolonged multi-session evidentiary hearings that conflict with SEMA No. 2 of 2014's five-month disposition ceiling\[cite: 5\].
