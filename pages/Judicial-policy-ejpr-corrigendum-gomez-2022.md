# Judicial-policy-ejpr-corrigendum-gomez-2022

**Corrigendum: When the chickens come home to roost** is an official corrigendum published in the *European Journal of Political Research* (Volume 61, Page 853, 2022) by the European Consortium for Political Research (ECPR).

## Summary

This document is an official correction to the research article titled *"When the chickens come home to roost: The long-term impact of party positions on religious voting"*, authored by R. Gomez (published in EJPR Volume 61, Issue 2, pages 566–585, 2022). The correction was issued to fix a typographical error on page 566 in the article's introductory paragraph.

## Legal Basis

- **Not found in the available sources:** This document is an academic journal correction notice and is not based on any specific statutory or judicial legislative instrument \[1–3\].

## Background

In the original article by R. Gomez (2022), the term "supranational beliefs" was mistakenly printed in the introduction while discussing the phenomenon of secularisation in Western Europe.

## Objectives

To correct the academic record by replacing the term "supranational beliefs" with "supernatural beliefs" so that it accurately reflects the intended context of the sociology of religion and voting behavior.

## Scope

This correction applies specifically to the introductory text on page 566 of the Gomez (2022) article in the *European Journal of Political Research*, DOI \`10.1111/1475-6765.12462\`.

## Key Provisions

- **Corrected Text:** "Unlike other parts of the world, in Western Europe the evidence of society's increasingly low reliance on supernatural beliefs, matched by a dwindling proportion of churchgoers, is so strong that current academic debates do not revolve around the existence of secularisation but, rather, around its causes and actual significance (Norris & Inglehart 2004: 83−110)."
- **Corrigendum Publication Details:** Published in EJPR Volume 61, Page 853 (2022), DOI \`10.1111/1475-6765.12543\`.

## Implementation

Published by John Wiley & Sons Ltd on behalf of the European Consortium for Political Research (ECPR © 2022).

## Policy Issues

Correcting academic terminology to preserve conceptual accuracy in political science literature on secularisation.

## Reform / Implications

Issuance of a formal corrigendum notice along with an apology from the publisher.
