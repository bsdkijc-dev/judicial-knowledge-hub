# Research:DAOs Under Corporate Statutes

# DAOs Under Corporate Statutes (Swiss vs Delaware)

## Abstract

This comparative study investigates the liability paradigms governing Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs). Absent formal corporate registration, many jurisdictions treat DAO token holders as general partners, exposing individual participants to unlimited joint and several liability. This paper evaluates the Swiss "Verein" model against Delaware's decentralized LLC alternatives.

## Structural Models

1.  **The Swiss Verein Structure:** Offers limited liability protection without rigid centralized board mandates.
2.  **Delaware Decentralized LLC (Series/Public Benefit):** Mandates formal agent registration but permits consensus-based operations.
