# Judicial AI Register

# Judicial AI Register

The Judicial AI Register records AI systems approved, piloted or deployed within the judiciary.

## Fields

- system ID;
- purpose;
- owner;
- vendor/model;
- data;
- risk tier;
- legal basis;
- human oversight;
- evaluation status;
- audit status;
- deployment status;
- review date;
- retirement status.
