# JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:KCCP-REF-001-KCCP-ENTITY-PRIME

## Summary / Ringkasan

## Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

- Entity ID: KCCP-REF-001
- Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
- Ingestion Status: Confirmed

## Description / Deskripsi

Reforms Indonesian court-connected mediation by establishing 3-member Tripartite Conciliation Panels (*Majelis Konsiliasi Tripartit*) for claims exceeding Rp 500 million, pairing 1 certified Judge Mediator with 2 accredited Non-Judge Expert Commissioners. The panel is empowered to issue a provisional "Order in Lieu of Settlement" (*Penetapan Pengganti Perdamaian*), which becomes a binding Settlement Deed (*Akta Perdamaian*) if no party objects within 14 calendar days.

## Problem / Masalah

- KCCP-PROB-001: Court mediation in general civil courts (*Pengadilan Negeri*) suffers from structural ritualization and stagnant success rates (~4.0%).

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- KCCP-PRACT-ID-001: Mandatory court mediation under PERMA No. 1 of 2016 relies on a solo mediator model (judge or uncompensated non-judge) conducting brief facilitative sessions without evaluative authority.

## Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

- KCCP-PRACT-JP-001 & KCCP-PRACT-JP-003: Under Articles 5, 6, and 17 of the Civil Conciliation Act, Japan utilizes 3-member Conciliation Committees (*Chōtei Iinkai*—1 judge + 2 lay commissioners) and empowers courts to issue ex-officio "Orders in Lieu of Conciliation" (*Chōtei ni kawaru kettei*).

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- KCCP-GAP-001: PERMA No. 1 of 2016 lacks multidisciplinary expert panels, evaluative settlement mechanisms, and enforced cost sanctions for bad-faith attendance.

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Institutionalize Tripartite Conciliation Panels for commercial, property, and inheritance disputes \>Rp 500 million, and introduce judicial *Penetapan Pengganti Perdamaian* subject to a 14-day objection window.

## Action / Aksi

- KCCP-ACT-001: Promulgate SK KMA pilot decree, establish steering committee, configure SIPP workflow, and train 120 expert commissioners.
- KCCP-ACT-002: Deploy panels across 6 pilot courts and issue evaluative orders upon impasse.
- KCCP-ACT-003: Audit SIPP performance metrics and draft permanent PERMA.

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- KCCP-PILOT-001: 90-day pilot across PN Jakarta Pusat, PN Surabaya, PN Medan, PA Jakarta Selatan, PA Surabaya, and PA Malang.

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- KCCP-KPI-001: PN mediation success rate \$\ge 25\\\$; religious court property settlement \$\ge 60\\\$; duration \$\le 21\$ calendar days.

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Issue Supreme Court Leadership Decree (SK KMA) and integrate lay expert rosters into SIPP. Submit amendments to Draft Civil Procedure Code (*RUU Hukum Acara Perdata*) to DPR.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgate new PERMA on Court-Connected Mediation and Conciliation superseding PERMA No. 1 of 2016.

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- PN general civil mediation success rate hovers between 4.0% and 4.08%.
- Japanese conciliation achieves \>50% settlement in general civil disputes and 71.8% in inheritance property cases.
