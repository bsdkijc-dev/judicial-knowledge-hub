# Conflict-of-Interest Intelligence

# Conflict-of-Interest Intelligence

Conflict-of-Interest Intelligence is proposed to support identification of potential conflict-of-interest signals.

## Function

The system may assist authorised personnel in identifying relationships or patterns requiring human review.

## Boundary

The system must not automatically determine that a conflict exists.

## Governance

- source validation;
- privacy protection;
- human review;
- auditability;
- appropriate access control.
