# Judicial AI Competency Framework

The future judiciary requires a national system for continuous AI competency rather than isolated technology training.

## Competency Architecture

The framework proposes five levels of AI competency.

1.  AI Awareness
2.  AI User
3.  AI Practitioner
4.  AI Governance Professional
5.  AI Strategic Leader

## Institutional Principle

Workforce capability should be developed before or alongside technological deployment.

## Assessment

Competency should be measurable through:

- knowledge assessment;
- practical capability;
- ethical judgement;
- governance capability;
- continuous professional development.
