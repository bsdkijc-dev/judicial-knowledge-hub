# Access Classification

# Access Classification

Judicial knowledge should be classified according to its appropriate access level.

## Proposed Classes

- Public
- Institutional
- Restricted
- Confidential
- Highly Sensitive

## Principle

Access classification should reflect the nature and sensitivity of the information.

## Important Boundary

A public Wiki does not imply that all judicial information should be public.
