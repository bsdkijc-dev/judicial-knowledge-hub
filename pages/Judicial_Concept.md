# Judicial Concept

# Judicial Concept

A Judicial Concept represents a recurring idea or analytical category used to understand judicial institutions, processes, or decisions.

Examples include:

- judicial independence;
- judicial accountability;
- access to justice;
- case management;
- judicial integrity;
- court administration;
- judicial education.
