# Confidential Knowledge Boundary

# Confidential Knowledge Boundary

Highly confidential or sensitive information should remain in systems with appropriate access-control, encryption, audit and institutional governance capabilities.

## Principle

**Not everything belongs in the Wiki.**

## Rule

The Wiki may contain metadata or references to restricted repositories without storing the underlying sensitive material.
