# Digital Judicial Administration Regulation

# Digital Judicial Administration and Case Management Regulation

The Renstra identifies the need to update judicial administration and case-management guidance for digital systems.

## Scope

The proposed regulatory framework concerns:

- judicial duties;
- case administration;
- judicial management;
- digital systems;
- updates to Buku II and Buku IV.

## Identified Problems

The Renstra identifies:

- incomplete reflection of digitalization in existing guidance;
- differences among electronic systems;
- inefficiency caused by system gaps;
- insufficient integration between technical guidance and Supreme Court

` information systems;`

- supervision that is not yet sufficiently data-based.

## Target

**2029**
