# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:31K MIL 2013

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 31 K/MIL/2013** (dated April 2, 2013) and **Military Appeals Court Decision No. PUT/26-K/PMU/BDG/AD/XI/2010** (dated November 8, 2010) are landmark special criminal law and military justice jurisprudence precedents compiled under the Supreme Court Jurisprudence framework. They adjudicate complex military criminal matters involving active soldiers' participation in transnational human smuggling, the abuse of superior authority over subordinates (*ex* Article 127 of the Military Criminal Code / *KUHPM*), failure to report incidents to superior commands (*ex* Article 121 *KUHPM*), and the imposition of dishonorable discharge from active military service (*pemecatan*).

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court and the Military Appeals Court established four primary legal principles:

1.  **Human Smuggling by Military Personnel (Article 120 paragraph (1) Immigration Law *jo.* Article 55 Criminal Code):** Active Indonesian National Armed Forces (*TNI*) personnel (such as regional military supervisors / *Babinsa*) who organize, coordinate, and cooperate with smuggling syndicates to secure boats, escort, and smuggle illegal foreign migrants from the Middle East to Australia through Indonesian coastal waters are legally and convincingly proven guilty of Human Smuggling *ex* Article 120 paragraph (1) of Law No. 6 of 2011 on Immigration *jo.* Article 55 paragraph (1) point 1 of the Criminal Code. Defendants are sentenced to imprisonment, a Rp 500,000,000 fine, and an additional penalty of dishonorable discharge from TNI-AD active service.
2.  **Abuse of Superior Influence Over Subordinates (Article 127 KUHPM):** A District Military Commander (*Dandim*) who abuses the influence of his office and authority to persuade or order subordinates (military intelligence personnel) to conduct unauthorized or violent actions outside official duties, resulting in the death of a civilian (victim \[Party B\] at Lake Singkarak), fulfills the elements of Abuse of Influence *ex* Article 127 of the Military Criminal Code (*KUHPM*).
3.  **Failure to Report to Superior Command (Article 121 KUHPM):** A military unit commander who intentionally conceals or neglects the duty to report prominent incidents (such as the discovery of a corpse or subordinate criminal offenses) to the Resort Military Commander (*Danrem*) as the Superior Command is proven to violate Article 121 of the Military Criminal Code (*KUHPM*).
4.  **Criteria for Dishonorable Discharge from TNI-AD Active Service (Article 26 KUHPM):** Mid-ranking officers (Lieutenant Colonels) or Non-Commissioned Officers committing crimes that tarnish institutional dignity, destroy the harmonious bond between the military and the people (*kemanunggalan TNI-Rakyat*), and strip away soldierly integrity are deemed unfit to remain in military service and must be subjected to the additional penalty of discharge from active service *ex* Article 26 of the Military Criminal Code (*KUHPM*).

The Supreme Court rejected the cassation petitions of the Petitioners for Cassation / Defendants (Serda \[Defendant\], Kopka Karyadi, Peltu Susiali, Serka Khoirul Anam) in Decision No. 31 K/MIL/2013, affirming the primary prison sentences and the additional penalty of dishonorable discharge from TNI-AD active service.

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Military Criminal Liability for Human Smuggling (Article 120 paragraph (1) Immigration Law No. 6 of 2011 *jo.* Article 55 Criminal Code)

*Can an active TNI soldier utilizing their position and facilities as a local military supervisor (*Babinsa*) to facilitate transit, transportation, and departure of illegal foreign immigrants be prosecuted under the Immigration Law and discharged from military service?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Prosecutable & Mandatory Discharge.** Participation by military personnel in international migrant smuggling syndicates constitutes a transnational organized crime damaging state sovereignty and military institutional standing. Such conduct fulfills the elements of Article 120 paragraph (1) of the Immigration Law *jo.* Article 55 of the Criminal Code, warranting prison terms, a Rp 500 million fine, and the additional penalty of dishonorable discharge from TNI-AD active service.

### 2. Limits of Superior Authority Abuse Over Subordinates in Military Justice (Article 127 Military Criminal Code)

*Does an oral order or direction from a Unit Commander (*Dandim*) instructing subordinates to handle civilians outside official statutory authority qualify as the crime of abusing superior influence*ex*Article 127 of the Military Criminal Code (*KUHPM*)?*

**Supreme Court / Military Appeals Court Rule: Fulfills Article 127 KUHPM Elements.** A military superior using positional influence to direct subordinates to commit unlawful acts (assault/unlawful detention) remains criminally liable for abusing official influence, even if executing subordinates are prosecuted separately.

### 3. Indicators of Propriety for Additional Military Discharge Penalties (Article 26 KUHPM)

Dishonorable discharge from military service (*pemecatan*) is applied when crimes committed by soldiers reflect bad character, subvert military discipline and leadership, and destroy the harmonious bond between the military and the people (*Kemanunggalan TNI dengan Rakyat*).

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (*Judex Juris* / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* Surabaya Military High Court III (Decision No. 86-K/PMT.III/BDG/AD/XI/2012) and Madiun Military Court III-13 (Decision No. 42-K/PM.III-13/AD/VIII/2012) correctly apply the law (*juiste toepassing van het recht* *ex* Article 120 Immigration Law) against the Defendant illegal immigrant smugglers?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. The *Judex Facti* correctly applied the law and committed no evidentiary errors. The Supreme Court rejected Serda \[Defendant\] *et al.*{{'}}s cassation petition and affirmed 5–6 years' imprisonment, a Rp 500 million fine, and discharge from TNI-AD active service.

### Question of Fact (*Judex Facti* / Padang Military Court I-03, Medan Military High Court I, & Military Appeals Court)

Verification of facts concerning the arrest of victim \[Party B\] on damaged roads in Nagari Kacang, X Koto Singkarak Sub-district, on May 20, 2007; physical torture in the garage of the Solok Dandim official residence; corpse disposal in Lake Singkarak; corpse discovery by fisherman Yunasri Sutan Man Cayo on May 25, 2007; *Visum et Repertum* from Dr. M. Djamil Hospital, Padang, No. Ym.01.08.1.5.1035; and verification of operational fund flows for illegal immigrants amounting to Rp 120,000,000 at Juanda Airport, Surabaya.

## Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

### Statutory Regulations

- **Law No. 6 of 2011 on Immigration:** Article 120 paragraph (1) (Human Smuggling).
- **Military Criminal Code (KUHPM):** Article 26 (Additional Discharge Penalty), Article 121 (Failure to Report), & Article 127 (Abuse of Positional Influence).
- **Law No. 31 of 1997 on Military Justice:** Articles 130, 171, 173 (Military Modes of Proof & Evidence).
- **Criminal Code (KUHP):** Article 55 paragraph (1) point 1 (Complicity / *Deelneming*) & Article 338 (Murder).

### Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- **Doctrine of Zero Tolerance for Transnational Crimes by TNI Personnel:** Military personnel involved in transnational syndicates (human smuggling/narcotics) must face absolute discharge to safeguard national sovereignty and institutional dignity.
- **Doctrine of Superior Responsibility / Abuse of Authority (*ex* Article 127 KUHPM):** Military command is a trust for troop development; abusing command authority for criminal acts destroys military hierarchy and legal obedience.

## What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

### Established by Evidence

- The Defendants (Serda \[Defendant\], Kopka Karyadi, Peltu Susiali, Serka Khoirul Anam) are **PROVEN LEGALLY AND CONVINCINGLY GUILTY OF HUMAN SMUGGLING** *ex* Article 120 paragraph (1) of the Immigration Law *jo.* Article 55 of the Criminal Code.
- Lt. Col. Untung Sunanto is **PROVEN GUILTY OF ABUSE OF POSITIONAL INFLUENCE (Article 127 KUHPM) AND FAILURE TO REPORT (Article 121 KUHPM)**.
- Defendants are sentenced to primary prison terms and a fine of Rp 500,000,000.
- Defendants are subjected to the additional penalty of **DISHONORABLE DISCHARGE FROM TNI-AD ACTIVE SERVICE**.
- Defendants' cassation petitions are **REJECTED ENTIRELY BY THE SUPREME COURT**.

### Dismissed Indictment Charges (Primary Murder Charge)

- The primary indictment charging murder (*ex* Article 338 Criminal Code *jo.* Article 55 Criminal Code) against Lt. Col. Untung Sunanto was **PROVEN UNFOUNDED MATERIALLY**, as physical torture was spontaneous subordinate initiative driven by fear of reprimand over escaped detainees.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Integrity of Military Criminal Law Enforcement Against Organized Crime:** This landmark ruling establishes strict boundaries for Indonesian Military Justice integrity: no tolerance or legal immunity exists for mid-ranking officers or NCOs abusing command authority or engaging in international human smuggling syndicates. Imprisonment and military discharge are absolute consequences for upholding troop discipline and national law.
