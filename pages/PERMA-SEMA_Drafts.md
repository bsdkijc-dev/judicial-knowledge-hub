**Draft Regulation Profile**

**Draft PERMA No. 3 of 2026: Digital Arbitration** is an active regulatory proposal hosted on the Judicial Knowledge Hub, the secure internal court encyclopedia.

This proposed regulation seeks to standardize the enforcement and execution of commercial arbitration awards handled entirely on digital communication platforms.

## Proposed clauses under review

### Proposed Paragraph 4.3: Cryptographic Signatures

Arbitrators must sign final declarations using secure cryptographic credentials conforming to Section 12 criteria.

## Framework intersection

To ensure the authenticity of awards signed under Paragraph 4.3, this draft explicitly intersects with established digital security practices:

- **Notary Verification:** Credentials must align with criteria specified under [SEMA No. 1 of 2026 (Remote Signature Validation)](Policy-_Judicial_Policy_Portal.md).
- **Data Integrity:** All digital evidence registries filed during the arbitration lifecycle must preserve a verifiable [chain of custody](Chain_of_custody.md).
