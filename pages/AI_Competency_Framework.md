# AI Competency Framework

The **AI Competency Framework** defines the knowledge, skills, behaviours, and professional capabilities required to use, evaluate, govern, and supervise artificial intelligence in a judicial environment.

## Competency Architecture

| Level | Competency                    | Core Capability                                |
|-------|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| C1    | AI Awareness                  | Understand basic AI concepts                   |
| C2    | AI Literacy                   | Use AI tools appropriately                     |
| C3    | AI-Assisted Professional Work | Integrate AI into defined workflows            |
| C4    | AI Evaluation                 | Assess accuracy, bias, risk, and evidence      |
| C5    | AI Governance                 | Design and supervise institutional AI controls |
| C6    | AI Strategy                   | Lead judicial AI transformation                |

## Core Domains

- AI literacy
- Data literacy
- Prompt literacy
- Legal-tech literacy
- Verification
- Privacy
- Cybersecurity
- AI ethics
- Risk assessment
- Governance

## Role-Based Competency

Different roles require different competency profiles.

**Judge**

→ AI literacy + verification + ethics + professional judgment

**Researcher**

→ research + prompting + source verification + data literacy

**Administrator**

→ workflow + governance + procurement + risk

**IT Professional**

→ architecture + security + deployment + monitoring

**Leadership**

→ strategy + governance + institutional risk

## Principle

**AI COMPETENCY IS NOT THE ABILITY TO USE A CHATBOT.**

It includes the ability to understand limitations and exercise professional judgment.
