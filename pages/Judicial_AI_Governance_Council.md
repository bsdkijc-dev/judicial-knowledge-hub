# Judicial AI Governance Council

The Judicial AI Governance Council is proposed as the institutional body responsible for AI policy, risk, standards and oversight.

## Proposed Functions

- AI policy;
- risk classification;
- standards;
- impact-assessment oversight;
- procurement governance;
- audit oversight;
- incident governance;
- deployment gates;
- competency requirements;
- strategic review.

## Relationship

The Council operates within the authority of Supreme Court leadership and coordinates with:

- BSDK/capability functions;
- technology/data functions;
- courts;
- judges and professional users;
- independent evaluation functions.

## Principle

AI governance should remain institutional governance, not merely technical administration.
