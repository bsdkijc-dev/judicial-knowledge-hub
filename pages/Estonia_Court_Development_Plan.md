# Estonia Court Development Plan

# Estonia Court Development Plan

Estonia provides a strategic reference for future judicial development.

## Development Foundations

- human-centred justice;
- timely and quality administration;
- foreseeable and consistent proceedings;
- digital solutions;
- workforce capacity;
- organisational openness;
- unity of the judiciary.

## Governance Lesson

The development plan was developed with judicial and court professional involvement and approved through judicial administration.

## Indonesian Lesson

The transferable lesson is not institutional replication but a combination of judicial ownership, professional administration, measurable priorities and implementation planning.
