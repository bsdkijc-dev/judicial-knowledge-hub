# Algorithmic Bias

**Algorithmic bias** refers to systematic patterns in an algorithmic or AI system that produce unfair, inaccurate, or disadvantageous outcomes for particular individuals or groups.

The presence, causes, and legal significance of bias must be evaluated empirically and contextually.

## Potential Sources

Bias may arise from:

- Training data
- Sampling
- Labels
- Historical practices
- Feature selection
- Model design
- Deployment context
- Human interaction

## Judicial Context

Potential concerns include:

- Case classification
- Risk assessment
- Resource allocation
- Search ranking
- Automated recommendations

## Detection

Potential methods include:

- Error-rate comparison
- Outcome analysis
- Dataset auditing
- Fairness testing
- Expert review

## Mitigation

Possible measures include:

- Better datasets
- Human oversight
- Model testing
- Transparency
- Monitoring
- Appeal mechanisms

## Important Distinction

A statistical difference does not automatically establish unlawful discrimination.

Legal conclusions require the applicable legal framework and evidence.
