## Policy Analysis

### Background & Data Coverage

- Analyzes national crime statistics through July 2024, using consolidated 2023 data from the National Police Agency (NPA), Public Prosecutor's Offices, Courts, Correctional Facilities, and Probation Offices.

### Penal Code Offense Key Indicators (2023)

- **Reported Cases**: 703,351 total Penal Code cases (Theft: 483,695 / 68.8%; Non-theft: 219,656).
- **Cleared Persons**: 183,269 individuals cleared, yielding an overall clearance rate of 38.3%.
- **Sexual Offenses**: Penetrative sexual assault recorded 2,711 reported cases and 2,073 cleared cases (76.5% clearance rate). Indecent assault recorded 6,096 reported cases and 4,813 cleared cases (79.0% clearance rate).

### Prosecution & Court Adjudication (2023)

- **Prosecutor Intake**: 786,707 total suspects received (Penal Code offenses: 200,373; Negligent driving: 288,827; Special Law offenses: 297,507).
- **Prosecution Outcomes**: 238,145 total prosecutions (75,384 public trials; 162,761 summary orders).
- **Non-Prosecution**: 507,221 suspects were not prosecuted, among whom 444,261 (69.1%) received a suspension of prosecution. The prosecution rate for Penal Code offenses stood at 45.4%.

### Institutional Corrections & Parole (2023)

- **Inmate Population**: 40,178 inmates as of end-2023 (32.3 per 100,000 population).
- **New Admissions**: 14,085 newly admitted prisoners (Male: 12,599 / 89.4%; Female: 1,486 / 10,6%).
- **Releases**: 16,202 prisoners released (10,211 paroled; 5,862 full-term release). The parole rate stood at 63.0%.

### Recidivism & Re-offending Trends

- **Repeat Offender Ratio**: 86,099 out of 183,269 cleared Penal Code offenders were repeat offenders (47.0%).
- **2-Year Reimprisonment Rate**: Inmates released in 2022 recorded a 13.0% overall 2-year reimprisonment rate.
- **Parole vs. Full-Term Disparity**: Parolees exhibited an 8.6% 2-year reimprisonment rate compared to 20.2% for full-term released inmates.
- **5-Year Reimprisonment Rate**: Inmates released in 2019 showed a 34.1% overall 5-year rate (Parolees: 27.4% vs. Full-term: 43.6%).

### Female & Elderly Offender Dynamics

- **Female Offenders**: Accounted for 39,370 cleared persons (21.5% of total Penal Code offenders). Shoplifting constituted 51.4% of female offenses.
- **Elderly Offenders (65+)**: Accounted for 41,099 cleared persons (22.4% of total Penal Code offenders). Among elderly females, shoplifting accounted for 71.4% of offenses.
- **Part 7 Feature**: A survey of 908 newly admitted inmates (461 female, 447 male) across 22 prisons highlighted severe mental health issues, domestic violence/partner victimization history, and social isolation among elderly female shoplifters.

### Legal Reforms & Juvenile Justice

- **Juvenile Act Amendments (2021)**: Established the "Specified Juvenile" framework for 18–19-year-olds, enforcing mandatory referral to prosecutors for serious offenses.
- **Penal Code Amendments (2023)**: Integrated sexual violence laws and expanded statutory protections for victims aged 13–15.

### Interpretations & Structural Inferences

- Re-offending rates demonstrate that full-term releases yield more than double the 2-year reimprisonment rate (20.2%) compared to parolees under community supervision (8.6%).
- Crime management in Japan is heavily impacted by population aging, where social isolation and health vulnerability drive elderly female shoplifting.
