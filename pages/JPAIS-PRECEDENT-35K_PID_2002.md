## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 35 K/Pid/2002 dated March 6, 2002, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal standards regarding the admissibility of cassation petitions against pre-trial (\*praperadilan\*) rulings and the legality of detention actions executed by Joint Connected Investigation Teams (\*Tim Penyidik Koneksitas\*). The Supreme Court affirmed that pre-trial rulings concerning the legality or illegality of detention executed by a Joint Connected Investigation Team in corruption cases (involving suspects holding active military status alongside civilian parties) are subject to cassation before the Supreme Court. Furthermore, the detention of corruption suspects by a Joint Connected Investigation Team under the coordination of the Attorney General is legally valid.

## Case Background

The Suspect, Marshal Madya (Ret.) Prof. DR. Ir. Ginanjar Kartasasmita, was tried on allegations of corruption during the formulation of a Technical Assistance Contract (TAC) between Pertamina and PT Ustraindo Petro Gas during 1992–1993, at which time the suspect served as the Minister of Mines and Energy and held active military status.

The Suspect was detained at the Attorney General's Office Detention Center (\*Rutan\*) starting April 6, 2001, pursuant to Detention Warrant No. Prin-052/F/FJP/04/2001 dated April 17, 2001, effective from April 9, 2001, to April 28, 2001. The Suspect filed a pre-trial application at the South Jakarta District Court. The South Jakarta District Court (Decision No. 11/Pid.Prap/2001/PN.Jak.Sel. dated May 2, 2001) granted the pre-trial application and declared the detention illegal. Dissatisfied with the pre-trial ruling, the Petitioner for Cassation (ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC OF INDONESIA cq. JUNIOR ATTORNEY GENERAL FOR SPECIAL CRIMES) filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  Pre-trial rulings regarding the legality or illegality of detention executed by a Joint Connected Investigation Team in corruption cases allegedly committed by a suspect subject to trial within the Military Justice environment alongside suspects subject to trial within the General Court environment \*\*are subject to cassation\*\*.
2.  Pursuant to Article 26 jo. Article 27 of Law No. 3 of 1971 and provisions governing connected case investigations (Article 89 of the KUHAP / Article 198 of Law No. 31 of 1997), the Attorney General as Law Enforcer and Supreme Public Prosecutor possesses the authority to direct and coordinate investigations into connected corruption cases, including establishing Joint Connected Investigation Teams (comprising elements of the Prosecutor's Office, Military Police, and Military Prosecutors) and executing detentions. Detentions executed by such Joint Connected Investigation Teams are legally valid.

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation ATTORNEY GENERAL OF THE REPUBLIC OF INDONESIA cq. JUNIOR ATTORNEY GENERAL FOR SPECIAL CRIMES; Annulling the South Jakarta District Court pre-trial decision dated May 2, 2001, No. 11/Pid.Prap/2001/PN.Jak.Sel.; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring the detention of Pre-Trial Petitioner / Respondent for Cassation Marshal Madya (Ret.) Prof. DR. Ir. Ginanjar Kartasasmita executed by the Joint Connected Investigation Team (pursuant to Attorney General Decree No. Kep. 141/A/JA/04/2001 dated April 9, 2001) to be \*\*legally valid\*\*; (2) Ordering the Respondent for Cassation / Pre-Trial Petitioner to pay court costs amounting to Rp 2,500 (two thousand five hundred rupiahs).
