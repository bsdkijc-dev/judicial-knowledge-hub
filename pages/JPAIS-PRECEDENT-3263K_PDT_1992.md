# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:3263K PDT 1992

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 3263 K/Pdt/1992** (dated June 30, 1994 / deliberation on April 15, 1994) is a fundamental civil law and civil procedure jurisprudence precedent establishing crucial rules concerning the legal standing (*persona standi in judicio*) of associations dissolved by the Government and the legal consequences of lawsuits filed by unauthorized entities.

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established a primary legal principle:

- **Dissolved Associations Have No Right to File Lawsuits:** "An association that has been dissolved has no right to file a lawsuit."

Where the Christian Lyceum Association (*Perkumpulan Lyceum Kristen* / PLK) filed a lawsuit seeking the eviction and vacation of the SMAK Dago school building complex (\[address removed\], Bandung), the Supreme Court held that PLK was not a legitimate name change or continuation of *Het Christelijk Lyceum* (HCL). Because *Het Christelijk Lyceum* was a prohibited foreign association officially dissolved by the Government pursuant to Government Regulation in Lieu of Law No. 50 of 1960 (*UU No. 50 Prp. Tahun 1960*), and PLK was not its lawful continuation, PLK lacked legal standing (*no legal standing* / *lack of persona standi in judicio*). Consequently, the lawsuit filed by PLK must be declared **Inadmissible** (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioner for Cassation (Foundation of the West Java Christian Middle School Educational Board / YBPSMK Jabar), annulled Bandung High Court Decision No. 218/Pdt/1992/PT.Bdg dated July 31, 1992, *jo.* Bandung District Court Decision No. 245/Pdt/G/1991/PN.Bdg dated April 29, 1992, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by declaring the Plaintiff's lawsuit in Convention and the Counterclaim in Reconvention both **Inadmissible** (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Formal Standing Requirements (*Persona Standi in Judicio*) for Private Associations / Legal Entities (Law No. 50 Prp. of 1960)

*Does a local association (i.e., Perkumpulan Lyceum Kristen) possess legal capacity (legal standing) to sue for asset vacation and rent on behalf of a foreign association (i.e., Het Christelijk Lyceum) that has been officially dissolved by the Government of the Republic of Indonesia under statutory law?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Lacks Legal Standing / Formally Defective.** Foreign/Dutch associations dissolved by the Government under Law No. 50 Prp. of 1960 no longer retain juridical existence. A newly formed association claiming to be a continuation or name change of such prohibited association without valid legal basis lacks legal capacity (*lack of persona standi in judicio*) to file civil lawsuits before courts.

### 2. Legal Consequences of Lawsuits Filed by Unauthorized Legal Subjects (*Error in Persona* *ex* Lack of Standing)

Civil lawsuits filed by unauthorized legal subjects lacking entitlement as owners or rights-holders over disputed objects contain a formal defect regarding the legal subject (*error in persona*), requiring courts to declare the lawsuit **Inadmissible** (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO) without examining the substantive merits.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* Bandung High Court (Decision No. 218/Pdt/1992/PT.Bdg) and Bandung District Court (Decision No. 245/Pdt/G/1991/PN.Bdg) err in applying civil procedure and organizational law (*foutieve toepassing van het recht*) by validating Perkumpulan Lyceum Kristen as a qualified Plaintiff entitled to sue YBPSMK Jabar, whereas the parent organization Het Christelijk Lyceum had been dissolved by the Government under Law No. 50 Prp. of 1960 and PLK was not its lawful continuation?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. The *Judex Facti* misapplied the law. Dissolved associations have no right to file lawsuits. The Supreme Court granted YBPSMK Jabar's cassation petition, annulled the lower court decisions, and independently adjudicated by declaring the lawsuits in Convention and Reconvention Inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Bandung District Court & Bandung High Court)

Verification of Exhibits T-14 and T-15 regarding the dissolution status of Het Christelijk Lyceum; inspection of the School Building Lease Agreement dated November 14, 1978; verification of physical possession over the SMAK Dago school complex at \[address removed\], Bandung, by YBPSMK Jabar; and testimonies of trial witnesses (T. Sumarna, Sahala Hamonangan Simatupang, Drs. Helmy Sukardi, Solias Pandi, Jo Soa Tedjayuana, Iwan Suwardi, Drs. Wahyu Kurnia, Margo Wahono, Soesanto Kartoatmodjo, Sasongko Endrawijaya).

## Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

### Statutory Regulations

- **Law No. 50 Prp. of 1960:** Prohibition of Prohibited Foreign Organizations / Associations Established by or for Foreigners Without Diplomatic Relations.
- **Dwikora Cabinet Presidium Regulation No. 5/Prk/1965:** Reaffirmation of House/Land/Building Status Belonging to Former Dutch Legal Entities.
- **HIR / RBg:** Provisions on Formal Lawsuit Requirements & Legal Subject Exceptions (*Persona Standi in Judicio* / *Error in Persona*).
- **Law No. 14 of 1970 *jo.* Law No. 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court:** Cassation and Independent Adjudication Authority.

### Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- **Doctrine of Legal Entity *Persona Standi in Judicio*:** Only definitive legal subjects possessing valid juridical existence and legally recognized rights may act as Plaintiffs before courts.
- **Established Precedent on Dissolved Associations:** Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 3263 K/Pdt/1992 serves as a landmark decision establishing that organizations/associations officially dissolved by the State forfeit all private law rights, including the right to file civil lawsuits in court.

## What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

### Established by Evidence

- *Het Christelijk Lyceum* (HCL) is **PROVEN TO HAVE BEEN DISSOLVED BY THE RI GOVERNMENT** pursuant to Law No. 50 Prp. of 1960.
- *Perkumpulan Lyceum Kristen* (PLK) is **PROVEN NOT TO BE A NAME CHANGE OR LAWFUL CONTINUATION** of *Het Christelijk Lyceum*.
- The Plaintiff (Perkumpulan Lyceum Kristen) is **PROVEN TO LACK LEGAL STANDING** (*Lack of Persona Standi in Judicio*).
- Bandung High Court Decision No. 218/Pdt/1992/PT.Bdg and Bandung District Court Decision No. 245/Pdt/G/1991/PN.Bdg are **NULL AND VOID**.
- Both the Plaintiff's Convention lawsuit (PLK) and Counterclaim Reconvention lawsuit (YBPSMK Jabar) are declared **INADMISSIBLE** (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).

### Unconsidered Substantive Issues

- Ultimate ownership rights over land and buildings of the SMAK Dago complex at \[address removed\], Bandung, **WERE NOT CONSIDERED BY THE SUPREME COURT**, as the lawsuit lapsed upon formal legal-subject testing.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Legal Certainty in Restructuring Assets of Former Prohibited Foreign Organizations:** This landmark ruling establishes rigorous procedural and organizational certainty in Indonesia: courts must not permit factions or groups to invoke prohibited foreign organizations dissolved by national statute to claim or demand rent over strategic assets (such as school complexes). Any claims brought on behalf of dissolved organizations must be declared inadmissible (NO) due to the absence of a valid legal subject.

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Does an association or organization officially dissolved by the Government under statutory law retain the right to file civil lawsuits in court pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence?  
**No Right / Inadmissible (NO).** Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence Reg. No. 3263 K/Pdt/1992:

- An association that has been dissolved has no right to file a lawsuit in civil court.
- Local associations claiming to be replacements or continuations of prohibited foreign associations dissolved *ex* Law No. 50 Prp. of 1960 lack legal capacity / legal standing (*lack of persona standi in judicio*).
- The Supreme Court annulled the lower court decisions and independently adjudicated by declaring the Plaintiff's lawsuit Inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).
