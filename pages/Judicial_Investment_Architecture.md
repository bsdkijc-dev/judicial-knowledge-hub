# Judicial Investment Architecture

Future investment is divided into seven layers.

1.  People
2.  Data
3.  Infrastructure
4.  Applications
5.  Security
6.  Governance
7.  Innovation

## Examples

**People:** AI Academy, specialists and change leaders.

**Data:** quality, metadata, governance and integration.

**Infrastructure:** secure cloud/on-premise capacity, networks and identity.

**Applications:** case services, knowledge tools and citizen services.

**Security:** cybersecurity, monitoring and incident response.

**Governance:** audit, standards, legal and ethical assurance.

**Innovation:** sandbox, pilots, evaluation and scaling.
