# Judicial AI Community of Practice

A **Judicial AI Community of Practice** is a professional network through which judicial personnel can exchange experience, lessons learned, practices, research, and governance knowledge concerning artificial intelligence.

## Objectives

Potential objectives include:

- Knowledge sharing
- Peer learning
- Experimentation
- Case-study development
- Risk sharing
- Good-practice identification
- Continuous professional development

## Possible Activities

- AI clinics
- Workshops
- Demonstrations
- Research seminars
- Prompt libraries
- Use-case reviews
- Governance discussions
- Lessons-learned sessions

## Knowledge Loop

**EXPERIMENT** → **DOCUMENT** → **EVALUATE** → **SHARE** → **STANDARDIZE** → **IMPROVE**

## Governance

A community of practice should not automatically create binding institutional policy.

Policy authority remains with the competent institution.
