# Human Rights and Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment

# Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment

High-impact judicial technology should be assessed for potential effects on fundamental rights.

## Assessment Areas

- privacy;
- equality;
- non-discrimination;
- due process;
- access to justice;
- human autonomy;
- freedom from arbitrary decision-making;
- effective remedy.

## Decision Logic

**Identify → Assess → Mitigate → Monitor → Reassess**

## Boundary

This page is a proposed research and governance framework, not a statement that every listed assessment is currently required by Indonesian law.
