## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 561 K/Pid/1982, dated July 30, 1983, established a criminal law rule regarding adultery (\*overspel\*) concerning the validity of complaints filed by husbands or wives without a prior divorce requirement.

The Supreme Court held that although jurisprudence dictates that Article 284 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code applies to a husband not subject to Article 27 of the Civil Code (\*BW\*) (such as Muslims or those subject to Law No. 1 of 1974), this does not mean that for a complaint from an aggrieved husband to be processed, there must first be a divorce between him and his adulterous wife. A complaint for the adultery complaint delict (\*klachtdelict\*) by an aggrieved husband is valid and can be criminally processed without any legal obligation to file for divorce beforehand.

## Executive Summary

The central criminal law principle established in this decision is that filing a criminal complaint for adultery does not require the aggrieved spouse to initiate divorce proceedings prior to criminal investigation and prosecution.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Adultery complaints (\*klachtdelict\*) lodged by an aggrieved spouse are fully valid on their own terms.
- Requiring prior civil divorce filings as a procedural prerequisite for criminal complaints constitutes an error of law by appellate courts.
- Defendants convicted of adultery under Article 284 of the Criminal Code are subject to custodial sentences upon cassation review.

## Case Background

The Defendant, **\[Defendant\]** (\[age removed\], farmer), was charged with rape (Article 285 KUHP / Primair) or adultery (Article 284 paragraph (1) 1st a KUHP / Subsidair) committed against Ny. \[Party C\] (wife of witness \[Party A\]) at the mBok Bawon prostitution rental house in Pelutan Village, Pemalang, in February 1979. Witness \[Party A\] (\[Party C\]'s husband) formally filed a criminal complaint regarding the act on April 6, 1980.

The Pemalang District Court (Decision No. 22/1981 KS. Pml dated February 14, 1981) acquitted the Defendant of the primary charge but convicted him on the subsidiary charge (Adultery), sentencing him to 7 months' imprisonment. The Semarang High Court (Decision No. 166/1981/Pid/PT. Smg dated November 17, 1981) annulled the Pemalang District Court decision and discharged the Defendant from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag\*) on the grounds that the complaint was not followed by a divorce application. The Public Prosecutor at the Pemalang State Prosecutor's Office subsequently filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Validity Requirements for Adultery Complaints (Article 284 KUHP jo. Law No. 1 of 1974)

The primary issue was whether a criminal complaint filed by a Muslim/non-BW husband (\[Party A\]) regarding his wife's (\[Party C\]) adultery with another man (\[Defendant\]) requires prior divorce petition proceedings in the Religious Court before criminal proceedings can be processed.

The Supreme Court held that prior divorce is not required. The Semarang High Court misapplied the law. Although adultery is an absolute complaint delict (\*absolut klachtdelict\*), the right of complaint rests solely with the aggrieved spouse. There is no legal obligation for the complainant husband to file for divorce or judicial separation (\*pisah meja dan tempat tidur\*) as a formal procedural prerequisite for criminal prosecution of adultery.

### Correction of Complainant Subject Misinterpretation by Appellate Court

The Semarang High Court previously confused the complainant subject by stating that "the complaint was not accompanied by a divorce application by husband and wife \[Defendant\]." The Supreme Court corrected this by clarifying that the complainant in this case was \[Party A\] (\[Party C\]'s husband), not the wife of Defendant \[Defendant\].

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Did the Semarang High Court misapply criminal procedural and substantive law (\*foutieve kepassing van het recht\*) by dismissing criminal prosecution for adultery and discharging the Defendant (\*ontslag\*) on the grounds that the complainant husband failed to file a divorce lawsuit?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes, the Semarang High Court misapplied the law. Divorce prerequisites do not apply to invalidate a wronged husband's complaint. The Supreme Court annulled the Semarang High Court decision and adjudicated afresh, sentencing the Defendant to 7 months' imprisonment.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Pemalang District Court & Semarang High Court)

Examination of the facts regarding sexual intercourse between Defendant \[Defendant\] (\[age removed\], farmer) and Ny. \[Party C\] (wife of witness \[Party A\]) at the mBok Bawon prostitution rental house in Pelutan Village, Pemalang, in February 1979, alongside examination of witness \[Party A\]'s formal Criminal Complaint Letter dated April 6, 1980.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The appellate \*Judex Facti\* of Semarang High Court misapplied the law. Although Article 284 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code applies to husbands not subject to Article 27 of the Civil Code (such as Muslims subject to Law No. 1 of 1974), this does not mean that an aggrieved husband's complaint requires a prior divorce from his adulterous wife.
2.  Witness \[Party A\]'s complaint was fully valid.
3.  The Supreme Court annulled the Semarang High Court decision and adjudicated afresh, convicting and sentencing the Defendant to 7 months' imprisonment.

## Ruling

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition from the Petitioner for Cassation (**PUBLIC PROSECUTOR AT THE PEMALANG STATE PROSECUTOR'S OFFICE**); annulled Semarang High Court Decision No. 166/1981/PT. Smg dated November 17, 1981; and adjudicated afresh: Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] unproven to have committed the crime charged under the Primary charge and acquitting him thereof; Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] guilty of committing the crime: "A married man committing adultery"; Sentencing the Defendant to a prison term of 7 (seven) months; and ordering the Defendant to pay court costs across all judicial instances.

### Established by Evidence

- Defendant \[Defendant\] was proven to have engaged in sexual intercourse with Ny. \[Party C\] (lawful wife of witness \[Party A\]) at the mBok Bawon rental house.
- Witness \[Party A\], as the lawful husband of Ny. \[Party C\], officially submitted a criminal complaint letter for adultery on April 6, 1980.
- The Defendant was legally and convincingly proven guilty of committing the crime of "A married man committing adultery" ex Article 284 paragraph (1) 1st a of the Criminal Code.

### Not Established / Unproven

- The Primary rape charge (Article 285 KUHP) involving violence or threat of violence was unproven, resulting in the Defendant's acquittal on the Primary charge.

### Uncertainty and Gaps

- Correction of Flawed Discharge Ruling (\*Ontslag\*) by Semarang High Court: The Semarang High Court (No. 166/1981/Pid/PT. Smg) previously annulled the Pemalang District Court's sentence and discharged the Defendant on formal complaint formalities. The Supreme Court decisively corrected this error to uphold legal certainty in protecting the harmony and dignity of the marital institution from adultery.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: Must a criminal complaint for adultery (Article 284 KUHP) filed by a husband or wife be accompanied by a prior divorce petition?**

**A:** No, it is not required. Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 561 K/Pid/1982, entertaining a complaint for the adultery delict from an aggrieved husband or wife does not require a prior divorce between the husband and the adulterous wife. The complaint remains valid, and the adulterer can be prosecuted and imprisoned without any obligation to file a divorce lawsuit in court.
