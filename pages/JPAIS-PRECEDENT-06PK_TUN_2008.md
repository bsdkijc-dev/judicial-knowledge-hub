## Overview

Supreme Court Judicial Review Decision No. 06 PK/TUN/2008 rejects the Petition for Judicial Review (PK) filed by the Minister of Forestry of the Republic of Indonesia against the Oil Palm Plantation Cooperative (KPKS) Bukit Harapan concerning the annulment of the principle approval for oil palm plantation management within the Register 40 Padang Lawas Forest Area. The Supreme Court affirmed that divergent legal interpretations between a PK petitioner and the Judex Juris cannot be categorized as a judicial error or a manifest mistake. Furthermore, a criminal conviction against an individual (Darianus Lungguk Sitorus) is irrelevant to invalidating an administrative decision that granted valid permits to a cooperative legal entity.

## Case Background

Indigenous communities in Luhat Simangambat and Luhat Ujung Batu (North Sumatra) partnered with PT Torganda and established the Oil Palm Plantation Cooperative (KPKS) Bukit Harapan to manage oil palm plantations in former customary/ulayat land areas. The Minister of Forestry issued Letter No. 1680/Menhut-III/2002 dated September 26, 2002, granting principle approval for managing a 23,000-hectare area. However, on October 13, 2004, the Minister of Forestry issued Letter No. S.419/Menhut-II/2004, unilaterally revoking the principle approval. KPKS Bukit Harapan filed an annulment lawsuit at the Jakarta State Administrative Court (PTUN). The Jakarta PTUN granted the Plaintiff's claim, but the Jakarta High State Administrative Court overturned the ruling. At the cassation level (Decision No. 134 K/TUN/2007), the Supreme Court reversed the appellate decision and granted KPKS Bukit Harapan's claim. The Minister of Forestry subsequently filed a Petition for Judicial Review citing a manifest error regarding lawsuit time limits and newly discovered evidence (\*novum\*) in the form of criminal cassation decision No. 2642 K/Pid/2006.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  Differences in opinion or legal interpretation within a PK memorandum between a PK Petitioner and the Judex Juris (the Supreme Court's cassation decision) essentially constitute a divergence in legal interpretation and therefore cannot be deemed or categorized as a judicial error or manifest mistake under Article 67 letter f of Law No. 5 of 2004.
2.  Administrative negligence by an Administrative Official in dispatching an administrative decision to a citizen, which causes a shift in the lawsuit filing timeframe, constitutes an administrative fault and must not become a burden that prejudices the Plaintiff's rights as a seeker of justice.
3.  The criminal verdict against Darianus Lungguk Sitorus (Director of PT Torganda) cannot be linked or connected to the lawful legal actions of KPKS Bukit Harapan as a distinct legal entity that obtained valid permits from an Administrative Official.

- **Ruling:** Rejecting the petition for judicial review from the PK Petitioner: THE MINISTER OF FORESTRY OF THE REPUBLIC OF INDONESIA; Ordering the PK Petitioner / Defendant to pay judicial review examination costs amounting to Rp2,500,000 (two million five hundred thousand rupiahs).
