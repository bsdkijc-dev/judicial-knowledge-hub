# Judicial-policy-ijca-issue-44-2013

**International Journal for Court Administration (Issue 44)** is an official publication issue of the International Association for Court Administration (IACA), containing an executive editorial analyzing executive political interference with the Extraordinary Chambers in the Courts of Cambodia (ECCC), a managing editorial on the Commission for the Efficiency of Justice, and comparative court management research from the United States, Australia, Switzerland, and Europe.

## Summary

This issue features a pointed commentary from Executive Editor Markus Zimmer on UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon's visit to Cambodia, during which Prime Minister Hun Sen unilaterally restricted the ECCC's jurisdiction to trying only four senior Khmer Rouge leaders and blocked prosecution of five additional suspects. The issue also includes scholarly articles on the High Performance Court Framework, the application of system dynamics at the Neighbourhood Justice Centre in Yarra, the first empirical study of caseload management in Swiss administrative and social insurance courts by Andreas Lienhard and Daniel Kettiger, and the differentiated use of electronic case management systems (ECMS) by Erwin Rooze.

- **International Treaty Framework Underlying the ECCC:** The agreement between the United Nations (UN) and the Royal Government of Cambodia establishing the ECCC.

## Background & Policy Analysis of the ECCC

UN Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon's visit to Phnom Penh exposed the political obstacles facing the ECCC. Cambodian Prime Minister Hun Sen asked the UN to close its local human rights office and refused to allow the ECCC's investigation to expand to five additional officials. The tribunal's lack of enforcement authority meant it repeatedly failed to summon senior government officials as witnesses, who consistently evaded subpoenas through technical maneuvering.

## Scope of Research Articles

1.  **High Performance Court Framework:** Research by Roger Hanson, Brian Ostrom, and Matthew Kleiman on achieving high court performance.
2.  **System Dynamics & Community Justice:** An analysis of organizational learning processes at the Neighbourhood Justice Centre in Yarra (Tim Hasslett, Chris Ballenden, Saroj Godbole, Kerry Walker).
3.  **Swiss Judicial Caseload Management:** The methodology and results of the first empirical study conducted in Swiss administrative and social insurance courts, by Prof. Andreas Lienhard and Daniel Kettiger.
4.  **Electronic Case Management Systems (ECMS):** A study of the differentiated use of ECMS by Erwin Rooze.

## Policy Issues

1\. **Executive Interference in a Hybrid Tribunal:** Restrictions imposed by the Cambodian government on the number of prosecutable suspects undermined the independence of international prosecution. 2. **Exclusion of Mid-Level Perpetrators:** The ECCC's narrow focus on top leadership left mid-level perpetrators untried, fueling public dissatisfaction in Cambodia. 3. **The Need for Objective Workload Measurement:** The importance of scientific workload measurement methods for administrative courts in Switzerland.
