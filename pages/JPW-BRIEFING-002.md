# JPW-BRIEFING-002

**Statements for the Fifth Diplomatic Briefing of the International Criminal Court** is an official publication of the International Criminal Court (ICC) detailing its headquarters contact details in The Hague, Netherlands, and announcing an extension of the nomination period for Assembly of States Parties (ASP) candidates beyond 6 November. Note: The majority of the source file text encountered corrupted character extraction \[1–74\].

## Summary

This document contains the official ICC headquarters address, phone and fax numbers, and notices regarding the availability of candidate details and nomination period extensions for the Assembly of States Parties \[1–74\].

## Objectives

- Provide official ICC headquarters contact details.
- Announce the availability of candidate information and nomination extensions beyond 6 November \[1–74\].

## Key Provisions

- \*\*ICC Headquarters\*\*: Located at Maanweg 174, 2516 AB The Hague, The Netherlands.
- \*\*ASP Nominations\*\*: Candidate information and nomination deadline extensions beyond 6 November are accessible via \`www.icc-cpi.int\` \[1–74\].
