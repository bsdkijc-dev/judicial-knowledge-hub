# JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:JPAIS-MED-REF-003

## Summary / Ringkasan

## Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

- Entity ID: JPAIS-MED-REF-003
- Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
- Ingestion Status: Confirmed

## Description / Deskripsi

Provides a comparative assessment of court case management between Indonesia and Japan, examining case intake, tracking, digital architectures (SIPP, EIS, e-Court, e-Berpadu vs. Japanese CCP amendments), and institutional bottlenecks, and proposes targeted administrative reforms and a 90-day pilot implementation plan\[cite: 3\].

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-001): EIS platform data aggregation masks granular non-compliance, forcing supervisory judges into manual audits across active SIPP case files\[cite: 3\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-002): SEMA No. 1 of 2023 postal summons delivery failures in rural regions lead to undelivered notices, default judgments (verstek), and appellate remands\[cite: 3\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-003): e-Berpadu digital criminal integration suffers from manual paper fallback exceptions during network downtime, causing data discrepancies with SIPP\[cite: 3\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-004](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-004): Legacy criminal procedure (KUHAP Law No. 8 of 1981) creates statutory gaps and procedural challenges for electronic trials and digital evidence transfers\[cite: 3\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-001): Dual-track intake via e-Court (PERMA No. 1/2019, PERMA No. 7/2022) and e-Berpadu (PERMA No. 8/2022), tracked through SIPP and evaluated via EIS\[cite: 3\].

## Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-001): Unified civil litigation processing under the 2022 Code of Civil Procedure Amendment, featuring front-loaded pre-trial issue organization (Preparatory Proceedings) and high pre-trial settlement rates via Chotei\[cite: 3\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-001): Indonesia prioritizes strict administrative timelines (5-month SEMA target) and compliance scoring over pre-trial issue formulation, creating lower court trial compression\[cite: 3\].
- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-002): Japan's historical reliance on physical documentation slowed digital adoption, and judicial performance metrics risk "mediatory adjudication" pressures\[cite: 3\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Upgrade EIS to version 4.0 for granular anomaly detection, establish an address verification API bridge with Dukcapil and PT Pos, codify pre-trial issue formulation conferences into a new PERMA, and permanently codify digital procedures into primary legislation (RUU KUHAP and RUU Hukum Acara Perdata)\[cite: 3\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-001): Form steering committee, deploy EIS 4.0 granular module, and establish API interoperability for postal verification\[cite: 3\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-002): Execute 90-day pilot across five Class IA Khusus and Class IA District Courts (PN Jakarta Pusat, PN Surakarta, PN Surabaya, PN Makassar, PN Medan)\[cite: 3\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-003): Monitor performance via automated dashboards, conduct mid-term reviews, and deliver national scaling blueprint\[cite: 3\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-003): 90-day pilot program across PN Jakarta Pusat, PN Surakarta, PN Surabaya, PN Makassar, and PN Medan\[cite: 3\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-003): EIS audit time burden \$\< 1.5\$ hours/week; summons delivery accuracy \$\> 92\\\$; average civil duration \$\< 3.5\$ months; e-Berpadu manual bypass \$\< 3\\\$; pre-trial issue narrowing \$\> 80\\\$; EIS compliance score \$\> 98.5\\\$\[cite: 3\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Establish inter-agency steering committees between Mahkamah Agung, PT Pos Indonesia, and the Ministry of Home Affairs to streamline digital workflows and address verification\[cite: 3\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgate a new PERMA mandating Pre-Trial Issue Formulation Conferences and pass legislative amendments to national procedural codes\[cite: 3\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Indonesian Supreme Court handled 31,138 cases in 2024 with a 99.26% clearance rate, while lower courts registered 410,754 e-Court cases\[cite: 3\].
- Japanese district courts handle ~216,000 filings annually, with summary courts handling ~1.45 million filings and Chotei resolving over 50% of civil disputes\[cite: 3\].
