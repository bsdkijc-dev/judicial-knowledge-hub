# AI Verification

**AI verification** is the process of checking AI-generated content against reliable evidence before relying upon it.

It is a core principle for high-stakes judicial knowledge.

## Verification Chain

**AI CLAIM** → **SOURCE** → **ORIGINAL DOCUMENT** → **PROPOSITION CHECK** → **CONTEXT CHECK** → **HUMAN REVIEW**

## Verification Questions

The reviewer should ask:

1.  Does the cited source exist?
2.  Does the source actually support the claim?
3.  Is the source current?
4.  Has the law been amended?
5.  Has the decision been reversed or distinguished?
6.  Is important context missing?
7.  Is the AI making an inference rather than reporting a source?

## Verification Levels

| Level | Meaning               |
|-------|-----------------------|
| V0    | Not verified          |
| V1    | Source identified     |
| V2    | Source checked        |
| V3    | Proposition checked   |
| V4    | Human expert verified |

## High-Stakes Principle

The greater the legal or institutional consequence of an AI output, the stronger the verification requirement should be.
