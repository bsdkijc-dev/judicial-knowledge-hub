# Judicial Independence

# Safeguarding Judicial Independence

Safeguarding judicial independence is the first mission of the Supreme Court.

## Institutional Dimensions

Judicial independence includes:

- institutional independence;
- administrative independence;
- financial independence;
- functional independence of judges.

## Strategic Principle

Institutional independence must support the ability of courts to perform judicial functions without inappropriate interference.
