# AI Risk Classification

Judicial AI systems should receive risk-based governance rather than uniform controls.

| Tier                    | Example                                     | Control                                              |
|-------------------------|---------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Low                     | Translation or formatting assistance        | Standard security and quality checks                 |
| Moderate                | Administrative classification               | Human review and monitoring                          |
| High                    | Workload or case-management recommendations | Impact assessment, audit and strong human oversight  |
| Prohibited / Restricted | Autonomous judicial decision                | Not permitted as a substitute for judicial authority |

## Principle

**Higher potential impact → stronger governance.**
