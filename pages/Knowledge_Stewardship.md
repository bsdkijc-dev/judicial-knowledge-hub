# Knowledge Stewardship

# Knowledge Stewardship

Knowledge stewardship assigns responsibility for the quality and continued validity of institutional knowledge.

## Responsibilities

- curate;
- verify;
- classify;
- update;
- archive;
- connect;
- preserve provenance.

## Principle

**Every important knowledge object needs stewardship.**
