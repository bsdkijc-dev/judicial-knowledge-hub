## 4. POLICY ANALYSIS

- **Background**: \[FACT\] The Ministry of Justice of Spain designed a comprehensive digital transformation project under the Justice 2030 Strategic Plan to build a modern, efficient, agile, intelligent, and transparent judicial system. Financed through the European Union's Recovery, Transformation and Resilience Plan (Next Generation EU), the initiative places Spain among the top 5 EU member states in digital public services.
- **Policy Problem**: \[FACT\] Slow traditional court procedures, heavy reliance on paper records, high travel costs and logistical time for citizens and legal professionals, procedural opacity, lack of system alignment across Autonomous Communities, and accessibility barriers for persons with disabilities.
- **Legal/Policy Gap**: \[FACT\] Absence of automated interoperability among civil registries, hospitals, and social security institutions (INSS); file size restrictions preventing the electronic transmission of large judicial documents exceeding 100MB; and the need for standardized digital courtroom setups across Spain's decentralized regional administration.
- **Objectives**: \[FACT\] Eliminate the necessity for physical travel, reduce waiting times, transition toward data-driven justice, automate and robotize repetitive administrative tasks, ensure secure electronic document exchange, and guarantee equal access for vulnerable groups and persons with disabilities through specialized digital platforms.
- **Implementation**: \[FACT\]
  - **Citizen Services**: Launch of the Justice Folder, deployment of over 7,700 Justice Offices in Municipalities, execution of more than 1,030,000 Telematic Trials since January 2020, and the expansion of DICIREG across 17 Autonomous Communities.
  - **Professional Portals**: LexNET Justicia managed 150,716,644 notifications and 26,370,991 procedural documents in H1-2023; handling of large files exceeding 100MB via Acceda; and operation of the HORUS viewer with data retrieval times under 20 seconds.
  - **Electronic Signatures**: Portafirmas 4.2 featuring cloud signatures (\*firma en la nube\*) and the @Mobile Signature APK, generating over 113 million digital signatures.
  - **Courtroom Infrastructure**: Modernized courtrooms equipped with Podium setups (IP cameras & microphones), Auxiliary Posts, Racks (Fidelius application), and designated Videoconference areas.
  - **Data-Driven Justice & Robotization**: Implementation of Datalab saving €6.2 million on CITA PREVIA appointment services; robotization of juvenile criminal record cancellations (\>19,000 cancellations processed); and automated processing of citizenship by residency applications (\>170,000 proposals submitted).
  - **AI Projects**: Development of Recording Textualisation Systems, legal/forensic dictation tools, and Document Anonymizers.
  - **EVID**: Deployment of the Digital Immediation Virtual Desktop (EVID) for secure videoconference hearings and adapted communication devices for persons with disabilities.
  - **Interoperability & Governance**: Data exchange via SIR (\>273,000 files transmitted); international connectivity via ECRIS, e-CODEX, and the e-EDES Portal; and co-governance via CTEAJE (Ministry of Justice, CGPJ, Prosecutor General's Office, and CCAATT).
- **Evidence & Impact**: \[FACT\]
  - **Efficiency & Cost Savings**: The Acceda platform processed over 370,000 procedures, generating cost savings exceeding €53,000,000, reducing 3,300 tons of CO2 emissions, equivalent to taking 380,000 vehicles off the road for a year.
  - **International Standing**: Spain ranks in the top 5 European Union countries for digital public services.
- **Reform & Unresolved Issues**: \[FACT\] Full integration of DICIREG with all judicial procedural management systems through 2024 under Next Generation EU funding, alongside continuous enhancements in proactive automation to prevent digital inequality.

    - **Factual Answer**: \[FACT\] A national strategic project under the Justice 2030 Strategic Plan developed by the Ministry of Justice of Spain and GDDTAJ to create a modern, efficient, agile, and data-driven judicial administration.

    - **Factual Answer**: \[FACT\] Funded by the European Union through the Recovery, Transformation and Resilience Plan – Next Generation EU, and established under the Justice 2030 Strategic Plan.

    - **Factual Answer**: \[FACT\] Published by the General Directorate for Digital Transformation of the Administration of Justice (GDDTAJ). 4.  **Related Institutions**: Which public bodies compose the co-governance body CTEAJE?
    - **Factual Answer**: \[FACT\] CTEAJE is composed of the Ministry of Justice, the General Council of the Judiciary (CGPJ), the Prosecutor General's Office, and Autonomous Communities with transferred competencies (CCAATT), with the presidency rotating every 2 years between the Ministry of Justice and the CGPJ.
5.  **Scope**: How many Justice Offices in Municipalities and Telematic Trials have been deployed and executed under this project?
    - **Factual Answer**: \[FACT\] Over 7,700 municipal justice offices have been deployed, and more than 1,030,000 telematic trial proceedings have been conducted nationwide since January 2020.
6.  **Policy Implementation**: How do LexNET and Portafirmas contribute to electronic judicial file management?
    - **Factual Answer**: \[FACT\] In H1-2023, LexNET processed 150,716,644 notifications and 26,370,991 procedural documents, while Portafirmas 4.2 facilitated over 113 million digital signatures using centralized cloud certificates and the @Mobile Signature APK.
7.  **Policy Problem**: What environmental and financial savings were achieved through the Acceda-Justicia platform?
    - **Factual Answer**: \[FACT\] By processing over 370,000 procedures, Acceda saved more than €53,000,000 in costs, reduced 3,300 tons of CO2 emissions, and achieved emission savings equivalent to removing over 380,000 cars from operation for a year.
8.  **Legal Relations**: Which platforms does Spain use for EU-wide exchange of criminal records and judicial information?
    - **Factual Answer**: \[FACT\] Spain uses ECRIS (European Criminal Record Information System), e-CODEX, and the e-EDES Portal for cross-border European Investigation Orders.
9.  **Reform & Robotization**: What automation milestones were achieved in criminal record cancellations and citizenship applications?
    - **Factual Answer**: \[FACT\] The system processed over 170,000 citizenship-by-residency application proposals (July 2022–September 2023) and automatically cancelled over 19,000 juvenile criminal records between October 2022 and September 2023.
10. **Accessibility Evidence**: How does the EVID system ensure courtroom accessibility for persons with disabilities?
    - **Factual Answer**: \[FACT\] EVID (Digital Immediation Virtual Desktop) provides adapted tablet-sized devices evolved from classic mobile phones, allowing individuals with disabilities to participate in court hearings securely from support centers.

## 6. MEDIAWIKI OUTPUT

## Summary

**Digital Transformation of Justice (Spain, 2023)** (Stable ID: **JPW-WORK-000023**) is a national strategic project executed by the *General Directorate for Digital Transformation of the Administration of Justice* (GDDTAJ) under the Ministry of Justice of Spain. Published in October 2023 under the *Justice 2030 Strategic Plan* and funded by European Union *Next Generation EU* grants, the project modernizes judicial administration into an agile, transparent, and data-driven system. Key pillars include the development of the \*Justice Folder\*, over 7,700 \*Justice Offices in Municipalities\*, over 1.03 million telematic trials, \*LexNET\* document management, \*Portafirmas\* e-signatures, automated robotization of criminal record cancellations, AI applications, and the disability-accessible \*EVID\* virtual courtroom platform.

## Legal Basis

Spain's judicial digital transformation is grounded in national strategic policies and European Union funding instruments:

- **Plan Justicia 2030**: The national strategic framework of the Ministry of Justice of Spain for modernizing public judicial services.
- **Recovery, Transformation and Resilience Plan (Next Generation EU)**: The European Union funding mechanism supporting procedural management integration and \*DICIREG\*.
- **EU Interoperability Framework**: Cross-border criminal justice data exchange via \*ECRIS\*, \*e-CODEX\*, and the \*e-EDES Portal\*.

## Background

Under the \*Justice 2030 Strategic Plan\*, Spain initiated a comprehensive digital modernization of its legal system to address administrative backlogs, paper dependency, and regional fragmentation across Autonomous Communities. Continuous ICT investments have positioned Spain among the top 5 European Union nations in digital public service efficiency.

## Objectives

1\. Eliminate mandatory physical travel through telematic trials and local municipal justice offices. 2. Reduce case processing times and accelerate electronic judicial file (\*EJE\*) management. 3. Advance toward a data-driven, intelligent, and transparent judicial system. 4. Automate and robotize repetitive administrative workflows. 5. Guarantee digital accessibility for persons with disabilities and vulnerable groups.

## Scope

The policy framework spans six main operational pillars: 1. *Citizen & Municipal Services*: \*Justice Folder\*, 7,700+ municipal offices, telematic trials, and \*DICIREG\*. 2. *Professional Portals*: \*LexNET Justicia\*, \*HORUS file viewer\*, \*Acceda-Justicia\*, and \*Portafirmas 4.2\*. 3. *Courtroom Infrastructure*: Digital courtroom setups featuring \*Podium\*, \*Auxiliary Posts\*, \*Racks\* (\*Fidelius\*), and \*Videoconferencing\*. 4. *Data-Driven Justice & Robotization*: The \*Datalab\* system, automated criminal record cancellation, and citizenship application processing. 5. *Artificial Intelligence (AI)*: Recording textualisation, forensic dictation, and \*Document Anonymizers\*. 6. *Interoperability & Co-governance*: Inter-agency data exchange via \*SIR\*, European integration through \*ECRIS\* and \*e-CODEX\*, and coordination via \*CTEAJE\*.

## Key Provisions

- **DICIREG Integration**: Interconnecting civil registries with hospitals, notaries, and judicial bodies across 17 Autonomous Communities.
- **High-Capacity File Exchange**: Enabling the transmission of large judicial files exceeding 100MB via the \*Acceda\* platform.
- **Automated Criminal Record Cancellation**: Robotized processing that automatically cancels juvenile criminal records and expedites residency-based citizenship claims.
- **Digital Immediation Virtual Desktop (EVID)**: Provision of secure virtual courtroom environments and adapted mobile/tablet devices for individuals with disabilities.

## Implementation

Implementation is managed continuously by the GDDTAJ alongside the Ministry of Justice, CGPJ, Prosecutor General's Office, and Autonomous Communities through periodic coordination in CTEAJE and the \*Justice Digital Transformation Forum\*.

## Policy Problems

- Technological coordination challenges in a decentralized state with Autonomous Communities holding transferred judicial competencies (CCAATT).
- Managing massive data volumes requiring high-capacity file transfer solutions above 100MB.
- Ensuring equitable digital access across remote or rural populations.

## Reform

- Achieving full operational integration of \*DICIREG\* across all procedural management systems through 2024.
- Deployment of \*Portafirmas 4.2\* featuring cloud-based signatures (\*firma en la nube\*) and mobile signature apps.

## Relationships with Other Entities

- **Implements**: Plan Justicia 2030 & Recovery, Transformation and Resilience Plan (EU).
- **Applies to**: Ministry of Justice of Spain, GDDTAJ, CGPJ, Prosecutor General's Office, CCAATT, INSS, legal professionals, and citizens.
