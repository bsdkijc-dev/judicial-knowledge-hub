# Initiative Portfolio Dashboard

# Initiative Portfolio Dashboard

The dashboard provides an institutional view of all strategic initiatives.

## Portfolio Metrics

- total initiatives;
- initiatives by portfolio;
- initiatives by risk tier;
- initiatives by phase;
- budget;
- KPI performance;
- dependencies;
- pilots;
- evaluations;
- scaled initiatives;
- retired initiatives.

## Executive Question

**Are resources being allocated to the initiatives most likely to produce measurable justice outcomes?**
