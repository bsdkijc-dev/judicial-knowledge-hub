# Legal-Tech Fellowship

The Legal-Tech Fellowship is proposed to develop multidisciplinary professionals able to connect law, technology, data and institutional processes.

## Potential Participants

- judges;
- legal researchers;
- technology specialists;
- data professionals;
- policy professionals.

## Strategic Objective

Create a bridge between judicial expertise and emerging legal technology.
