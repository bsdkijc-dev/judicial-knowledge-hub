# Digital Evidence

# Digital Evidence

**Digital evidence** refers to information in digital form that may be relevant to a legal proceeding.

Its admissibility, authenticity, integrity, relevance, and evidentiary weight depend on the applicable law and procedure.

**\[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## Examples

Potential forms include:

- Electronic documents
- Emails
- Messages
- Photographs
- Videos
- Audio recordings
- Metadata
- System logs
- Database records
- Digital signatures

## Evidence Lifecycle

**CREATION** → **COLLECTION** → **PRESERVATION** → **AUTHENTICATION** → **PRESENTATION** → **EVALUATION**

## Integrity

Relevant considerations may include:

- Hash values
- Metadata
- Chain of custody
- Access logs
- Original files
- Copies
- Transformation history

Specific requirements require legal verification.

## AI and Digital Evidence

AI may assist with:

- Classification
- Search
- Transcription
- OCR
- Entity extraction
- Timeline construction
- Duplicate detection

AI-generated analysis should be distinguished from the underlying evidence.
