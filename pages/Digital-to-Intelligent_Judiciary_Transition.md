# Digital-to-Intelligent Judiciary Transition

The proposed transformation consists of fifteen structural shifts.

1.  Paper Court → Digital-by-Default Court
2.  E-Court → Intelligent Court
3.  Case Management → Intelligent Case Management
4.  Legal Research → AI-Augmented Legal Research
5.  Judicial Knowledge → Judicial Knowledge Graph
6.  Institutional Memory → Machine-Readable Judicial Memory
7.  Reactive Justice → Preventive and Early-Warning Justice
8.  Manual Supervision → Data-Driven Integrity
9.  Training → Continuous Judicial Learning
10. ICT Skills → AI, Data and Legal-Tech Competency
11. Court User → Justice Journey Citizen
12. Closed Data → Responsible Open Justice Data
13. Individual Innovation → Institutional Innovation
14. Court Network → One Indonesian Judiciary Ecosystem
15. Digital Judiciary → Trusted Intelligent Judiciary
