# Transparent Administrative Audit Trail

# Transparent Administrative Audit Trail

The initiative proposes stronger traceability of administrative actions.

## Audit Information

Where appropriate, systems should preserve:

- who performed an action;
- when it occurred;
- what changed;
- which workflow was used;
- relevant approvals;
- system-generated events.

## Objective

Improve accountability without exposing protected information.
