# AI Ethics

**AI ethics** concerns principles for developing and using artificial intelligence in ways that respect human dignity, rights, fairness, responsibility, and institutional values.

## Potential Principles

- Human autonomy
- Human dignity
- Fairness
- Accountability
- Transparency
- Privacy
- Safety
- Non-discrimination
- Professional responsibility

## Judicial Context

Judicial AI ethics has additional significance because courts exercise public authority and protect legal rights.

## Ethical Questions

Before deployment, institutions may ask:

1.  What purpose does the system serve?
2.  Who benefits?
3.  Who may be harmed?
4.  What assumptions are embedded?
5.  What data is used?
6.  Can errors be detected?
7.  Can humans override the system?
8.  Can affected persons challenge outcomes?

## Ethics vs Law

An AI application may be:

- Legally permitted but ethically problematic;
- Ethically desirable but legally unauthorized; or
- Both legally and ethically acceptable.

Legal compliance therefore does not necessarily exhaust ethical assessment.
