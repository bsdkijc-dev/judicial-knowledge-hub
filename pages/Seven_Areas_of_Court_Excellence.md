# Seven Areas of Court Excellence

# Seven Areas of Court Excellence

The Blueprint uses a Court Excellence framework consisting of seven major areas.

## Seven Areas

1.  Court leadership and management
2.  Court policies
3.  Human resources, infrastructure and finance
4.  Court proceedings
5.  Needs and satisfaction of court users
6.  Affordable court services
7.  Public trust and confidence in the courts

## Strategic Structure

The seven areas are grouped into:

- drivers and controls;
- systems and enablers;
- results.
