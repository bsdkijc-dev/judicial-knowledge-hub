# Initiative Retirement

# Initiative Retirement

Retirement formally ends an initiative, system or capability that is no longer justified.

## Retirement Triggers

- obsolete technology;
- unacceptable risk;
- inadequate results;
- excessive cost;
- legal incompatibility;
- replacement;
- strategic reprioritisation.

## Requirement

Retirement should preserve relevant institutional knowledge and lessons learned.
