# Evidence-Based Judicial Policies

# Evidence-Based Judicial Policy

**Evidence-Based Judicial Policy** is an approach to policy development that systematically uses relevant evidence alongside law, institutional values, professional judgment, and stakeholder considerations.

## Policy Cycle

**PROBLEM** → **EVIDENCE** → **OPTIONS** → **ASSESSMENT** → **DECISION** → **IMPLEMENTATION** → **EVALUATION**

## Evidence Types

Potential evidence includes:

- Legal evidence
- Empirical evidence
- Administrative data
- Research
- Comparative evidence
- Expert knowledge
- User experience

## Evidence Hierarchy

Not every evidence type answers the same question.

The decision-maker should consider:

- Relevance
- Quality
- Reliability
- Timeliness
- Context

## AI Role

AI can assist with evidence synthesis.

However:

**AI SYNTHESIS ≠ EVIDENCE**

The underlying sources must remain traceable.
