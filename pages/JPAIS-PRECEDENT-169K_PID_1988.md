## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 169 K/Pid/1988 dated March 17, 1988, establishes fundamental Criminal Procedure jurisprudence regarding the validity of court judgments: A High Court that fails to include complete details of the Defendant's identity (such as place and date of birth) and fails to state the Defendant's detention status in the dispositive portion of its judgment, as mandated by Article 197 paragraph (1) letters b and k of the KUHAP, renders the High Court judgment null and void (\*van rechtswege nietig\*) pursuant to Article 197 paragraph (2) of the KUHAP. The Supreme Court consequently annulled the High Court judgment and independently adjudicated the narcotics and maltreatment criminal case.

## Case Background

The Defendant (\[Defendant\], \[age removed\], entrepreneur, Sukabumi) was brought before the Cibadak District Court facing two charges:

- **First Charge (Article 23 paragraph (3) jo. Article 36 paragraph (3) letter a of Law No. 9 of 1976 on Narcotics):** Unlawfully possessing, storing to possess, or controlling narcotics consisting of 356.4 milligrams of marijuana and 10 BK pills on April 4, 1987, around Hotel Pondok Dewata, Pelabuhan Ratu.
- **Second Charge (Article 351 paragraph (1) of the KUHP):** Intentionally committing maltreatment against witness \[Party B\] using a machete (\*golok\*), resulting in a laceration on the lower right elbow. The maltreatment occurred because the Defendant, in a state of intoxication, was mobbed/pushed by a group of people, fled, and grabbed a machete belonging to a young coconut vendor by the roadside.

The Cibadak District Court (Decision No. 69/Pid.B/1987/PN.Cbd dated August 22, 1987) sentenced the Defendant to 10 months of imprisonment and a fine of Rp 200,000, subsidiary 3 months of confinement. The Bandung High Court (Decision No. 231/Pid/B/1987 dated October 21, 1987) amended the Cibadak District Court judgment by sentencing the Defendant to 1 (one) year of imprisonment and a fine of Rp 300,000, subsidiary 3 months of confinement. Dissatisfied with the Bandung High Court ruling, the Public Prosecutor at the Cibadak State Prosecutor's Office filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The Bandung High Court erred in applying the law because it failed to include the Defendant's complete identity (place/date of birth) and failed to state the Defendant's detention status in the dispositive portion of its judgment, as required by Article 197 paragraph (1) letters b and k of the KUHAP.
2.  Pursuant to Article 197 paragraph (2) of the KUHAP, the failure to satisfy the requirements under Article 197 paragraph (1) letters b and k of the KUHAP renders the Bandung High Court judgment null and void (\*van rechtswege nietig\*).
3.  Considering that the High Court has examined the case and in the interest of the principles of fast, low-cost, and simple judicial proceedings, the Supreme Court independently adjudicated the matter. Regarding the substance of the case, the first charge (narcotics) and second charge (maltreatment) were legally and convincingly proven. Taking into account mitigating circumstances (the Defendant is young, frank, supports small children, and committed the maltreatment out of fear of being mobbed), the Defendant is sentenced to 1 (one) year of imprisonment and a fine of Rp 300,000.

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation: Public Prosecutor at the State Prosecutor's Office in Cibadak; Declaring null and void (\*batal demi hukum\*) the Bandung High Court decision dated October 21, 1987, No. 231/Pid/B/1987; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] legally and convincingly proven guilty of committing the crimes of "Unlawfully possessing marijuana-type narcotics" and "Maltreatment"; (2) Sentencing the Defendant to 1 (one) year of imprisonment; (3) Ordering that the period of detention already served be deducted in full; (4) Sentencing the Defendant to pay a fine of Rp 300,000, subsidiary 3 (three) months of confinement; (5) Ordering that evidence items (356.4 grams of dried marijuana leaves, 10 BK pills, 1 machete) be confiscated for destruction; (6) Ordering that the Defendant remain in detention; (7) Ordering the Defendant to pay court costs across all levels of jurisdiction (cassation assessed at Rp 2,500). \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Cassation Panel of Justices on Wednesday, March 2, 1988, and pronounced in an open session on Thursday, March 17, 1988, by Soerjono, S.H. \[Presiding Judge\], Bismar Siregar, S.H., and Mohammad Djanis, S.H.).\*
