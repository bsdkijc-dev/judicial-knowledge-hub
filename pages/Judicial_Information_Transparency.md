# Judicial Information Transparency

Transparency is identified as a major component of judicial accountability.

## Strategic Objectives

Transparency supports:

- accountability;
- public access;
- institutional legitimacy;
- public trust.

## Historical Milestone

The Blueprint refers to Supreme Court Decision No. 144/KMA/SK/VIII/2007 concerning information transparency.

## AI Connection

Structured public information creates a foundation for:

- research;
- knowledge discovery;
- transparency;
- policy intelligence.
