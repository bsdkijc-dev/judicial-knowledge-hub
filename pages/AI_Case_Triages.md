# AI Case Triages

# AI Case Triage

AI Case Triage may assist administrative identification and routing of case characteristics.

## Boundary

It must not determine substantive judicial outcomes or unlawfully influence judicial allocation.

## Controls

- human review;
- transparency;
- bias assessment;
- audit;
- appeal/correction mechanism.
