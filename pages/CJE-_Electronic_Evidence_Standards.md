# CJE: Electronic Evidence Standards

# Module 2: Electronic Evidence Standards

*Continuing Judicial Education (CJE) — Advanced Curriculum*

## Admissibility Verification Framework

Before any digital artifact can be formally entered into the case record, it must satisfy three criteria:

1.  **Integrity:** Proven via strict cryptographic hash matching (SHA-256).
2.  **Provenance:** A clear, documented chain of custody tracking who handled the evidence drive.
3.  **Relevance:** Direct structural link to the claims being litigated.
