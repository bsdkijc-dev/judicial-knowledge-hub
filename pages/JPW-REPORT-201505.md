# JPW-REPORT-201505

## Summary

This document is an official judicial policy research report published by the Judicial Policy Research Institute (JPRI) of the Supreme Court of Korea. The study develops 7 level-differentiated mock trial scripts along with a comprehensive guidance manual for judges serving as instructors in middle and high school court experience programs. The initiative directly addresses the historical imbalance of mock trial materials—which heavily favored criminal cases—by balancing civil and criminal proceedings, aligning legal complexity with students' educational levels, and fostering legal literacy and public trust in the Korean judiciary.

## Legal Basis

The operational framework and guidelines analyzed in this report include:

**Law-Related Education (LRE) Framework (법교육)**: A public legal education initiative designed to build legal awareness, civic responsibility, and critical thinking among secondary students.

**Advanced Court Experience Program Guidelines (고급 법원체험프로그램)**: Internal court regulations governing student visits and mock trial execution supervised by judges acting as educational instructors (지도관).

## Background

Scripted mock trials have proven to be an effective law-related education tool for secondary students. However, traditional mock trial scenarios exhibited significant structural flaws:

Excessive reliance on criminal cases (형사 사건 편중 현상), which skewed student understanding of the broader judicial system and ignored civil litigation.

A lack of differentiation between the cognitive and legal educational levels of middle school versus high school students.

The absence of a standardized operational manual for judges conducting court experience programs.

## Objectives

Develop 7 level-differentiated mock trial scripts tailored for middle school (3 scripts) and high school (4 scripts) students.

Establish a quantitative balance between civil and criminal litigation in educational scenarios.

Formulate a standardized, 3-stage guidance manual for judges (법관을 위한 모의재판 지도안) leading mock trial programs.

## Scope

**Middle School Scripts (3 Scenarios)**: 1 civil case and 2 criminal cases.

**High School Scripts (4 Scenarios)**: 2 civil cases and 2 criminal cases.

**Judicial Operational Manual**: A structured, 3-stage instructional guide (Pre-trial, Trial, and Post-trial) for judge-instructors.

## Key Provisions

**Scenario Development Principles**:

`Clear level separation between middle and high school educational tiers.`  
`Topic selection based on real-world incidents and everyday situations to maximize engagement.`  
`A balanced mix of civil and criminal cases.`  
`Legal complexity set slightly above the students' current educational baseline.`

**Three-Stage Guidance Manual for Judges**:

**`Pre-trial Stage (실시 전 단계)`**`: Scenario selection, script adaptation, time allocation, facility/prop preparation, student orientation, and role assignment.`  
**`Trial Stage (실시 중 단계)`**`: Script execution, outcome prediction discussions, student opinion sheet completion, and mock citizen jury deliberations (국민참여재판 평의·평결).`  
**`Post-trial Stage (실시 후 단계)`**`: Summary of procedural and legal outcomes by the judge, Q&A session, and in-depth debriefing.`

## Implementation

Courts across South Korea implement these level-differentiated scripts and the judge's guidance manual within the Advanced Court Experience Program (고급 법원체험프로그램), directly supervised by active judges.

## Policy Issues

Over-concentration on criminal law in past educational materials.

Absence of a leveled curriculum adapted to the developmental stages of secondary students.

Lack of standardized instructional training for judges serving as educators.

## Reforms

Promulgation of 7 leveled mock trial scripts covering civil and criminal law.

Standardized 3-stage operational manual for judge-instructors.

Integration of citizen participation trial mechanisms (국민참여재판) into judicial education.

## Key Policy Improvements

**Digital Distribution Standardization**: Integrating the 7 scripts into the Supreme Court portal as accessible e-books for secondary schools and regional courts.

**Institutionalizing Citizen Jury Deliberations**: Embedding mock citizen jury voting and deliberation (국민참여재판 평의·평결) into high school criminal scenarios to foster understanding of participatory justice.

**Judicial Instructor Training-of-Trainers (ToT)**: Developing periodic workshops based on the operational manual to maintain consistent instructional quality across district courts nationwide.
