# Judicial Knowledge Wiki Operating Model

# Judicial Knowledge Wiki Operating Model

The Wiki should operate as the human-facing layer of institutional knowledge.

## Content

- validated definitions;
- procedures;
- research notes;
- training resources;
- implementation lessons;
- authoritative-source links.

## Metadata

- owner;
- source;
- version;
- status;
- review date;
- access level;
- related concepts.

## Strategic Position

The Wiki is the human-readable layer.

The knowledge graph is the machine-readable relationship layer.

AI services consume approved knowledge through controlled architecture.
