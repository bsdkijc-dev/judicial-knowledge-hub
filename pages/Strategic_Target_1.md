# Strategic Target 1

# Strategic Target 1

**An effective, transparent, accountable, responsive, and modern** judiciary.

## Strategic Focus

The target emphasizes:

- timely adjudication;
- transparency;
- accountability;
- responsiveness;
- information technology;
- alternative dispute resolution;
- access to justice.

## Key Digital Components

- e-Court;
- e-Berpadu;
- electronic information services.

## Related Indicators

- timely case completion;
- timely delivery of judgments;
- judgment publication;
- execution of judgments;
- restorative justice;
- mediation;
- diversion;
- e-Court utilization;
- e-Berpadu utilization.
