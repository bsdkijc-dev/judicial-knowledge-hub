# Judicial Intelligence Index

The Judicial Intelligence Index is proposed as a strategic capability measurement instrument.

## Potential Dimensions

- data quality;
- knowledge maturity;
- analytical capability;
- AI competency;
- institutional learning;
- decision support;
- governance.

## Status

The methodology requires formal development and validation.
