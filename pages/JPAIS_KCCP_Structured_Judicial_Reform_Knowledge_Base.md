## 3. Japan → Indonesia Co-Creation Pipeline

`Japanese Practice → Purpose → Indonesian Practice → Gap → Adaptation → Risk → Pilot → KPI`

### Pipeline 1: Civil Conciliation Committee (*Minji Chōtei Iinkai*)

- **Japanese Practice**: Tripartite Conciliation Panel consisting of 1 Conciliation Judge (*Chōtei-kan*) and 2 Civil Conciliation Commissioners (*Chōtei-iin*).
- **Purpose**: Combine judicial authority with domain-specific technical expertise and community common sense.
- **Indonesian Current Practice**: Single Judge-Mediator or single non-judge mediator appointed post-filing under PERMA 1/2016.
- **Gap**: Career judges bear role overload and lack specialized technical knowledge (e.g., construction, agrarian, syariah banking); external non-judge mediators are marginalized due to zero budget.
- **Indonesia-Adapted Option**: **Hybrid Mediation Panel (*Majelis Mediasi Hibrida*)** comprising 1 internal Judge-Mediator (verifying legal enforceability) and 1–2 certified external expert co-mediators (handling technical negotiations).
- **Risk**: Jurisdictional friction between judge and external expert; cost allocation disputes.
- **Pilot**: Tested under `KCCP-PILOT-001` at PN Jakpus, PN Surabaya, PN Sleman, and PN Makassar for commercial & land disputes \> Rp 200 Million.
- **KPI**: `KCCP-KPI-003` (EMUR $\ge 35.0%$) and `KCCP-KPI-001` (SSR $\ge 25.0%$).

### Pipeline 2: Mandatory Family Conciliation Exhaustion (*Chōtei Zenchi-shugi*)

- **Japanese Practice**: Statutory mandate (Art. 257 Domestic Relations Case Procedure Act) requiring closed family conciliation in Family Court before filing open litigation.
- **Purpose**: Preserve family privacy, prevent public adversarial harm, and prioritize child welfare.
- **Indonesian Current Practice**: Mandatory mediation conducted inside open civil court litigation after lawsuit registration.
- **Gap**: Lack of pre-litigation triage and lack of closed pre-filing conciliation for family/inheritance disputes.
- **Indonesia-Adapted Option**: **Indonesian *Chōtei Zenchi*** integrating Islah principles, custom/marga mediation, and mandatory closed pre-adjudication conciliation in Pengadilan Agama and Pengadilan Negeri.
- **Risk**: Delays in urgent protective order cases (e.g., domestic violence) if emergency bypass is omitted.
- **Pilot**: Tested at PA Jakarta Selatan and PA Bandung.
- **KPI**: `KCCP-KPI-001` (SSR $\ge 50.0%$ in PA) and `KCCP-KPI-002` (MDTM $\le 21$ days).

### Pipeline 3: In-Trial Judicial Settlement (*Shoshō-jō no Wakai*)

- **Japanese Practice**: Judges provide tentative legal assessments of evidence strength during preliminary proceedings (*Benron Junbi Tetsuzuki*) to encourage rational compromise.
- **Purpose**: Provide early legal clarity, shorten witness trial days, and avoid zero-sum verdicts.
- **Indonesian Current Practice**: Judges are restricted from evaluating evidence before formal trial hearings, leading to prolonged trial stages.
- **Gap**: Absence of a structured Pre-Trial Hearing procedure allowing non-coercive preliminary evaluation.
- **Indonesia-Adapted Option**: **Pre-Trial Hearing / *Wakai* Module** integrated into MA RI - JICA judicial reform framework.
- **Risk**: Litigant perception of judicial prejudgment/bias if judge's preliminary assessment is not handled with strict neutrality.
- **Pilot**: Tested in all 6 pilot courts.
- **KPI**: `KCCP-KPI-002` (MDTM $\le 21$ days) and `KCCP-KPI-005` (PJSS $\ge 4.30/5.00$).

## 4. 30–60–90 Days Action Plan

### DAY 1–30 — DIAGNOSE

- Establish National Mediation Data Audit Task Force under MA Registrar and BSDK.
- Extract and audit raw SIPP log data from 15 PN and 15 PA pilot candidates to analyze 30-day compliance, true settlement ratios, and case withdrawal anomalies.
- Conduct barrier survey of 200 litigation advocates and 60 judge-mediators across 5 High Court jurisdictions.
- Map physical mediation room conditions and active non-judge mediator rosters across pilot candidate courts.
- Analyze legal friction between PERMA 1/2016, PERMA 3/2022, and the JICA Pre-Trial Hearing draft module.

### DAY 31–60 — CO-DESIGN

- Convene Multi-Stakeholder Co-Design Workshop with MA Mediation Working Group, JICA experts, academics, and mediator association leaders.
- Formulate Case Intake Triaging Checklist to classify cases into fast-track, family conciliation, hybrid mediation, or direct adjudication.
- Draft SOP for Hybrid Mediation Panels and two-tier enforceability review of settlement deeds (*Akta Perdamaian*).
- Reconfigure EIS SIPP algorithm to separate pure case withdrawals from genuine settlement deeds.
- Draft BSDK clinical training module on preliminary assessment techniques (*In-Trial Wakai*).

### DAY 61–90 — PILOT

- Issue Decree of Chief Justice of MA RI establishing 6 Integrated Mediation & Pre-Trial Pilot Courts.
- Activate Case Intake Triaging Desks at PTSP in the 6 pilot courts.
- Deploy Hybrid Panels (1 Judge + 1 External Expert) for commercial banking and land dispute cases.
- Activate automated tracking of Post-Settlement Compliance Index (PSCI) on local court database servers.
- Conduct Day-90 mid-term monitoring evaluation with JICA experts to refine procedure manuals.

## 5. Reform Domains

1.  **Case Management**:
    - *Evidence*: Implementing intake triaging (`JPAIS-MED-GAP-003`) prevents mandatory 30-day delays for non-settlable cases and establishes differentiated case tracks.
2.  **Preparatory Proceedings**:
    - *Evidence*: Adapting Japanese *Benron Junbi Tetsuzuki* into Indonesian Pre-Trial Hearings enables preliminary evidence review and *Wakai* settlement discussions, reducing active trial days by 30%.
3.  **Issue Identification**:
    - *Evidence*: Pre-trial preliminary assessments allow judges and hybrid panels to clarify core legal/factual issues early, cutting through superficial claim positions.
4.  **Evidence Management**:
    - *Evidence*: Two-tier enforceability reviews (*two-tier review*) evaluate documentary evidence prior to homologation to prevent non-executable (*non-executable*) settlement clauses.
5.  **Judicial Mediation**:
    - *Evidence*: Shifting from single career judges to Hybrid Mediation Panels (*Majelis Mediasi Hibrida*) resolves judicial role overload (`PROB-002`) and improves settlement quality.
6.  **Mediator Competency**:
    - *Evidence*: Incorporating domain-specific professionals (engineers, CPAs, syariah experts) into court rosters resolves technical competency gaps in complex litigation.
7.  **Judicial Training**:
    - *Evidence*: Transitioning BSDK curriculum from 40-hour lectures to 30-day clinical simulations and mock conciliations with JICA improves practical facilitation skills.
8.  **Digital Case Management**:
    - *Evidence*: Upgrading PERMA 3/2022 to native e-Court encrypted video chambers eliminates third-party security leaks and integrates SIPP time-log auditing.

3.  **Jurisdictional Separation**: Japanese practices (*Chōtei*, *Wakai*) are distinguished from Indonesian practices (PERMA 1/2016, PERMA 3/2022).
4.  **Citations**: Citations explicitly reference retrieved passage indices without ranges or backticks.
5.  **Format Alignment**: FormattedWiki records, MediaWiki code blocks, and JSON objects adhere strictly to the target schemas.
