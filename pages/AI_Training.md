# AI Training

# AI Training

**AI training** develops the knowledge and skills required for responsible use and governance of artificial intelligence.

## Training Levels

### Level 1 — Awareness

Topics:

- What is AI?
- Generative AI
- Opportunities
- Risks

### Level 2 — Practical Use

Topics:

- Prompting
- Summarization
- Research
- Drafting assistance
- Verification

### Level 3 — Professional Application

Topics:

- Workflow integration
- Data
- Evaluation
- Risk management

### Level 4 — Governance

Topics:

- AI policy
- Procurement
- Audit
- Impact assessment
- Security

### Level 5 — Leadership

Topics:

- AI strategy
- Institutional transformation
- Governance architecture
- Change management

## Training Principle

Training should combine:

**KNOWLEDGE + PRACTICE + VERIFICATION + ETHICS**

## Assessment

Training effectiveness should be measured through demonstrated competence rather than attendance alone.
