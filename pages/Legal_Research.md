# Legal Research

**Legal research** is the systematic process of identifying, analyzing, interpreting, and evaluating legal authorities and relevant facts to answer a legal question.

## Research Cycle

**QUESTION** → **ISSUE IDENTIFICATION** → **SOURCE DISCOVERY** → **AUTHORITY VERIFICATION** → **ANALYSIS** → **SYNTHESIS** → **CITATION**

## Sources

Potential sources include:

- Constitution
- Legislation
- Regulations
- Court decisions
- Treaties
- Official documents
- Scholarly literature

The authority of each source must be assessed.

## AI-Assisted Legal Research

AI can potentially assist with:

- Query generation
- Search expansion
- Summarization
- Issue spotting
- Citation extraction
- Comparative research

However:

**AI DISCOVERY → HUMAN SOURCE VERIFICATION**

must remain a core principle.

## Hallucination Risk

AI systems may generate:

- Nonexistent cases
- Incorrect citations
- Misstated holdings
- Outdated provisions
- Unsupported legal propositions

Every consequential legal proposition should therefore be checked against the underlying source.
