# Judicial Best Practice

**Judicial Best Practice** refers to a practice that has demonstrated useful results and is supported by sufficient evidence for consideration or adaptation.

This page does not declare any particular practice to be a best practice.

## Evaluation Criteria

A candidate practice may be assessed for:

- Effectiveness
- Efficiency
- Legality
- Sustainability
- Scalability
- Accessibility
- User impact
- Institutional compatibility
- Evidence quality

## Best Practice vs Good Idea

**GOOD IDEA**

→ appears promising

**PRACTICE**

→ has been implemented

**EVIDENCED PRACTICE**

→ supported by evidence

**BEST PRACTICE**

→ sufficiently demonstrated and suitable for consideration

These categories should not be treated as legally authoritative.

## Transferability

A practice successful in one jurisdiction may not automatically work in another.

Context matters.
