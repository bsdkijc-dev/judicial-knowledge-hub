# Judicial-policy-ngeu-nrrp-administrative-shift-2022

The **NGEU-NRRP Economic Planning and Administrative Transformation Framework** (officially centered on the work *Economic Planning and Administrative Transformations in the NGEU and NRRP: A Paradigm Shift*) is a supranational European and Italian administrative law and economic planning policy framework. Originating from the Next Generation European Union (NGEU) recovery plan and the Recovery and Resilience Facility (RRF), it redirects state economic planning under the central oversight of the European Commission and induces an organizational transformation in public administration from a process-based model to project-based management [^1].

## Overview

Authored by [Aldo Sandulli](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Aldo_Sandulli), Professor of Administrative Law, this study outlines how the NGEU and RRF funding mechanisms triggered a paradigm shift in European and national administrative law [^2]. Drawing upon the scholarship of [Sabino Cassese](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Sabino_Cassese) regarding the evolution of European integration through crisis response, the framework highlights how NGEU revived state economic planning functions under European Commission guidance while restructuring Italian public bureaucracy from rigid process-oriented workflows to project-based management [^3].

## Legal Basis

The framework is grounded in key supranational instruments and national planning frameworks:

- **[Next Generation European Union](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Next_Generation_European_Union) (NGEU)**: The European Union recovery package funding grants and loans through joint debt issuance [^4].
- **[Recovery and Resilience Facility](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Recovery_and_Resilience_Facility) (RRF)**: The core mechanism of NGEU establishing targets for ecological transition, digital transformation, and social cohesion [^5].
- **[European Semester Mechanism](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/European_Semester_Mechanism)**: The EU financial coordination framework and financial conditionality system [^6].
- **[National Recovery and Resilience Plan](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/National_Recovery_and_Resilience_Plan) (NRRP / PNRR)**: Italy's national recovery plan approved by the Council of the European Union [^7].

## Background

The COVID-19 pandemic and the conflict in Ukraine accelerated European integration through the establishment of NGEU [^8]. For decades prior, national economic planning capacity in Member States was marginal, as European economic governance deferred development choices to regulated markets [^9]. NGEU reintroduced public economic planning under European institutional direction [^10].

## Objectives

- Accelerate ecological and digital transitions while bolstering territorial and social cohesion across the EU [^11].
- Align national economic development planning with supranational strategic priorities [^12].
- Enhance the speed and operational efficiency of public investment absorption via administrative reform [^13].

## Scope

The scope covers European administrative law, national economic planning, Italian bureaucratic organization, public project management, and public financial oversight [^14].

## Key Provisions & Administrative Structure

- **Central Role of the European Commission**: The Commission performs semi-annual verifications of milestones and targets before disbursing funds, holding the authority to suspend funding in cases of non-compliance or rule-of-law violations [^15].
- **Parallel Administration in Italy**: Establishment of a Control Room (\*Cabina di Regia\*) within the Presidency of the Council of Ministers, expanded responsibilities for the State General Accounting Office (\*Ragioneria Generale dello Stato\*), and dedicated Mission Units (\*Unità di Missione\*) across ministries [^16].
- **Project-Based Organization**: Transitioning from rigid traditional administrative procedures to corporate project management techniques, turning the Single Responsible Officer (\*RUP\*) into an active project manager [^17].
- **Concomitant Audit by the Court of Auditors**: Strengthening real-time external oversight (\*concomitant control\*) by the Italian Court of Auditors (\*Corte dei Conti\*) throughout project implementation [^18].

## Implementation

Implemented across all Italian ministries and regional authorities as the primary beneficiary of NGEU funds through strict semi-annual performance schedules [^19].

## Policy Issues

- **Procedural Time Constraints**: Public performance evaluations risk shifting focus away from substantive investment quality toward strict compliance with time constraints [^20].
- **Organizational Strain**: Demanding deadlines impose significant workloads on administrative staff, raising risks of human resource allocation conflicts across concurrent projects [^21].

## Proposed Reforms

Integrates corporate project management and business engineering methodologies into the public sector while leveraging concomitant external controls by the \*Corte dei Conti\* to secure timely target fulfillment [^22].
