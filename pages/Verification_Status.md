# Verification Status

# Verification Status

Every substantive Wiki object should display its verification state.

## Proposed States

- Draft
- Under Review
- Verified
- Approved
- Superseded
- Archived

## Rule

**Verified** does not necessarily mean **legally authoritative**.

The Wiki must distinguish institutional verification from legal authority.
