# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:1690K PID 1987

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 1690 K/Pid/1987** (dated November 26, 1987 / pronounced December 16, 1987) is an Indonesian criminal law and criminal procedure precedent establishing two fundamental rules regarding the authority of High Courts to issue detention orders at the appeal level *ex* Article 238 paragraph (2) *jo.* Article 27 of the Criminal Procedure Code (KUHAP) and the sentencing of heroin trafficking crimes:

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established two primary legal principles:

1.  **High Court Authority to Issue Detention Orders (Article 238(2) *jo.* Article 27 of KUHAP):** A High Court's verdict ruling that "the High Court lacks the authority to issue a detention order against the Defendant" misapplies the law and is annulled by the Supreme Court. Pursuant to Article 238(2) *jo.* Article 27 of KUHAP, High Courts possess full authority to issue detention orders for the interests of appeal proceedings for a maximum period of 30 (thirty) days.
2.  **Crime of Selling Heroin Narcotics (Article 23(5) *jo.* Article 36(5)(b) of Law No. 9 of 1976):** The Defendant \[Defendant\] was legally and convincingly proven guilty of the crime of "unlawfully selling narcotics in the form of heroin" totaling 2 *tail* weighing 80,000 milligrams (80 grams) for Rp 900,000, and was accordingly sentenced to 20 (twenty) years of imprisonment and a fine of Rp 500,000 (five hundred thousand rupiahs) with a subsidiary of 6 (six) months of confinement, while the 80,000 mg heroin evidence was confiscated for destruction.

The Supreme Court granted the Defendant's (\[Defendant\]) cassation petition, annulled Jakarta High Court Decision No. 96/Pid./1987/PT.DKI dated May 16, 1987, regarding the detention portion, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by declaring the Defendant guilty of unlawfully selling heroin narcotics and sentencing him to 20 (twenty) years of imprisonment and a Rp 500,000 fine subsidiary 6 months of confinement.

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. High Court Authority to Issue Detention Orders During Appeal Proceedings (Article 238(2) *jo.* Article 27 of KUHAP)

*Does a High Court possess authority to issue a detention order against a Defendant during the appeal examination period?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Full Authority.** The Jakarta High Court's verdict stating that "the High Court lacks authority to issue a detention order against the Defendant" misapplied the law. Pursuant to Article 238(2) *jo.* Article 27 of KUHAP, detention authority shifts to the High Court upon filing an appeal petition, empowering the High Court to issue detention orders for a maximum of 30 (thirty) days.

### 2. Qualification of Elements for "Unlawfully Selling Heroin/Morphine Narcotics" (Article 23(5) *jo.* Article 36(5)(b) of Law No. 9 of 1976)

The Defendant's actions in fulfilling an order for 2 *tail* of heroin (80 grams) for Rp 900,000, agreeing upon a transaction location, and entrusting the evidence to witness \[Intermediary\] for handover to the buyer (\[Buyer\]) perfectly fulfilled all elements of the crime of "unlawfully selling narcotics in the form of heroin."

### 3. Evidentiary Value of Defendant's Interrogation Record (BAP) Withdrawal at Trial (Article 188 *jo.* Article 189 of KUHAP)

Unilateral withdrawal of a Defendant's BAP statements under claims of fear/threats without supporting valid evidence fails to displace the correspondence of witness testimonies (buyer, intermediary, and arresting officers), instead acting as circumstantial evidence (*aanwijzing*) reinforcing the Defendant's guilt.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the Jakarta High Court err in applying criminal procedure law (*foutieve toepassing van het recht*) by declaring that High Courts lack authority to issue detention orders, and how does this apply under Article 238(2) *jo.* Article 27 of KUHAP?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. Jakarta High Court erred in applying the law. The Supreme Court granted the Defendant's cassation petition, annulled Jakarta High Court Decision No. 96/Pid./1987/PT.DKI regarding the detention portion, and independently adjudicated by declaring that High Courts possess authority to detain Defendants *ex* Article 27 of KUHAP and sentencing Defendant \[Defendant\] to 20 (twenty) years of imprisonment and a fine of Rp 500,000 subsidiary 6 months of confinement.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / West Jakarta District Court & Jakarta High Court)

Examination of facts regarding the order of 2 *tail* of heroin (80,000 mg) by \[Buyer\] to the Defendant on October 7, 1983, at PB Nasional Alley, Jelambar, West Jakarta; delivery on October 8, 1983, via \[Intermediary\]; arrest by Polsek/Metro Jaya Regional Police officers (Sertu Supangat, Mayor Harry YN Montolalu, Letkol Syaeri); National Police Criminalistics Laboratory test results confirming the crystals tested positive for heroin; facts of the Defendant fleeing to Lampung until arrested on April 6, 1986; and the Defendant's prior criminal record resulting in a conviction in Medan for a marijuana case.

## What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

### Established by Evidence

- Defendant \[Defendant\] is legally and convincingly proven guilty of committing the crime of "unlawfully selling narcotics in the form of heroin."
- Physical evidence comprising 2 *tail* of heroin weighing 80,000 milligrams (80 grams) was proven to have been sold by the Defendant for Rp 900,000.
- High Courts possess authority to issue detention orders against Defendants at the appeal level *ex* Article 27 of KUHAP.
- The Defendant was sentenced to imprisonment for 20 (twenty) years and a fine of Rp 500,000 subsidiary 6 months of confinement.
- Physical evidence of 2 *tail* of 80,000 mg heroin was ordered confiscated for destruction.

### Rejected / Dismissed Arguments

- The Defendant's alibi claiming residence in Lampung since September 1983 and allegations of police torture were rejected by the Court as contradicting witness testimonies and BAP circumstantial evidence.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Enforcement of High Court Detention Authority During Appeal File Transitions:** This landmark ruling affirms certainty in Indonesian criminal procedure law: High Courts must not reject detention authority under the pretext that case dossiers have not been fully registered, because upon appeal declarations being officially lodged at the District Court, authority over the Defendant's detention status legally transfers to the High Court *ex* Article 238(2) of KUHAP.

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Does a High Court possess authority to detain a defendant during appeal proceedings, and what is the prison sentence for selling 80 grams of heroin pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence?  
**High Courts possess detention authority, and offenders face 20 years imprisonment.** Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence No. 1690 K/Pid/1987:

- Pursuant to Article 238(2) *jo.* Article 27 of KUHAP, High Courts possess full authority to issue detention orders against Defendants for appeal proceedings for a maximum of 30 days.
- A Defendant proven to have unlawfully sold 80 grams (2 *tail*) of heroin narcotics for Rp 900,000 is sentenced to 20 (twenty) years of imprisonment and a fine of Rp 500,000 subsidiary 6 months of confinement.
