# Legal Claim

A **Legal Claim** is a proposition concerning law, judicial practice, institutional policy, or legal interpretation.

## Claim Structure

**Claim → Source → Evidence → Verification → Context**

## AI Rule

AI-generated claims must be distinguishable from source-derived claims.
