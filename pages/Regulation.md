# Regulation

# Regulation

A **regulation** is a legal or administrative instrument issued by an authority empowered to regulate a specified subject.

The legal status, hierarchy, issuing authority, and binding force must be determined according to the applicable legal system.

**\[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## Regulatory Metadata

Each regulation should ideally record:

- Title
- Number
- Issuing authority
- Date
- Effective date
- Subject
- Legal basis
- Status
- Amendments
- Repeal
- Official source

## Regulatory Lifecycle

**PROPOSAL** → **ADOPTION** → **PUBLICATION** → **EFFECTIVE** → **AMENDMENT** → **REPEAL**

## Judicial Regulations

Judicial institutions may issue instruments governing matters within their lawful authority.

Specific instruments and authority must be verified from official sources.

## AI Knowledge Extraction

AI may assist in extracting:

- Articles
- Definitions
- Obligations
- Exceptions
- Cross-references
- Amendment relationships

AI extraction must be checked against the authoritative document.
