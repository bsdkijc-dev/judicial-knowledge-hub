# International Benchmarking Matrix

# International Benchmarking Matrix

International judicial systems should be compared systematically.

## Benchmark Fields

- jurisdiction;
- reform objective;
- technology;
- governance model;
- legal framework;
- data architecture;
- workforce capability;
- measurable outcome;
- transferability to Indonesia.

## Principle

International examples should inform Indonesian design rather than being copied without contextual analysis.
