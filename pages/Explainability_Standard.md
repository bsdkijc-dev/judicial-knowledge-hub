# Explainability Standard

# Explainability Standard

Judicial AI should provide an appropriate explanation of its role, limitations and relevant basis according to the use case.

## Explanation Should Address

- what the system was asked to do;
- what information it used;
- what it produced;
- relevant limitations;
- uncertainty;
- appropriate human review.

## Boundary

Explainability requirements should be proportionate to the risk and function of the system.
