# JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:JPAIS-MED-REF-014

## Summary / Ringkasan

## Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

- Entity ID: JPAIS-MED-REF-014
- Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
- Ingestion Status: Confirmed

## Description / Deskripsi

Evaluates systemic policy bottlenecks arising from the application of Supreme Court Regulation (PERMA) No. 3 of 2021 governing objections to KPPU decisions in Commercial Courts, addressing issues such as the absolute ban on new evidence (novum), discrepancies in day-count definitions, unproportional bank guarantees, partial inkracht risks in joint conduct (cartel) cases, and the absence of specialized economic guidelines, while proposing a comprehensive two-track reform framework and ICLA normative recommendations\[cite: 14, 16, 23, 24\].

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-001): Absolute restriction on new evidence (novum) under Article 13(5) of PERMA 3/2021 violates defensive rights and \*Due Process of Law\*, especially when KPPU introduces economic analyses or penalty computations in final decisions without prior commission hearings\[cite: 14\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-002): Procedural and administrative ambiguities—such as the conflict between PP No. 44/2021 (working days) and PERMA 3/2021 (calendar days), restrictive 14-day appeal windows from excerpts, non-proportional bank guarantees for non-monetary sanctions, and partial \*inkracht\* risks in multi-party cartel cases (e.g., 97 respondents)—create systemic legal uncertainty\[cite: 16, 18, 23\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-003): Institutional capacity gaps, including an absence of formal standards for reviewing economic evidence (\*economic evidence\*) and econometric models, a deficit of certified competition law judges, and lack of secure data rooms or \*inzage\* access, reduce commercial courts to formalistic administrative "stamps"\[cite: 17, 24, 27, 31\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-001): Commercial Courts handle objections to KPPU decisions pursuant to PERMA No. 3 of 2021, operating under tight administrative timelines and restricted evidentiary review confines based solely on the KPPU dossier\[cite: 14, 27, 28\].

## Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-001): Developed jurisdictions balance integrated administrative enforcement bodies (like the EU Commission or KFTC) with deep, substantive judicial scrutiny (\*legality review with verification\*) and robust protections for the equality of arms\[cite: 27, 31\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-001): Disconnect between KPPU's integrated administrative model (investigating, prosecuting, and adjudicating simultaneously) and commercial courts' restricted review scope, which risks violating \*audi et alteram partem\* when review is reduced to a superficial "rubber stamp"\[cite: 27, 28, 31\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Implement a Two-Track Reform Approach: (1) Limited amendment of PERMA No. 3 of 2021 to harmonize day counts, relax novum restrictions when new economic models are introduced, provide proportional bank guarantees, and adopt confidentiality data rooms/inzage; (2) Issue dedicated Judicial Guidelines on Economic Analysis (\*Pedoman Mengadili\*) alongside mandatory continuous judicial certification and training\[cite: 23, 24\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-001): Phase 1 (Q3 2026): Conduct public consultations on Draft Academic Papers (DIM) with MA, KPPU, and academics across 5 Commercial Court regions, finalizing the urgency text\[cite: 16\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-002): Phase 2 (Q4 2026): Draft amendments to PERMA 3/2021, issue SK KMA guidelines for economic assessment (MSEJR), and structure Supreme Court circulars (SEMA) on PK and executory status\[cite: 16\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-003): Phase 3 (Q1 2027): Upgrade SIPP and secure data rooms, and launch mandatory specialized economic training and certification programs for commercial judges\[cite: 16\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-014](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-014): 5-Region Commercial Court Deployment across Jakarta Pusat, Surabaya, Medan, Makassar, and Semarang\[cite: 16\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-014](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-014): Net Impact Score of +7 for Option I reforms; 100% synchronization of working-day definitions; zero unjust procedural dismissals due to unproportional bank guarantees; complete adoption of economic evidence review standards across 5 Commercial Courts\[cite: 16\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Shift commercial court oversight from a mechanical \*Crime Control\* model (\*assembly line\*) to a rigorous \*Due Process\* model (\*obstacle course\*) that prioritizes substantive material justice and the protection of defensive rights\[cite: 14, 16, 24\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgate an amended PERMA No. 3 of 2021, issue specialized SK KMA guidelines on economic analysis, and pursue long-term statutory harmonization via amendments to Law No. 5 of 1999 and the laws governing extraordinary legal remedies (Peninjauan Kembali)\[cite: 16, 19, 23\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Empirical observations from ICLA and policy evaluations indicate that PERMA 3/2021 successfully established forum and procedural certainty, but current restrictions on new evidence and limited economic expertise risk reducing judicial review to a rubber stamp, especially in complex joint conduct cases like the 97-respondent online lending (pinjol) cartel\[cite: 14, 17, 24, 27\].
