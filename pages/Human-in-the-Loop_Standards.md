# Human-in-the-Loop Standard

Human oversight must be meaningful rather than merely procedural.

## Meaningful Oversight Requires

- sufficient time;
- sufficient information;
- sufficient authority;
- sufficient competence;
- ability to reject AI output;
- ability to document disagreement.

## Automation Bias

Human review should be monitored for excessive acceptance of AI recommendations.

## Core Test

**Can the human reviewer realistically disagree with the system?**
