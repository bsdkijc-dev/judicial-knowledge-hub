## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 10 K/Pdt/1984 establishes an important legal principle in Civil Procedure Law regarding the formal requirements of third-party opposition (\*derden verzet\*). The Supreme Court outlined that an opposition (\*derden verzet\*) filed by a wife against the execution of a judgment ordering her husband to vacate a property is inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*), because a wife who resides together with her husband in the disputed object does not qualify as a pure, independent third party separate from the principal case.

## Case Background

The Opponent (Mrs. Inarti, wife of Eng Kie Men) filed a third-party opposition (\*derden verzet\*) at the Surabaya District Court against the execution of the Surabaya District Court judgment No. 80/1978 Pdt. jo. Surabaya High Court No. 565/1980 Pdt. jo. Supreme Court No. 355 K/Sip/1981. The judgment of the principal case had ruled in favor of the late Mrs. Sumiati's lawsuit and ordered Eng Kie Men (Mrs. Inarti's husband) to vacate and surrender the house and land located at Jalan Raya Arjuno No. 142, Surabaya. Mrs. Inarti argued that the house and building situated on state land belonged to her personally based on Grant Deed No. 124 dated May 28, 1977, issued by Liauw Kon Tjin, rather than her husband. The Surabaya District Court (No. 423/1981/G) and the Surabaya High Court (No. 5/1983 Perdata) granted Mrs. Inarti's opposition and cancelled the execution. Dissatisfied with this ruling, the heirs of the late Mrs. Sumiati and Kastamin filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** The Opponent is the wife of the defendant in the contested judgment against which the opposition is directed. Because the Opponent is the wife of the original Defendant I who continues to jointly occupy the disputed house and land, and given that the evidentiary grant documents were already examined and rejected during the principal lawsuit, the Opponent does not constitute a pure third party (\*derden\*). The third-party opposition (\*derden verzet\*) filed by the defendant's wife is consequently declared inadmissible.
- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from the Petitioners for Cassation (heirs of the late Sumiati and Kastamin); Annulling the Surabaya High Court Decision No. 5/1983 Perdata dated June 30, 1983, and the Surabaya District Court Decision No. 423/1981/G dated July 12, 1982; and Independently Adjudicating: Declaring the opposition filed by the Opponent (Mrs. Inarti) inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*), and ordering the Respondent for Cassation / Opponent to bear the procedural costs.
