## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 991 K/Pid/2001, dated December 13, 2001, established a fundamental criminal procedural and material criminal law rule concerning violations of the witness testimony rule (\*Unus Testis Nullus Testis\* ex Article 185 paragraph 2 KUHAP) and the offense of instigating the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed (Article 266 paragraph 1 KUHP):

The \*Judex Facti\* of the Medan District Court misapplied evidentiary law by relying solely on the testimony of a single witness (the Notary), while ignoring the rights and testimonies of other sworn witnesses (the remaining heirs) (\*Unus Testis Nullus Testis\* ex Article 185 paragraph 2 KUHAP). The Defendant's (\[Defendant\]) action in approaching Notary H. Djaidir, SH, and instigating the insertion of false statements into Authentic Deed Minutes of Meeting No. 37 of 1995—falsely claiming that a General Meeting of Shareholders (\*RUPS\*) of PT Kwala Gunung had been held appointing himself as President Director without the consent and inclusion of 8 other lawful heirs—violated the 1982 Will and was qualified as the crime of instigating the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed ex Article 266 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code.

## Executive Summary

The central legal principle established in this decision is that single-witness reliance violating statutory corroboration rules invalidates acquittals, and corporate takeovers via fabricated notarial meeting records constitute prosecutable criminal falsification.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Convictions or acquittals resting on uncorroborated single-witness testimony violate statutory witness corroboration rules (\*unus testis nullus testis\*).
- Falsely recording shareholder meeting outcomes in notarial deeds without required heir consent constitutes criminal falsification under Article 266 KUHP.
- Acquittals based on flawed evidentiary interpretations are impure acquittals reviewable and reversible on cassation.

## Case Background

The Original Defendant / Respondent for Cassation (**\[Defendant\]**, \[age removed\], entrepreneur) was charged with instigating the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed (Article 266 paragraph 1 KUHP) for approaching Notary H. Djaidir, SH, in Medan on January 11, 1995, and requesting the drafting of GMS Minutes Deed No. 37 of 1995 for PT Kwala Gunung reflecting a fictitious GMS appointing himself as President Director. In reality, no such meeting was held, and the action lacked the presence, consent, or participation of 8 other lawful heirs of the late Suandi Kongsi, violating Will No. 2 dated April 2, 1982.

The Medan District Court (Decision No. 1441/Pid.B/2000/PN.Mdn dated December 18, 2000) acquitted the Defendant (\*vrijspraak\*) based solely on the Notary's testimony. The Public Prosecutor at the Medan State Prosecutor's Office filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Violation of Independent and Corroborative Witness Evidence Rules (\*Unus Testis Nullus Testis\* ex Article 185 paragraph 2 KUHAP)

The primary issue was whether a District Court judgment acquitting a Defendant based solely on the uncorroborated testimony of a single witness (the drafting Notary), while ignoring testimonies of victim witnesses (other heirs) who were sworn under oath, is valid under criminal procedure.

The Supreme Court held that it is Invalid (\*Null and Void\*). Pursuant to Article 185 paragraph (2) of the KUHAP, the testimony of a single witness alone unsupported by other lawful evidence is insufficient to prove that a Defendant is guilty (\*Unus Testis Nullus Testis\*). The \*Judex Facti\* of Medan District Court committed a manifest legal error in evidentiary evaluation by elevating the dubious testimony of the Notary while unlawfully setting aside other heir witnesses bound by religious oaths who testified to the true facts that no GMS ever occurred and their rights were subverted.

### Proof of the Element "Instigating the Insertion of False Statements into an Authentic Deed" (Article 266 paragraph 1 KUHP)

The action of an heir approaching a Notary and requesting the recording of GMS Minutes for Corporate Management Replacement as if a valid meeting had been conducted, when such GMS was never attended or approved by other lawful shareholders/heirs and contradicted the testamentary will, legally and perfectly satisfies all elements of the crime under Article 266 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code.

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Did the \*Judex Facti\* (Medan District Court) misapply evidentiary law (\*foutieve kepassing van het bewijsrecht\*) ex Article 185 paragraph (2) KUHAP by ignoring the testimonies of sworn heir witnesses and acquitting the Defendant of charges under Article 266 paragraph (1) KUHP?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes, the Medan District Court misapplied evidentiary law. The Supreme Court granted the Public Prosecutor's cassation petition, annulled Medan District Court Decision No. 1441/Pid.B/2000/PN.Mdn, and adjudicated afresh, declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] proven legally and convincingly guilty of committing the crime of "Instigating the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed" ex Article 266 paragraph (1) KUHP and sentencing him to 2 years' imprisonment.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Medan District Court)

Examination of facts concerning the death of late Suandi Kongsi (Khong Tong Tian) on November 11, 1991; existence of Will No. 2 dated April 2, 1982, by Notary Raskami Sembiring, SH; status of 9 lawful heirs; execution of Deed No. 37 dated January 11, 1995, before Notary H. Djaidir, SH, on A. Yani Street, Medan; claims of a fictitious GMS attended solely by the Defendant, his wife, and child; and control over corporate assets of PT Kwala Gunung, PT Sijabut, and PT Sri Perlak valued at tens of billions of rupiahs.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The \*Judex Facti\* of Medan District Court misapplied evidentiary law by acquitting the Defendant based solely on the testimony of a single witness (the Notary) while ignoring other sworn heir witnesses, violating the \*Unus Testis Nullus Testis\* principle ex Article 185 paragraph (2) KUHAP.
2.  This acquittal constituted an impure acquittal reviewable and reversible via cassation ex Article 244 KUHAP.
3.  Witness testimonies established that Deed No. 37 of 1995 contained false statements, subverted the rights of 8 heirs, and contradicted the 1982 Will.
4.  The Supreme Court annulled the Medan District Court decision and adjudicated afresh, sentencing Defendant \[Defendant\] to 2 years' imprisonment.

## Ruling

The Supreme Court ruled: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation (**Public Prosecutor at the Medan State Prosecutor's Office**); Annulling the Medan District Court decision dated December 18, 2000, No. 1441/Pid.B/2000/PN.Mdn; and ADJUDICATING AFRESH: 1. Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] proven legally and convincingly guilty of committing the crime of "Instigating the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed"; 2. Sentencing the Defendant to a prison term of 2 (two) years; 3. Stipulating that evidence items remain attached to the case file; and ordering the Respondent for Cassation / Defendant to pay cassation court costs amounting to Rp 2,500.—.

## Evidence Status and Gaps

### Established by Evidence

- Authentic Deed Minutes of Meeting No. 37 of 1995 drafted by Notary H. Djaidir, SH, contained false statements concerning the outcomes of PT Kwala Gunung's GMS.
- Defendant \[Defendant\] intentionally instigated the insertion of such false statements without the presence and consent of 8 other lawful heirs.
- The drafting of the fictitious deed violated Will No. 2 of 1982 and caused material damages amounting to tens of billions of rupiahs to the lawful heirs.
- The Defendant was sentenced to 2 years' imprisonment.

### Not Established / Reversal of Acquittal

- The Medan District Court's considerations declaring the Defendant unproven based on Notary testimony were annulled entirely by the Supreme Court due to formal evidentiary defects.

### Uncertainty and Gaps

- Protection of Corporate Authentic Deeds from Heir Manipulation: This landmark ruling establishes strict boundaries for notarial practice and corporate law: Notaries may not facilitate unilateral claims by managers or heirs without verifying GMS authenticity and the consent of all shareholders, confirming prison penalties for individuals falsifying family corporate GMS records.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: Can a District Court acquittal be annulled by the Supreme Court if the trial judge only considered the testimony of a single witness?**

**A:** Yes, it can be annulled. Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 991 K/Pid/2001, a District Court acquittal considering only a single witness testimony (such as a Notary) while ignoring other sworn victim witnesses misapplies evidentiary law by violating the \*Unus Testis Nullus Testis\* principle (Article 185 paragraph 2 KUHAP). The Supreme Court possesses authority to annul such acquittals and sentence the Defendant to imprisonment.
