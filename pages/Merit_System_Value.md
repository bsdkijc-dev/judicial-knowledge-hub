# Merit System Value

# Merit System Value

Indicator 3.3 measures implementation of a merit-based human-resource management system.

## Eight Assessment Dimensions

1.  Workforce planning
2.  Recruitment
3.  Career development
4.  Promotion and transfer
5.  Performance management
6.  Compensation, rewards and discipline
7.  Protection and services
8.  Information systems

## Strategic Purpose

The Renstra describes merit-based management as supporting professional and integrity-based recruitment, fair compensation, employee development, and protection from politicization and practices inconsistent with merit principles.
