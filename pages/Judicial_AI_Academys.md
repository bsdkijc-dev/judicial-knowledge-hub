# Judicial AI Academys

# Judicial AI Academy

The Judicial AI Academy provides role-based AI competency development.

## Potential Tracks

- judges;
- court leaders;
- researchers;
- legal researchers;
- administrators;
- data professionals;
- technology professionals;
- educators.

## Principle

AI capability should be role-specific rather than a single generic course.
