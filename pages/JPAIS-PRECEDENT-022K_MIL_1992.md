## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 22 K/Mil/1992 dated January 8, 1994, establishes an important legal precedent in Military Justice regarding the proof of peacetime desertion and the formal requirement for adequate legal reasoning (\*motivering\*) in sentencing: 1. **Unauthorized Absence / Peacetime Desertion (Article 86 item 1 of the KUHPM):** The criminal offense of "intentionally committing desertion in peacetime" is proven when a Defendant abandons their unit without permission from their Commander or Superior for 19 consecutive days while the country is not in a state of war and units nationwide are not on alert status. 2. **Legal Defect in Sentencing Considerations (Onvoldoende Gemotiveerd):** The appellate court ruling (Medan High Military Court I) was annulled by the Supreme Court due to an error in law resulting from insufficient consideration (\*onvoldoende gemotiveerd\*) regarding the grounds and basis for sentencing, leading the Supreme Court to independently adjudicate the case.

## Case Background

The Defendant, \[Defendant\] (Police Sergeant / Serda Pol/Nrp. 64100480, Sabhara Member of the South Padang Sector Police, Padang Sub-Resort Police), was brought before the Padang Military Court I-03 under three cumulative charges: 1. **First (Article 266(1) of the KUHP):** Causing false statements to be entered into an authentic deed (marriage deeds at the Office of Religious Affairs (\*KUA\*) in Lubuk Kilangan Subdistrict, Padang, and KUA Pacet, Cipanas) by claiming to work as a student/private employee and maintaining an unmarried/single status. 2. **Second (Article 279(1) item 1 of the KUHP):** Contracting a secondary marriage without the permission of the first wife (\[Party B\]) or the authorization of the competent Superior in accordance with marriage regulations for ABRI/POLRI personnel, with Yulia Dewi Arjuna. 3. **Third (Article 86 item 1 of the KUHPM):** Committing unauthorized absence (desertion) from his unit at the South Padang Sector Police starting from July 22, 1989, to August 10, 1989 (approximately 19 days) to travel to West Java with Yulia Dewi Arjuna.

The Padang Military Court I-03 (Decision No. PUT/74/K/POL/IX/1990 dated September 11, 1990) sentenced the Defendant to 1 year and 6 months of imprisonment. On appeal, the Medan High Military Court I (Decision No. PUT/B-11/K/POL/MMT-I/VI/1991 dated June 26, 1991) modified the sentence to a Principal Penalty of 1 year of imprisonment alongside an Additional Penalty of dismissal from military/POLRI service. The Defendant subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The criminal offense of "intentionally committing desertion in peacetime" (Article 86 item 1 of the KUHPM) is proven because the Defendant abandoned his unit without permission from his Unit Commander or designated Superior for 19 days, whereas at that time the country was not at war and military units across all regions were not placed on alert.
2.  The Medan High Military Court I misapplied the law because it insufficiently considered (\*onvoldoende gemotiveerd\*) the penalty imposed. Therefore, the decision of the Medan High Military Court I must be annulled, and the Supreme Court shall independently adjudicate the \*a quo\* case.

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation Defendant \[Defendant\], Serda Pol/Nrp: 64100480; Annulling the Medan High Military Court I Decision No. PUT/B-11/K/POL/MMT-I/VI/91 dated June 26, 1991, and the Padang Military Court I-03 Decision No. PUT/74/K/POL/IX/1990 dated September 11, 1990; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] legally and convincingly proven guilty of the crimes of: I. Causing false statements to be entered into an official deed regarding facts that should be proven by that deed, with intent to use that deed; II. Contracting a secondary marriage while knowing that an existing marriage constitutes a valid legal impediment; III. Intentionally committing desertion in peacetime; (2) Sentencing the Defendant to Principal Penalty: Imprisonment for 1 (one) year, and Additional Penalty: Dismissal from Military/POLRI Service; (3) Crediting the period of arrest and detention already served by the Defendant in full against the sentence imposed; (4) Ordering that evidence items remain attached to the case file; (5) Ordering the Petitioner for Cassation / Defendant to pay court costs amounting to Rp 2,500.
