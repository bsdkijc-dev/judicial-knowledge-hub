# Case Resolution Time Indicator

# Percentage of Timely Case Resolution

The indicator measures timely case resolution according to applicable standards.

## Coverage

The dictionary specifies measurement for:

- cassation;
- judicial review;
- consideration of clemency;
- judicial review of regulations;
- jurisdictional disputes;
- opinion-review proceedings;
- electoral administrative violations;
- appellate proceedings;
- first-instance proceedings.

## Exclusions

Cases whose summons process is specifically governed by law, including certain mass-media summons and parties located overseas, are excluded from the calculation.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Cases resolved on time}} {\text{Cases resolved}}\times100\\ \</math\>
