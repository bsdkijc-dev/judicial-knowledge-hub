# Timely Delivery of Criminal, Military and Jinayat Judgment Copies

Indicator 1.5 measures timely provision or delivery of copies of criminal, military and jinayat judgments by the referring courts to the parties.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Copies provided/sent on time}} {\text{Copies of appellate, cassation and judicial review judgments received}} \times100\\ \</math\>

## Strategic Function

The indicator supports timely access by parties to judgment documents.
