# Initiative Objective

# Initiative Objective

The objective defines the measurable change an initiative seeks to produce.

## Objective Structure

**Improve \[outcome\] for \[users\] from \[baseline\] to \[target\] by \[time\].**

## Quality Test

An objective should be:

- specific;
- measurable;
- relevant;
- time-bound;
- linked to a justice outcome.
