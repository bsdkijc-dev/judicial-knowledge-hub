## Description / Deskripsi

This page records the official policy submission and position paper presented by the Indonesian Competition Lawyers Association (ICLA)—authored by General Chairman Asep Ridwan, S.H., M.H., on 3 August 2026—to the Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia. Applying Herbert L. Packer's dual model framework (Crime Control vs. Due Process), ICLA identifies 10 formal filing issues, 7 substantive trial issues, and 8 administrative obstacles at the KPPU level. The submission urges the Supreme Court to transition from an administrative "assembly line" review to a comprehensive Due Process Model ("obstacle course"), given that administrative fines under Law No. 11/2020 (Job Creation Law) now reach up to 50% of net profit or 10% of total sales without nominal caps (e.g., SANY Heavy Industry truck cartel fine of Rp 449 billion, Google Play Billing fine of Rp 202 billion, and Fintech/Pinjol cartel fine of Rp 755 billion across 97 business actors). All legal analyses and proposed amendments to PERMA No. 3/2021 have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher Cecep Mustafa.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-038](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-038): Strict Evidence Lock (Article 13(5) PERMA No. 3/2021), where prohibiting new documentary, witness, or expert evidence at the Commercial Court level forces judges to act merely as \*judex juris\* (reviewing administrative dossiers) rather than \*judex facti\*, preventing business actors from challenging novel market definitions or fine formulas introduced for the first time in KPPU's final decision.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-039](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-039): Inkracht Fragmentation in Joint Conduct Cases, where under Article 2(4) PERMA No. 3/2021, if only a subset of respondents files an objection, KPPU's decision becomes final and enforceable for non-appealing parties, producing contradictory legal outcomes where a shared contract clause is invalidated for appealing parties but legally binding on non-appealing co-parties in large-scale cartel proceedings.

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-037](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-037): Under current Supreme Court practice pursuant to PERMA No. 3/2021, objection review is confined to administrative dossiers barring new evidence; filing deadlines (14 days) are strictly enforced based on oral reading of decisions rather than physical receipt of complete copies; bank guarantees are demanded indiscriminately; and judicial review (\*Peninjauan Kembali\* / PK) is not explicitly regulated despite high-value precedents.

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-037](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-037): Comprehensive international comparison across six jurisdictions shows that advanced legal systems—such as the UK Competition Appeal Tribunal (CAT), EU General Court, and Japan High Court—guarantee full merits review, independent economic evaluation, confidentiality rings, data rooms, and unrestricted access to investigative files (\*Statement of Objections\*).

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-037](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-037): Indonesia's PERMA No. 3/2021 remains stuck in a hybrid administrative review model lacking international standards of \*due process\*—specifically missing full merits review, limited evidence lock relaxation, formal access to file (\*inzage\*), and confidentiality ring mechanisms.

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Adopt four foundational amendments to PERMA No. 3/2021: expand judicial review scope to full merits and relevant market definition (Article 13(1)); relax the evidence lock (Article 13(5)) to permit new expert and economic evidence; implement a two-stage filing model (Statement followed by Memory of Objection); and mandate full access to file (\*inzage\*) alongside confidentiality ring and data room protocols.

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-038](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-038): Issue a Supreme Court Circular (SEMA) harmonizing the definition of "days" to working days, suspending execution against non-appealing co-respondents until final judgment, and exempting non-monetary sanction recipients from bank guarantees.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-039](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-039): Promulgate the comprehensive PERMA Amendment incorporating ICLA's proposed 8-stage case workflow.

## Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-037](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-037): Central Jakarta Commercial Court pilot implementation handling major cartel objections (e.g., Fintech/Pinjol 97-party case).

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-037](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-037): 100% compliance with access to file (\*inzage\*) prior to substantive hearings; zero procedural dismissals (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\*) caused by relative competence or master case e-Court registration errors; adjudication within \$\le 60\$ working days.

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Amending PERMA No. 3/2021 to explicitly establish Judicial Review (\*Peninjauan Kembali\* / PK) procedures based on new evidence (\*novum\*) and manifest error, aligned with Article 67 of Law No. 14/1985, alongside establishing a dedicated legal framework for partnership (\*kemitraan\*) appeals.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Restructuring administrative review procedures into an equitable adversarial framework that balances KPPU's enforcement authority with robust constitutional due process safeguards.

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris
