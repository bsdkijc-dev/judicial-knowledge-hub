# Judicial Knowledge Wiki

The Judicial Knowledge Wiki is proposed as a curated institutional knowledge base.

## Knowledge Scope

- validated procedures;
- doctrines;
- training materials;
- research;
- lessons learned.

## Strategic Purpose

The Wiki supports institutional memory and structured knowledge preservation.

## Relationship to Knowledge Graph

The Wiki can provide human-readable knowledge objects that may subsequently be connected through a [Judicial Knowledge Graph](Judicial_Knowledge_Graph.md).
