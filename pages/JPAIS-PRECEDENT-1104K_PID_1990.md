## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 1104 K/Pid/1990, dated February 27, 1993, established a fundamental material criminal law rule concerning the absence of negligence (\*culpa\*) in traffic offenses under Article 359 of the Criminal Code when a motor vehicle driver is operating lawfully on the correct lane:

A motor vehicle driver operating a vehicle orderly on the correct lane (left side/own lane) cannot be convicted on the grounds of negligence (\*culpa\* ex Article 359 KUHP) when an accident occurs resulting in a fatality because the victim (a bicycle rider) first grazed/struck another bicycle rider ahead, fell, and ricocheted to the right directly underneath/into the path of the Defendant's bus. Because the Defendant's actions did not satisfy the element of negligence (\*culpa\*), the Defendant must be acquitted of all charges (\*vrijspraak\*) and his honor and dignity restored.

## Executive Summary

The central legal principle established in this decision is that cautious drivers operating in their proper lanes are not criminally liable for traffic fatalities caused by third-party collisions that throw victims unexpectedly into their path.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Drivers operating lawfully in their correct lanes without violating traffic rules lack the necessary negligence (\*culpa\*) required for criminal liability under Article 359 KUHP.
- Intervening collisions caused by third parties that propel victims into ongoing traffic constitute unavoidable accidents beyond driver control.
- Lower court convictions of compliant drivers are subject to outright reversal on cassation, resulting in pure acquittals (\*vrijspraak\*).

## Case Background

The Original Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation, **\[Defendant\]** (\[age removed\], bus driver), was charged with negligence resulting in death (Article 359 KUHP) because on February 13, 1987, in Desa Kejawar, Banyumas, the bus he drove ran over a bicycle rider (victim \[Victim\]), causing his death. Trial evidence proved the Defendant was driving slowly (20–30 km/h) on his correct lane (left side). The victim fell and ricocheted into the bus's path because his bicycle first grazed/struck another bicycle rider ahead while attempting to overtake a truck.

The Banyumas District Court (Decision No. 35/Pts.Pid.B/1987/PN.Bms dated March 23, 1988) convicted the Defendant to 6 months' imprisonment. The Semarang High Court (Decision No. 443/Pid/1989/PT.Smg dated September 14, 1989) enhanced the sentence to 8 months' imprisonment. The Defendant subsequently filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Construction of the Element of Negligence (\*Culpa\* ex Article 359 KUHP) in Traffic Accident Cases

The primary issue was whether a bus driver operating lawfully on their proper lane can be blamed or convicted of negligence (\*culpa\*) when a bicycle rider falls and ricochets under the bus wheels after colliding with another bicycle rider ahead.

The Supreme Court held that they Cannot Be Convicted (\*Element of Culpa Unproven\*). Negligence (\*culpa\*) requires a manifestation of carelessness or lack of vigilance whose harmful consequences could objectively be foreseen by the driver (\*voorzienbaarheid\*). When a driver travels slowly on their correct lane (left side) and an accident arises solely due to consecutive collisions among fellow bicycle riders causing the victim to suddenly ricochet into the bus path, the event constitutes a pure accident (\*musibah\* beyond the Defendant's control), leaving the element of negligence unproven. The Defendant must be granted a pure acquittal (\*vrijspraak\*).

### Supreme Court Authority to Correct Judex Facti Errors in Evaluating Negligence (Judex Juris vs. Judex Facti)

The issue evaluated whether the Supreme Court on cassation is authorized to annul conviction rulings from District and High Courts that erroneously classify accidental factual events as legal negligence.

The Supreme Court held that it is Authorized. Assessing whether a sequence of facts satisfies the juridical construction of the element "due to his fault/negligence" (\*culpa\*) under Article 359 KUHP constitutes a question of law (\*Question of Law\*). When \*Judex Facti\* (Banyumas District Court and Semarang High Court) impose criminal penalties despite trial facts showing the driver operated on their lawful lane, \*Judex Facti\* are declared to have misapplied the law (\*foutieve kepassing van het recht\*).

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Did the \*Judex Facti\* (Semarang High Court & Banyumas District Court) misapply the law (\*foutieve kepassing van het recht\*) by considering the Defendant negligent under Article 359 KUHP, despite trial facts proving the Defendant traveled on his correct lawful lane and the victim ricocheted into the bus path due to colliding with another bicycle ahead?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes, the \*Judex Facti\* misapplied the law. The Supreme Court granted Petitioner for Cassation \[Defendant\]'s cassation petition, annulled Semarang High Court Decision No. 443/Pid/1989/PT.Smg in conjunction with Banyumas District Court Decision No. 35/Pts.Pid.B/1987/PN.Bms, and adjudicated afresh, declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] unproven legally and convincingly guilty of committing the crime violating Article 359 KUHP (Pure Acquittal / \*Vrijspraak\*).

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Banyumas District Court & Semarang High Court)

Examination of facts concerning the accident on Friday, February 13, 1987, around 06:30 WIB on the public road in Desa Kejawar, Banyumas; facts regarding Santoso Bus AA 2587 A traveling from Purwokerto to Semarang on an upgrade/downgrade road at 20–30 km/h; presence of 3 bicycle riders behind a truck; facts where the first bicycle overtook the truck, the second bicycle overtook, grazed the truck side and fell, and the third bicycle (victim \[Victim\]) collided with the second bicycle, ricocheting to the right of the road directly beneath the Defendant's bus; Visum et Repertum No. 331/250/6/III/1987 by Dr. Budi Santoso; and the Defendant's humanitarian assistance to the victim's family.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The appellate and district \*Judex Facti\* misapplied the law. The bus driven by the Defendant was on the correct lane (left side), whereas the victim fell because he was grazed by the bicycle ahead, causing him to fall to the right and be crushed by the Defendant's bus wheels.
2.  Because the Defendant traveled on his lawful lane and the accident arose from another bicycle collision, no element of negligence/culpa was proven against the Defendant under Article 359 KUHP.
3.  The Supreme Court granted the Defendant's cassation, annulled lower court rulings, and adjudicated afresh, declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] unproven guilty of violating Article 359 KUHP (Pure Acquittal / \*Vrijspraak\*) and restoring his rights, position, honor, and dignity.

## Ruling

The Supreme Court ruled: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation (**\[Defendant\]**); Annulling Semarang High Court Decision No. 443/Pid/1989/PT.Smg dated September 14, 1989, in conjunction with Banyumas District Court Decision No. 35/Pts.Pid.B/1987/PN.Bms dated March 23, 1988; and ADJUDICATING AFRESH: 1. Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] not proven legally and convincingly guilty of committing the crime charged in the indictment; 2. Acquitting the Defendant therefore from such indictment; 3. Restoring the Defendant's rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity; 4. Charging State finances with court costs across all judicial instances.

## Evidence Status and Gaps

### Established by Evidence

- Defendant \[Defendant\] operated Santoso Bus AA 2587 A on his correct lawful lane (left side) at a reasonable speed (20–30 km/h).
- Victim \[Victim\] fell and ricocheted into the bus driver's lane due to consecutive collisions/grazing among fellow bicycle riders while attempting to overtake a truck.
- The Defendant possessed no physical space or avoidable opportunity to prevent running over the victim who suddenly ricocheted into his lane.
- The Defendant was established as UNPROVEN guilty of violating Article 359 KUHP and was acquitted of all charges.

### Not Established / Set Aside

- Considerations of Banyumas District Court and Semarang High Court declaring the Defendant negligent/lacking vigilance were annulled entirely by the Supreme Court for lacking valid legal constructions of negligence (\*culpa\*).

### Uncertainty and Gaps

- Legal Protection of Law-Abiding Drivers Against Accident Criminalization: This landmark ruling establishes a vital traffic criminal law doctrine in Indonesia: vehicle drivers who act prudently and remain on their proper lawful lanes must not be made "scapegoats" or criminalized for victim fatalities arising from the fault or collisions of third parties ricocheting into their path.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: Can a bus driver traveling on their own lane be convicted of negligence (Article 359 KUHP) if a bicycle rider ricochets into their path due to colliding with another bicycle?**

**A:** Cannot Be Convicted (\*Mandatory Pure Acquittal\*). Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 1104 K/Pid/1990, a vehicle driver traveling slowly on the correct lane (left side) is unproven to have committed negligence (\*culpa\* ex Article 359 KUHP) when a fatal accident arises because the victim ricocheted into the bus path after first grazing another bicycle rider. The absence of negligence requires the Defendant to be acquitted of all charges (\*vrijspraak\*).
