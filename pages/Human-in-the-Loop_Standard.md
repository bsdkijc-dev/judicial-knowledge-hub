# Human-in-the-Loop Standard

The Human-in-the-Loop Standard establishes human responsibility in AI-supported judicial workflows.

## Core Principle

**AI output is assistance, not judicial authority.**

## Minimum Controls

- identifiable human owner;
- review of high-impact outputs;
- ability to reject AI output;
- escalation mechanism;
- audit trail;
- uncertainty disclosure;
- incident reporting.

## Judicial Rule

The final exercise of judicial authority remains with the authorised human decision-maker.
