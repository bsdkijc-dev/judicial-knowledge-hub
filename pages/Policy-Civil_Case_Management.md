# Policy:Civil Case Management

# Civil Case Management Benchbook

*Latest 2026 Edition — Supreme Court Authorized Directory*

## Section 1: Pretrial Timelines

All civil actions filed under the fast-track tier must conclude discovery within 120 days of the initial answer filing. Extensions require an exceptional-cause motion signed by the administrative judge.

## Section 2: Remote Deposition Guidelines

- Transcripts from remote video video feeds must include absolute timestamping.
- Digital exhibits must be uploaded to the official portal 24 hours prior to deposition.
