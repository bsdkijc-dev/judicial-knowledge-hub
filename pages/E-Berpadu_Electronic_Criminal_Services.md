# e-Berpadu Electronic Criminal Services

Indicator 1.13 measures the percentage of criminal, jinayat, and military-criminal services submitted electronically through e-Berpadu.

## Covered Services

The Renstra identifies services including:

- seizure authorization/approval;
- search authorization/approval;
- detention-extension applications;
- suspension of detention;
- diversion determinations;
- online detention visitation permission;
- permission to leave detention;
- evidence loan/use authorization;
- transfer of detention;
- pretrial registration;
- other services determined by Supreme Court regulations.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Electronic criminal services}} {\text{Total criminal services}}\times100\\ \</math\>
