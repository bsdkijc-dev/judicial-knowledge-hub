## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 1130 K/PID/1985, deliberated on October 29, 1987 and pronounced in an open court session on December 18, 1987, established an important rule of criminal procedural and substantive law concerning the distinction between an acquittal (*vrijspraak*) and a release from all legal prosecution (*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging*) in a receiving-stolen-goods case under Article 480 of the Indonesian Criminal Code (KUHP).

The Supreme Court held that where one of the objective or subjective elements of Article 480 KUHP, particularly the element that the defendant "knew or should have suspected" that the goods originated from a crime, cannot be proven at trial, the defendant must be acquitted from the charge (*vrijspraak*) under Article 191 paragraph (1) KUHAP.

An *ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging* under Article 191 paragraph (2) KUHAP is applicable where the act charged against the defendant is materially proven, but the act does not constitute a criminal offense.

In this case, the defendant was considered a good-faith purchaser who did not know or suspect that the bicycle he purchased originated from a crime. The Supreme Court therefore corrected the lower court's *ontslag* judgment and entered an acquittal (*vrijspraak*).

## Executive Summary

The central legal principle is the distinction between:

- **Acquittal (*Vrijspraak*)** under Article 191 paragraph (1) KUHAP, where the defendant's guilt is not legally and convincingly proven because one or more elements of the charged offense have not been established; and
- **Release from All Legal Prosecution (*Ontslag van Alle Rechtsvervolging*)** under Article 191 paragraph (2) KUHAP, where the charged act is materially proven but the act does not constitute a criminal offense.

Because the prosecution failed to establish that \[Defendant\] knew or should have suspected that the bicycle originated from a crime, the element required under Article 480 KUHP was not proven.

The Supreme Court therefore held that the proper judgment was an acquittal (*vrijspraak*), not an *ontslag*.

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Public Prosecutor of the Pematang Siantar District Prosecutor's Office, annulled Simalungun District Court Decision No. 137/Pid/B/1985/PN.SIM dated July 29, 1985, and adjudicated the case itself by acquitting \[Defendant\] from all charges and restoring his rights, position, honor, and dignity through rehabilitation.

## Case Background

The Defendant / Respondent in Cassation, **\[Defendant\]**, \[age removed\], born \[place removed\], male, Indonesian citizen, and a farmer residing in \[address removed\], was charged with receiving stolen goods under Article 480 KUHP.

On June 3, 1985, \[Defendant\] purchased a blue women's Forever-brand bicycle from witness \[Witness\] for Rp 25,000.

The transaction took place during the daytime and was conducted openly. The initial payment was Rp 15,000, with the remaining Rp 10,000 to be paid after the bicycle documents were delivered.

The bicycle was subsequently established to have been stolen from witness \[Witness B\].

Witness \[Witness B\] had reported the bicycle missing on May 31, 1985. Witness \[Witness\] was arrested by the police on June 6, 1985.

The Simalungun District Court, through Decision No. 137/Pid/B/1985/PN.SIM dated July 29, 1985, released the Defendant from all legal prosecution (*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging*).

The Public Prosecutor of the Pematang Siantar District Prosecutor's Office filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Distinction Between *Vrijspraak* and *Ontslag van Alle Rechtsvervolging*

The principal procedural issue was whether a court may impose *ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging* where one of the essential elements of the charged criminal offense, particularly the subjective element of Article 480 KUHP, has not been proven.

The Supreme Court held that this was legally incorrect.

The two forms of judgment have different legal bases and consequences:

- **Acquittal (*Vrijspraak*) — Article 191 paragraph (1) KUHAP:** Applies where the defendant's guilt in relation to the charged act is not legally and convincingly proven because one or more elements of the offense have not been established.

<!-- -->

- **Release from All Legal Prosecution (*Ontslag van Alle Rechtsvervolging*) — Article 191 paragraph (2) KUHAP:** Applies where the act charged against the defendant is materially proven, but the act does not constitute a criminal offense.

Because the Simalungun District Court found that the Defendant was a good-faith purchaser who did not know or suspect that the bicycle originated from a crime, the "should have suspected" element under Article 480 KUHP was not proven.

The appropriate judgment was therefore an acquittal (*vrijspraak*).

### Cassation by the Public Prosecutor

The Supreme Court also considered the Public Prosecutor's cassation petition concerning the lower court's incorrect legal qualification of the judgment.

The Supreme Court held that the lower court had incorrectly applied the law (*foutieve toepassing van het recht*) in determining the appropriate form of judgment.

The Supreme Court therefore had authority to annul the lower court's decision and adjudicate the case itself.

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law

**Question:**

Did the Simalungun District Court incorrectly apply the law by imposing *Ontslag van Alle Rechtsvervolging* under Article 191 paragraph (2) KUHAP when the subjective element of receiving stolen goods under Article 480 KUHP had not been proven?

**Supreme Court Answer:**

Yes.

The Simalungun District Court had incorrectly applied the law.

Where the defendant does not know, suspect, or have reason to suspect that goods originated from a crime, an element of Article 480 KUHP is not established.

The legal consequence is an acquittal (*Vrijspraak*) under Article 191 paragraph (1) KUHAP, rather than *Ontslag van Alle Rechtsvervolging* under Article 191 paragraph (2) KUHAP.

The Supreme Court therefore granted the cassation petition of the Public Prosecutor, annulled the Simalungun District Court judgment, and adjudicated the case itself by acquitting \[Defendant\].

### Question of Fact

The factual issues concerned:

- The purchase of one blue women's Forever-brand bicycle on June 3, 1985 in Kp. Silau Bayu.
- The negotiated purchase price of Rp 25,000, reduced from Rp 30,000.
- Initial payment of Rp 15,000.
- Agreement to pay the remaining Rp 10,000 after the bicycle documents were delivered.
- Witness \[Witness\]'s statement that the bicycle documents were at his house.
- The fact that the transaction took place openly during the daytime.
- The theft of the bicycle from witness \[Witness B\].
- The arrest of witness \[Witness\] by Serbelawan Police Sector on June 6, 1985.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The *judex facti* at the Simalungun District Court incorrectly applied the law by characterizing the proper judgment as *ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging*.

<!-- -->

1.  Where the defendant does not know or suspect that goods originate from a crime, an element of Article 480 KUHP, particularly the "knew or should have suspected" element, cannot be established.

<!-- -->

1.  Failure to establish an element of the charged criminal offense requires an acquittal (*vrijspraak*) under Article 191 paragraph (1) KUHAP.

<!-- -->

1.  *Ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging* under Article 191 paragraph (2) KUHAP is applicable only where the charged act is proven but the act does not constitute a criminal offense.

<!-- -->

1.  The Defendant's status as a good-faith purchaser was relevant to the failure to establish the required subjective element under Article 480 KUHP.

<!-- -->

1.  The Supreme Court therefore granted the Public Prosecutor's cassation petition, annulled the Simalungun District Court decision, and adjudicated the case itself.

## Ruling

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the **Public Prosecutor of the Pematang Siantar District Prosecutor's Office** and annulled Simalungun District Court Decision No. 137/Pid/B/1985/PN.SIM dated July 29, 1985.

The Supreme Court then adjudicated the case itself:

1.  Declaring that the alleged offense committed by Defendant **\[Defendant\]** was not legally and convincingly proven.

<!-- -->

1.  **Acquitting the Defendant from the charges (*Vrijspraak*).**

<!-- -->

1.  Restoring the Defendant's rights, position, honor, and dignity through rehabilitation.

<!-- -->

1.  Ordering the bicycle evidence, a Forever-brand bicycle, to be returned to witness \[Witness B\].

<!-- -->

1.  Ordering that the court costs at all levels of judicial proceedings be borne by the State.

## Evidence Status

### What the Evidence Establishes

- Witness \[Witness\] was established to have stolen the Forever-brand bicycle belonging to witness \[Witness B\] on May 31, 1985 from the Dolok Ilir Palm Oil Plantation.

<!-- -->

- Defendant \[Defendant\] purchased the bicycle from \[Witness\] for Rp 25,000 in circumstances characterized as open, ordinary, and in good faith.

<!-- -->

- The Defendant did **not** know or have reason to suspect that the bicycle was stolen.

<!-- -->

- The Defendant was therefore entitled to an acquittal (*vrijspraak*) from all charges and rehabilitation of his rights and dignity.

### What the Evidence Does Not Establish

The evidence did not establish the Defendant's knowledge or reasonable suspicion that the bicycle originated from a criminal offense.

Consequently, the subjective element required for liability under Article 480 KUHP was not proven.

The lower court's *ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging* judgment was therefore annulled by the Supreme Court because it incorrectly characterized the legal consequence of the failure to prove an element of the offense.

## Uncertainty and Gaps

The decision is identified in the source as important jurisprudence concerning the consistent application of *vrijspraak* and *ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging*.

The source emphasizes that courts should distinguish between:

- Failure to prove one or more elements of a criminal offense; and
- Proof of the charged act where the act nevertheless does not constitute a criminal offense.

The source does not provide additional analysis of subsequent jurisprudential developments beyond this decision.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: Should a good-faith purchaser who does not know that second-hand goods were stolen receive an acquittal (*vrijspraak*) or release from all legal prosecution (*ontslag*)?**

**A:** The appropriate judgment is an **acquittal (*Vrijspraak*)**. According to Supreme Court Decision No. 1130 K/PID/1985, where a purchaser does not know or suspect that the goods originated from a crime, the "should have suspected" element under Article 480 KUHP is not proven. The legal consequence of failure to establish an element of the criminal offense is an acquittal under Article 191 paragraph (1) KUHAP, rather than *Ontslag van Alle Rechtsvervolging* under Article 191 paragraph (2) KUHAP.
