# JPW-REGULATION-001

**United Nations Security Council Resolution 1970 (2011)** is an international legal instrument adopted by the United Nations Security Council at its 6491st meeting on 26 February 2011 under Chapter VII of the UN Charter, responding to violence and attacks against civilian populations in the Libyan Arab Jamahiriya \[\].

## Summary

The resolution condemns the use of force by Libyan authorities against peaceful demonstrators, reaffirms state responsibility to protect populations, and imposes comprehensive sanctions including an arms embargo, travel ban, asset freeze, and refers the situation in Libya to the International Criminal Court (ICC) \[\].

## Legal Basis

Adopted under Chapter VII of the UN Charter, taking measures under Article 41 \[\]. It also references Article 16 of the [Rome Statute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rome_Statute) regarding deferral of investigations and UN Human Rights Council Resolution A/HRC/S-15/2 \[\].

## Background

Widespread and systematic attacks against civilians in Libya were determined to amount to crimes against humanity, triggering international concern from the Arab League, African Union, and the Organization of the Islamic Conference \[\].

## Objectives

The core objectives of the resolution include:

- Demanding an immediate end to violence in Libya \[\].
- Promoting fulfillment of legitimate popular demands and respect for freedom of assembly and expression \[\].
- Ensuring legal accountability for those responsible for attacks against civilians \[\].
- Guaranteeing safe access for humanitarian assistance and protection of foreign nationals \[\].

## Key Provisions

- \*\*ICC Referral\*\*: Refers the situation in the Libyan Arab Jamahiriya since 15 February 2011 to the Prosecutor of the ICC \[\].
- \*\*Arms Embargo\*\*: Obligates all UN Member States to prevent the supply, sale, or transfer of arms and related military materiel to Libya \[\].
- \*\*Travel Ban\*\*: Prevents the entry into or transit through member states' territories of individuals listed in Annex I \[\].
- \*\*Asset Freeze\*\*: Freezes without delay all funds and economic resources owned or controlled by individuals or entities listed in Annex II \[\].

## Implementation

To oversee implementation, the Security Council established a Sanctions Committee comprising all Council members \[\]. Neighboring states and UN members were authorized to inspect cargo of vessels and aircraft suspected of carrying prohibited items to or from Libya \[\].

## Policy Issues

Key legal and policy issues included restricting ICC referral financing so costs are not borne by the UN budget, establishing exclusive jurisdiction for non-state parties' personnel from outside Libya, and structuring mechanisms to release frozen assets for the benefit of the Libyan people \[\].
