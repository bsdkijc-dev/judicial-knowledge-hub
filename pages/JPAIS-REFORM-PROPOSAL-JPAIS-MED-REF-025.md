## Description / Deskripsi

Provides an exhaustive benchbook for adjudicating transnational maritime crime, examining UNCLOS spatial zones, flag State jurisdiction over stateless vessels, shiprider cooperative mechanisms, digital and physical evidence chain of custody, chemical fingerprinting via GC-MS and IRMS, corporate veil piercing, Habitat Equivalency Analysis (HEA) for ecological restoration valuation, and ITLOS Prompt Release proceedings under Article 292\[cite: 7\].

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-019](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-019): Maritime criminal jurisdiction is layered, functional, and spatially attenuated, requiring courts to establish precise geographic coordinates relative to baselines before exercising adjudicative authority\[cite: 7\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-020](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-020): Environmental crimes involving oil pollution face defense challenges contending that hydrocarbons originated from urban runoff or natural seeps, requiring complex chemical fingerprinting to disprove\[cite: 7\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-021](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-021): Multi-jurisdictional shell companies and single-vessel special purpose vehicles registered under flags of convenience attempt to insulate beneficial owners from criminal exposure and civil liabilities\[cite: 7\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-025](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-025): Indonesian courts adjudicate maritime infractions by verifying compliance with archipelagic sovereignty, fisheries laws, and marine environmental protection mandates\[cite: 7\].

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-025](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-025): Advanced tribunals apply rigorous scientific validation standards (Daubert/Frye), multi-sensor digital data fusion, and biophysical HEA metrics to quantify marine ecological damages\[cite: 7\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-008](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-008): Traditional tort theories based on market property values fail to account for lost ecosystem services, complicating judicial quantification of environmental harm\[cite: 7\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Adopt a structured six-phase trial framework—spanning jurisdictional verification, evidence admissibility, scientific fact-finding, liability attribution, environmental remediation valuation via HEA, and prompt release balancing—to guide judicial decision-making in maritime crime\[cite: 7\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-019](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-019): Require multi-sensor data fusion and SHA-256 cryptographic hashing to verify digital tracking and navigation evidence\[cite: 7\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-020](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-020): Apply refractory biomarker diagnostic ratios (GC-MS) and stable isotope profiling (IRMS) to establish definitive pollution source attribution\[cite: 7\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-021](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-021): Utilize Habitat Equivalency Analysis (HEA) to calculate biophysical debits and scale compensatory ecological restoration orders\[cite: 7\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-025](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-025): 120-day judicial training workshop on environmental forensics, HEA modeling, and ITLOS prompt release bond calculations for maritime judges\[cite: 7\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-025](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-025): 100% judicial compliance with UNCLOS spatial zoning verification standards; zero unverified digital chain-of-custody admissions; precise biophysical scaling of all environmental restoration judgments\[cite: 7\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Harmonization of judicial approaches across regional maritime courts to ensure consistent application of international law of the sea standards and environmental accountability\[cite: 7\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Adoption of standardized forensic protocols for oil spill sampling (EN 15522) and corporate transparency mandates targeting beneficial ownership behind flags of convenience\[cite: 7\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Stable carbon isotope ratios (IRMS) remain conserved during physical weathering processes, providing high-probability proof of origin even when 80% of spilled fuel has evaporated\[cite: 7\].
- Habitat Equivalency Analysis successfully replaces speculative monetary valuations with biophysical service-acre-year metrics\[cite: 7\].
