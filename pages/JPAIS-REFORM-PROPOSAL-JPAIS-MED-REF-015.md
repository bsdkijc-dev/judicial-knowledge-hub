# JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:JPAIS-MED-REF-015

## Summary / Ringkasan

## Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

- Entity ID: JPAIS-MED-REF-015
- Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
- Ingestion Status: Confirmed

## Description / Deskripsi

Synthesizes the August 2026 integrated Inventory of Issues (DIM) evaluating PERMA No. 3 of 2021 on procedures for filing and examining objections against KPPU decisions in Commercial Courts, covering 35 specific problem points distributed across 9 thematic issue groups (ranging from court jurisdiction, deadlines, formal requirements, examination stages, inspection scope, evidence/files, witnesses/experts, decisions/execution, to institutional support), plus 12 novel issues requiring regulatory incorporation\[cite: 27\].

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-001): Jurisdictional ambiguity for foreign respondents (foreign entities), conflicting day-count definitions between PERMA 3/2021 (calendar days) and PP 44/2021 (working days), and rigid filing deadlines tied to decision excerpts rather than complete copies create severe procedural barriers\[cite: 27\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-002): Unproportional bank guarantees required without fines, multi-party joinder complexities (Turut Termohon), SIPP/e-Court multi-applicant tracking limitations, and uncertainties surrounding judicial review scope (\*judex facti\* assessment of economic analysis and relevant markets) impair substantive justice\[cite: 27\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-003): Evidentiary restrictions against new evidence (\*novum\*), lack of standards for indirect evidence and economic models, limited access to dossiers (\*inzage\*), absence of confidentiality data rooms, lack of online witness/expert examination frameworks (\*hot tubbing\*), and dual \*inkracht\* statuses in joint conduct cases hinder fair adjudication\[cite: 27\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-001): Commercial Courts examine objections against KPPU decisions pursuant to PERMA No. 3 of 2021, relying on decentralized SIPP tracking and tight administrative rules across designated regional courts\[cite: 27\].

## Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-001): Comparative developed jurisdictions integrate specialized economic expertise, structured evidentiary testing, and comprehensive digital case management to ensure rigorous judicial review of administrative antitrust decisions\[cite: 27\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-001): 23 problem points touch 14 existing articles of PERMA 3/2021, while 12 critical issues (such as partnership dispute pathways, economic evidence standards, confidentiality data rooms, and execution of non-monetary remedies) completely lack regulatory guidance\[cite: 27\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Execute comprehensive amendments to PERMA No. 3 of 2021 addressing the 35 DIM items: harmonize calendar/working days, introduce proportional bank guarantees, clarify Commercial Court scope over relevant markets and economic evidence, permit limited reciprocal new evidence, establish data rooms and \*inzage\* access, modernize online witness/expert examinations (\*hot tubbing\*), and streamline enforcement\[cite: 27\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-001): Consolidate the August 2026 DIM across all eight internal court sources (Jakarta Pusat, Medan, Semarang, Surabaya, Makassar, and expert association papers)\[cite: 27\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-002): Draft the Amending PERMA text targeting the 14 existing articles and incorporating provisions for the 12 unregulated new issue domains\[cite: 27\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-003): Upgrade SIPP and e-Court software modules to support multi-applicant bundling, big file management, and secure virtual proceedings\[cite: 27\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-015](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-015): Multi-region Commercial Court deployment across Central Jakarta, Medan, Semarang, Surabaya, and Makassar\[cite: 27\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-015](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-015): 100% harmonization of day-count definitions; zero improper dismissals due to unproportional bank guarantees; complete integration of economic evidence review standards across all Commercial Courts\[cite: 27\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Provide legal and procedural certainty for economic objection proceedings, closing procedural gaps while maintaining trial speed and institutional accountability\[cite: 27\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgate an amended PERMA No. 3 of 2021, issue supplementary Supreme Court Circulars (SEMA) on case consolidation and extraordinary remedies, and align competition rules with the New Criminal Code (UU 1/2023)\[cite: 27\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- The August 2026 integrated DIM aggregates data and operational friction points from Commercial Court registries and expert association presentations across Jakarta, Medan, Semarang, Surabaya, and Makassar\[cite: 27\].
