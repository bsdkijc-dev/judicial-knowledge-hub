# Judicial Data Quality

# Judicial Data Quality

AI and evidence-based judicial management depend on trustworthy data.

## Quality Dimensions

- accuracy;
- completeness;
- consistency;
- timeliness;
- provenance;
- interoperability;
- validity.

## Principle

**Better AI cannot compensate for fundamentally unreliable data.**
