# Judicial Performance Intelligence

Judicial Performance Intelligence proposes balanced dashboards that combine multiple dimensions of institutional performance.

## Dimensions

- quality;
- timeliness;
- access;
- workload.

## Principle

No single metric should be treated as a complete measure of judicial performance.
