## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 86 K/Pid/1982 dated March 31, 1983, establishes two important legal principles in Material Criminal Law and Criminal Procedure Law:

- **Criminal Procedure Law (Examination of Similar Indictments):** Because the primary charge (Article 317 of the KUHP — Defamation via False Complaint) and the secondary charge (Article 311 of the KUHP — Defamation) are of a similar nature, functioning as alternative or primary-subsidiary charges, once the primary charge has been proven, the secondary charge does not need to be further examined or considered.

<!-- -->

- **Crime of Defamation via Complaint (Article 317 KUHP):** The Defendant's act of intentionally submitting a false complaint or notification letter to the Police alleging that another person had embezzled his bus fleet, despite knowing that the buses had been legally sold and that National Police Crime Laboratory (\*Labkrim\*) examinations confirmed the authenticity of the Defendant's signature, while refusing to retract the complaint, legally and convincingly satisfies the elements of the crime of defamation via false complaint under Article 317 of the KUHP.

## Case Background

The Defendant, \[Defendant\], \[age removed\], Director of PT Solo Express and residing in \[address removed\], was brought before the Surakarta District Court facing two alternative charges:

- **Primary Charge (Article 317 of the KUHP):** Intentionally submitting and handing over a false complaint/notification letter to Police Precinct (\*Komres\*) 951 Surakarta containing allegations that witnesses \[Witness\] / Sutjipto Santoso had embezzled 4 (four) bus vehicles belonging to him, whereas the Defendant knew or should reasonably have suspected that such allegations were false, with the intent to prompt Police investigation against the witnesses.

<!-- -->

- **Secondary Charge (Article 311 paragraph (1) of the KUHP):** Writing down defamation/libel against witness \[Witness\] by accusing him of embezzling 4 buses.

The Surakarta District Court (Decision No. 432/1977/Pid.B. dated December 11, 1979) declared the Defendant guilty of the crime of defamation via complaint and sentenced him to 4 (four) months of imprisonment.

The Semarang High Court (Decision No. 67/1980/Pid/PT.Smg. dated October 22, 1980) annulled the Surakarta District Court judgment and acquitted the Defendant of all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*) on the grounds that the Defendant's complaint was aimed at reclaiming his rights over the buses.

Dissatisfied with the Semarang High Court ruling, the Public Prosecutor at the Surakarta State Prosecutor's Office filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  Because the primary charge (Article 317 of the KUHP) and secondary charge (Article 311 of the KUHP) are of a similar nature, these charges ought to be treated as alternative. Therefore, with the primary charge having been proven, the secondary charge requires no further consideration.

<!-- -->

1.  The Semarang High Court erred and lacked sufficient legal reasoning (\*onvoldoende gemotiveerd\*) because it based its ruling solely on the Defendant's appellate memorandum without examining or investigating the factual testimonies of witnesses, sales receipt evidence, and the findings of the National Police Crime Laboratory (\*Labkrim\*).

<!-- -->

1.  Trial facts proved that the Defendant knew the buses had been sold to witness \[Witness\] under receipts signed by the Defendant himself, with the authenticity of the signature positively confirmed by Labkrim. Although advised by the Police to retract his complaint, the Defendant refused to do so and persisted in accusing the witness of embezzlement. Such conduct was found to constitute defamation via false complaint.

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from the Petitioner for Cassation, Public Prosecutor at the State Prosecutor's Office in Surakarta; annulling the Semarang High Court decision dated October 22, 1980, No. 67/1980/Pid/PT.Smg.; and independently adjudicating:

1.  Declaring that Defendant **\[Defendant\]** is guilty of committing the crime of: "Defamation via Complaint (\*Memfitnah dengan pengaduan\*)".

<!-- -->

1.  Sentencing him accordingly to 4 (four) months of imprisonment.

<!-- -->

1.  Ordering the Petitioner for Cassation to pay court costs at this level amounting to Rp 2,500 (two thousand five hundred rupiahs).
