# Wiki Editorial Policy

# Wiki Editorial Policy

Wiki content should be accurate, source-grounded and transparent about uncertainty.

## Rules

- no fabricated citations;
- distinguish fact from analysis;
- preserve provenance;
- flag uncertainty;
- identify proposals as proposals;
- review high-impact content.
