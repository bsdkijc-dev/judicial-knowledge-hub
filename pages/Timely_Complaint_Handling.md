# Timely Complaint Handling

# Timely Complaint Handling

Indicator 2.2 measures the percentage of complaints handled within the prescribed time.

The measurement begins when a complaint is received and registered in the SIWASMARI application at the Supervisory Agency and ends when the complaint handling process is completed.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Complaints resolved on time}} {\text{Complaints received}}\times100\\ \</math\>

## Targets

| 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| 70%  | 71%  | 73%  | 76%  | 80%  |
