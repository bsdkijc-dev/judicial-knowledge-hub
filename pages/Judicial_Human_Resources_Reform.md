# Judicial Human Resources Reform

# Judicial Human Resources Reform

The Blueprint identifies human resources management as a core support function requiring systematic reform.

## Core Areas

- recruitment;
- competency;
- career development;
- rotation;
- transfer;
- performance;
- professionalism;
- integrity;
- personnel distribution.

## Strategic Principle

Human resources management should use objective parameters and support competent, professional, and ethical personnel.
