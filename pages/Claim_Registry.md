# Claim Registry

The Claim Registry records substantive claims appearing in Wiki knowledge objects.

## Claim Fields

- claim ID;
- claim text;
- source;
- evidence level;
- date;
- jurisdiction;
- reviewer;
- verification status;
- related entities.

## Purpose

A claim should be traceable to the evidence supporting it.

## AI Use

AI-generated claims must remain distinguishable from verified institutional knowledge.
