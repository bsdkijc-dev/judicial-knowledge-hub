## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 90 K/AG/1992, dated 30 September 1993, established jurisprudence and a standard guideline for formulating judgments in divorce cases before the Religious Courts.

The Supreme Court held that the operative part of a divorce judgment based on continuous disputes and discord (\*syiqaq\*), where the plaintiff's divorce claim is granted, must be formulated accurately and according to the established judicial wording:

**“Declaring that one ba'in sughra talaq has fallen from the Defendant (\[Party A\]) upon the Plaintiff (\[Party B\]).”**

The Supreme Court corrected the wording used by the Palembang High Religious Court because it was not formulated according to the established standard, particularly the use of the expression "divorcing" and the specification of a particular date from which the divorce was stated to take effect.

## Case Background

The original Plaintiff / Respondent in Cassation, \[Party B\], \[age removed\] and a farmer, filed a divorce petition against her husband, the original Defendant / Petitioner in Cassation, \[Party A\], \[age removed\] and a farmer, before the Lubuk Linggau Religious Court.

The parties were legally married on 6 February 1982 in Megang Sakti II Village, Musi Rawas, as recognized by Lubuk Linggau Religious Court Decision No. 191/G/1991/PA.LLG, and had two children. Since April 1991, they had lived separately because of continuous disputes and discord. The Plaintiff alleged that the Defendant had behaved harshly toward the Plaintiff's child from a previous relationship, had been dishonest concerning financial matters and agricultural harvest proceeds, and had frequently ridden together with another woman.

The Lubuk Linggau Religious Court, through Decision No. 192/G/1991/PA.LLG dated 17 October 1991, granted the Plaintiff's divorce claim and pronounced one ba'in sughra talaq.

The Palembang High Religious Court, through Decision No. 35/G/1991/PTA.Plg dated 16 March 1992, upheld the Lubuk Linggau Religious Court's decision but changed the operative wording to:

**"Divorcing the Plaintiff, \[Party B\], from the Defendant, \[Party A\], with one ba'in sughra talaq effective from 17 October 1991."**

The Defendant subsequently filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  Objections raised by the Petitioner in Cassation concerning the assessment of evidence constituted an appreciation of factual circumstances and therefore could not be reconsidered at the cassation level pursuant to Article 30 of Law No. 14 of 1985.

<!-- -->

1.  The operative part of the Palembang High Religious Court's judgment, which stated *"Divorcing the Plaintiff... from the Defendant... with one ba'in sughra talaq effective from 17 October 1991,"* was considered insufficiently precise in formulating the judgment.

<!-- -->

1.  In accordance with Islamic civil law and the established principles governing Religious Court judgments, the proper formulation of the operative part of a divorce judgment must expressly state:

**“Declaring that one ba'in sughra talaq has fallen from the Defendant (\[Party A\]) upon the Plaintiff (\[Party B\]).”**

1.  Accordingly, the cassation petition was rejected, with a correction to the operative part of the Palembang High Religious Court's judgment.

- **Ruling:** The Supreme Court rejected the cassation petition of the Petitioner in Cassation, **\[Party A\]**, while correcting the operative part of Palembang High Religious Court Decision No. 35/G/1991/PTA.Plg dated 16 March 1992 in conjunction with Lubuk Linggau Religious Court Decision No. 192/G/1991/PA.LLG dated 17 October 1991, as follows:

1.  Granting the appeal petition of the Appellant.

<!-- -->

1.  Correcting the operative part of the Lubuk Linggau Religious Court judgment dated 17 October 1991, No. 192/G/1991/PA.LLG, so that it reads:
    1.  Granting the Plaintiff's claim.
    2.  **Declaring that one ba'in sughra talaq has fallen from the Defendant (\[Party A\]) upon the Plaintiff (\[Party B\]).**
    3.  Ordering the Plaintiff to pay the court costs at the first-instance level in the amount of Rp 56,500 (fifty-six thousand five hundred rupiah).

<!-- -->

1.  Ordering the Appellant to pay the court costs at the appellate level in the amount of Rp 13,500 (thirteen thousand five hundred rupiah).

<!-- -->

1.  Ordering the Petitioner in Cassation to pay the court costs at the cassation level in the amount of Rp 20,000 (twenty thousand rupiah).
