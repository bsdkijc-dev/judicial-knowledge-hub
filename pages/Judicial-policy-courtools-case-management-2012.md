# Judicial-policy-courtools-case-management-2012

**The CourTools Case Management Performance Evaluation Framework in Urban Trial Courts** (*Applying the Case Management CourTools: Findings from an Urban Trial Court Framework*) is a policy framework, performance evaluation methodology, and caseflow management guideline based on four key CourTools indicators developed by the National Center for State Courts (NCSC)—Measure 2: Clearance Rate, Measure 3: Time to Disposition, Measure 4: Age of Active Pending Caseload, and Measure 5: Trial Date Certainty. It was empirically tested and applied in the Criminal Division of a large urban trial court.

## Summary

Published by Collins E. Ijoma and Dr. Giuseppe M. Fazari in the *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA), this policy framework evaluates the flexibility of the NCSC's CourTools instruments at the South Mountain District (SMD / Superior Court of New Jersey, Essex Vicinage in Newark, NJ). The study proved that CourTools acts as an effective management dashboard (*vehicle's dashboard*) to prevent unnecessary delays and eliminate statistical overload for judges.

## Legal Basis

- **New Jersey Criminal Trial Court Processing Standards:** Establishes completion time targets for pre-indictment (100% within 60 days) and post-indictment (100% within 120 days).
- **American Bar Association (ABA) & COSCA Case Processing Standards:** National standards for handling criminal cases in the United States.
- **Trial Court Performance Standards (TCPS):** Trial court performance standards from the BJA and NCSC.
- **CourTools Performance Measures (NCSC 2005):** A framework of 10 court performance indicators.

## Background

Caseflow management is the key to preventing unnecessary delays, which are the primary cause of declining public trust in the judiciary. The NCSC published CourTools in 2005, and this study tested its application in the SMD Criminal Division, which manages ~150,000 cases annually with 50 judges and 950 staff.

## Objectives

1\. Validate the use of CourTools indicators 2, 3, 4, and 5 in urban trial courts. 2. Provide a concise performance information dashboard to prevent statistical overload. 3. Encourage courts nationally and internationally to use similar productivity measurement instruments. 4. Improve trial date certainty and reduce case backlog ratios.

## Scope

This policy was tested in the Criminal Division of a general trial court in a metropolitan area dubbed the South Mountain District (SMD / Essex Vicinage, Newark, New Jersey), covering three fiscal years of court data.

## Key Provisions

- **Measure 2: Clearance Rate:** Measures the ratio of outgoing cases to incoming cases, with a completion target of \$\ge 100\\\$. The SMD achieved a pre-indictment clearance of 104% (Year 1), 98% (Year 2), and 99% (Year 3).
- **Measure 3: Time to Disposition:** Measures the percentage of cases resolved within standard deadlines. The median resolution time for SMD criminal trials was 415 days (Year 1) and 349 days (Year 2).
- **Measure 4: Age of Active Pending Caseload:** Calculates the age distribution of unresolved active cases. The pre-indictment backlog (\>60 days) in the SMD reached 56%–59% of total active cases.
- **Measure 5: Trial Date Certainty:** Measures the ratio of trials that actually took place on the first scheduled date. Out of 2,625 trials scheduled in the SMD, only 29.8% proceeded through to a verdict.

## Implementation

- **Concise Management Dashboards:** Transformed complex AOC reports into simple, graphic-format dashboards for judges' consumption.
- **Shared Role Leadership Model:** Integrated executive cooperation between the Assignment Judge (AJ) / Presiding Judge (PJ) and the Trial Court Administrator (TCA) / Division Manager (DM).

## Policy Problems

- **Statistical Overload:** The delivery of excessively detailed and complex data fatigues judicial leadership, leading to ignored policy recommendations.
- **Low Trial Date Certainty:** Nearly 70% of registered trials experienced rescheduling or postponement.

## Reform / Change

- Continuous training for clerical staff in accurately entering case status data into automated information systems.
- Implementation of concise, monthly reports based on CourTools' central tendencies.

### 3 Most Important Improvements

1\. **Adoption of Concise Graph-Based Dashboard Reports:** Convert the AOC data delivery format from complex statistical tables into concise visual CourTools dashboards to prevent statistical overload among judicial leaders. 2. **Improvement of Clerical Data Entry Accuracy:** Conduct continuous, structured training for clerical staff to ensure that case status entries in automated databases accurately reflect the physical reality of the files. 3. **Strengthening Trial Date Certainty:** Implement strict continuance policies so that criminal trials proceed on their first scheduled date, slashing trial cancellations that hover around ~70%.
