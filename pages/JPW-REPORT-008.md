# JPW-REPORT-008

**Twenty-Fourth Report of the Prosecutor of the International Criminal Court to the United Nations Security Council on the Situation in Libya** is the 24th periodic report submitted by the Office of the Prosecutor (OTP) to the UN Security Council on 9 November 2022, pursuant to UNSC Resolution 1970 (2011), presenting progress on the accountability roadmap and updated strategy for Libya.

## Summary

The report records significant progress in implementing the Libya accountability roadmap, including sustained physical presence in the country for the first time since 2011, the collection of over 1,000 items of evidence, full participation in the Joint Team on crimes against migrants, and the arrest and extradition of key suspects located in Ethiopia.

## Legal Basis

- [UN Security Council Resolution 1970 (2011)](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/UN_Security_Council_Resolution_1970_%282011%29), invoking Chapter VII of the UN Charter.
- [Rome Statute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rome_Statute): Article 42(6) (Prosecutor Self-Recusal), Article 56 (Preservation of Evidence by the Pre-Trial Chamber), and Article 68 (Protection and Welfare of Victims and Witnesses).

## Background

Since receiving the UNSC referral in 2011, the OTP has investigated serious crimes in Libya. The 24th report evaluates implementation of the updated four-principle strategy first presented in the April 2022 report.

## Objectives

- Prioritize resource allocation for investigation and prosecution of the Libya situation.
- Strengthen empowerment of victims, witnesses, and civil society organizations (NGOs).
- Advance direct complementarity dialogue with Libyan law enforcement authorities.
- Expand international cooperation with third states, Europol, and the Joint Team.

## Key Provisions

The four lines of inquiry include:

- **2011 Violence**: Confirmation of death and termination of proceedings against suspect Al-Tuhamy Mohamed Khaled.
- **Detention Facilities**: Investigation of killings, torture, and gender-based violence in official and secret detention facilities.
- **2014–2020 Military Operations**: Investigation of extrajudicial killings in Tarhuna and Tripoli, and termination of proceedings against suspect Mahmoud Al-Werfalli.
- **Crimes Against Migrants**: Investigation of human trafficking and torture in cooperation with the Joint Team.

## Implementation

The OTP maintained a sustained field presence, conducted 20+ missions to 6 countries, collected 1,000+ items of evidence, and leveraged Microsoft Azure infrastructure and AI tools for processing Arabic-language material. On 7 September 2022, the OTP formally became a full member of the Joint Team investigating crimes against migrants.

## Policy Issues

- Political and security deadlock in Libya continuing to affect investigators' access.
- Delays in fulfilling requests for assistance (RFAs) and visa issuance.
- Constrained Court operational budget in the final quarter of the year.

## Relationships with Other Entities

- **[Office of the Prosecutor (ICC)](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Office_of_the_Prosecutor_%28ICC%29)**: Organ that published the report.
- **[United Nations Security Council](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council)**: Recipient of the periodic report.
- **[Joint Team](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Joint_Team)**: Joint investigative team comprising Europol and partner states investigating migrant-related crimes.
- **[Karim A.A. Khan QC](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Karim_A.A._Khan_QC)**: ICC Prosecutor who led preparation of the report and scheduled an official visit to Libya for November 2022.
- **[Nazhat Shameem Khan](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Nazhat_Shameem_Khan)**: ICC Deputy Prosecutor responsible for direct oversight of the Libya situation, who conducted an official technical visit to Tripoli in June 2022.
- **[Mohamed Younis A. Menfi](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Mohamed_Younis_A._Menfi)**: Head of the Presidential Council of Libya, who held an official meeting with Prosecutor Karim A.A. Khan QC in New York on 21 September 2022.
- **[Taher El-Sonni](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Taher_El-Sonni)**: Permanent Representative of Libya to the United Nations, present at the New York meeting with the Prosecutor.
- **[Abdoulaye Bathily](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Abdoulaye_Bathily)**: Special Representative of the UN Secretary-General for Libya and Head of the UN Support Mission in Libya (UNSMIL).
- **[Al-Tuhamy Mohamed Khaled](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Al-Tuhamy_Mohamed_Khaled)**: Former subject of an ICC arrest warrant related to 2011 detention-related crimes; proceedings formally terminated by Pre-Trial Chamber I on 7 September 2022 after Libyan authorities confirmed his death certificate.
- **[Mahmoud Mustafa Busayf Al-Werfalli](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Mahmoud_Mustafa_Busayf_Al-Werfalli)**: Former commander of the Al-Saiqa Brigade whose proceedings were formally terminated by Pre-Trial Chamber I on 15 June 2022 following confirmation of his death.
- **[Saif Al-Islam Gaddafi](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Saif_Al-Islam_Gaddafi)**: Subject of an ICC arrest warrant related to 2011 violence, which remains valid and unexecuted.
- **[Abdullah Al-Senussi](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Abdullah_Al-Senussi)**: Former subject of ICC prosecution whose case was declared inadmissible on 24 July 2014, as it was being handled by Libya's domestic judiciary.
