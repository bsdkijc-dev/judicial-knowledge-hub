## Overview

Supreme Court Judicial Review Decision No. 29 PK/TUN/2001 dated July 31, 2003, establishes an important legal principle regarding the principles of proportionality and justice in imposing disciplinary sanctions upon Civil Servants (\*PNS\*). The Supreme Court affirmed that imposing the sanction of honorable dismissal not at one's own request (\*pemberhentian dengan hormat tidak atas permintaan sendiri\*) for disciplinary violations under Article 4a of Government Regulation No. 30 of 1980 is excessively severe, unjust, and contrary to procedural/formal law. A more appropriate and proportional form of sanction for such a violation is a disciplinary penalty consisting of a demotion in rank by one level for a maximum duration of 1 (one) year.

## Case Background

The Petitioner for Judicial Review / Plaintiff (Dra. \[Plaintiff\], Junior Administrative Officer / Rank III/a Employee of the Ministry of Finance of the RI) was subjected to disciplinary punishment in the form of honorable dismissal not at her own request pursuant to the Decree of the Minister of Finance No. 504/KMK.01/UP.7/1996, which was subsequently upheld by the Civil Service Advisory Board (\*BAPEK\*) Decree No. 174/KPTS/BAPEK/1997 dated November 25, 1997. The Plaintiff was accused of assisting her ex-husband (\[Ex-husband\]) in committing fraud/extortion against prospective applicants for civil servant recruitment at the Ministry of Finance for the 1995/1996 fiscal year. In reality, the Plaintiff had separated and legally divorced her husband, and the misuse of applicant file photocopies had been perpetrated by her ex-husband without the Plaintiff's knowledge.

The Jakarta High State Administrative Court granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit. However, at the cassation level (Decision No. 127 K/TUN/1999), the Supreme Court overturned the Jakarta High State Administrative Court ruling and dismissed the Plaintiff's lawsuit. The Plaintiff subsequently filed a Petition for Judicial Review (\*Peninjauan Kembali\*), arguing that a manifest error and mistake existed in the cassation decision.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** The honorable dismissal not at one's own request for disciplinary violations under Article 4a of Government Regulation No. 30 of 1980 contradicts procedural/formal laws because disciplinary violations under Article 4a of Government Regulation No. 30 of 1980 are more appropriately penalized with a demotion in rank by one level for a maximum period of 1 (one) year. Imposing the sanction of dismissal is exceedingly severe and fails to uphold a sense of justice for a civil servant who has served for a long duration and has never previously been subjected to disciplinary punishment.
- **Ruling:** Granting the Petition for Judicial Review from Petitioner for Judicial Review Dra. \[Plaintiff\]; Annulling the Supreme Court decision dated November 25, 1999, No. 127 K/TUN/1999; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit in full; (2) Declaring void the Defendant's (\*BAPEK\*) Decree dated November 25, 1997, No. 174/KPTS/BAPEK/1997; (3) Ordering the Respondent for PK / Defendant (\*BAPEK\*) to issue a new Decree containing: a. Modifying the disciplinary punishment against Dra. \[Plaintiff\] NIP \[removed\] from honorable dismissal not at her own request to a demotion in rank by one level for a maximum period of 1 (one) year; b. Reinstating and rehabilitating the Petitioner for Judicial Review as a Civil Servant at the Ministry of Finance of the RI along with all civil service rights; (4) Charging all procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State.
