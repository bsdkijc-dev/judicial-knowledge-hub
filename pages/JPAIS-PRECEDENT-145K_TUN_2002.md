## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 145 K/TUN/2002 dated October 2, 2003, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal rules regarding the limits of judicial authority in civil service disciplinary execution disputes: Under Civil Service Laws, the determination of the effective date of a rank demotion sanction falls within the authority of the respective administrative official (State Administrative Official / BAPEK); however, an error by the State Administrative High Court in determining the effective date of the sanction does not invalidate the High Court's decision and can be remedied simply by amending its ruling.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Respondent for Cassation (\[Party A\], S.Sos., Civil Servant Head of PMD Section at the Poasia Sub-District Office, Kendari Municipality) was subjected to a disciplinary punishment of Dishonorable Discharge as a Civil Servant pursuant to the Decree of the Minister of Home Affairs No. 815.488.2701 dated July 15, 1998 jo. Decree of BAPEK No. 007/KPTS/BAPEK/2000 dated February 15, 2000. This punishment was imposed because the Plaintiff violated Government Regulation No. 30 of 1980 (having multiple wives / cohabiting without a valid marriage with two women named \[Party B\] and Waode Aziza). Regarding this violation, the Plaintiff had expressed remorse, received guidance from his direct superior (the Poasia Sub-District Head), both women signed statements declaring they would not make claims, and the first wife (\[Party C\]) divorced the Plaintiff at the Kendari Religious Court (Divorce Certificate No. 08/AC/1999/PA Kendari).

The Plaintiff filed a lawsuit against BAPEK's decree at the Jakarta State Administrative High Court. The Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 45/G/2001/PT.TUN.JKT dated August 22, 2001) granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part, annulled BAPEK's decree, and ordered BAPEK to change the disciplinary punishment to a Rank Demotion by one level for a maximum of 1 year effective as of February 15, 2000. Dissatisfied with the ruling, BAPEK filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The consideration of the Jakarta State Administrative High Court in altering the disciplinary sanction from dishonorable discharge to a rank demotion by one level for 1 year was accurate and correct, given the facts of administrative guidance and the Plaintiff's expression of remorse.
2.  The Jakarta State Administrative High Court committed an error in applying the law by determining the effective date of the rank demotion sanction (as of February 15, 2000), because under Civil Service Laws, determining the effective date of rank demotion is the prerogative of the respective administrative official (State Administrative Official / BAPEK).
3.  Nevertheless, this error does not invalidate the judgment of the Jakarta State Administrative High Court and can be sufficiently remedied by deleting the clause specifying the effective date of the sanction from the dispositive portion of the ruling.

- **Ruling:** Rejecting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation THE CHAIRMAN OF THE CIVIL SERVICE ADVISORY BOARD (\*KETUA BADAN PERTIMBANGAN KEPEGAWAIAN\*), with an amendment to the Jakarta State Administrative High Court decision dated August 22, 2001, No. 45/G/2001/PT.TUN.JKT concerning the 3rd dispositive item by omitting the phrase "as of February 15, 2000", so that the dispositive ruling reads as follows: (1) Granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part; (2) Declaring null and void the Decree of the Defendant Number: 007/KPTS/BAPEK/2000 dated February 15, 2000; (3) Ordering the Defendant to issue a new decree altering the disciplinary punishment imposed upon the Plaintiff, \[Party A\], S.Sos., a Civil Servant at the Poasia Sub-District Office, Kendari Municipality, from dishonorable discharge not at own request as a Civil Servant to a Rank Demotion to the adjacent lower rank for a maximum of 1 (one) year; (4) Rejecting the remainder of the lawsuit; (5) Ordering the Petitioner for Cassation to pay cassation-level court costs amounting to Rp 200,000 (two hundred thousand rupiahs). \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Thursday, October 2, 2003, by the Cassation Panel of Justices: Prof. Dr. Paulus Effendi Lotulung, SH. \[Junior Chairman for State Administrative Chamber / Presiding Judge\], Iskandar Kamil, SH., and Ny. Chairani A. Wani, SH. \[Associate Justices\]).\*
