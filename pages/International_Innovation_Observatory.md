# International Innovation Observatory

# International Judicial Innovation Observatory

The Observatory tracks selected international judicial innovations and translates them into Indonesian design questions.

## Priority Coverage

- Estonia;
- Singapore;
- European institutions;
- Australia;
- United Kingdom;
- Korea;
- other relevant systems.

## Method

**Observe → Verify → Compare → Assess → Adapt**

## Principle

The Observatory should not become a catalogue of foreign technology. Its purpose is strategic learning.
