# Strategic Evaluation Mechanism

# Evaluation Mechanism for Renstra 2025–2029

The Renstra requires structured, continuous and data-based evaluation.

## Monitoring Cycle

1.  Periodic monitoring
2.  Performance measurement
3.  Performance audit and external evaluation
4.  Feedback for subsequent improvement

## Monitoring

Performance is monitored quarterly, semi-annually or annually through the e-SAKIP application.

## Consolidation

Echelon I units measure their performance against established targets. The Planning Bureau consolidates the results and reports them to the Secretary of the Supreme Court for submission to the Ministry of Administrative and Bureaucratic Reform.

## External Evaluation

The Renstra identifies:

- Supreme Court Supervisory Agency;
- BPKP;
- Ministry of Administrative and Bureaucratic Reform

as institutions involved in external audit, accountability evaluation and planning-quality assessment.

## Feedback Loop

**Measurement → Evaluation → Feedback → Planning Improvement**
