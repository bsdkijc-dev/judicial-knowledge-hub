# JPW-REPORT-202510

## Summary

**A Study on the Enforcement of Child Handover Orders** (JPW-REPORT-202510) is an official judicial policy publication issued by the [Judicial Policy Research Institute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Judicial_Policy_Research_Institute) (JPRI) under the [Supreme Court of Korea](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Supreme_Court_of_Korea). Lead-authored by Research Officer Shim, Byungjun, the report comprehensively analyzes child handover enforcement procedures in South Korea across domestic family cases and cross-border Hague child return proceedings.

The publication details the procedural evolution from the legacy 1982 Practice Direction (재특 82-1) to the 2024 Hague Child Return Execution Rules (재특 2024-1) and the 2025 Revised Domestic Child Handover Rules. It provides international comparative legal models from Japan, Germany, and France, and proposes concrete statutory amendments to the Family Litigation Act and Civil Execution Act to establish a child-centered, court-authorized enforcement system.

## Legal Basis

The policy report anchors its analysis in domestic statutory law, court practice rules, and international conventions:

- **Constitution of the Republic of Korea**: Article 10 (Human Dignity & Right to Pursue Happiness), Article 36(1) (Protection of Marriage & Family Life).
- **Family Litigation Act**: Article 41 (Enforceability), Article 42 (Provisional Execution), Article 62 (Preservative Measures), Article 64 (Fulfillment Orders), Article 67 (Administrative Fines), Article 68 (Detention Orders).
- **Civil Execution Act**: Article 3 (Execution Courts), Article 5 (Use of Force by Bailiffs), Article 24 (Executory Titles), Article 257 (Delivery of Movable Property), Article 260 (Substitutive Execution), Article 261 (Indirect Compulsory Performance).
- **Hague Child Abduction Implementation Act**: Articles 11–14 (Seoul Family Court Exclusive Jurisdiction, Preservative Measures, Fulfillment Orders).
- **Supreme Court Practice Directions**:
  - *Former Rules*: Child Handover Execution Rules (재특 82-1, 1982).
  - *Hague Rules*: Hague Child Return Execution Rules (재특 2024-1, Effective April 1, 2024).
  - *Revised Domestic Rules*: Revised Child Handover Execution Rules (재특 82-1, Effective Feb 1, 2025).
- **International Treaties**: 1980 Hague Convention on the Civil Aspects of International Child Abduction; UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (Arts. 3 & 12).

## Background & Policy Problems

1\. **Structural Defects of the 1982 Practice Direction**: Historically, child handover was executed by applying movable property execution rules (Civil Execution Act Art. 257). Under the 1982 practice direction, if a child with cognitive capacity expressed verbal refusal, the bailiff was required to halt execution immediately, labeling it "non-executable." This clause was frequently exploited by taking parents, driving down enforcement success rates. 2. **International Non-Compliance Designation**: Due to repeated execution failures in Hague child return cases, the U.S. Department of State designated South Korea as a "Non-Compliant Country" under the Hague Abduction Convention for four consecutive years (2022–2025). 3. **Lack of Statutory Authority**: Executing human delivery using movable property provisions without an explicit statutory framework generated legal uncertainty, exceeding bailiff mandates and risks of psychological trauma to children.

## Phased Reforms & Policy Recommendations

- **Phase 1 (April 2024 - International Rules)**: Enactment of Supreme Court Practice Direction 재특 2024-1, eliminating the child refusal halting clause, clarifying bailiff authority, and mandating child-related experts as execution assistants.
- **Phase 2 (February 2025 - Domestic Rules)**: Revision of domestic practice direction 재특 82-1 to align domestic procedures with international Hague execution standards.
- **Phase 3 (Legislative Proposals - JPRI Report 2025-10)**:
  - *Family Litigation Act Amendments*: Replacing archaic terms ("infant/유아" to "minor child/미성년 자녀"); establishing child handover as an independent enforcement proceeding (Article 64-2); requiring prior Family Court Authorization (\`가정법원의 집행개시 허가\`); and codifying the priority of indirect enforcement (fines/detention before direct execution).
  - *Civil Execution Act Amendments*: Enacting Article 257-2 specifically for child handover; standardizing bailiff site-entry and search powers; mandating creditor presence; and strictly limiting physical force to non-injurious levels.

## Comparative Legal Analysis

- **Japan**: 2013 Hague Implementation Act & 2019 Civil Execution Act Amendments established direct execution with indirect priority and FPIC expert presence. Execution success rate increased from 44% to 83%.
- **Germany**: FamFG framework designates the Family Court as the execution court, utilizing coercive fines (Ordnungsgeld) up to €25,000, Ordnungshaft detention, and Jugendamt youth welfare support.
- **France**: Code civil Art. 373-2 allows the Public Prosecutor (Procureur de la République) to mobilize public force as a last resort, combined with daily astreinte fines.

## 정책 배경 및 문제점

1\. **1982년 집행예규의 구조적 한계**: 종래 유아인도집행은 민사집행법 제257조(유체동산인도)를 준용하여 실시되었음. 구 예규는 의사능력이 있는 아동이 인도를 거부할 경우 집행관이 즉시 집행을 중단하고 '집행불능' 처리하도록 규정하여, 양육자의 유임 및 집행 방해 도구로 악용됨. 2. **국제적 불이행 국가 지정**: 헤이그 아동반환 판결의 집행 실패가 누적됨에 따라, 미국 국무부는 2022년부터 2025년까지 4년 연속 대한민국을 '헤이그아동탈취협약 미이행국'으로 지정함. 3. **법률 유보 원칙 위배**: 물건이 아닌 아동의 인도를 단순 동산인도 규정에 의존하여 집행예규 수준에서 처리함에 따라, 집행관의 권한 범위와 강제력 행사의 한계에 대한 법률적 근거가 미비하다는 지적을 받음.

## 단계별 제도 개선 및 입법 제언

- **1단계 (2024년 4월 - 국제사건)**: 대법원 예규(재특 2024-1) 제정을 통해 아동의 거부 의사에 따른 집행중단 조항을 삭제하고, 집행관의 권한을 명확히 하며, 아동 관련 전문가의 집행보조 참여를 제도화함.
- **2단계 (2025년 2월 - 국내사건)**: 국내 유아인도집행예규(재특 82-1)를 개정하여 국제사건과 동일한 아동 중심 집행 기준을 적용함.
- **3단계 (개정 법률안 제언 - 본 보고서)**:
  - *가사소송법 개정안*: '유아' 용어를 '미성년 자녀'로 변경, 미성년 자녀 인도집행을 독립된 절차로 신설(제64조의2), 가정법원의 사전 '집행개시 허가' 의무화, 간접강제(이행명령·과태료·감치) 우선 적용 원칙 명문화.
  - *민사집행법 개정안*: 제257조의2(미성년 자녀의 인도집행) 신설, 집행관의 수색 및 방문 권한 규정, 채권자/대리인 출석 의무화, 아동의 신체·정신적 해를 미치는 강제력 행사 금지.

## 비교법적 분석

- **일본**: 2013년 헤이그이행법 제정 및 2019년 민사집행법 개정을 통해 직접집행 전 간접강제 우선 적용, 가정법원 허가, FPIC 심리전문가 동석 제도를 도입함. 개정 후 반환집행 성공률이 44%에서 83%로 상승함.
- **독일**: 가사소송법(FamFG)에 따라 가정법원이 집행법원이 되며, 최대 25,000유로의 질서과태료(Ordnungsgeld), 질서감치(Ordnungshaft), 청소년청(Jugendamt)의 개입을 활용함.
- **프랑스**: 민법(Code civil) 제373-2조에 따라 검사장(Procureur de la République)을 통한 공권력 투입과 이행강제금(astreinte) 제도를 병행함.
