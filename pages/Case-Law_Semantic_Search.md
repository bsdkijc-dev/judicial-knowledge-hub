# Case-Law Semantic Search

# Case-Law Semantic Search

Case-Law Semantic Search is proposed to enable retrieval based on meaning and legal concepts rather than keywords alone.

## Dependencies

- [Judicial Knowledge Graph](Judicial_Knowledge_Graph.md);
- [Indonesian Legal Ontology](Indonesian_Legal_Ontology.md);
- structured metadata;
- source validation;
- high-quality case-law data.

## Objective

Improve discovery of relevant judicial knowledge while preserving source traceability.
