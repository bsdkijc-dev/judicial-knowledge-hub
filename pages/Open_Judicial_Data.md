# Open Judicial Data

# Open Judicial Data

Open Judicial Data proposes publication of appropriate aggregate judicial data in reusable formats.

## Principles

- transparency;
- privacy protection;
- security;
- data quality;
- responsible reuse.

## Boundary

Openness must not compromise protected personal information, security or due process.
