# Case

# Case

A **case** is a judicial matter brought before a court for adjudication or other judicial action.

## Case Knowledge Object

A structured case object may include:

- Case identifier
- Court
- Jurisdiction
- Filing date
- Parties
- Subject matter
- Claims
- Defenses
- Evidence
- Procedural events
- Decisions
- Appeals
- Final disposition

## Case Lifecycle

**FILING** → **REGISTRATION** → **PREPARATION** → **HEARING** → **EVIDENCE** → **DECISION** → **REMEDY/APPEAL**

The exact lifecycle depends on the type of proceeding.

## Case Knowledge Graph

Example:

Case → filed at → Court

Case → governed by → Law

Case → produces → Court Decision

Court Decision → interprets → Legal Principle

## Case Analytics

Aggregated case data may support:

- Workload analysis
- Duration analysis
- Backlog analysis
- Policy research
- Resource planning

Individual case information requires appropriate privacy and access controls.

## AI

AI may assist with:

- Case classification
- Document organization
- Search
- Summarization
- Timeline extraction

High-impact applications require additional safeguards.
