# Evidence-Based International Learning

# Evidence-Based International Learning

International examples should be evaluated using evidence rather than reputation.

## Evidence Questions

- What changed?
- What was the baseline?
- What intervention occurred?
- What outcomes followed?
- Over what period?
- What were the costs?
- What risks emerged?
- What evidence supports causation?

## Rule

**Famous does not mean effective.**
