# JPW-REPORT-016

**Thirty-Sixth Report of the Prosecutor of the International Criminal Court to the United Nations Security Council on the Situation in Darfur** is the 36th periodic report submitted by the Office of the Prosecutor (OTP) to the UN Security Council on 25 January 2023, pursuant to UNSC Resolution 1593 (2005), covering investigative progress, developments in the trial of Ali Muhammad Ali Abd-Al-Rahman, the Government of Sudan's continued lack of cooperation, and the Office's case-completion strategy.

## Summary

The report evaluates implementation of the Darfur situation's investigative strategy and roadmap for the period August 2022 to January 2023. Key achievements include rapid progress in the trial of Ali Muhammad Ali Abd-Al-Rahman, while the principal obstacle remains the Government of Sudan's failure to honor its cooperation commitments.

## Legal Basis

- [UN Security Council Resolution 1593 (2005)](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/UN_Security_Council_Resolution_1593_%282005%29): Referral of the situation in Darfur, Sudan.
- [Rome Statute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rome_Statute): Article 42(6), on the Prosecutor's voluntary recusal.

## Background

Since the UNSC referral in 2005, the OTP has investigated international crimes in Darfur, dating to 1 July 2002. The 36th report follows the Prosecutor's visit to Sudan and to refugee camps in August 2022.

## Objectives

- Complete presentation of the Prosecution's case in the trial of Ali Abd-Al-Rahman.
- Secure fulfillment of the Government of Sudan's cooperation commitments.
- Expand evidence-sharing cooperation with third states and civil society organizations.

## Key Provisions

- **Trial of Ali Abd-Al-Rahman**: The first trial arising from the UNSC referral, on 31 counts of war crimes and crimes against humanity.
- **Cases of Al Bashir, Hussein, and Harun**: The suspects remain in detention in Sudan without surrender to the ICC.
- **Case of Abdallah Banda**: Oversight assumed by Deputy Prosecutor Nazhat Shameem Khan following Karim A.A. Khan KC's recusal.
- **Sudan's Non-Cooperation**: 34 requests for assistance (RFAs) remain unanswered by Sudanese authorities.

## Implementation

The OTP heard testimony from 50 witnesses over 78 trial days in the Ali Abd-Al-Rahman trial, developed Guidelines for Civil Society Organisations jointly with Eurojust, and added Arabic-speaking personnel to the Darfur Unified Team.

## Evidence

- Presentation of evidence on 31 charges of war crimes and crimes against humanity relating to attacks on Kodoom, Bindisi, Mukjar, and Deleig.
- Collection of third-state evidence meeting the January 2023 benchmark.
- Testimony from 50 trial witnesses across 267+ hours, including 31 written statements, 24 full written testimonies, and 9 witnesses via video link.
- 274 victims authorized to participate in the trial.

## Policy Issues

- Obstacles to establishing a field office in Khartoum and visa restrictions.
- Denial of physical access to public and government archival documents in Sudan.

## Relationships with Other Entities

- **[Office of the Prosecutor (ICC)](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Office_of_the_Prosecutor_%28ICC%29)**: Organ that published the report.
- **[United Nations Security Council](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council)**: Recipient of the report.
- **[Government of Sudan](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Government_of_Sudan)**: Party that failed to execute 34 requests for assistance and denied field-office access.
- **[Ali Muhammad Ali Abd-Al-Rahman](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Ali_Muhammad_Ali_Abd-Al-Rahman)** ("Ali Kushayb"): Principal defendant, a senior Militia/Janjaweed leader, tried on 31 counts of crimes against humanity and war crimes at Wadi Salih and Mukjar, West Darfur.
- **[Omar Hassan Ahmad Al Bashir](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Omar_Hassan_Ahmad_Al_Bashir)**: Former President of Sudan, suspected of war crimes, crimes against humanity, and genocide in Darfur between 2003 and 2008; his arrest warrant remains outstanding.
- **[Abdel Raheem Muhammad Hussein](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Abdel_Raheem_Muhammad_Hussein)**: Former senior Sudanese official, suspected of war crimes and crimes against humanity in Darfur between 2003 and 2004; held in detention in Sudan without surrender to the ICC.
- **[Ahmad Muhammad Harun](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Ahmad_Muhammad_Harun)** ("Ahmad Harun"): Former senior Sudanese official, suspected of war crimes and crimes against humanity in Darfur between 2003 and 2004; also not surrendered to the ICC.
- **[Abdallah Banda Abakaer Nourain](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Abdallah_Banda_Abakaer_Nourain)** ("Abdallah Banda"): Fugitive suspected of three war crimes relating to a 29 September 2007 attack on a peacekeeping mission in Darfur.
- **[Karim A.A. Khan KC](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Karim_A.A._Khan_KC)**: ICC Prosecutor who voluntarily recused himself under Article 42(6) from the Abdallah Banda case due to his prior role as defence counsel.
- **[Nazhat Shameem Khan](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Nazhat_Shameem_Khan)**: ICC Deputy Prosecutor who assumed full oversight of the Abdallah Banda case following the Prosecutor's recusal.
- **[Amal Clooney](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Amal_Clooney)**: Special Adviser for the Darfur situation, who facilitated review and analysis of large volumes of evidence and supported operational cooperation.
- **[General Abdel Fattah Al-Burhan](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/General_Abdel_Fattah_Al-Burhan)**: Leader of the Government of Sudan, who made a direct commitment to the Prosecutor regarding access to documents and witnesses, which has not yet been fulfilled.
- **[Eurojust](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Eurojust)**: Partner organization in developing the Guidelines for Civil Society Organisations.
