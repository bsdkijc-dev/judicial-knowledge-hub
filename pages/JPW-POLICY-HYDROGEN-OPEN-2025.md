# JPW-POLICY-HYDROGEN-OPEN-2025

**Unlocking Hydrogen: Construction and Supply Chain Strategies** is a global energy infrastructure thought leadership series and project strategy guide published by [Eversheds Sutherland](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Eversheds_Sutherland) on September 16, 2025, addressing construction complexities, procurement contracting strategies, and supply chain risk mitigation for global hydrogen projects \[1–6\].

## Executive Summary

As the scale of global hydrogen energy projects expands, construction challenges have emerged as critical determinants of project delivery \[1–6\]. This publication provides strategic insights for project developers to select appropriate contracting structures early and manage supply chain bottlenecks to mitigate operational risks and secure financial bankability \[1–6\].

## Legal Basis & Legal Disclaimer

- **Formal Legal Basis**: Not found in available sources \[1–6\].
- **Informational Disclaimer**: The content presented in the publication is provided strictly for general informational purposes and does not constitute formal legal advice \[1–6\].

## Background

Global hydrogen project developments face substantial technical construction complexities and supply chain constraints \[1–6\]. Developers are required to make strategic contracting and procurement decisions at early project stages to prevent construction delays, cost overruns, and financial non-bankability \[1–6\].

## Objectives

1.  Assist hydrogen project developers in navigating complex energy infrastructure construction issues \[1–6\].
2.  Identify and mitigate global supply chain bottlenecks affecting key components and materials \[1–6\].
3.  Encourage early-stage selection of appropriate procurement and contracting strategies \[1–6\].
4.  Ensure long-term bankability and successful commercial execution of hydrogen assets \[1–6\].

## Key Provisions & Recommendations

- **Early Contracting Decisions**: Mandates that developers finalize project contracting strategies early in the planning phase to avoid downstream execution risks \[1–6\].
- **Proactive Supply Chain Mitigation**: Recommends proactive measures to secure supply chains for critical components and long-lead items \[1–6\].
- **Non-Reliance Provision**: Clarifies that the publication serves as an industry insights series and does not substitute for tailored legal counsel \[1–6\].

## Implementation & Deployment

- **Publication Release**: Released globally and in the UK on September 16, 2025 \[1–6\].
- **Key Contact Team**: Managed under the leadership of UK Partners Peter Scurlock and Richard Black, along with UK Principal Associate Ray Hetherington \[1–6\].

## Policy & Operational Challenges

- **Construction Uncertainty**: High technical and engineering uncertainty during the construction phase of utility-scale hydrogen facilities \[1–6\].
- **Supply Chain Vulnerabilities**: Risks associated with component supply disruptions that jeopardize project milestones and lender bankability criteria \[1–6\].
