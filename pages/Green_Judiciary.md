# Green Judiciary

# Green Judiciary

The Green Judiciary initiative integrates environmental sustainability into judicial operations and infrastructure.

## Potential Areas

- energy efficiency;
- reduced paper dependency;
- sustainable procurement;
- efficient digital infrastructure;
- environmental performance measurement.
