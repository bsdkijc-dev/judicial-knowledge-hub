# AI Transparency

**AI transparency** concerns the availability of meaningful information about an AI system, its purpose, operation, limitations, governance, and use.

## Transparency Dimensions

Transparency may concern:

- Purpose
- Developer/provider
- Data
- System capabilities
- Limitations
- Performance
- Risk
- Human oversight
- Version
- Governance

## Transparency Is Contextual

Complete disclosure of technical details may not always be appropriate or sufficient.

The relevant question is:

**What information is necessary for meaningful understanding** and accountability?

## Judicial Context

Transparency may be particularly important where AI:

- Influences public services
- Supports legal research
- Processes evidence
- Classifies cases
- Assists institutional decisions

## Public Transparency

Possible mechanisms include:

- AI system registry
- Plain-language descriptions
- Governance documentation
- Impact assessments
- Audit reports
