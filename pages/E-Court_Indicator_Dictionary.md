# E-Court Indicator Dictionary

# e-Court Utilization Indicator

The indicator measures use of e-Court for civil, religious civil, and administrative cases at first-instance and appellate levels.

## Electronic Administration

The Renstra describes electronic case administration as covering:

- filing;
- payment;
- summons and notifications;
- answers;
- replies;
- rejoinders;
- conclusions;
- appeals;
- management;
- delivery;
- storage of case documents.

## Electronic Hearings

Electronic hearings comprise examination and adjudication supported by information and communication technology.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Eligible cases filed through e-Court}} {\text{Eligible cases filed}}\times100\\ \</math\>
