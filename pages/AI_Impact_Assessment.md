# AI Impact Assessment

**AI impact assessment** is a structured process for examining the potential effects of an AI system on people, institutions, rights, processes, and society.

## Assessment Dimensions

Potential dimensions include:

- Human rights
- Privacy
- Equality
- Fairness
- Autonomy
- Security
- Accessibility
- Institutional integrity
- Judicial independence

## Assessment Lifecycle

**IDENTIFY** → **ASSESS** → **MITIGATE** → **APPROVE** → **MONITOR** → **REASSESS**

## Judicial AI Impact Assessment

A judicial institution may consider:

1.  What problem is the AI intended to solve?
2.  Who is affected?
3.  What data is used?
4.  What decisions or processes are influenced?
5.  What could go wrong?
6.  What safeguards exist?
7.  Who remains accountable?
8.  How can an affected person challenge an error?

## Documentation

A useful assessment record may include:

- System description
- Purpose
- Data
- Stakeholders
- Risks
- Mitigations
- Responsible authority
- Monitoring indicators
- Review date
