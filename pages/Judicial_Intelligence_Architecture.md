# Judicial Intelligence Architecture

# Judicial Intelligence Architecture

The proposed architecture is an ecosystem rather than a single AI application.

## Layers

1.  Supreme Court and judicial governance
2.  Judicial data governance
3.  National judicial data and interoperability
4.  Judicial knowledge repository and knowledge graph
5.  Secure AI and analytics
6.  Judicial Copilot services
7.  Court administration services
8.  Public and professional access services
9.  Monitoring, audit and cybersecurity

## Architecture Principle

**Governance → Data → Knowledge → AI → Services → Monitoring**
