## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 33 K/Mil./1985 establishes an important legal principle in Criminal Procedure Law regarding the material requirements for the validity of an indictment. The Supreme Court outlined that if the Public Prosecutor / Military Prosecutor fails to include or formulate one of the core material elements of a criminal offense (such as the element of "causing potential loss" in the crime of document forgery under Article 263 paragraph (1) of the KUHP), the indictment is deemed not formulated with precision, clarity, and completeness as mandated by Article 143 paragraph (2) letter b of the KUHAP. This omission results in the indictment being declared \*\*null and void\*\* (\*batal demi hukum\*) pursuant to Article 143 paragraph (3) of the KUHAP.

## Case Background

The Defendant (\[Party B\], Corporal / Koptu-AD Nrp. 467187, Bamin Si 32 Yonif 731/BS) was charged with document forgery (Article 263 paragraph (1) of the KUHP) for altering the effective date (\*t.m.t.\*) of his Corporal rank and that of Witness I (Koptu \[Party A\]) on photocopies of their Appointment Extracts from 1976/1975 to 1974. This act was committed so that the Defendant would fulfill the minimum 8-year Corporal rank seniority requirement to participate in the Short Non-Commissioned Officer Candidate Selection (\*Secaba Singkat\*) for the 1983/1984 Fiscal Year at the Army Finance Education Center (\*Pusdikku\*) in Bandung.

The Ambon Military Court III-18 (Decision No. PUT/23/III-18/IX/1985 dated September 30, 1985) ruled that the Defendant was not legally proven guilty of document forgery and released the Defendant from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*). Dissatisfied with this acquittal ruling, the Military Prosecutor filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** Aside from the cassation arguments raised by the Petitioner for Cassation, the Supreme Court held that the Military Prosecutor's indictment in the \*a quo\* case was not formulated with precision, clarity, and completeness as required by Article 143 paragraph (2) letter b of the KUHAP, because the Prosecutor failed to formulate the element of "the use of such document could cause a loss" as one of the core material elements of document forgery (Article 263(1) of the KUHP). This omission prevented the court from examining the Defendant's case properly based on the indictment. Consequently, pursuant to Article 143 paragraph (3) of the KUHAP, the Military Prosecutor's indictment must be declared null and void.
- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation Military Prosecutor at Military Prosecutor's Office III-18; Annulling the Ambon Military Court III-18 decision dated September 30, 1985, No. PUT/23/III-18/IX/1985; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring the Military Prosecutor's indictment null and void, alongside all trial proceedings conducted based on that indictment; (2) Charging all procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State.
