## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 189 K/AG/1996 dated January 8, 1998, establishes important Islamic Civil Procedure jurisprudence concerning the assessment of vague lawsuits (\*obscuur libel\*): In joint property (\*gono gini\*) disputes, a High Religious Court decision that annuls a Religious Court judgment and declares the Plaintiff's lawsuit inadmissible on the grounds of vagueness (\*obscuur libel\*), without providing complete argumentation and legal reasoning regarding its obscurity, constitutes an error in applying the law and must be annulled by the Supreme Court. The Supreme Court reinstated the Gorontalo Religious Court judgment, which granted the equitable division of joint property between former spouses.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Petitioner for Cassation (\[Plaintiff\], \[age removed\], merchant, residing in \[address removed\]) and the Original Defendant / Respondent for Cassation (\[Defendant\], entrepreneur, Gorontalo) married lawfully in 1977 and divorced on May 24, 1994 (Gorontalo Religious Court Decision No. 28/Pdt.G/1994/PA.Gorontalo), and were blessed with 4 children (\[Child\], \[Child\], \[Child\], \[Child\]). The Plaintiff filed a lawsuit for the division of joint property acquired during the marriage (1977–1994), comprising a house in Tenda Village (SHM No. 44/1978), a multi-story house in Dolong, Central Sulawesi, a Vespa motorcycle, clove trees, a motorboat, and cash.

The Gorontalo Religious Court (Decision No. 80/Pdt.G/1994/PA.GTLO dated March 29, 1995 AD / 27 Shawwal 1415 AH) granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part and stipulated an equitable division of the joint property at 1/2 share each. The Manado High Religious Court (Decision No. 17/Pdt.G/1995/PTA.Mdo dated December 28, 1995 AD / 5 Sha'ban 1416 AH) annulled the Gorontalo Religious Court decision and declared the Plaintiff's lawsuit inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*) on the grounds that the lawsuit was vague (\*obscuur libel\*). The Plaintiff (\[Plaintiff\]) filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The Manado High Religious Court committed an error in applying the law by declaring the Petitioner for Cassation / Original Plaintiff's lawsuit unclear (\*obscuur libel\*) without providing argumentation regarding its obscurity, rendering the legal reasoning of the Manado High Religious Court incomplete (\*onvoldoende gemotiveerd\*).
2.  The \*posita\* and \*petitum\* of the Original Plaintiff's lawsuit detailed the evidence, locations, areas, and boundaries of the joint property objects acquired during the marriage, meaning the Gorontalo Religious Court acted correctly and accurately in considering and ruling upon the division of such joint property.
3.  Consequently, the Petitioner for Cassation's petition is granted, the Manado High Religious Court decision is annulled, and the Supreme Court independently adjudicates by upholding the Gorontalo Religious Court decision dated March 29, 1995, No. 80/Pdt.G/1994/PA.GTLO.

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation \[Plaintiff\]; Annulling the Manado High Religious Court decision dated December 28, 1995 AD, No. 17/Pdt.G/1995/PTA.Mdo; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part; (2) Declaring that the disputed properties listed under ad. I, II, III, IV, VI, and VII of the lawsuit petition constitute the joint property of the Plaintiff and Defendant; (3) Allocating the Plaintiff's share of the joint property as the property listed under ad. I of the lawsuit petition and 1/2 (one-half) of the properties listed under ad. III, VI, and VII of the lawsuit petition; (4) Allocating the Defendant's share of the joint property as the property listed under ad. II of the lawsuit petition and 1/2 (one-half) of the properties listed under ad. III, VI, and VII of the lawsuit petition; (5) Ordering the Plaintiff and Defendant to divide the joint properties listed under points III, VI, and VII of the lawsuit petition into 1/2 (one-half) shares each; (6) Declaring that the security confiscation (\*Conservatoir Beslag\*) executed by the Bailiff of the Gorontalo Religious Court on September 22, 1994, No. 80/Pdt.G/1994/PA.Gtlo is valid and enforceable; (7) Rejecting the remainder of the Plaintiff's lawsuit; (8) Ordering the Plaintiff to pay first-instance court costs and ordering the Appellant to pay appellate-level court costs; Ordering the Petitioner for Cassation to pay cassation-level court costs amounting to Rp 50,000 (fifty thousand rupiahs). \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Thursday, January 8, 1998, by the Cassation Panel of Justices: H. Yahya Harahap, SH. \[Junior Chairman / Presiding Judge\], Drs. H. Taufiq, SH., and H. Chabib Sjarbini, SH.).\*
