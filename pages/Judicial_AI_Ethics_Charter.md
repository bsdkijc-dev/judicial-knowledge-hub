# Judicial AI Ethics Charter

# Judicial AI Ethics Charter

The proposed Charter establishes safeguards for the use of AI in judicial institutions.

## Principles

- purpose limitation;
- legality;
- fundamental rights;
- non-discrimination;
- information security;
- data minimisation;
- traceability;
- appropriate explainability;
- human oversight;
- contestability;
- auditability;
- procurement accountability.
