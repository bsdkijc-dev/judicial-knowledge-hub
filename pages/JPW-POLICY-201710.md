# JPW-POLICY-201710

## Abstract & Executive Summary

**Improving the Procedure for Refugee Status Determination and Judicial Review in Korea** is an official judicial policy research report published by the [Judicial Policy Research Institute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Judicial_Policy_Research_Institute) (JPRI) of the Supreme Court of Korea (Research Series 2017-10). Co-authored by Research Fellows [Lee, Hyeyoung](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Lee%2C_Hyeyoung) and [Pyo, Hyunduk](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Pyo%2C_Hyunduk), the report presents a comprehensive analysis of the administrative refugee status determination (RSD) procedure conducted by immigration authorities under the [Ministry of Justice](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_%28Korea%29) and the subsequent judicial review proceedings handled by administrative courts.

Evaluating comparative asylum frameworks across eight international jurisdictions (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Germany, France, and Japan) as well as [UNHCR](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/UNHCR) and [EU](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/European_Union) asylum directives, the report formulates a structural reform package designed to enhance both procedural fairness (\*절차의 공정성\*) and operational efficiency (\*신속성·효율성\*).

## Statutory & Legal Basis

The research was conducted under the 2017 Supreme Court Annual Research Plan. The analytical framework evaluates compliance and operational friction across several statutory and international instruments:

- **[Korean Refugee Act](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Refugee_Act_%28Korea%29) (Act No. 11298)**: Enacted February 10, 2012; effective July 1, 2013.
- **[Korean Immigration Control Act](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Immigration_Control_Act_%28Korea%29)** and **[Korean Administrative Litigation Act](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Administrative_Litigation_Act_%28Korea%29)**.
- **[1951 Refugee Convention](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Convention_Relating_to_the_Status_of_Refugees) & 1967 Protocol**.
- **[Convention Against Torture](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Convention_Against_Torture) (CAT)**, specifically Article 3 (Non-Refoulement).

## Policy Background & Problem Statement

Following the enactment of the stand-alone [Refugee Act](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Refugee_Act_%28Korea%29) in 2012, asylum applications in South Korea surged from 2,896 in 2014 to 7,542 in 2016. First-instance administrative lawsuits contesting refugee status denials similarly rose from 423 cases in 2014 to 3,161 in 2016. Despite this volume, recognition rates remained exceptionally low compared to [OECD](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/OECD) averages, while appeal rates to the High Court (76.9%) and Supreme Court (71.3%) remained disproportionately high in 2016.

Key systemic bottlenecks identified include: 1. **Airport Port-of-Entry Screening Bottlenecks**: Applicants receiving a non-referral decision (\*불회부결정\*) lack transparent appeal mechanisms and face prolonged isolation in Departure Waiting Rooms (\*출국대기실\*) without adequate legal protection. 2. **Administrative Determination Flaws**: Inadequate interview recording, shortage of qualified certified interpreters, and overlapping duties between immigration enforcement personnel and asylum assessors. 3. **Formalistic Administrative Appeals**: The [Refugee Committee](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Refugee_Committee) under the Ministry of Justice operates non-permanently and primarily conducts documentary reviews without mandatory oral hearings. 4. **Four-Tier Fact-Finding Duplication**: The repetitive full fact-finding process (First-instance Administrative Assessment → Refugee Committee Review → First-instance Administrative Court → High Court) induces severe judicial backlog and creates opportunities for bad-faith applications intended solely to extend temporary stay status (G-1 visa).

## International Comparative Analysis

The study systematically evaluates eight comparative international legal frameworks:

- **United States**: Structural bifurcation between affirmative RSD under [USCIS](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/USCIS) and defensive proceedings under [EOIR](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Executive_Office_for_Immigration_Review), incorporating the one-year filing deadline.
- **United Kingdom**: Home Office triage routing coupled with the specialized two-tier tribunal system ([First-tier Tribunal](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/First-tier_Tribunal) and [Upper Tribunal](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Upper_Tribunal)).
- **Canada**: Specialized quasi-judicial body—the [Immigration and Refugee Board of Canada](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Immigration_and_Refugee_Board_of_Canada) (IRB)—housing the Refugee Protection Division (RPD) and Refugee Appeal Division (RAD).
- **France**: Autonomous first-instance administrative authority ([OFPRA](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/OFPRA)) paired with the specialized asylum court ([CNDA](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/CNDA)).
- **New Zealand**: Centralized Country Research Branch (CRB) model providing real-time Country of Origin Information (COI).

## Key Reform Recommendations

1\. **Airport Screening Reform**: Restrict non-referral decisions strictly to manifestly unfounded or fraudulent applications, issue formal written rationale, and guarantee a 3-day emergency appeal channel. 2. **Mandatory Interview Recording & Separation of Functions**: Mandate audio/video recording of asylum interviews, grant applicants right of review over interview transcripts (\*면접조서\*), and strictly separate asylum assessor qualifications from immigration enforcement functions. 3. **Establishment of a Quasi-Judicial Refugee Tribunal**: Reconstruct the passive Refugee Committee into an independent Quasi-Judicial Tribunal (\*난민심판원\*) featuring permanent adjudicators and mandatory oral hearings. 4. **Judicial Evidentiary Paradigm Shift**: Transition from civil law foundationalism (demanding strict documentary proof) to a \*coherentism\* paradigm (\*정합주의\*), emphasizing overall narrative consistency, supported by a centralized national Country of Origin Information (COI) Center. 5. **Differentiated Fast-Track Procedures**: Implement accelerated determination tracks for manifestly unfounded or repetitive claims lacking new factual grounds.

## 개요

**난민인정과 재판 절차의 개선 방안**은 대한민국 [대법원 사법정책연구원](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/%EB%8C%80%EB%B2%95%EC%9B%90_%EC%82%AC%EB%B2%95%EC%A0%95%EC%B1%85%EC%97%B0%EA%B5%AC%EC%9B%90)(JPRI)이 발간한 공식 사법정책 연구보고서(연구총서 2017-10)이다. 본 보고서는 [이혜영](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/%EC%9D%B4%ED%98%9C%EC%98%81) 연구위원과 [표현덕](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/%ED%91%9C%ED%98%84%EB%8D%95) 연구위원이 공동 연구책임자로 작성하였으며, [법무부](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/%EB%B2%95%EB%AC%B4%EB%B6%80) 출입국·외국인관서의 행정적 난민인정절차(RSD)와 [행정법원](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/%ED%96%89%EC%A0%95%EB%B2%95%EC%9B%90)의 난민재판(사법심사) 절차 전반을 다각도로 분석하고 있다.

세계 8개 주요국(미국, 영국, 캐나다, 호주, 뉴질랜드, 독일, 프랑스, 일본)의 비교법적 제도와 [UNHCR](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/UNHCR) 및 [EU](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/%EC%9C%A0%EB%9F%BD_%EC%97%B0%ED%95%A9) 난민인정절차 지침을 평가하여, 절차의 공정성 확보와 신속성·효율성 제고를 동시에 달성하기 위한 법·제도적 개혁 방안을 제시한다.

## 정책적 배경 및 문제점

2013년 단독 [난민법](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/%EB%82%9C%EB%AF%BC%EB%B2%95) 시행 이후, 행정 단계의 난민신청은 2014년 2,896건에서 2016년 7,542건으로 급증하였다. 이에 따라 1심 행정법원의 난민 불인정처분 취소소송 역시 2014년 423건에서 2016년 3,161건으로 대폭 증가하였다. 그러나 한국의 난민인정률은 [OECD](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/OECD) 회원국 평균 대비 현저히 낮은 수준을 유지하였으며, 고등법원 항소율(76.9%) 및 대법원 상고율(71.3%)은 극도로 높은 수치를 기록하였다.

주요 절차적 병목 및 제도적 문제점은 다음과 같다: 1. **출입국항 회부심사(공항 심사)의 한계**: 불회부결정을 받은 신청자에 대한 불복 절차가 부재하며, 출국대기실 내 장기 대기로 인한 인권 침해 우려가 존재함. 2. **1단계 행정심사의 불투명성**: 면접 과정의 녹음·녹화 미비, 전문 난민통역인 부족, 단속·체류 관리 기능과의 미분리로 인한 전문성 한계. 3. **난민위원회의 형식적 운영**: 법무부 산하 [난민위원회](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/%EB%82%9C%EB%AF%BC%EC%9C%84%EC%9B%90%ED%9A%8C)가 비상설로 운영되어 서면심사 위주로 진행되며, 구술심문 기회가 보장되지 않음. 4. **4단계 사실심 반복에 따른 비효율**: 행정심사 → 난민위원회 이의신청 → 1심 법원 → 고등법원으로 이어지는 4차례의 사실심 반복이 남용적 신청자의 체류자격(G-1) 연장 수단으로 악용되는 문제 발생.

## 국제 비교법적 분석

보고서는 주요 8개국의 난민심사 및 재판 체계를 비교 분석한다:

- **미국**: [USCIS](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/USCIS)의 적극적 신청(Affirmative)과 [EOIR](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/EOIR) 출입국법원의 방어적 신청(Defensive)의 구조적 분리 및 1년 신청 시한제 운영.
- **영국**: 내무성(Home Office)의 분류(Triage) 절차 및 전문화된 2단계 심판원([First-tier Tribunal](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/First-tier_Tribunal) / [Upper Tribunal](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Upper_Tribunal)) 운영.
- **캐나다**: 독립적 준사법기관인 [이민난민심판원](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/%EC%BA%90%EB%82%98%EB%8B%A4_%EC%9D%B4%EB%AF%BC%EB%82%9C%EB%AF%BC%EC%8B%AC%ED%8C%90%EC%9B%90)(IRB) 산하 난민보호부(RPD) 및 난민이의신청부(RAD)를 통한 전문적 심사.
- **프랑스**: 독립 행정청인 [OFPRA](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/OFPRA)와 전문 행정법원인 [국립난민재판소](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/%EA%B5%AD%EB%A6%BD%EB%82%9C%EB%AF%BC%EC%9E%AC%ED%8C%90%EC%86%8C)(CNDA) 중심의 이원적 체계.
- **뉴질랜드**: 국가정보조사국(CRB)을 통한 국가원산지정보(COI)의 체계적 제공.

## 주요 개혁 제안

1\. **출입국항 회부심사 개선**: 불회부결정을 명백히 명분 없는 신청으로 한정하고, 서면 통지 및 3일 이내의 긴급 불복 절차 마련. 2. **면접 녹음·녹화 의무화 및 기능 분리**: 난민면접의 음성·영상녹화를 의무화하고 면접조서 확인권을 보장하며, 난민심사관의 자격을 단속 업무와 분리. 3. **준사법기관 '난민심판원' 설립**: 비상설 난민위원회를 상설 전문 준사법기관인 난민심판원으로 개편하고 구술심문을 의무화. 4. **증명책임 및 재판 패러다임 전환**: 물리적 문서 검증 중심(기초주의)에서 진술의 전체적 일관성을 평가하는 정합주의(Coherentism)로 전환하고, 국가원산지정보센터(COI Center)를 구축. 5. **신속절차(Fast-Track) 도입**: 명백히 이유 없는 신청이나 동일한 사유의 재신청에 대한 신속 심사 절차 마련.
