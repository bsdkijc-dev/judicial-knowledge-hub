# AI Timeline and Evidence Mapping

# AI Timeline and Evidence Mapping

The initiative assists authorised users in organising dates, events and documents.

## Critical Boundary

The system must **not determine evidentiary weight**.

Its role is organisational and analytical assistance.

## Requirements

- source traceability;
- human validation;
- controlled pilot;
- defined risk tier;
- measurable outcome;
- cybersecurity;
- interoperability.
