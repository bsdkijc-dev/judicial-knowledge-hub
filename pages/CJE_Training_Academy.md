# Continuing Judicial Education (CJE) Training Academy

Welcome to the CJE Training Hub. This portal hosts structured modular courses designed to keep judicial officers updated on modern evidence and technological legal frameworks.

## Active Training Tracks

- **[Module 1: Cybercrime & Digital Evidence](CJE-_Cybercrime_and_Digital_Evidence.md)**: Learn about cryptographic file snapshots, metadata analysis, and handling digital chains of custody.
- **[Module 2: Electronic Evidence Standards](CJE-_Electronic_Evidence_Standards.md)**: Deep dive into data admissibility frameworks and verification rules.
