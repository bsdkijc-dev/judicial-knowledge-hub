# Explainability

**Explainability** concerns the ability to provide meaningful information about how an AI system produces or supports an output.

## Explainability Questions

Depending on the system, users may need to understand:

- What information was considered?
- What factors influenced the result?
- What limitations exist?
- How reliable is the output?
- What uncertainty exists?
- How can an error be challenged?

## Explanation vs Technical Detail

A technical description of an algorithm does not automatically provide a useful explanation to a judge, administrator, lawyer, or member of the public.

## Judicial Context

Explainability requirements should increase where AI outputs have greater consequences for:

- Rights
- Legal interests
- Institutional decisions
- Access to justice

## Limits

Some AI systems may have significant technical limitations on interpretability.

This should be treated as a governance issue rather than hidden from users.
