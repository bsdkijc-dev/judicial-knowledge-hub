# Judicial Research and Development

The Blueprint positions research and development as an institutional center of excellence and center of thought.

## Requirements

Research and development should be:

- systematic;
- programmed;
- supported by competent personnel;
- supported by integrity;
- adequately funded.

## Strategic Role

Research supports:

- adjudication;
- development of legal substance;
- Supreme Court policy;
- organizational learning.
