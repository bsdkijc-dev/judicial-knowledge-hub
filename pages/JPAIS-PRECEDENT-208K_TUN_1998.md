## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 208 K/TUN/1998 dated November 25, 1999, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal principles regarding absolute judicial competence in tax collection disputes: Objections or lawsuits against the implementation of tax collection Warrants of Execution (\*Surat Paksa\*) may only be submitted to the Tax Judiciary Board (BPSP/MPP). Prior to the establishment of the Tax Judiciary Board, such objections or lawsuits were to be submitted to the District Court pursuant to Article 23 paragraph (2) and its Elucidation of Law No. 9 of 1994 concerning Amendments to Law No. 6 of 1983 on General Provisions and Tax Procedures. Consequently, the State Administrative Court (PTUN/PTTUN) lacks the competence to adjudicate lawsuits challenging the execution of Warrants of Execution.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Respondent for Cassation (PT Natra Raya, represented by President Director Donald C. Loebach) filed a lawsuit against the Original Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation (Head of the Foreign Investment Tax Service Office / KPP PMA) at the Jakarta State Administrative Court. The lawsuit challenged the issuance of Warrant of Execution No. Print. 315/WPJ.06/KP.0208 dated September 5, 1995, for collecting tax arrears under tax assessment notices (SKP, SKPT, STP) for Income Tax and Luxury Goods Sales Tax. The Plaintiff argued that the Warrant of Execution was issued while objections or appeals regarding the underlying tax assessments were still being processed by the Directorate General of Taxes / Tax Advisory Board (MPP), meaning the tax debt did not yet possess permanent legal force (\*in kracht van gewijsde\*) and could not yet be collected via a Warrant of Execution.

The Jakarta State Administrative Court (Decision No. 159/G.TUN/1995/PTUN-JKT dated June 5, 1996) rejected the Defendant's exception, granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in its entirety, and annulled the disputed Warrant of Execution. The Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 106/B/1997/PT.TUN.JKT dated November 12, 1997) upheld the Jakarta State Administrative Court judgment. Dissatisfied with the ruling, the KPP PMA filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court asserting the absolute competence exception.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The cassation objections raised by the Petitioner for Cassation regarding absolute competence are well-founded because the Jakarta State Administrative High Court committed an error in applying the law.
2.  Pursuant to Article 23 paragraph (2) and its Elucidation of Law No. 9 of 1994 concerning Amendments to Law No. 6 of 1983 on General Provisions and Tax Procedures, objections or lawsuits against the implementation of a Warrant of Execution may only be submitted to the Tax Judiciary Board. Before the Tax Judiciary Board was formed, such matters were submitted to the District Court.
3.  Because the State Administrative Court lacks absolute competence to examine and adjudicate disputes concerning the implementation of tax collection Warrants of Execution, the Defendant's exception must be sustained and the Plaintiff's lawsuit declared inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*).

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation HEAD OF THE FOREIGN INVESTMENT TAX SERVICE OFFICE (\*KEPALA KANTOR PELAYANAN PAJAK PENANAMAN MODAL ASING\*); Annulling the Jakarta State Administrative High Court decision dated November 12, 1997, No. 106/B/1997/PT.TUN.JKT and the Jakarta State Administrative Court decision dated June 5, 1996, No. 159/G.TUN/1995/PTUN-JKT; and Independently Adjudicating: IN EXCEPTION: Sustaining the Defendant's exception; IN THE PRINCIPAL CASE: Declaring the Plaintiff's lawsuit inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*); Ordering the Respondent for Cassation (PT Natra Raya) to pay all court costs across all levels of jurisdiction (assessed at Rp 100,000 at the cassation level). \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Cassation Panel of Justices on Thursday, November 25, 1999, by H. Soeharto, SH. \[Presiding Judge\], Iskandar Kamil, SH., and Achmad Kowi AS., SH.).\*
