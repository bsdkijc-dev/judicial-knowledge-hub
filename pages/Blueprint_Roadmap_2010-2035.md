# Blueprint Roadmap 2010–2035

The Blueprint contains a roadmap for Indonesian judicial reform covering the period 2010–2035.

## Strategic Horizon

The roadmap establishes a long-term transformation framework.

The document describes a staged implementation process, with the final period intended to consolidate and evaluate the reform agenda.
