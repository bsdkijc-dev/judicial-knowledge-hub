## Description / Deskripsi

This page records the theoretical foundation, economic methodology guidelines, and proposed draft articles for the amendment of Supreme Court Regulation No. 3 of 2021 (PERMA No. 3/2021), presented by senior competition economist Ine Minara S. Ruky on 2 August 2026. Entitled \*“Judicial Review of Economic Evidence dari Sisi Ekonomi”\*, the expert submission establishes that under Indonesia's Integrated Administrative Model—where the Commission for the Supervision of Business Competition (KPPU) combines investigative, prosecutorial, and adjudicative powers—judicial review of economic reasoning is not an impermissible expansion of court authority, but the essential institutional counterpart required under the Rule of Law. The study formulates a 3-tiered Minimum Standard of Economic Judicial Review (MSEJR), establishes a 5-point minimum test for relevant market definitions, sets strict quality thresholds for indirect evidence (\*indirect evidence\*) in cartel cases, and drafts concrete normative text for PERMA No. 3/2021. All analytical frameworks, international comparisons, and proposed regulatory articles have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher **Cecep Mustafa**.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-061](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-061): Misapplication of Evidence Lock to Evolving Market Data, where Article 13(5) of PERMA No. 3/2021 bars new documentary evidence, preventing judges from considering updated market data (e.g., 2023 market entrance data disproving a 2021 KPPU market dominance finding) or counter economic analyses prepared to challenge novel penalty formulas introduced in KPPU's final decision.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-062](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-062): Unchecked Reliance on Low-Quality Indirect Evidence & Expert Scope Restriction, where cartel adjudications frequently rely on circumstantial evidence (such as parallel pricing) without testing whether price alignment stems from external market factors, while commercial courts routinely restrict economic experts to abstract theoretical statements.

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-049](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-049): Commercial Court judges lack explicit methodological guidelines for testing KPPU's economic inferences, treating economic reports as unexaminable administrative findings, evaluating indirect evidence on a binary basis ("admissible vs. inadmissible") rather than testing inference strength, and barring economic experts from applying methodologies directly to case facts.

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-049](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-049): International benchmark analysis across the United States (strict \*Daubert\* expert admissibility standards), the European Union (CJEU precedents requiring accurate, complete, and methodologically consistent data), Australia (Concurrent Expert Evidence / \*Hot Tubbing\*), and Japan (reasoned decision standards for JFTC methodology) demonstrates advanced judicial economic scrutiny.

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-049](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-049): The primary structural gap is the absence of explicit normative provisions in PERMA No. 3/2021 defining the Minimum Standard of Economic Judicial Review (MSEJR) and establishing quality thresholds for indirect evidence in cartel cases.

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Establish a 3-tiered Minimum Standard of Economic Judicial Review (MSEJR) model, codify a mandatory 5-point relevant market minimum test, and incorporate five specific draft articles into PERMA No. 3/2021 covering economic analysis review standards, relevant market testing, reciprocal evidence lock relaxation, indirect evidence quality criteria, and expanded expert witness scope.

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-052](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-052): Integrate the five economic adjudication draft articles into the ongoing PERMA No. 3/2021 amendment process under Supreme Court Working Group oversight.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-053](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-053): Develop joint BSDK MA RI and academic training programs to build judicial capacity in industrial organization and econometric evaluation.

## Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-049](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-049): Commercial Court pilot implementation handling complex cartel and market power objection proceedings applying MSEJR standards.

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-049](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-049): 100% compliance with Level 1 Minimum Standard of Economic Judicial Review (MSEJR) in all competition judgments; mandatory application of the 5-point relevant market test prior to evaluating market power or antitrust violations.

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Incorporating economic proof as an integral component of competition law procedure, shifting Indonesian enforcement from a formalistic form-based approach to an effect-based analysis, and strengthening institutional capacity building via BSDK MA RI.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Codifying objective economic review standards and indirect evidence quality thresholds into binding Supreme Court regulations.

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Expert presentation document (\*Presentasi Ine Ruky -2 Agustue 2026.pdf\*) submitted by senior competition economist Ine Minara S. Ruky to the Supreme Court Working Group on 2 August 2026.
