# National Court Data Lakes

# National Court Data Lake

The National Court Data Lake is proposed as a common analytical infrastructure for judicial data.

## Potential Uses

- performance analysis;
- workload analysis;
- research;
- early warning;
- policy analysis;
- institutional intelligence.

## Governance

Use must remain subject to purpose limitation, access control, security and applicable legal authority.
