# JPW-REPORT-011

**Fortieth Report of the Prosecutor of the International Criminal Court to the United Nations Security Council pursuant to Resolution 1593 (2005)** is the official periodic evaluation document submitted by the Office of the Prosecutor (OTP) of the International Criminal Court on 27 January 2025.

## Summary

This report outlines key developments in the execution of the investigative strategy in Darfur for the reporting period spanning August 2024 to January 2025. Key achievements include the conclusion of the trial proceedings for Ali Muhammad Ali Abd-Al-Rahman ("Ali Kushayb"), the compilation of over 6,000 items of evidence regarding crimes committed since April 2023, and preparations for filing new arrest warrant applications.

## Legal Basis

- [United Nations Security Council Resolution 1593 (2005)](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_1593_%282005%29) under Chapter VII of the UN Charter.
- [Rome Statute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rome_Statute): Article 42(6) (Self-Recusal of the Prosecutor), Article 53 (Initiation of Investigation), and Article 58 (Issuance of Arrest Warrants).

## Background

Since the UN Security Council referral in 2005, the OTP has prosecuted international crimes committed in Darfur. This fortieth report responds to a renewed wave of violence erupting in April 2023 between the Sudanese Armed Forces (SAF) and the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) across West and North Darfur.

## Objectives

- Submit multiple new applications for arrest warrants concerning 2023 and ongoing crimes.
- Broaden complementarity cooperation with domestic authorities and the UN Independent International Fact-Finding Mission for the Sudan (FFM-Sudan).
- Secure the arrest and surrender of main fugitives (Omar Al Bashir, Abdel Raheem Hussein, and Ahmad Harun).

## Key Provisions

- \*\*Investigation of 2023–Ongoing Crimes\*\*: Investigations focus on killings, looting, attacks on internally displaced person (IDP) camps, and gender-based crimes in Al Geneina and El Fasher.
- \*\*Trial of Ali Abd-Al-Rahman\*\*: Trial proceedings concluded in December 2024 across 31 counts of war crimes and crimes against humanity.
- \*\*Main Fugitive Cases\*\*: Evaluation of Ahmad Harun's whereabouts and the Government of Sudan's obligations under Resolution 1593.
- \*\*Abdallah Banda Case\*\*: Proceedings overseen by Deputy Prosecutor Nazhat Shameem Khan following the Prosecutor's recusal.

## Implementation

The OTP conducted investigative missions to Chad (Adre refugee camp), gathered 6,000 digital evidence items and testimonies, utilized the OTPLink platform, and collaborated closely with the UK War Crimes Team.

## Policy Issues

- Failure of the RSF to execute requests for assistance.
- Budgetary constraints and an urgent need for Arabic-speaking seconded experts.
- Field security challenges and witness protection measures.
