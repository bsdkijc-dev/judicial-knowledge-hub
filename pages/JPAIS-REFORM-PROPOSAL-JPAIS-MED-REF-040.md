## Description / Deskripsi

This page records the detailed Problem Inventory List (Daftar Inventaris Masalah / DIM) matrix analysis synthesized from the official working document *"Kompilasi Data Statistik Perkara dan Daftar Inventaris Masalah Penyelesaian Perkara Keberatan Terhadap Putusan Komisi Pengawas Persaingan Usaha di Pengadilan Niaga"* (prepared for the inaugural Supreme Court Working Group Kick-Off Meeting on 3–4 August 2026 in Jakarta). Focusing on the empirical submissions from the Central Jakarta Commercial Court (Table 19) and the Semarang Commercial Court (Table 20), this page evaluates critical technical-juridical bottlenecks in PERMA No. 3/2021, including the prohibition against new witnesses/experts (\*evidence lock\*), the e-Court registration lockout in multi-petitioner cases (e.g., the Cooking Oil cartel trial), the total absence of a judicial appeal mechanism for MSME partnership (\*kemitraan\*) decisions, and the lack of confidentiality protections (\*confidentiality rings\*) for trade secrets during public court proceedings. All problem matrices, legal bases, and proposed solutions have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher **Cecep Mustafa**.

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-042](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-042): Infringement of Audi Alteram Partem via Information Asymmetry, where Article 13(3) of PERMA No. 3/2021 does not grant petitioners full access to KPPU investigation files (\*BAP penyelidikan\*) or witness identities, preventing effective defense preparation.
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-043](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-043): Absence of Trade Secret Protections (\*Confidentiality Rings\*), where PERMA No. 3/2021 lacks mechanisms to protect sensitive corporate financial data and trade secrets disclosed during public Commercial Court hearings, exposing business actors to commercial harm.

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-040](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-040): Judges in Commercial Courts face contradictory procedures when handling new witness requests, with some panels permitting new experts and others strictly enforcing the evidence lock under Article 13(5) PERMA No. 3/2021, while SIPP/e-Court systems reject secondary filings in multi-party cartel cases, and partnership (\*kemitraan\*) dispute appeals are routinely dismissed for lack of jurisdiction (\*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard\*).

## Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-040](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-040): In Japan (Tokyo High Court / JFTC), Japan Fair Trade Commission orders are evaluated under administrative litigation rules, permitting full review of relevant market definitions and indirect economic evidence via independent expert panels (\*Concurrent Expert Evidence\*), while the EU General Court implements formal \*\*Data Room Procedures\*\* and \*\*Confidentiality Rings\*\*.

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-040](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-040): The primary structural gap is the rigid, dossier-based administrative review model under PERMA No. 3/2021, which lacks modern procedural tools such as e-Court multi-party consolidation, remote video expert testimony, confidentiality rings, and partnership appeal pathways.

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Harmonize deadline calculations strictly to working days (\*hari kerja\*), relax the evidence lock (Article 13(5)) to permit new documentary and expert evidence whenever KPPU introduces novel market definitions or fine formulas in its final ruling, mandate e-Court Master Case Consolidation features for multi-petitioner filings, and establish explicit Commercial Court jurisdiction for MSME Partnership (\*kemitraan\*) appeals.

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-040](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-040): Issue a Supreme Court Circular (SEMA) harmonizing working days, waiving bank guarantees for non-monetary remedies, and authorizing remote witness teleconferencing under PERMA No. 6/2022.
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-041](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-041): Promulgate the PERMA Amandemen 3/2021 incorporating ICLA and Pokja DIM recommendations.

## Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-040](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-040): Central Jakarta Commercial Court pilot implementation handling complex multi-party and high-tech cartel objections.

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-040](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-040): 100% elimination of e-Court registration lockouts for co-respondents; zero procedural dismissals of partnership (\*kemitraan\*) appeals based on jurisdictional ambiguity; adjudication completed strictly within \$\le 60\$ working days per statutory standard.

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Amending Law No. 5/1999 and Law No. 20/2008 to grant explicit statutory rights of judicial appeal for all KPPU administrative decisions, including partnership enforcement and merger notification delays, alongside establishing formal inter-agency data-sharing protocols between Mahkamah Agung, KPPU, and Ministry of Finance (DJKN) for PNBP fine execution.

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Restructuring administrative review procedures into an equitable adversarial framework that balances KPPU's enforcement authority with robust constitutional due process safeguards.
