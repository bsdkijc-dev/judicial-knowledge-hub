# Renstra Mahkamah Agung 2025–2029

The **Strategic Plan of the Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia** 2025–2029 **(Renstra 2025–2029) is the medium-term institutional** planning document for the Supreme Court.

It establishes the strategic direction, objectives, strategic targets, performance indicators, programs, activities, risk management, and funding framework for the 2025–2029 planning period.

## Strategic Position

The Renstra 2025–2029 is aligned with:

- national development priorities;
- RPJMN 2025–2029;
- the Judicial Reform Blueprint 2010–2035;
- lessons from the evaluation of Renstra 2020–2024.

[Vision](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Vision) → [Mission](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Mission) → [Strategic Objectives](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Strategic_Objectives) → [Strategic Targets](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Strategic_Targets) → [Performance Indicators](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Performance_Indicators) → [Programs](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Programs) → [Funding](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Funding) → [Monitoring and Evaluation](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Monitoring_and_Evaluation)

## Relationship with the 2010–2035 Blueprint

The Renstra represents a medium-term implementation layer within the longer-term judicial reform trajectory.
