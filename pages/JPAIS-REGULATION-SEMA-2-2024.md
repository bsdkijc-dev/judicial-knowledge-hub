# JPAIS:REGULATION:SEMA-2-2024

## Summary / Ringkasan

### Bahasa Indonesia

Surat Edaran Mahkamah Agung (SEMA) Nomor 2 Tahun 2024 menetapkan hasil rumusan Rapat Pleno Kamar Mahkamah Agung yang diselenggarakan pada tanggal 5 s.d. 7 November 2024 di Bandung sebagai pedoman resmi pelaksanaan tugas bagi seluruh pengadilan tingkat pertama, banding, dan Mahkamah Agung. Penerapan sistem kamar ini ditujukan untuk menjaga kesatuan penafsiran hukum (*unity of law*) dan konsistensi putusan peradilan di seluruh Indonesia.

SEMA ini mengintegrasikan seluruh rumusan rapat pleno kamar periode tahun 2012 sampai dengan 2024 sebagai satu kesatuan yang utuh dan tidak terpisahkan. Rumusan mencakup kaidah hukum teknis yudisial pada Kamar Pidana, Kamar Perdata, Kamar Agama, Kamar Militer, dan Kamar Tata Usaha Negara, serta kebijakan operasional Kamar Kesekretariatan. Secara tegas ditentukan bahwa rumusan pleno kamar tahun 2012–2023 yang bertentangan atau direvisi dinyatakan tidak berlaku lagi.

### English

Supreme Court Circular Letter (SEMA) Number 2 of 2024 enacts the legal and institutional formulations produced at the Supreme Court Chamber Plenary Meeting held from 5 to 7 November 2024 in Bandung as mandatory guidelines for first-instance courts, appellate courts, and the Supreme Court. The overarching purpose of the chamber system plenary is to ensure the uniform interpretation of law and judicial consistency across Indonesia.

This circular integrates all chamber plenary formulations from 2012 through 2024 into a single cohesive body of jurisprudence. It establishes substantive and procedural directives across the Criminal, Civil, Religious, Military, and State Administrative Chambers, as well as institutional development policies under the Secretarial Chamber. Preceding plenary formulations (2012–2023) that conflict with or are expressly modified by this circular are superseded.

## Definition / Definisi

### Bahasa Indonesia

Berdasarkan konteks yuridis SEMA Nomor 2 Tahun 2024:

Rapat Pleno Kamar  
Forum permusyawaratan tertinggi para Hakim Agung dalam kamar teknis dan kesekretariatan di Mahkamah Agung untuk membahas permasalahan hukum, menyamakan persepsi, dan memecahkan disparitas putusan.

Sistem Kamar (Chamber System)  
Tata kelola peradilan pada Mahkamah Agung yang mengelompokkan Hakim Agung ke dalam kamar spesialisasi (Pidana, Perdata, Agama, Militer, dan Tata Usaha Negara) guna mewujudkan profesionalitas, kedalaman keilmuan, dan konsistensi yurisprudensi.

Kaidah Hukum Kamar  
Standar interpretasi hukum dan preseden yang mengikat bagi hakim di semua tingkatan peradilan dalam memutus sengketa dan perkara konkret.

Kamar Kesekretariatan  
Unsur pendukung non-teknis yudisial yang merumuskan regulasi, manajemen sumber daya manusia, keuangan, sarana prasarana, dan kebijakan organisasi peradilan.

### English

Within the judicial framework of SEMA Number 2 of 2024:

Chamber Plenary Meeting (Rapat Pleno Kamar)  
The consultative forum of Supreme Court Justices convened within technical and secretarial chambers to resolve legal ambiguities, unify interpretations, and eliminate disparities in adjudications.

Chamber System (Sistem Kamar)  
The organizational framework dividing Supreme Court Justices into specialized chambers (Criminal, Civil, Religious, Military, and State Administrative) to enhance specialization, judicial competence, and doctrinal coherence.

Chamber Legal Formulation (Kaidah Hukum Kamar)  
Binding legal standards and interpretive doctrines designed to guide judges across all court levels in resolving concrete cases.

Secretarial Chamber (Kamar Kesekretariatan)  
Non-adjudicative administrative arm responsible for institutional governance, human resources, financial management, digital infrastructure, and regulatory drafting.

## Legal Basis / Landasan Hukum

### Bahasa Indonesia

- Undang-Undang Nomor 14 Tahun 1985 tentang Mahkamah Agung sebagaimana telah beberapa kali diubah terakhir dengan Undang-Undang Nomor 3 Tahun 2009.
- Undang-Undang Nomor 48 Tahun 2009 tentang Kekuasaan Kehakiman.
- Keputusan Ketua Mahkamah Agung Republik Indonesia tentang Penerapan Sistem Kamar pada Mahkamah Agung Republik Indonesia.
- Surat Edaran Mahkamah Agung Hasil Rapat Pleno Kamar Mahkamah Agung Tahun 2012 sampai dengan Tahun 2023 *(sebagai satu kesatuan pedoman yang saling melengkapi)*.

### English

- Law Number 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court, as amended by Law Number 5 of 2004 and Law Number 3 of 2009.
- Law Number 48 of 2009 on Judicial Power.
- Decree of the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia on the Implementation of the Chamber System.
- SEMA Chamber Plenary Formulations from 2012 to 2023 *(forming an integrated body of continuous judicial directives)*.

<td><ul>
<li><strong>Electoral Offences</strong>: First-instance acquittal or dismissal judgements may only be appealed to the High Court as final judgments (no cassation or judicial review/PK). Any improper appeal must be formally rejected by court decree and withheld from the Supreme Court.</li>
<li><strong>Asset Confiscation in Joinder Corruption and Money Laundering</strong>: Seized assets must prioritize state financial restitution (compensation payments). Only remaining assets exceeding state losses may be forfeited under money laundering sanctions.</li>
<li><strong>State Financial Losses (Modifies SEMA 4/2016)</strong>: The Supreme Audit Agency (BPK) holds constitutional authority to assess state losses; however, audits by BPKP, Inspectorates, and certified Public Accountants remain admissible. Trial judges retain full authority to determine the existence and quantum of financial loss based on evidentiary facts.</li>
<li><strong>Juvenile Re-examination (PK)</strong>: Judicial review requested by juvenile convicts who have turned 21 must follow adult open-court proceedings, except in morality cases.</li>
</ul></td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td><p><strong>Civil Chamber</strong></p></td>
<td><ul>
<li><strong>Ex-Officio Judicial Grounds (Art. 178 HIR / 189 RBg)</strong>: Judges may formulate supplementary legal reasoning based on verified data available in the Case Tracking Information System (SIPP).</li>
<li><strong>BLBI Debt Collection</strong>: PUPN auctions must use Government Appraisers; eviction orders for auctioned assets are lodged directly with the district court chief without a fresh lawsuit; bona fide buyers must be non-affiliated; seized assets remain outside bankruptcy estates; unpaid debts survive bankruptcy termination (Gov. Reg. 28/2022).</li>
<li><strong>Personal Guarantees (Borgtocht)</strong>: A guarantor is obligated strictly to the designated creditor; guarantor assets are excluded from the debtor's bankruptcy estate unless proved otherwise.</li>
<li><strong>Industrial Disputes</strong>: Maritime crew disputes are resolved primarily under Sea Work Agreements (PKL), the Commercial Code (KUHD), and Shipping Acts; forum venue lies at the execution port or last employment station.</li>
</ul></td>

<tr class="odd">
<td><p><strong>Religious Chamber</strong></p></td>
<td><ul>
<li><strong>Marriage Annulment (Modifies SEMA 2/2019)</strong>: Annulment claims filed after dissolution by death are inadmissible (NO), unless bad faith is substantiated.</li>
<li><strong>Overseas Marriage Endorsement (Modifies SEMA 3/2015)</strong>: Citizens whose foreign marriages were denied local registration may file for judicial marriage legalization (<em>isbat nikah</em>) in their domestic domicile court.</li>
<li><strong>Islamic Economics</strong>: Third-party tort liabilities under murabahah wakalah contracts cannot be shifted to Islamic financial institutions.</li>
<li><strong>Jinayat &amp; Child Custody</strong>: Non-concurrent offenses with a single victim trigger the maximum severe penalty (<em>thariqah al-jabb</em>). Hadanah custody claims must be lodged in the court where the child factually resides (best interest of the child).</li>
</ul></td>
</tr>

<td><p><strong>Military Chamber</strong></p></td>
<td><ul>
<li><strong>Restorative Justice (PERMA 1/2024)</strong>: Applicable to military desertion (absence ≤ 60 days, first offenders, unblemished discipline record, commander approval; penalized with community service) and military domestic violence (reconciliation confirmed).</li>
<li><strong>Military Directives</strong>: Breaches of military service telegrams do not constitute offenses under Art. 103(1) Military Penal Code (unless specified under SEMA 10/2020).</li>
<li><strong>Child Witnesses</strong>: Trial officers must wear child-friendly civilian attire rather than official PDU IV military uniforms.</li>
<li><strong>PK Cassation Inadmissibility</strong>: A petition for judicial review (PK) against a military cassation ruling declared inadmissible due to expiration/lateness is construed as a PK petition against the final and binding appellate court decision.</li>

<li><strong>Minerba One Data Indonesia (MODI) Disputes (Modifies SEMA 1/2017)</strong>: Official silence on MODI registration constitutes an administrative refusal under Art. 3 of Law 5/1986, rather than a factual omission.</li>
