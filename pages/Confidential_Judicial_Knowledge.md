# Confidential Judicial Knowledge

Not all judicial knowledge should be publicly published.

## Classification

- Public
- Internal
- Restricted
- Confidential
- Highly Sensitive

## Rule

Confidential or sensitive material must remain in repositories with appropriate access controls.

The public Wiki should contain only material authorized for publication.
