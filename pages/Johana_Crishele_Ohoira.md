# Johana Crishele Ohoira

## Contributor Profile

**Johana Crishele Ohoira** is a core research contributor at the Judicial Knowledge Hub, specializing in judicial case analytics, comparative legal research, and institutional documentation structuring for the BSDK Mahkamah Agung RI corpus.

### Portal Navigation

- [Main Page](Main_Page.md) \> [Johana Crishele Ohoira](Johana_Crishele_Ohoira.md)

### Research Scope & Contributions

- \*\*Judicial Case Analytics\*\*: Structuring and analyzing judicial case records, alternative dispute resolution mechanisms, and institutional documentation frameworks.
- \*\*Repository Architecture\*\*: Indexing research reports, comparative case studies, and digital knowledge assets within the JPAIS architecture.
