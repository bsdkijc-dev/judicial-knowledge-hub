# National Court Data Lake

# National Court Data Lake

The National Court Data Lake is proposed as governed analytical infrastructure for judicial data.

## Core Requirements

- data provenance;
- access controls;
- data governance;
- interoperability;
- quality controls;
- security.

## Strategic Function

The Data Lake provides an analytical foundation for evidence-based policy and judicial performance intelligence.
