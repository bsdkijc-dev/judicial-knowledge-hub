# JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:JPAIS-MED-REF-013

## Summary / Ringkasan

## Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

- Entity ID: JPAIS-MED-REF-013
- Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
- Ingestion Status: Confirmed

## Description / Deskripsi

Provides comprehensive administrative guidelines and regulations for international participants attending JICA's Knowledge Co-Creation Program (KCCP) in Japan, detailing legal status, code of conduct, financial allowances, medical services, pre-departure checklists, and terminal navigation maps for major Japanese airports\[cite: 13\].

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-001): International participants navigating foreign legal systems, strict regulatory standards, and complex transportation hubs face potential compliance risks, health vulnerabilities, and administrative friction\[cite: 13\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-001): Official nomination and acceptance procedures through diplomatic/official channels connecting nominating governments with Japanese Embassies, JICA overseas offices, and domestic program partners\[cite: 13\].

## Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-001): Standardized administration of international training programs under JICA's domestic and international operational guidelines, enforcing strict participant obligations and comprehensive support structures\[cite: 13\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-001): Potential misunderstandings regarding strict Japanese laws concerning drug possession, copyright infringement, sexual harassment, and financial allowances management\[cite: 13\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Strict adherence to JICA KCCP operational guidelines, participant rules and regulations, health requirements, and standardized allowance management protocols\[cite: 13\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-001): Complete pre-departure checklists (Checklists 1, 2, 3, 4), secure appropriate visas (Trainee/Scholar status), and prepare required reports and medical records\[cite: 13\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-002): Observe JICA Center regulations, including return times, stay-out applications, fire prevention policies, and environmental guidelines\[cite: 13\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-003): Utilize medical insurance cards, adhere to emergency/non-emergency care protocols, and complete arrival health examinations for stays exceeding six months\[cite: 13\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-013](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-013): Standardized nationwide implementation across all JICA domestic training centers and partner institutions\[cite: 13\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-013](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-013): Zero regulatory infractions; successful completion of course curricula; maintenance of good health status throughout the program duration\[cite: 13\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Reinforce institutional capacity building, human resource development, and international friendship through reciprocal knowledge co-creation between Japan and partner countries\[cite: 13\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Strict compliance with Japanese laws, ordinances, JICA Center rules, and copyright regulations during the training period\[cite: 13\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Over the past 60 years, more than 349,000 participants from 198 countries have participated in JICA's Knowledge Co-Creation Program, with approximately 10,000 annual participants across diverse development sectors\[cite: 13\].
