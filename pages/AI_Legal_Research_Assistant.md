# AI Legal Research Assistant

The AI Legal Research Assistant is proposed as a source-grounded research-support capability.

## Core Functions

- retrieve approved legal sources;
- assist legal research;
- provide citation traceability;
- identify relevant legal materials;
- explicitly communicate uncertainty.

## Safeguard

The system supports research but does not replace legal judgement.

## Implementation Requirements

- defined problem;
- accountable owner;
- baseline;
- risk tier;
- measurable outcome;
- controlled pilot;
- data-quality assessment;
- legal-authority assessment;
- cybersecurity;
- user training;
- interoperability;
- retirement criterion.
