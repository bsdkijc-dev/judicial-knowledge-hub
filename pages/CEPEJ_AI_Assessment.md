# CEPEJ AI Assessment

CEPEJ's AI work demonstrates how ethical principles can be translated into operational assessment questions.

## Assessment Questions

- What data are used?
- Can discrimination occur?
- Can users understand the system's role?
- Is meaningful human oversight present?
- Can the system be audited?
- Can the institution respond to failure?

## Indonesian Adaptation

These questions can form part of an Indonesian Algorithmic Impact Assessment.
