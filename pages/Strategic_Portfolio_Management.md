# Strategic Portfolio Management

The 50 initiatives should be managed as a portfolio rather than as 50 independent projects.

## Required Portfolio Information

- owner;
- outcome;
- dependencies;
- risk;
- budget;
- indicators;
- phase;
- scale/stop decision.
