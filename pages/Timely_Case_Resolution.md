# Timely Case Resolution

**Percentage of cases resolved within the prescribed time limit** is Indicator 1.1 under Strategic Target 1.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Cases resolved on time}} {\text{Total cases resolved}}\times100\\ \</math\>

## Strategic Function

The indicator measures the efficiency of case handling against the prescribed time limit.
