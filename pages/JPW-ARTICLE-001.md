# JPW-ARTICLE-001

**Legal Opinion Article: ‘Als een land in nood verkeert wordt het internationaal recht even in de ijskast gezet’** is an academic interview and legal opinion publication featuring Prof. Dr. Mr. Göran Sluiter. This publication analyzes the application of international law, humanitarian law, and war crimes within the context of the Israel-Gaza conflict \[\].

## Summary

This article addresses the phenomenon where international law standards are set aside or compromised when a state claims to be in an emergency situation \[\]. The text asserts that self-defense actions must always remain strictly subject to universal legal rules without compromise \[\].

## Legal Basis

**Not found in the available sources.** (This article is an academic legal opinion publication rather than a formal public regulation or statute) \[\].

## Background

The armed conflict between Israel and Hamas following the events of October 7, 2023, sparked extensive public debate \[\]. Prof. Göran Sluiter highlighted the prevalence of flawed legal interpretations by politicians and military experts that disregard basic principles of civilian protection \[\].

## Objectives

The primary objective of this publication is to provide objective legal education, rectify public misconceptions, and prevent the descent into a slippery slope (\*hellend vlak\*) that compromises the rules of war \[\].

## Implementation

This publication was disseminated through the official news and research portal of the University of Amsterdam as an academic reference for scholars, media, and the general public \[\].

## Policy Issues

The existence of political pressure to freeze international law for reasons of self-defense, the use of human shield arguments to justify attacks on civilians, and potential violations of the obligation to prevent genocide under the Genocide Convention \[\].
