# Legal and Regulatory Mapping

# Legal and Regulatory Mapping

Future judicial technology should be mapped against applicable legal authority and regulatory requirements.

## Mapping Questions

1.  What legal authority permits the activity?
2.  What restrictions apply?
3.  What rights may be affected?
4.  What institutional responsibilities arise?
5.  What regulations require amendment?
6.  What new standards may be necessary?

## Principle

**Technology deployment must follow legal authority.**
