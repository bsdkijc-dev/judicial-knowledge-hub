# JPW-POLICY-QAT-AI-001

**Regional Training Workshop on Global Tools for Artificial Intelligence and the Rule of Law** is a regional capacity-building initiative organized by the [Center for Legal and Judicial Studies (CLJS)](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Center_for_Legal_and_Judicial_Studies) under the [Ministry of Justice of Qatar](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Justice_of_Qatar), in collaboration with the [Qatar National Commission for Education, Culture, and Science](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Qatar_National_Commission_for_Education%2C_Culture%2C_and_Science) and the [UNESCO Office in Doha](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/UNESCO_Office_in_Doha) [Passage 1](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_1). The initiative aims to leverage artificial intelligence (AI) technologies to uphold the rule of law, expedite judicial proceedings, and facilitate the transition toward *e-justice* across the Arab region [Passage 1](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_1).

## Executive Summary

This regional training workshop convened on December 9, 2024, in Doha, Qatar [Passage 1](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_1). It was attended by ministers, judges, and prosecutors representing 10 Arab nations [Passage 4](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_4). The program is designed to equip judicial practitioners with technical and ethical competencies to utilize global AI tools within judicial workflows [Passage 1, Passage 5](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_1%2C_Passage_5).

## Legal Basis

The execution of AI strategies within Qatar's judicial sector is grounded on:

- **Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2021**, which established the [Artificial Intelligence Committee](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Qatar_Artificial_Intelligence_Committee) to oversee and execute the national AI strategy [Passage 2](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_2).
- **Directives of Amir HH Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani**, emphasizing continuous judicial development to ensure judicial independence and legal modernization [Passage 2](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_2).

## Background

Rapid technological advances under the Fourth Industrial Revolution require judicial frameworks to modernize to keep pace with societal progress [Passage 5](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_5). Judicial institutions are called upon to adapt legal frameworks to support effective and accessible justice (\*effective justice\*) [Passage 5](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_5).

## Objectives

1.  Build capacity among judges, prosecutors, and legal experts across the Arab region in adopting AI tools [Passage 1](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_1).
2.  Address and mitigate case backlog challenges across judicial bodies [Passage 3, Passage 5](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_3%2C_Passage_5).
3.  Expedite judicial procedures and accelerate institutional transitions toward *e-justice* [Passage 1, Passage 5](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_1%2C_Passage_5).
4.  Strengthen regional cooperation among Arab states in applying AI technologies within the legal sector [Passage 1, Passage 4](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_1%2C_Passage_4).

## Key Provisions

- **AI Oversight Committee**: Establishing the Artificial Intelligence Committee pursuant to Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2021 to guide national policy [Passage 2](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_2).
- **AI as an Ancillary Tool**: Positing AI technology as an operational support tool (\*ancillary tool\*) to assist human decision-makers rather than replace judicial discretion [Passage 3, Passage 5](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_3%2C_Passage_5).
- **Legal Framework Adaptation**: Modernizing regulatory provisions to align with contemporary technological developments without hindering innovation [Passage 5, Passage 6](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_5%2C_Passage_6).

## Implementation

- **Public Prosecution of Qatar**: Direct operational integration of AI tools to accelerate case processing and improve legal accuracy [Passage 3](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_3).
- **Regional Participation**: Participation of judicial representatives from 10 Arab nations: Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Algeria, Egypt, Iraq, and Yemen [Passage 4](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_4).
- **UNESCO Global Partnership**: Drawing on UNESCO's global initiative that has engaged over 36,000 judicial stakeholders across 160 nations [Passage 4](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_4).

## Policy & Compliance Issues

- **Legal Personality of AI**: Ongoing academic and legislative debates on whether AI systems or autonomous agents should be granted legal personality (\*legal personality of robots\*) [Passage 6](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_6).
- **Liability for AI Damages**: Ambiguities in establishing legal responsibility and liability for damages caused by automated or AI-assisted outcomes [Passage 6](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_6).
- **Case Backlogs**: Resolving procedural delays while maintaining strict adherence to human rights and due process standards [Passage 3, Passage 5](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Passage_3%2C_Passage_5).
