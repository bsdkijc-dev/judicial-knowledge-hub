# Algorithmic Impact Assessment

Every high-impact judicial AI system should undergo an assessment before deployment and periodically thereafter.

## Assessment Questions

1.  What is the purpose?
2.  Who is affected?
3.  What data is used?
4.  What risks exist?
5.  What error modes exist?
6.  What bias risks exist?
7.  What cybersecurity risks exist?
8.  What human oversight applies?
9.  What fallback mechanism exists?
10. What indicators will be monitored?

## Lifecycle

**Assessment → Deployment → Monitoring → Reassessment**
