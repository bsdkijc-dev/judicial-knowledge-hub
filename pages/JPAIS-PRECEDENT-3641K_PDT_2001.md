# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:3641K PDT 2001

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 3641 K/Pdt/2001** (dated September 11, 2002) is a fundamental civil law and contract law jurisprudence precedent establishing vital rules concerning the limitation of the principle of freedom of contract (*pacta sunt servanda*), the doctrine of abuse of circumstances (*misbruik van omstandigheden*), and the legal consequences of executing contract deeds and debt recognitions inside police detention cells.

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established two primary legal principles:

1.  **Freedom of Contract Is Not Absolute:** "Within the principle of freedom of contract, a Judge possesses the authority to examine and declare that the positions of the parties are unbalanced, such that one party is deemed unfree in expressing their will."
2.  **Application of Standards of Propriety and Justice:** "In open-ended contracts, legal values living within society aligned with propriety, justice, and humanitarianism may be applied as a means to modify terms agreed upon in a contract."

Where notary deeds (Deed Nos. 41, 42, and 31) containing a fictitious debt recognition amounting to Rp 215,837,852,000 and personal asset guarantees were presented by creditors/notary to a debtor (\[Party A\]) while he was detained in the Polda Metro Jaya detention cell, the Supreme Court qualified the transaction as arising from an abuse of circumstances (*misbruik van omstandigheden* / *abuse of circumstances*). The debtor lacked physical and psychological freedom to express his will, rendering the agreement null and void / subject to cancellation *ex* Article 1320 *jo.* Article 1338 of the Civil Code.

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioner for Cassation (\[Party A\]), annulled Jakarta High Court Decision No. 393/PDT/2000/PT.DKI dated November 21, 2000 (which had overturned South Jakarta District Court Decision No. 442/Pdt.G/1999/PN.Jak.Sel. dated May 11, 2000), and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by partially granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit in Convention, declaring that the Defendants committed an Unlawful Act (Tort), declaring Notary Deeds Nos. 41, 42, and 31 along with their derivative transfer deeds null and void, ordering the return of the Plaintiff's land and building assets, and holding Defendants I and IV jointly and severally liable for material and moral damages, while rejecting the Reconvention counterclaim entirely.

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Limitation of Freedom of Contract (*Pacta Sunt Servanda* *ex* Article 1338 Civil Code) via the Doctrine of Abuse of Circumstances (*Misbruik van Omstandigheden*)

*Can a contract deed or debt recognition signed by a debtor/suspect inside a police detention cell under psychological pressure and promises of detention suspension from a creditor qualify as a valid, binding agreement*ex*Article 1338 of the Civil Code?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Invalid / Null and Void / Subject to Cancellation.** Freedom of contract is not absolute. Judges possess the authority to examine unbalanced bargaining power (*undue influence* / *unconscionability*). Executing deeds inside a police detention cell is qualified as a transaction arising from abuse of circumstances (*misbruik van omstandigheden* / *abuse of circumstances*). The debtor lacks free will (physical and psychological freedom impaired), rendering the consent defective (*defect of consent*) and requiring the cancellation of the contract deeds *ex* Article 1320 *jo.* Article 1338 of the Civil Code.

### 2. Qualification of Unlawful Acts (Torts / PMH) for Fabricating Police Reports to Compel Contract Execution (Article 1365 Civil Code)

Utilizing police/investigative institutions unlawfully (fabricating banking/corruption criminal reports to detain a debtor) as an instrument of coercion or psychological pressure (*duress* / *coercion*) to force a debtor to execute debt recognition deeds and transfer personal assets qualifies as an Unlawful Act (Tort) *ex* Article 1365 of the Civil Code.

### 3. Protection of Third Parties and Cancellation of Derivative Deeds (Chain of Title Nullity)

Where primary debt recognition and encumbrance deeds (Deeds Nos. 41, 42, and 31) are declared null and void due to defective consent, all derivative/successor deeds—such as Sale and Purchase Deed No. 1363, Sale and Purchase Binding Deed No. 163, and Rights Transfer Deed No. 36—likewise become null and void, possessing no binding legal force, requiring defendants to return all assets belonging to the Plaintiff.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* Jakarta High Court (Decision No. 393/PDT/2000/PT.DKI) err in applying contract law (*foutieve toepassing van het verbintenisrecht* *ex* Article 1338 Civil Code) by validating Notary Deeds Nos. 41, 42, and 31, whereas such deeds were executed by the Petitioner for Cassation (\[Party A\]) inside the Polda Metro Jaya detention cell under psychological pressure and abuse of circumstances?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. Jakarta High Court misapplied the law. Freedom of contract is not absolute, and signing deeds in a detention cell constitutes abuse of circumstances (*misbruik van omstandigheden*). The Supreme Court granted \[Party A\]'s cassation petition, annulled Jakarta High Court's decision, and independently adjudicated by declaring the notary deeds null and void and ordering Defendants to return all assets to the Plaintiff.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / South Jakarta District Court & Jakarta High Court)

Verification of facts regarding \[Party A\]'s detention in the Polda Metro Jaya Detention Center from May to December 1997 upon complaints filed by PT Bank Arta Prima / PT Bank Artha Graha; verification of Notary \[Notary\], S.H.'s entry into the detention cell to present Deeds Nos. 41 and 42 (Oct 29, 1997) and Deed No. 31 (Nov 26, 1997); verification of debt recognition amounting to Rp 215,837,852,000 and surrender of giro checks of Rp 20B and Rp 15B; verification of collateral assets comprising Permata Hijau plot Block A-5, 6, 7 measuring 4,500 m<sup>2</sup> and Four Seasons Park Apartment in Singapore; and verification of detention suspension applications (P-16, P-17) filed by Defendant I with the Prosecutor's Office/Police following deed execution.

## Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

### Statutory Regulations

- **Civil Code (KUHPerdata / BW):** Article 1320 (Validity Requirements of Contracts / Freedom of Will), Article 1338 (Principle of Freedom of Contract / *Pacta Sunt Servanda*), & Article 1365 (Unlawful Acts / Tort).
- **Open Contract Doctrine:** Application of Principles of Propriety, Justice, and Humanitarianism in Indonesian Contract Law.
- **Law No. 14 of 1970 *jo.* Law No. 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court:** Cassation and Independent Adjudication Authority.

### Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- **Doctrine of Abuse of Circumstances (*Misbruik van Omstandigheden*):** Defective consent (*wilsgebrek*) is not limited to coercion (*dwang*), error (*dwaling*), or fraud (*bedrog*), but encompasses abuse of circumstances and unbalanced bargaining positions (*undue influence* / *abuse of circumstances*).
