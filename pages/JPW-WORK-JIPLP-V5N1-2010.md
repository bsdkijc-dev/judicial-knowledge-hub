# JPW-WORK-JIPLP-V5N1-2010

The **Journal of Intellectual Property Law & Practice (Vol. 5, No. 1)** is a monthly peer-reviewed intellectual property law journal issue published by [Oxford University Press](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) in January 2010 [JIPLP Publication](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JIPLP_Publication).

## Overview

This publication contains scholarly articles and practical reviews covering full-spectrum intellectual property (IP) law, including patents, trade marks, copyright, competition law, and privacy [JIPLP Overview](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JIPLP_Overview). JIPLP is distributed globally via print editions and digital *Advance Access* channels [Journal Access](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Journal_Access).

## Legal Basis

- **Formal Legal Basis**: Not found in the available sources [Legal Basis](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Legal_Basis).
- **Publishing Policy**: Subject to ethical and environmental publishing standards established by [Oxford Journals](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Oxford_Journals) [Ethical Policy](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Ethical_Policy).

## Background

Rapid developments in international IP law necessitate periodic academic forums that deliver rigorous legal analysis for legal practitioners, scholars, and policymakers [IP Background](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/IP_Background).

## Key Objectives

1.  Present timely and relevant intellectual property law research to the global legal community [Research Goal](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Research_Goal).
2.  Facilitate cross-jurisdictional commentary on patent, trade mark, and copyright disputes [Legal Dialogue](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Legal_Dialogue).
3.  Enhance research accessibility through integrated print and digital publishing systems [Access Goal](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Access_Goal).

## Core Provisions

- **Publication Frequency**: Published monthly (12 issues per year for Volume 5 in 2010) [Publication Frequency](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Publication_Frequency).
- **Alerting Services**: Offers *E-Table of Contents* and *Advance Access alerter* services for subscribers [Alerting Services](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Alerting_Services).
- **Identification Standards**: Standardized under ISSN 1747-1532 and Digital Object Identifiers (DOI) [DOI Standard](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/DOI_Standard).

## Implementation & Administration

- **Editorial Leadership**: Led by Jeremy Phillips as Editor and Phillip Johnson as Reviews Editor [Editorial Team](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Editorial_Team).
- **Distribution**: Distributed in the USA by Mercury International and worldwide via the Oxford University Press network [Global Distribution](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Global_Distribution).

## Policy Considerations

- **E-Commerce Platform Liability**: Analysis of liability thresholds for online marketplaces (such as eBay) regarding counterfeit goods [E-Commerce Liability](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/E-Commerce_Liability).
- **Patents & Competition Law**: Examination of patent settlement agreements under European Commission competition regulations [Competition Law](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Competition_Law).
