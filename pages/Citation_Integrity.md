# Citation Integrity

Citation Integrity ensures that Wiki claims can be traced to the sources from which they were derived.

## Requirements

- precise source identification;
- stable reference;
- quotation accuracy;
- context preservation;
- version awareness;
- distinction between source and interpretation.

## AI Requirement

AI systems should provide source links or citations rather than presenting unsupported legal propositions.
