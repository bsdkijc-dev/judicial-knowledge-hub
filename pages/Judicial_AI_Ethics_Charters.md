# Judicial AI Ethics Charter

The Judicial AI Ethics Charter establishes principles governing the entire lifecycle of judicial AI.

## Core Principles

- legality;
- purpose limitation;
- fundamental rights;
- non-discrimination;
- information security;
- data minimisation;
- traceability;
- appropriate explainability;
- human oversight;
- contestability;
- auditability;
- procurement accountability.

## Scope

The Charter should apply from design and procurement through deployment, monitoring and retirement.
