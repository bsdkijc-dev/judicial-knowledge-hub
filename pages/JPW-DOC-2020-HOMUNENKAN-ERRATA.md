## Policy Analysis

- **Background & Policy Problem**:
  - **FACT**: The Ministry of Justice published this official Errata Sheet (正誤表) to correct statistical data and administrative errors within the 2020 MOJ Annual Report (\*法務年鑑 令和２年\*).
  - **FACT**: Personal Information Protection (p. 38) and Information Disclosure (p. 40) statistical processing periods were corrected from FY2020 (令和２年度) to FY2019 (令和元年度).
  - **FACT**: Corrected FY2019 Information Disclosure requests received: 867 cases at MOJ HQ (本省) and 95,235 cases at Delegated Agencies (委任機関).
  - **FACT**: Corrected FY2019 Disclosure decisions rendered: MOJ HQ rendered 808 decisions (305 full disclosure, 417 partial disclosure, 86 denials); Delegated Agencies rendered 94,501 decisions (1,491 full disclosure, 92,184 partial disclosure, 826 denials).
  - **FACT**: Timeframe extensions under Article 10(2) occurred in 291 cases (HQ) and 761 cases (Delegated Agencies); special extensions under Article 11 occurred in 32 cases (HQ) and 318 cases (Delegated Agencies).
  - **FACT**: MOJ HQ recorded 108 administrative appeals (審査請求), 0 appeal withdrawals, 73 determinations (裁決・決定), and 1 lawsuit (訴訟).
- **Statutory Definitions & Exclusions**:
  - **FACT**: Delegated Agencies (委任機関) operate under delegated authority from the Minister of Justice pursuant to Article 17 of the Information Disclosure Act (Act No. 42 of 1999).
  - **FACT**: Statistical tables for Delegated Agencies explicitly exclude the Immigration Services Agency (出入国在留管理庁), Public Security Examination Commission (公安審査委員会), Public Security Intelligence Agency (公安調査庁), and Public Prosecutors Offices (検察庁).
- **Correctional Facility Address Corrections**:
  - **FACT**: Gifu Detention Branch (岐阜拘置支所): Corrected to 1769-19 Sagiyama, Gifu City.
  - **FACT**: Shimabara Detention Branch (島原拘置支所): Corrected to 1-1204 Jounai, Shimabara City.
  - **FACT**: Oita Prison (大分刑務所): Corrected to 5-4-1 Hatanaka, Oita City.
  - **FACT**: Kokura Detention Branch (小倉拘置支所): Corrected to 1-1-8 Hayamachi, Kokuraminami-ku, Kitakyushu City.
  - **FACT**: Higashi-Nihon Juvenile Medical and Educational Center (東日本少年矯正医療・教育センター): Corrected to 2-1-3 Mokuseinomori, Akishima City.
- **Interpretations & Structural Inferences**:
  - **INTERPRETATION**: Issuing this official errata demonstrates the Ministry of Justice's commitment to public accountability, factual integrity in judicial publications, and transparency in administrative delegatory frameworks.
  - **INFERENCE**: The overwhelming concentration of disclosure requests at Delegated Agencies (95,235 vs. 867 at HQ) illustrates that regional delegation under Article 17 of the Information Disclosure Act serves as the main conduit for public information access.
