# Singapore Benchmark Registry

# Singapore Judiciary Benchmark

Singapore is identified as a priority jurisdiction for comparative research.

## Required Research

- digital court architecture;
- judicial technology;
- AI use;
- court-user services;
- workforce capability;
- governance;
- cybersecurity;
- data architecture;
- measurable outcomes.

## Status

The current Blueprint identifies Singapore as a benchmark jurisdiction but does not provide sufficient detailed evidence in the available draft to make specific institutional claims.
