# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:3114K PDT 1991

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 3114 K/Pdt/1991** (dated November 28, 1992 / deliberation on November 12, 1992) is a fundamental civil law, evidentiary law, and Central Javanese customary inheritance law jurisprudence precedent establishing three core legal principles:

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established three primary legal principles:

1.  **Violation of the Principle of *Unus Testis Nullus Testis* (Single Witness Rule):** An appellate court errs in applying the law of evidence when validating the transfer or conveyance of disputed land from a deceased owner to his son-in-law (Defendant II) based solely on the testimony of a single witness without corroborating documentary evidence or other valid supporting proof. A single witness does not constitute legal witness testimony (*unus testis nullus testis* *ex* Articles 171/174 HIR).
2.  **No Prescription (*Daluwarsa*) in Customary Inheritance Claims:** The right to claim a share of an estate under customary law (*hukum adat*) recognizes no statutory time limits and is not subject to prescription (*daluwarsa*). The High Court erred in dismissing an inheritance lawsuit merely because the land had been occupied by the defendants for 33 years.
3.  **Determination of Joint Marital Property (*Gono-Gini*) from Land Right Conversions (UUPA):** Communal/customary land (*tanah sanggan/norowito*) converted into Ownership Rights (*Hak Milik*) pursuant to Article 7 of the Conversion Provisions of Law No. 5 of 1960 (*UUPA*) *jo.* Minister of Agrarian Affairs Regulation No. 2 of 1960 in the year 1960 (during the husband's second marriage) constitutes joint marital property (*gono-gini*) of the second marriage. Consequently, the ½ share belonging to the late father (\[Party G\]) devolves as an inheritance estate to his biological children (the Plaintiffs), while the remaining ½ share constitutes the absolute right of the second wife (\[Party A\]), which is inherited entirely by her pre-marital child (*anak gawan* / Defendant I).

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioners for Cassation (\[Plaintiff I\] & \[Plaintiff II\]), annulled Semarang High Court Decision No. 249/Pdt/1991/PT.Smg dated June 24, 1991, *jo.* Pati District Court Decision No. 97/Pdt.G/1990/PN.Pt dated January 17, 1991, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by partially granting the Plaintiffs' lawsuit, declaring ½ of disputed land Certificate C 1308 as \[Party G\]'s inheritance share devolving to the Plaintiffs, and ordering Defendants I and II to surrender ½ of the disputed land to the Plaintiffs.

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Evidentiary Formalities of Single Witness Testimony (*Unus Testis Nullus Testis* *ex* Articles 171 / 174 HIR)

*Is an appellate court judgment valid under civil procedural law when it validates the transfer of disputed land solely upon the testimony of one witness without documentary corroboration or corresponding proof?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Invalid / Evidentiary Defect.** The High Court violated the principle of *unus testis nullus testis* (a single witness is not a witness *ex* Articles 171/174 HIR). Standalone single-witness testimony unsupported by other valid evidence fails to satisfy the minimum evidentiary threshold required to validate real property title transfers.

### 2. Prescription (*Daluwarsa*) in Central Javanese Customary Inheritance Claims

*Can an inheritance partition lawsuit under customary law be dismissed or barred by prescription (*daluwarsa*) when the disputed land has been occupied by a defendant for 33 years?*

**Supreme Court Rule: No Prescription.** The right to claim an inheritance partition under customary law recognizes no statutory time limits or prescription periods (*daluwarsa*). As long as a deceased parents' estate has not been legally partitioned among rightful heirs, the right of biological heirs to claim their inheritance shares remains valid regardless of how long the property has been occupied by others.

### 3. Legal Status of *Norowito/Sanggan* Land Converted Under the Basic Agrarian Law (Article 7 Conversion Provisions of UUPA *jo.* Minister of Agrarian Affairs Regulation No. 2 of 1960)

*Does former*norowito/sanggan*land converted into Ownership Rights (*Hak Milik*) in the husband's name in 1960 qualify as joint marital property (*gono-gini*) of a second marriage or estate property of a first marriage?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Second Marriage Joint Property (*Gono-Gini*).** Because the factual conversion of customary *norowito* land into Ownership Rights occurred in 1960 (during the husband's marriage to his second wife, \[Party A\]), the land by operation of law constitutes joint marital property (*gono-gini*) of the second marriage. Consequently, the husband's ½ share constitutes the inheritance estate for all his biological children (the Plaintiffs), while the second wife's remaining ½ share is inherited entirely by her own biological child (Defendant I).

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* Semarang High Court (Decision No. 249/Pdt/1991/PT.Smg) err in applying evidentiary and customary inheritance law (*error in iudicando*) by ruling in favor of Defendant II based on a single witness and holding that the Plaintiffs' inheritance claim was time-barred (*daluwarsa*)?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. Semarang High Court misapplied the law. The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of \[Plaintiff I\] and \[Plaintiff II\], annulled the Semarang High Court *jo.* Pati District Court decisions, and independently adjudicated by holding that ½ of disputed land Certificate C 1308 constitutes \[Party G\]'s estate which must be surrendered to the Plaintiffs as biological children.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Pati District Court & Semarang High Court)

Examination of \[Party G\] \[Plaintiffs' parent\]'s marital genealogy with \[Party D\] (1st wife, divorced 1944) and \[Party A\] (2nd wife, married 1945/1960); verification of the status of stepchild \[Party E\] (Defendant I, \[Party A\]'s child) and her husband \[Party B\] (Defendant II); verification of property location and boundaries for Certificate C 1308 covering ± 1 *bahu* in Karangwetan Village, Pucakwangi, Pati; verification of \[Party G\]'s death date (1965) versus Village Book C No. 1404 transfer recording date (April 26, 1966); and testimonies of trial witnesses (\[Party C\], Senen, Komari, \[Party F\]).
