# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:55K PIDSUS 2014

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 55 K/Pid.Sus/2014** (dated November 20, 2013) is a landmark special criminal law jurisprudence precedent established under the Supreme Court Jurisprudence Compilation. It adjudicates the corruption case involving the acceptance of bribes/gratuities in budgetary allocations for project development and procurement at the Ministry of National Education and the Ministry of Youth and Sports by the Defendant, \[Defendant\].

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established three primary legal principles:

1.  **Qualification of Public Budget Steering by Members of the House of Representatives (Article 12 letter a Anti-Corruption Law):** A Member of the House of Representatives (*DPR RI*) / Member of the Budget Board (*Badan Anggaran*) who utilizes their position, office, and authority to accept gifts or promises in the form of monetary rewards (a fee of 5% of the project value) from private entities (Permai Group) to steer and smooth out budgetary allocations for projects at the Ministry of National Education (*Kemendiknas*) and the Ministry of Youth and Sports (*Kemenpora*) is legally and convincingly proven guilty of Continuing Corruption *ex* Article 12 letter a *jo.* Article 18 of Law No. 31 of 1999 *jo.* Law No. 20 of 2001 *jo.* Article 64 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code.
2.  **Imposition of Additional Replacement Money Penalties on Bribe Proceeds (Article 18 Anti-Corruption Law):** The *Judex Facti* of the High Court erred in applying the law by refusing to impose an additional replacement money penalty (*uang pengganti*). Based on the philosophy and provisions of Article 18 paragraph (1) letter b of the Anti-Corruption Law, all assets/money acquired from corruption (including bribe money/fees amounting to Rp 12,580,000,000 and US\$ 2,350,000 received by the Defendant) must be confiscated for the State as an additional replacement money penalty to recover state financial and economic losses.
3.  **Validity of Electronic Evidence (Articles 5 & 6 ITE Law *jo.* Article 26A Anti-Corruption Law):** Electronic Information and/or Electronic Documents in the form of Blackberry Messenger (BBM) text logs, mobile phone conversation logs, and computer file extractions fulfilling formal and material requirements constitute valid evidence and an extension of binding indicative evidence (*alat bukti petunjuk*) in corruption court proceedings.

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of Petitioner for Cassation I (Public Prosecutor at the Corruption Eradication Commission / KPK) and rejected the cassation petition of Petitioner for Cassation II / Defendant (\[Defendant\]). The Supreme Court annulled the Corruption Court at DKI Jakarta High Court Decision No. 11/PID/TPK/2013/PT.DKI dated May 22, 2013, *jo.* Central Jakarta District Court Decision No. 54/Pid.B/TPK/2012/PN.JKT.PST dated January 10, 2013, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by sentencing the Defendant to 12 (twelve) years' imprisonment, a fine of Rp 500,000,000 subsidiary 8 (eight) months' confinement, and ordering the Defendant to pay replacement money amounting to Rp 12,580,000,000 and US\$ 2,350,000 subsidiary 5 (five) years' imprisonment.

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Qualification of Bribery / Fee Reward Corruption in Public Budget Steering by Legislative Officials (Article 12 letter a Anti-Corruption Law *jo.* Article 64 Criminal Code)

*Does the conduct of a DPR RI / Budget Board member requesting and receiving a 5% fee reward from project values to smooth the budgetary allocations of Kemendiknas and Kemenpora fulfill the elements of Article 12 letter a of the Anti-Corruption Law?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Fulfills Corruption Elements of Article 12a.** Legislative officials assigned to working committees and budget boards who accept monetary rewards from private entrepreneurs/contractors to direct state expenditure allocations (DIPA) are legally proven to commit passive corruption (receiving bribes/gifts) contrary to their official duties.

### 2. Enforceability of Additional Replacement Money Penalties over All Bribe/Gratuity Proceeds (Article 18 paragraph (1) letter b Anti-Corruption Law)

*Can additional replacement money penalties be imposed solely on corruption crimes directly damaging state finances (Articles 2 & 3), or must they also be imposed on bribery/fee acceptance corruption (Article 12)?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Mandatory for All Bribe Proceeds.** Pursuant to the philosophy of Article 18 of the Anti-Corruption Law, additional replacement money penalties aim to confiscate all wealth, money, or advantages unlawfully acquired by perpetrators from corruption. Bribe money/fees amounting to Rp 12,580,000,000 and US\$ 2,350,000 flowing from state budget project funds must be confiscated by the State. Failure to pay within 1 month after permanent legal force results in asset seizure and auction, or substitution with 5 years' imprisonment.

### 3. Standards of Validity and Evidentiary Force of Electronic BBM / Digital Communication Evidence (Articles 5 & 6 ITE Law *jo.* Article 26A Anti-Corruption Law)

Instant messaging conversation recordings (Blackberry Messenger / BBM), telephone call logs, and computer data files lawfully seized and extracted by KPK investigators constitute an extension of indicative evidence and valid electronic documentary evidence possessing perfect evidentiary force in Corruption Courts.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (*Judex Juris* / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* DKI Jakarta High Court (Decision No. 11/PID/TPK/2013/PT.DKI) err in applying the law (*foutieve toepassing van het recht*) by omitting additional replacement money penalties *ex* Article 18 of the Anti-Corruption Law and applying Article 11 instead of Article 12 letter a of the Anti-Corruption Law?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. The *Judex Facti* misapplied evidentiary and material criminal law. The Supreme Court granted KPK's cassation petition, annulled the appellate and district rulings, and independently adjudicated by sentencing the Defendant to 12 years' imprisonment, a Rp 500 million fine, and imposing replacement money of Rp 12,580,000,000 and US\$ 2,350,000 subsidiary 5 years' imprisonment.

### Question of Fact (*Judex Facti* / Central Jakarta District Court, DKI Jakarta High Court, & Supreme Court)

Verification of facts concerning incremental fund delivery across 16 transactions by Permai Group (totaling Rp 12,580,000,000 and US\$ 2,350,000) through couriers Jefri, Lutfie Adriansyah, Bayu Wijokongko, and Harsono; verification of BBM communications between PIN 20E342D9 (\[Defendant\]) and PIN 21CAD148 (Mindo Rosalina Manulang) utilizing code terms such as "Washington Apple", "Malang Apple", "Watermelon", and "Kilo"; and verification of budget allocation discussion meetings at Foodism Restaurant FX Senayan, Sultan Hotel, Batik Spa, and DPR RI Building.

## Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

### Statutory Regulations

- **Law No. 31 of 1999 *jo.* Law No. 20 of 2001 on Eradication of Corruption Crimes:** Article 12 letter a (Corruption in Receiving Gifts/Promises), Article 18 (Additional Replacement Money Penalties), & Article 26A (Electronic Indicative Evidence).
- **Law No. 11 of 2008 on Information and Electronic Transactions (ITE):** Articles 5 & 6 (Validity of Electronic Evidence).
- **Criminal Code (KUHP):** Article 64 paragraph (1) (Continuing Act / *Voortgezette Handeling*).
- **Criminal Procedure Code (KUHAP):** Articles 182 paragraph (6) & 253 (Cassation Review).

### Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- **Supreme Court Decision No. 109 PK/Pid.Sus/2009 (Nov 2, 2009 — H. Bulyan Royan Case):** Landmark decision establishing that bribe money/fee rewards received by state administrators from state budget project contractors must be confiscated for the state through additional replacement money penalties.
- **Doctrine of Asset Recovery & Deterrence Effect in Political Corruption:** Imposing maximum prison sentences (12 years) alongside asset recovery via confiscation of bribe proceeds ensures a deterrent effect and prevents systemic corruption in state budget allocations within the DPR RI.

## What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

### Established by Evidence

- The Defendant (\[Defendant\]) is **PROVEN LEGALLY AND CONVINCINGLY GUILTY OF CONTINUING CORRUPTION** *ex* Article 12 letter a *jo.* Article 18 of the Anti-Corruption Law *jo.* Article 64 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code.
- Total bribe/fee money received by the Defendant from Permai Group is **PROVEN TO AMOUNT TO Rp 12,580,000,000 AND US\$ 2,350,000**.
- The Defendant is sentenced to 12 (twelve) years' imprisonment and a fine of Rp 500,000,000 subsidiary 8 (eight) months' confinement.
- The Defendant is ordered to pay an additional replacement money penalty of Rp 12,580,000,000 and US\$ 2,350,000 subsidiary 5 (five) years' imprisonment.
- Electronic BBM message evidence (PIN 20E342D9) is **PROVEN VALID AND POSSESSES EVIDENTIARY FORCE AS INDICATIVE EVIDENCE**.
- DKI Jakarta High Court Decision No. 11/PID/TPK/2013/PT.DKI and Central Jakarta District Court Decision No. 54/Pid.B/TPK/2012/PN.JKT.PST are **NULL AND VOID**.

### Dismissed Contentions (Defendant's Defense)

- The Defendant's defense asserting non-ownership of BBM pins, non-receipt of funds, or lack of budgetary authority was **REJECTED ENTIRELY BY THE SUPREME COURT** as conflicting with witness testimony, electronic evidence, and state budget DIPA documents.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Strict Sanctions for Legislative Budgetary Function Abuse in Indonesian Criminal Law:** This landmark ruling delivers an uncompromising law enforcement message in Indonesia: DPR RI/Budget Board members cannot hide behind collective institutional authority to extract bribe fees from public project budget allocations. The Supreme Court affirms that all proceeds of public sector corruption will be seized down to their roots via replacement money mechanisms (*asset recovery*).

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Can a Member of the DPR RI who receives fees for steering state budget allocations be subjected to additional replacement money penalties for all received bribe monies pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence?  
**Can Be and Must Be Subjected to Replacement Money.** Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence Reg. No. 55 K/Pid.Sus/2014:

- A DPR RI / Budget Board member receiving fee rewards (such as 5% of project values) to steer public budget allocations is proven to commit corruption *ex* Article 12 letter a of the Anti-Corruption Law.
- Pursuant to Article 18 paragraph (1) letter b of the Anti-Corruption Law, all assets/money acquired from corruption (including bribes/fees) **MUST BE CONFISCATED FOR THE STATE** through additional replacement money penalties to recover state economic losses.
- The Supreme Court annulled appellate rulings and independently adjudicated by sentencing the Defendant to 12 years' imprisonment, a Rp 500 million fine, and replacement money of Rp 12.58 billion and US\$ 2.35 million subsidiary 5 years' imprisonment.
