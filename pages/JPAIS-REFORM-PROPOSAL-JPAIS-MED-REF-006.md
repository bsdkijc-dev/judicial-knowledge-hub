# JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:JPAIS-MED-REF-006

## Summary / Ringkasan

## Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

- Entity ID: JPAIS-MED-REF-006
- Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
- Ingestion Status: Confirmed

## Description / Deskripsi

Evaluates judicial training architectures through a comparative study between Indonesia and Japan, identifying systemic gaps in Indonesia's segmented professional training pipeline and proposing the Indonesian Competency-Based Judicial Training Model (J-CBTM) modeled after Japan's LTRI and Yoken Jijitsu doctrine\[cite: 6\].

## Problem / Masalah

- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-001): Indonesian judicial training lacks a comprehensive outcome-based competency matrix and suffers from irregular recruitment cycles, curriculum compressions (65-week PPC Terpadu), and immediate solo judge appointments\[cite: 6\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-002): Judgments display structural inconsistencies due to the absence of a standardized drafting taxonomy, while clinical apprenticeships lack pedagogical rigor and protected mentorship allocations\[cite: 6\].
- [JPAIS-MED-PROB-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PROB-003): Assessment systems produce near 100% graduation rates without a rigorous practical gateway exam, and judicial education lacks binding continuing education tiers and AI governance frameworks\[cite: 6\].

## Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

- [JPAIS-MED-ID-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ID-001): Judicial training is administered autonomously by BSDK through Pusdiklat Teknis Peradilan, separating candidate judge education (PPC Terpadu) from prosecutors and private advocates\[cite: 6\].

## Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

- [JPAIS-MED-JP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-JP-001): Japan administers unified legal training for judges, prosecutors, and attorneys through the Legal Training and Research Institute (LTRI / Shihō Kenshūjo) featuring a 12-month apprenticeship, Yoken Jijitsu methodology, and the high-stakes Nikai Shiken exit exam\[cite: 6\].

## Gap / Kesenjangan

- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-001): Structural divergence between Japan's cohesive professional culture and Indonesia's segmented training model, coupled with the absence of a post-induction transitional collegiate bench requirement (Hanjiho equivalent)\[cite: 6\].
- [JPAIS-MED-GAP-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-GAP-002): Lack of a standardized analytical drafting taxonomy, blinded national qualifying exit examinations, and ethical boundaries for generative AI deployment in judicial operations\[cite: 6\].

## Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Establish the Indonesian Competency-Based Judicial Training Model (J-CBTM) featuring four progressive educational gates, a three-year transitional collegiate rank (Hakim Pertama Pratama), the DFKUM structured drafting doctrine, the UNKY gateway exam, and AI governance rules\[cite: 6\].

## Action / Aksi

- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-001](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-001): Promulgate Supreme Court Regulations establishing the Judicial Competency Framework, structured drafting benchbooks, and the UNKY qualification exam\[cite: 6\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-002](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-002): Deploy the J-CBTM pilot across designated jurisdictions, implementing protected mentorship allocations (SMYB) and unscripted trial simulations\[cite: 6\].
- [JPAIS-MED-ACT-003](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-ACT-003): Audit pilot training outcomes, evaluate UNKY inter-rater reliability, and submit permanent structural reform recommendations to the Supreme Court leadership\[cite: 6\].

## Pilot / Pilot Proyek

- [JPAIS-MED-PILOT-006](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-PILOT-006): 90-day pilot program across trial simulation labs in Megamendung, pilot commercial courts in Jakarta and Surabaya, and appellate prep academies\[cite: 6\].

## KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

- [JPAIS-MED-KPI-006](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/JPAIS-MED-KPI-006): Evidentiary consensus \$\ge 90\\\$; new judge appellate reversal rate \$\< 5.0\\\$; judgment page length reduced by \$35\\\$; UNKY inter-rater reliability (Kendall's W) \$\ge 0.88\$; AI citation verification accuracy \$100\\\$\[cite: 6\].

## Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Promulgate joint ministerial and Supreme Court decrees recognizing inter-agency training modules and establishing ethical AI governance protocols for judicial operations\[cite: 6\].

## Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Amend SK KMA guidelines on candidate judge induction, judgment drafting standards, promotional career gates, and digital trial conduct\[cite: 6\].

## Evidence / Bukti Empiris

- Japan's unified LTRI apprenticeship and Yoken Jijitsu methodology ensure uniform analytical baselines and high adjudication quality across the legal profession\[cite: 6\].
- National baseline appellate reversal rates for newly appointed Indonesian judges stand at 18.4% under compressed induction pipelines\[cite: 6\].
