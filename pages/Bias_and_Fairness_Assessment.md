# Bias and Fairness Assessment

# Bias and Fairness Assessment

High-impact judicial AI should be assessed for potential discriminatory effects and unequal performance.

## Assessment

- affected groups;
- training/retrieval data;
- error distribution;
- differential performance;
- proxy variables;
- potential discriminatory effects;
- mitigation;
- monitoring.

## Principle

Fairness assessment must be contextualized to the actual judicial use case.
