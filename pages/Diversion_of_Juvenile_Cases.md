# Diversion of Juvenile Cases

# Juvenile Case Diversion

Indicator 1.10 measures the percentage of juvenile cases successfully resolved through diversion.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Juvenile cases successfully resolved through diversion}} {\text{Juvenile cases that completed the diversion process}} \times100\\ \</math\>

## Strategic Purpose

The indicator measures compliance with diversion procedures under applicable legislation.

## Data Quality Note

The Renstra's formula specifically uses juvenile cases that have completed the diversion process as the denominator.
