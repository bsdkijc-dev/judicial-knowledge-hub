## Policy Analysis

<Image src="image_agent_tag_4711888604885160504" alt="Ministry of Justice building in Tokyo, Japan" caption="Ministry of Justice, Japan" />

- **Background & Policy Problem**:
  - **FACT**: The White Paper on Crime 2020 provides a comprehensive summary and analysis of crime trends and the criminal justice system in Japan based on official statistical data through late July 2020 (primarily FY2019 data sourced from NPA, Public Prosecutors Offices, Supreme Court, Correction Bureau, and Rehabilitation Bureau).
  - **FACT**: Reported Penal Code offenses in 2019 dropped to 748,559 cases (532,565 excluding negligent driving), with 215,994 cleared persons and a clearance rate of 39.3%. Theft accounted for over 70% of all Penal Code offenses.
  - **FACT**: Special fraud (特殊詐欺) recorded 16,851 reported cases in 2019 with total financial losses reaching 19.65 billion yen, driven by "It's me" schemes, fictitious billing, loan deposit fraud, and cash card theft.
- **Major Legal & Empirical Findings**:
  - **Sexual Offenses Reform (Act No. 72 of 2017)**: Expanded rape definitions (including oral and anal penetration regardless of victim gender), raised minimum statutory terms, criminalized custodian sexual abuse, and converted offenses into non-complaint-based crimes.
  - **Stimulant Offender Survey (2017)**: Analyzed 699 newly admitted stimulant prisoners (8,730 total Stimulants Control Act offenders cleared in 2019). Findings showed a 74.1% reoffending rate, 57.3% unemployment, 7.2% Boryokudan affiliation, and high co-use history with cannabis (52.7%) and organic solvents (61.0%). Primary relapse triggers included contact with user peers (53.2% male, 60.6% female) and emotional distress/frustration (55.3% male, 47.4% female).
  - **Prosecution & Parole Systems**: Out of 900,752 suspects received by public prosecutors in 2019, 282.844 were prosecuted (81,186 formal trials, 201,658 summary procedures) and 576,677 were non-prosecuted (513,757 suspended prosecutions). The parole rate stood at 58.3% (11,640 paroled vs. 8,313 fully served). Japan maintained 46,763 Volunteer Probation Officers (Hogoshi, 35.0% aged 70+) and 103 Halfway Houses.
- **Interpretations & Structural Inferences**:
  - **INTERPRETATION**: The 2020 White Paper illustrates Japan's dual policy approach: intensifying penal sanctions for sexual violence while shifting toward empirical, rehabilitative interventions for drug dependencies and recidivism prevention.
  - **INFERENCE**: Although post-war crime rates hit historic lows in 2019, the high repeat offender rate among stimulant users (74.1%) combined with an aging volunteer probation network (35.0% over 70) underscores an urgent need to modernize community-based rehabilitation infrastructure.
