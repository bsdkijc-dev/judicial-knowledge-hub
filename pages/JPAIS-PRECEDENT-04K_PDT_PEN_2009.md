## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 04 K/Pdt.Pen/2009 establishes the constitutional legal principle that the Supreme Court and the General Judiciary lack the legal authority to evaluate, correct, or review decisions rendered by other judicial bodies such as the Constitutional Court (MK). Although the Supreme Court acknowledged the Petitioner's plight regarding disqualification from regional elections resembling a form of "civil death," the Supreme Court, in exercising its jurisdiction, cannot exceed the powers conferred upon it by statute.

## Case Background

The Petitioner (H. \[Party A\], SH., paired with H. Hartawan) won the South Bengkulu Regency regional election for the 2009–2014 term with 51.7% of the vote based on South Bengkulu KPUD Decree No. 59 dated December 10, 2008. The losing candidates (Reskan Effendi and Drh. Rohidin Mersyah, MMA.) filed a lawsuit at the Constitutional Court (Register No. 57/PHPU.D-VI/2008). In its decision dated January 8, 2009, the Constitutional Court nullified the regional election results by law (\*void ab initio\*) and ordered a revote excluding H. \[Party A\]'s ticket due to a 1985 criminal record. The Petitioner subsequently petitioned the Manna District Court to declare Constitutional Court Decisions No. 57/PHPU.D-VI/2008 and No. 4/PUU-VII/2009 null and void as if they never existed. The Manna District Court (Determination No. 02/Pdt.P/2009/PN.Mn dated May 11, 2009) declared the Petitioner's request inadmissible (\*niet ontvankelijke verklaard\*). Dissatisfied with this determination, the Petitioner filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The Supreme Court holds no authority to evaluate and review decisions of the Constitutional Court.
2.  Although the Supreme Court empathizes with the Petitioner's situation—where prohibition from participating in regional elections effectively results in civil death—the Supreme Court, while executing its functions as a General Judiciary institution, cannot correct or review decisions from other judicial bodies like the Constitutional Court because statutory law grants no such power.
3.  Regarding the enjoyment and loss of civic rights, the established legal maxim dictates that "No punishment shall result in Civil Death or the loss of all civil rights."

- **Ruling:** Rejecting the cassation petition filed by Petitioner H. \[Party A\], SH. (thereby upholding Manna District Court Determination No. 02/Pdt.P/2009/PN.Mn).
