# JPW-MANUAL-001

**Chambers Practice Manual of the International Criminal Court** (Eighth Edition, October 2024) is the official judicial policy document containing consolidated operational guidelines and procedural deadlines for ICC Judges \[1, 2–11\].

## Summary

This Manual establishes operational standards for pre-trial proceedings, trial preparation, judgment delivery deadlines, victim participation schemes, confidential evidence redaction protocols, as well as guidelines for judgment drafting and the registration of dissenting or separate opinions \[1, 12–136, 137–211\].

## Legal Basis

- [Rome Statute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rome_Statute): Articles 15, 58, 60, 61, 64, 67, 74, 76, 82, 83, 89, 91, 101.
- [Rules of Procedure and Evidence](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rules_of_Procedure_and_Evidence): Rules 68, 76, 77, 81, 85, 89, 118, 121, 122, 129, 130, 132, 141, 155.
- [Regulations of the Court](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Regulations_of_the_Court): Regulations 24, 34, 42, 53.

## Background

First published in September 2015, the Manual has undergone periodic updates, culminating in the Eighth Edition adopted on 21 October 2024 following the judicial retreat. It is designed to address procedural delays and ensure consistency in judicial practices across Chambers.

## Objectives

- Accelerate international criminal justice proceedings (expeditiousness).
- Establish strict deadlines for key decisions.
- Standardize judgment drafting and the management of confidential documents/witnesses.

## Key Provisions

- \*\*Pre-Trial Deadlines\*\*: Article 15 investigation authorization within a maximum of 120 days; confirmation of charges decision within 60 days; pre-confirmation responses within 5 days.
- \*\*Trial Deadlines\*\*: Article 74 written judgment within 10 months of the end of closing statements; Article 76 sentencing decision within 4 months of conviction.
- \*\*Appeals Deadlines\*\*: Appeals judgment on conviction/reparation within 10 months; Article 82 interlocutory appeals within 4 months \[77–79\].
- \*\*Victim Participation\*\*: A-B-C categorization scheme administered by the Registry \[83–88\].
- \*\*Evidence Redaction\*\*: Coding systems categories A.1–A.8 (Rule 81(2)) and B.1–B.5 (Rule 81(4)) \[89–100\].
- \*\*Judgment Drafting\*\*: Establishment of dedicated drafting sub-teams and the adoption of Structure Option 1 or 2 \[146, 194–201\].

## Implementation

Mandatorily applied by all Judges across the Pre-Trial, Trial, and Appeals Divisions in coordination with the Registry and the OTP \[12–211\].

## Policy Issues

- Restriction on deadline extensions, permitted only under exceptional circumstances.
- Risk management regarding the leakage of witness identities during investigations and inter-party contacts.
