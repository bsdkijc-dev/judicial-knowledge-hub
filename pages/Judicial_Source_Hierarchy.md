# Judicial Source Hierarchy

# Judicial Source Hierarchy

Judicial knowledge should distinguish sources according to authority and evidentiary value.

## Source Classes

1.  Constitution and legislation
2.  Regulations
3.  Official judicial regulations
4.  Official judgments
5.  Official institutional documents
6.  Official statistics
7.  Peer-reviewed research
8.  Professional commentary
9.  Secondary sources
10. Unverified online material

## Rule

The Wiki must never treat secondary commentary as equivalent to an authoritative legal source.
