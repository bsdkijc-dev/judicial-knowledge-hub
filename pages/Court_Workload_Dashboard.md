# Court Workload Dashboard

The Court Workload Dashboard is proposed as an institutional management and monitoring interface.

## Potential Information

- incoming cases;
- pending cases;
- resolved cases;
- workload;
- duration;
- emerging backlog;
- administrative capacity.

## Governance

Data must be appropriately governed and access controlled.
