# Judicial-policy-dutch-public-trust-bridges-2008

**The Bridges Built By Judges: Justice And Credibility Policy Framework** is a policy framework and socio-legal analysis examining the gap between the perceptions of the Dutch public and the Judiciary regarding justice and punitiveness. It outlines a strategy to balance two dimensions (cognitive and normative) to build the public credibility of the judiciary (*judicial credibility*) \[FACT\].

## Summary

Published by Prof. Gabriël van den Brink in the *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA) with the support of the Netherlands Council for the Judiciary (*Raad voor de rechtspraak*), this policy framework dissects the phenomenon of the gap between judges and society. Based on research data from the *Social and Cultural Planning Office* (SCP) and studies by Elffers, De Keijser, and Wagenaar, the majority of the public considers criminal sentences in the Netherlands to be too lenient. This framework maps this gap along two axes (cognitive and normative) and offers four dimensions of credibility for judges to build communicative "bridges" without sacrificing judicial independence.

## Legal Basis

- **Article 1 of the Dutch Constitution (Grondwet):** Guarantees the principle of equality and the prohibition of discrimination in legal services.
- **Article 1 of the Dutch Code of Criminal Procedure (Wetboek van Strafvordering):** Establishes the principle of legality while providing judicial discretion for judges to fill legal gaps.
- **Judiciary Organization Act 2002 (Wet op de rechterlijke organisatie):** The foundation for the reorganization of the judicial structure and the establishment of the Netherlands Council for the Judiciary.
- **Not found in the available sources:** Other specific formal statutory regulations are not mentioned in the text \[FACT\].

## Background

Although the Netherlands is classified as a *high-trust society* (61% of the public trusts the judiciary), public trust fluctuates due to media coverage of judicial scandals (such as the 2000 Schiedam Park murder and the Lucia de B. case). Judges face societal demands for harsher punishments, while professional legal reasoning remains bound by strict formal evidentiary criteria.

## Objectives

1\. Understand the sociological roots of the gap between public perception and judicial legal reasoning. 2. Analyze the differences between *legal truth* and *media logic*. 3. Map citizen profiles based on education levels and normative attitudes (*active, awaiting, anxious citizens*). 4. Formulate strategies to increase the public credibility of judges based on four leadership dimensions.

## Scope

This policy evaluates the Dutch criminal justice system, the interaction between judicial panels and society, the role of mass media, and a comparison of public trust levels across European countries.

## Key Provisions

- **The Punitiveness Gap:** Experiments prove that laypeople who do not receive full information impose criminal sentences 3 to 4 times harsher than professional judges.
- **Cognitive Axis:**
  - *Legal Truth Policy:* Based on rational arguments, formal evidence, and the state's monopoly on prosecution.
  - *Media Logic:* Based on framing, the personalization of scapegoats, dramatization, and emotion.
- **Normative Axis:**
  - *Active Citizens:* Highly educated, tolerant, and comprehend legal rationality (the profile of the majority of judges).
  - *Anxious Citizens:* Less educated, anxious, and demand life imprisonment or strong authoritative leaders.

## Implementation

- **Four Dimensions of Judicial Credibility (Kouzes & Posner Model):**

1.  *Interactive Dimension:* Judges explain the reasoning of their verdicts directly to the public/media.
2.  *Normative Dimension:* Open explanation of the moral standards and legal principles underlying the sentence.
3.  *Performative Dimension:* The use of narratives, stories, and rhetoric that can be understood by the general public.
4.  *Cognitive Dimension:* Understanding the lived realities of the lower class and affected minority groups.

## Policy Problems

- **Inaccessibility of Legal Language:** The use of overly technical legal reasoning language makes it difficult for the public and journalists to understand.
- **Limited Effectiveness of Harsher Sentences:** Mechanically increasing the severity of criminal sentences does not automatically increase the trust of less-educated citizens (*anxious citizens*).

## Reform / Change

- Strengthening *victims' rights* in trials to bridge citizens' experiences.
- Developing direct normative communication by judges outside the judicial "ivory tower."

## Multilingual Summaries (Swiss National Languages)

### Deutsch (Schweizer Hochdeutsch)

**Gerechtigkeit und Glaubwürdigkeit: Über von Richtern gebaute Brücken** Dieser von Prof. Gabriël van den Brink im *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA, 2008) veröffentlichte Rahmen analysiert die soziologisch-rechtliche Kluft zwischen der niederländischen Öffentlichkeit und der Justiz in Bezug auf Strafmass (Punitiveness Gap) und Gerechtigkeit. Die Studie zeigt, dass eine rein mechanische Verschärfung von Strafen das Vertrauen "ängstlicher Bürger" (anxious citizens) nicht stärkt. Stattdessen schlägt der Rahmen vier Dimensionen richterlicher Glaubwürdigkeit vor, um die Lücke zwischen professioneller "rechtlicher Wahrheit" und emotionaler "Medienlogik" zu schliessen, ohne die richterliche Unabhängigkeit zu gefährden.

- **Rechtsgrundlage:** Niederländische Verfassung (Grondwet Art. 1); Strafprozessordnung (Art. 1); Gesetz über die Gerichtsorganisation 2002.

### Français

**Justice et crédibilité : Des ponts construits par les juges** Publié par le Prof. Gabriël van den Brink dans l' *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA, 2008), ce cadre analyse le fossé socio-juridique entre le public néerlandais et la magistrature concernant la sévérité des peines (punitiveness gap) et la justice. L'étude montre que l'alourdissement mécanique des peines ne renforce pas la confiance des « citoyens anxieux ». Au lieu de cela, le cadre propose quatre dimensions de crédibilité judiciaire pour combler l'écart entre la « vérité juridique » professionnelle et la « logique médiatique » émotionnelle, sans compromettre l'indépendance de la justice.

- **Base Légale:** Constitution néerlandaise (Grondwet Art. 1) ; Code de procédure pénale (Art. 1) ; Loi sur l'organisation judiciaire de 2002.

### Italiano

**Giustizia e credibilità: Ponti costruiti dai giudici** Pubblicato dal Prof. Gabriël van den Brink nell' *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA, 2008), questo quadro analizza il divario socio-legale tra il pubblico olandese e la magistratura in merito alla severità delle pene (punitiveness gap) e alla giustizia. Lo studio dimostra che un inasprimento meccanico delle pene non rafforza la fiducia dei "cittadini ansiosi". Invece, il quadro propone quattro dimensioni di credibilità giudiziaria per colmare il divario tra la "verità legale" professionale e la "logica dei media" emotiva, senza compromettere l'indipendenza giudiziaria.

- **Base Legale:** Costituzione olandese (Grondwet Art. 1); Codice di procedura penale (Art. 1); Legge sull'organizzazione giudiziaria del 2002.

### Rumantsch

**Giustia e credibladad: Punts construidas da derschaders** Publitgà dal Prof. Gabriël van den Brink en l' *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA, 2008), analisescha quest cadro la largia socio-giuridica tranter il public ollandais ed ils derschaders pertutgant la severitad da las painas (punitiveness gap) e la giustia. Il studi mussa ch'in augment mecanic da las painas na rinforza betg la fidanza dals "burgais anxius". Empè da quai propona il cadro quatter dimensiuns da credibladad giudiziala per surmuntar la differenza tranter la "vardad giuridica" professiunala e la "logica da las medias" emoziunala, senza periclitar l'independenza giudiziala.

- **Basa Legala:** Constituziun ollandaisa (Grondwet Art. 1); Cudesch da procedura penala (Art. 1); Lescha davart l'organisaziun da las dretgiras 2002.

### 3 Most Important Improvements

1\. **Institutionalization of Direct Normative Communication by Trial Panels:** Educate judges to directly explain the moral values and legal principles underlying criminal sentences to the public and the community, rather than relying solely on press releases. 2. **Strengthening Victim Participation Rights in Criminal Trials:** Effectively adopt victim impact statement mechanisms so that the real experiences of citizens are integrated into the judicial domain. 3. **Simplification of Legal Reasoning Language in Judgments:** Restructure the writing of judgments to make them more accessible to court reporters, mass media, and lay citizens without diminishing legal precision.
