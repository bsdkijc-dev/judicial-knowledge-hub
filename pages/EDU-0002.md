# EDU-0002

# AI Competency Framework

The **AI Competency Framework** provides a conceptual structure for developing the knowledge, skills, judgment, and governance capacity required to use artificial intelligence responsibly in judicial environments.

This framework is a proposed knowledge model and does not represent an official competency standard unless separately validated and adopted.

**\[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## Competency Architecture

The framework may contain six dimensions:

| Dimension         | Core Capability                                 |
|-------------------|-------------------------------------------------|
| AI Literacy       | Understand AI concepts and limitations          |
| Prompt Literacy   | Communicate effectively with AI systems         |
| Verification      | Evaluate AI outputs against evidence            |
| Data Literacy     | Understand data quality, provenance, and risks  |
| Ethics and Rights | Identify rights and ethical implications        |
| Governance        | Apply institutional controls and accountability |

## Level 1 — Awareness

Learner can:

- Explain basic AI concepts
- Identify common AI use cases
- Recognize hallucination
- Recognize limitations

## Level 2 — Assisted Use

Learner can:

- Use AI for low-risk tasks
- Write structured prompts
- Summarize documents
- Generate research questions
- Check outputs against source material

## Level 3 — Professional Use

Learner can:

- Integrate AI into professional workflows
- Evaluate output quality
- Detect unsupported claims
- Manage citations
- Apply data and privacy principles

## Level 4 — Governance

Learner can:

- Assess AI risks
- Design governance controls
- Evaluate vendors
- Define human oversight
- Develop AI policies
- Conduct impact assessments

## Level 5 — Strategic Leadership

Learner can:

- Develop institutional AI strategy
- Design transformation roadmaps
- Align AI with judicial values
- Govern organizational adoption
- Evaluate institutional outcomes

## Verification Competency

A core principle is:

**AI OUTPUT → SOURCE → EVIDENCE → HUMAN JUDGMENT**

AI fluency without verification competency creates significant risk.

## Judicial Application

Potential audiences include:

- Judges
- Court staff
- Researchers
- Judicial educators
- Administrators
- Policy makers
- IT personnel

Different roles require different competency profiles.

## Assessment

Possible assessment methods include:

- Knowledge tests
- Prompt exercises
- Source verification
- Case simulations
- AI risk assessment
- Practical projects
- Portfolio assessment
