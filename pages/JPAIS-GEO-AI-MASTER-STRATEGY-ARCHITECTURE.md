## Description / Deskripsi

This master specification fuses the MediaWiki Vector 2022 custom institutional theme for the \*\*Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) / Mahkamah Agung Corporate University\*\* with the advanced \*\*GEO AI Metadata & Content Brief Architecture\*\*\[cite: 32\]. It establishes how judicial knowledge bases must be structured, written, and optimized not merely for traditional Google search rankings, but specifically for generative engine citations (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and AI Overview)\[cite: 32\].

## III. Vector 2022 UI Skin & BSDK Corporate University Theme Styling

To maintain visual harmony with the Mahkamah Agung Corporate University identity (incorporating the BSDK pillar and book logo), the wiki enforces the official color hierarchy\[cite: 32\]:

- <span style="color:#C62828; font-weight:bold;">Red Pillar (#C62828):</span> Applied to critical headings, judicial red badges, execution alerts, and primary structural accents.
- <span style="color:#1565C0; font-weight:bold;">Blue Pillar (#1565C0):</span> Applied to primary institutional UI elements, navigation headers, links, and the core navy background base (\`#123B5D\`).
- <span style="color:#2E7D32; font-weight:bold;">Green Pillar (#2E7D32):</span> Applied to verification badges, success states, confirmed ingestion markers, and institutional green highlights.
- <span style="color:#F57F17; font-weight:bold;">Gold Crown (#F57F17 / \#C98A16):</span> Applied to knowledge badges, metadata labels, featured research markers, and academic distinction highlights.
