# Judicial AI Academy

# Judicial AI Academy

The proposed Judicial AI Academy provides role-based AI learning rather than a single generic AI course.

## Role-Based Pathways

- Judges — source verification and ethical reasoning
- Administrators — workflow and data literacy
- Engineers — model evaluation and security
- Leaders — governance and procurement

## Learning Model

Training should use:

- realistic judicial scenarios;
- sandbox environments;
- peer review;
- continuously refreshed content.

The Academy should also generate Indonesian judicial AI case studies.
