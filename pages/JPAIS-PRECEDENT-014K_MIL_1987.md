## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 14 K/Mil/1987 dated September 30, 1987, establishes an essential legal rule regarding the prerequisites for imposing the death penalty. The Supreme Court outlined that for an individual sentenced to death, no mitigating factors may exist, and all judicial considerations must be strictly aggravating. If a Judge's considerations cite mitigating factors (such as the Defendant's past service and contributions to the State), the imposition of the death penalty is legally improper (\*onvoldoende gemotiveerd\*) and must be commuted to life imprisonment.

## Case Background

The Defendant, \[Defendant\] (Sergeant / Sertu NRP. 406266, MPP Status Denma Kodam III/Siliwangi), was charged with the premeditated murder of Sergeant Samuel Bulung (a Kopassus/RPKAD member) on May 3, 1983, arising from a dispute and fear that his intimate relationship with the victim's wife would be exposed. The Defendant was also charged with murdering Jhoni Parorongan (son of Sergeant Samuel Bulung) in the early hours of May 4, 1983, because Jhoni demanded the return of his parents' debt money managed by the Defendant, and the Defendant feared prior murder exposure. Furthermore, the Defendant embezzled and sold the victims' belongings (a Toyota Kijang car and a Honda GL motorcycle). The Bandung Military Court II-09 (Decision No. PTS-44-15/MAHMIL II-09/AD/K/III/1986) and the Jakarta High Military Court II (Decision No. PTS-04/BDG/K-AD/MMT-II/VIII/1986) sentenced the Defendant to death and ordered his dismissal from the armed forces. The Defendant subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** For any individual sentenced to death, there can be no mitigating factors, and all considerations must be aggravating. Because the considerations of the Jakarta High Military Court II Judge explicitly noted mitigating circumstances regarding the Defendant (taking into account the Defendant's past contributions and services to the State), the imposition of the death penalty was legally improper (\*onvoldoende gemotiveerd\*). Consequently, the death sentence must be annulled and commuted to life imprisonment. Additionally, the additional penalty of revoking the right to wear service medals/honors imposed by the \*judex facti\* is unwarranted as it is unregulated under the Military Criminal Code (KUHPM).
- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from the Petitioner for Cassation (\[Defendant\]); Annulling the Jakarta High Military Court II Decision No. PTS-04/BDG/K-AD/MMT-II/VIII/1986 and Bandung Military Court II-09 Decision No. PTS-44-15/MAHMIL II-09/AD/K/III/1986; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] legally and convincingly proven guilty of the criminal offenses of "Premeditated Murder," "Murder preceded by another criminal offense committed with intent to evade punishment," and "Embezzlement"; (2) Sentencing the Defendant to Principal Penalty: Life Imprisonment, and Additional Penalty: Dismissal from military service coupled with the revocation of the right to enter the Armed Forces.
