## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 536 K/Pid/2005, dated October 10, 2007, established four primary legal rules in corruption offenses involving the formulation and receipt of Regional Revenue and Expenditure Budgets (APBD):

First, Drafting Concept/Budget Drafts: The act of the DPRD Budget Committee in drafting an expenditure budget concept not based on Government Regulation No. 110 of 2000 does not constitute an unlawful act (\*wederrechtelijk\*), as a draft possesses no binding legal force.

Second, Setting Aside PP No. 110 of 2000: Setting aside PP No. 110 of 2000 does not constitute an unlawful act because said regulation was annulled by the Supreme Court via Judicial Review (Supreme Court Decision No. 04 G/HUM/2001 dated September 9, 2002) for conflicting with higher legislation (Law No. 4 of 1999 and Law No. 22 of 1999).

Third, Validity of Regional Regulations: A Regional Regulation (\*Perda APBD\*) that has not been annulled by the Government through repressive supervisory authority remains valid under the law (ex Law No. 22 of 1999 jo. Law No. 32 of 2004).

Fourth, Receiving Funds Based on Valid Regional Regulations: Implementing a valid Regional Regulation (such as receiving DPRD allowances/finances in accordance with the Perda) is a lawful act and not an unlawful act. Based on the principle of \*geen straf zonder schuld\*, the Defendants committed the physical acts, but those acts do not constitute crimes or violations, resulting in their release from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*).

## Executive Summary

The central legal principle established in this decision is that regional legislative policy products and financial receipts executed under unrevoked regional regulations (\*Perda\*) cannot be criminalized as corruption, especially when underlying executive regulations have been formally annulled by judicial review.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

- Budget drafts formulated by legislative committees lack binding legal force and cannot independently form the basis of unlawful acts (\*wederrechtelijk\*).
- Executive regulations annulled by the Supreme Court via material judicial review cannot be enforced as standards of legality in criminal proceedings.
- Financial receipts authorized by valid regional regulations (\*Perda\*) protected by the presumption of legality shield officials from criminal culpability (\*schuld\*).

## Case Background

The Original Defendants / Petitioners for Cassation (10 Members of the West Sumatra DPRD Budget Committee for the 1999–2004 Period: **H. Marfendi, Dra. Hilma Hamid, Drs. H. Sueb Karsono, Ir. Hendra Irwan Rahim, Drs. Djufri Hadi, Ir. Lief Warda, Ir. Alfian, Drs. Mahardi Efendi, M.Si., Syahril BB, H. Muhammad Yunus Said**) were charged with joint corruption (Primary: Article 2 paragraph (1); Subsidiary: Article 3 of Law No. 31 of 1999 jo. Law No. 20 of 2001 jo. Article 55 paragraph (1) 1st of the Criminal Code) for drafting the 2002 West Sumatra APBD and receiving DPRD financial allowances deviating from PP No. 110 of 2000, totaling Rp 5,904,105,350.—.

The Padang District Court (Decision No. 128/PID.B/2003/PN.PDG dated May 17, 2004) acquitted the Defendants of the primary charge but convicted them on the subsidiary charge, sentencing them to 2 years' imprisonment, a Rp 100 million fine, and restitution. The Padang High Court (Decision No. 105/PID/2004/PT.PDG dated December 24, 2004) annulled the district court decision and increased the sentence to 4 years' imprisonment and a Rp 200 million fine. The Petitioners for Cassation subsequently filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Unlawful Element (\*Wederrechtelijkheid\*) in Corruption Offenses (Articles 2 & 3 of Law No. 31 of 1999 jo. Law No. 20 of 2001)

The primary issue was whether formulating an APBD draft allocating DPRD allowances outside PP No. 110 of 2000, along with receiving finances based on an APBD Perda enacted with the Governor and unrevoked by the Central Government, qualifies as a corruption offense.

The Supreme Court held that it does not. Formulating concepts/drafts carries no binding legal effect. Furthermore, setting aside PP No. 110 of 2000 was legally justified because the regulation was declared void by the Supreme Court via Judicial Review No. 04 G/HUM/2001 for conflicting with Law No. 4/1999 and Law No. 22/1999 (\*lex superior derogat legi inferiori\*). As long as the APBD Perda remains valid and unrevoked by competent authorities via repressive supervision, financial receipts based on such Perda are lawful and non-unlawful.

### Application of Geen Straf Zonder Schuld and Ontslag van Alle Rechtsvervolging

Where physical acts (\*actus reus\*) of allocating and receiving funds are proven factually, but executed pursuant to valid Regional Regulation norms and without subjective guilt (\*mens rea\* / \*schuld\*), the Judge is mandated to issue a discharge ruling (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*).

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

**Question:**

Did the \*Judex Facti\* (Padang High Court & Padang District Court) misapply the law (\*foutieve kepassing van het recht\*) by treating APBD draft formulation and execution of valid APBD Perdas as corrupt unlawful acts, while ignoring the legal consequences of the Supreme Court's annulment of PP No. 110 of 2000?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

Yes, the \*Judex Facti\* misapplied the law. The Supreme Court annulled the Padang High Court and Padang District Court decisions, and adjudicated afresh by releasing the 10 Members of the West Sumatra DPRD Budget Committee from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*).

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Padang District Court & Padang High Court)

Examination of meeting chronologies of the West Sumatra DPRD Budget Committee (Nov 27, 2001 to Jan 31, 2002), enactment of DPRD Decrees Nos. 16/SB/2001 & 02/SB/2002 jo. Perda No. 2 of 2002 on the 2002 West Sumatra APBD, disbursement of DPRD operational support allowances totaling Rp 5,904,105,350.—, and specific receipt breakdowns for Defendants I through X.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The appellate and district \*Judex Facti\* misapplied the law.
2.  Formulating draft budgets not based on PP No. 110/2000 is not unlawful because drafts are non-binding.
3.  Setting aside PP No. 110/2000 is not unlawful because the regulation was annulled by the Supreme Court (Decision No. 04 G/HUM/2001) for conflicting with Law No. 4/1999 and Law No. 22/1999.
4.  Regional regulations not annulled by the Government via repressive supervision are valid under law, and receiving money based on a valid Perda is lawful and non-unlawful.
5.  Pursuant to the principle of \*geen straf zonder schuld\*, the Defendants committed the physical acts, but those acts do not constitute crimes or violations. The Supreme Court annulled the lower court rulings and adjudicated afresh, discharging the Defendants (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*).

## Ruling

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition from the Petitioners for Cassation / Defendants (**I. H. MARFENDI, II. Dra. HILMA HAMID, III. Drs. H. SUEB KARSONO, IV. Ir. HENDRA IRWAN RAHIM, V. Drs. DJUFRI HADI, VI. Ir. LIEF WARDA, VII. Ir. ALFIAN, VIII. Drs. MAHARDI EFENDI, M.Si., IX. SYAHRIL BB, X. H. MUHAMMAD YUNUS SAID**); annulled Padang High Court Decision No. 105/PID/2004/PT.PDG dated December 24, 2004, in conjunction with Padang District Court Decision No. 128/PID.B/2003/PN.PDG dated May 17, 2004; and adjudicated afresh: Declaring that the Defendants are proven to have committed the acts charged, but that such acts do not constitute crimes or violations; Discharging the Defendants from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*); Restoring the Defendants' rights regarding capacity, standing, and dignity; and charging the state with court costs across all judicial instances.

## Evidence Status and Gaps

### Established by Evidence

- Defendants I through X were Members of the West Sumatra DPRD Budget Committee for the 1999–2004 Period who formulated drafts and deliberated on the 2002 Fiscal Year APBD.
- The 2002 West Sumatra APBD was enacted into Regional Regulation No. 2 of 2002 by the DPRD and the Governor of West Sumatra.
- The Defendants received allowances and DPRD operational support funds in accordance with valid allocations under Regional Regulation No. 2 of 2002.

### Not Established / Unproven (Not a Crime)

- Formulating APBD drafts and receiving finances based on valid Regional Regulations were unproven to contain elements of unlawful acts (\*wederrechtelijk\*) or subjective culpability (\*schuld\*).

### Uncertainty and Gaps

- Legal Certainty of DPRD Public Policy Policies: This landmark ruling establishes a clear boundary that regional legislative policy products (\*Perda\*) enacted within constitutional authority cannot be arbitrarily criminalized through corruption enforcement as long as such Perdas remain unannulled by competent bodies and no private bribery or fictitious engineering is proven.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: Can DPRD members who formulate an APBD not in compliance with PP No. 110/2000 and receive allowances based on a valid Regional Regulation be prosecuted for corruption?**

**A:** No, they cannot be penalized (\*Ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*). Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 536 K/Pid/2005, formulating an APBD draft is not unlawful because drafts are non-binding. Setting aside PP No. 110/2000 is not unlawful because the regulation was annulled by the Supreme Court (No. 04 G/HUM/2001). Financial receipts based on an APBD Perda that is valid and unrevoked by the Government are lawful under the law. Pursuant to the principle of \*geen straf zonder schuld\*, the Defendants are released from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag\*).
