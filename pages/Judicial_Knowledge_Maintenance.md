# Judicial Knowledge Maintenance

Every institutional knowledge page should have an update strategy.

## Maintenance Signals

- new regulation;
- amendment;
- repeal;
- new judgment;
- new statistics;
- new policy;
- institutional restructuring.

## Status Labels

- Current
- Under Review
- Superseded
- Repealed
- Historical
- Verification Required
