# AI Use-Case Registry

The AI Use-Case Registry records specific judicial tasks for which AI is proposed, piloted or approved.

## Use-Case Fields

- task;
- user;
- expected benefit;
- risk;
- prohibited uses;
- data;
- model;
- human decision point;
- evaluation;
- approval;
- status.

## Purpose

The Registry separates the question **Can AI perform this task?** from **Should AI be permitted to perform this task?**
