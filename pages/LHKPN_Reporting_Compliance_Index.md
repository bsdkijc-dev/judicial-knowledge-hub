# LHKPN Reporting Compliance Index

The LHKPN Reporting Compliance Index measures the compliance of state officials in submitting LHKPN reports on time and in accordance with applicable requirements.

The measurement relates to the reporting period of the preceding year (t−1).

## 2025–2029 Targets

| 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 |
|------|------|------|------|------|
| 98%  | 98%  | 98%  | 99%  | 99%  |
