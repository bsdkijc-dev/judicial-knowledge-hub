# Justice Experience Index

The Justice Experience Index measures how users experience the justice journey.

## Potential Indicators

- accessibility;
- clarity;
- waiting;
- procedural burden;
- digital usability;
- responsiveness;
- perceived fairness.

## Status

Indicators require empirical validation.
