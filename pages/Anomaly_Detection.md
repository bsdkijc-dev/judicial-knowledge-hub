# Anomaly Detection

# Judicial Anomaly Detection

Anomaly Detection is proposed to identify unusual administrative or institutional patterns for human examination.

## Boundary

An anomaly is a signal, not proof of misconduct.

## Requirements

- transparent methodology;
- appropriate thresholds;
- human investigation;
- audit trails;
- safeguards against false positives.
