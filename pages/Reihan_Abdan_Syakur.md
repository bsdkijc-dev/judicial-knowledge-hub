# Reihan Abdan Syakur

## Contributor Profile

**Reihan Abdan Syakur** is a core research contributor at the Judicial Knowledge Hub, specializing in digital repository configuration, workflow automation, and systematic data archiving for the BSDK Mahkamah Agung RI corpus.

### Portal Navigation

- [Main Page](Main_Page.md) \> [Reihan Abdan Syakur](Reihan_Abdan_Syakur.md)

### Research Scope & Contributions

- \*\*Repository Configuration\*\*: Assisting in the setup, structuring, and maintenance of digital knowledge assets within the JPAIS architecture.
- \*\*Workflow Automation\*\*: Supporting automated data ingestion pipelines and metadata organization for institutional research papers.
