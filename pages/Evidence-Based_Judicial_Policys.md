# Evidence-Based Judicial Policys

# Evidence-Based Judicial Policy

Judicial policy should increasingly be informed by reliable evidence, analysis and evaluation.

## Policy Cycle

**Question → Data → Analysis → Options → Decision → Implementation → Evaluation**

## Principle

Evidence supports institutional decision-making; it does not replace legal authority or judicial independence.
