## Core Definition

**Module 2: Electronic Evidence Standards** provides systematic procedural, technical, and evidentiary protocols for judges, judicial policy analysts, and court officers to evaluate the acquisition, chain of custody, authentication, admissibility, and probative value of electronic information, digital records, and algorithmic outputs.

- **Simple Definition:** A standardized judicial module explaining how courts verify, admit, and weigh digital evidence—such as emails, chat messages, CCTV footage, server logs, digital contracts, and electronic signatures.
- **Legal / Technical Definition:** The authoritative procedural framework harmonizing statutory electronic transaction laws (UU ITE) with general procedural codes (KUHAP/HIR/RBg), defining the operational criteria for treating electronic records as valid documentary evidence (*alat bukti surat*) or expansionary standalone evidence (*perluasan alat bukti yang sah*) subject to verifiable integrity, authenticity, and legal retrieval standards.
- **Plain-Language Explanation:** It sets clear rules ensuring digital records presented in court are genuine, unmanipulated, legally obtained, and properly understood by the judge.

## Why It Matters

- **Prevents Digital Fabrication and Tampering:** Digital files are easily altered, deepfaked, or fabricated; rigorous standards safeguard against manipulated evidence entering the record.
- **Ensures Constitutional Due Process & Privacy:** Establishes strict warrant and authorization baselines for lawful interception, preventing unlawful surveillance from being legitimized in court.
- **Harmonizes Cross-Jurisdictional Adjudication:** Creates unified evaluation criteria across civil, criminal, religious, and state administrative courts.
- **Bridges the Technical-Judicial Divide:** Translates complex digital forensic concepts (hashing, metadata, chain of custody) into actionable legal tests for bench evaluations.

## Key Principles

Principle of Functional Equivalence (Asas Kesetaraan Fungsional)  

- **Meaning:** Electronic information and electronic documents carry the same legal weight and validity as paper-based written documents, provided their accessibility, integrity, and authenticity are guaranteed.
- **Why It Matters:** Foundation of digital modernization; prevents courts from rejecting evidence solely because it exists in digital form.
- **Legal Basis / Source:** UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Commerce (1996); Law No. 11/2008 jo. Law No. 1/2024 Art. 5(1).
- **Related Concepts:** Digital original, electronic writing requirement.

Principle of Chain of Custody & Cryptographic Integrity (Keutuhan Rantai Penjagaan)  

- **Meaning:** The party proffering digital evidence must prove an unbroken, documented record tracing the collection, preservation, analysis, and court submission of the digital asset without unauthorized modification.
- **Why It Matters:** Guarantees the digital file examined at trial is identical to the file captured at the primary source.
- **Legal Basis / Source:** Law No. 11/2008 Art. 6; ISO/IEC 27037 standards; SEMA Kamar Pidana/Perdata.
- **Related Concepts:** Cryptographic hash value (SHA-256), bit-stream image, write-blocking.

Principle of Lawful Acquisition & Judicial Authorization (Keabsahan Perolehan Bukti)  

- **Meaning:** Digital evidence obtained via interception, wiretapping, or device seizure must be conducted pursuant to statutory authority and prior judicial authorization (warrants).
- **Why It Matters:** Upholds fundamental constitutional privacy protections and bars tainted evidence (\*Exclusionary Rule\*).
- **Legal Basis / Source:** 1945 Constitution Art. 28G(1); MK Decision No. 20/PUU-XIV/2016; KUHAP Art. 33, 42.
- **Related Concepts:** Illegal search and seizure, fruit of the poisonous tree.

Principle of Explainability & Contextual Corroboration (Keterangan Ahli Forensik Digital)  

- **Meaning:** Raw electronic data requires contextual explanation by certified digital forensic experts whenever its extraction method, algorithm, or authenticity is challenged.
- **Why It Matters:** Prevents misinterpretation of complex system logs, metadata timestamps, or automated algorithmic outputs.
- **Legal Basis / Source:** KUHAP Art. 184(1) jo. Art. 186; Law No. 1/2024 Art. 5(4).
- **Related Concepts:** Digital forensics, expert testimony.

## Indonesia Section

- **Statutory Position of Electronic Evidence:**
  - Under Article 5(1) & (2) of UU ITE (Law No. 11/2008 jo. Law No. 1/2024), Electronic Information and/or Electronic Documents are legally recognized as valid evidence and constitute an expansion of valid means of proof (*perluasan alat bukti yang sah*) under Indonesian procedural law.
  - *Exceptions (Art. 5(4) UU ITE):* Electronic evidence is not valid for letters/documents that by statute must be drawn up in notarial authentic deed form or handwritten will format.
- **The Milestone of MK Decision No. 20/PUU-XIV/2016:**
  - The Constitutional Court affirmed that electronic interception/recording submitted as evidence is valid ONLY if performed by authorized law enforcement officers based on statutory powers and judicial warrants (prohibiting unauthorized private interception as primary prosecution evidence).
- **Electronic Signatures (Tanda Tangan Elektronik / TTE):**
  - *Certified Electronic Signature (TTE Tersertifikasi):* Generated via accredited Indonesian Electronic Certification Providers (PSrE / Balai Sertifikasi Elektronik); carries the highest evidentiary weight, equivalent to a notarized or recognized handwritten signature.
  - *Uncertified Electronic Signature (TTE Tidak Tersertifikasi):* Valid as preliminary evidence but requires corroborating technical proof (audit logs, IP tracking) if disputed.

## Important Decisions & Jurisprudential Restatements

Decision  
Constitutional Ruling on Wiretapped Electronic Evidence

- **Issuing Body:** Mahkamah Konstitusi RI
- **Case Reference:** Decision No. 20/PUU-XIV/2016 (Review of UU ITE & KUHAP)
- **Legal Principle:** Electronic documents obtained through interception/wiretapping without formal law enforcement authorization and proper judicial warrants are inadmissible in judicial proceedings.
- **Why It Matters:** Established the foundational constitutional boundary protecting citizen privacy from indiscriminate surveillance.
- **Official Source:** Constitutional Court Reports.

Decision  
Supreme Court Guidance on WhatsApp and Instant Message Transcripts

- **Issuing Body:** Mahkamah Agung RI (Criminal & Civil Chamber Plenary)
- **Instrument / SEMA Ref:** SEMA No. 1/2017 & SEMA No. 3/2023
- **Legal Principle:** Printed screenshots of instant messaging applications are considered copy/preliminary evidence; they require the presentation of the original digital device, digital extraction logs, or party recognition to achieve definitive probative value.
- **Why It Matters:** Prevents fraudulent convictions or liability based on fabricated image screenshots.
- **Official Source:** Supreme Court Chamber Reports.

## Debates & Controversies

Debate  
Admissibility of Deepfake / AI-Synthesized Recordings

- **Position A (Heightened Forensic Burden):** Given the rapid evolution of generative AI, all audio-visual digital evidence should be presumed unverified and inadmissible unless accompanied by a certified algorithmic verification and forensic provenance audit.
- **Position B (Traditional Party-Objection Model):** Electronic evidence should remain prima facie admissible unless the opposing party raises credible, substantiated technical objections demonstrating manipulation.
- **Evidence:** Increasing incidence of AI voice-cloning in fraud litigation and disputed election/corporate records.
- **Current Status:** The Supreme Court requires judges to order digital forensic examination (*Uji Forensik Digital*) whenever the authenticity of an audiovisual file is formally contested.

## Rule-of-Law Connection

- **Legal Certainty:** Predictable technical thresholds ensure business entities can rely on certified digital contracts and automated logs.
- **Due Process:** Protecting litigants from unverified electronic submissions ensures judgments rest on authentic, untampered facts.
- **Integrity of the Record:** Digital storage of evidence in court management systems (e-Court) maintains permanent, tamper-resistant archives.

## AI & Future of Law

- **AI-Generated Evidence & Hallucination:** Algorithmic calculations or AI summary outputs must not be admitted as standalone factual evidence; they must be accompanied by the underlying raw dataset and algorithm methodology.
- **Automated Chain-of-Custody via Distributed Ledger:** Integration of blockchain-based hash anchors to prove that evidentiary files stored in court repositories have not been modified post-filing.
- **Facial Recognition and Biometric Scans:** Chapter/Module standards mandate forensic error-rate analysis (False Match Rates) when biometric data is tendered in identification disputes.

## Common Misconceptions

Myth  
A simple screenshot (printout) of a WhatsApp chat is fully sufficient as authentic documentary evidence.

- **Reality:** A printed screenshot is merely a paper copy of a visual output. If contested, the proponent must present the original device, active database, or forensic export file to verify metadata, sender phone identity, and cryptographic integrity.
- **Source:** UU ITE Art. 6; Supreme Court Technical Guidelines (Buku II).

Myth  
Anyone can record a private phone conversation and present it as primary prosecution evidence in court.

- **Reality:** Under Constitutional Court Decision No. 20/PUU-XIV/2016, non-consensual secret private wiretaps are inadmissible as primary criminal prosecution evidence; lawful interception requires prior statutory authorization or judicial warrants.
- **Source:** MK Decision No. 20/PUU-XIV/2016.

## Practical Example

- **Scenario:** In a commercial breach of contract lawsuit, the Plaintiff submits an email chain and a PDF agreement signed via an uncertified electronic signature platform. The Defendant claims the agreement was altered after signature.
- **Legal Question:** How should the civil judge evaluate the admissibility and evidentiary weight of the PDF document?
- **Applicable Principle:** Functional Equivalence & Chain of Custody (UU ITE Art. 5, 6, 11).
- **Possible Analysis:**

1.  The judge confirms the PDF is admissible in principle under UU ITE Art. 5.
2.  Because the signature is uncertified, the judge evaluates the digital audit trail (IP logs, timestamps, email exchange records).
3.  If the Defendant demonstrates a mismatch in the cryptographic hash between the signed version and the filed version, the judge must reject the document's authenticity or order an independent forensic examination.

- **Limitation:** If the agreement had used a Certified Electronic Signature (PSrE), the burden of proving alteration would have rested entirely on the Defendant under statutory presumption of integrity.
