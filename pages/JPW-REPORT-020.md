# JPW-REPORT-020

## ICC Annual Report to UNGA 2022/23 — Wiki Entry

**Report of the International Criminal Court on its Activities in 2022/23** is the ICC's official annual report to the UN General Assembly for the period 1 August 2022 to 31 July 2023, submitted pursuant to Article 6 of the Relationship Agreement between the United Nations and the ICC and UN General Assembly Resolution 77/6.

## Summary

The report summarizes the ICC's judicial, prosecutorial, and institutional activities during the period, recording the issuance of arrest warrants for Vladimir Putin and Maria Lvova-Belova, the final confirmation of Dominic Ongwen's sentence, the launch of the 2023–2025 Strategic Plans, the election of new Registrar Osvaldo Zavala Giler, and continued obstacles in executing 16 outstanding public arrest warrants.

## Legal Basis

- [Relationship Agreement between the United Nations and the International Criminal Court](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Relationship_Agreement_between_the_United_Nations_and_the_International_Criminal_Court): Article 6, on submission of annual reports.
- [UN General Assembly Resolution 77/6](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/UN_General_Assembly_Resolution_77/6): Paragraph 31.
- [Rome Statute](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Rome_Statute): The judicial framework, including Articles 8, 18, 56, 61, and 93(10).

## Background

Submission of this annual report to the 78th session of the UN General Assembly serves as the principal official communication channel between the ICC and the UN, strengthening international law-enforcement cooperation under the Rome Statute mandate.

## Objectives

- Report on judicial and prosecutorial developments across active situations.
- Report on alignment of the 2023–2025 Strategic Plans under the One Court principle.
- Strengthen the complementarity framework and international cooperation with the UN and States Parties.

## Key Provisions

- **Prosecutorial Activity**: Continued investigations in Afghanistan, Venezuela I, and the Philippines; conclusion of investigations in Georgia, CAR II, and Guinea; receipt of a second referral from the DRC concerning crimes in North Kivu since 1 January 2022.
- **Judicial Decisions**: Arrest warrants for Vladimir Putin and Maria Lvova-Belova; final confirmation of Dominic Ongwen's sentence; determination of Ntaganda's estimated reparations liability at \$31.3 million; termination of proceedings against Paul Gicheru and Al-Tuhamy Khaled following their deaths.
- **Institutional Development**: Election of Osvaldo Zavala Giler as Registrar; launch of the 2023–2025 Strategic Plans; adoption of the Policy on the Crime of Gender Persecution.
- **Victim Assistance**: The TFV assisted nearly 17,000 victims across 7 countries.

## Implementation

Carried out through close coordination among the Court's organs (Presidency, OTP, Registry), the UN (including OLA, UNDP), field missions, and civil society organizations. The Court opened a field office in Ukraine under a 23 March 2023 agreement, and signed an operational cooperation framework with UNDP in October 2022.

## Evidence

- Issuance of 534 written decisions and 227 hearings held by the Chambers.
- Participation of over 15,000 victims in proceedings, with 8,900+ new victim application forms received.
- TFV programs directly benefiting close to 17,000 individual victims in 7 countries.
- Submission of 536 visa requests and 300 principal cooperation requests by the Registry, and 458 requests for assistance by the OTP.
- Collection of €3.8 million in voluntary contributions from 32 States Parties for the TFV.

## Policy Issues

- Lack of substantive UN Security Council response to 16 findings of non-cooperation.
- Unacceptable threats against elected ICC officials in the course of their mandate.
- Challenges executing 16 outstanding public arrest warrants.
- The impact of active armed conflict in Sudan on the Darfur investigation.
- A \$2.5 million funding gap for reparations in the Lubanga case.

## Relationships with Other Entities

- **[International Criminal Court](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/International_Criminal_Court)**: Reporting institution.
- **[United Nations General Assembly](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/United_Nations_General_Assembly)**: Recipient of the report.
- **[Vladimir Vladimirovich Putin](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Vladimir_Vladimirovich_Putin)**: President of the Russian Federation, subject of an ICC public arrest warrant issued on 17 March 2023 over alleged war crimes involving the deportation and forcible transfer of children from occupied Ukrainian territory.
- **[Maria Alekseyevna Lvova-Belova](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Maria_Alekseyevna_Lvova-Belova)**: Commissioner for Children's Rights in the Office of the President of the Russian Federation, subject of an ICC public arrest warrant issued on 17 March 2023 over the alleged forcible transfer of Ukrainian children.
- **[Dominic Ongwen](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Dominic_Ongwen)**: Former Lord's Resistance Army (LRA) commander whose conviction and 25-year sentence were finally confirmed by the Appeals Chamber on 15 December 2022; the case is now at the stage of defining a representative sample for victim reparations.
- **[Ali Muhammad Ali Abd-Al-Rahman](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Ali_Muhammad_Ali_Abd-Al-Rahman)** ("Ali Kushayb"): Defendant in the Darfur, Sudan situation, whose trial on war crimes and crimes against humanity charges continued before Trial Chamber I, marking the ICC's first trial arising from a UN Security Council referral.
- **[Osvaldo Zavala Giler](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Osvaldo_Zavala_Giler)**: Ecuadorian national elected by the judges on 10 February 2023 as ICC Registrar for a five-year term beginning 17 April 2023, succeeding Peter Lewis.
- **[Paul Gicheru](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Paul_Gicheru)**: Defendant in the Kenya situation, whose proceedings were formally terminated by Trial Chamber III on 14 October 2022 following confirmation of his death.
- **[Al-Tuhamy Mohamed Khaled](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Al-Tuhamy_Mohamed_Khaled)**: Suspect in the Libya situation, whose proceedings were terminated by Pre-Trial Chamber I on 7 September 2022 following receipt of an official death certificate from Libyan authorities.
- **[Joseph Kony](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Joseph_Kony)**, **[Vincent Otti](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Vincent_Otti)**, **[Mahamah Nouradine Adam](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Mahamah_Nouradine_Adam)**, **[Ahmad Harun](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Ahmad_Harun)**, **[Omar Al-Bashir](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Omar_Al-Bashir)**, **[Abdel Raheem Muhammad Hussein](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Abdel_Raheem_Muhammad_Hussein)**, **[Abdallah Banda](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Abdallah_Banda)**, **[Walter Barasa](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Walter_Barasa)**, **[Philip Kipkoech Bett](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Philip_Kipkoech_Bett)**, **[Saif Al-Islam Gaddafi](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Saif_Al-Islam_Gaddafi)**, **[Mikhail Mayramovich Mindzaev](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Mikhail_Mayramovich_Mindzaev)**, **[Gamlet Guchmazov](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Gamlet_Guchmazov)**, **[David Georgiyevich Sanakoev](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/David_Georgiyevich_Sanakoev)**, **[Sylvestre Mudacumura](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Sylvestre_Mudacumura)**: 14 other individuals with outstanding public arrest warrants.
