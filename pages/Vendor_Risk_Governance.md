# Vendor Risk Governance

Critical judicial technology requires institutional control over vendor-related risks.

## Assessment Areas

- security;
- data handling;
- model updates;
- service continuity;
- audit access;
- data portability;
- migration;
- termination;
- subcontractors;
- institutional dependency.

## Sovereignty Principle

Outsourcing implementation must not eliminate the judiciary's capacity to understand and supervise critical technology.
