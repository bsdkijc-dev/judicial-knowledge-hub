# Post-2035 Judiciary Framework

The Post-2035 Judiciary Framework is a proposed strategic architecture for the Indonesian judiciary after the completion of the 2010–2035 judicial reform period.

It is designed as a successor-generation framework rather than an immediate replacement of the existing Blueprint.

## Strategic Continuity

The framework connects:

- [Judicial Reform Blueprint 2010–2035](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Judicial_Reform_Blueprint_2010%E2%80%932035);
- [Renstra Mahkamah Agung 2025–2029](Renstra_Mahkamah_Agung_2025-2029.md);
- transition experiments during 2026–2034;
- the proposed 2035–2045 target state.

## Core Question

How should courts operate when digital records, electronic evidence, AI-assisted research, remote services and data analytics become ordinary components of justice?
