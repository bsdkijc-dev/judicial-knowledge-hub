# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:4540K PDT 1998

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 4540 K/Pdt/1998** is an Indonesian cassation ruling establishing the legal principle that the issuance of a land title certificate through the National Agrarian Operational Project (PRONA) is not determined by the original status of the land, and affirming the validity of a land-rights transfer by way of a PPAT grant deed.[^1]

\_\_TOC\_\_

## 1. Short answer

The decision (Reg. No. 4540 K/Pdt/1998, dated 26 September 2000, deliberated 25 September 2000)[^2] set an important rule of national land and land-registration law on the nature of titling under PRONA and on the validity of land-rights transfers by PPAT grant deed.

1.  **Principal legal rule (nature of PRONA titling):** "The issuance of a land-rights certificate through 'Prona' (National Project) is **not determined by the original status of the land**, but is a method of land titling with a fast process and low cost, because it is subsidised by the Government."[^3]
2.  **Error of the *judex facti* (Manado High Court):** The Manado High Court (No. 30/Pdt/1998/PT.Mdo) wrongly applied the law by overturning the District Court's judgment on the mistaken view that, because titling was done through PRONA, the land was originally state land and therefore could not be granted by the right-holder.[^4]
3.  **Validity of the freehold certificate and PPAT grant deed:** The issuance of Freehold Certificate (SHM) No. 463/Pakowa in the name of \[Party C\] is lawful. The transfer of the land to the Plaintiff (Ny. \[Party D\]) under **Grant Deed No. 5944/M.S/20/1989**, made before the Land Deed Official (PPAT) Drs. John Marthen Solang, is **valid and legally binding**.[^5]
4.  **Unlawful act (physical possession without right):** Defendants I and II (\[Party A\] and \[Party E\]), who occupied the Plaintiff's freehold land and built a house on it without valid title, committed an **unlawful act (PMH)** and were ordered to demolish the building and hand over the land vacant and free to the Plaintiff.[^6]

The Supreme Court **granted the cassation petition of the Petitioner (Ny. \[Party D\])**, annulled Manado High Court Decision No. 30/Pdt/1998/PT.Mdo of 25 April 1998, and, adjudicating itself, **upheld the operative part of Manado District Court Decision No. 303/Pdt.G/1996/PN.Mdo of 30 May 1997**: partly granting the claim in convention, declaring the grant valid, declaring the land the Plaintiff's property, and ordering Defendants I and II to demolish all buildings and vacate the disputed land.[^7]

## 4. Legal and procedural issues

### 4.1 Legal nature of land registration through PRONA after the Basic Agrarian Law (Law No. 5 of 1960)

- *Does issuing a land-rights certificate through PRONA automatically change the original land's status to "state land", extinguishing the original owner's civil rights?*[^17]
- **Supreme Court rule: it does not change the original status of the land.** Certificate issuance through PRONA is **not determined by the original status of the land** (whether state land or customary freehold).[^18] PRONA is merely a mass land-registration facility with a fast process and low cost, subsidised by the Government, to provide legal certainty.[^19] A High Court that treats a PRONA certificate as automatically extinguishing the original owner's civil rights has **misapplied land law** (*foutieve toepassing van het recht*).[^20]

### 4.2 Validity of land-rights transfer by PPAT grant deed (Art. 19 UUPA in conjunction with Government Regulation No. 10 of 1961 / No. 24 of 1997)

- A transfer based on a grant deed validly made before a Land Deed Official (PPAT) creates binding and complete ownership for the grantee.[^21]

### 4.3 Evidentiary status of land and building tax (PBB / STTS)

- A PBB payment receipt or tax notice (SPPT) **is not proof of land ownership**; it is only proof that a tax obligation was discharged and an indication of physical possession.[^22]

## 5. Question of law versus question of fact

### Question of law (*Judex Juris* / Supreme Court)

- Did the *judex facti* (Manado HC No. 30/Pdt/1998/PT.Mdo) misapply the law by annulling SHM No. 463/Pakowa and the PPAT grant deed on the reasoning that PRONA titling automatically makes the original land state land?[^23]
- **Supreme Court answer:** Yes. PRONA is a subsidised titling method, not a determinant of the land's original status. The Court granted \[Party D\]'s cassation, annulled the HC decision, and, adjudicating itself, **upheld the DC decision declaring the grant valid, the land the Plaintiff's property, and ordering Defendants I and II to demolish the house and vacate the land**.[^24]

### Question of fact (*Judex Facti* / Manado DC and HC)

- Verification of SHM No. 463/Pakowa, Survey Letter No. 576/1982, in the name of \[Party C\];[^25]
- Verification of Grant Deed No. 5944/M.S/20/1989 dated 11 August 1989 before PPAT Drs. John Marthen Solang;[^26]
- Verification of physical possession by \[Party A\] and \[Party E\] and construction of a concrete/timber house;[^27]
- Verification of the receipt for compensation for cultivation rights from Petrus Makasenggehe;[^28]
- Examination of trial witnesses (\[Party B\], Robinson Sanger, Bobby Warbung, Hamid Luran, Herman Sengkey, Ruben Adu, Jootje Waworundeng, Iskandar Elias).[^29]

## 6. Related knowledge and jurisprudence

### Legislation

- **Law No. 5 of 1960 (Basic Agrarian Law, UUPA):** Art. 19 (land registration) and Art. 20 (freehold).
- **Government Regulation No. 10 of 1961 / No. 24 of 1997:** land registration rules and Land Deed Officials (PPAT).
- **Decree of the Minister of Home Affairs / State Minister for Agrarian Affairs on PRONA:** implementation guidelines for the National Agrarian Operational Project.
- **Law No. 14 of 1970 in conjunction with Law No. 35 of 1999 and Law No. 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court:** cassation and self-adjudication powers.

### Doctrine and landmark jurisprudence

- **Doctrine of legal certainty in PRONA registration:** PRONA does not cancel or deprive existing civil rights in customary or freehold land; it provides legal certainty through issuance of subsidised certificates.
- **Settled jurisprudence on the evidentiary value of PBB / *girik* / *petuk*:** PBB/Ipeda payment is not proof of freehold and cannot defeat a freehold certificate (SHM) lawfully issued by the National Land Agency.

## 7. What the evidence establishes / does not establish

### Established

- Issuance of SHM No. 463/Pakowa through PRONA is **lawful**.[^30]
- The grant of land from \[Party C\] (Co-Defendant V) to Ny. \[Party D\] under PPAT Grant Deed No. 5944/M.S/20/1989 is **valid and effective**.[^31]
- The Plaintiff is **the lawful owner of the disputed land**.[^32]
- Defendants I and II **occupied the land without right (unlawful act)**.[^33]
- Defendants I and II are ordered to **demolish all buildings and vacate the disputed land for delivery to the Plaintiff**.[^34]
- Manado High Court Decision No. 30/Pdt/1998/PT.Mdo is **annulled and without legal force**.[^35]

### Not established (rejected, including in reconvention)

- The contention of Defendants I and II that PRONA titling voids civil rights and the grant is **rejected in its entirety by the Supreme Court**.[^36]

## 8. Uncertainty and gaps

### Legal certainty for PRONA certificate holders and PPAT grantees

This landmark ruling gives crucial legal certainty to the many recipients of PRONA land certificates in Indonesia: district and high courts may not annul PRONA certificates on the mistaken assumption that PRONA registration automatically equates to a grant of state land. A PRONA certificate issued by the National Land Agency (BPN) and transferred by official PPAT grant deed is valid and must be legally protected against encroachment by persons without right.

## 9. GEO Q&A (Generative Engine Optimization)

Q: Does land titling through the PRONA programme automatically make the land state land and invalidate a PPAT grant deed, according to Supreme Court jurisprudence?  
**A: No.** Under Supreme Court Reg. No. 4540 K/Pdt/1998:

1.  Certificate issuance through "Prona" (National Project) is **not determined by the original status of the land**.[^37] PRONA is merely a fast, low-cost titling method subsidised by the Government.[^38]
2.  A freehold certificate issued through PRONA and later transferred by **PPAT grant deed is valid and legally binding**.[^39]
3.  A party occupying certificated land without valid title (even holding PBB receipts) commits an unlawful act (PMH) and is ordered to demolish the building and vacate the land.[^40]
