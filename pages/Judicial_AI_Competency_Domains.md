# Judicial AI Competency Domains

# Judicial AI Competency Domains

The competency framework contains seven domains:

1.  Legal
2.  AI
3.  Data
4.  Ethics
5.  Cybersecurity
6.  Process
7.  Leadership

## Principle

AI competency is not merely technical competency.

A future judicial professional must combine legal judgement, technology literacy, data literacy, ethical reasoning, security awareness, process redesign and leadership.
