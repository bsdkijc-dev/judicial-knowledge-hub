## Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 325 K/Pid/1985 dated October 27, 1986, establishes fundamental criminal and civil jurisprudence regarding the separation between the criminal offense of fraud and civil disputes: An indictment stating solely that the Defendant sold a rice field for Rp 1,500,000, which land turned out to be non-existent, does not constitute the crime of fraud under Article 378 of the KUHP or any other criminal offense, but rather constitutes an ordinary civil matter. Consequently, even if proven to have been committed by the Defendant, the Defendant must be released from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*), and the Defendant's rights regarding capacity, position, honor, and dignity must be restored.

## Case Background

The Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation (\[Defendant\], \[age removed\], retired Notary, residing in \[address removed\]) was brought before the Simalungun District Court charged with committing the crime of fraud under Article 378 of the KUHP. The Public Prosecutor alleged that on July 16, 1973, the Defendant sold 6 hectares of rice field in Kampung Merayap-II, Tanah Jawa Sub-District, Simalungun Regency, for Rp 1,500,000 to Kongsi Lumbanraja. However, upon investigation, the sold rice field did not exist (it existed only on paper).

The Simalungun District Court (Decision No. 147/KTS/1983/PN-Sim dated June 12, 1984) sentenced the Defendant to 3 (three) months of imprisonment with a 6-month probation period. The Medan High Court (Decision No. 202/PID/1984/PT-MDN dated December 31, 1984) upheld the Simalungun District Court judgment. Dissatisfied with the ruling, the Defendant filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The formulation of facts (\*feitelijke weergave\*) in the Public Prosecutor's indictment fails to legally incorporate the elements of the crime of fraud under Article 378 of the KUHP, merely stating that the Defendant sold 6 hectares of rice field for Rp 1,500,000, which land turned out to be non-existent.
2.  What was charged does not constitute the crime of fraud under Article 378 of the KUHP or any other criminal offense, but rather represents an ordinary civil dispute.
3.  Because the charged act is proven but does not constitute a criminal offense, the Defendant must be released from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*), and his rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity must be restored (\*rehabilitation\*).
4.  Legal Principle: "An indictment stating solely that the Defendant sold a rice field for Rp 1,500,000, which land turned out to be non-existent, does not constitute the crime of fraud under Article 378 of the KUHP or any other criminal offense, but rather constitutes an ordinary civil matter. Thus, even if proven to have been committed by the Defendant, the Defendant must be released from all legal prosecutions, and the Defendant's rights regarding capacity, position, honor, and dignity must be restored."

- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation \[Defendant\]; Annulling the Medan High Court decision No. 202/PID/1984/PT-MDN dated December 31, 1984, and the Simalungun District Court decision No. 147/KTS/1983/PN-Sim dated June 12, 1984; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring that the act charged against Defendant \[Defendant\] is proven; (2) Declaring further that the proven act does not constitute a criminal offense; (3) Releasing Defendant \[Defendant\] from all legal prosecutions (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\*); (4) Restoring the Defendant's rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity (\*rehabilitation\*); (5) Ordering that photocopies of documentary evidence remain annexed to the case file; (6) Charging court costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State. \*(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Wednesday, October 8, 1986, and pronounced in an open session on Monday, October 27, 1986, by Palti Radja Siregar, S.H. \[Supreme Court Justice / Session Chairman\], R. Soebijantono, S.H., and H. Agus Djamili, S.H. \[Associate Justices\]).\*
