# JPW-REGULATION-GER-GEOBG-2025

The **Geothermal Energy Acceleration Act** (German: *Geothermie-Beschleunigungsgesetz* – GeoBG) is a draft legislative proposal approved by the [German Federal Cabinet](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/German_Federal_Cabinet) on August 6, 2025, aimed at accelerating the transition toward a climate-neutral heat supply by streamlining approval processes for geothermal energy, heat pumps, and thermal storage [GeoBG](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/GeoBG).

## Overview

The legislation aims to eliminate regulatory and bureaucratic bottlenecks in geothermal energy deployment across Germany [Heat Transition](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Heat_Transition). GeoBG establishes statutory maximum approval deadlines, digitalizes permit processing, and legally categorizes geothermal infrastructure as an **overriding public interest** [Public Interest](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Public_Interest).

## Legal Basis

- **Geothermie-Beschleunigungsgesetz (GeoBG)**: Drafted and submitted by the German Federal Cabinet on August 6, 2025 [German Cabinet](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/German_Cabinet).
- **EU Renewable Energy Directive (RED III)**: Partially transposed into national German law via GeoBG [RED III](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/RED_III).
- **Statutory Amendments**: Amends the *Water Resources Act* (WHG), *Federal Mining Act* (BBergG), *Environmental Impact Assessment Act* (UVPG), and *Code of Administrative Court Procedure* (VwGO) [German Mining Law](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/German_Mining_Law).

## Background

Geothermal and district heating projects in Germany previously experienced long development delays due to complex administrative procedures under mining and water regulations [Bureaucratic Bottlenecks](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Bureaucratic_Bottlenecks). GeoBG was created to overcome these obstacles and hit national climate neutrality targets.

## Key Objectives

1.  Accelerate planning and permitting procedures for geothermal energy and heat grid infrastructure [Permit Acceleration](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Permit_Acceleration).
2.  Establish legally binding maximum deadlines for mining and water authority approvals [Legal Timelines](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Legal_Timelines).
3.  Facilitate subsurface exploration and streamlined access to seismic data [Seismic Exploration](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Seismic_Exploration).
4.  Encourage recovery from heat sources such as river water, wastewater, and industrial waste heat [Industrial Waste Heat](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Industrial_Waste_Heat).

## Core Provisions

- **Overriding Public Interest**: Legally grants geothermal developments prioritized status in administrative land-use balancing decisions [Public Interest](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Public_Interest).
- **Landowner Tolerance Obligations**: Requires landowners to allow subsurface seismic testing, subject to property restoration and compensation for direct financial loss [Land Use](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Land_Use).
- **Heat Pipelines**: Aligns planning procedures for heat transfer pipelines with established fast-track rules for natural gas and hydrogen pipelines [Heat Pipelines](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Heat_Pipelines).
- **Year-Round Exploration**: Eases exploration permits in protected areas under strict conditions to prevent seasonal delays [Exploration Permits](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Exploration_Permits).

## Implementation & Timeline

- **Cabinet Approval**: Adopted as part of a comprehensive federal energy package on August 6, 2025 [Energy Package](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Package).
