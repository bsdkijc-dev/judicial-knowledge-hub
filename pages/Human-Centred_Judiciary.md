# Human-Centred Judiciary

# Human-Centred Judiciary

The future judiciary should be designed around the needs of people rather than around the capabilities of technology.

## Principles

- citizen needs are the starting point;
- accessibility is designed into services;
- technology reduces unnecessary administrative burden;
- digital services remain understandable;
- human assistance remains available;
- efficiency must not undermine fundamental rights.

## Transformation

**Technology for citizens**

rather than

**citizens adapting to technology**.
