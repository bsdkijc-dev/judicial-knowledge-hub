# JPW-PUBLICATION-IFN-AUG2025

**International FundsNet Country Updates – August 2025** is a global financial and investment regulatory thought leadership publication released by [Eversheds Sutherland](https://judicialknowledge.miraheze.org/wiki/Eversheds_Sutherland) on September 2, 2025 (August 2025 edition) \[1–6\]. The report summarizes recent legal, policy, and regulatory changes across international jurisdictions that impact investment managers, fund operators, and institutional investors \[1–6\].

## Executive Summary

The publication provides a global regulatory horizon-scanning framework to assist asset managers in anticipating legal shifts across multiple jurisdictions \[1–6\]. Key updates cover regulatory burden reductions in Croatia, account transfer and liquidity rule updates in Canada, expanded alternative asset access for 401(k) plans in the United States, and tax treatment clarifications under Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTA) in Cambodia \[1–6\].

## Legal Basis & Legal Disclaimer

- **Formal Legal Basis**: Not found in available sources (The document is compiled from various national regulatory updates) \[1–6\].
- **Informational Disclaimer**: The content presented in the publication is provided strictly for general informational purposes and does not constitute formal legal advice \[1–6\].

## Background

Rapidly evolving international financial market regulations require investment managers to continuously monitor legal developments across multiple jurisdictions \[1–6\]. This publication serves as a structured intelligence brief to help market participants maintain cross-border regulatory compliance and optimize fund operational strategies \[1–6\].

## Objectives

1.  Deliver a concise global summary of regulatory developments affecting investment managers \[1–6\].
2.  Detail proposed regulatory burden reductions for AIF and UCITS managers in Croatia \[1–6\].
3.  Outline modernized account transfer rules and fund liquidity relief measures implemented in Canada \[1–6\].
4.  Explain expanded alternative asset investment access for US 401(k) plan participants \[1–6\].
5.  Clarify Cambodian DTA tax treatments regarding share premiums and capital gains \[1–6\].

## Key Provisions & Recommendations

- **Croatia (HANFA)**: The Croatian Financial Services Supervisory Agency (HANFA) proposed major reforms to reduce regulatory burdens on Alternative Investment Fund (AIF) and UCITS managers \[1–6\].
- **Canada (CIRO, AMF, CSA)**: The Canadian Investment Regulatory Organization (CIRO) and Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF) modernized account transfer rules, while the Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA) provided targeted liquidity relief for investment funds \[1–6\].
- **United States**: A Presidential Executive Order aims to democratize access to alternative asset classes for 401(k) retirement plan investors \[1–6\].
- **Cambodia**: Clarified tax treatment of share premiums and capital gains under Double Taxation Avoidance Agreements (DTA) \[1–6\].

## Implementation & Deployment

- **Publication Release**: Officially published globally on September 2, 2025 \[1–6\].
- **Lead Practice Team**: Led by Eversheds Sutherland UK legal practitioners Lindi Rudman (Legal Director) and Michaela Arter (Partner) \[1–6\].

## Policy & Operational Challenges

- **Cross-Border Regulatory Friction**: Investment managers face increasing compliance complexity when harmonizing global fund operations with localized country rules \[1–6\].
- **Liquidity and Tax Uncertainty**: Navigating localized fund liquidity relief and cross-border DTA tax implications requires ongoing legal assessment \[1–6\].
