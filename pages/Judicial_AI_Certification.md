# Judicial AI Certification

# Judicial AI Certification

Judicial AI Certification is proposed to establish measurable competency standards for personnel working with judicial AI.

## Certification Areas

- AI fundamentals;
- prompting;
- source verification;
- data governance;
- ethics;
- cybersecurity;
- AI evaluation;
- human oversight.

## Principle

Certification should be role-based and linked to the [Five-Level Judicial AI Competency Framework](Five-Level_Judicial_AI_Competency_Framework.md).
