# Mediation Performance Indicator

# Mediation Performance Indicator

The indicator measures cases successfully resolved through mediation.

## Successful Mediation

The Renstra includes:

- cases completely settled through a settlement deed or withdrawal;
- cases partially settled.

Mediation may be conducted by:

- judge mediators;
- non-judge mediators.

Only cases required to undergo mediation and that have completed the mediation process are included.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Cases successfully resolved through mediation}} {\text{Cases required to undergo mediation}}\times100\\ \</math\>
