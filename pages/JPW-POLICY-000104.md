## 4. POLICY ANALYSIS

- **Background**: \[FACT\] Spain's judicial system historically required physical presence at court headquarters for hearings, testimony, and document submissions. Law enforcement officers (such as the Civil Guard), legal professionals, and citizens living in remote rural regions faced severe time and financial burdens due to mandatory travel, often causing operational disruptions such as temporary closures of local security barracks during police report ratifications.
- **Policy Problem**: \[FACT\]
  - **Operational Disruptions & Inefficiencies**: Physical attendance mandates created severe procedural bottlenecks, prolonged travel times, and manual document delivery delays.
  - **Geographic & Physical Barriers to Justice**: Citizens in remote or underserved areas and individuals with special accessibility needs experienced unequal barriers to participating in judicial proceedings.
  - **Security & Traceability Needs**: Remote court proceedings required guaranteed participant identity verification, protection of sensitive personal data, and tamper-proof recording of digital evidence.
- **Legal/Policy Gap**: \[FACT\] The gap between traditional procedural laws requiring physical court presence (\*inmediación física\*) and the technical infrastructure necessary to guarantee remote digital immediacy (\*inmediación digital\*) with full legal validity.
- **Policy Objectives**: \[FACT\] Streamline judicial proceedings through remote court hearings; eliminate travel costs and operational disruptions; ensure tamper-proof audit trails via blockchain; provide accessible tools for citizens with special needs; and maintain compliance with data privacy standards via pseudonym-based identification.
- **Policy Implementation**: \[FACT\]
  - **Cloud & Blockchain Architecture**: Deployed as a scalable cloud platform integrating blockchain technology to create an immutable, tamper-proof ledger recording all actions (document submissions, participant entries, hearing logs).
  - **Core Security Guarantees**: Engineered to achieve three core legal-technical objectives: non-repudiation (\*no repudio\*), non-manipulation (\*no manipulación\*), and non-impersonation (\*no suplantación de identidad\*).
  - **Pseudonym-Based Identification**: Implemented pseudonymized identity verification protocols for law enforcement personnel to protect officer safety and privacy during remote testimonies.
  - **Stakeholder Co-Design & Workshops**: The Ministry conducted focus groups and workshops in May 2024 with judges, lawyers, law enforcement, and citizens to refine platform features and accessibility tools.
  - **Deployment Options**: Configured for public cloud, private SaaS, or on-premises deployment, facilitating future integration across Autonomous Communities with transferred justice competencies.
- **Evidence & Impact**: \[FACT\] EVID significantly reduced administrative overhead (scanning, filing, cataloguing) and travel times for law enforcement and legal professionals. Blockchain audit trails provided decentralized data breach resilience without single points of failure.
- **Reform & Unresolved Issues**: \[FACT\] Ongoing coordination to integrate EVID across all Autonomous Communities with delegated judicial powers; addressing regional disparities in digital infrastructure; and ongoing legal professional training for remote hearing protocols.

## Summary

**Virtual Desktop for Digital Immediation (EVID)** (\*Escritorio Virtual de Inmediación Digital\*) (Stable ID: **JPW-POLICY-000104**) is a cloud-based and blockchain-integrated e-justice platform developed by the Spanish Ministry of the Presidency, Justice, and Parliamentary Relations (\*Ministerio de la Presidencia, Justicia y Relaciones con las Cortes\*) in partnership with Councilbox, Telefónica, and Kawaru Consulting. The platform enables secure remote court hearings, digital document management, pseudonym-based identification for law enforcement participants, and tamper-proof audit trails, ensuring non-repudiation, non-manipulation, and non-impersonation across Spain's judicial proceedings.

## Legal Basis

The policy and technical framework governing EVID is anchored in Spanish legislative provisions and EU public sector initiatives:

- **Spanish Judicial Digitalisation Framework**: Spanish Judicial System Digital Modernisation Plan and the digitalisation mandates under the Organic Law on the Judiciary (\*Ley Orgánica del Poder Judicial - LOPJ\*).
- **EU Public Sector Governance**: Public Sector Tech Watch Case PSTW-2160 and the EU Interoperable Europe Framework.
- **Data Protection Regulations**: Regulation (EU) 2018/1725 and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Background

Spain's judicial system historically required physical attendance at court headquarters for proceedings, testimonies, and document submissions. This created severe procedural delays and financial burdens for legal professionals, citizens in rural regions, and law enforcement officers (such as the \*Guardia Civil\*), whose physical travel often required temporary closures of local security barracks.

## Objectives

1\. Streamline judicial proceedings through secure, legal-grade remote court hearings. 2. Eliminate geographical and physical barriers for citizens in remote areas and individuals with accessibility requirements. 3. Ensure tamper-proof traceability and auditability of digital evidence through blockchain technology. 4. Protect law enforcement officer safety and privacy using pseudonym-based identification protocols.

## Scope

EVID encompasses several primary operational modules: 1. *Cloud & Blockchain Architecture*: Provides a scalable cloud environment integrated with an enterprise blockchain layer to create immutable logs of all hearing activities. 2. *Pseudonym Identification*: Enables police and security officers to present remote testimony without exposing personal identifiers. 3. *Accessibility Integration*: Incorporates specialized interface tools designed to support participants with accessibility needs. 4. *Deployment Flexibility*: Supports public cloud, private SaaS, and on-premises deployment configurations.

## Key Provisions & Principles

- **Immutable Audit Trail**: Hearing events and document uploads are recorded on a tamper-proof blockchain ledger.
- **Three Technical-Legal Guarantees**: Engineered to ensure non-repudiation (\*no repudio\*), non-manipulation (\*no manipulación\*), and non-impersonation (\*no suplantación de identidad\*).
- **Participatory Co-Design**: Features are continuously refined through focus groups involving judges, court clerks, prosecutors, lawyers, and citizens.

## Implementation

Deployed nationally by the Ministry of the Presidency, Justice, and Parliamentary Relations in collaboration with Councilbox (software vendor), Telefónica (telecommunications and ICT consulting), and Kawaru Consulting (implementation management).

## Policy Problems

- Aligning remote procedural immediacy (\*inmediación digital\*) with traditional procedural rules mandating physical presence (\*inmediación física\*).
- Mitigating risks of cyber threats, identity impersonation, and unauthorized data breaches during remote testimonies.
- Addressing regional disparities in technological infrastructure across Autonomous Communities.

## Reform

- Continuous execution of specialized training programs for judicial officers and legal practitioners.
- Strategic expansion to integrate EVID across all Autonomous Communities with transferred judicial powers.

## 6. SALIDA MEDIAWIKI

## Resumen

**Escritorio Virtual de Inmediación Digital (EVID)** (ID Estable: **JPW-POLICY-000104**) es una plataforma de justicia digital en la nube e integrada con blockchain desarrollada por el Ministerio de la Presidencia, Justicia y Relaciones con las Cortes de España en alianza con Councilbox, Telefónica y Kawaru Consulting. La plataforma permite celebrar vistas judiciales telemáticas seguras, gestionar documentos digitales, utilizar identificación por pseudónimos para miembros de las fuerzas de seguridad y disponer de un registro de auditoría inalterable, garantizando el no repudio, la no manipulación y la no suplantación de identidad en las actuaciones judiciales.

## Base Legal

El marco normativo y técnico que regula EVID se apoya en disposiciones legislativas españolas e iniciativas del sector público de la UE:

- **Marco Español de Digitalización Judicial**: Plan de Modernización Digital del Sistema Judicial Español y los mandatos de digitalización de la Ley Orgánica del Poder Judicial (\*LOPJ\*).
- **Gobernanza del Sector Público de la UE**: Caso Public Sector Tech Watch PSTW-2160 y el Marco Europeo de Interoperabilidad.
- **Normativa de Protección de Datos**: Reglamento (UE) 2018/1725 y Reglamento General de Protección de Datos (RGPD).

## Antecedentes

El sistema judicial español requería tradicionalmente la asistencia presencial en los juzgados para las vistas, declaraciones y entrega de documentos. Esto generaba retrasos en los procesos y elevados costes de desplazamiento para abogados, ciudadanos de zonas rurales y agentes de las Fuerzas y Cuerpos de Seguridad (como la \*Guardia Civil\*), cuyo traslado obligaba en ocasiones a cerrar temporalmente cuarteles locales.

## Objetivos

1\. Agilizar los trámites judiciales mediante vistas telemáticas con plena validez jurídica. 2. Suprimir las barreras geográficas y físicas para los ciudadanos de zonas alejadas y personas con necesidades de accesibilidad. 3. Asegurar la trazabilidad y auditabilidad inalterable de las pruebas digitales mediante blockchain. 4. Proteger la seguridad y privacidad de los agentes de las fuerzas de seguridad mediante identidades seudonimizadas.

## Alcance

EVID comprende varios módulos operacionales principales: 1. *Arquitectura Cloud y Blockchain*: Proporciona un entorno en la nube escalable integrado con una capa de blockchain empresarial para registrar de forma inalterable las actividades de las vistas. 2. *Identificación por Pseudónimos*: Permite a los agentes policiales declarar telemáticamente sin exponer sus datos personales de identidad. 3. *Integración de Accesibilidad*: Incorpora herramientas adaptadas en la interfaz para facilitar la participación de personas con necesidades especiales. 4. *Flexibilidad de Despliegue*: Admite configuraciones en nube pública, SaaS privado e instalaciones \*on-premises\*.

## Disposiciones y Principios Clave

- **Registro de Auditoría Inalterable**: Los eventos de las vistas y las cargas de documentos quedan inscritos en un libro blockchain a prueba de manipulaciones.
- **Tres Garantías Jurídico-Técnicas**: Diseñado para garantizar el no repudio (\*no repudio\*), la no manipulación (\*no manipulación\*) y la no suplantación de identidad (\*no suplantación de identidad\*).
- **Diseño Participativo**: Las funciones se perfeccionan de forma continua mediante grupos de trabajo con jueces, letrados de la Administración de Justicia, fiscales, abogados y ciudadanos.

## Implementación

Implantado a nivel nacional por el Ministerio de la Presidencia, Justicia y Relaciones con las Cortes en alianza con Councilbox (proveedor de software), Telefónica (consultoría de telecomunicaciones y TIC) y Kawaru Consulting (gestión de la implantación).

## Problemas de Política Pública

- Compatibilizar la inmediación telemática (\*inmediación digital\*) con las normas procesales tradicionales que exigen presencia física (\*inmediación física\*).
- Mitigar riesgos de ciberataques, suplantación de identidad y accesos no autorizados durante las declaraciones telemáticas.
- Atender las diferencias de equipamiento e infraestructura tecnológica entre Comunidades Autónomas.

## Reforma

- Desarrollo continuo de programas de formación especializada para el personal judicial y profesionales del derecho.
- Planes de expansión para integrar EVID en todas las Comunidades Autónomas con competencias de justicia transferidas.
