# Financial Statement Audit Opinion

# Financial Statement Audit Opinion

Indicator 3.6 uses the audit opinion issued by the Audit Board of the Republic of Indonesia (BPK) regarding the Supreme Court's financial statements.

## Definition

An audit opinion is the professional conclusion resulting from an audit concerning the reasonableness of information presented in financial statements.

The Renstra measures this indicator using the financial statements of the preceding year (t−1).

## 2025–2029 Target

**WTP — Unqualified Opinion**

for every year from 2025 through 2029.
