## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 04 K/MIL/2000 establishes a crucial legal principle regarding the relationship between criminal charges (embezzlement/land encroachment) and civil ownership disputes (\*prejudisieel geschil\*). The Supreme Court affirmed that where land parcel ownership status remains unclear and constitutes a civil dispute falling under the authority of Civil Judges within the General Judiciary, neither the element of "unlawfulness" nor the ownership of another's property under Articles 372 and 385 of the KUHP can be legally proven. Consequently, Military Judges lack the authority to convict the Defendant prior to the resolution of ownership status within the General Judiciary, leading to the Defendant's complete acquittal of all charges.

## Case Background

The Defendant, \[Defendant\] (Sergeant Major / Serda Nrp. 572356, Babinsa Koramil 1403-15 Malili), was charged with embezzlement (Article 372 of the KUHP) or land encroachment/sale of inherited land (Article 385 item 1 of the KUHP) in Ujung Pandang between 1990 and 1991, following a report filed by witness-complainant \[Party B\] Dg. Bollo. The Defendant had controlled and managed inherited land originating from his grandfather (Djaenang / Djamu bin Laikang) based on provisional registration evidence and family lineage records. The Military Court III-16 Ujung Pandang sentenced the Defendant to 4 months and 15 days of imprisonment. On appeal, the High Military Court III Surabaya annulled that decision while independently adjudicating the case and sentencing the Defendant to 4 months and 15 days of imprisonment under Article 372 of the KUHP. The Defendant subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** Because the element of ownership—specifically who owns the land parcel—remains unclear and constitutes a civil dispute falling under the competence of Civil Judges within the General Judiciary rather than Military Judges, the criminal charges under Articles 372 and 385 of the KUHP are unproven, requiring the Defendant's total acquittal.
- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner \[Defendant\], Serda Nrp. 572356; Annulling the High Military Court III Surabaya Decision No. PTS/401/BDG/MMT.III/K/AD/II/1998 and the Military Court III-16 Ujung Pandang Decision No. PUT/027-K/MM.III-16/AD/VI/1996; and Adjudicating Independently: Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\] not legally and convincingly proven guilty of the criminal offense as charged; Acquitting the Defendant of all charges; Restoring the Defendant's rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity (rehabilitation); and Charging all procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State.
