## Overview

Supreme Court Judicial Review (\*Peninjauan Kembali / PK\*) Decision No. 78 PK/Pid/2000 annulled the Supreme Court Cassation Decision No. 1 K/Pid/2000 dated September 22, 2000, and acquitted the Petitioner for PK / Convict \[Defendant\] of all charges brought by the Public Prosecutor concerning the corruption case involving the asset swap (\*ruilslag\*) between the National Logistics Agency (Bulog) and PT Goro Batara Sakti. The Supreme Court established the legal rule that an individual serving as a Commissioner of a limited liability company cannot be held criminally liable for unlawful acts committed by the company's Board of Directors, and a Commissioner's approval given to the Board of Directors within the boundaries of supervisory authority pursuant to Law No. 1 of 1995 on Limited Liability Companies does not constitute a criminal unlawful act.

## Case Background

The Defendant, \[Defendant\] (President Commissioner / shareholder of PT Goro Batara Sakti), was indicted jointly with H.M. Ricardo Gelael (President Director of PT Goro Batara Sakti) and Prof. DR. Ir. Beddu Amang, MA (Head of Bulog) for committing corruption in annuity with the execution of the land/warehouse asset swap (\*ruilslag\*) belonging to Bulog in Kelapa Gading, North Jakarta, with replacement land assets in Marunda, North Jakarta, which caused financial losses to the State cq. Bulog amounting to Rp 95,407,486,000.

The South Jakarta District Court (Decision No. 198/Pid.B/1999/PN.Jak.Sel. dated October 14, 1999) ruled that the charged acts were proven but constituted a civil legal relationship (\*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging\* / released from all legal prosecutions). Upon the Public Prosecutor's cassation appeal, the Supreme Court at the Cassation level (Decision No. 1 K/Pid/2000 dated September 22, 2000) annulled the South Jakarta District Court decision, independently adjudicated the matter, declared the Defendant guilty of continuous corruption, and sentenced him to 1 (one) year and 6 (six) months of imprisonment, a fine of Rp 10,000,000, and restitution of Rp 30,680,306,400. The Defendant subsequently filed a petition for Judicial Review (\*PK\*) to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  An individual who no longer holds office as a Commissioner of a company cannot be held accountable for unlawful acts committed by that company, let alone actions occurring prior to or during their tenure if outside personal operational execution.
2.  The legal act of \*ruilslag\* was executed by the Board of Directors of PT Goro Batara Sakti (Ricardo Gelael) acting for and on behalf of the company, whereas the Defendant served as President Commissioner whose fundamental function under the Deed of Incorporation jo. Article 100 of Law No. 1 of 1995 on Limited Liability Companies is to perform supervision over the management of the company run by the Board of Directors. A Commissioner does not manage the operational affairs of the company and cannot be held criminally liable for the actions of the Board of Directors.
3.  The approval granted by a Commissioner to the Board of Directors to execute certain legal actions does not contravene Article 100 paragraph (1) of Law No. 1 of 1995 on Limited Liability Companies, and no unlawful act was proven under Article 1(1) sub a jo. Article 28 of Law No. 3 of 1971 jo. Article 55(1) item 1 jo. Article 64(1) of the KUHP.

- **Ruling:** Granting the petition for Judicial Review (\*PK\*) from Petitioner for Judicial Review / Convict \[Defendant\]; Annulling the Supreme Court decision dated September 22, 2000, No. 1 K/Pid/2000; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring the guilt of Defendant \[Defendant\] regarding the acts charged against him NOT PROVEN legally and convincingly; (2) Acquitting the Defendant of all charges brought by the Public Prosecutor (\*vrijspraak\*); (3) Ordering the restoration of the Defendant's rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity; (4) Ordering that evidence items in the form of documents be returned to the Public Prosecutor to be utilized in other proceedings; (5) Charging procedural costs in this Judicial Review examination to the State.
