# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:3574K PDT 2000

**Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 3574 K/Pdt/2000** (dated September 5, 2002) is a fundamental civil law and Islamic inheritance law jurisprudence precedent establishing two vital rules concerning the limitation of heirs' liability for a decedent's debts and the legal protection of a wife's separate property (*harta bawaan*) from attachment over a husband's debts.

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established two primary legal principles:

1.  **Heirs' Liability Limited to the Estate Value:** An heir's liability for a decedent's debts is strictly limited to the amount or value of the decedent's estate (pursuant to Article 175 paragraph (2) of the Compilation of Islamic Law / KHI).
2.  **Protection of a Wife's Separate Property from Attachments over Husband's Debts:** A wife's separate property (*harta bawaan*) cannot be attached as collateral for her deceased husband's debts, as separate property does not constitute the deceased husband's estate.

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioner for Cassation (Ny. \[Party B\]), annulled Central Java High Court in Semarang Decision No. 586/Pdt/1999/PT.Smg dated February 28, 2000, *jo.* Magelang District Court Decision No. 28/Pdt.G/1998/PN.Mgl dated June 15, 1999, and independently adjudicated (*mengadili sendiri*) by declaring the Plaintiff's lawsuit in Convention Inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO), ordering the lifting of the attachment (*sita jaminan*) over the land and house at \[address removed\], Magelang, and declaring the Counterclaim in Reconvention Inadmissible (NO).

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Limitations on Heirs' Liability for a Decedent's Debts / Obligations (Article 175 paragraph (2) of the Compilation of Islamic Law / KHI)

*Are heirs (wife and children) obligated to pay off all debts of a deceased debtor using their own personal or separate property if the decedent's estate is insufficient or unspecified in the lawsuit?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Limited Liability (*Intra Vires Hereditatis*).** Pursuant to Article 175 paragraph (2) of the Compilation of Islamic Law (KHI), an heir's liability for a decedent's debt is strictly limited to the amount or value of the estate left by the decedent. Heirs cannot be compelled to pay a decedent's debts exceeding the net value of the decedent's estate.

### 2. Legal Protection of a Wife's Separate Property Against Attachment for Husband's Debts (Article 35 paragraph (2) & Article 36 of Law No. 1 of 1974 on Marriage)

*Can land and a house acquired by a wife prior to marriage (*harta bawaan*) be attached or sold by creditors to settle business debts incurred by her late husband?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Cannot Be Attached.** Separate property acquired by a wife prior to marriage remains under her exclusive control and ownership. Such property neither constitutes joint marital property (*harta bersama*) nor forms part of her late husband's estate. Consequently, conservatory attachments (*conservatoir beslag*) over a wife's separate property to satisfy a late husband's debts are null and void by operation of law and must be lifted by the court.

### 3. Formal Defect in Petitum for Decedent Debt Lawsuits (*Obscuur Libel* / Execution Defect)

A lawsuit demanding settlement of a late husband's debts from heirs without specifying and detailing the exact amount or value of the decedent's estate is classified as vague and defective, requiring courts to declare the lawsuit **Inadmissible** (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO).

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the *Judex Facti* Semarang High Court (Decision No. 586/Pdt/1999/PT.Smg) and Magelang District Court (Decision No. 28/Pdt.G/1998/PN.Mgl) err in applying the law (*foutieve toepassing van het recht*) by ordering heirs to pay a late decedent's debt of Rp 114,500,000 and declaring valid an attachment over a wife's separate property home (SHM No. 348, \[address removed\], Magelang), whereas Article 175(2) KHI limits heirs' liability to estate value and Article 35(2) of Law No. 1 of 1974 protects separate property?

**Supreme Court Legal Answer:** Yes. The *Judex Facti* misapplied the law. The Supreme Court granted Ny. \[Party B\]'s cassation petition, annulled the lower court decisions, and independently adjudicated by declaring the Plaintiff's lawsuit Inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO) and ordering the lifting of the attachment over the Petitioner's separate property.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Magelang District Court & Semarang High Court)

Verification of the project cooperation/loan agreement dated October 2, 1996, amounting to Rp 114,500,000 between \[Party A\] and the late Singgih Sumarsono, S.H.; verification of the death of Singgih Sumarsono, S.H. during court proceedings; verification of SHM No. 348 in South Rejowinangun Sub-district in the name of Ny. \[Party B\] acquired prior to marriage with the decedent; and verification of the Magelang District Court Attachment Minutes dated December 16, 1998 (No. 28/Pdt.G/1998/PN.Mgl).

## Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

### Statutory Regulations

- **Compilation of Islamic Law (KHI):** Article 175 paragraph (2) (Heirs' Liability Limited to Decedent Estate Value).
- **Law No. 1 of 1974 on Marriage:** Article 35 paragraph (2) (Separate Property Under Respective Control) & Article 36 paragraph (2) (Exclusive Rights Over Separate Property).
- **HIR (Herziene Inlandsch Reglement):** Article 227 (Conditions for Conservatory Attachment / *Conservatoir Beslag*).
- **Law No. 14 of 1970 *jo.* Law No. 35 of 1999 *jo.* Law No. 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court:** Cassation and Independent Adjudication Authority.

### Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- **Doctrine of Limited Heirs' Liability (*Intra Vires Hereditatis*):** Heirs bear no personal liability for a decedent's debts using personal assets. Decedents' debts are satisfied exclusively out of the decedent's estate pool (*boedel waris*).
- **Doctrine of Separate Property Protection (Separation of Ante-Nuptial Property):** Separate property acquired prior to marriage cannot be attached to satisfy obligations or debts incurred by a spouse without the separate property owner's explicit consent.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Importance of Inventorying Net Decedents' Estates Before Filing Debt Lawsuits:** This landmark ruling establishes a crucial civil procedural principle for creditors in Indonesia: when a debtor passes away, creditors cannot simply sue heirs and attach arbitrary assets occupied by a grieving family. Creditors must investigate and prove precisely what constitutes the net estate (*boedel waris*) of the late debtor. Targeting separate property or personal assets of heirs results in fatal formal defects, leading to lawsuit dismissal (NO) and the lifting of attachments.

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Can a wife's separate property be attached to pay her late husband's debts, and what are the limits of heirs' liability for a decedent's debts pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence?  
**Cannot Be Attached & Limited Liability.** Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence Reg. No. 3574 K/Pdt/2000:

- A wife's separate property (*harta bawaan*) **cannot be attached as collateral for her late husband's debts**, as separate property does not form part of the late husband's estate.
- An heir's liability for a decedent's debt is **strictly limited to the amount or value of the decedent's estate** pursuant to Article 175 paragraph (2) of the Compilation of Islamic Law (KHI).
- Creditor lawsuits targeting a wife's separate property without clarifying estate value limits contain formal defects, prompting the Supreme Court to annul appellate rulings and independently adjudicate by declaring the lawsuit Inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard* / NO) and ordering the lifting of attachments.
