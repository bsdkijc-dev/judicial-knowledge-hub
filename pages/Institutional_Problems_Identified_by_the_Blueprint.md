# Institutional Problems Identified by the Blueprint

The Blueprint identifies institutional problems through evaluation and organizational diagnostic assessment.

## Major Problem Clusters

- uneven understanding of institutional vision and mission;
- organizational capability;
- overlapping functions;
- workload distribution;
- organizational culture;
- case workload;
- consistency of judgments;
- human resources management;
- financial management;
- infrastructure;
- information technology;
- supervision;
- information transparency.

## Historical Status

These findings represent the institutional conditions assessed during the preparation of the 2010 Blueprint.

They must not automatically be treated as current conditions.
