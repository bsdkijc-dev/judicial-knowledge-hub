# Benchbook: Pretrial Phase

# Chapter 1: Pretrial Phase Requirements

*Supreme Court Authorized Benchbook Protocols*

## Fast-Track Scheduling Rules

All standard civil actions fall under a 120-day strict window for pretrial discovery phases. Extensions require an exceptional-cause motion signed by the administrative judge.

## Filing Deadlines

- **Initial Disclosures:** Must be submitted within 14 days of the initial status conference.
- **Motion Practice:** All dispositive motions must be filed no later than 30 days before the scheduled trial date.
