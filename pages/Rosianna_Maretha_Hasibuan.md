# Rosianna Maretha Hasibuan

## Contributor Profile

**Rosianna Maretha Hasibuan** is a core research contributor at the Judicial Knowledge Hub, specializing in empirical data ingestion, regulatory entity verification, and knowledge graph architecture for the BSDK Mahkamah Agung RI corpus.

### Portal Navigation

- [Main Page](Main_Page.md) \> [Rosianna Maretha Hasibuan](Rosianna_Maretha_Hasibuan.md)

### Research Scope & Contributions

- \*\*Data Ingestion & Verification\*\*: Managing entity tracking, regulatory framework indexes, and repository compliance within the JPAIS architecture\[cite: 3\].
- \*\*Knowledge Graph Analysis\*\*: Structuring comparative law documents and digital records.
