# Judgment Publication in the Decision Directory

Indicator 1.6 measures the percentage of court judgments uploaded to the Supreme Court Decision Directory.

## Formula

\<math\> \frac{\text{Judgments uploaded to the Decision Directory}} {\text{Judgments finalized}}\times100\\ \</math\>

## Strategic Purpose

The indicator supports public access to judicial decisions and is presented as a mechanism for information transparency and institutional accountability.
