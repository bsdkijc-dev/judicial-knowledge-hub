# AI Audit Framework

# AI Audit Framework

The AI Audit Framework is proposed to provide systematic assessment of judicial AI systems.

## Audit Dimensions

- purpose;
- data;
- model/service;
- performance;
- risk;
- security;
- human oversight;
- explainability;
- traceability;
- incident management.

## Lifecycle

**Pre-deployment → Operational Audit → Periodic Review**
