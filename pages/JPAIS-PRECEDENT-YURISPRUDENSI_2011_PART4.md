# JPAIS:PRECEDENT:YURISPRUDENSI 2011 PART4

**Supreme Court Landmark Decisions Compendium (2011 Part 4)** (encompassing Supreme Court Decisions Reg. Nos. 154 PK/TUN/2010 and 216 K/TUN/2010) represents a pivotal compilation of Supreme Court jurisprudence established under Chief Justice Decree No. 096/KMA/SK/VII/2011. It consolidates strategic landmark rulings across State Administrative Law (TUN), addressing the limitation of multiple Reconsideration (*Peninjauan Kembali* / PK) motions in land administrative disputes (corrective justice) and the absolute exclusive authority of the President of the Republic of Indonesia over the dismissal or temporary suspension of Principal Functional Officials (*Widyaiswara Utama* Grade IV/e).

## Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established two primary legal principles:

1.  **Restriction of Multiple Reconsideration (PK) Motions & Absolute Competence of Civil Courts:** "In the event of a Reconsideration (*Peninjauan Kembali*) application filed more than one (1) time, it must be evaluated on a case-by-case basis, and in this case, the legally valid reconsideration is the first one." Reconsideration is an extraordinary legal remedy permitted only once *ex* Article 66 paragraph (1) of Law No. 14 of 1985 *jo.* Law No. 3 of 2009 and Article 23 paragraph (2) of Law No. 4 of 2004. The invalidation of a State Administrative Decision (such as land certificates SHM/SHGB) does not automatically extinguish civil rights over land; land ownership verification remains within the absolute competence of General (Civil) Courts, and Administrative Officials must base remedial decisions on civil court rulings.
2.  **Exclusive Presidential Authority Over Principal Functional Officials (Grade IV/e):** "Only the President possesses the right to dismiss and/or temporarily suspend Civil Servants holding Principal Functional Positions / Principal Educators (*Widyaiswara Utama* Grade IV/e) from and in their positions." A Ministerial Decree (Minister of Finance Decree No. 113/KMK.01/UP.11 2009) temporarily suspending Drs. Achyar Rusli, Ak., M.A. from the position of Principal Educator Grade IV/e is qualified as legally flawed because it was enacted outside the Minister's authority (*ultravires*) under Law No. 43 of 1999, Gov. Reg. No. 9 of 2003, and Joint Regulation of the Head of the National Civil Service Agency (*LAN & BKN*).

The Supreme Court granted the Reconsideration application of PT Lippo Karawaci, Tbk., annulled the second Reconsideration ruling No. 49 PK/TUN/2007, and affirmed the first Reconsideration ruling No. 20 PK/TUN/2003 which declared the Plaintiff's lawsuit inadmissible (*niet ontvankelijk verklaard*). Simultaneously, the Supreme Court rejected the Minister of Finance's cassation petition and affirmed rulings canceling the Ministerial Suspension Decree.

## Legal / Procedural Issues

### 1. Constitutionality and Legal Limits of Multiple Reconsideration (PK) Filings in Administrative Law (Article 66(1) Law No. 14 of 1985 *jo.* Law No. 3 of 2009 & Article 23(2) Law No. 4 of 2004)

*Is it permissible in land disputes within Administrative Courts to file multiple Reconsideration (PK) motions, and which ruling prevails when conflicting Reconsideration rulings emerge?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Permitted Only Once / First PK Prevails.** The Reconsideration institution is an extraordinary legal remedy grounded in corrective justice and legal certainty (*litis finis imponi oportet*), permitting filing only once. Where two conflicting Reconsideration rulings arise over the same object and subject, the Supreme Court annuls the second ruling and establishes that the first Reconsideration ruling is legally valid.

### 2. Absolute Competence of Civil Courts Over Land Ownership Post-Annulment of Administrative Decisions (Article 55 Law No. 5 of 1986)

The invalidation of an administrative decision regarding land certificates (SHM/SHGB) by an Administrative Court does not automatically extinguish civil rights over land. Civil ownership rights remain intact until a General (Civil) Court issues a permanent ruling (*inkracht*) determining lawful land ownership.

### 3. Legality of Government Action (*Bestuursbevoegdheid*) & Ultravires Principle (Article 25 Law No. 43 of 1999 *jo.* Gov. Reg. No. 9 of 2003)

*Does the Minister of Finance possess authority to issue a temporary suspension decree against a Civil Servant holding a Principal Functional Position (Principal Educator Grade IV/e)?*

**Supreme Court Rule: Minister Lacks Authority / Ultravires.** Pursuant to Article 25 of Law No. 43 of 1999 *jo.* Article 11 of Gov. Reg. No. 9 of 2003, appointment, dismissal, and temporary suspension of Civil Servants holding Principal Functional Positions (Grade IV/e) fall under the absolute authority of the President. Ministerial decrees suspending Principal Grade Civil Servants are legally flawed due to lack of authority (*gebrek aan bevoegdheid*) and are null and void.

## Question of Law vs Question of Fact

### Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

- *Lippo Karawaci Case:* Did the Second Reconsideration Panel (Decision No. 49 PK/TUN/2007) commit a judicial error (*foutieve toepassing van het procesrecht*) by granting a second reconsideration over a dispute finalized in the first reconsideration (No. 20 PK/TUN/2003)?

  
**Supreme Court Answer:** Yes. A judicial error occurred. Reconsideration is permitted only once. The Supreme Court annulled the second PK and reinstated the first PK ruling as valid.

- *Principal Educator Case:* Did the Minister of Finance possess authority to issue a temporary suspension decree against Drs. Achyar Rusli, Ak., M.A. (Principal Educator Grade IV/e)?

  
**Supreme Court Answer:** No. Authority over Grade IV/e Principal Officials rests exclusively with the President. The Supreme Court rejected the Minister of Finance's cassation petition.

### Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Bandung Administrative Court, Jakarta Administrative Court, Jakarta High Administrative Court)

Verification of SHM No. 39/Bencongan measuring 4,223 m² in the name of Amin Lembe dated December 19, 1992, and Auction Minutes No. 37/1996; HGB Certificate No. 6957/Bencongan measuring 4,400 m² in the name of PT Tunggal Reksa Kencana (PT Lippo Karawaci Tbk) dated October 26, 1995; local land inspections (*decharge*); Ministry of Finance Decree No. 113/KMK.01/UP.11 2009 dated April 1, 2009; and DUPAK credit point calculations for Principal Educators.

## Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

### Statutory Regulations

- **Law No. 14 of 1985 *jo.* Law No. 5 of 2004 *jo.* Law No. 3 of 2009 on the Supreme Court:** Article 66 paragraph (1) & Article 67 (Single Reconsideration Limit).
- **Law No. 5 of 1986 *jo.* Law No. 9 of 2004 *jo.* Law No. 51 of 2009 on State Administrative Courts:** Articles 53 paragraph (2), 55 (Statutes of Limitation & Grounds for Administrative Lawsuits).
- **Law No. 43 of 1999 on Civil Service Principles:** Article 25 (Presidential vs. Appointing Official Authority).
- **Gov. Reg. No. 9 of 2003 on Appointment, Transfer, and Dismissal of Civil Servants:** Articles 11 & 12.
- **Joint Regulation of LAN & BKN No. 7 / No. 17 of 2005:** Principal Educator Functional Position Rules.

### Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

- **Supreme Court Decision No. 021 PK/PDT.SUS/2009 (March 25, 2009):** Landmark precedent establishing the strict single Reconsideration rule (*strict single PK rule*).
- **Corrective Justice Doctrine in Administrative Adjudication:** Rectifying judicial errors via a second reconsideration is executed strictly to halt conflicting execution rulings and restore judicial legal certainty.

## What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

### Established by Evidence

- First Reconsideration Ruling No. 20 PK/TUN/2003 dated June 11, 2004, is **PROVEN VALID AND BINDING AS THE FINAL COMPLETED RULING**.
- Second Reconsideration Ruling No. 49 PK/TUN/2007 is **PROVEN INVALID AND ANNULLED FOR VIOLATING THE SINGLE PK RULE**.
- Land overlap disputes between SHM No. 39 and HGB No. 6957 are **PROVEN TO CONSTITUTE CIVIL OWNERSHIP DISPUTES REQUIRING GENERAL COURT ADJUDICATION**.
- Minister of Finance Decree No. 113/KMK.01/UP.11 2009 is **PROVEN LEGALLY FLAWED (*ULTRAVIRES*) AND NULL AND VOID**.
- Temporary suspension of Principal Educators Grade IV/e is **PROVEN TO BE THE EXCLUSIVE AUTHORITY OF THE PRESIDENT OF INDONESIA**.

### Dismissed Contentions (Minister of Finance's Defense)

- The Minister of Finance's contention that ministerial authority extended to suspending Grade IV/e officials based on LAN technical guidelines was **REJECTED ENTIRELY BY THE SUPREME COURT AS CONFLICTING WITH GOV. REG. NO. 9/2003 AND LAW NO. 43/1999**.

## Uncertainties and Gaps

**Reconsideration Legal Certainty & Civil Service Functional Protection:** These landmark administrative rulings establish two unyielding pillars of legal certainty in Indonesia: (1) The Indonesian judicial system rejects protracted litigation through multiple Reconsideration applications; the first PK is final and mandatory; and (2) State administrative officials (Ministers) must comply with statutory hierarchies and are strictly barred from encroaching upon Presidential authority over Principal Functional Officials.

## Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Does the Minister of Finance possess authority to temporarily suspend Principal Educators Grade IV/e, and can Reconsideration applications be filed more than once pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence?  
**Minister Lacks Authority & Reconsideration Permitted Only Once.** Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence Reg. Nos. 154 PK/TUN/2010 and 216 K/TUN/2010:

- Only the President possesses the right to dismiss and/or temporarily suspend Civil Servants holding Principal Functional / Principal Educator Positions (Grade IV/e). Ministerial suspension decrees are null and void as *ultravires*.
- Reconsideration (PK) is an extraordinary legal remedy permitted only one time. Where multiple conflicting Reconsideration rulings occur, the first Reconsideration ruling is legally valid.
- The Supreme Court annulled second Reconsideration rulings and affirmed that land ownership disputes must be settled in Civil Courts.
