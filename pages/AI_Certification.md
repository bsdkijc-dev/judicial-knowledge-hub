# AI Certification

**AI certification** is a formal mechanism for recognizing demonstrated competence in specified areas of judicial AI use or governance.

This page describes a conceptual framework, not an existing certification program.

## Possible Certification Levels

| Level        | Capability                             |
|--------------|----------------------------------------|
| Foundation   | AI awareness                           |
| Practitioner | Responsible AI-assisted work           |
| Advanced     | AI evaluation and workflow integration |
| Specialist   | AI governance and technical evaluation |
| Leadership   | Institutional AI strategy              |

## Certification Requirements

Potential requirements:

- Training
- Examination
- Practical assessment
- Continuing education
- Ethical commitment

## Validity

Certification systems should define:

- Validity period
- Renewal
- Continuing professional development
- Revocation
- Appeals

## Principle

Certification should demonstrate competence, not merely attendance at an AI course.
