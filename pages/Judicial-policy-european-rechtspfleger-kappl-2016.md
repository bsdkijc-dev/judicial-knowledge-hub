# Judicial-policy-european-rechtspfleger-kappl-2016

**The Thomas Kappl European Rechtspfleger Proposal Framework** is a judicial governance and harmonization policy framework for senior non-judge judicial officers (*Rechtspfleger*) in Europe. Formulated by Thomas Kappl (Former President of the European Union of Rechtspfleger / EUR) in the *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA), Vol. 8 No. 1 (October 2016), the framework proposes the creation of a *European Rechtspfleger* to handle cross-border uncontested legal matters, European land and commercial registries, the execution of judgments, and the management of court backlogs caused by the economic crisis in Europe \[1–6\].

## Summary

Published by Thomas Kappl in the IJCA (October 2016), this policy framework proposes the establishment of a transnational senior judicial officer position (the *European Rechtspfleger*). Although European integration in the fields of economics and immigration has developed rapidly, the resolution of uncontested legal matters that cross national borders remains time-consuming, complicated, and expensive. The EUR formulated the *Green Paper for a European Rechtspfleger*, which was discussed with justice ministries across 17 European countries and presented by EUR President Jean-Jacques Kuster to the Legal Committee of the European Parliament. This policy aims to grant the *Rechtspfleger* the authority to manage property registrations, business registrations, European Inheritance Certificates, and cross-border judgment executions directly from a citizen's hometown.

## Legal Basis

- **EUR Green Paper for a European Rechtspfleger:** The primary policy document championed by the EUR to initiate discussions on the harmonization of the *Rechtspfleger* profession in Europe.
- **CEPEJ Report "European Judicial Systems – 2014 Edition":** The official report by the Council of Europe that included discussions on the *European Rechtspfleger* proposal.
- **European Union Legal Harmonization Initiatives:** The framework for establishing European Inheritance Certificates, the European Commercial Registry, and the European Land Registry.
- **Not found in the available sources:** Other specific formal statutory regulations are not mentioned in the source text \[FACT\].

## Background

European citizens conducting cross-border transactions (such as purchasing a home in another country or opening a branch office) face bureaucratic hurdles in civil law that are disjointed across borders.

## Objectives

1\. Facilitate fast, affordable, and transparent access to justice for European citizens in uncontested matters. 2. Encourage the establishment of integrated European land and commercial registries. 3. Assist courts in untangling the backlog of insolvency cases and forced sales resulting from the European economic crisis.

## Scope

This policy encompasses 17 member states of the European Union/Europe and 2 non-European countries (Japan and South Korea) involved in the EUR discussions.

## Key Provisions

- **Jurisdiction over Uncontested Matters:**
  - Transferring authority over family law, probate/inheritance, property registration, and business licensing from judges to the *Rechtspfleger*.
- **The "Live Europe" Principle:**
  - Empowering citizens to complete foreign legal transactions through a *Rechtspfleger* located in their city of residence.
- **Integrated Registry Harmonization:**
  - The creation of a unified European Land Register and European Commercial Register.

## Implementation

- **Submission to the European Parliament:** The presentation of the *EUR Green Paper* by Jean-Jacques Kuster to the Legal Committee of the European Parliament.
- **Formulation of the EUR White Paper:** The drafting of concrete proposals as the next step in public and professional discourse.

## Policy Problems

- **Court Backlogs:** A surge in applications for forced sales and insolvencies following the European economic crisis.
- **Disparate Legal Systems:** Not all EU member states currently recognize the position of a *Rechtspfleger* within their judicial systems (e.g., common law systems).

## Reform / Change

- Drafting concrete proposals in the *EUR White Paper* to establish formal EU regulations.
- Accelerating the harmonization of electronic, integrated land and business registration procedures.

### Deutsch (Schweizer Hochdeutsch)

**Rahmenwerk für den Europäischen Rechtspfleger von Thomas Kappl** Dieser von Thomas Kappl im *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA, 2016) veröffentlichte Rahmen schlägt die Schaffung eines "Europäischen Rechtspflegers" vor. Obwohl Europa wirtschaftlich stark integriert ist, sind grenzüberschreitende Angelegenheiten der freiwilligen Gerichtsbarkeit (unbestrittene Rechtsangelegenheiten) oft komplex und teuer. Das Grünbuch der EUR schlägt vor, dass Europäische Rechtspfleger grenzüberschreitende Grundbuch- und Handelsregistereinträge, europäische Nachlasszeugnisse sowie Zwangsvollstreckungen direkt vom Heimatort des Bürgers aus bearbeiten. Dies soll die Effizienz steigern und Gerichte von Rückständen entlasten, die durch die Wirtschaftskrise entstanden sind.

- **Rechtsgrundlage:** EUR-Grünbuch für einen Europäischen Rechtspfleger; CEPEJ-Bericht 2014.

### Français

**Cadre de proposition du Greffier européen (Rechtspfleger) de Thomas Kappl** Publié par Thomas Kappl dans l' *International Journal for Court Administration* (IJCA, 2016), ce cadre propose la création d'un "Rechtspfleger européen" (greffier en chef / officier de justice). Bien que l'Europe soit économiquement intégrée, les questions juridiques transfrontalières non contentieuses restent complexes et coûteuses. L'objectif est d'accroître l'efficacité et de soulager les tribunaux des arriérés causés par la crise économique.

- **Base Légale:** Livre vert de l'EUR pour un Rechtspfleger européen ; Rapport de la CEPEJ 2014.
