# MediaWiki Namespace Architecture

The Judicial Knowledge Wiki should separate knowledge content, governance metadata, technical documentation and operational records.

## Proposed Logical Areas

- Main — public knowledge
- Knowledge — structured knowledge objects
- Case — judicial cases
- Judgment — judgments and decisions
- Law — legislation and regulations
- Institution — judicial institutions
- Policy — judicial policies
- Initiative — strategic initiatives
- Research — research and evidence
- Training — learning resources
- Glossary — controlled terminology
- Help — user guidance

## Governance

The exact technical namespace configuration should follow the capabilities and administrative policies of the Miraheze installation.
