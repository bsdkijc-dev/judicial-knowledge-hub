## Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 02 K/AG/1985 establishes an important legal principle regarding the marriage requirements for an adult woman holding the status of a widow. The Supreme Court outlined that for the validity of a marriage involving a woman \[age removed\] who is a widow, parental consent is not required, and guardianship is not absolute or strictly mandatory under Islamic Law.

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff (\[Party C\]) filed a lawsuit for marriage annulment (fasid marriage) at the Brebes Religious Court against Defendant I (\[Party A\]) and Defendant II (\[Party B\]). The lawsuit was grounded on the premise that the marriage between Defendant I and Defendant II—solemnized on October 12, 1981, under Marriage Certificate No. 323/49/VI/1981—was conducted without parental consent and utilized an invalid guardian. The Brebes Religious Court (Decision No. 471/1984) and the Surakarta High Religious Court (Decision No. 27/1984) both granted the claim and annulled the marriage. Dissatisfied with these rulings, Defendants I and II filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** For the validity of a marriage of an adult woman \[age removed\] holding widow status, parental consent is not required, and the issue of guardianship is not absolute or strictly necessary. Because the relevant lineage guardian (wali nasab) explicitly stated no objection to the marriage, the marriage performed is valid according to Islamic Law.
- **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition of Petitioners (1. \[Party A\], 2. \[Party B\]); annulling the Surakarta High Religious Court Decision No. 27/1984 and the Brebes Religious Court Decision No. 471/1984; and Independently Adjudicating by rejecting the Plaintiff's claim in its entirety.
