## Overview

Supreme Court Judicial Review Decision No. 01 PK/Mil/2003 adjudicates a military criminal case involving the Defendant, SERTU \[Defendant\], who was charged with unauthorized bigamy under Article 279 paragraph (1) item 1 of the KUHP. The Supreme Court annulled the prior rulings of the lower instances following the discovery of new evidence (novum) demonstrating that the contracted secondary marriage was legally invalid under religious law due to a failure to meet essential pillars (namely, the absence of a lawful marriage guardian and qualified witnesses). Because the marriage was invalid, the statutory elements of the crime under Article 279 KUHP were unfulfilled, resulting in the Defendant's complete acquittal and full rehabilitation.

## Case Background

The Defendant, SERTU \[Defendant\] (NRP 64040970, BASATSABHARA Poltabes Ujung Pandang), was charged with committing the crime of "Entering into a marriage knowing that an existing marriage constitutes a valid legal impediment" under Article 279 paragraph (1) item 1 of the KUHP. At the first instance, the Military Court III-16 Ujung Pandang sentenced the Defendant to 3 months of imprisonment and dismissal from the armed forces/police service. On appeal, the High Military Court III Surabaya modified the sentence to 4 months of imprisonment alongside dismissal. The Supreme Court subsequently rejected the Defendant's cassation petition. The Defendant then filed a Petition for Judicial Review (PK) accompanied by new evidence (novum) in the form of a Marriage Statement Letter, proving that the informal marriage with Witness II, Yuniar, was invalid due to the absence of a guardian and witnesses.

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:** Because the Defendant's marriage to Yuniar was invalid due to the absence of a lawful Guardian and Witnesses, the elements of the charge under Article 279 were unfulfilled, requiring the annulment of the Supreme Court decision jo. the High Military Court II Surabaya decision jo. the Military Court III-16 Ujung Pandang decision.
- **Ruling:** Granting the petition for judicial review from the Judicial Review Petitioner / Convict \[Defendant\], SERTU NRP 64040970; Annulling the Supreme Court Decision No. 43 K/MIL/1998, High Military Court III Surabaya Decision No. PTS/425/BDG/MMT.III/K/POL/IX/1997, and Military Court III-16 Ujung Pandang Decision No. PUT/032-K/MM II-16/VII/1997; and Adjudicating Independently: Declaring Defendant \[Defendant\], SERTU NRP 64040970 not legally and convincingly proven guilty as charged; Acquitting the Defendant from all charges; Restoring the Defendant's rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity (rehabilitation); and Charging all judicial costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State.
