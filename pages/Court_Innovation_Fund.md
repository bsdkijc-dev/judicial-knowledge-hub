# Court Innovation Fund

# Court Innovation Fund

The Court Innovation Fund is proposed to support controlled experimentation and evaluated innovation.

## Funding Logic

**Proposal → Pilot → Evaluation → Scale / Stop**

## Principle

Funding should be linked to measurable outcomes rather than novelty alone.
