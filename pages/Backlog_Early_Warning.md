# Backlog Early Warning

# Backlog Early Warning

Backlog Early Warning is proposed to identify emerging workload pressures before they become systemic backlogs.

## Strategic Function

**Detect → Alert → Investigate → Intervene**

## Safeguard

Alerts should support administrative management and should not be used to determine judicial outcomes.
