# Judicial-policy-ejpr-issue-info-2025

**European Journal of Political Research (Vol. 64, No. 4, November 2025)** is the official publication information document, editorial governance structure, contributor guidelines, and table of contents for Volume 64, Issue 4 (November 2025) of the *European Journal of Political Research* (EJPR). The journal is published four times a year on behalf of the European Consortium for Political Research (ECPR) by John Wiley & Sons Ltd.

## Summary

This document sets out EJPR's editorial leadership structure, led by seven editors (Isabelle Borucki, Nicole Curato, Caterina Froio, Emilie van Haute, Airo Hino, Alessandro Nai, and Markus Wagner), the technical guidelines for article submission ("Notes for Contributors"), and the full table of contents for Volume 64, Issue 4, which comprises 29 scholarly works (22 full research articles and 7 research notes) \[2, 3, 4, 23–32\]. This issue covers major topics in comparative European politics, including democratic backsliding, EU redistribution, public consultation participation, candidate-level voting advice applications, affective polarization, and the urban–rural divide \[2, 3, 23–32\].

## Background

EJPR was established as ECPR's official scholarly journal to publish comparative empirical and theoretical research in political science. It is published on a regular schedule in February, May, August, and November.

## Objectives

1\. Publish original, substantive contributions to comparative European political studies. 2. Provide rigorous editorial standards and methodological criteria for the global political science community. 3. Facilitate Open Access publishing through a hybrid Creative Commons licensing scheme.

## Scope

The journal's coverage spans all subfields of comparative political science, encompassing quantitative and qualitative approaches, review articles, research notes, and forum sections.

## Key Provisions & Contributor Guidelines

- **Maximum Word Limit:** Manuscripts may not exceed **10,000 words** (including main text, footnotes, tables, figures, and references).
- **Abstract Limit:** Maximum of **500 words**.
- **File Format:** Must be compatible with Microsoft Office; PDF and LaTeX submissions are **not accepted**.
- **Referencing Style:** Harvard style of referencing.
- **Submission Portal:** All submissions are made online via Manuscript Central (\`http://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/ejpr\`).

Editorial management is handled by Charlie Brown (\`\[email removed\]\`), while the production process is managed by Amar Singh at John Wiley & Sons Ltd. (\`\[email removed\]\`). ## Policy Issues

1\. Automatic rejection of manuscripts that exceed the word limit. 2. The challenge of integrating comparative research drawn from a range of quantitative and qualitative methodologies.
