# Initiative Evaluation

# Initiative Evaluation

Evaluation determines whether an initiative produces its intended results.

## Questions

- Did the intervention work?
- For whom?
- Under what conditions?
- At what cost?
- With what risks?
- Compared with what baseline?
- Should it be scaled?
