# Initiative Risk Tier

# Initiative Risk Tier

Each strategic initiative should receive a documented risk classification before implementation.

## Risk Dimensions

- legal;
- institutional;
- fundamental rights;
- privacy;
- cybersecurity;
- operational;
- reputational;
- financial;
- technological.

## Rule

Higher-risk initiatives require stronger governance and evaluation.
