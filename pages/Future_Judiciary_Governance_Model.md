# Future Judiciary Governance Model

# Future Judiciary Governance Model

The future judiciary requires governance capable of coordinating law, technology, data, people and service design.

## Proposed Layers

- strategic judicial leadership;
- AI governance;
- capability and knowledge;
- technology and data;
- court implementation;
- independent evaluation.

## Principle

Governance should be sufficiently centralised to establish standards and sufficiently decentralised to enable learning.
