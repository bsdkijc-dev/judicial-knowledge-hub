# Mission of the Supreme Court

# Mission of the Supreme Court

The Renstra identifies four principal missions:

1.  **Safeguarding the independence of the judiciary**
2.  **Providing equitable legal services to justice seekers**
3.  **Improving judicial leadership quality**
4.  **Increasing judicial credibility and transparency**

## Strategic Function

The missions translate the institutional vision into strategic directions for action.
