# Mediation

# Mediation

**Mediation** is a dispute-resolution process in which a neutral third party assists disputing parties in seeking a mutually acceptable resolution.

The legal framework, mandatory character, procedure, and effect of mediation vary by jurisdiction and type of case.

**\[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED\]**

## Mediation Process

A generic model is:

**REFERRAL** → **OPENING** → **ISSUE IDENTIFICATION** → **NEGOTIATION** → **AGREEMENT / NO AGREEMENT**

Actual procedural requirements must be verified.

## Judicial Mediation

Where mediation is integrated into judicial proceedings, knowledge objects should document:

- Legal basis
- Eligible cases
- Referral mechanism
- Mediator role
- Time limits
- Agreement
- Enforcement
- Failure to settle

## AI and Mediation

Potential low-risk applications include:

- Scheduling
- Translation
- Administrative support
- Document organization
- Information retrieval

AI should not improperly replace the human role in mediation or coercively determine settlement outcomes.
