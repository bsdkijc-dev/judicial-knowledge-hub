# JPW-POLICY-000021

The **Vietnam Online Trial Framework** (Stable ID: `JPW-POLICY-000021`) is a national statutory, procedural, technical, and operational policy framework regulating the organization, technology infrastructure, connection point limits, evidentiary rules, and courtroom protocols for online trials across criminal, administrative, civil, and juvenile cases in the Socialist Republic of Vietnam. Codified under *The Online Trial Skills Handbook* (2024) by Chief Author Justice Nguyen Bien Thuy and court experts from the Supreme People's Court of Vietnam, with technical support from the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) and funding from the Bureau of International Narcotics and Law Enforcement Affairs (INL) of the U.S. Department of State, the policy operationalizes National Assembly Resolution No. 33/2021/QH15, Joint Circular No. 05/2021/TTLT, and Chief Justice Decision No. 163/QD-TANDTC. Note: Named entities *Cecep Mustafa* and *Reihan Abdan Syakur* are *Not found in the available sources*.

## Summary

The framework guides judges, court clerks, prosecutors, and attorneys through the digital transformation of Vietnam's judicial branch into an "e-court" system. It outlines six core structural pillars: (1) Dual Connection Architecture, mandating 1 Central Connection Point (courtroom handling the case) and no more than 3 Component Connection Points (detention facilities, legal aid centers, or approved litigant sites); (2) Case Eligibility Screening, restricting online criminal trials to less serious, serious, or very serious crimes with clear evidence while excluding state secrets and national security offenses; (3) Pre-Trial Readiness Protocols, requiring eligibility assessment 7 days prior, notice issuance, 1-day pre-test connections, and 1-hour pre-trial ready mode; (4) Real-Time Audio-Visual Interaction, enforcing screen sharing of evidence, synchronized video/audio channels, and digital/electronic signature confirmations; (5) Inter-Agency Prosecution Security, assigning joint operational and network security duties across Courts, Procuracies, Police forces, and Defense Ministries; and (6) Strict Cyberspace Privacy Controls, imposing administrative penalties for unauthorized screen recording, credential leaks, or live-streaming.

## Legal Basis

Authority and execution derive from National Assembly resolutions, joint ministerial circulars, and Chief Justice decisions:

- **National Assembly Resolutions & Ordinances:** Resolution No. 33/2021/QH15 of the 15th National Assembly (adopted November 12, 2021); Ordinance No. 03/2022/UBTVQH15; Ordinance No. 01/2022/UBTVQH15 & Ordinance No. 02/2022/UBTVQH15.
- **Joint Ministerial Circulars:** Joint Circular No. 05/2021/TTLT-TANDTC-VKSNDTC-BCA-BQP-BTP (dated December 15, 2021) of the Supreme People's Court, Supreme People's Procuracy, Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of National Defense, and Ministry of Justice.
- **Supreme People's Court Decisions & Circulars:** Decision No. 163/QD-TANDTC (dated June 20, 2024) on technical equipment installation; Circular No. 01/2017/TT-TANDTC on courtrooms; Circular No. 02/2017/TT-TANDTC on court session regulations.
- **Procedural Codes:** Criminal Procedure Code, Civil Procedure Code, Law on Administrative Procedures, Law on Cyber Security, Law on Protection of State Secrets.

## Background & Policy Problems

### Background

To fulfill Party and State directives on proactively participating in the Fourth Industrial Revolution, the Supreme People's Court of Vietnam prioritized judicial reform through digital technology. Following Resolution No. 33/2021/QH15, Vietnam rapidly expanded online hearings to reduce prison transport costs, eliminate logistical delays, and protect trial participants during emergencies. The 2024 Handbook was compiled to provide standardized skills for judicial personnel.

### Policy Problems

- Physical & Logistical Constraints: Travel delays, geographical distance, and high financial costs for litigants, attorneys, and witnesses attending court in person.
- Escort & Security Costs for Detained Defendants: Transporting detained defendants from remote detention facilities to courtrooms demands substantial police personnel, vehicle fuel, and security safeguards.
- Cyberspace & Data Confidentiality Risks: Cyber security threats, unauthorized screen recording, and potential leakages of personal or state secret information during remote transmissions.
- Unexpected Technical Outages: Mid-trial network disconnections or power losses disrupting proceedings.

## Objectives

1\. Enhance judicial efficiency and ensure trial completion within statutory deadlines. 2. Guarantee procedural rights, human rights, and legal protections for all trial participants. 3. Increase public accessibility and transparency in court operations. 4. Standardize online courtroom equipment, lighting, transmission, and data storage. 5. Build professional capacity for judges, clerks, prosecutors, and legal aid officers.

## Scope & Implementation

### Scope

Applies across all High People's Courts, Provincial People's Courts, District People's Courts, Military Courts, Procuracies, detention facilities, and legal aid centers in Vietnam.

### Key Provisions

- **Max 3 Component Connection Points:** Restricting online trials to 1 central connection point courtroom and up to 3 component points.
- **Pre-Trial Testing Mandate:** Mandatory 1-day prior test connection and 1-hour pre-trial ready status.
- **State Secret Exclusion:** Absolute bar on online trials for state secrets or national security offenses.
- **Prohibition of Unconsented Recording:** Strict prohibition against unauthorized audio/video recording, credential sharing, or internet live-streaming.

### Implementation

- **Technical Installation:** Equipping courtrooms under Decision No. 163/QD-TANDTC with TV signal decoders, camera arrays, object projectors, and backup power storage.
- **Inter-Agency Coordination:** Conducting joint interdisciplinary coordination meetings between Courts, Police, and Procuracies.
- **Publication of Skills Handbook:** Publishing \*The Online Trial Skills Handbook\* (2024) across 6 parts.

## Policy Problems

- Unexpected Technical Outages: Network disconnections or power losses during trial proceedings.
- Complex Physical Evidence: Difficulty evaluating physical exhibits remotely, requiring trial adjournment.
- Litigant Digital Literacy Gaps: Assisting vulnerable or rural litigants with remote connection quality.

## Reform

- Installing 24/7 technical monitoring and backup generators.
- Expanding digital evidence sharing portals and electronic signature tools.
- Strengthening inter-agency legal aid and police coordination.
