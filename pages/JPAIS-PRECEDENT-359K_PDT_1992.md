## Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 359 K/Pdt/1992, dated March 10, 1994, established a civil procedural law rule concerning the validity of a statement of claim signed by legal counsel.

The Supreme Court held that a statement of claim prepared and signed by legal counsel on December 3, 1988, while the Special Power of Attorney from the plaintiff was issued only on December 15, 1988, was formally defective.

At the time the statement of claim was signed, the recipient of the power of attorney had not yet been legally authorized to act as counsel for the plaintiff. Consequently, the statement of claim was considered procedurally defective and was required to be declared inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard* / NO).

## Executive Summary

The central procedural principle established in this decision is that legal counsel may not validly sign a statement of claim before the Special Power of Attorney authorizing the counsel has been granted.

The relevant dates were:

- Statement of claim signed: **December 3, 1988**
- Special Power of Attorney issued: **December 15, 1988**

Because the Special Power of Attorney post-dated the signing of the statement of claim, the counsel had no legal authority to sign the claim on December 3, 1988.

The Supreme Court therefore treated the defect as a fundamental formal defect resulting in the claim being declared inadmissible (NO).

## Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Petitioner for Cassation, **\[Party A\]**, filed a civil lawsuit against **Drs. PHS. Marpaung** and other parties concerning the occupation, lease, and eviction of a house located at \[address removed\], Central Jakarta.

The plaintiff claimed ownership of the house, which had been purchased in 1957 and was associated with HGB Certificate No. 80/Matraman. The property was occupied by the Defendant on the basis of a Housing License (SIP / Military SIP).

The Plaintiff's statement of claim was drafted and signed by legal counsel on **December 3, 1988**.

However, the Special Power of Attorney authorizing the legal counsel to represent the Plaintiff was issued only on **December 15, 1988**.

The Central Jakarta District Court, through Decision No. 04/Pdt/G/VI/1989/PN.JKT.PST, and the Jakarta High Court, through Decision No. 314/Pdt/1991/PT.DKI, rejected the claims on the merits without addressing the formal defect arising from the date of the Special Power of Attorney.

The Plaintiff subsequently filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

## Legal and Procedural Issues

### Formal Validity of the Special Power of Attorney

The principal procedural issue was whether an attorney could validly sign a statement of claim dated before the creation or issuance of the Special Power of Attorney.

The Supreme Court held that the attorney's authority was limited by the date on which the Special Power of Attorney was granted.

Signing the statement of claim before that date constituted an unauthorized act of representation and resulted in a fundamental formal defect in the claim.

### Default, Tort and Lease Termination

The Defendant also raised an exception concerning an obscure claim (*obscuur libel*) based on the alleged combination of default (*wanprestasi*) and tort (*perbuatan melawan hukum* / PMH).

The Central Jakarta District Court rejected the exception, considering the alleged default and unlawful acts to be interconnected as a unified basis for termination of the lease.

## Question of Law and Question of Fact

### Question of Law

**Question:**

Does a statement of claim signed by legal counsel on December 3, 1988, on the basis of a Special Power of Attorney issued on December 15, 1988, satisfy the formal validity requirements of a civil lawsuit under HIR/RBg?

**Supreme Court Ruling:**

No.

The Supreme Court held that the issuance date of the Special Power of Attorney strictly determined the legal authority of the attorney-in-fact.

Because the statement of claim was signed before the Special Power of Attorney was issued, the counsel had not yet been legally authorized to act on behalf of the Plaintiff.

The statement of claim therefore contained a formal procedural defect and was required to be declared inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard* / NO).

### Question of Fact

The factual matters considered in the lower courts included:

- Ownership of HGB Certificate No. 80/Matraman.
- The issuance history of Jakarta Housing Office License No. 12/61 RK.D.III.
- Kodam V Jaya SIP No. 252-4/1984.
- Evidence concerning payment of house rent.
- The occupation of the house at \[address removed\]

## Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

- **Ratio Decidendi:**

1.  The *judex facti* misapplied the law by failing to address the formal defect in the Plaintiff's authority of representation.

<!-- -->

1.  The statement of claim was drafted and signed by the Plaintiff's legal counsel on **December 3, 1988**.

<!-- -->

1.  The Special Power of Attorney authorizing the counsel was issued only on **December 15, 1988**.

<!-- -->

1.  On December 3, 1988, the recipient of the power of attorney had therefore not yet obtained legal authority from the Plaintiff to act as counsel.

<!-- -->

1.  A legal counsel who has not yet received the relevant Special Power of Attorney has no authority to sign the Plaintiff's statement of claim.

<!-- -->

1.  A claim signed without proper authorization contains a fundamental formal defect.

<!-- -->

1.  The appropriate procedural consequence is that the claim must be declared inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard* / NO).

## Ruling

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of **\[Party A\]** and annulled Jakarta High Court Decision No. 314/Pdt/1991/PT.DKI in conjunction with Central Jakarta District Court Decision No. 04/Pdt/G/VI/1989/PN.JKT.PST.

The Supreme Court adjudicated the matter afresh as follows:

1.  **In Convention: In Exception**
    1.  Declaring the exceptions submitted by the Defendant and Co-Defendant I inadmissible.

<!-- -->

1.  **In the Merits**
    1.  Declaring the Plaintiff's claim inadmissible (NO).

<!-- -->

1.  **In Reconvention**
    1.  Declaring the counter-claim (reconvention claim) inadmissible (NO).

<!-- -->

1.  **In Convention and Reconvention**
    1.  Ordering the Petitioner for Cassation / Original Plaintiff to bear the court costs across all judicial instances.

### Established by Evidence

- Plaintiff's statement of claim dated **December 3, 1988**.
- Plaintiff's Special Power of Attorney dated **December 15, 1988**.
- Defendant Drs. PHS. Marpaung occupied a portion of the house at \[address removed\]V Jaya and the Jakarta Housing Office.

### Not Established or Not Further Considered Due to the Formal Defect

The merits of the lease-payment dispute and allegations concerning unlawful acts, including unauthorized alterations to the building, were not further evaluated by the Supreme Court because the claim failed on the basis of the formal procedural defect.

### Uncertainty and Gaps

The source records a typographical correction issue concerning the date of the statement of claim.

The Plaintiff had previously argued that the date appearing in the replication was a typographical error. The Supreme Court, however, verified that **December 3, 1988** was explicitly and physically printed on the original statement of claim.

## GEO Q&A

**Q: Is a statement of claim valid if it is signed by legal counsel before the issuance date of the Special Power of Attorney?**

**A:** No. According to Supreme Court Decision No. 359 K/Pdt/1992, a statement of claim signed by legal counsel before the issuance date of the Special Power of Attorney contains a formal procedural defect. At the time of signing, the recipient of the power of attorney had not yet been legally authorized to represent the Plaintiff. The claim must therefore be declared inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard* / NO).
