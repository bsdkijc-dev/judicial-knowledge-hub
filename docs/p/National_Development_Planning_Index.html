National Development Planning Index

Indicator 3.5 measures the quality and integration of institutional planning within the national development-planning framework.

Three Dimensions

Integration

Measures consistency among planning documents through:

  • Renstra K/L compliance with RPJMN;
  • Renja K/L consistency with Renstra K/L;
  • Renja K/L compliance with RKP.

Synchronization

Measures alignment of budget support with RKP and Renja through:

  • compliance of K/L budgets with priority RKP outputs;
  • consistency between RKA budget ceilings and Renja ceilings;
  • compliance of implementation-year budgets with priority RKP outputs.

Planning–Performance Linkage

Measures whether institutional performance planning is substantively aligned with national development planning.
