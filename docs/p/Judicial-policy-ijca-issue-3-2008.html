The International Journal for Court Administration (IJCA) Issue 3 is the third official scientific publication issue of the International Association for Court Administration (IACA). Published under Executive Editor Markus Zimmer and Managing Editor Philip M. Langbroek, it features research and policies on court governance, management, and administration. Topics include the relationship between judges and society (Bridges Built By Judges by Gabriël van den Brink), workflow reform (Case Flow Management by Kerry Connelly), the history and future of court management (Past And Future For Management of Courts by Bert Maan), managerial oversight (Supervisory Control And Court Management by Andreas Lienhard), specialized courts (Specialized Courts by Markus Zimmer), and the position of the European Rechtspfleger in the EU (Thomas Kappl) [FACT].

Summary

Published by IACA, the third issue of the IJCA compiles various international scientific articles focusing on the effectiveness of court governance, management, and administration. The issue opens with a message from Executive Editor Markus Zimmer announcing the 4th IACA International Conference in Istanbul, Turkey. This conference addressed crucial issues such as multi-door court systems, the impact of the global financial crisis on courts, corruption prevention, court facility design, and pandemic preparedness. This issue contains six main scientific articles from global court administration experts.

Background

IACA was established to strengthen the professionalism of judicial governance globally. The publication of IJCA Issue 3 aims to provide a platform for knowledge exchange among judges, court administrators, and academics in facing financial crises, the challenges of organized crime, and the need for modernizing court management.

Objectives

1. Publish high-quality research regarding court governance, management, and administration. 2. Facilitate the 4th IACA International Conference in Istanbul, Turkey. 3. Discuss strategies for handling global financial crises, pandemic preparedness, and preventing corruption in the judicial environment. 4. Review models of case flow management, supervisory control, specialized courts, and the role of the European Rechtspfleger.

Scope

This publication policy applies internationally, covering court administration practitioners and academics worldwide under the auspices of IACA.

Key Provisions

  • Agenda of the IACA Istanbul Conference:
    • Multi-Door Court Systems: The development of varied dispute resolution channels.
    • Judicial Integrity: Maintaining judicial effectiveness against the threats of corruption, bribery, and organized crime.
    • Crisis & Pandemic Response: Pandemic emergency preparedness and survival strategies amidst a global financial crisis.
  • List of Main Articles in IJCA Issue 3:
    • Bridges Built By Judges (Gabriël van den Brink)
    • Case Flow Management: A Format For Making Change (Kerry Connelly)
    • Past And Future For Management of Courts (Bert Maan)
    • Supervisory Control And Court Management (Andreas Lienhard)
    • Specialized Courts (Markus Zimmer)
    • The European Rechtspfleger for the European Union (Thomas Kappl).

Implementation

  • Hosting Writing Workshops: A special session during the Istanbul Conference to train practitioners in drafting and submitting scientific articles to the IJCA.
  • International Dissemination: The distribution of the journal to all IACA members and partners globally.

Policy Problems

  • The Threat of Organized Crime & Corruption: Protecting court integrity in conflict zones or areas experiencing civil unrest.
  • Budgetary Constraints of Financial Crises: The need to maintain court performance amidst global budget tightening.

Reform / Change

  • Implementation of case flow management frameworks to reduce trial delays.
  • Strengthening the professional role of the European Rechtspfleger to support judicial efficiency in the European Union.
