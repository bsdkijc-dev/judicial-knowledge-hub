Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 209 K/TUN/2004 dated October 14, 2004, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal rules regarding the validity of a plaintiff's legal standing / capacity in a dispute concerning a State Administrative Decree on corporate share transfers: A Limited Liability Company (*PT*) acting as a buyer of another Limited Liability Company (*PT*) lacks the legal capacity or standing to sue a State Administrative Decree concerning the target company being acquired, as long as the buying company has not fully paid the entire purchase price as stipulated.

Case Background

Plaintiff I (PT Asianagro Abadi) and Plaintiff II (PT Pusakamegah Buminusantara) filed a lawsuit against the Minister of Justice and Human Rights of the Republic of Indonesia (Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation I) alongside [Party A] and Ngarijan Salim (Intervention Defendant II / Petitioner for Cassation II) at the Jakarta State Administrative Court. The object of the dispute consisted of the Decree of the Minister of Justice and Human Rights No. C-13525 HT.01.04 TH.2003 concerning the Approval of the Deed of Amendment of Articles of Association of PT Pusakamegah Buminusantara and the Receipt of Notification of Deed of Amendment of Articles of Association No. C-13526 HT.01.04 TH.2003 dated June 13, 2003.

Plaintiff I argued that it had purchased all shares of Plaintiff II pursuant to Agreement Deed No. 75 dated September 24, 1997. However, Plaintiff I had only paid a down payment / earnest money of Rp 3,000,000,000 out of the total share purchase price of Rp 17,000,000,000. The Jakarta State Administrative Court (Decision No. 150/G.TUN/2003/PTUN.JKT dated January 19, 2004) declared the Plaintiff's lawsuit inadmissible. The Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 42/B/2004/PT.TUN.JKT dated March 16, 2004) annulled the State Administrative Court's judgment and granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit. Dissatisfied with the ruling, Petitioner for Cassation I (Minister of Justice and Human Rights) and Petitioner for Cassation II ([Party A] and Ngarijan Salim) filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. Pursuant to Agreement Deed No. 75 dated September 24, 1997, PT Asianagro Abadi had not fully settled the entire purchase price for the shares of PT Pusakamegah Buminusantara amounting to Rp 17,000,000,000, having only paid Rp 3,000,000,000.
  2. As long as the entire purchase price stipulated in the agreement remains unpaid, PT Asianagro Abadi does not possess full rights as a buyer and has not become a lawful shareholder possessing the capacity or legal standing to act as a Plaintiff challenging a State Administrative Decree concerning PT Pusakamegah Buminusantara.
  3. Because PT Asianagro Abadi lacked capacity or standing to sue from the outset, the lawsuit filed by the Plaintiffs / Respondents for Cassation must be rejected.
  4. There was also an erroneous application of procedural law by the Registry of the Jakarta State Administrative Court in failing to provide an opportunity for inspection of case files (*inzage*) to Intervention Defendant II before transmitting the files to the High Court, violating procedural provisions under Article 126 paragraph (1) of Law No. 5 of 1986 (*fair trial*).
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioners for Cassation: (1) THE MINISTER OF JUSTICE AND HUMAN RIGHTS OF THE REPUBLIC OF INDONESIA, (2) [Party A] and NGARIJAN SALIM; Annulling the Jakarta State Administrative High Court decision dated March 16, 2004, No. 42/B/2004/PT.TUN.JKT jo. the Jakarta State Administrative Court decision dated January 19, 2004, No. 150/G.TUN/2003/PTUN.JKT; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Rejecting the Plaintiffs' lawsuit; (2) Ordering the Respondent for Cassation to pay cassation-level court costs amounting to Rp 500,000 (five hundred thousand rupiahs). *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Thursday, October 14, 2004, by the Cassation Panel of Justices: Prof. Dr. Paulus Effendie Lotulung, S.H. [Presiding Judge], Ny. Titi Nurmala Siagian, S.H., and Ny. Chairani A. Wani, S.H. [Associate Justices]).*
