Policy Analysis

  • Background & Policy Problem:
    • FACT: Severe labor shortages severely impacted micro, small, and medium-sized enterprises across multiple industrial sectors in Japan.
    • FACT: Existing efforts to boost productivity and domestic labor supply fell short, necessitating the acceptance of job-ready foreign workers with specific technical skills.
    • FACT: Structural reform was required to elevate the Immigration Bureau to the Immigration Services Agency (ISA) to unify foreign resident administration and social integration policies.
  • Policy Objectives:
    • FACT: Provide a clear legal framework to accept foreign workers with specified skills in designated industrial sectors.
    • FACT: Enhance governance and foreign worker protection through the "Comprehensive Measures for Acceptance and Coexistence of Foreign Talent."
    • FACT: Eliminate dishonest labor brokers and exploiters through bilateral cooperation frameworks.
  • Key Provisions & Reforms:
    • FACT: Specified Skilled Worker Statuses: Created "Specified Skilled Worker (i)" (intermediate skills) and "Specified Skilled Worker (ii)" (expert/advanced skills).
    • FACT: Establishment of ISA: Replaced the old Immigration Bureau with the Immigration Services Agency as an external agency of MOJ on April 1, 2019.
    • FACT: Policy Guidelines: Cabinet issued the Basic Policy and Sectoral Operational Policies to regulate sector quotas and qualification criteria.
    • FACT: Bilateral Arrangements: Signed Memorandums of Cooperation with 11 sending countries (including the Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia, Cambodia, and Nepal) to prevent illegal broker intervention.
  • Implementation Schedule & Evaluation:
    • FACT: Passed by the Diet on Dec 8, 2018, promulgated on Dec 14, 2018, and fully enforced on Apr 1, 2019.
    • FACT: Comprehensive Measures outlined 126 action items, including regional One-Stop Consultation Centers and multi-language Life and Work Guidebooks.
