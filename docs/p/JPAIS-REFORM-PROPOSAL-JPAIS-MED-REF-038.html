Description / Deskripsi

This page records the empirical research findings and policy recommendations published in the monograph "Kewenangan Pengadilan Niaga dalam Mengadili Permohonan Keberatan atas Putusan Komisi Pengawas Persaingan Usaha Tidak Sehat" (ISBN 978-623-384-090-3), conducted by the Research and Development Center for Law and Justice (Puslitbang Kumdil MA RI) under lead researchers Dr. Ismail Rumadan, M.H. et al. The study evaluates the shifting of absolute jurisdiction over KPPU objections from District Courts to Commercial Courts pursuant to Article 118 of Law No. 11/2020 (Job Creation Law), PP No. 44/2021, SEMA No. 1/2021, and PERMA No. 3/2021. It identifies a profound procedural impasse caused by forcing a public-administrative enforcement mechanism into a private civil procedural framework (HIR/RBg), proposing the adoption of the United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) judicial remand doctrine. All legal frameworks and comparative analysis data have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher Cecep Mustafa.

Problem / Masalah

  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-038: Eversion of Judex Facti Role & Evidence Lock, where while PP No. 44/2021 orders Commercial Courts to examine formal and substantive aspects, Article 13(5) of PERMA No. 3/2021 strictly prohibits petitioners from submitting new documentary evidence or *novum*, depriving judges of fact-finding authority and trapping them into passively confirming administrative records.
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-039: Misalignment of Evidentiary Standards & Execution Deficit, where KPPU investigations rely on criminal-analogous evidence under Article 42 of Law No. 5/1999 while Commercial Courts apply civil evidence rules (Article 164 HIR), creating severe admissibility confusion, compounded by a lack of asset-tracing powers for PNBP fine execution.

Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

  • JPAIS-MED-ID-038: Under current practice pursuant to SEMA No. 1/2021 and PERMA No. 3/2021, objections filed after 2 February 2021 are processed exclusively by the 5 regional Commercial Courts, bypassing formal court mediation and relying primarily on administrative dossier review, with Cassation judgments serving as final review as PERMA No. 3/2021 bars Judicial Review (*Peninjauan Kembali* / PK).

Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

  • JPAIS-MED-JP-038: International comparison evaluated in the Puslitbang Kumdil monograph highlights the United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) model applying the **Judicial Remand Doctrine**, where appellate courts finding incomplete evidentiary records issue remand orders returning cases to agencies to re-examine facts without nullifying actions outright or touching substantive market merits.

Gap / Kesenjangan

  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-038: The primary gap is the absence of a dedicated **Commercial Court Procedural Law Act** (*UU Hukum Acara Peradilan Niaga*), where current reliance on civil code procedure (HIR/RBg) fails to accommodate quasi-judicial administrative reviews, leaving judges without statutory tools like remand power or specialized economic evidence evaluation rules.

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Adopt the judicial remand doctrine by amending PERMA No. 3/2021 to authorize Commercial Court judges to issue a Remand Order (*Putusan Sela / Pengembalian Berkas*), re-align examination timeframes from 3–12 months back to a strict maximum of 60 working days, and relax the evidence lock for novel arguments introduced in KPPU final decisions.

Action / Aksi

  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-038: Issue a Supreme Court Regulation (PERMA Amandemen 3/2021) incorporating the remand mechanism and clarifying the 60-working-day trial cap.
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-039: Draft a comprehensive Bill on Commercial Court Procedural Law (*RUU Hukum Acara Peradilan Niaga*) establishing specialized competition procedure.

Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

  • JPAIS-MED-PILOT-038: Central Jakarta Commercial Court pilot implementation evaluating remand order execution and trial duration reduction.

KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

  • JPAIS-MED-KPI-038: Reduction of average case disposal time to $\le 60$ working days; successful implementation of remand orders for incomplete administrative dossiers, reducing outright annulments based on minor procedural gaps.

Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Harmonizing PP No. 44/2021 and PERMA No. 3/2021 regarding the definition of working days and execution mechanisms for PNBP fines, alongside establishing specialized economic certification programs for Commercial Court judges in coordination with BSDK MA RI.

Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgating specialized commercial court procedural rules that reconcile public administrative enforcement powers with private civil due process safeguards.

Evidence / Bukti Empiris

  • Official research monograph titled *“Kewenangan Pengadilan Niaga dalam Mengadili Permohonan Keberatan atas Putusan Komisi Pengawas Persaingan Usaha Tidak Sehat”* (ISBN 978-623-384-090-3) published by Puslitbang Kumdil MA RI (2021) under lead researcher Dr. Ismail Rumadan, M.H. et al.
