JPW-REGULATION-EU-PPWR-2025

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 923, 2026-09-15 · wiki source · Markdown

EU Packaging & Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) is a European Union legal framework mandating sustainable packaging standards across all EU Member States Passage 1.

Executive Summary

The EU Packaging & Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR) officially entered into force on February 11, 2025 Passage 1. The regulation targets the elimination of over-packaging and mandates that all packaging on the EU market must be fully recyclable or reusable by 2030 Passage 1, Passage 2.

Legal Basis

  • EU Packaging & Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR), in force since February 11, 2025 Passage 1.
  • Eversheds Sutherland Executive Compliance Guide, published on September 22, 2025 Passage 4.

Background

Escalating volumes of packaging waste in the EU internal market necessitated binding statutory intervention Passage 1. The PPWR was enacted to curtail wasteful packaging habits and accelerate the transition toward a sustainable circular economy Passage 1, Passage 2.

Objectives

  1. Reduce over-packaging and excessive material consumption across EU Member States Passage 1.
  2. Require 100% of commercial packaging to be recyclable or reusable by 2030 Passage 2.
  3. Enforce corporate compliance and incentivize sustainable supply chain operations Passage 3.

Key Provisions

  • Sustainable Packaging Mandate: Establishes strict statutory requirements for resource-efficient packaging design Passage 1.
  • 2030 Recyclability Deadline: Requires all packaging formats to satisfy strict reusability or recyclability criteria by 2030 Passage 2.
  • Non-Compliance Sanctions: Failure to meet PPWR criteria risks immediate market access restrictions and heavy fines for corporations and executive management Passage 3.

Implementation

  • Territorial Applicability: Direct legal binding force across all EU Member States effective February 11, 2025 Passage 1.
  • Legal Navigation Support: Eversheds Sutherland released executive guidance on September 22, 2025, to aid commercial entities in adapting to compliance obligations Passage 4.

Policy & Compliance Issues

  • Market Access Risk: Failure to adapt supply chains risks exclusion from doing business in EU markets Passage 3.
  • Executive Liability: Potential direct monetary fines and accountability measures targeting corporate management teams Passage 3.
