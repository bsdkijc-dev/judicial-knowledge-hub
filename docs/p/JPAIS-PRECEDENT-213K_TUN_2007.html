Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 213 K/TUN/2007 dated November 6, 2007, establishes two fundamental jurisprudence and legal rules in State Administrative and Mining Law:

  • Supreme Court Cassation Jurisdiction Over Regional Official Decrees in the Mining Sector (Article 45A paragraph (2) letter c of Law No. 5 of 2004): Because the operation of Mining Authorizations (*Kuasa Pertambangan*) is cross-sectoral between the Central Government and Regional Governments (ex Gov. Reg. No. 75 of 2001), a Regent's or Regional Official's Decree concerning Mining Authorization is not a purely regional decision whose scope is strictly limited to the local region. Consequently, the cassation restriction under Article 45A paragraph (2) letter c of Law No. 5 of 2004 does not apply, and the Supreme Court retains jurisdiction to examine the cassation petition.
  • Duty of Prudence & Principle of Thoroughness of State Administrative Officials (AAUPB): Although under Government Regulation No. 75 of 2001 a State Administrative Official (Regent) has the authority to issue Mining Authorization decisions within their region, the official is obligated to exercise caution and thoroughly consider the existence of pre-existing mining areas held by other parties (in casu the Coal Mining Concession Agreement / PKP2B area of PT Arutmin Indonesia) to prevent mining area overlaps. The issuance of a Mining Authorization Decree without thoroughness violates the Principles of Good Governance (*AAUPB*), specifically the Principle of Thoroughness and the Principle of Prudence.

Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Petitioner for Cassation (PT Arutmin Indonesia) is the holder of rights over a coal mining area pursuant to a Coal Mining Concession Agreement (*PKP2B*) jo. Decree of the Director-General of General Mining No. 198/K/2014/DDJP/1995 dated May 5, 1995 (mining area code DU 322/Kalsel covering 12,473 ha).

However, the Original Defendant / Respondent for Cassation (Regent of Tanah Laut) issued Decree No. 545.3.006/PU/DPE/2004 dated September 27, 2004, concerning the Grant of Coal Exploitation Mining Authorization (KW.106 TW.I) to Intervention Defendant II / Turut Respondent for Cassation (PT Surya Kencana Jorong Mandiri) covering an area of 203 ha in Kintap Kecil and Bukit Mulia Villages, Kintap Sub-District, Tanah Laut Regency, which turned out to overlap (*overlapping*) within PT Arutmin Indonesia's PKP2B concession area.

PT Arutmin Indonesia filed a lawsuit against the Regent of Tanah Laut's decree at the Banjarmasin State Administrative Court. The Banjarmasin State Administrative Court (Decision No. 15/G/2006/PTUN.BJM dated September 1, 2006) granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in its entirety and annulled the Regent of Tanah Laut's decree. However, the Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 176/B/2006/PT.TUN.JKT dated December 18, 2006) annulled the Banjarmasin State Administrative Court judgment and rejected the Plaintiff's lawsuit on the grounds of lawsuit duplication (*ne bis in idem*) and considering the decree to be regional in scope, thereby triggering the cassation restriction. PT Arutmin Indonesia filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. Regarding the restriction on cassation legal remedies pursuant to Article 45A paragraph (2) letter c of Law No. 5 of 2004, under Government Regulation No. 75 of 2001, the administration of Mining Authorizations is cross-sectoral between the Central and Regional Governments. A Regent's authority is derivative of national regulations; therefore, Mining Authorization decrees issued by a Regent are not subject to Article 45A paragraph (2) letter c of Law No. 5 of 2004, and the Supreme Court has jurisdiction to examine the cassation remedy filed by the Petitioner for Cassation.
  2. Regarding the ground of lawsuit duplication, under Law No. 11 of 1967 and Government Regulation No. 75 of 2001, Exploration Mining Authorizations and Exploitation Mining Authorizations are two distinct and independent mining business categories. Consequently, the Plaintiff's lawsuit challenging the Exploration Mining Authorization decree (Case No. 02/G/2006/PTUN.BJM) and the lawsuit challenging the Exploitation Mining Authorization decree (Case No. 15/G/2006/PTUN.BJM jo. 213 K/TUN/2007) do not constitute lawsuit duplication and do not amount to *ne bis in idem*.
  3. The State Administrative Official (Regent of Tanah Laut) was proven to have violated the Principles of Good Governance (*AAUPB*), namely the Principle of Thoroughness and the Principle of Prudence. The Defendant should have exercised caution and thoroughly considered pre-existing mining areas prior to issuing the disputed decree by first obtaining a clear picture of relevant facts and third-party interests (PT Arutmin Indonesia) to avoid legal issues arising from Mining Authorization area overlaps.
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation: PT ARUTMIN INDONESIA; Annulling the Jakarta State Administrative High Court decision dated December 18, 2006, No. 176/B/2006/PT.TUN.JKT, which had annulled the Banjarmasin State Administrative Court decision dated September 1, 2006, No. 15/G/2006/PTUN.BJM; and Independently Adjudicating: IN EXCEPTION: Rejecting the Exceptions of the Defendant and Intervention Defendant II; IN THE PRINCIPAL CASE: (1) Granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit in its entirety; (2) Declaring null and void the Decree of the Regent of Tanah Laut No. 545.3.006/PU/DPE/2004 dated September 27, 2004, concerning the Grant of Coal Exploitation Mining Authorization (KW.106 TW.I) to PT Surya Kencana Jorong Mandiri issued by the Defendant; (3) Ordering the Defendant to revoke the Decree of the Regent of Tanah Laut No. 545.3.006/PU/DPE/2004 dated September 27, 2004; (4) Ordering the Respondent for Cassation / Defendant to pay cassation-level court costs amounting to Rp 500,000 (five hundred thousand rupiahs). *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Tuesday, November 6, 2007, by the Cassation Panel of Justices: Prof. DR. Paulus E. Lotulung, S.H. [Junior Chairman for State Administrative Chamber / Presiding Judge], H. Imam Soebechi, S.H., M.H., and Titi Nurmala Siagian, S.H., M.H. [Associate Justices]).*
