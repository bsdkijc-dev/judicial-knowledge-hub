JPW-RESEARCH-001

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 897, 2026-09-15 · wiki source · Markdown

Research Project: ‘A failed kopie after the Englisch model?’ is a historical legal research project examining the role of the British constitution in the history of Dutch political constitutional law and related literature between 1812 and 1983 [].

Summary

This research project focuses on the core question of how the British constitution influenced Dutch political constitutional law and its legal literature from 1812 to 1983 []. The research is conducted at Radboud University [].

Legal Basis

Not found in the available sources. (This project is an academic research activity rather than a formal public policy or legal product) [].

Background

The Netherlands and United Kingdom share many constitutional characteristics, such as a constitutional monarchy, a decentralized unitary state, and a bicameral parliamentary system []. The influence of the British constitution is evident in the formation of the Upper House referencing the House of Lords, the application of the right of parliamentary inquiry, the figure of the Prime Minister, and the revitalization of the Council of State [].

Objectives

The objective of this research is to compile a systematic overview and reveal the historical developmental trajectory that has largely lacked attention in constitutional legal literature [].

Key Provisions

  • **Project Duration**: 2023 to 2026 [].
  • **Researcher**: Simon van Oort [].
  • **Managing Institution**: Research Centre for State and Law, Radboud University [].

Implementation

This research project is carried out under the auspices of the Research Centre for State and Law, Faculty of Law, Radboud University, located at Houtlaan 4, 6525 XZ Nijmegen [].

Policy Issues

The lack of systematic studies regarding the extent to which British constitutional models were adopted or modified within the history of Dutch political constitutional law in the 19th century [].
