JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:KCCP-REF-001

Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

  • Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
  • Ingestion Status: Confirmed

Problem & Gap Mapping / Pemetaan Masalah & Kesenjangan

  • Baseline Problem (KCCP-PROB-001): Court-connected mediation in Indonesian general civil courts (Pengadilan Negeri) suffers from procedural ritualization, resulting in stagnant settlement success rates between 4.0% and 4.08%.
  • Current Practice (KCCP-PRACT-ID-001): PERMA No. 1 of 2016 relies on a solo mediator model (a busy career judge or an uncompensated non-judge mediator) conducting brief facilitative sessions without evaluative authority.
  • Institutional Gap (KCCP-GAP-001): Lack of multidisciplinary expert panels, absent evaluative settlement mechanisms when parties reach an impasse, and unenforced cost sanctions for bad-faith attendance.

Comparative Legal Grounding / Landasan Hukum Komparatif

  • Japanese Practice (KCCP-PRACT-JP-001 & KCCP-PRACT-JP-003): Under Articles 5, 6, and 17 of the Civil Conciliation Act (Minji Chōtei Hō, Act No. 222/1951), Japan utilizes 3-member Conciliation Committees (Chōtei Iinkai: 1 judge + 2 lay commissioners) and empowers courts to issue ex-officio Orders in Lieu of Conciliation (Chōtei ni kawaru kettei), achieving >50% settlement in civil cases and 71.8% in inheritance divisions.

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

  • Majelis Konsiliasi Tripartit: Institutionalize 3-member panels for commercial, property, and inheritance disputes exceeding Rp 500 million, pairing 1 certified Judge Mediator with 2 accredited Non-Judge Expert Commissioners (e.g., accountants, engineers, land valuers).
  • Penetapan Pengganti Perdamaian: Authorize panels to issue a provisional evaluative settlement order upon negotiation deadlock. The order converts into a binding Settlement Deed (Akta Perdamaian) if no party files a formal objection within 14 calendar days.

Execution Roadmap / Peta Jalan Eksekusi

  • Phase 1 (Preparation / Persiapan): Promulgate a Chief Justice Decree (SK KMA), establish the Steering Committee, configure SIPP workflow modules, and train 120 expert commissioners.
  • Phase 2 (Implementation / Implementasi): Deploy panels across the 6 designated pilot courts (KCCP-PILOT-001).
  • Phase 3 (Evaluation / Evaluasi): Audit SIPP performance metrics and draft a permanent Supreme Court Regulation (PERMA).

Pilot Scope & KPI Targets / Cakupan Pilot & Target KPI

  • Pilot Courts (KCCP-PILOT-001): PN Jakarta Pusat, PN Surabaya, PN Medan, PA Jakarta Selatan, PA Surabaya, and PA Malang (90-day duration).
  • Target KPIs (KCCP-KPI-001):

 * District Court (PN) General Civil Settlement Rate:  ≥ 25 (up from ~4.0%).
 * Religious Court (PA) Property/Sharia Banking Settlement Rate:  ≥ 60.
 * Average Mediation Duration:  ≤ 21 calendar days (down from 30 working days).

Policy & Regulatory Impact / Dampak Kebijakan & Regulasi

  • Administrative: Issue an SK KMA for pilot authorization and integrate lay expert rosters directly into SIPP.
  • Regulatory: Promulgate a new PERMA on Integrated Court Mediation superseding PERMA No. 1 of 2016.
  • Legislative: Submit amendments to the House of Representatives (DPR) for inclusion in the Draft Civil Procedure Code (RUU Hukum Acara Perdata).
