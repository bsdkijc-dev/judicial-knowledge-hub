Policy Analysis

Structural Framework & Actors

  • Governed by the Constitution of Japan, establishing the Supreme Court and Lower Courts.
  • Outlines roles for Justices, Lower-Court Judges, non-judge Court Officials, Public Prosecutors, and Attorneys.

Procedural Frameworks & Public Participation

  • Incorporates Citizen Participation in the Judicial Process.
  • Provides official procedural rules and FAQs across five domains: Civil Procedure, Summary Court Civil Procedure, Criminal Procedure, Family Related Cases, and Juvenile Cases.

Crime Victim Protection & Guidance Materials

  • Establishes procedural safeguards via Systems for Crime Victims in Criminal Proceedings and Systems for Persons Who Have Suffered Damage from Juvenile Crime.
  • Publishes multilingual guides including *Outline of Civil Procedure in Japan*, *Outline of Criminal Justice in Japan*, and *Guide to the Family Court of Japan* (available in English, Chinese, and Korean).

Interpretations & Structural Inferences

  • Serves as a public transparency and access-to-justice initiative managed directly by the Supreme Court of Japan.
  • Focus on victim protection and civic engagement demonstrates a policy push toward public accountability and rights protection in Japanese court proceedings.
