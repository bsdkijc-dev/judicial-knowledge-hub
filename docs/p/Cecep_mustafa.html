Core Definition

The **Federal Judicial Center (FJC)** is the research and education agency of the United States federal courts, established by Congress to conduct orientation and continuing education programs for federal judges and judicial personnel.

Key Operational Pillars

  • Master Training Body: Federal Judicial Center (FJC).
  • Primary Training Focus: Federal procedural rules, judicial ethics, and scientific evidence evaluation (*Daubert* standards).
  • Integration of Legal Tech: Case management (CM/ECF) and virtual courtroom technology seminars.
  • Continuing Education Mandate: Voluntary ongoing educational seminars and specialized judicial workshops.
