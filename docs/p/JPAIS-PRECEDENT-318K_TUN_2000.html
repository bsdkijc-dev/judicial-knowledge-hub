Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 318 K/TUN/2000 dated March 19, 2002, establishes fundamental state administrative and land law jurisprudence: Pursuant to Article 45 paragraph (1) of Government Regulation No. 24 of 1997 on Land Registration, the Head of the Land Office is prohibited/not permitted to register the transfer of land rights if the land in question is currently the object of a dispute in court. The issuance of a new Freehold Title (*Sertifikat Hak Milik*) over land currently undergoing judicial proceedings and subject to a State Administrative Court suspension order constitutes an unlawful act violating the authority of a State Administrative Official (*ultra vires*), and consequently, such certificate must be declared null and void and without legal force.

Case Background

The Original Plaintiffs / Petitioners for Cassation (1. Nyonya Taily Aida and 2. Hendra Santoso) are the lawful owners of 5 (five) plots of Freehold Land located in Dukuh Pakis Village, Karangpilang Sub-District (currently Dukuh Pakis Sub-District), Surabaya Municipality (SHM No. 90/Dukuh Pakis in the name of Ny. Taily Aida; and SHM Nos. 142, 143, 144, and 145/Dukuh Pakis in the name of Hendra Santoso). On June 30, 1998, the Minister of Agrarian Affairs / Head of BPN annulled the registration of the Plaintiffs' property rights by issuing Decree No. 8-XI-1998. Challenging said decree, the Plaintiffs filed a cancellation lawsuit at the Jakarta State Administrative Court (No. 072/G.TUN/1998/PTUN.Jkt), where the Chief Judge of the Jakarta State Administrative Court on September 3, 1998, issued an Order for the Suspension of Execution of Decree No. 8-XI-1998 prohibiting the transfer or encumbrance of the disputed land.

Although the Original Defendant / Respondent for Cassation (Head of the Land Office of Surabaya Municipality) had official knowledge of the pending dispute at the Jakarta State Administrative Court and the aforementioned Suspension Order, the Defendant on October 23, 1998, nevertheless issued Freehold Title (SHM) No. 669/Dukuh Pakis in the name of Drs. Suladi Abdus Shomad over the Plaintiffs' disputed land. The Plaintiffs subsequently filed a lawsuit seeking the annulment of SHM No. 669 at the Surabaya State Administrative Court (Decision No. 152/G.TUN/1999/PTUN.SBY dated December 23, 1999), which granted the Plaintiffs' lawsuit in its entirety. However, the Surabaya State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 38/B/TUN/2000/PT.TUN.SBY dated May 16, 2000) annulled the Surabaya State Administrative Court judgment and declared the Plaintiffs' lawsuit inadmissible on the grounds that it was premature because the proceedings at the Jakarta State Administrative Court were still undergoing cassation review. Dissatisfied with the Surabaya State Administrative High Court ruling, the Plaintiffs filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The cassation objections raised by the Petitioners for Cassation are well-founded because the Surabaya State Administrative High Court (*PTTUN*) committed an error in applying the law.
  2. The Defendant (Head of the Land Office of Surabaya Municipality) was aware that the Decree of the Minister of Agrarian Affairs / Head of BPN annulling the Plaintiffs' SHM was being litigated at the Jakarta State Administrative Court (No. 072/G.TUN/1998/PTUN.Jkt jo. Jakarta State Administrative High Court No. 26/B.TUN/1999/PT.TUN.JKT) and remained under cassation review at the Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia.
  3. In those proceedings, the Jakarta State Administrative Court had issued an Order for the Suspension of Execution of the disputed object.
  4. Pursuant to Article 45 paragraph (1) of Government Regulation No. 24 of 1997, the Head of the Land Office is prohibited from registering a transfer of rights if the land in question is the object of a dispute in court.
  5. The Defendant's action in persisting to issue Freehold Title (SHM) No. 669/Dukuh Pakis in the name of Drs. Suladi Abdus Shomad over the disputed land violated Article 45 paragraph (1) of Government Regulation No. 24 of 1997. Therefore, pursuant to Article 51 paragraph (2)(c) of the State Administrative Court Law, the Defendant should not have issued said Freehold Title, and the certificate improperly issued must be declared null and void by operation of law.
  6. Legal Principle: "Pursuant to Article 45 paragraph (1) of Government Regulation No. 24 of 1997, the Head of the Land Office is not permitted to register a transfer of rights if the land in question is the object of a dispute in court."
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioners for Cassation: 1. Ny. TAILY AIDA and 2. HENDRA SANTOSO; Annulling the Surabaya State Administrative High Court decision dated May 16, 2000, No. 38/B/TUN/2000/PT.TUN.SBY; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Granting the Plaintiffs' lawsuit in its entirety; (2) Ordering the Defendant to halt all legal acts or actions as implementation of the State Administrative Decree concerning Freehold Title (SHM) No. 669/Dukuh Pakis in the name of Drs. Suladi Abdus Shomad; (3) Declaring null, void, and without legal force Freehold Title (SHM) No. 669/Dukuh Pakis in the name of Drs. Suladi Abdus Shomad; (4) Ordering the Defendant to revoke and declare void Freehold Title (SHM) No. 669/Dukuh Pakis in the name of Drs. Suladi Abdus Shomad; (5) Ordering the Defendant to strike off Freehold Title (SHM) No. 669/Dukuh Pakis in the name of Drs. Suladi Abdus Shomad from the land book register; (6) Ordering the Respondent for Cassation to pay all court costs across first instance, appellate, and cassation levels (assessed at Rp 100,000 at the cassation level). *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Tuesday, March 19, 2002, by Prof. Dr. Paulus Effendi Lotulung, S.H. [Junior Chairman of the Supreme Court / Presiding Judge], Ny. Emin Aminah Achadiat, S.H., and Ny. Asma Samik Ibrahim, S.H. [Associate Justices]).*
