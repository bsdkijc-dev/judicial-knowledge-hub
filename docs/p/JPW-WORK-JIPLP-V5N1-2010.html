The Journal of Intellectual Property Law & Practice (Vol. 5, No. 1) is a monthly peer-reviewed intellectual property law journal issue published by Oxford University Press in January 2010 JIPLP Publication.

Overview

This publication contains scholarly articles and practical reviews covering full-spectrum intellectual property (IP) law, including patents, trade marks, copyright, competition law, and privacy JIPLP Overview. JIPLP is distributed globally via print editions and digital Advance Access channels Journal Access.

Legal Basis

  • Formal Legal Basis: Not found in the available sources Legal Basis.
  • Publishing Policy: Subject to ethical and environmental publishing standards established by Oxford Journals Ethical Policy.

Background

Rapid developments in international IP law necessitate periodic academic forums that deliver rigorous legal analysis for legal practitioners, scholars, and policymakers IP Background.

Key Objectives

  1. Present timely and relevant intellectual property law research to the global legal community Research Goal.
  2. Facilitate cross-jurisdictional commentary on patent, trade mark, and copyright disputes Legal Dialogue.
  3. Enhance research accessibility through integrated print and digital publishing systems Access Goal.

Core Provisions

  • Publication Frequency: Published monthly (12 issues per year for Volume 5 in 2010) Publication Frequency.
  • Alerting Services: Offers E-Table of Contents and Advance Access alerter services for subscribers Alerting Services.
  • Identification Standards: Standardized under ISSN 1747-1532 and Digital Object Identifiers (DOI) DOI Standard.

Implementation & Administration

  • Editorial Leadership: Led by Jeremy Phillips as Editor and Phillip Johnson as Reviews Editor Editorial Team.
  • Distribution: Distributed in the USA by Mercury International and worldwide via the Oxford University Press network Global Distribution.

Policy Considerations

  • E-Commerce Platform Liability: Analysis of liability thresholds for online marketplaces (such as eBay) regarding counterfeit goods E-Commerce Liability.
  • Patents & Competition Law: Examination of patent settlement agreements under European Commission competition regulations Competition Law.
