Overview

Supreme Court Judicial Review Decision No. 034 PK/Pdt/1984 establishes two fundamental legal principles in Civil Procedure Law and Civil / Agrarian Law: 1. Civil Procedure Law (Judicial Review Novum): Grounds for a petition for judicial review are justified when the Petitioner for PK submits new documentary evidence (*novum*) from competent authorities (Directorate General of Immigration of the Ministry of Law and Human Rights and the Royal Netherlands Embassy) proving that the grantor held Dutch citizenship at the time the grant was executed. 2. Civil / Land Administration Law: A grant deed executed by a foreign national whose assets in Indonesia have fallen under or been directly controlled by the State (pursuant to Emergency Law No. 3 of 1960) is null and void (*batal demi hukum*) because it was executed without legal entitlement (*without valid causa*). Consequently, decisions rendered by the Governor of DKI Jakarta and the Director General of Agraria / Minister of Home Affairs (including Building Use Rights / HGB Certificates) predicated upon such a grant are unlawful and possess no legal force.

Case Background

The Petitioners for Judicial Review / Original Plaintiffs (Haji Bahasan Pohan and Nn. Otje Lie) were lawful occupants and tenants of land and buildings formerly under *Eigendom Verp.* No. 16482 & 16483 located at Jalan Raden Saleh No. 44, Central Jakarta, pursuant to Housing Occupancy Permits (*SIP B*) issued in 1953 and 1956. Original Defendant II (Ny. [Party A]) claimed the land and house based on Grant Deed No. 22 dated February 4, 1961, from Herman Zadrach Maarten Apitulay and Grant Acceptance Deed No. 93 dated July 18, 1961. Based on this grant, Original Defendant III (Director General of Agraria / Minister of Home Affairs) issued Decree No. SK.170/HGB/DA/71 and HGB Certificates No. 90/Cikini & 91/Cikini in 1972 under the name of Original Defendant II, while Original Defendant I (Governor of DKI Jakarta) issued an eviction order against the Plaintiffs.

The Central Jakarta District Court granted the Plaintiffs' lawsuit (Decision No. 201/1980 G). However, the Jakarta High Court reversed the district court ruling (Decision No. 278/1981 *PT. Perdata*), and the Supreme Court at the cassation level rejected the Plaintiffs' cassation petition (Decision No. 3053 K/Sip/1982). The Plaintiffs subsequently filed for Judicial Review (*Peninjauan Kembali*) incorporating *novum* in the form of official letters from the Director General of Immigration and the Dutch Embassy confirming that the grantor had become a Dutch citizen as of June 11, 1960.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. Based on new documentary evidence (*novum*) from competent immigration and diplomatic institutions, it is conclusively proven that the grantor (Herman Zadrach Maarten Apitulay) became a Dutch citizen on June 11, 1960.
  2. Because the grantor held Dutch citizenship as of June 11, 1960, pursuant to Emergency Law No. 3 of 1960, all of his estate assets in Indonesia were directly placed under State control (P3MB). Consequently, the grant executed via Notarial Deed by Jan Cartian In't Hoot dated February 4, 1961, and the Grant Acceptance Deed dated July 18, 1961, are null and void (*batal demi hukum*) because the grantor no longer possessed the legal authority to grant those assets.
  3. State Administrative official decrees (issued by the Governor of DKI Jakarta and the Director General of Agraria / Minister of Home Affairs) alongside HGB Certificates No. 90 & 91/Cikini issued upon the foundation of such void grants are unlawful and declared to have no legal force.
  4. Considering that the disputed land and buildings remain under State control, Original Plaintiffs I & II as lawful tenants/occupants possess the right to apply to purchase the land/house from the State.
  • Ruling: Granting the Petition for Judicial Review from Petitioner for PK (Haji Bahasan Pohan and Nn. Otje Lie); Annulling the Supreme Court decision dated August 15, 1983, Reg. No. 3053 K/Sip/1982 jo. Jakarta High Court Decision No. 278/1981 jo. Central Jakarta District Court Decision No. 201/1980 G; and Independently Re-Adjudicating: (1) Granting the lawsuit of Plaintiffs I and II in part; (2) Declaring the appellate decision of Defendant I (Decree of the Governor of DKI Jakarta No. Kpts. Banding-012/UP/DKI/II/1980) devoid of legal force; (3) Declaring null and void Grant Deed No. 22 dated February 4, 1961, by Notary Jan Cartian In't Hoot (Rotterdam) and Grant Acceptance Deed No. 93 dated July 18, 1961, by Notary Noezar (Bandung); (4) Declaring Defendant III's Decree No. SK.170/HGB/DA/71 dated June 5, 1971, devoid of legal force; (5) Declaring HGB Certificates No. 90/Cikini and No. 91/Cikini of 1972 under the name of Defendant II devoid of legal force; (6) Declaring Plaintiffs I and II entitled to apply to purchase the land/house located at Jalan Raden Saleh No. 44, Central Jakarta; (7) Rejecting all other and remaining claims.
