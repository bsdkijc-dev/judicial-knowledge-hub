Claim

A claim is a proposition asserted as true within a knowledge object.

Claim Structure

A structured claim should ideally contain:

  • Claim ID
  • Proposition
  • Source
  • Evidence level
  • Date
  • Jurisdiction
  • Verification status

Example

Claim:

A particular regulation establishes a specified procedural requirement.

The proposition should then be connected to:

SOURCE → PROVISION → VERIFICATION

Claim Types

Potential types include:

  • Legal claim
  • Empirical claim
  • Historical claim
  • Institutional claim
  • Statistical claim
  • Analytical claim
  • Predictive claim

Fact vs Analysis

The Wiki should distinguish:

SOURCE-BASED FACT

from

EDITORIAL ANALYSIS

from

AI-GENERATED HYPOTHESIS

AI

AI should not convert:

POSSIBILITY → FACT

or

INFERENCE → AUTHORITATIVE CLAIM

without evidence.

Verification

Every high-impact claim should have a traceable source.
