Restorative Justice Performance Indicator

The indicator measures cases successfully resolved using a restorative justice approach among cases satisfying the applicable criteria.

Eligibility Criteria Identified in the Renstra

  • minor offences or specified loss thresholds;
  • complaint-based offences;
  • offences with a maximum imprisonment threshold of five years in one

 charge, including certain jinayat offences;

  • offences involving children where diversion was unsuccessful;
  • specified traffic offences.

Exclusions

Judges may not apply restorative justice where:

  • the victim or defendant refuses reconciliation;
  • a power relationship exists;
  • the defendant repeats a similar offence within the specified period.

Formula

<math> \frac{\text{Successfully resolved restorative-justice cases}} {\text{Eligible restorative-justice cases}}\times100\ </math>

The Renstra states that the indicator follows the relevant Supreme Court regulations and circulars and refers to PERMA No. 1 of 2024.
