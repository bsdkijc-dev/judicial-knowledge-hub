Judicial Knowledge Wiki as Strategic Initiative

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 369, 2026-08-28 · wiki source · Markdown

Judicial Knowledge Wiki

The Judicial Knowledge Wiki is one of the proposed strategic initiatives under Judicial Knowledge Infrastructure.

Strategic Function

It provides a structured environment for:

  • judicial knowledge curation;
  • institutional memory;
  • legal concepts;
  • case-law knowledge;
  • policy knowledge;
  • training knowledge;
  • machine-readable relationships.

Related Initiatives

  • Indonesian Judicial Knowledge Graph
  • Case-Law Semantic Search
  • Indonesian Legal Ontology
  • Institutional Memory Platform
