JPW-REGULATION-EU-EWC-2025

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 908, 2026-09-15 · wiki source · Markdown

EU European Works Council Directive (Revised) is a European Union regulatory framework agreed upon on October 9, 2025, to strengthen worker rights, enhance employer Information and Consultation (I&C) obligations, and enforce stricter non-compliance penalties for European Works Councils Passage 7.

Executive Summary

The revised Directive ensures European Works Councils (EWCs) can be formed more easily, are better funded, and receive robust legal protections Passage 7. Member States must transpose the regulations into national law by 2027, with full enforcement taking effect in 2028 Passage 7.

Legal Basis

  • EU European Works Council Directive (Agreed October 9, 2025) Passage 7.
  • EU/EEA Member State Transposition Acts, mandated for statutory enactment by 2027 Passage 7.

Background

Accelerated cross-border corporate restructurings, outsourcing, and redundancies necessitate worker consultation at earlier strategic phases Passage 7. Previous legislation contained legal loopholes regarding transnational scope definitions and EWC expense coverage Passage 7.

Objectives

  1. Expand the scope of transnational information and consultation (I&C) Passage 7.
  2. Eliminate exemption status for "legacy" EWCs created prior to the 1994 Directive Passage 7.
  3. Ensure funding certainty for expert advisors, training, and legal representation Passage 7.
  4. Increase gender balance across EWC delegations Passage 7.

Key Provisions

  • Coverage Thresholds: Applies to undertakings employing at least 1,000 workers in the EU/EEA and at least 150 workers in each of at least two Member States Passage 7.
  • Timely Consultation: Management must provide a reasoned written response to EWC opinions prior to finalizing executive decisions Passage 7.
  • Gender Targets: Parties must target a minimum of 40% representation of each gender in EWC membership Passage 7.
  • Confidentiality Rules: Management withholding of confidential information requires objective justification based on Member State criteria Passage 7.

Implementation

  • Phasing Out Legacy EWCs: Exemptions for pre-1994 EWCs are abolished (by end-2027), triggering a 2-year window for renegotiating agreements Passage 7.
  • Plenary Meetings: Increases mandatory plenary sessions to two in-person meetings per year Passage 7.
  • Enhanced Sanctions: Member States must establish effective financial penalties calculated relative to corporate annual turnover Passage 7.

Policy & Compliance Issues

  • Employer Cost Burden: Uncapped funding for expert assistance, legal defense, and mandatory training raises operational cost concerns Passage 7.
  • National Variation: Potential divergence across EU Member States regarding confidentiality thresholds and sanction caps Passage 7.
