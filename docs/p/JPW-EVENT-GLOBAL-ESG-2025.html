JPW-EVENT-GLOBAL-ESG-2025

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 934, 2026-09-15 · wiki source · Markdown

Global Environmental Regulatory Trends – Fall 2025 Update is a global environmental regulatory update webinar and publication hosted by Eversheds Sutherland on September 29, 2025, as part of its Global Sustainability & ESG series [1–5].

Summary

This initiative focused on major developments in environmental regulatory compliance requirements affecting businesses operating across international jurisdictions [1–5]. The webinar examined how evolving environmental rules reshape corporate sustainability frameworks and legal ESG obligations [1–5].

Legal Basis & Disclaimer

  • Formal Legal Basis: Not found in the available sources [1–5].
  • Disclaimer Notice: Materials presented during the event and published on the platform are provided strictly for general informational purposes and do not constitute formal legal advice [1–5].

Background

The global environmental regulatory landscape is evolving rapidly, demanding continuous updates to corporate governance and compliance programs [1–5]. Multinational corporations face growing challenges in aligning cross-border operations with increasingly stringent ESG standards [1–5].

Objectives

  1. Provide insights into the latest international environmental regulatory trends [1–5].
  2. Assist commercial organizations in evaluating the impact of new rules on sustainability and ESG compliance [1–5].
  3. Encourage proactive compliance with emerging global environmental standards [1–5].

Key Provisions & Themes

  • Cross-Border Scope: Addresses environmental regulatory compliance obligations across multiple international jurisdictions [1–5].
  • ESG Integration: Emphasizes incorporating Environmental, Social, and Governance criteria into core corporate compliance strategies [1–5].
  • Non-Consultative Notice: Explicitly clarifies that the information delivered does not replace specialized legal counsel [1–5].

Implementation & Leadership

  • Delivery Format: Conducted virtually on September 29, 2025, under the Eversheds Sutherland Global Sustainability & ESG webinar series [1–5].
  • Primary Contacts: Led by R. Todd Silliman (Partner, Atlanta, US), Annamária Tóthová (Partner, Bratislava, Slovakia), and Claire Gregory (Partner, UK) [1–5].

Policy Issues & Analysis

  • Cross-Jurisdictional Complexity: Challenges faced by international enterprises in harmonizing divergent regional environmental compliance standards [1–5].
  • ESG Risk Mitigation: The necessity of rapid internal adjustments to meet regulatory ESG demands and mitigate legal or reputational exposure [1–5].
