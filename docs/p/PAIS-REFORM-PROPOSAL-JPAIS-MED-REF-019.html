Description / Deskripsi

This case entity examines operational bottlenecks and legal frameworks governing containerized cargo infiltration ("rip-on/rip-off" schemes) and unrated shadow fleet transits across Southeast Asian shipping lanes. It proposes reinforced inter-agency judicial cooperation and digital case integration through SIPP.

Case Overview & Legal Analysis

  • **Case Reference ID:** `JPAIS:CASE:2026-MARITIME-019`
  • **Operational Context:** Investigation into illicit transshipment hubs and container supply chain vulnerabilities within regional transit archipelagos.
  • **Legal Issues:** Jurisdictional boundaries under UNCLOS Article 73/111 (Right of Hot Pursuit), port state control enforcement against unrated P&I insurance, and digital evidence validation in transnational maritime smuggling trials.

Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

  • JPAIS-MED-ID-019: Enforcement relies on inter-agency coordination between the Indonesian Navy (TNI AL), Bakamla, and the Indonesian Marine Police (IMP), supported by port-level customs inspections.

Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan (Jepang & Internasional)

  • JPAIS-MED-JP-019: Advanced maritime jurisdictions employ real-time satellite Automatic Identification System (AIS) monitoring coupled with automated container anomaly scoring models to intercept high-risk shipments prior to port discharge.

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Integrate real-time port telemetry with electronic court bundle filing (e-Berpadu / SIPP) to accelerate judicial review and warrant issuance for suspicious container inspections and shadow fleet detentions.
