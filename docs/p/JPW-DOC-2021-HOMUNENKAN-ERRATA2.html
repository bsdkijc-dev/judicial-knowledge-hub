Policy Analysis

  • Background & Core Facts:
    • FACT: This errata sheet (正誤表) was published by the Ministry of Justice (MOJ) of Japan to rectify two primary administrative sections within the 2021 Ministry of Justice Annual Report (法務年鑑 令和３年):
      • The overview table of major bills reviewed (主な審議法案, pp. 121–123).
      • Statistics on international criminal cooperation regarding fugitive extraditions (国際犯罪関係 ⑵ 逃亡犯罪人引渡関係, p. 132).
    • Inter-Ministerial Legislative Review Details (FY 2021):
      • Office of Criminal Legislative Management (刑事法制管理官室): Reviewed Civil Code & Inherited Land Escheatment Bills (received Feb 12, 2021), Judges Childcare Leave Act Amendment Bill (received Sep 16, 2021), Arbitration Act Amendment Bill (received Oct 13, 2021), Critical Facilities Surrounding Land Use Bill (Cabinet Secretariat, received Feb 5, 2021), Individual Number Bank Account Usage Bill (Cabinet Office, received Jan 22, 2021), and Railway/River/Aviation Bills (MLIT).
      • Criminal Affairs Division (刑事課): Reviewed Copyright Act Amendment Bill (MEXT, received Feb 17, 2021), Banking Act Amendment Bill (FSA, received Feb 19, 2021), Stalker Control Act Amendment Bill (NPA, received Jan 18, 2021), Digital Platform Consumer Protection Bill (Consumer Affairs Agency, received Feb 4, 2021), Patent Act Amendment Bill (METI, received Feb 4, 2021), and National Parks/Plastic Pollution Bills (MOE, received Jan 29 & Feb 9, 2021).
      • Public Security Division (公安課): Reviewed Support for Women with Complex Problems Bill (MHLW, received Feb 24, 2021), Health/Pharmaceutical Revisions (MHLW), and Health/Employment Insurance Revisions.
      • General Affairs Division (総務課): Reviewed Digital Agency Establishment Bill (Cabinet Secretariat, received Jan 15, 2021), Digital Society Formulation Bill (received Jan 25, 2021), Prevention of Educator Violence Bill (MEXT, received Apr 28, 2021), and Broadcasting Revisions (MIC).
    • Correction of Extradition Statistics (Page 132):
      • Original Text (Incorrect): Stated zero instances of surrendering fugitive offenders to foreign countries upon external request in 2021.
      • Corrected Text: Confirms exactly **1 case** of surrendering a fugitive offender to a foreign country based on an external request in 2021 (我が国が令和３年中に外国からの請求に基づき、犯罪人を引き渡した事例は１件ある).
      • Incoming Extraditions: Records 2 cases of receiving surrendered fugitives from foreign countries based on requests by Japanese prosecutors in 2021.
  • Interpretations & Structural Inferences:
    • INTERPRETATION: Reflects MOJ's administrative accountability and commitment to maintaining reliable public historical archives regarding legislative review and international judicial assistance.
    • INFERENCE: Active review by internal MOJ units (e.g., 刑事法制管理官室 and 刑事課) over draft legislation across nearly all executive ministries underscores MOJ's role as a gatekeeper for criminal and civil legal harmonization in Japan.
