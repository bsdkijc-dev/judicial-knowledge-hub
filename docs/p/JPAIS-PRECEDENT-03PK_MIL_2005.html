Overview

Supreme Court Judicial Review Decision No. 03 PK/Mil/2005 annuls the in absentia ruling rendered by the Medan Military Court I-02 against Defendant Sergeant Major (Serda) [Defendant]. The Supreme Court granted the Petition for Judicial Review after discovering a manifest error committed by the Judex Facti. The Defendant had never deserted from Korem 023/KS Sibolga; pursuant to official transfer orders issued by superior authorities, the Defendant had been officially reassigned to Korem 031/WB in Batam. Furthermore, the Medan Military Court I-02 lacked jurisdiction to try a Defendant stationed outside its legal territory without a valid transfer of authority.

Case Background

The Defendant, [Defendant] (Serda Nrp. 534920, Ba Kima Korem-023/KS), was charged with peacetime desertion under Article 87 paragraph (1) item 2 jo. paragraph (2) of the KUHPM, on the grounds of allegedly abandoning Unit Korem 023/KS Sibolga without authorization starting October 1, 1999. On March 24, 2003, the Medan Military Court I-02 rendered an in absentia decision (Decision No. PUT/67-K/MM.I-02/AD/III/2003) declaring the Defendant guilty and sentencing him to 2 years and 8 months of imprisonment alongside an additional penalty of dismissal from military service.

The Defendant filed a Petition for Judicial Review (PK) presenting evidence that he was never stationed at Korem 023/KS Sibolga during the contested period; rather, pursuant to the Regional Military Commander I/BB Order (Sprin/1578/X/1999) jo. Commander of Yonif Linud-100/PS Order (Sprin/166/XI/1999), the Defendant had been officially reassigned to a new duty station at Korem 031/WB based in Batam as of October 29, 1999.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi: The grounds for judicial review are justified due to a manifest error committed by the Judex Facti, given that the Defendant never committed the criminal offense as charged by the Military Prosecutor. Since October 29, 1999, the Defendant never abandoned service, was actively serving at Korem 031/WB, and was never stationed at Korem 023/KS Sibolga. The Medan Military Court I-02 lacked competence to try TNI personnel operating outside its territorial jurisdiction absent a valid delegation of authority from another competent military court.
  • Ruling: Granting the petition for judicial review from Petitioner [Defendant]; annulling the Medan Military Court I-02 decision dated March 24, 2003, No. PUT/67-K/MM.I-02/AD/III/2003; and Adjudicating Independently: (1) Declaring Defendant [Defendant], Serda Nrp. 534920 not legally and convincingly proven guilty of the criminal offense of "peacetime desertion"; (2) Acquitting the Defendant of all charges; (3) Restoring the Defendant's rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity (rehabilitation); (4) Charging all procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State.
