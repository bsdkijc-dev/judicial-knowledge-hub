Description / Deskripsi

This page records the empirical analysis of official judicial statistics and the Problem Inventory List (Daftar Inventaris Masalah / DIM) for business competition objection cases (Komisi Pengawas Persaingan Usaha / KPPU) at the Makassar Commercial Court at the Makassar District Court, covering the reporting period from 2021 through 2026. The primary dataset reveals a sustained administrative readiness—maintaining a specialized panel of 6 (six) certified competition law judges annually—contrasted with an extraordinarily low litigation density, recording only 1 (one) objection case across the entire 6-year period. The official DIM document dated 10 July 2026, signed by Court Leadership and the Registrar, officially records a status of "Nihil" (Nil). All structural entities, numerical records, and signatories have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher Cecep Mustafa.

Problem / Masalah

  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-034: Resource Over-Allocation vs. Low Caseload Density, where maintaining 6 specialized competition law judges continuously over 6 years to handle a single case (Case No. 1/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2024/PN Niaga Mks) represents a severe imbalance in judicial workload distribution compared to high-density commercial courts.
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-035: Geographic Disincentive & Access Barriers, where the near-absence of objection filings across Eastern Indonesia reflects significant geographic and logistical cost barriers for local business actors traveling to Makassar to challenge KPPU administrative sanctions.

Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

  • JPAIS-MED-ID-034: The Makassar Commercial Court fulfills formal statutory requirements by maintaining a dedicated judicial roster of 6 certified judges, successfully adjudicating Case No. 1/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2024/PN Niaga Mks to completion by issuing a judgment rejecting the objection without Supreme Court cassation escalation, while submitting periodic DIM reports recording procedural obstacles as "Nihil".

Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

  • JPAIS-MED-JP-034: In Japan (Tokyo High Court / JFTC), the court utilizes fully integrated electronic hearings (*online proceedings*) and circuit hearings to prevent geographic disincentives for business actors located in distant prefectures (e.g., Hokkaido or Okinawa), ensuring equitable access without requiring physical presence.

Gap / Kesenjangan

  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-034: The primary structural gap is the absence of an integrated digital filing incentive under PERMA No. 3/2021 tailored to regional commercial courts, where current reliance on physical court presence discourages regional enterprises from exercising their statutory right to judicial review.

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Transform the Makassar Commercial Court into a specialized *e-Court Digital Center* for competition review across Eastern Indonesia and implement a flexible judicial deployment model where certified judges in low-density courts can be assigned virtually to support high-density commercial courts via cross-court electronic panels.

Action / Aksi

  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-034: Issue a Supreme Court Circular (SEMA) establishing full digital trial options for competition cases in regional Commercial Courts.
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-035: Integrate cross-jurisdictional virtual judicial panels within the SIPP/e-Court architecture under BSDK oversight.

Pilot / Pilot Proyek

  • JPAIS-MED-PILOT-034: Implementation of the Makassar Commercial Court as a Digital Hub for Business Competition Dispute Services in Eastern Indonesia.

KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

  • JPAIS-MED-KPI-034: 100% adoption of electronic filing (*e-Court*) and remote evidentiary hearings for regional KPPU objections; dispute resolution duration $\le 60$ working days per case.

Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Re-evaluating judicial certification quotas based on empirical caseload density rather than static court status, alongside optimizing mandatory full electronic trial provisions to reduce litigation costs for regional enterprises.

Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Amending PERMA No. 3/2021 to explicitly mandate virtual trials for parties residing outside the physical seat of regional Commercial Courts and refining Supreme Court DIM reporting mechanisms.

Evidence / Bukti Empiris

  • Official DIM document dated July 10, 2026, signed by the Chief Judge of Makassar District Court (Dr. I Wayan Gede Rumega, S.H., M.H.) and Registrar (Handri Mamudi, S.H., M.H.), combined with registration records for Case No. 1/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2024/PN Niaga Mks.
