Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 136 K/TUN/2003 dated October 15, 2003, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal rules regarding the limits of State Administrative Court jurisdiction in civil service disputes: State Administrative Courts do not have the authority to determine the form or type of disciplinary punishment against a Civil Servant (*PNS*), as such authority rests entirely with the State Administrative Official. State Administrative judges are not justified in usurping the discretionary power of State Administrative Officials by altering or mitigating the imposition of civil servant disciplinary sanctions.

Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Respondent for Cassation ([Plaintiff], Junior Administrative Officer Class III/b, NIP [removed], former Civil Servant at the Regional Office of the Department of Justice and Human Rights of Maluku) was subjected to a disciplinary punishment of Honorable Discharge Not at Own Request (*Pemberhentian Dengan Hormat Tidak Atas Permintaan Sendiri*) as a Civil Servant pursuant to the Decree of the Minister of Justice of the Republic of Indonesia No. M.130-KP.05.07 of 1998 dated September 1, 1998. This punishment was imposed because the Plaintiff was proven to have committed a disgraceful act, namely cohabiting (*kumpul kebo*) without a valid marriage bond with a woman named [Third party] / [Third party] in Ambon (despite the Plaintiff already being married to Mrs. Halidjah and having 3 children), as well as being absent from work without legitimate reason for 81 working days (October 1996 to January 1997).

The Plaintiff pursued an administrative appeal to the Civil Service Advisory Board (*BAPEK*). BAPEK issued Decree No. 119/KPTS/BAPEK/2000 Jakarta dated July 27, 2000, upholding the Minister of Justice's decision. The Plaintiff subsequently challenged BAPEK's decree at the Jakarta State Administrative High Court (serving as the first-instance court for administrative appeal efforts pursuant to Article 48 jo. Article 51 paragraph (3) of Law No. 5 of 1986). The Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 103/G/2001/PT.TUN.JKT dated November 28, 2001) granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part, annulled BAPEK's decree, and ordered BAPEK to change the type of disciplinary punishment from discharge to a demotion in rank by one level for 1 year. Dissatisfied with the ruling, BAPEK filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The cassation objections raised by the Petitioner for Cassation (BAPEK) are well-founded because the Jakarta State Administrative High Court committed an error in applying the law in its considerations stating that BAPEK violated the principle of justice and ordering a demotion in rank by one level for 1 year.
  2. Pursuant to Article 7 of Government Regulation No. 30 of 1980, State Administrative Courts lack the authority to determine the form and type of disciplinary punishment imposed upon Civil Servants, as such authority belongs exclusively to the State Administrative Official.
  3. The conduct of the Plaintiff / Respondent for Cassation in cohabiting with another woman without marriage and being absent from work for 81 working days is legally proven to violate Article 2 letters c, g, h, w, x jo. Article 3 paragraph (1) letter a of Government Regulation No. 30 of 1980 jo. Government Regulation No. 10 of 1983 jo. Government Regulation No. 45 of 1990. The decisions of BAPEK and the Minister of Justice ordering honorable discharge not at own request are in accordance with the law and are not arbitrary, rendering the Jakarta State Administrative High Court judgment subject to annulment.
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation CIVIL SERVICE ADVISORY BOARD (*BADAN PERTIMBANGAN KEPEGAWAIAN / BAPEK*); Annulling the Jakarta State Administrative High Court decision dated November 28, 2001, No. 103/G/2001/PT.TUN.JKT; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Rejecting the objection of [Plaintiff] as stated in his letter dated December 18, 1998; (2) Affirming the disciplinary punishment decision of the Minister of Justice No. M.130-Kp.05.07 of 1998 dated September 1, 1998, in the form of Honorable Discharge Not at Own Request as a Civil Servant imposed upon [Plaintiff], born [details removed], NIP [removed], Junior Administrative Officer, Class III/b, employee at the Regional Office of the Department of Justice and Human Rights of Maluku; (3) If [Plaintiff]'s salary was temporarily suspended, such salary shall be paid in accordance with prevailing laws and regulations; (4) [Plaintiff] is not granted pension rights as he fails to meet the requirements stipulated under prevailing laws and regulations; (5) This decision takes effect from the date of its enactment; Ordering the Respondent for Cassation to pay cassation-level court costs amounting to Rp 200,000 (two hundred thousand rupiahs). *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Wednesday, October 15, 2003, by the Cassation Panel of Justices: Prof. DR. Paulus E. Lotulung, SH. [Junior Chairman for State Administrative Chamber / Presiding Judge], Ny. Titi Nurmala Siagian, SH., and Widayatno Sastrohardjono, SH., MSc. [Associate Justices]).*
