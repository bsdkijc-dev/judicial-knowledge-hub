By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 1959, 2026-09-28 · wiki source · Markdown

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 1974 K/Pdt/2001 (dated September 29, 2003) is an Indonesian land and civil evidence law jurisprudence precedent establishing a fundamental rule regarding the evidentiary requirements for establishing signature forgery in land rights transfers / authentic Deeds of Sale and Purchase (AJB):

"The transfer of land rights declared legally flawed due to signature forgery, rendering the land sale and purchase null and void, MUST BE PROVEN THROUGH AN EXAMINATION BY THE CRIMINOLOGY LABORATORY (NATIONAL POLICE LABKRIM) OR PURSUANT TO A CRIMINAL JUDGMENT DECLARING THE SIGNATURE FORGED."

Executive Summary

The Supreme Court affirmed that appellate courts are unjustified in annulling Deeds of Sale and Purchase (Akta Jual Beli / AJB) and certificate name transfers solely based on visual signature comparison assessments conducted by judges without expert assistance. Because the Plaintiff failed to substantiate allegations of signature forgery via National Police Criminology Laboratory examination results or a legally binding criminal court judgment, the Plaintiff's lawsuit must be declared inadmissible (Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard / NO).

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioners for Cassation ([Party C], S.H., Ny. [Party B], S.H., PT Puri Ayu Lestari, [Party D]), annulled Bandung High Court Decision No. 445/Pdt/1999/PT.Bdg dated June 29, 2000, and independently adjudicated (mengadili sendiri) by declaring the Plaintiff's ([Party A], S.H.) Convention and Reconvention Lawsuit Inadmissible (Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard / NO).

Legal / Procedural Issues

1. Legal Standards for Proving Signature Forgery in Authentic Deeds / Land AJB (Article 1868 of the Civil Code / Civil Evidence Law)

Is an Appellate Court legally justified in declaring a signature within an authentic Deed of Sale and Purchase (AJB) forged solely based on visual observations made by judges in court without an examination by the National Police Criminology Laboratory or a criminal judgment?

Supreme Court Rule: Unjustified / Evidentiary Defect. Evaluating the falsity of a signature on an authentic deed drawn before a Notary/PPAT must not rest upon the naked visual inspection of judges (judicial subjectivity). To prove a signature was forged, Civil Evidence Law mandates scientific proof in the form of National Police Criminology Laboratory (Labkrim) Examination Results or a legally binding criminal court judgment declaring the signature forged.

2. Appropriate Cassation Verdict Qualification for Evidentiary Defects (Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard / NO)

When a Plaintiff grounds a lawsuit on the premise that a transfer of land rights is null and void due to signature forgery, yet fails to tender National Police Labkrim examination results or a criminal judgment into evidence, the lawsuit must be declared inadmissible (Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard / NO).

Question of Law vs Question of Fact

Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the Judex Facti Bandung High Court (Decision No. 445/Pdt/1999/PT.Bdg) err in applying the law of evidence (foutieve toepassing van het bewijsrecht) by annulling deeds of sale and purchase and land rights transfers based solely on visual judge comparisons of signatures, and what constitutes the legal evidentiary standard for signature forgery in civil disputes?

Supreme Court Legal Answer: Yes. Bandung High Court misapplied the law of evidence. The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioners for Cassation, annulled Bandung High Court Decision No. 445/Pdt/1999/PT.Bdg dated June 29, 2000, and independently adjudicated by declaring the Plaintiff's Lawsuit Inadmissible (Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard / NO).

Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

Statutory Regulations

  • Civil Code (KUHPerdata / BW): Articles 1868, 1870, 1871 (Evidentiary Force of Authentic Deeds).
  • HIR / RBg: Provisions on Civil Procedural Law and Witness/Expert/Documentary Evidence.
  • Law No. 14 of 1970 jo. Law No. 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court: Cassation and Independent Adjudication Authority.

Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

  • Doctrine of Authentic Deed Falsity Proof: Authentic deeds drawn before Public Officials (Notaries/PPAT) possess complete evidentiary force (volledig bewijskracht). Rebuttal evidence attempting to defeat an authentic deed on grounds of signature forgery must be scientifically proven via Police Criminology / Forensic Laboratory testing or pursuant to a legally binding criminal judgment (inkracht van gewijsde).

  • The Bandung High Court verdict declaring AJBs and certificate name transfers null and void while ordering Rp 2 Billion in damages was entirely annulled by the Supreme Court.

Uncertainties and Gaps

Separation Between Civil and Criminal Proof in Document Forgery: This landmark ruling establishes absolute legal clarity in Indonesia: in land disputes involving authentic deeds (Notary/PPAT AJBs), Plaintiffs cannot invalidate certificates or deeds merely by asserting unilaterally that "my signature was forged." Without scientific proof from the National Police Criminology Laboratory or a criminal court judgment declaring such signature forged, civil lawsuits must be declared inadmissible (NO).

Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

What evidentiary requirements apply when someone seeks to cancel a land sale or certificate on grounds of signature forgery pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence?
Mandatory examination by a Criminology Laboratory or a Criminal Judgment. Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence No. 1974 K/Pdt/2001:

  • The transfer of land rights based on allegations of signature forgery cannot be declared null and void merely based on visual observations made by judges in court.
  • Allegations of signature forgery must be proven through an examination conducted by the Criminology Laboratory (National Police Labkrim) or pursuant to a legally binding criminal court judgment declaring the signature forged.
  • If a Plaintiff fails to establish signature forgery via Labkrim evidence or a criminal judgment, the lawsuit seeking cancellation of land rights transfers must be declared inadmissible (Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard / NO).
