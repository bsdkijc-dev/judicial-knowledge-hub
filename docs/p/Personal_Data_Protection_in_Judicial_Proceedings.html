Personal Data Protection in Judicial Proceedings

The Renstra identifies the need for a Supreme Court regulation concerning personal-data protection in judicial proceedings.

Identified Rationale

Digital transformation increases the risk of data leakage.

The regulatory challenge is therefore to maintain transparency while protecting personal data.

Target

2028

Responsible Body

Supreme Court Working Group

Strategic Relationship

Digital Judiciary → creates → Data Protection Risk

Data Protection Regulation → balances → Judicial Transparency + Privacy
