Overview

Supreme Court Judicial Review (*Peninjauan Kembali / PK*) Decision No. 55 PK/PID/1996 dated October 25, 1996, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal standards regarding two critical legal aspects:

  • **Authority of the Public Prosecutor to File for Judicial Review:** The Public Prosecutor / Prosecutor's Office, acting in representation of State and public interests, possesses the right and authority to file a petition for Judicial Review (*PK*) against a Supreme Court cassation ruling that acquitted a Defendant of the charges, in the name of upholding the law, justice, and the public interest.
  • **Proof of Incitement Offenses (Articles 160 & 161 of the KUHP):** A series of acts committed by the Defendant (General Chairperson of the Central Executive Board of the Indonesian Prosperous Labor Union / SBSI)—consisting of public speeches, strike calls, speech texts, and written circulars provoking worker masses to stage unauthorized demonstrations resulting in riots and property damage in Medan—legally and convincingly satisfy the elements of continuous incitement (Article 160 jo. Article 64 paragraph (1) of the KUHP) and disseminating inciting writings (Article 161 paragraph (1) of the KUHP).

Case Background

The Defendant, Dr. [Defendant], SH, MA (General Chairperson of the Central Executive Board of SBSI), was charged with continuous oral and written incitement (Article 160 jo. Article 64(1) of the KUHP) alongside disseminating inciting writings (Article 161(1) of the KUHP). These acts were executed via leadership lectures in Pematang Siantar, the "Strike/Demonstration Call" circular, and SBSI central board circular letters provoking workers to strike, which culminated in a mass demonstration of approximately 20,000 workers in Medan on April 14, 1994, resulting in rioting, destruction of buildings, factories, and vehicles.

The Medan District Court (Decision No. 966/Pid.B/1994/PN.Mdn dated November 7, 1994) sentenced the Defendant to 3 years of imprisonment. The Medan High Court (Decision No. 188/Pid/1994/PT.Mdn dated January 16, 1995) enhanced the sentence to 4 years of imprisonment. Upon cassation, the Supreme Court (Decision Reg. No. 395 K/Pid/1995 dated September 29, 1995) annulled the lower and appellate court rulings and acquitted the Defendant of all charges. The Head of the Medan State Prosecutor's Office, acting as Public Prosecutor, subsequently filed a petition for Judicial Review (*PK*) to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • **Ratio Decidendi:**
  1. The Public Prosecutor, acting in representation of State and Public interests, is entitled to file a petition for Judicial Review (*PK*) against a Supreme Court cassation decision that acquits a Defendant.
  2. The previous Supreme Court cassation decision Reg. No. 395 K/Pid/1995 contained manifest error/mistake (*kekhilafan/kekeliruan yang nyata*) because it disregarded applicable positive criminal law provisions (Articles 160 and 161 of the KUHP) and acquitted the Defendant despite the material acts of incitement having been proven legally and convincingly at trial.
  3. Grounded upon the Supreme Court's own reasoning and the well-founded reasoning of the *Judex Facti*, the Supreme Court declared sufficient evidence legally and convincingly establishing that the Defendant is guilty of continuous incitement (Article 160 jo. Article 64(1) of the KUHP) and disseminating inciting writings (Article 161(1) of the KUHP).
  • **Ruling:** Granting the petition for Judicial Review from Petitioner for PK Head of Medan State Prosecutor's Office / Public Prosecutor; Annulling the Supreme Court decision dated September 29, 1995, Reg. No. 395 K/Pid/1995; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring Defendant Dr. [Defendant], SH, MA legally and convincingly proven guilty of committing the criminal offenses of: "Publicly inciting orally and in writing to commit a criminal offense, to disobey lawful commands issued pursuant to statutory regulations and to disobey statutory provisions, committed as a continuous act" and "Publicly disseminating inciting writings to incite persons to commit a criminal offense, to fail to comply with lawful commands issued pursuant to statutory regulations, and to disobey statutory provisions with intent that the contents of the inciting writings become publicly known"; (2) Sentencing the Defendant to imprisonment for a term of 4 (four) years; (3) Crediting the period of detention already served by the Defendant in full against the sentence imposed; (4) Ordering that evidence items remain attached to the case file; (5) Ordering the Defendant to pay procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction (assessed at Rp 2,500 at the PK level).
