Core Definition

Small Claims Procedure (Gugatan Sederhana) is an expedited, simplified civil adjudication mechanism designed to resolve low-value monetary disputes efficiently through a single-judge panel within strict statutory timeframes, without formal written replies (Replik/Duplik) and without ordinary appeal or cassation remedies.

  • Simple Definition: A fast-track court process for straightforward money disputes under a specific financial ceiling that allows citizens and businesses to obtain a judgment within 25 days without needing a lawyer.
  • Legal / Technical Definition: A specialized summary civil proceeding established under Supreme Court Regulations (PERMA No. 2/2015 jo. PERMA No. 4/2019) that waives traditional lengthy pleadings, permits self-representation or direct corporate employee representation, restricts legal remedies to internal judge objections, and limits jurisdiction strictly to breach of contract (*wanprestasi*) and tort (*perbuatan melawan hukum*) claims meeting statutory monetary thresholds.
  • Plain-Language Explanation: It functions as a judicial "express lane" for smaller financial disputes, cutting through bureaucratic red tape so plaintiffs do not spend more on legal fees than the amount being claimed.

Why It Matters

  • Expedites Dispute Resolution: Compresses the entire trial cycle from months or years down to a strict 25-working-day limit, preventing commercial stagnation.
  • Reduces Litigation & Transaction Costs: Eliminates mandatory attorney representation fees and reduces court costs, making justice economically viable for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) and individual citizens.
  • Alleviates Court Dockets: Diverts minor commercial disputes away from general civil panels, freeing up judicial resources for complex high-value litigation.
  • Enhances Commercial Trust and Predictability: Provides micro-entrepreneurs and retail creditors with an enforceable legal mechanism to recover debts without expensive, protracted litigation.

Key Principles

Principle of Summary and Expedited Adjudication (Asas Pemeriksaan Singkat dan Cepat)

  • Meaning: The entire examination—from filing, mediation, and evidentiary hearings to final judgment—must be completed within a strict limit of 25 working days.
  • Why It Matters: Prevents procedural stalling and ensures timely economic justice.
  • Legal Basis / Source: PERMA No. 2/2015 Art. 10; Law No. 48/2009 Art. 2(4).
  • Related Concepts: Fast-track litigation, procedural speed.

Principle of Self-Representation and Simplified Pleading (Asas Beracara Tanpa Wajib Advokat)

  • Meaning: Plaintiffs and defendants may represent themselves personally or through authorized corporate employees without needing a licensed attorney.
  • Why It Matters: Lowers economic barriers to court access for ordinary citizens and small business owners.
  • Legal Basis / Source: PERMA No. 2/2015 Arts. 4 & 5; PERMA No. 4/2019 Art. 4.
  • Related Concepts: Pro se litigation, simplified claim forms.

Principle of Finality and Exclusion of Ordinary Appeals (Asas Putusan Tingkat Pertama dan Terakhir)

  • Meaning: The judgment of the single judge is final and binding immediately; ordinary legal remedies such as Appeal (*Banding*) and Cassation (*Kasasi*) are strictly barred.
  • Why It Matters: Ensures absolute finality and prevents wealthy litigants from using endless appellate loops to exhaust small creditors.
  • Legal Basis / Source: PERMA No. 2/2015 Art. 30; Law No. 14/1985.
  • Related Concepts: Non-appealable judgment, internal judge objection (*Keberatan*).

Principle of Substantive Limitation on Claim Scope (Asas Batasan Nilai dan Sengketa Tertentu)

  • Meaning: The procedure is restricted exclusively to contractual breach (*wanprestasi*) and torts (*PMH*) where the total financial claim does not exceed the statutory monetary ceiling.
  • Why It Matters: Keeps the procedure simple and prevents complex multi-party land or family disputes from bypassing regular civil safeguards.
  • Legal Basis / Source: PERMA No. 2/2015 Art. 3 jo. PERMA No. 4/2019 Art. 3.
  • Related Concepts: Monetary threshold, breach of contract.

Indonesia Section

  • Statutory Eligibility Thresholds (PERMA No. 4/2019):
    • Maximum Claim Value: Up to **IDR 500,000,000** (Five Hundred Million Rupiah). *(Note: Originally IDR 200 million under PERMA 2/2015).*
    • Substantive Subject Matter: Strictly limited to:
  1. Breach of contract (*Wanprestasi*);
  2. Unlawful acts / Torts (*Perbuatan Melawan Hukum / PMH*).
    • Geographic and Party Conditions: Plaintiff and Defendant must reside within the same court jurisdiction (district/municipality), and the dispute cannot involve land title ownership disputes or matters falling under specialized religious or commercial (HKI/NIAGA) courts unless explicitly commercial contract-based.

  • Procedural Steps & Strict Timeline (25 Working Days):

  1. Days 1–3: Filing via e-Court or direct court registry; judge preliminary examination of admissibility.
  2. Days 4–14: Appointment of single judge (*Hakim Tunggal*), summons, mandatory mediation session (max 30 days under PERMA 1/2016 adapted for fast track, or integrated 14-day window).
  3. Days 15–22: Reading of claim, defendant's answer (*Jawaban* without Replik/Duplik), and presentation of documentary/witness evidence.
  4. Days 23–25: Pronouncement of judgment and electronic delivery.
  • Exclusive Legal Remedy (Keberatan):
    • Dissatisfied parties cannot appeal to the High Court; instead, they may file an objection (*Keberatan*) to the Head of the District Court within 7 days of judgment, adjudicated by a 3-judge panel whose decision is final.

Singapore State Courts — Small Claims Tribunals (SCT) Up to S$20,000 (extendable to S$30,000 by consent) Strict no-lawyer rule; parties must represent themselves. Orders are legally binding; appeal restricted strictly to points of law before High Court.

Attorneys generally barred in small claims hearings to keep proceedings informal. Plaintiff has no right to appeal; defendant may appeal for a completely new trial (*Trial de Novo*).
United Kingdom County Court Small Claims Track Up to £10,000 for general claims (£1,000 for personal injury). Parties represent themselves or use lay representatives; legal costs rarely recoverable. Appeal limited to narrow judicial review or procedural error grounds.

Important Decisions & Chamber Plenary Guidance

  • Decision: Supreme Court Plenary Clarification on Overlapping Land and Small Claims

 * *Issuing Body:* Mahkamah Agung RI (Civil Chamber Plenary)
 * *Instrument / SEMA Ref:* SEMA No. 2/2019 jo. SEMA No. 1/2023
 * *Legal Principle:* Small claims procedures cannot be used to circumvent regular civil proceedings when the underlying dispute touches upon disputed land ownership titles (*hak atas tanah*), even if framed as a monetary compensation claim.
 * *Why It Matters:* Prevents litigants from abusing fast-track procedures to bypass complex evidentiary property trials.
 * *Official Source:* Supreme Court Chamber Reports.

Timeline

2015-08-06 Promulgation of PERMA No. 2/2015 Introduced the Small Claims Procedure (*Gugatan Sederhana*) with a monetary ceiling of IDR 200 million.
2018-04-05 Integration with e-Court (PERMA No. 3/2018) Allowed small claims to be filed electronically, accelerating processing times.
2019-08-07 Promulgation of PERMA No. 4/2019 Expanded the monetary ceiling from IDR 200 million to IDR 500 million and explicitly included tort (*Perbuatan Melawan Hukum*) claims.
2022-11-15 Integration with PERMA No. 7/2022 Fully digitized small claims hearings, enabling remote e-Litigation for commercial debt recovery.

Debates & Controversies

  • Debate: Expanding Small Claims Ceilings vs. Judicial Quality Control

 * *Position A (Pro-Expansion Advocates):* Raising the monetary ceiling beyond IDR 500 million would help clear commercial backlogs and give SMEs faster access to debt recovery.
 * *Position B (Pragmatic Jurists):* Fast-track single-judge proceedings lack the rigorous appellate safety nets of ordinary civil trials; raising ceilings too high risks hasty miscarriages of justice in complex commercial cases.
 * *Evidence:* High compliance rates and low objection error rates in current IDR 500 million commercial debt dockets.
 * *Current Status:* The threshold remains fixed at IDR 500 million under PERMA No. 4/2019, balanced by e-Court integration.

Rule-of-Law Connection

  • *Access to Justice:* Removes the financial barrier of hiring lawyers for small-scale commercial disputes.
  • *Legal Certainty:* Provides predictable, time-bound debt recovery for creditors, strengthening market confidence.
  • *Judicial Efficiency:* Optimizes resource distribution across the court system.

AI & Future of Law

  • *Automated Claim Intake:* AI form-fillers guiding pro se litigants through standardized small claims templates.
  • *Pre-Screening Algorithms:* Automated checks filtering out ineligible claims (e.g., land disputes or claims exceeding IDR 500 million) before judge assignment.
  • *Instant Judgment Templates:* Standardized electronic judgment generation for uncontested default debt recoveries.

Common Misconceptions

  • *Myth:* A plaintiff must hire an advocate to file a Small Claims action (*Gugatan Sederhana*).

 * *Reality:* PERMA No. 2/2015 explicitly permits plaintiffs to represent themselves (*pro se*) or use authorized corporate staff without a lawyer.
 * *Source:* PERMA No. 2/2015 Art. 4.

  • *Myth:* If a party loses a Small Claims case, they can appeal to the High Court (*Banding*).

 * *Reality:* Ordinary appeals and cassation are barred; the only available remedy is an internal objection (*Keberatan*) reviewed by a 3-judge district court panel.
 * *Source:* PERMA No. 2/2015 Art. 30.

Practical Example

  • *Scenario:* A software vendor in Bandung delivers an IT system worth IDR 350,000,000 to a retail client who refuses to pay, committing a clear breach of contract (*wanprestasi*).
  • *Legal Question:* Can the vendor use the Small Claims Procedure, and how long will it take?
  • *Applicable Principle:* Monetary Ceiling & Fast-Track Adjudication (PERMA 4/2019).
  • *Possible Analysis:*

 1. The claim value (IDR 350M) is below the IDR 500M ceiling, and it is a pure contractual breach between parties within the same court jurisdiction.
 2. The vendor files online via e-Court without hiring an attorney.
 3. The single judge schedules mediation; if unsuccessful, the case is tried and decided within 25 working days.

  • *Limitation:* If the client files a counterclaim exceeding IDR 500 million or raises complex non-contractual property ownership issues, the single judge must issue an order terminating the small claims track and converting it to ordinary civil proceedings.

Key Terms

Term Definition Related Term
Hakim Tunggal A single judge assigned to examine and adjudicate a Small Claims case from intake to judgment. Fast-Track Adjudication
Keberatan The exclusive internal legal remedy (objection) filed against a Small Claims judgment before a 3-judge district court panel. Non-Appealable Judgment
Wanprestasi Breach of contract; one of the two exclusive substantive legal grounds permitted under Small Claims jurisdiction. Civil Claim, PERMA 2/2015
Perbuatan Melawan Hukum (PMH) Tort or unlawful act; added as an eligible small claims category under PERMA No. 4 of 2019. Civil Liability
