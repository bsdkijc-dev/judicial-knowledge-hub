Comparative Legal Research on the Effect of Defendant's Sensibility to Punishment on Sentencing (Korean: 피고인의 형벌감수성이 양형에 미치는 영향에 관한 비교법적 연구) is an official judicial policy research report published by the Judicial Policy Research Institute (JPRI) of the Supreme Court of Korea in 2016.1 It analyzes the theoretical basis, doctrinal concepts, domestic judicial practice and international comparative law on the defendant's "sensibility to punishment" (Strafempfindlichkeit, 형벌감수성) and collateral consequences, with the aim of framing fair and proportionate sentencing.

Legal basis

The research was conducted under the Supreme Court Annual Research Plan 2016. The provisions evaluated include:

  • Criminal Act, Article 51 (형법 제51조)
  • Court Organization Act, Article 81-7 (법원조직법 제81조의7)
  • Civil Act, Article 765 (민법 제765조)

Foreign provisions compared: US Federal Rules of Criminal Procedure Rule 32, USSG §5H and 18 U.S.C. § 3553; German StGB §§ 46, 42, 54, 60 and StPO § 267; Swedish Criminal Code (Brottsbalken) Ch. 29 §§ 5–7; French Code of Criminal Procedure Art. 81 and Criminal Code Art. 132-19; Japanese Criminal Code Draft Art. 48 and Code of Criminal Procedure Art. 482.

Background

"Sensibility to punishment" is the degree of subjective, individual suffering or impact that a defendant experiences from a criminal sanction and its accompanying collateral consequences. Korea's Sentencing Guidelines (양형기준), adopted in 2007, reduced sentencing disparity. Public controversy arises, however, when judges treat secondary effects, such as dismissal from work, loss of a professional licence or family dependents, as mitigating factors.2

A date-violence case involving a medical student at the Gwangju District Court drew wide criticism because a fine was imposed so that the defendant would not be expelled from university; the public perceived it as a favoritism ruling (봐주기 판결).

Objectives

  1. Prevent mechanical sentencing by weighing the defendant's whole personality (전인격적 특성) in a measured and transparent way.
  2. Uphold distributive justice based on "noble compassion" and substantive equality without undermining legal certainty.
  3. Provide an international comparative reference and recommendations for amending procedure and sentencing law.

Scope

The study covers a survey of 502 sitting Korean judges, evaluation of controversial domestic cases (the Gwangju medical-student case), the German Strafempfindlichkeit doctrine, the US Sentencing Guidelines departure framework, the Swedish billighetshänsyn factors, and the French pre-sentence investigation system.

Policy problems

  • Mechanical sentencing. Overly rigid application of guidelines risks ignoring the personal characteristics of the defendant that Criminal Act Art. 51 requires courts to consider.
  • Substantive justice versus public perception. Ignoring sensibility to punishment offends substantive equality (실질적 평등) and distributive justice (배분적 정의, cf. Rawls' Difference Principle), because the same nominal penalty imposes very unequal suffering on different people; considering it without explicit standards invites accusations of discrimination and favoritism.
  • Terminological confusion. Practice often conflates "sensibility" with "responsiveness" (형벌감응성), the degree to which a defendant is deterred from reoffending.

Legal and policy gaps

  • No explicit guideline provision. The current guidelines do not list loss of status (신분상 불이익) or dismissal as an official factor, and for bribery offenses they instead bar consideration of loss of status.
  • No collateral-consequences database. Korea has no integrated system recording secondary administrative and legal sanctions of conviction, unlike the US National Inventory of the Collateral Consequences of Conviction (NICCC).
  • Weak duty to give reasons. Judges are not strictly required to explain in detail their choice of sanction and their treatment of sensibility to punishment.

Comparative analysis

Jurisdiction Approach
United States Departure and variance under the Federal Rules and USSG. Koon v. United States (1996) permits downward departure where collateral effects on employment or treatment in prison are extraordinary. Public NICCC database of collateral consequences.
United Kingdom Broad judicial discretion under the Criminal Justice Act 2003 within personal mitigation and the principles of parsimony and mercy.
Germany StGB § 46(1) requires courts to consider the effect of punishment on the defendant's future life in society; StPO § 267 requires written reasons for sentencing.
Sweden Brottsbalken Ch. 29 § 5 lists eight equity considerations (billighetshänsyn), including risk of dismissal or severe personal harm, allowing mitigation or, under § 6, waiver of sanction.
France Individualization of punishment (l'individualisation de la peine), pre-sentence personality investigation (l'enquête de personnalité) and exclusion of a conviction from criminal record Bulletin n°2 to prevent job loss.
Japan Secondary harm counted as "alternative harm" (대체적 해악) within the sentencing market (양형시세), together with social punitive sentiment (사회의 처벌 감정).

Reform proposals

  1. Fuller written sentencing reasons (판결문 양형이유의 충실한 기재): adopt an obligation like German StPO § 267, requiring detailed treatment of sensibility to punishment and of departures from the guidelines.
  2. Revise the Sentencing Guidelines: list loss of status and economic factors explicitly and set standards for choice of sanction (fine versus imprisonment).
  3. Amend Criminal Act Art. 51: add an explicit clause on the defendant's sensibility to punishment as a sentencing consideration.
  4. Institutionalize sentencing investigation and a database: strengthen the role of court investigators (법원조사관) and build a national collateral-consequences database on the NICCC model.

Frequently asked questions

How does the report define "sensibility to punishment"?
The degree of suffering, subjective impact or special effect that an individual defendant experiences from a criminal sanction and its collateral consequences.

Who wrote and issued it?
Ha, Minkyung (lead), Pyo, Hyunduck and Kang, Hyunjoo, issued by the Judicial Policy Research Institute of the Supreme Court of Korea in October 2016.

How do "sensibility" and "responsiveness" differ?
Sensibility measures how heavily punishment is felt by the defendant; responsiveness (형벌감응성) measures how effectively it changes behavior so the defendant does not reoffend.

What did the survey of 502 judges find?
83.9% had considered sensibility to punishment, 89.2% agreed it should be considered, and job or status loss was the most-cited factor (76.7%).

How does US federal law treat collateral consequences?
Through USSG and Koon v. United States, courts may depart downward when collateral harm is extraordinary; the NICCC database catalogs such consequences.

How does Sweden express the concept?
Brottsbalken Ch. 29 § 5 lists eight equity factors, such as dismissal, severe personal harm or deportation, supporting mitigation or, under § 6, waiver of sanction.

How does France prevent loss of employment?
Through pre-sentence personality investigation and the power to leave a conviction off criminal record Bulletin n°2, which employers can access.

Why was the Gwangju medical-student case controversial?
A fine was imposed so the defendant would not be expelled from university, prompting accusations of a favoritism ruling (봐주기 판결) for an elite profession.

What are the four main reform proposals?
Fuller written sentencing reasons; revised sentencing guidelines; amendment of Criminal Act Art. 51; stronger sentencing investigation and a collateral-consequences database.

배경

"형벌감수성"이란 형사제재와 이에 수반되는 부수적 불이익으로 개별 피고인이 겪는 주관적·개별적 고통 또는 영향의 정도를 말한다. 2007년부터 시행된 양형기준은 양형 편차를 줄였으나, 해고, 전문직 면허 상실, 부양가족 등 2차적 영향을 감경 사유로 고려하면 국민의 불신과 논란이 생긴다.3

광주지방법원의 의대생 데이트 폭력 사건에서는 피고인이 퇴학되지 않도록 벌금형을 선고하였다는 이유로 "봐주기 판결"이라는 비판이 광범위하게 제기되었다.

연구 목적

  1. 피고인의 전인격적 특성을 측정 가능하고 투명하게 반영하여 양형의 기계화를 방지한다.
  2. "고귀한 연민"에 기초한 배분적 정의와 실질적 평등을 법적 안정성을 해치지 않는 범위에서 실현한다.
  3. 국제 비교 기준과 소송법·양형 관련 법률 개정 권고안을 제공한다.

연구 범위

현직 법관 502명 설문조사, 국내 논란 사례(광주 의대생 사건) 평가, 독일 Strafempfindlichkeit 법리, 미국 양형기준의 이탈(departure) 체계, 스웨덴 형평 고려사항(billighetshänsyn), 프랑스 판결 전 조사 제도를 다룬다.

정책 문제

  • 양형의 기계화: 양형기준을 지나치게 경직되게 적용하면 형법 제51조가 요구하는 피고인의 개인적 특성 고려가 무시될 위험이 있다.
  • 실질적 정의와 국민 인식의 충돌: 형벌감수성을 무시하면 같은 형량이 사람마다 크게 다른 고통을 준다는 점에서 실질적 평등과 배분적 정의(롤스의 차등 원칙)에 반하고, 명확한 기준 없이 고려하면 차별·특혜 시비가 생긴다.
  • 용어의 혼동: 실무에서 "형벌감수성"과 "형벌감응성"(재범하지 않도록 피고인이 반응하는 정도)이 자주 혼용된다.

법제 공백

  • 양형기준의 명시 규정 부재: 현행 양형기준은 신분상 불이익이나 해고를 공식 요소로 열거하지 않으며, 뇌물범죄에서는 오히려 신분 상실의 고려를 배제한다.
  • 부수적 불이익 데이터베이스 부재: 미국의 NICCC(유죄판결의 부수적 불이익 전국 목록)와 같이 유죄판결에 따른 2차 행정·법적 제재를 통합 기록하는 제도가 없다.
  • 이유 기재 의무의 미약: 형의 종류 선택과 형벌감수성 고려에 관한 이유를 상세히 기재할 의무가 엄격하지 않다.

형벌감수성과 형벌감응성의 차이는?

피고인이 퇴학되지 않도록 벌금형을 선고하여 엘리트 직군에 대한 "봐주기 판결"이라는 비판을 받았다.
