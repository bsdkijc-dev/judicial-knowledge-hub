Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 283 K/TUN/1998 dated December 14, 2000, establishes very fundamental labor and state administrative jurisprudence: Pursuant to Article 116 of Government Regulation No. 30 of 1990, foundations operating in the field of higher education act as an extension of the government to carry out governmental affairs in the field of education. Consequently, the Committees for Labor Dispute Settlement (P4P/P4D) lack the authority to resolve disputes between lecturers and foundations operating in higher education, as such disputes do not constitute industrial relations but rather fall within the domain of educational relations.

Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Petitioner for Cassation (Yayasan Perguruan Dharma Agung / Institut Sains dan Teknologi TD. Pardede, represented by Chairperson Ny. [Plaintiff]. Siregar br. Pardede) filed a lawsuit against the Original Defendant / Respondent for Cassation (Central Committee for Labor Dispute Settlement / P4-Pusat) at the Jakarta State Administrative High Court (No. 09/G/1997/PT.TUN.JKT). The object of the dispute consisted of the Decree of P4-Pusat No. 845/787/77-14/II/PHK/9-1996 dated September 30, 1996 (Letter No. TAR.951/M/KP4P/19 dated October 31, 1996) concerning the Termination of Employment (*PHK*) dispute between the Plaintiff and Prof. DR. J.A. Napitupulu, Msc. et al. (6 lecturers).

P4-Pusat altered the decision of the North Sumatra P4-Regional Committee No. 148/105-08/W2/PHK-VI/96 dated June 27, 1996, by declaring that the employment relationship of the lecturers was never terminated, while ordering the Employer (Foundation) to pay wages, holiday allowances (*THR*), and processing wages. The Plaintiff filed a state administrative lawsuit arguing that P4P/P4D lacked the authority to adjudicate disputes involving private higher education lecturers because the lecturers' legal relationship is governed by Government Regulation No. 30 of 1990 and pure civil status/transfer agreements. The Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 09/G/1997/PT.TUN.JKT dated February 19, 1998) rejected the Plaintiff's lawsuit in its entirety. Dissatisfied with the Jakarta State Administrative High Court ruling, Yayasan Perguruan Dharma Agung filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The Jakarta State Administrative High Court committed an error in applying the law. The Plaintiff is a foundation operating in the field of higher education, which serves as an extension of the government to administer governmental affairs in education pursuant to Article 116 of Government Regulation No. 30 of 1990.
  2. Lecturers working at such foundations are educational staff specifically appointed for teaching duties within the higher education institutions under the foundation (ex Article 1 points 2, 3 jo. Article 29 paragraph (3) sub 1 of Gov. Reg. No. 30 of 1990).
  3. From these provisions, it is clear that the relationship between the Plaintiff and the workers (Prof. Dr. J.A. Napitupulu, Msc. et al.) does not constitute an industrial relations relationship, but rather an educational relationship between lecturers and universities within the Plaintiff's working environment.
  4. Disputes between the Plaintiff and the lecturers / Rector of Dharma Agung University do not fall within the authority of the Central Committee for Labor Dispute Settlement (Defendant) to resolve.
  5. The issuance of the disputed decree by the Defendant satisfies the legal defect criteria under Article 53 paragraph (2)(a) of Law No. 5 of 1986 because it was issued outside of official authority (*ultra vires*), and therefore must be declared null and void by operation of law.
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation YAYASAN PERGURUAN DHARMA AGUNG (INSTITUT SAINS DAN TEKNOLOGI TD. PARDEDE); Annulling the Jakarta State Administrative High Court decision dated February 19, 1998, No. 09/G/1997/PT.TUN.JKT; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring that the Defendant lacks the authority to resolve this case (Government Regulation No. 30 of 1990); (2) Declaring null and void by operation of law the Decree of the Defendant Number TAR.951/M/KP4P/19; (3) Ordering the Respondent for Cassation to pay all court costs across first instance, appellate, and cassation levels (assessed at Rp 100,000 at the cassation level). *(Decided in tabular deliberation of the Supreme Court on Thursday, November 30, 2000, and pronounced in an open session on Thursday, December 14, 2000, by H. German Hoediarto, SH. [Junior Chairman of the Supreme Court / Presiding Judge], Mangatas Nasution, SH., and Ny. Hj. Emin Aminah Achadiat, SH. [Associate Justices]).*
