Policy Analysis

  • Background & Policy Problem:
    • FACT: While overall penal code offenses declined after peaking in 2002, the re-offender rate among all arrested individuals steadily rose to 48.7% in 2016.
    • FACT: Analysis shows ~30% of re-offenders account for ~60% of total reported crimes.
    • FACT: Unemployed offenders face a re-offending rate 3 to 5 times higher than those with stable employment.
    • FACT: Traditional criminal justice mechanisms faced limits because ex-offenders frequently suffer from social isolation, poverty, substance dependency, mental disorders, and homelessness.
  • Policy Objectives:
    • FACT: Facilitate smooth social reintegration of ex-offenders and juvenile delinquents without social isolation.
    • FACT: Prevent repeat victimization and build a safe, secure society.
    • FACT: Realize the "leave no one behind" principle in accordance with Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
  • Key Provisions & Reforms:
    • FACT: State & Local Responsibilities: Obligates Central and Local Governments to formulate integrated re-offending prevention policies.
    • FACT: Awareness Month: Designates July annually as Re-offending Prevention Awareness Month.
    • FACT: National Promotion Plan (Article 7): Mandates a 5-year Cabinet plan covering 5 core principles, 7 priority issues, and 115 concrete actions.
    • FACT: Employment & Housing Support: Mandates vocational certification in correctional facilities, expands Cooperative Employers (協力雇用主), and improves public housing access.
  • Implementation Schedule & Targets:
    • FACT: Act No. 104 promulgated and enforced in December 2016.
    • FACT: Promotion Plan approved by Cabinet on Dec 15, 2017 (FY2018–FY2022).
    • FACT: Key Target: Reduce the 2-year re-incarceration rate by 20% or more by 2021.
