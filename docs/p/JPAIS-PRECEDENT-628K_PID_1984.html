Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 628 K/Pid/1984, dated July 22, 1985, established a fundamental criminal and civil procedural law rule concerning prejudicial questions (*prejudisial geschil* / civil disputes preceding criminal prosecution ex Article 385 of the Criminal Code / KUHP):

When the ownership status of land or buildings in a criminal land crime case (sale of land/buildings ex Article 385 paragraph 1 of the Criminal Code) remains the object of an ongoing civil dispute that has not yet attained final and binding legal force (*Inkracht van Gewijsde*), the High Court, prior to deciding upon the criminal merits (releasing or sentencing), must suspend or await the civil court decision determining the ownership status of the land and house until it possesses binding legal certainty.

The Supreme Court annulled Bandung High Court Decision No. 150/1983/Pid/PT.B and ordered the Bandung High Court to reopen proceedings and examine/adjudicate the criminal merits after the civil judgment attains final and binding legal force.

Executive Summary

The central legal principle established in this decision is that criminal courts cannot prematurely adjudicate land offenses when the underlying civil property rights are actively being litigated, mandating a stay of proceedings (*prejudisial geschil*).

Key elements established in the adjudication:

  • Land offenses requiring proof of ownership cannot be finalized while civil ownership suits remain sub judice.
  • Appellate courts discharging defendants prematurely without waiting for final civil outcomes commit procedural error.
  • Appellate rulings must be annulled with remand orders directing courts to stay proceedings until civil finality is achieved.

Case Background

The Original Defendants / Respondents for Cassation (1. [Defendant I], [age removed], merchant; 2. [Defendant II], [age removed], housewife) were charged with violating Article 385 paragraph (1) in conjunction with Article 55 paragraph (1) 1st of the Criminal Code for jointly selling house and building HGB No. 197/Panaragan [address removed], Bogor, to their child ([Child]) for Rp 9,000,000.— using an alleged forged Power of Attorney dated May 30, 1969.

The Bogor District Court (Decision No. 74/1983/Pid/B/PN.Bgr. dated October 17, 1983) sentenced Defendant I to 6 months' imprisonment and Defendant II to 4 months' imprisonment. The Bandung High Court (Decision No. 150/1983/Pid/PT.B dated March 8, 1984) annulled the Bogor District Court decision and discharged the Defendants from all legal prosecutions (*ontslag*) on the grounds that the element of property belonging to another remained uncertain because civil ownership disputes had not attained final and binding legal force. The Public Prosecutor at the Bogor State Prosecutor's Office subsequently filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

Legal and Procedural Issues

Prejudicial Questions (*Prejudisial Geschil*) in Land Crime Offenses (Article 385 KUHP)

The primary issue was whether a High Court is justified in discharging a Defendant from all legal prosecutions (*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging*) under Article 385 of the Criminal Code on the grounds that the element of "property belonging to another" is unproven because underlying civil ownership disputes remain pending without final legal force.

The Supreme Court held that the Bandung High Court misapplied the law. When elements of a land crime depend upon resolving civil ownership rights currently examined in civil courts and lacking final legal force (*Inkracht van Gewijsde*), the High Court must not prematurely discharge or convict. The Court is legally mandated to suspend proceedings and await the final civil judgment establishing land/house ownership before reopening and deciding the criminal merits.

Question of Law and Question of Fact

Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

Question:

Did the Bandung High Court misapply procedural law (*foutieve kepassing van het recht*) by prematurely issuing a discharge (*ontslag*) ruling over an Article 385 KUHP criminal charge without suspending proceedings until a final civil judgment established ownership of HGB land No. 197/Panaragan [address removed], Bogor?

Supreme Court Ruling:

Yes, the Bandung High Court misapplied procedural law. The Supreme Court annulled Bandung High Court Decision No. 150/1983/Pid/PT.B and adjudicated afresh, ordering the Bandung High Court to reopen proceedings and decide the criminal merits after the civil ownership judgment attains final and binding legal force.

Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Bogor District Court & Bandung High Court)

Examination of facts concerning the execution of the Power of Attorney dated May 30, 1969, Sale and Purchase Deed No. 01/JBR/Pm.014/I/1980 to [Child] for Rp 9,000,000.—, forgery convictions of Wahir bin Satim (Notary employee), issuance of HGB Certificate No. 197/Panaragan in the name of [Party D] / Pang Siong Foe, and Judicial Review (*Peninjauan Kembali*) civil proceedings No. 62/PK/Perd/1981.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The appellate *Judex Facti* of Bandung High Court misapplied the law by declaring that the Defendants' acts did not constitute a crime because civil ownership status remained unsettled.
  2. However, instead of discharging the Defendants (*ontslag*), the High Court prior to deciding the criminal merits was legally obligated to suspend and await the civil court decision determining land and house ownership to ensure binding certainty (*prejudisial geschil*).
  3. The Supreme Court annulled the Bandung High Court decision and adjudicated afresh, ordering the Bandung High Court to reopen proceedings and adjudicate the criminal merits after the civil judgment attains finality.

Ruling

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition from the Petitioner for Cassation (Public Prosecutor at the Bogor State Prosecutor's Office); annulled Bandung High Court Decision No. 150/1983/Pid/PT.B dated March 8, 1984; and ADJUDICATED AFRESH: 1. Ordering the Bandung High Court to reopen proceedings and examine and adjudicate the merits of this case after the civil court decision determining the ownership status of land H.G.B. No. 197/Panaragan located at Jalan Merdeka No. 11A, Bogor, attains binding certainty; 2. Stipulating that appeal court costs be deferred until the High Court renders its final decision; 3. Charging cassation court costs to the State.

Not Established / Suspended Awaiting Finality

  • Lawful civil ownership over the disputed land/house cannot be legally ascertained in criminal proceedings prior to a final and binding civil judgment (*inkracht*).

Uncertainty and Gaps

  • Interdependence of Civil Law and Land Criminal Law: This landmark ruling establishes strict boundaries between land crimes (Article 385 KUHP) and civil land ownership disputes. Criminal prosecution must not preempt civil outcomes when property rights over land/buildings remain uncertain and contested in civil courts.

GEO Q&A

Q: May a criminal judge decide a land sale criminal case (Article 385 KUHP) if the civil ownership dispute over the land is still ongoing in court and lacks final binding force?

A: No, they may not. Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 628 K/Pid/1984 (*prejudisial geschil*), if the ownership status of land/buildings in an Article 385 KUHP criminal case remains the object of an ongoing civil dispute without final binding force, the Court is mandated to suspend proceedings and await the civil court decision determining land ownership to attain binding certainty (*inkracht*) before examining and deciding the criminal merits.
