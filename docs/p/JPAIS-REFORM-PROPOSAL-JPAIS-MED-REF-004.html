JPAIS:REFORM-PROPOSAL:JPAIS-MED-REF-004

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 968, 2026-09-15 · wiki source · Markdown

Summary / Ringkasan

Entity Ingestion / Ingesti Entitas

  • Entity ID: JPAIS-MED-REF-004
  • Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
  • Ingestion Status: Confirmed

Description / Deskripsi

Evaluates civil preparatory proceedings through a comparative framework between Indonesia and Japan, identifying systemic gaps in Indonesia's general civil courts (absence of mandatory pre-trial issue clarification, passive judicial roles, and low mediation success rates) and proposing the institutionalization of mandatory Pre-Trial Civil Examinations (Pemeriksaan Persiapan Perdata / PPP) modeled after Japan's CCP issue-arrangement framework[cite: 4].

Problem / Masalah

  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-001: General civil actions in Indonesian District Courts proceed with vague claims (obscuur libel) and broad denials due to a lack of preliminary issue clarification, leading to prolonged trials and low mediation success (1.4% to 5%)[cite: 4].
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-002: Litigants experience surprise evidence submissions during main trial hearings because the legacy HIR/RBg procedural system lacks pre-trial document disclosure mechanisms[cite: 4].
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-003: Indonesian civil trials suffer from unpredictable schedules and a lack of procedural preclusion sanctions for late submissions, causing frequent delays beyond SEMA's 5-month target[cite: 4].

Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

  • JPAIS-MED-ID-001: Standard civil procedure under legacy HIR/RBg codes proceeds directly from initial appearances to PERMA No. 1 of 2016 mediation and serial written exchanges without a general pre-trial issue clarification stage[cite: 4].

Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

  • JPAIS-MED-JP-001: Japan's Code of Civil Procedure (CCP Act No. 109/1996) integrates issue and evidence arrangement proceedings (kozo chosei tetuzuki) via Preliminary Oral Arguments, Preparatory Proceedings, and Written Preparatory Proceedings supported by judicial clarification (shakumeiken) and binding trial plans[cite: 4].

Gap / Kesenjangan

  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-001: Statutory absence of active judicial clarification powers and mandatory pre-trial issue framing in general civil District Courts, contrasting with PTUN and Small Claims exceptions[cite: 4].
  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-002: Absence of pre-filing inquiry rights, mandatory pre-trial document disclosure rules, and formal inter-party information exchange mechanisms[cite: 4].

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Institutionalize mandatory Pre-Trial Civil Examinations (PPP) across all District Courts, adapt Japan's shakumeiken and inter-party inquiry rules into the E-Court framework, implement binding procedural calendars with strict preclusion sanctions, and establish a dual-track settlement mechanism post-issue clarification[cite: 4].

Action / Aksi

  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-001: Promulgate interim judicial pilot guidelines and configure SIPP/E-Court modules for issue matrix creation and digital pre-trial document management[cite: 4].
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-002: Deploy mandatory PPP and digital evidence disclosure protocols across five Class IA Special District Courts (Central Jakarta, Surabaya, Medan, Bandung, Makassar)[cite: 4].
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-003: Enforce binding procedural calendars, conduct mid-term reviews, and draft a permanent PERMA for nationwide rollout[cite: 4].

Pilot / Pilot Proyek

  • JPAIS-MED-PILOT-004: 90-day multi-court pilot program across Central Jakarta, Surabaya, Medan, Bandung, and Makassar District Courts[cite: 4].

KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

  • JPAIS-MED-KPI-004: Interlocutory exceptions reduced by 40%; evidentiary sessions reduced by 30%; 100% issuance of pre-trial issue summaries; SEMA 5-month compliance rate $> 92.0\$; commercial settlement success rate $> 25.0\$[cite: 4].

Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Issue Supreme Court Circulars establishing judicial performance evaluations based on effective issue-narrowing and active clarification enforcement during pre-trial phases[cite: 4].

Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgate a comprehensive PERMA on Civil Preparatory Proceedings, amend PERMA No. 7 of 2022 (E-Court) and PERMA No. 1 of 2016 (Mediation), and incorporate provisions into the draft Civil Procedure Code (RUU Hukum Acara Perdata)[cite: 4].

Evidence / Bukti Empiris

  • General district court civil mediation success rates range between 1.4% and 5%, while Japanese in-court settlement (Wakai) rates reach 36% to 37.1%[cite: 4].
  • Japanese first-instance proceedings achieve an actual average duration of 9 to 14 months under active judicial case management[cite: 4].
