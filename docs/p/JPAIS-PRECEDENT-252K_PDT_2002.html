Land Sale and Purchase, Unlawful Act (Fabricated Sale and Purchase), & Legal Protection for Auction Winners in Good Faith
346.043
Ir. [Party A], Ny. [Party B], Ir. Theodorus Tedja Lawu, Notary Ny. Mulyani Sjafei SH, PT Alam Sari Lestari, PT Bank Bali, Auction Winner in Good Faith, Creditor Auction Bidder, Fabricated Sale and Purchase, HGB No. 106/Bantarjati, HGB No. 93/Babakan, Bogor District Court, Bandung High Court
Civil Code (BW) Articles 1320, 1338, 1365; HIR Article 178; Vendu Reglement (S. 1908 No. 189); Law No. 4 of 1996 on Mortgage Rights; Law No. 14 of 1970 jo. Law No. 4 of 2004; Law No. 14 of 1985 jo. Law No. 5 of 2004

Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 252 K/Pdt/2002 dated June 11, 2004, establishes fundamental civil jurisprudence and legal rules regarding fabricated sales and the lack of good faith by auction winners: An auction winner is deemed to be acting in bad faith and is not entitled to legal protection if the auction winner turns out to be the creditor themselves who purchased the property at a price far below the appraised collateral value. Furthermore, a land/house sale and purchase transaction unaccompanied by actual delivery of the object and executed based on a fabricated debt-security arrangement must be declared legally flawed and null and void together with all its legal consequences.

Case Background

The Original Plaintiffs / Petitioners for Cassation I (Ir. [Party A], M.Sc., subsequently succeeded by his heirs: Ny. [Party B], Komaruddin Brotoatmodjo, Taufik Hidayat Brotoatmodjo, Siti Nooraini Williamson, and Muhamad Seisar Reza) are the lawful owners of a plot of land and building under HGB No. 106/Bantarjati (currently HGB No. 93/Babakan) located at [address removed], Bogor. To process *eigendom* land documents in Jakarta, the Plaintiff borrowed Rp 1,500,000,000 from Defendant I (Ir. Theodorus Tedja Lawu) secured by the land certificate and executed a blank, uncompleted Sale and Purchase Deed form before Notary Ny. Mulyani Sjafei, SH (Defendant II). Defendant I defaulted by only handing over Rp 560,000,000, but unlawfully utilized the blank form to draft Sale and Purchase Deed No. 25/Bgr/Utara/II/JB/1995 dated February 24, 1995, priced at Rp 300,000,000 (whereas market value was Rp 2,000,000,000), subsequently transferring ownership rights and encumbering it as credit collateral until it was auctioned off by the Bogor State Auction Office. The execution auction winner was Defendant I (the creditor himself) via PT Alam Sari Lestari / Bank Bali at a heavily depressed price.

The Bogor District Court (Decision No. 13/Pdt/G/1999/PN.Bgr dated December 6, 1999) granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part, annulled the Sale and Purchase Deed, the Power of Attorney to Sell, the Auction Minutes, and declared the certificate in the name of Defendant I / Bank Bali to have no legal force. However, the Bandung High Court (Decision No. 261/Pdt/2000/PT.Bdg dated October 19, 2000) annulled the Bogor District Court judgment. Petitioners for Cassation I (Plaintiff's Heirs) and Petitioner for Cassation II (Defendant I) filed cassation petitions to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The sale and purchase transaction between the Plaintiff/seller and Defendant I/buyer regarding the land and building under HGB No. 93/Babakan ([address removed], Bogor) was unnatural because it was unaccompanied by the physical delivery of the land and house to the buyer and was executed based on the completion of a blank sale and purchase deed form signed during the creation of a secured debt instrument. Therefore, the transaction constitutes a mere fabrication and must be declared legally defective together with all its legal consequences.
  2. The auction winner of the disputed object turned out to be the creditor himself (Defendant I) with a purchase price far below the collateral value. This demonstrates that the auction winner acted in bad faith and is consequently not entitled to legal protection.
  3. The *Judex Facti* (Bandung High Court) committed an error in applying the law; thus, the Bandung High Court decision is annulled, and the Supreme Court independently adjudicates by adopting the Bogor District Court's reasoning, which was accurate and correct.
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioners for Cassation I (1. NY. [Party B], 2. KOMARUDDIN BROTOATMODJO, 3. TAUFIK HIDAYAT BROTOATMODJO, 4. SITI NOORAINI WILLIAMSON, 5. MUHAMAD SEISAR REZA) and Petitioner for Cassation II (IR. THEODORUS TEDJA LAWU); Annulling the Bandung High Court decision dated October 19, 2000, No. 261/Pdt/2000/PT.Bdg; and Independently Adjudicating: IN EXCEPTION: Rejecting the Defendants' exceptions in their entirety; IN THE PRINCIPAL CASE: (1) Granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part; (2) Declaring valid and enforceable the security attachment (*conservatoir beslag*) executed by the Bailiff of the Bogor District Court on September 2, 1999, No. 13/Pdt/G/1999/PN.Bgr jo. No. 16/CB/Pdt/1999/PN.Bgr; (3) Declaring that the Plaintiff is the lawful owner of the land and building under HGB Certificate No. 106/Bantarjati / presently HGB Certificate No. 93/Babakan known as Jalan Ceremai No. 18, Bogor; (4) Declaring that Defendants I, II, and III have committed an unlawful act (*onrechtmatige daad*); (5) Declaring: (a) Null and void Sale and Purchase Deed No. 25/Bgr/Utara/II/JB/1995 dated February 24, 1995, between the Plaintiff and Defendant I together with all legal consequences arising therefrom; (b) Null and void and without legal force the Power to Mortgage and Power to Sell No. 133 dated March 30, 1995, together with its legal consequences; (c) Null and void the execution carried out based on Auction Minutes No. 279/1997-1998 dated October 7, 1997; (d) Null and void and without binding legal force HGB Certificate No. 93/Babakan Village dated March 15, 1996, in the name of Ir. Theodorus Tedja Lawu, now in the name of Bank Bali (Defendant IV); (6) Ordering all parties related to this case to restore conditions to their original state and reinstate the legal status of the disputed object under HGB Certificate No. 93/Babakan Village in the name of Bank Bali Jakarta Branch; (7) Ordering the Respondents for Cassation to pay court costs across all levels of jurisdiction (cassation assessed at Rp 200,000). *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Friday, June 11, 2004, by Prof. DR. Paulus E. Lotulung, SH. [Junior Chairman of the Supreme Court / Presiding Judge], Ny. Chairani A. Wani, SH., and Prof. DR. Valerine J.L.K., SH., MA. [Associate Justices]).*
