LEGAL-0001

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 216, 2026-08-28 · wiki source · Markdown

Law

Law refers to a system of legally binding rules recognized and enforced within a particular legal order.

The meaning, sources, hierarchy, interpretation, and enforcement of law depend on the applicable jurisdiction.

[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED]

Legal Knowledge Model

A legal rule may be represented as:

SOURCE → RULE → SUBJECT → OBLIGATION/RIGHT → CONSEQUENCE

Sources of Law

Depending on the jurisdiction, legal sources may include:

  • Constitution
  • Legislation
  • Regulations
  • Judicial decisions
  • Treaties
  • Recognized legal principles
  • Other legally recognized sources

The legal status of each source must be verified within the relevant jurisdiction.

Law and Judiciary

Courts may be required to:

  • Interpret law
  • Apply law
  • Resolve disputes
  • Determine legal consequences
  • Develop jurisprudence within applicable legal boundaries

Specific judicial powers require authoritative verification.

Machine-Readable Law

For AI-assisted legal research, legal rules can be structured using:

  • Citation
  • Jurisdiction
  • Effective date
  • Provision
  • Subject
  • Legal issue
  • Relationship
  • Precedent
  • Amendment history

Versioning

Legal knowledge should preserve:

  • Original provision
  • Amendment
  • Effective date
  • Repeal
  • Current status

A historical legal rule must not automatically be presented as currently applicable law.
