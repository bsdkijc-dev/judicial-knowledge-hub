Description / Deskripsi

Provides an exhaustive examination of comprehensive best practices in maritime criminal and casualty investigations, detailing jurisdictional frameworks under UNCLOS, pre-boarding intelligence and risk planning, tactical VBSS execution, BISI/EISI safety inspections, compartmentized vessel search methodologies, environmental and MARPOL evidence sampling, Voyage Data Recorder (VDR) binary extraction and hashing, seafarer human rights under IMO Resolution LEG.7(112), and unbroken chain of custody management.

Problem / Masalah

  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-010: Procedural errors during initial maritime boardings, crime scene isolation, or evidence recovery can permanently compromise judicial admissibility in shore-based courts.
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-011: Volatile bridge audio and sensor data on Voyage Data Recorders (VDR) continuously overwrite older loops, risking the permanent loss of critical investigative evidence if emergency save mechanisms are delayed.
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-012: Maritime environments expose crime scenes to salt spray, humidity, and soot, rapidly degrading trace biological and physical evidence unless immediate protective enclosures are established.

Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

  • JPAIS-MED-ID-022: Multi-agency maritime enforcement units coordinate interdictions and shipboard inspections, requiring standardized protocols for digital forensics and evidence continuity.

Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

  • JPAIS-MED-JP-022: Advanced maritime jurisdictions deploy specialized boarding teams equipped with digital hashing tools and structured sector search grids to secure conviction-ready evidentiary packages.

Gap / Kesenjangan

  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-005: Inconsistent handling of chain-of-custody logs and failure to calculate cryptographic hashes (SHA-256) during digital extractions leave electronic evidence vulnerable to defense challenges regarding tampering.

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Standardize end-to-end maritime investigation workflows across seven distinct phases—from pre-boarding intelligence to judicial handover—incorporating rigorous TFMA digital categorization and seafarer fair treatment protections.

Action / Aksi

  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-010: Mandate immediate activation of VDR emergency save functions and raw binary extraction using SHA-256 hashing protocols.
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-011: Enforce strict sectorized search methodologies and IMO Resolution A.1091(28) crime scene containment guidelines.
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-012: Maintain master evidence ledgers and dual-signature transfer manifests to guarantee an unbroken chain of custody.

Pilot / Pilot Proyek

  • JPAIS-MED-PILOT-022: 90-day specialized training program for maritime boarding officers focusing on advanced digital forensics, MARPOL oil sampling, and human rights compliance under IMO Resolution LEG.7(112).

KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

  • JPAIS-MED-KPI-022: 100% adherence to chain-of-custody documentation standards; zero evidentiary rejections in court due to digital corruption or mishandling; complete compliance with seafarer fair treatment guidelines.

Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Adoption of harmonized national guidelines for maritime crime scene management and digital evidence preservation aligned with UNODC and IMO frameworks.

Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Strengthening statutory provisions requiring mandatory VDR data preservation and standardized evidentiary reporting following maritime casualties or criminal allegations.

Evidence / Bukti Empiris

  • Structured sector searches and BISI/EISI safety protocols significantly reduce tactical boarding risks and uncover hidden contraband compartments.
  • Cryptographic hash verification proves data integrity for digital extractions from VDR and ECDIS systems.
