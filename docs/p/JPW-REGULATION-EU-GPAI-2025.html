JPW-REGULATION-EU-GPAI-2025

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 924, 2026-09-15 · wiki source · Markdown

EU General Purpose AI (GPAI) Model Guidelines are official implementation rules under the EU AI Act that clarify transparency, technical documentation, and data confidentiality obligations for general-purpose AI providers Passage 1.

Executive Summary

Core compliance mandates under the EU AI Act for GPAI models became legally binding on August 2, 2025 Passage 1. These guidelines set clear definitions for operational scope, transparency standards, and enforcement mechanics applicable to both EU and non-EU providers selling or operating AI models within the European Union Passage 1, Passage 2.

Legal Basis

  • EU Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act), specifically statutory provisions governing General Purpose AI models Passage 1.
  • European Commission GPAI Guidelines, published to establish enforcement procedures and compliance templates Passage 1.
  • GPAI Code of Practice, released by the European Commission on July 10, 2025 Passage 1.

Background

The rapid proliferation of foundation and general-purpose AI models created urgent demands for standardized regulatory governance across EU Member States Passage 1. The European Commission enacted these practical guidelines to ensure developer compliance ahead of the August 2025 statutory deadline Passage 1.

Objectives

  1. Enforce complete transparency regarding datasets and sources used to train GPAI models Passage 1.
  2. Maintain mandatory standards for technical documentation and corporate data confidentiality Passage 1, Passage 2.
  3. Define predictable legal enforcement mechanisms for AI products operating within the EU internal market Passage 2.

Key Provisions

  • Extraterritorial Reach: Mandates full compliance from both EU-based and non-EU AI developers if their models are deployed within the EU Passage 2.
  • Training Data Summary Template: Obligates providers to submit structured disclosures detailing the content and sources used in model training Passage 1.
  • Transparency Obligations: Imposes mandatory technical records retention and data governance rules effective August 2, 2025 Passage 1.

Implementation

  • Code of Practice Release: Issued by the European Commission on July 10, 2025 Passage 1.
  • Statutory Activation: Mandatory transparency and documentation obligations entered full effect on August 2, 2025 Passage 1.
  • Executive Compliance Guidance: Eversheds Sutherland published an analysis guide on September 26, 2025, assisting enterprises with legal navigation Passage 4.

Policy & Compliance Issues

  • Cross-Border Compliance: Operational challenges related to auditing and enforcing compliance on foreign AI developers operating outside EU borders Passage 2.
  • Proprietary Balance: Harmonizing public training data transparency mandates with corporate trade secret protection Passage 1, Passage 5.
