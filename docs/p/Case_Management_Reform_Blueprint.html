Case Management Reform Blueprint

The Blueprint identifies case management as a major area of judicial technical and administrative reform.

Strategic Objectives

  • control case flows;
  • improve efficiency;
  • improve case administration;
  • improve case information;
  • support consistency and quality;
  • improve judicial processes.
