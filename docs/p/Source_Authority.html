Source Authority

Source authority describes the reliability and legal or institutional status of a source used to support a knowledge claim.

Proposed Source Classes

Class Example
A Official primary legal/institutional source
B Official secondary institutional source
C Peer-reviewed scholarly source
D Reputable secondary source
E Unverified or informal source

This classification is a proposed knowledge-governance model.

Primary Sources

Examples may include:

  • Constitution
  • Legislation
  • Official regulations
  • Official court decisions
  • Official institutional documents

Secondary Sources

May include:

  • Scholarly analysis
  • Research reports
  • Commentary
  • Textbooks

AI Requirement

For legal propositions, AI systems should preferentially rely on authoritative primary sources where available.

Source Evaluation

Consider:

  • Authority
  • Authenticity
  • Currency
  • Relevance
  • Completeness
  • Independence
