JPW-POLICY-QAT-AI-001

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 910, 2026-09-15 · wiki source · Markdown

Regional Training Workshop on Global Tools for Artificial Intelligence and the Rule of Law is a regional capacity-building initiative organized by the Center for Legal and Judicial Studies (CLJS) under the Ministry of Justice of Qatar, in collaboration with the Qatar National Commission for Education, Culture, and Science and the UNESCO Office in Doha Passage 1. The initiative aims to leverage artificial intelligence (AI) technologies to uphold the rule of law, expedite judicial proceedings, and facilitate the transition toward e-justice across the Arab region Passage 1.

Executive Summary

This regional training workshop convened on December 9, 2024, in Doha, Qatar Passage 1. It was attended by ministers, judges, and prosecutors representing 10 Arab nations Passage 4. The program is designed to equip judicial practitioners with technical and ethical competencies to utilize global AI tools within judicial workflows Passage 1, Passage 5.

Legal Basis

The execution of AI strategies within Qatar's judicial sector is grounded on:

  • Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2021, which established the Artificial Intelligence Committee to oversee and execute the national AI strategy Passage 2.
  • Directives of Amir HH Sheikh Tamim bin Hamad Al Thani, emphasizing continuous judicial development to ensure judicial independence and legal modernization Passage 2.

Background

Rapid technological advances under the Fourth Industrial Revolution require judicial frameworks to modernize to keep pace with societal progress Passage 5. Judicial institutions are called upon to adapt legal frameworks to support effective and accessible justice (*effective justice*) Passage 5.

Objectives

  1. Build capacity among judges, prosecutors, and legal experts across the Arab region in adopting AI tools Passage 1.
  2. Address and mitigate case backlog challenges across judicial bodies Passage 3, Passage 5.
  3. Expedite judicial procedures and accelerate institutional transitions toward e-justice Passage 1, Passage 5.
  4. Strengthen regional cooperation among Arab states in applying AI technologies within the legal sector Passage 1, Passage 4.

Key Provisions

  • AI Oversight Committee: Establishing the Artificial Intelligence Committee pursuant to Cabinet Decision No. (10) of 2021 to guide national policy Passage 2.
  • AI as an Ancillary Tool: Positing AI technology as an operational support tool (*ancillary tool*) to assist human decision-makers rather than replace judicial discretion Passage 3, Passage 5.
  • Legal Framework Adaptation: Modernizing regulatory provisions to align with contemporary technological developments without hindering innovation Passage 5, Passage 6.

Implementation

  • Public Prosecution of Qatar: Direct operational integration of AI tools to accelerate case processing and improve legal accuracy Passage 3.
  • Regional Participation: Participation of judicial representatives from 10 Arab nations: Qatar, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Kuwait, Oman, Bahrain, Algeria, Egypt, Iraq, and Yemen Passage 4.
  • UNESCO Global Partnership: Drawing on UNESCO's global initiative that has engaged over 36,000 judicial stakeholders across 160 nations Passage 4.

Policy & Compliance Issues

  • Legal Personality of AI: Ongoing academic and legislative debates on whether AI systems or autonomous agents should be granted legal personality (*legal personality of robots*) Passage 6.
  • Liability for AI Damages: Ambiguities in establishing legal responsibility and liability for damages caused by automated or AI-assisted outcomes Passage 6.
  • Case Backlogs: Resolving procedural delays while maintaining strict adherence to human rights and due process standards Passage 3, Passage 5.
