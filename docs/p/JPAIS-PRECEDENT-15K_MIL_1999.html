Overview

Supreme Court Decision No. 15 K/MIL/1999 establishes an essential legal precedent within the military judiciary that narcotics abuse committed by Indonesian National Armed Forces (*TNI*) soldiers constitutes a grave crime that ruins families, the younger generation, and the state. The Supreme Court affirmed that sentences imposed on TNI soldiers proven guilty of narcotics crimes cannot be limited solely to imprisonment and fines, but must mandatorily include the additional penalty of dismissal from military service. The appellate court's omission of the additional penalty of dismissal lacked adequate justification from an educational, preventive, corrective, and repressive standpoint.

Case Background

The Defendant (Second Lieutenant / Letda Inf. Agus Isrok, Deputy Commander of the Special Unit of Detachment 411 Group 4 Kopassus) was caught in flagrante delicto alongside Witness I ([Defendant]) on August 8, 1999, in Room 408 of the Travel Hotel, West Jakarta, by officers from the West Jakarta Metro Police. Upon searching, authorities discovered evidence consisting of 1.6 kg of methamphetamine (*sabu-sabu*), 27.6 grams of heroin (*putauw*), 6,218 ecstasy tablets, 25 strips of valium, marijuana, and a lighter containing methamphetamine crystals.

The Jakarta Military Court II-08 (Decision No. PUT/58-K/MM II-08/AD/VI/2000) sentenced the Defendant to 4 years of imprisonment, a fine of Rp 10,000,000, and the additional penalty of dismissal from military service. On appeal, the Jakarta High Military Court II (Decision No. PUT.34-14/BDG/K-AD/MMT-II/XI/2000) modified the sentence to 2 years of imprisonment and a fine of Rp 3,000,000, while VACATING the additional penalty of dismissal from the TNI-AD. The Military Prosecutor subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi: Because the criminal offense committed by the defendant involves narcotics abuse—which is regarded by both society and the Government as a serious crime capable of destroying families, the younger generation, and the State—the penalty imposed on the defendant is insufficient when limited to imprisonment and a fine, but must also incorporate the additional penalty of dismissal from the Kopassus TNI membership; consequently, the decision of the Jakarta High Military Court II must be annulled.
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition filed by the Petitioner for Cassation / MILITARY PROSECUTOR AT THE MILITARY PROSECUTOR'S OFFICE II-08 JAKARTA; Annulling the Jakarta High Military Court II Decision No. PUT.34-14/BDG/K-AD/MMT-II/XI/2000; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring Defendant Letda Inf. Agus Isrok legally and convincingly proven guilty of the criminal offense of "unlawfully and without right possessing, storing for possession or supply, or controlling Category I Narcotics other than plants"; (2) Sentencing the Defendant to Principal Penalty: Imprisonment for 2 (two) years and a fine amounting to Rp 10,000,000 (ten million rupiahs) with a subsidiary confinement of 6 (six) months; (3) Additional Penalty: Dismissal from Military Service; (4) Charging all procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the Respondent for Cassation / Defendant.
