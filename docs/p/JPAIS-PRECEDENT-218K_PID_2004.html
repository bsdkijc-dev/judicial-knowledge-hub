Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 218 K/Pid/2004 dated July 14, 2004, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal rules regarding the separation of criminal and civil legal domains in land disputes: The *Judex Facti* erred in applying the law by failing to thoroughly consider the documentary evidence of land ownership presented at trial. Disputes concerning the validity and truth of land ownership between heirs and other parties claiming rights based on a power of attorney or agreement fall within the scope of Civil Court jurisdiction. If a Defendant's failure to execute a power of attorney to sell land constitutes a breach of contract (*wanprestasi*) rather than a criminal offense, the Defendant must be released from all legal prosecutions (*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging*).

Case Background

The Defendants (1. [Defendant], 2. Murhali bin Misin, 3. Murtabak bin Misin, 4. Rinan bin Misin) were brought before the West Jakarta District Court charged under Primary counts of Article 266 paragraph (1) jo. Article 55 paragraph (1) point 1 of the KUHP (ordering the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed) and Subsidiary counts of Article 385 paragraph (1) point 1 jo. Article 55 paragraph (1) point 1 of the KUHP (embezzlement of rights over immovable property / *Delict Stellionaat*). The case originated from a dispute over inherited land under Girik C.647 parcel 113b D.III in Kapuk Village, Cengkareng, West Jakarta. Reporting witness [Party B] claimed to have purchased the land from H. Mursahid bin Misin measuring 6,000 m² in 1992, but subsequent land measurement revealed an area of 8,342 m² (an excess of 2,342 m²). On February 1, 2000, the Defendants granted a power of attorney to [Party B] to sell the excess land, but on May 19, 2000, the Defendants together with Sutrisno sold the land themselves to another party via Notary Marlon Silitonga, SH.

The West Jakarta District Court (Decision No. 423/PID/B/2002/PN.JKT.BAR dated May 6, 2002) acquitted the Defendants of the Primary charge, but convicted them on the Subsidiary charge (Article 385 paragraph (1)e of the KUHP), sentencing each to 1 (one) year of imprisonment. The Jakarta High Court (Decision No. 64/PID/2003/PT.DKI dated May 23, 2003) upheld the West Jakarta District Court judgment. Dissatisfied with the ruling, the Defendants filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The *Judex Facti* erred in applying the law and failed to properly evaluate the documentary evidence submitted during trial.
  2. Pursuant to Supreme Court civil decision No. 4502 K/Pdt/1998, the Defendants ([Defendant], Murhali, Murtabak, Rinan) are the lawful owners of the disputed land and never sold it to witness [Party B]. Witness [Party B] was merely asked to handle the land case at the Supreme Court and borrowed the Defendants' land documents. Furthermore, [Party B] was himself convicted under cassation decision No. 1739 K/Pid/2002 for forging the land sale agreement.
  3. The power of attorney to sell land dated February 1, 2000, given by the Defendants to [Party B], when unexecuted by the Defendants, constitutes a breach of contract (*wanprestasi*) within the domain of civil law.
  4. Land ownership disputes fall within the absolute competence of the Civil Courts. Because the acts committed by the Defendants under the Subsidiary charge do not constitute a criminal offense, the Defendants must be released from all legal prosecutions (*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging*).
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioners for Cassation / Defendants: 1. [Defendant], 2. MURHALI bin MISIN, 3. MURTABAK bin MISIN, 4. RINAN bin MISIN; Annulling the West Jakarta District Court decision dated May 6, 2002, No. 423/PID/B/2002/PN.JKT.BAR and the Jakarta High Court decision dated May 23, 2003, No. 64/PID/2003/PT.DKI; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring that the acts of the Defendants (1. [Defendant], 2. Murhali bin Misin, 3. Murtabak bin Misin, and 4. Rinan bin Misin) as charged under the Subsidiary count do not constitute a criminal offense; (2) Releasing the Defendants from all legal prosecutions (*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging*); (3) Restoring the Defendants' rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity; (4) Determining the status of evidence items (sale agreement and receipt in the name of H. Mursahid seized from Sutrisno shall be destroyed, while other evidence items shall be returned to witness [Party B] or annexed to the file); (5) Charging cassation-level court costs to the State. *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Wednesday, July 14, 2004, by Prof. Dr. Paulus Effendie Lotulung, SH. [Presiding Judge / Session Chairman], Ny. Titi Nurmala Siagian, SH., and Ny. Chairani A. Wani, SH. [Associate Justices]).*
