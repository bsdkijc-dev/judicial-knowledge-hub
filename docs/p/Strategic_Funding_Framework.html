Strategic Funding Framework

The funding framework of the Supreme Court for 2025–2029 is designed to connect strategic targets, performance targets, and available resources.

Funding Priorities

  • strengthening core judicial functions;
  • improving judicial services;
  • institutional transformation;
  • e-Court and e-Berpadu;
  • public-service quality;
  • human-resource management;
  • asset management;
  • financial management;
  • access to justice for vulnerable groups;
  • judicial competence.

Access-to-Justice Funding

Funding is directed toward:

  • fee-waiver programs;
  • hearings outside court buildings;
  • increased judicial competence.

Strategic Principle

Strategic Planning → Performance Targets → Resource Allocation → Outputs → Outcomes
