Empirical Grounding & Systemic Context / Landasan Empiris & Konteks Sistemik

  • The Indonesian Baseline Problem (KCCP-PROB-001): Mandatory court-annexed mediation under PERMA No. 1 of 2016 operates as an obligatory procedural prerequisite before trial, recording nationwide general civil settlement success rates between 6.8% and 7.42% in District Courts (Pengadilan Negeri). Active judge mediators face heavy trial dockets, while external non-judge mediators lack dedicated state budget (DIPA) funding, resulting in high roster attrition and low success rates (e.g., 7.04% at Tangerang Religious Court).
  • The Japanese Comparative Model (KCCP-PRACT-JP-001 & 003): Under the Civil Conciliation Act of 1951 (Act No. 222 of 1951), Japan’s Minji Chōtei framework consistently resolves over 50% of petitions. Japan utilizes Conciliation Committees (Chōtei Iinkai) comprising 1 presiding Conciliation Judge (Chōtei Kan-an) and 2 lay Conciliation Commissioners (Chōtei Iin) drawn from specialized backgrounds. Article 17 empowers courts to issue ex-officio Orders in Lieu of Conciliation (Chōtei ni kawaru kettei), which gain binding force if no party objects within two weeks.

Core Reform Adaptation / Adaptasi Reformasi Inti

  • Tripartite Conciliation Panels (Majelis Konsiliasi Tripartit): Pairs 1 certified Judge Mediator with 2 accredited Non-Judge Expert Commissioners for commercial, land, and property disputes exceeding Rp 500 million.
  • Orders in Lieu of Settlement (Penetapan Pengganti Perdamaian): Authorizes panels to issue a provisional evaluative settlement order upon impasse, converting into a binding Settlement Deed (Akta Perdamaian) unless a party files an objection within 14 calendar days.
  • State Budget Allocations: Establishes fixed per-case honoraria for certified non-judge mediators via a Joint Ministerial Decree (SKB) between the Supreme Court, Ministry of Law, and Ministry of Finance.

Phased Implementation & Pilot Targets / Implementasi Bertahap & Target Pilot

  • 90-Day Pilot Deployment (KCCP-PILOT-001): Executed across representative trial courts including PN Jakarta Selatan, PA Jakarta Selatan, PN Surabaya, and PN Sleman.
  • Target Benchmarks (KCCP-KPI-001):

 * Increase District Court settlement rates to  ≥ 25.0.
 * Reduce average settlement duration from 30 days to  ≤ 18 calendar days.
 * Expand non-judge panel allocation to  ≥ 45.0 of pilot cases.
