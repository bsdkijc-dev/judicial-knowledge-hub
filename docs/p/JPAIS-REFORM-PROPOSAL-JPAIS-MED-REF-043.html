Description / Deskripsi

This page records the comparative legal analysis of the four-generation regulatory framework governing objections against decisions of the Commission for the Supervision of Business Competition (KPPU), authored by competition law expert Prof. Dr. Ningrum Natasya Sirait, S.H., M.Li. (Universitas Sumatera Utara). The study traces twenty years of procedural rulemaking by the Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia—from PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2003, PERMA No. 3 Tahun 2005, PERMA No. 3 Tahun 2019, to the current PERMA No. 3 Tahun 2021. It highlights major paradigm shifts, including the nature of objection petitions (from civil lawsuits to administrative review permohonan), trial duration limits (from 30 days to 3–12 months), definition of days (from working days to calendar days), and the absolute jurisdictional transfer from 412 District Courts to 5 specialized Commercial Courts pursuant to Article 118 of Law No. 11/2020 (Job Creation Law). All historical comparison matrices and normative evolutions have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher Cecep Mustafa.

Problem / Masalah

  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-050: Normative Oscillations in Time Management, where moving from a strict 30-day trial cap (PERMA 3/2005 and 3/2019) to an expanded 3-to-12-month window (PERMA 3/2021) created severe friction with general summary proceedings in Commercial Courts.
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-051: Calendar Days Conflict & Strict Evidence Lock, where defining "days" as calendar days in PERMA No. 3/2021 conflicts directly with PP No. 44/2021 (specifying working days), and a total ban on new documentary evidence traps judges into acting as dossier checkers.

Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

  • JPAIS-MED-ID-043: All competition objections are filed exclusively at 5 regional Commercial Courts via *e-Court* pursuant to PERMA No. 3/2021, requiring submission of legal power of attorney, petition, KPPU ruling copy, and a legalized bank guarantee, proceeding without court mediation and bypassing Judicial Review (*Peninjauan Kembali*).

Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

  • JPAIS-MED-JP-043: In Japan (Tokyo High Court / JFTC), specialized review of Japan Fair Trade Commission orders evolved over decades from administrative panel reviews to direct High Court litigation, establishing consistent evidentiary standards without shifting definitions of trial days, providing a benchmark for stable statutory procedural rules.

Gap / Kesenjangan

  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-043: The primary structural gap is the ongoing instability of procedural rules caused by relying on administrative Supreme Court Regulations (PERMAs) that frequently alter filing definitions, trial durations, and evidence admissibility standards without a unifying parliamentary statute.

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Harmonize PERMA No. 3/2021 to restore "working days" as the uniform metric matching PP No. 44/2021; codify clear criteria permitting new documentary evidence when KPPU introduces novel market definitions or fine formulas; and replace the 3–12 month window with a strict maximum cap of 60 working days.

Action / Aksi

  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-043: Issue a Supreme Court Circular (SEMA) resolving calendar vs. working day conflicts and clarifying bank guarantee waivers for non-monetary remedies.
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-044: Promulgate PERMA Amandemen 3/2021 incorporating Prof. Ningrum Natasya Sirait's comparative findings and Pokja DIM recommendations.

Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

  • JPAIS-MED-PILOT-043: National Commercial Court harmonization pilot rollout across Central Jakarta, Medan, Semarang, Surabaya, and Makassar.

KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

  • JPAIS-MED-KPI-043: 100% harmonization of procedural deadline calculations across all Commercial Courts; adjudication efficiency maintained strictly at $\le 60$ working days per case.

Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Drafting a comprehensive Bill on Commercial Court Procedural Law (*RUU Hukum Acara Peradilan Niaga*) to unify competition, bankruptcy, and intellectual property litigation, alongside strengthening BSDK MA RI training programs on the historical evolution of competition law procedure.

Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Providing a cohesive historical evaluation of Supreme Court rulemaking to guide future PERMA amendments and legislative codification.

Evidence / Bukti Empiris

  • Comparative analysis presentation document (*Ningrum_2026 agustus 1 Perbandingan PerMA KPPU Sejak 2003.pdf*) authored by Prof. Dr. Ningrum Natasya Sirait, S.H., M.Li. (Universitas Sumatera Utara) in August 2026.
