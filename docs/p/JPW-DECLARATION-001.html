JPW-DECLARATION-001

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 916, 2026-09-15 · wiki source · Markdown

Kenya Declaration on Non-Acceptance of Jurisdiction over the Crime of Aggression (2015) is an official international legal instrument issued by the Republic of Kenya declaring its non-acceptance of the International Criminal Court's (ICC) jurisdiction over the crime of aggression pursuant to paragraph 4 of Article 15bis of the Rome Statute [].

Summary

This declaration conveys the decision of the Republic of Kenya that it does not consider itself bound by the provisions of Article 8bis of the Rome Statute concerning the Crime of Aggression []. Kenya evaluated that the definition of the crime of aggression adopted at the 2010 Kampala Review Conference contains ambiguities and conflicts with the Constitution of the Republic of Kenya [].

Legal Basis

  • Rome Statute: Article 15bis(4), Article 8bis, and Article 12 [].
  • Constitution of the Republic of Kenya [].
  • United Nations General Assembly Resolution 3314 (XXIX) of 14 December 1974 [].

Background

The Assembly of States Parties adopted amendments on the crime of aggression at the Review Conference in Kampala from 31 May to 11 June 2010 []. Upon examining the amendments, Kenya determined that provisions within Article 8bis paragraph 2(f) could restrict a state's right to request intervention from neighboring states to restore peace and security within its territory [].

Objectives

  • Prevent risks of abuse arising from ambiguities in the definition of the crime of aggression [].
  • Protect Kenya's sovereign right to request regional defense assistance or intervention [].
  • Reaffirm that matters concerning the crime of aggression fall squarely within the primary mandate of the UN Security Council [].

Key Provisions

  • **Non-Acceptance of Jurisdiction**: Kenya explicitly declines ICC jurisdiction over the crime of aggression in accordance with Article 15bis(4) of the Rome Statute [].
  • **Constitutional Incompatibility**: Article 8bis provisions were declared incompatible with the Kenyan Constitution [].
  • **Resolution 3314 Reference**: Kenya referenced the distinction between an "act of aggression" and a "war of aggression," noting that only a war of aggression constitutes a crime against international peace [].

Implementation

The declaration was transmitted via Diplomatic Note No. MFA. 1NT. 8/14AVOL. X (86) dated 30 November 2015 by Amb. (Dr.) Amina C. Mohamed (Cabinet Secretary for Foreign Affairs and International Trade) directly addressed to the Registrar of the ICC in The Hague, Netherlands [].

Policy Issues

Legal tensions arose between ICC jurisdiction over international crimes and domestic constitutional authority of member states, alongside potential restrictions on collective military assistance frameworks among neighboring states [].

Relationships with Other Entities

  • **International Criminal Court**: Recipient organ of the official declaration instrument [].
  • **Amb. (Dr.) Amina C. Mohamed**: Government official who signed the instrument [].
  • **United Nations Security Council**: International organ recognized by Kenya as holding primary authority over aggression [].
