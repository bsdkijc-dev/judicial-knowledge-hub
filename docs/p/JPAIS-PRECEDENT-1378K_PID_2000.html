By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 1738, 2026-09-23 · wiki source · Markdown

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 1378 K/Pid/2000 (dated November 16, 2000) is an Indonesian criminal jurisprudence precedent on psychotropic offenses establishing legal rules regarding the qualification of **distributing psychotropics** (Article 60 paragraph (1) letter c of Law No. 5 of 1997) versus mere **possession/storage** (Article 62 of Law No. 5 of 1997).

Executive Summary

The Defendant's actions in controlling and storing psychotropics in large quantities (353 ecstasy pills and 14 packages of methamphetamine), equipped with weighing instruments (electronic scales) and packaging materials (plastic bundles and aluminum foil), coupled with proven delivery of methamphetamine to a buyer (witness [Witness]) in exchange for cash, **do not merely constitute possession or storage** (Article 62 of Law No. 5 of 1997), but fully satisfy the elements of "distributing psychotropics in the form of drugs not registered with the government department responsible for health" ex Article 60 paragraph (1) letter c of Law No. 5 of 1997 on Psychotropics.

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition from the Public Prosecutor at the Sleman District Attorney's Office, annulled the Yogyakarta High Court Decision No. 43/Pid/2000/PT.Y, and **independently adjudicated** (*mengadili sendiri*) by reinstating the considerations of the Sleman District Court Decision No. 15/Pid.B/2000/PN.SLMN, sentencing the Defendant ([Defendant]) to **6 (six) years** of imprisonment and a fine of **Rp 25,000,000** (twenty-five million rupiahs) with a subsidiary of 3 (three) months of confinement.

Legal / Procedural Issues

1. Differentiation of Legal Qualifications Between the Offense of "Distributing Psychotropics" and "Possessing/Storing Psychotropics"

Does the control of psychotropics in large quantities (353 ecstasy pills and 14 methamphetamine packages) accompanied by weighing instruments (electronic scales), packaging materials, and proven delivery of goods in exchange for cash qualify merely as passive possession/storage (Article 62 of Law No. 5/1997)?

Supreme Court Rule: It cannot be classified merely as storage (proven distribution). The Yogyakarta High Court erred in applying the law by narrowly interpreting the term "distributing" (*mengedarkan*). The sequence of trial facts—digital weighing instruments, commercial packaging tools, large-scale inventory supplies, and actual sales transactions to witness [Witness] for cash compensation—demonstrates conclusively that the Defendant operated commercial distribution activities, fulfilling the formulation of "distributing psychotropics in the form of drugs not registered with the Health Department" ex Article 60 paragraph (1) letter c of Law No. 5 of 1997.

2. Admissibility of Witness [Witness]'s Testimony Tried in a Severed Case File (Crown Witness / De Cautelo) as Valid Evidence (Article 185 of KUHAP)

The High Court erred in disregarding witness [Witness]'s testimony on the grounds that she was tried in a separate case file (*splitsing*). Witness testimony given under oath during trial and corroborated by physical seizure evidence constitutes valid witness testimony ex Article 185 of the Criminal Procedure Code (KUHAP) to prove distribution elements.

Question of Law vs Question of Fact

Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the Judex Facti (Yogyakarta High Court Decision No. 43/Pid/2000/PT.Y) err in applying the law (foutieve toepassing van het recht) by acquitting the Defendant of the Primary Charge under Article 60 paragraph (1) letter c of Law No. 5 of 1997 and reclassifying the offense under Article 62 of Law No. 5 of 1997, despite trial facts proving the presence of scales, packaging, and psychotropic sales transactions?

Supreme Court Legal Answer: Yes. The Yogyakarta High Court erred in applying the law. The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Sleman District Attorney's Office, annulled Yogyakarta High Court Decision No. 43/Pid/2000/PT.Y, and independently adjudicated by affirming the legal considerations of Sleman District Court Decision No. 15/Pid.B/2000/PN.SLMN: declaring Defendant [Defendant] legally and convincingly guilty of violating Article 60 paragraph (1) letter c of Law No. 5 of 1997 and sentencing her to 6 (six) years of imprisonment and a fine of Rp 25,000,000 subsidiary 3 (three) months of confinement.

Jurisprudence and Related Knowledge

Law No. 5 of 1997 on Psychotropics

  • Article 60 paragraph (1) letter c: Crime of distributing psychotropics in the form of drugs not registered with the Department of Health (punishable by a maximum of 15 years imprisonment and a maximum fine of Rp 200,000,000).
  • Article 62: Crime of unlawfully possessing, storing, and/or carrying psychotropics (punishable by a maximum of 5 years imprisonment and a maximum fine of Rp 100,000,000).

Law No. 8 of 1981 (Criminal Procedure Code / KUHAP)

  • Article 185 KUHAP: Evidentiary weight of witness testimony at trial.
  • Article 253 paragraph (1) KUHAP: Supreme Court cassation authority regarding errors of law applied by the Judex Facti.

Law No. 14 of 1970 jo. Law No. 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court

  • Authority to independently adjudicate criminal cassation cases.

What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

Established by Evidence

  • Defendant [Defendant] is legally and convincingly proven guilty of committing the criminal offense of "distributing psychotropics in the form of drugs not registered with the department responsible for health" ex Article 60 paragraph (1) letter c of Law No. 5 of 1997.
  • Recovery of large-scale evidence (353 ecstasy pills, 14 methamphetamine packages) alongside distribution instruments (digital electronic scales, aluminum foil, packaging plastic, and Rp 1,600,000 in cash proceeds).
  • The Defendant was proven to have executed a methamphetamine transfer transaction to witness [Witness].
  • The Defendant was sentenced to imprisonment for 6 (six) years and a fine of Rp 25,000,000 subsidiary 3 months of confinement.
  • Narcotic/psychotropic physical evidence, scales, and packaging were ordered confiscated for destruction, while cash amounting to Rp 1,600,000 was confiscated for the State.

Annulled (Yogyakarta High Court Verdict)

  • The High Court's qualification treating the Defendant's actions merely as "storage/possession" ex Article 62 of Law No. 5/1997 was entirely annulled by the Supreme Court as contradicting commercial trade/distribution facts.

Uncertainties and Gaps

Objective criteria separating storage/users and distributors of psychotropics: This landmark ruling establishes crucial jurisprudence in Indonesian narcotics and psychotropic criminal law: courts are prohibited from reclassifying distributors into passive storage offenders if objective commercial indicators (digital scales, commercial packaging/clip plastics, large-scale inventory, and transactional cash flow evidence) are recovered at the arrest site. Possession of weighing and packaging instruments constitutes legally valid presumption of distribution.

Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Does the possession of large quantities of methamphetamine and ecstasy equipped with electronic scales and packaging plastics qualify as distributing (Article 60 of Law No. 5/1997) or merely storing (Article 62 of Law No. 5/1997)?
Qualified as distributing (Article 60 paragraph (1) letter c of Law No. 5 of 1997). Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence No. 1378 K/Pid/2000, storing large quantities of psychotropics (353 ecstasy pills and 14 methamphetamine packages) equipped with electronic scales, aluminum foil, and plastic packaging bundles, alongside proven sales transactions involving the transfer of goods for cash, does not constitute mere possession/storage (Article 62), but satisfies the elements of "distributing psychotropics" ex Article 60 paragraph (1) letter c of Law No. 5 of 1997.
