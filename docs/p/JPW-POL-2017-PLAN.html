Policy Analysis

  • Background & Policy Problem:
    • FACT: General Penal Code offenses in Japan have declined since peaking in 2002, yet the re-offender rate reached a record high of 48.8% in 2018.
    • FACT: Statistical data indicates ~30% of re-offenders account for ~60% of total reported crimes.
    • FACT: Unemployed offenders experience a re-offending rate 3 to 5 times higher than employed individuals.
    • FACT: Traditional criminal justice measures face clear limits without cross-ministerial coordination and community involvement to address social isolation, employment barriers, addiction, and mental health issues.
  • Policy Objectives:
    • FACT: Prevent ex-offenders and juvenile delinquents from social isolation while facilitating smooth recovery and community reintegration.
    • FACT: Protect citizens from repeat victimization to construct the "world's safest society."
    • FACT: Implement the "leave no one behind" principle in alignment with social inclusion goals.
  • Key Provisions & Reforms:
    • FACT: 5 Basic Policies (5つの基本方針): Outlines central-local-private collaboration, seamless support at every justice stage, respect for crime victims' rights, evidence-based evaluation, and public awareness.
    • FACT: 7 Priority Issues (7つの重点課題): Groups 115 concrete action measures into: (1) Employment & Housing Support, (2) Health, Medical & Welfare Services, (3) Educational Support, (4) Tailored Guidance, (5) Private Volunteers & Public Relations, (6) Local Government Collaboration, and (7) Human & Physical Infrastructure.
    • FACT: Key Performance Indicators (KPIs): Targets a reduction in the 2-year re-incarceration rate by 20% or more by 2021 (baseline: 16.9% in 2017).
  • Implementation Schedule:
    • FACT: Covers a 5-year operational term across FY2018 through FY2022.
    • FACT: Mandates plan review and revision at least once every 5 years.
