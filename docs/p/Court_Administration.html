Court Administration

Court administration concerns the organizational, managerial, financial, technological, and operational activities required to support the functioning of courts.

[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED]

Core Functions

Potential functions include:

  • Human resources
  • Budget management
  • Facilities
  • Information technology
  • Case administration
  • Records management
  • Public services
  • Security
  • Procurement
  • Judicial education

Administration and Adjudication

Court administration should be distinguished conceptually from judicial decision-making.

Administrative efficiency should support, rather than improperly interfere with, judicial independence.

Digital Administration

Digital court administration may include:

  • Electronic records
  • Workflow management
  • Digital correspondence
  • Scheduling
  • Dashboards
  • Data integration
  • Digital archives

AI Applications

Potential applications include:

  • Administrative automation
  • Document classification
  • Workflow prediction
  • Resource planning
  • Chatbots for public information
  • Data analytics

High-impact applications require appropriate governance.
