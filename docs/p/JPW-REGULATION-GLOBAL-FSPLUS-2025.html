JPW-REGULATION-GLOBAL-FSPLUS-2025

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 931, 2026-09-15 · wiki source · Markdown

FS+ Country Updates – September 2025 is the inaugural edition of a global regulatory update publication issued by Eversheds Sutherland to provide key insights into legal and regulatory changes affecting investment managers across multiple jurisdictions [1–6].

Summary

This publication was previously released under the name International FundsNet Country Updates [1–6]. Each edition summarizes major regulatory shifts across global jurisdictions, including Ireland, Canada, Taiwan, and Mauritius [1–6].

Legal Basis

The contents of this publication are provided for general information purposes only and do not constitute formal legal advice [1–6]. The legal basis for each update is derived from the respective local regulatory authorities:

  • Ireland: AIF Rulebook consultation issued by the Central Bank of Ireland [1–6].
  • Canada: New AML/CTF supervisory framework released by FINTRAC [1–6].
  • Taiwan: Securities firm rules and sustainability reporting updates issued by FSC and SITCA [1–6].
  • Mauritius: New ESG fund guidelines published by the FSC [1–6].

Background

Investment managers face increasingly complex compliance challenges due to rapid regulatory shifts across international borders [1–6]. Eversheds Sutherland rebranded and structured this publication series to offer clearer, actionable regulatory intelligence for financial market participants [1–6].

Objectives

  1. Provide a centralized summary of global regulatory developments for asset and investment managers [1–6].
  2. Outline updates to the QIAIF regime and UCITS regulations in Ireland [1–6].
  3. Inform market participants about new AML/CTF monitoring frameworks and penalty policies in Canada [1–6].
  4. Drive compliance with evolving ESG disclosure standards in Taiwan and Mauritius [1–6].

Key Provisions

  • Ireland: The Central Bank of Ireland confirmed the publication of an AIF rulebook consultation in September 2025, focusing on enhancing the QIAIF regime and updating UCITS rules [1–6].
  • Canada: FINTRAC introduced a new AML/CTF oversight framework and penalty policy targeting mutual fund dealers [1–6].
  • Taiwan: FSC amended securities firm rules and sustainability reporting obligations; SITCA updated fund sales requirements and ESG disclosure guidelines [1–6].
  • Mauritius: FSC published new guidelines for ESG funds establishing explicit disclosure, reporting, and compliance standards [1–6].

Implementation & Governance

  • Publication Date: Formally released as the inaugural FS+ edition in September/October 2025 [1–6].
  • Editorial Contacts: Oversight and primary contact roles are held by Lindi Rudman (Legal Director, UK) and Michaela Arter (Partner, UK) at Eversheds Sutherland [1–6].

Policy Issues & Analysis

  • ESG Disparities: Significant variation remains in ESG disclosure thresholds between jurisdictions such as Taiwan and Mauritius [1–6].
  • AML/CTF Enforcement: Enhanced penalty structures under FINTRAC necessitate stricter internal compliance for global investment funds operating in Canada [1–6].
