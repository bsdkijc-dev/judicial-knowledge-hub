Policy Analysis

  • Background & Core Facts:
    • Covers post-COVID-19 international traffic recovery in 2023. Total foreign arrivals reached 25,830,810 (a 515.3% increase over 2022), consisting of 23,751,693 new arrivals and 2,079,117 re-entries.
    • Foreign Residents Stock (2023): Record high of 3,410,992 foreign residents (2.74% of Japan's total population). Top nationalities: China (821,838 / 24.1%), Vietnam (565,026 / 16.6%), South Korea (410,156 / 12.0%), Philippines (322,046 / 9.4%), and Brazil (211,840 / 6.2%). Top statuses: Permanent Resident (891,569), Engineer/Specialist in Humanities (362,346), Technical Intern Training (404,556), Student (340,883), and Specified Skilled Worker (i) (208,425).
    • Technical Intern Training (TITP) & Specified Skilled Workers (SSW): OTIT supervised 3,718 licensed organizations and conducted 26,153 field inspections in FY2023. Revoked accreditation for 120 intern plans and 5 organization licenses. Absconded trainees rose to 9,753 (led by Vietnam: 5,481 and Myanmar: 1,765). SSW holders reached 251,747 by June 2024, concentrated in food manufacture, machinery/electronics, nursing care, and construction.
    • Enforcement, Overstay, & Deportation: Status revocations rose 10.2% to 1,240 cases (led by Technical Interns: 79.3%). Overstayers estimated at 79,113 as of Jan 1, 2024. Enforcement procedures initiated against 18,198 individuals; 12,384 were illegal workers primarily employed in agriculture and construction.
    • Refugee & Complementary Protection: Asylum applications surged to 13,823 in 2023. 289 were recognized as Convention refugees, and 1,005 granted humanitarian stay. Launched the Complementary Protection System on Dec 1, 2023, receiving 678 applicants (669 from Ukraine).
  • Interpretations & Structural Inferences:
    • Reflects a strategic balance between accelerating economic/tourism recovery, expanding skilled labor intake (SSW), enforcing stricter supervision over intern programs, and broadening humanitarian asylum paths.
