Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 119 K/Pid/1982 dated March 17, 1983 (pronounced on March 31, 1983), establishes fundamental Criminal Procedure jurisprudence regarding prosecutorial appeals against acquittals: An appeal cannot be lodged by the Public Prosecutor against a pure acquittal (*vrijspraak*), unless it can be proven that the acquittal is in fact an impure acquittal (*verkapte ontslag van rechtsvervolging*), which must be elaborated and proven by the Prosecutor within their appellate memorandum. If the Public Prosecutor fails to submit an appellate memorandum detailing or proving an impure acquittal, the Prosecutor's appeal must be declared inadmissible (*niet ontvankelijke verklaard*).

Case Background

The Defendants ([Party C] [Defendant I] and [Party B] [Defendant II], alongside Nawar, Sjafri, and Zakaria) were brought before the Batusangkar District Court indicted on primary charges of premeditated murder (Article 340 jo. Article 55 of the KUHP) and subsidiary charges of severe maltreatment resulting in death (Article 351 paragraph (1) sub 3 jo. Article 55 of the KUHP) against the victim [Party A] / Bachrum alias Mendek on August 15, 1968, in Parak Pandan, Jorong Tanjung Pauh, Lintau Buo, Tanah Datar.

The Batusangkar District Court (Decision No. 101/1969/PN.BS dated August 26, 1971) ruled to acquit all Defendants of all charges (*vrijspraak*). Against this acquittal, the Public Prosecutor filed an appeal but never filed or submitted an appellate memorandum. The Padang High Court (Decision No. 34/1976/PT.Pdg dated November 23, 1976) nevertheless accepted the Prosecutor's appeal, annulled the Batusangkar District Court decision specifically regarding Defendants I and II, and independently adjudicated the matter by sentencing Defendants I and II to 4 (four) years of imprisonment each. Dissatisfied with the Padang High Court ruling, Defendant I and Defendant II filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The cassation objections raised by the Petitioners for Cassation (Defendants I and II) are well-founded, because the Padang High Court should not have accepted the Public Prosecutor's appeal.
  2. Against a judgment of acquittal (*vrijspraak*), an appeal cannot be lodged by the Prosecutor unless it can be proven that the acquittal is actually an impure acquittal (*verkapte ontslag van rechtsvervolging*), which the Prosecutor is obligated to detail and prove within their appellate memorandum.
  3. Because the Public Prosecutor in this case never filed an appellate memorandum detailing an impure acquittal, the Prosecutor's appeal must legally be declared inadmissible (*niet ontvankelijke verklaard*). The action of the Padang High Court in examining and annulling the District Court's acquittal without the presence of an appellate memorandum violates criminal procedure law.
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioners for Cassation I. [Party C] and II. [Party B]; Annulling the Padang High Court decision dated November 23, 1976, No. 34/1976/PT.Pdg; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring that the Public Prosecutor's appeal against Defendant I ([Party C]) and Defendant II ([Party B]) is inadmissible (*niet ontvankelijke verklaard*); (2) Charging procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State. *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Cassation Panel of Justices on Thursday, March 17, 1983, by R. Djoko Soegianto, S.H. [Junior Chairman], Ny. Dora Sasongko Kartono, S.H., and H. Syafiar, S.H., and pronounced on Thursday, March 31, 1983).*
