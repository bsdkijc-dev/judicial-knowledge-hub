The NACM-IACA Joint Partnership Policy Framework for Court Administration Excellence is a strategic partnership and evaluation framework aimed at advancing court administration and global rule of law enforcement. Formulated by Scott Griffith (President of the National Association for Court Management / NACM) and Vladimir Freitas (President of the International Association for Court Administration / IACA) in the Presidents' Message of the International Journal for Court Administration (IJCA), Special Issue, Vol. 8 No. 3 (July 2017), it establishes a cooperative framework between the world's two largest professional court management organizations. Initiated during leadership discussions in the summer of 2014 and supported by the National Center for State Courts (NCSC), the framework operationalized the NACM-IACA Joint Annual Conference to share knowledge, provide educational programs based on practical tools and tips, expand remote learning through live streaming, and strengthen the global leadership of the court administration profession [FACT].

Summary

Published by Scott Griffith and Vladimir Freitas in the IJCA (July 2017), this policy framework outlines the strategic partnership between the National Association for Court Management (NACM) and the International Association for Court Administration (IACA). Originating from leadership discussions in the summer of 2014, and supported by the National Center for State Courts (NCSC), volunteer committees from both organizations dedicated hundreds of hours to designing a joint annual educational conference. The alliance between the world's two largest professional court associations aims to advance the rule of law globally, deliver practical tools and tips for modern court administration challenges, and provide inclusive educational access via live streams and the NACM website.

Background

The increasingly complex challenges of modern court administration require global collaboration beyond fragmented local perspectives, demanding unified efforts from national and international court management associations.

Objectives

1. Strengthen the enforcement of the rule of law and advance court administration governance globally. 2. Facilitate a process for court professionals worldwide to learn from, challenge, and inspire one another. 3. Provide practical tools and tips alongside remote educational access via live streaming and web repositories.

Scope

This policy encompasses the international partnership between NACM, IACA, and the NCSC in organizing joint conferences and disseminating global judicial education programs.

Key Provisions

  • Partnership of the World's Two Largest Court Management Organizations:
    • Merging the networks of NACM and IACA to direct and dictate the course of the court administration profession globally.
  • Integrated and Practical Educational Programs:
    • Structuring conference curricula focused on practical tools to address contemporary court management challenges.
  • Inclusive Digital Learning Access:
    • Providing live streaming facilities and educational material repositories on official NACM platforms for professionals unable to attend in person.

Implementation

  • Volunteer Committee Collaboration (Since 2014): The execution of joint meetings, conference calls, and budget planning between NACM, IACA, and the NCSC.
  • IJCA Special Issue Vol. 8 No. 3 (2017) Publication: The release of a dedicated scientific issue supporting the joint annual conference.

Policy Problems

  • Limited Reach of Physical Conferences: The necessity of providing educational access for judicial professionals who cannot physically attend the event location.
  • Challenges of Modern Court Administration: The critical need for practical, scalable solutions to address the workloads and complexities of current court management.

Reform / Change

  • Institutionalizing the NACM-IACA joint annual conference as a permanent global strategic agenda.
  • Expanding the reach of judicial management education through robust ICT platforms and digital streaming.

  • International Association for Court Administration (IACA): The international judicial administration organization led by President Vladimir Freitas.
