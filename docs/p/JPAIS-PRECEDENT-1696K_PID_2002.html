By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 1919, 2026-09-28 · wiki source · Markdown

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 1696 K/Pid/2002 (dated May 28, 2003) is a landmark Indonesian banking corruption (misappropriation of Bank Indonesia Liquidity Support / BLBI funds) and criminal procedure jurisprudence precedent establishing three vital legal rules regarding the non-extinguishment of criminal unlawfulness by civil settlements or asset restitution, the admissibility of cassation petitions against impure acquittals (niet-zuivere vrijspraak ex Article 244 of KUHAP), and the criminal liability of a President Commissioner in the management of BLBI funds.

Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established three primary legal principles:

  1. Civil Settlements / Asset Restitution Do Not Extinguish Criminal Unlawfulness: The actions of a President Commissioner (the Defendant) in signing the Master Refinancing and Note Issuance Agreement (MRNIA) and executing deeds for the surrender of all Bank Modern assets to the Indonesian Bank Restructuring Agency (IBRA / BPPN) / State as security for debt repayment constitute civil arrangements that cannot eliminate or extinguish the "criminal unlawfulness" (wederrechtelijkheid) of the corrupt misappropriation of BLBI funds causing financial losses to the State.
  2. Admissibility of Cassation Against Impure Acquittals (Niet-Zuivere Vrijspraak ex Article 244 of KUHAP): The Supreme Court possesses full authority to review and annul a District Court acquittal when such acquittal is based on an erroneous legal interpretation (misinterpretation of unlawful acts) regarding the elements of the indicted corruption offenses.
  3. Criminal Liability of President Commissioners / Bank Executives: A President Commissioner who exercises factual control and approval authority over transactions exceeding Rp 10 Billion, and who approves the utilization of BLBI funds outside their designated purposes (for related-party credit expansion, promissory note purchases, import L/C settlements, etc.) in direct contravention of Bank Indonesia's strict prohibitions, bears joint criminal liability alongside the Board of Directors for corruption causing state financial losses amounting to Rp 169,472,986,461.54.

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition from the Public Prosecutor at the Central Jakarta District Attorney's Office, annulled the Central Jakarta District Court Decision No. 1146/Pid.B/2001/PN.Jkt.Pst dated August 1, 2002, and independently adjudicated (mengadili sendiri) by declaring the Defendant [Defendant] legally and convincingly guilty of committing the criminal offense of "Corruption committed jointly and continuously," sentencing the Defendant to 4 (four) years of imprisonment, a fine of Rp 20,000,000 (twenty million rupiahs) with a subsidiary of 4 (four) months of confinement, and ordering him to pay restitution (replacement money) amounting to Rp 169,472,986,461.54.

Legal / Procedural Issues

1. Existence of Criminal Unlawfulness (Wederrechtelijkheid) Regarding Civil Settlements and Asset Restitution (IBRA Restitution / MRNIA)

Does signing a civil settlement agreement (MRNIA) and surrendering collateral assets by bank executives/shareholders to IBRA eliminate the criminal unlawfulness of misappropriating Bank Indonesia Liquidity Support (BLBI) funds?

Supreme Court Rule: Criminal Unlawfulness Persists and Is Not Erased.

The Supreme Court established that misappropriating BLBI funds in contravention of Bank Indonesia Supervisory Letters and Banking Laws constitutes a corruption offense committed at the moment of execution (tempus delicti). Subsequent civil settlement actions through the Master Refinancing and Note Issuance Agreement (MRNIA) or asset surrenders to IBRA represent manifestations of good faith in restoring state losses, but cannot eliminate or erase the criminal unlawfulness (wederrechtelijkheid) that had already been fully consummated.

2. Supreme Court Cassation Competence Over Impure Acquittals (Niet-Zuivere Vrijspraak ex Article 244 of KUHAP)

Does the Public Prosecutor have the right to file a cassation petition against a Central Jakarta District Court decision acquitting the Defendant of BLBI corruption charges, and does the Supreme Court possess authority to annul it?

Supreme Court Rule: Admissible and Competent to Annul.

The acquittal of the Defendant by the Central Jakarta District Court based on an erroneous legal interpretation—treating BLBI fund misappropriation as a purely civil dispute—constitutes an impure acquittal (niet-zuivere vrijspraak). Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence jo. Article 244 of KUHAP, the Supreme Court possesses authority to examine and annul such acquittals in the interest of fair banking law enforcement.

3. Factual Role and Criminal Liability Qualification of President Commissioners in Corporate Banking Crimes (Prudential Banking Principle)

A President Commissioner who does not merely exercise passive supervisory functions but holds factual control, approves credit/fund expansion transactions exceeding Rp 10 Billion, and ignores written Bank Indonesia warnings concerning the placement of BLBI funds into productive assets bears criminal liability (qualified as co-perpetration / mededader) ex Article 1 paragraph (1) sub a jo. Article 28 jo. Article 34(c) of Law No. 3 of 1971 jo. Law No. 31 of 1999 jo. Article 55 paragraph (1) 1st of the Criminal Code (KUHP).

Question of Law vs Question of Fact

Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the Judex Facti (Central Jakarta District Court No. 1146/Pid.B/2001/PN.Jkt.Pst) err in applying evidentiary law and substantive law (foutieve toepassing van het recht) by acquitting the Defendant on the grounds that the BLBI matter had been resolved civilly via MRNIA/IBRA, and how is the qualification of criminal unlawfulness determined?

Supreme Court Legal Answer: Yes. Central Jakarta District Court erred in applying the law. The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Public Prosecutor at the Central Jakarta District Attorney's Office, annulled Central Jakarta District Court Decision No. 1146/Pid.B/2001/PN.Jkt.Pst dated August 1, 2002, and independently adjudicated by declaring Defendant [Defendant] guilty of committing the criminal offense of Joint and Continuous Corruption, sentencing him to 4 years of imprisonment, a Rp 20 Million fine subsidiary 4 months of confinement, and imposing Restitution amounting to Rp 169,472,986,461.54.

Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Central Jakarta District Court)

Examination of facts regarding debit balances / overdrafts of PT Bank Modern Tbk. at Bank Indonesia since October 20, 1997; receipt of Bank Indonesia Liquidity Support (Fasdis I, Fasdis II, and SBPUK) valued at Rp 1.79 Trillion; utilization of BLBI funds to purchase Promissory Notes of PT Total Central Finance USD 1.516.000 (Rp 5 Billion) and PT PLN Rp 11.9 Billion; disbursement of related-party credits to PT Jakarta Steel Perdana (Rp 9 Billion), PT Awani Modern Indonesia, PT Modern Putratama, and PT Gunung Sewu Kencana; settlement of import L/Cs for PT Masterina Keramika Pratama via Bank Hokindo USD 4,348,500; testimonies of Bank Indonesia officials (Onny Widjanarko, Fathoni Achmad, Sarwanto, Soedradjad Djiwandono, Miranda S. Goeltom, Aulia Pohan); and verification of MRNIA execution dated December 18, 1998, and asset encumbrances to IBRA.

  • Criminal Code (KUHP): Article 55 paragraph (1) 1st (Complicity) & Article 64 paragraph (1) (Continuous Act).

Legal Doctrines & Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

  • Doctrine of Criminal Law Independence from Civil Law: Civil dispute resolution or financial loss recovery subsequent to a crime does not halt criminal prosecution nor eliminate criminal culpability.
  • Supreme Court Decision No. 2477 K/Pid/1988 dated March 20, 1993: Sentencing bank commissioners/management misusing credit for personal/group interests.

Established by Evidence

  • Defendant [Defendant] is legally and convincingly proven guilty of committing the criminal offense of "Corruption committed jointly and continuously."
  • Proven to have unlawfully disbursed and utilized Bank Modern BLBI funds outside their designated purposes, resulting in state financial losses amounting to Rp 169,472,986,461.54.
  • Signing the MRNIA and surrendering assets to IBRA was proven not to erase the Defendant's criminal unlawfulness.

Annulled (Central Jakarta District Court Verdict)

  • Central Jakarta District Court considerations acquitting the Defendant on grounds that the matter was resolved civilly via IBRA were entirely annulled by the Supreme Court as erroneous application of evidentiary law and substantive corruption law.

Uncertainties and Gaps

Strict Separation Between Corporate Settlements (IBRA / PKPS) and Criminal Accountability (Corruption): This landmark ruling stands as one of the most vital legal precedent pillars in Indonesia handling the BLBI scandal: banking debt settlement policies through IBRA/PKPS schemes (MRNIA) constitute civil/administrative state law domains that cannot serve as an "immunity shield" to erase criminal corruption liability for the unlawful misappropriation of public moneys.

Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Can civil asset surrender to IBRA / MRNIA extinguish BLBI corruption crimes, and can a bank President Commissioner be penalized for BLBI fund misappropriation?
Asset surrender does not extinguish crimes, and President Commissioners remain penally liable. Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence No. 1696 K/Pid/2002:

  • Signing the MRNIA and surrendering civil assets to IBRA cannot eliminate or erase the criminal unlawfulness (wederrechtelijkheid) of corrupt BLBI fund misappropriation that caused state financial losses.
  • A President Commissioner holding approval control over transactions exceeding Rp 10 Billion and approving BLBI fund utilization outside designated purposes (for related-party credit expansion and productive assets prohibited by Bank Indonesia) bears joint criminal liability alongside the Board of Directors.
  • The Supreme Court annulled the Central Jakarta District Court acquittal and sentenced Defendant [Defendant] to 4 years imprisonment, a Rp 20 Million fine, and Restitution of Rp 169.47 Billion.
