Electronic Court

Electronic court refers to judicial services and processes supported by electronic or digital systems.

The precise scope and legal basis depend on the jurisdiction.

[HUMAN VERIFICATION REQUIRED]

Potential Components

Electronic court systems may include:

  • Electronic registration
  • Electronic filing
  • Electronic payment
  • Electronic notifications
  • Digital document management
  • Scheduling
  • Online hearings
  • Digital case tracking

Access to Justice

Electronic services may reduce barriers related to:

  • Travel
  • Time
  • Administrative procedures
  • Physical access

They may also create digital exclusion risks.

Security

Important considerations include:

  • Authentication
  • Authorization
  • Encryption
  • Integrity
  • Availability
  • Audit logs
  • Incident response

AI Integration

AI may potentially be integrated into low-risk supporting functions such as:

  • Search
  • Classification
  • Translation
  • Summarization
  • Helpdesk services

High-impact uses require additional governance.
