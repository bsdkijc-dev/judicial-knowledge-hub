Overview

Supreme Court Judicial Review (*Peninjauan Kembali / PK*) Decision No. 45 PK/Pid/Ham.Ad Hoc/2004 dated November 4, 2004, establishes fundamental jurisprudence and legal standards regarding the application of the doctrine of superior/command responsibility in gross human rights violation cases. The Supreme Court affirmed that when direct subordinates (the Regent of Liquisa and the Regent of Covalima) have been declared not legally proven to have committed gross human rights violations based on final and binding Supreme Court decisions, the legal element of "criminal responsibility for gross human rights violations committed by subordinates" pursuant to Article 42 paragraph (2) of Law No. 26 of 2000 charged against the Governor as a Superior likewise fails to be proven. Furthermore, emotional affiliation cannot serve as proof of a superior-subordinate relationship or effective control.

Case Background

The Convict / Petitioner for PK ([Defendant], Former Governor of Level I Region of East Timor) was charged with omission (*crime by omission*) regarding crimes against humanity perpetrated by his subordinates (Liquisa Regent Leonito Martins, Covalima Regent Drs. Herman Sedyono, and PPI Deputy Commander [name removed]) at the Liquisa Church Complex, Manuel Carrascalao's Residence in Dili, the Dili Diocese, Bishop Bello's Residence, and the Ave Maria Suai Church.

The Ad Hoc Human Rights Court at the Central Jakarta District Court sentenced the Convict to 3 years of imprisonment (Decision No. 01/Pid.HAM/ADHOC/2002/PN.JKT.PST). The Ad Hoc High Human Rights Court of Jakarta upheld the ruling (Decision No. 01/PID.HAM/AD.HOC/2002/PT.DKI). Upon cassation, the Supreme Court rejected the Defendant's cassation petition (Decision No. 04 K/Pid.HAM.Ad Hoc/2003). The Convict subsequently filed a petition for Judicial Review (*PK*) presenting *novum* in the form of Supreme Court Decisions No. 01 K/Pid/HAM Ad Hoc/2004 and No. 01 K/Pid.HAM/Ad Hoc/2004, which had acquitted Covalima Regent Drs. Herman Sedyono and Liquisa Regent Leonito Martins of all charges of gross human rights violations.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. There exists decisive new evidence (*novum*), namely Supreme Court Decisions No. 01 K/Pid/HAM Ad Hoc/2004 and No. 01 K/Pid.HAM/Ad Hoc/2004 possessing permanent legal force, wherein the Convict's subordinates (Covalima Regent Drs. Herman Sedyono and Liquisa Regent Leonito Martins) were declared not guilty and acquitted of all charges of gross human rights violations.
  2. As a direct juridical consequence, because the Regents alleged to have committed gross human rights violations were declared not proven guilty, the indictment element requiring "criminal responsibility for gross human rights violations committed by subordinates" under Article 42 paragraph (2) of Law No. 26 of 2000 against the Convict in his capacity as Governor (Superior) is likewise unfulfilled and unproven.
  3. Regarding [name removed] (PPI Deputy Commander), the *judex facti* erred in concluding that [name removed] was a subordinate of the Convict, given that *de jure* and *de facto* the Convict possessed no effective control or chain of command over [name removed]. Emotional ties cannot serve as a legal basis for establishing a superior-subordinate relationship.
  • Ruling: Granting the petition for judicial review from Petitioner for PK [Defendant]; Annulling the Supreme Court cassation decision dated April 1, 2004, No. 04 K/PID.HAM.AD HOC/2003 jo. Ad Hoc High Human Rights Court of Jakarta Decision No. 01/PID.HAM/AD HOC/2002/PT.DKI jo. Ad Hoc Human Rights Court at Central Jakarta District Court Decision No. 01/PID.HAM/AD HOC/2002/PH.JKT.PST; and Re-Adjudicating: (1) Declaring Convict [Defendant] not legally and convincingly proven guilty of committing the criminal offenses set forth in the First and Second Charges; (2) Acquitting the Convict of all charges; (3) Restoring the Convict's rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity; (4) Ordering that the Convict be immediately released from the Penitentiary; (5) Charging all procedural costs across all levels of jurisdiction to the State. (Decision rendered by majority vote / *Dissenting Opinions* by Supreme Court Justices Artidjo Alkostar, SH., LL.M. and Prof. DR. Sumaryo Suryokusumo, SH., LL.M.).
