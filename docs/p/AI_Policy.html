AI Policy

An AI Policy establishes institutional rules and principles for the responsible use of artificial intelligence.

Policy Components

A comprehensive policy may address:

  • Permitted uses
  • Prohibited uses
  • Confidential information
  • Personal data
  • Verification
  • Human oversight
  • Procurement
  • Security
  • Transparency
  • Training
  • Incident reporting

Use Classification

Potential categories:

Category Description
Permitted Routine low-risk applications
Controlled Applications requiring additional safeguards
Restricted High-risk applications requiring authorization
Prohibited Applications incompatible with institutional requirements

The actual classification requires institutional and legal determination.

Minimum Rule

Personnel using AI should know:

WHAT MAY BE ENTERED

WHAT MAY BE GENERATED

WHAT MUST BE VERIFIED

WHO IS ACCOUNTABLE

Policy Review

AI policies should be periodically reviewed because:

  • Technology changes
  • Risks change
  • Law changes
  • Institutional experience develops
