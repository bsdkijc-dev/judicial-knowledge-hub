Overview

Supreme Court Judicial Review (*Peninjauan Kembali / PK*) Decision No. 38 PK/Pid/2003 establishes important legal precedents regarding criminal judicial review concerning unlawful firearms/ammunition possession and premeditated murder. The Supreme Court ruled to release the Defendant (H. [Defendant]) from legal prosecution regarding the charge of evading execution in the Goro case (Charge IV), because the underlying execution ruling had previously been annulled via a prior Judicial Review, thereby negating material unlawfulness. However, regarding the offenses of firearms possession and the premeditated murder of Supreme Court Justice H.M. Syafiuddin Kartasasmita, SH (Charges I, II, and III), the Supreme Court upheld the conviction while reducing the prison sentence from 15 (fifteen) years to 10 (ten) years, taking into account principles of justice and the emotional distress experienced by the Defendant due to unfair treatment during prior legal proceedings.

Case Background

The Defendant, H. [Defendant] alias [Defendant], was indicted upon four charges: 1. Charge I (Emergency Law No. 12/1951 jo. Article 55(1) item 1 of the KUHP): Complicity in unlawfully controlling, storing, and concealing various types of firearms and ammunition at Cemara Apartment, Jalan Cemara No. 23-25, Menteng, Central Jakarta; 2. Charge II (Emergency Law No. 12/1951): Unlawfully controlling, storing, and concealing firearms, ammunition, and explosives at a residence on Jalan Alam Segar III No. 23, Pondok Indah, South Jakarta; 3. Charge III (Article 340 jo. Article 55(1) item 2 of the KUHP): Instigating (*uitlokker*) R. Moulawarman al. Molla and Noval Hadad bin Harun to commit premeditated murder against Supreme Court Justice H.M. Syafiuddin Kartasasmita, SH; 4. Charge IV (Article 216(1) of the KUHP): Intentionally disobeying orders or thwarting the actions of public officials executing statutory regulations (evading/becoming a fugitive from the execution of the Goro case criminal judgment).

The Central Jakarta District Court (Decision No. 407/Pid.B/2002/PN.JKT.PST dated July 26, 2002) sentenced the Defendant to 15 (fifteen) years of imprisonment. The Defendant subsequently filed a petition for Judicial Review directly to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. Regarding Charge IV, because the Cassation Decision No. 1 K/Pid/2000 dated September 22, 2000, in the "Goro" case forming the basis of execution was annulled by the Supreme Court via Judicial Review No. 78 PK/Pid/2000 dated October 1, 2001, the Defendant's act of evading execution no longer possesses material unlawfulness (*materiele wederrechtelijk*). Such conduct does not constitute a criminal offense; hence, the Defendant must be released from all legal prosecutions (*ontslag van alle rechtsvervolging*) specifically with respect to Charge IV.
  2. Regarding Charges I, II, and III, the Supreme Court adopted the Central Jakarta District Court *judex facti* reasoning holding that the criminal elements were legally and convincingly proven. However, the *judex facti* failed in its legal reasoning to account for principles of justice regarding the Petitioner for PK, who suffered emotional distress due to unfair treatment during the Goro case execution. Pursuant to Article 263 paragraph (2) of the KUHAP and considerations of equity, the imposed sentence is modified and reduced to 10 (ten) years of imprisonment.
  • Ruling: Granting the petition for judicial review from Petitioner for PK H. [Defendant] alias [Defendant]; Annulling the Central Jakarta District Court Decision No. 407/Pid.B/2002/PN.JKT.PST dated July 26, 2002; and Independently Re-Adjudicating: (1) Declaring that the Defendant is proven to have committed the acts under Charge IV, but that such acts do not constitute a criminal offense; (2) Releasing the Defendant from legal prosecution regarding Charge IV, and restoring the Defendant's rights in capacity, position, honor, and dignity regarding Charge IV; (3) Declaring Defendant H. [Defendant] alias [Defendant] legally and convincingly proven guilty of committing the crimes of: I. Complicity in unlawfully controlling, storing, and concealing firearms and explosives; II. Unlawfully controlling, storing, and concealing firearms and explosives; III. Instigating (*uitlokker*) the commission of premeditated murder; (4) Sentencing the Defendant to imprisonment for a term of 10 (ten) years, with full credit for the period of detention and imprisonment already served; (5) Charging judicial review court costs amounting to Rp 2,500.
