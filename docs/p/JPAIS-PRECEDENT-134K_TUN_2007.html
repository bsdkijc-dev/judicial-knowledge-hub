Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 134 K/TUN/2007 dated June 19, 2007, establishes fundamental State Administrative procedural jurisprudence regarding the calculation of the time limit for filing a lawsuit:

  • Calculation of State Administrative Lawsuit Time Limit (Article 55 of Law No. 5 of 1986 jo. Law No. 9 of 2004): For parties whose names are directly listed/addressed in a State Administrative Decree, the 90 (ninety) day time limit to file a lawsuit is calculated from the date the said Decree is physically received by the concerned party, rather than from the date it was merely known.
  • Negligence in Delivering Decrees via Postal Mail (*Re-tour*): If a decree sent via postal mail by a State Administrative Official is returned (*re-tour*) to the sender and is never physically received by the Plaintiff, such negligence or failure in delivery cannot be imposed as a prejudicial burden upon the Plaintiff. The time limit calculation only commences once the Plaintiff physically receives the decree (such as during the preliminary examination at the State Administrative Court).
  • Cancellation of Forest Management Principle Permits: The unilateral cancellation or withdrawal of an oil palm plantation management principle permit within a forest area by the Minister of Forestry, despite the community/cooperative having fulfilled all requirements and compensation obligations, violates the Principles of Good Governance (*AAUPB*), specifically the Principle of Legal Certainty and the Principle of Thoroughness (*Asas Kecermatan*).

Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Petitioner for Cassation (Bukit Harapan Oil Palm Plantation Cooperative / KPKS Bukit Harapan, represented by Chairman Ir. Djonggi Sitorus) cooperated with PT Torganda (Darianus Lungguk Sitorus / D.L. Sitorus) to establish an oil palm plantation on customary lands of Luhat Simangambat and Luhat Ujung Batu within the Register 40 Padang Lawas area, South Tapanuli, North Sumatra. The Original Defendant / Respondent for Cassation (Minister of Forestry of the Republic of Indonesia) issued Letter No. 1680/Menhut-III/2002 dated September 26, 2002, concerning the Principle Permit for Oil Palm Plantation Management covering an area of 23,000 hectares, subject to compensation payments for standing timber amounting to Rp 21,852,760,000 and IUPHHBK provisions. KPKS Bukit Harapan fulfilled these obligations through Statement of Undertaking Deed No. 145 dated April 25, 2003.

However, the Minister of Forestry unilaterally issued Letter No. S.419/Menhut-II/2004 dated October 13, 2004, withdrawing the principle permit and ordering the cessation of all activities. The decree was sent via postal mail but never arrived and was returned (*re-tour*) to the Ministry of Forestry. The Plaintiff only received the physical decree during the preliminary examination at the Jakarta State Administrative Court on February 9, 2006.

The Jakarta State Administrative Court (Decision No. 12/G/2006/PTUN-JKT dated July 12, 2006) granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit and annulled the Minister of Forestry's decree. The Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 151/B/2006/PT.TUN.JKT dated October 12, 2006) annulled the State Administrative Court's judgment and declared the lawsuit inadmissible on the grounds that it exceeded the 90-day time limit. KPKS Bukit Harapan subsequently filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The Jakarta State Administrative High Court erred in applying the law regarding the determination of the lawsuit filing time limit pursuant to Article 55 of Law No. 5 of 1986 jo. Law No. 9 of 2004. The calculation of the lawsuit filing time limit for a party addressed in a State Administrative Decree must be computed from the time the decree is physically received (*ontvangan*).
  2. Trial facts proved that the decree sent via postal mail by the Defendant to the Plaintiff was returned (*re-tour*) to the sender and was never received by the Plaintiff. The Plaintiff only received the physical decree *in litis* during the preliminary examination at the Jakarta State Administrative Court on February 9, 2006. The negligence of the Defendant as a State Administrative Official cannot be shifted as a prejudicial burden upon the Plaintiff as a seeker of justice.
  3. The Supreme Court concurs with the legal reasoning of the Jakarta State Administrative Court, which annulled the Minister of Forestry's Decree No. S.419/Menhut-II/2004 because the unilateral revocation of the principle permit without valid legal grounds violated the Principles of Good Governance (*AAUPB*).
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation BUKIT HARAPAN OIL PALM PLANTATION COOPERATIVE (*KOPERASI PERKEBUNAN KELAPA SAWIT BUKIT HARAPAN*); Annulling the Jakarta State Administrative High Court decision dated October 12, 2006, No. 151/B/2006/PT.TUN.JKT, which had annulled the Jakarta State Administrative Court decision dated July 12, 2006, No. 12/G/2006/PTUN.JKT; and Independently Adjudicating: IN EXCEPTION: Declaring the Defendant's exceptions inadmissible in their entirety; IN THE PRINCIPAL CASE: (1) Granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit; (2) Declaring null and void the Letter of the Minister of Forestry No. S.419/Menhut-II/2004 dated October 13, 2004, regarding: Application to Manage Plantations within Forest Area Register 40 Padang Lawas, North Sumatra Province; (3) Ordering the Defendant to revoke the Letter of the Minister of Forestry No. S.419/Menhut-II/2004 dated October 13, 2004; (4) Ordering the Respondent for Cassation / Defendant to pay court costs across all levels of jurisdiction, assessed at this cassation level at Rp 500,000 (five hundred thousand rupiahs). *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Cassation Panel of Justices on Tuesday, June 19, 2007, by Titi Nurmala Siagian, SH., MH. [Presiding Judge], Marina Sidabutar, SH., MH., and H. Imam Soebechi, SH., MH.).*
