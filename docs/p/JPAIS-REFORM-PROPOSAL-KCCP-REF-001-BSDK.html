Integration with Judicial Education & Training (BSDK Kumdil) / Integrasi dengan Pendidikan & Pelatihan Yudisial

The comparative judicial training research highlights how adapting Japanese judicial education practices at the Legal Training and Research Institute (Shihō Kenshūjo) provides the pedagogical foundation required to operationalize KCCP-REF-001:

  • Co-Mediation Simulation & Panel Leadership: Deploying 3-member Tripartite Panels requires shifting away from solo, purely facilitative mediation toward collaborative evaluative co-mediation. BSDK's In-Service Specialization Chamber conducts 20-hour clinical co-mediation workshops training judge mediators and accredited non-judge expert commissioners.
  • Claim Deconstruction via the DFKUM Doctrine: Adapting Japan's Requirements-Facts Doctrine (Yōken Jijitsu) into the Substantive Elements and Causal Facts Doctrine (Doktrin Fakta Kausal dan Unsur Materiil / DFKUM). Training enables panel members to analyze pre-trial evidence disclosures and draft precise Orders in Lieu of Settlement (Penetapan Pengganti Perdamaian).
  • Continuous Roster Governance & Certification: Tiered career accreditation under the Competency-Based Judicial Training Model (J-CBTM) with digital roster management integrated into SIPP.

Implementation Mapping across Training Tiers / Pemetaan Implementasi Lintas Tingkat Diklat

  • Pre-Service Induction (Pre-Service Apprenticeship Chamber): Doctrinal element mapping (DFKUM), preliminary issue clarification, and court-connected mediation procedures (Exit Credential: Basic Judicial Qualification).
  • In-Service Specialization (In-Service Specialization Chamber): Co-mediation dynamics, financial/land dispute deconstruction, and drafting Penetapan Pengganti Perdamaian (Exit Credential: Certified Court Conciliator / Expert Commissioner Status).
  • Court Leadership & Supervision (Leadership & Harmonization Chamber): Monitoring SIPP 14-day objection timers, auditing Substantive Settlement Rates (SSR), and evaluating panel performance (Exit Credential: Court Leadership Eligibility).
