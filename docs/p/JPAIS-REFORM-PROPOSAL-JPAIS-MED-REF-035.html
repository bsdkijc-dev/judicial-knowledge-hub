Description / Deskripsi

This page records the empirical analysis of official judicial statistics and the Problem Inventory List (Daftar Inventaris Masalah / DIM) for business competition objection cases (Komisi Pengawas Persaingan Usaha / KPPU) at the Semarang Commercial Court at the Semarang District Court, covering the reporting period from 2021 through 2026. The dataset presents a notable structural dynamic: while recording 0 (zero) incoming objection cases across the entire 6-year period, the court maintained a significant roster of certified competition law judges—peaking at 14 judges in 2021, readjusting to 5 judges between 2023 and 2025, and expanding to 8 certified judges in 2026. Furthermore, the accompanying DIM highlights critical technical-juridical bottlenecks in competition enforcement, including the lack of direct execution power, evidentiary barriers regarding digital data and indirect evidence, and the strict evidence lock under PERMA No. 3/2021. All structural entities and numerical data have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher Cecep Mustafa.

Problem / Masalah

  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-035: Resource Readjustment & Human Capital Imbalance, where maintaining a high allocation of certified judges (14 judges in 2021, stabilizing at 5–8 judges through 2026) alongside zero registered objection filings over six consecutive years reflects a structural misalignment in judicial resource allocation between regional Commercial Courts.
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-036: Evidentiary & Structural Friction, where the comprehensive DIM submitted by the court emphasizes severe systemic gaps—including KPPU's lack of direct execution power (*irah-irah*), absence of subpoena powers during investigation, and the rigid prohibition against submitting new evidence (*novum*) at the Commercial Court level.

Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

  • JPAIS-MED-ID-035: The Semarang Commercial Court maintains formal institutional readiness by ensuring a roster of 5 to 8 certified competition judges annually, processing objections against KPPU decisions strictly on the administrative record (*dossier*) within 14 days under PERMA No. 3/2021 without a mediation phase, while fine execution remains reliant on civil execution procedures through District Courts.

Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

  • JPAIS-MED-JP-035: In Japan (Tokyo High Court / JFTC), judicial review of Japan Fair Trade Commission decisions is concentrated in specialized panels at the Tokyo High Court, utilizing economic experts and a flexible evidentiary standard (*rule of reason* evaluation) rather than rigid civil evidence locks.

Gap / Kesenjangan

  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-035: The primary gap lies in the rigid application of the evidence lock (*Pasal 13 PERMA No. 3/2021*) and the 14-day statutory limit, which severely restricts business actors from submitting comprehensive economic counter-analyses when appealing KPPU decisions.

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Enable certified judges stationed at low-caseload courts like Semarang to participate in virtual judicial panels (*e-Court*) supporting high-caseload centers like Central Jakarta, and modify PERMA No. 3/2021 to allow limited new documentary and expert economic evidence when KPPU decisions introduce novel market definitions or penalty calculations.

Action / Aksi

  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-035: Issue a Supreme Court Circular (SEMA) standardizing the definition of working days for objection filings and establishing guidelines for evaluating indirect economic evidence.
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-036: Amend PERMA No. 3/2021 to incorporate specialized economic evidence procedures (*Hot Tubbing* expert testimony and confidential *Data Room* mechanisms).

Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

  • JPAIS-MED-PILOT-035: Semarang Commercial Court (Central Java Judicial Region) integration into the national virtual judicial pool via *e-Court*.

KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

  • JPAIS-MED-KPI-035: 100% integration of Semarang's certified competition judges into the national virtual judicial pool via *e-Court*; dispute resolution duration $\le 60$ working days per case upon docket entry.

Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Amending Law No. 5/1999 to clarify statutory phrases ("dapat mengakibatkan") and grant explicit direct execution mechanisms for KPPU administrative fines.

Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Establishing clear procedural rules for digital evidence and algorithmic market manipulation under updated Supreme Court regulations, alongside relaxing the evidence lock in PERMA No. 3/2021.

Evidence / Bukti Empiris

  • Official caseload and DIM report document from Semarang Commercial Court (*data statistik KPPU_Pn_Niaga_Semarang.pdf*) covering the reporting period 2021–2026.
