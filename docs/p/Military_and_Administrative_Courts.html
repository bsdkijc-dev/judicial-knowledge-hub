Military and Administrative Courts Case Resolution

The Renstra establishes a program outcome for certain and equitable case resolution within the Military Courts and Administrative Courts.

Example Indicators

  • timely appellate case resolution in Military Courts;
  • timely first-instance case resolution in Military Courts.
