Description / Deskripsi

This page records the executive leadership policy brief dated August 2026 entitled *"Policy Brief untuk Pimpinan: Reformasi Hukum Acara dan Kelembagaan Pengadilan Niaga dalam Penanganan Keberatan Putusan Komisi Pengawas Persaingan Usaha"*. Addressed to the Leadership of the Supreme Court of RI, KPPU, and the Legislature (DPR RI/Government), the document evaluates five years of absolute jurisdictional transfer of KPPU objections from District Courts to Commercial Courts pursuant to Article 118 of Law No. 11/2020 on Job Creation and Law No. 6/2023. It identifies five critical operational bottlenecks: (1) public vs. private law incompatibility; (2) trial duration contradiction (3–12 months); (3) evidentiary restrictions (*evidence lock*); (4) geographical access disparities; and (5) limitations in fine execution and asset tracing. To establish legal certainty and a healthy investment climate, the policy brief formulates a Three Horizons Action Plan (2026–2028+). All structural entities, legal analyses, and strategic frameworks have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher Cecep Mustafa.

Problem / Masalah

  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-057: Public-Private Procedural Disorientation, where treating KPPU as a civil defendant (*Termohon Keberatan*) under HIR/RBg forces commercial judges to evaluate public administrative rulings using strict civil standard of proof (Article 164 HIR), creating profound friction with public/criminal evidentiary rules under Article 42 of Law No. 5/1999.
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-058: Evidence Lock Paradox & Execution Impasse, where PERMA No. 3/2021 restricts new documentary evidence and turns commercial courts into mere dossier-checking bodies (*judex juris*), violating due process rights when KPPU introduces novel market definitions in final rulings, compounded by an inability to execute PNBP fines due to a lack of banking asset-tracing instruments.

Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

  • JPAIS-MED-ID-047: Commercial courts examine objections without pre-trial mediation under PERMA No. 3/2021, restricting review strictly to administrative dossiers, while PNBP fine collection relies on conventional civil execution procedures (Article 196 HIR) that lack direct access to OJK or banking data.

Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

  • JPAIS-MED-JP-047: Comparative analysis reveals advanced international models: the United States FTC applies the **Judicial Remand Doctrine** to return incomplete dossiers; Australia's ACCC utilizes **Hot-Tubbing (Concurrent Expert Evidence)** for econometric testing; and Japan/Germany concentrate review in specialized higher courts with expert economic judges.

Gap / Kesenjangan

  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-047: The primary structural gap is the absence of a dedicated *sui generis* Commercial Court Procedural Law Act separate from HIR/RBg, alongside regulatory gaps regarding evidence lock relaxation, end-to-end e-Court integration, and delegated PNBP fine execution.

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Execute a Three Horizons Action Plan: Horizon 1 (Short-Term 2026 — revise PERMA No. 3/2021 to relax evidence locks, establish a strict 60-working-day cap, and mandate end-to-end e-Court); Horizon 2 (Medium-Term 2027 — draft a Commercial Court Procedural Law Bill / Comprehensive PERMA, delegate PNBP fine execution to local district courts, and establish asset-tracing cooperation with OJK/banks); and Horizon 3 (Long-Term 2028+ — amend Law No. 5/1999, appoint specialized competition ad hoc judges, and expand Commercial Courts to strategic regional hubs).

Action / Aksi

  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-048: Puslitbang Kumdil & Civil Chamber MA RI draft Academic Papers and PERMA revision texts during Year I (2026).
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-049: Supreme Court Registrar & KPPU integrate end-to-end e-Court systems and formulate PNBP Fine Execution Guidelines during Year II (2027).

Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

  • JPAIS-MED-PILOT-047: Implementation across all 5 Commercial Courts (Central Jakarta, Medan, Semarang, Surabaya, Makassar) under the Three Horizons Action Plan.

KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

  • JPAIS-MED-KPI-047: Year I (2026): Promulgation of updated PERMA; Year II (2027): 100% objection cases filed and examined via e-Court with average disposal duration $\le 60$ working days; Year III (2028+): Inclusion of Commercial Court Procedural Law Bill in the DPR RI Priority National Legislation Program (Prolegnas).

Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Fundamental revision of technical adjudication guidelines for KPPU objections in the Supreme Court, strengthening inter-agency coordination between Mahkamah Agung, KPPU, Ministry of Finance (DJKN), OJK, and Ministry of Law and Human Rights.

Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Providing a clear legislative roadmap to resolve substantive and procedural conflicts arising from the absolute jurisdictional transfer under the Job Creation Law.

Evidence / Bukti Empiris
