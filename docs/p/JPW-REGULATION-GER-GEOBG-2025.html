JPW-REGULATION-GER-GEOBG-2025

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 962, 2026-09-15 · wiki source · Markdown

The Geothermal Energy Acceleration Act (German: Geothermie-Beschleunigungsgesetz – GeoBG) is a draft legislative proposal approved by the German Federal Cabinet on August 6, 2025, aimed at accelerating the transition toward a climate-neutral heat supply by streamlining approval processes for geothermal energy, heat pumps, and thermal storage GeoBG.

Overview

The legislation aims to eliminate regulatory and bureaucratic bottlenecks in geothermal energy deployment across Germany Heat Transition. GeoBG establishes statutory maximum approval deadlines, digitalizes permit processing, and legally categorizes geothermal infrastructure as an overriding public interest Public Interest.

Legal Basis

  • Geothermie-Beschleunigungsgesetz (GeoBG): Drafted and submitted by the German Federal Cabinet on August 6, 2025 German Cabinet.
  • EU Renewable Energy Directive (RED III): Partially transposed into national German law via GeoBG RED III.
  • Statutory Amendments: Amends the Water Resources Act (WHG), Federal Mining Act (BBergG), Environmental Impact Assessment Act (UVPG), and Code of Administrative Court Procedure (VwGO) German Mining Law.

Background

Geothermal and district heating projects in Germany previously experienced long development delays due to complex administrative procedures under mining and water regulations Bureaucratic Bottlenecks. GeoBG was created to overcome these obstacles and hit national climate neutrality targets.

Key Objectives

  1. Accelerate planning and permitting procedures for geothermal energy and heat grid infrastructure Permit Acceleration.
  2. Establish legally binding maximum deadlines for mining and water authority approvals Legal Timelines.
  3. Facilitate subsurface exploration and streamlined access to seismic data Seismic Exploration.
  4. Encourage recovery from heat sources such as river water, wastewater, and industrial waste heat Industrial Waste Heat.

Core Provisions

  • Overriding Public Interest: Legally grants geothermal developments prioritized status in administrative land-use balancing decisions Public Interest.
  • Landowner Tolerance Obligations: Requires landowners to allow subsurface seismic testing, subject to property restoration and compensation for direct financial loss Land Use.
  • Heat Pipelines: Aligns planning procedures for heat transfer pipelines with established fast-track rules for natural gas and hydrogen pipelines Heat Pipelines.
  • Year-Round Exploration: Eases exploration permits in protected areas under strict conditions to prevent seasonal delays Exploration Permits.

Implementation & Timeline

  • Cabinet Approval: Adopted as part of a comprehensive federal energy package on August 6, 2025 Energy Package.
  • Legal Analysis: Eversheds Sutherland published its legal analysis briefing on September 2, 2025 Eversheds Sutherland.
