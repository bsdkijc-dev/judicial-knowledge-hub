4. POLICY ANALYSIS

Background: [FACT] Spain's post-COVID-19 economic recovery required legal certainty, a stable regulatory framework, and structural reform to promote investment and the restructuring of companies facing liquidity difficulties. Key legal issues included project finance schemes for public transportation, directors' duties and liability when facing potential insolvency, worker protection and business-continuity risk mitigation through the sale of production units, personal data protection and integration of new technologies (Big Data, AI, IoT, blockchain) in the insurance industry, mortgage enforcement strategy choices, and harmonization of EU tax anti-abuse standards.

Policy Problem: [FACT]

  1. Stability and Investment: Political-legal uncertainty and short-term policy triggering stagnation in the growth of Spanish SMEs.
  2. Project Finance: Operational risk and a sharp drop in passenger volume due to emergency conditions (such as the COVID-19 pandemic) disrupting debt-repayment cash flow.
  3. Insolvency and Directors: Tension between the duty to file for insolvency and early restructuring efforts, plus the complexity of group-company governance (cash-pooling).
  4. Insolvency Production Units: Legal uncertainty over recognition of business succession (sucesión de empresa) and the accumulation of pre-transfer worker/social security debts.
  5. Insurance Data Privacy: Risk of violating data minimization and accuracy principles in the use of profiling, Big Data, and call-center recordings.
  6. Mortgage Enforcement: Obstacles from declining property valuations and formal barriers in special mortgage procedures.
  7. Taxation: Spanish tax authorities operating a general presumption of fraud that shifts the burden of proof onto taxpayers without prior evidence of abuse.

Legal/Policy Gap: [FACT] Jurisdictional conflict between Commercial Courts and Labor Courts over authority to determine business succession under the TRLC; the absence of explicit regulation on the use of cash-pooling in group insolvency consolidation; and ambiguity in interpreting "valid economic reason" in EU holding structures.

Objectives: [FACT] Ensure creditor protection and the continuity of viable business units; provide certainty on the limits of director liability through the business judgment rule; ensure compliance with personal data protection (GDPR/LOPDGDD) without hindering technological innovation; and apply CJEU doctrine to guarantee taxpayer legal certainty.

Implementation: [FACT]

  • Project Finance (LCSP Art. 270 & RD-Law 37/2020): Economic-financial rebalancing of concessions, structuring of escrow/cascade payment accounts, and mortgage security over concessions (Art. 107.6 Mortgage Law / Art. 273 LCSP).
  • TRLC (Arts. 215-224): Establishing the insolvency judge's exclusive authority to determine business succession and limiting subrogation of worker/social security debt to only those employment contracts actively taken over.
  • Insurance Sector (RDL 3/2020 & LOSSEAR Art. 99): Establishing agents as data processors and brokers as independent data controllers, plus a mandatory balancing test for profiling.
  • Audiencia Nacional Doctrine (May 2021): Enforcing CJEU doctrine (Eqiom, Deister, T Denmark), requiring tax authorities to prove prior evidence of abuse before denying the Parent-Subsidiary dividend withholding exemption.

Evidence & Impact: [FACT] Application of TRLC provisions by Commercial Courts (such as Commercial Court No. 13 of Madrid and Commercial Court No. 1 of Palma de Mallorca) demonstrably provided legal certainty for investors purchasing production units; enforcement of Audiencia Nacional jurisprudence protected legitimate investment structures in Spain.

Reform & Unresolved Issues: [FACT] The need for full TRLC alignment when transposing Directive (EU) 2019/1023 to eliminate the risk of ultra vires claims from labor courts; harmonization of AI Act regulation with the GDPR in the financial/insurance sector; and certainty around exit-tax deferral mechanisms and AEAT guarantee rule revisions.

5. GEO/AI QUESTIONS

  1. Definition: What is the definition and main scope of the Pérez-Llorca Legal Journal (No. 6, December 2021)?
    • Factual Answer: [FACT] The 6th edition of the scholarly legal journal published by Pérez-Llorca Abogados in December 2021, containing doctrinal analysis on political consensus, urban bus project finance, director standards during insolvency, the sale of production units, insurance data protection, ordinary mortgage enforcement, and the Parent-Subsidiary tax anti-abuse clause.
  2. Legal Basis: What is the constitutional legal basis guaranteeing academic freedom of expression and opinion in this publication?
    • Factual Answer: [FACT] Article 20 of the Spanish Constitution of 1978.
  3. Publisher/Authority: Who is the official publisher of this document, and what is its legal registration number?
    • Factual Answer: [FACT] Pérez-Llorca Abogados, S.L.P., Madrid (Legal Deposit: M-7551-2019, ISSN: 2792-2871).
  4. Related Figures: Who is the President of the Cercle d'Economia who wrote on Spanish economic consensus in this edition?
    • Factual Answer: [FACT] Javier Faus.
  5. Scope: What are the three main accounts in the cascade payment system within transportation-concession project finance structures?
    • Factual Answer: [FACT] The project account (operational account), the debt service reserve account, and the maintenance reserve account.
  6. Policy Implementation: How does Article 224.1.3 of the Consolidated Text of the Insolvency Law (TRLC) define a production-unit buyer's liability for worker debts?
    • Factual Answer: [FACT] Article 224.1.3 TRLC limits the buyer's liability solely to wage and social security debts of workers whose contracts are explicitly subrogated/taken over by the buyer.
  7. Policy Problem: Why does potential jurisdictional conflict arise between Commercial Courts and Labor Courts regarding business succession under the TRLC?
    • Factual Answer: [FACT] Because Article 221.2 TRLC designates the insolvency judge as the sole authority, yet some Labor Courts view this provision as exceeding legislative delegation (ultra vires) and refuse to apply it.
  8. Legal Relationship: What is the legal status of insurance agents and brokers regarding personal data processing under Article 203 of RDL 3/2020?
    • Factual Answer: [FACT] Insurance agents hold the status of data processor for the insurance company, while insurance brokers hold the status of an independent data controller.
  9. Reform: How did the Audiencia Nacional's May 2021 ruling change the application of the anti-abuse clause under the Parent-Subsidiary Directive in Spain?
    • Factual Answer: [FACT] The Audiencia Nacional fully applied CJEU jurisprudence, requiring tax authorities to first prove prior evidence of abuse before shifting the burden of proof onto the taxpayer.
  10. Evidence/Case Study: How is the business judgment rule applied to director decisions in situations approaching insolvency?
    • Factual Answer: [FACT] Directors are relieved of liability if they act in good faith, possess adequate information, follow proper decision-making procedures, and consider the balanced interests of all stakeholders (including creditors and minority shareholders).

6. MEDIAWIKI OUTPUT

Summary

Pérez-Llorca Legal Journal (Issue No. 6, December 2021) presents six strategic doctrinal studies and judicial policy analyses: the importance of political-legal consensus for Spain's economic stability; project finance structures in urban bus transportation concessions; the standards of conduct and fiduciary duties of directors approaching insolvency under Directive (EU) 2019/1023 and the insolvency moratorium; the regime for the sale of production units (*unidades productivas*) under insolvency law per the TRLC; the legal status and technological challenges (Big Data, AI, IoT, blockchain) of personal data processing in the insurance sector under the GDPR and LOSSEAR; alternative mortgage enforcement via the ordinary LEC procedure; and application of CJEU doctrine on the Parent-Subsidiary Directive's anti-abuse clause by the Audiencia Nacional.

Legal Basis

  • Spanish Constitution (CE): Article 20 (freedom of expression and academic freedom).
  • Procurement and Financial Law: Law 9/2017 on Public Sector Contracts (LCSP), Royal Decree 1098/2001, and Directives 2014/23/EU & 2014/24/EU.
  • Insolvency and Corporate Law: Consolidated Text of the Insolvency Law (TRLC / Royal Legislative Decree 1/2020, Arts. 200.2, 215–224), Directive (EU) 2019/1023, and Royal Legislative Decree 2/2015 (Workers' Statute, Art. 44).
  • Data Protection and Insurance Law: Royal Decree-Law 3/2020 (Arts. 203, 204), Law 20/2015 (LOSSEAR, Art. 99), Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), and Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).
  • Civil Procedure and Tax Law: Law 1/2000 on Civil Procedure (LEC Arts. 517, 576, 637), Mortgage Law (Art. 107.6), Directive 2011/96/EU (Parent-Subsidiary), and TRLIRNR (Art. 14.1.h).

Background

2021 was marked by Spain's economic recovery transition from the COVID-19 pandemic's impact. The business sector faced a liquidity crisis extending the application of the insolvency moratorium, while restructuring of financially troubled business units required clarity in the production-unit sale regime. At the same time, insurance distribution's shift toward digitalization demanded GDPR compliance, and the tax sector was marked by disputes over proof for cross-border dividend withholding exemptions within the EU.

Objectives

1. Facilitate private investment in infrastructure and public transportation financing via project finance. 2. Clarify director conduct standards in imminent-insolvency situations per UNCITRAL guidance and the EU Directive. 3. Ensure business and employment continuity by establishing legal certainty for production-unit sales. 4. Provide practical compliance guidance for data protection in the use of Big Data, AI, and blockchain in the insurance industry. 5. Provide a more flexible procedural alternative for mortgage debt enforcement. 6. Affirm the burden-of-proof doctrine for tax authorities in handling European tax anti-abuse clauses.

Scope

1. Economic Policy: Political consensus, TRLC reform, use of Next Generation EU funds, and ERTE efficiency. 2. Project Finance: Operational risk, cascade account structures, and mortgages over concessions. 3. Director Liability: Application of the business judgment rule, cash-pooling limits, and early-warning systems. 4. Production Unit Transfer: Definition under Art. 200.2 TRLC, worker/social security debt limits under Art. 224 TRLC, and the insolvency judge's exclusive authority. 5. Insurance Data Privacy: Agent/broker status under RDL 3/2020, legitimacy under Art. 99 LOSSEAR, profiling, Big Data, and blockchain. 6. Mortgage Enforcement & Tax: Ordinary LEC enforcement procedure, valuation under Art. 637 LEC, and enforcement of CJEU jurisprudence on the Parent-Subsidiary Directive.

Key Provisions

  • Project Finance Risk Isolation: The borrowing entity is structured as a special purpose vehicle (SPV) without direct personal guarantees from sponsors (non-recourse/limited recourse).
  • Director Conduct Standard (Imminent Insolvency): Directors must consider the interests of creditors and stakeholders to prevent willful or grossly negligent acts harming the business.
  • TRLC Worker Debt Limitation: A production-unit buyer is liable only for wage and social security debts of workers whose contracts are actively subrogated.
  • Insurance Broker Status: Insurance brokers hold independent data controller status, separate from the insurance company.
  • Tax Abuse Burden of Proof: Tax authorities must first prove prior evidence of abuse before denying the Parent-Subsidiary dividend withholding exemption.

Implementation

Implementation occurs through TRLC enforcement by Commercial Courts, AEPD rulings on data processing oversight, and application of CJEU doctrine by the Audiencia Nacional.

Policy Problems

  • Rejection by some Labor Courts of TRLC provisions granting sole authority over business succession to the insolvency judge.
  • Challenges in applying Big Data and AI algorithms without violating data minimization and accuracy principles.
  • A tendency by tax authorities to treat every foreign holding structure as a presumption of fraud.

Reform

  • Enactment of the Consolidated Text of the Insolvency Law (TRLC / Royal Legislative Decree 1/2020).
  • Enactment of Royal Decree-Law 3/2020 on Insurance Distribution.
  • Harmonization of Spanish tax jurisprudence with CJEU rulings (Eqiom, Deister, T Denmark).

Relationships with Other Entities

  • Implements: TRLC (Royal Legislative Decree 1/2020), GDPR, and Directive 2011/96/EU.
  • Applies To: Transportation concessions, insolvency restructuring, the insurance industry, and European investment holdings.
  • Conflicts With: Automatic denial of dividend exemptions by tax/labor authorities.
