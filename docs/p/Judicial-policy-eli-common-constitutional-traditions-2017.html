The ELI Research Framework on Common Constitutional Traditions in Europe (Kerangka Penelitian Tradisi Konstitusional Bersama di Eropa) is a transnational scientific research framework conducted under the auspices of the European Law Institute (ELI) to analyze and map common constitutional traditions across EU Member States as a foundational source of general principles of EU law pursuant to Article 6(3) TEU 1.

Summary

Authored by Giacinto della Cananea, this report outlines the progress of a transnational research project proposed by Sabino Cassese and Mario Comba within the European Law Institute (ELI) 2. The initiative brings together European legal scholars to examine common constitutional traditions not only through statutory legal texts, but also through practical implementation and underlying legal cultures 3.

Legal Basis

This framework is grounded in primary European legal instruments:

  • Article 6(3) TEU: Establishes that fundamental rights resulting from the common constitutional traditions of Member States constitute general principles of EU law 4.
  • European Convention on Human Rights (ECHR): Serves as the primary reference standard for human rights protection across Europe 5.

Background

This project marked a major institutional shift for ELI, expanding its research scope beyond private law into transnational public and constitutional law 6. Participating scholars include Piotr Bogdanowicz, Iain Cameron, Riccardo De Caria, Jörg Fedtke, Michele Graziadei, Francis Jacobs, Jeffrey Jowell, Andras Sajo, Guy Scoffoni, Takis Tridimas, Daria De Pretis, and Jacques Ziller 7.

Objectives

  • Define the scope and core elements of common constitutional traditions using case law and legal doctrine 8.
  • Test common tradition hypotheses through targeted domains, notably freedom of expression and judicial independence 9.
  • Provide conceptual clarity regarding national identities within European legal integration 10.

Scope

The research encompasses transnational EU constitutional law, comparative methodology, fundamental human rights guarantees, and judicial dialogue between the Court of Justice of the European Union (CJEU) and domestic courts 11.

Research Streams & Deliverables

  • General Concepts Project: Baseline conceptual research published in *Rivista trimestrale di diritto pubblico* (n. 4/2017) and IJPL 12.
  • Freedom of Expression Report: Official ELI report published on the ELI website, accompanied by national reports in IJPL 13.
  • Judicial Independence Stream: Studies on the rule of law and judicial autonomy in Europe published in IJPL (notably issue n. 2/2020) 14.

Implementation

Operationalized through judicial references made by domestic courts to the CJEU under the preliminary reference procedure 15.

Policy Issues

  • Comparative Methodology: Developing comparative legal methods specifically tailored to the European legal area 16.
  • Harmonization vs. National Identity: Balancing EU legal integration with constitutional respect for Member State national identities 17.
