Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 2563 K/Pdt/1988 (deliberated on March 1, 1990 / pronounced on March 15, 1990) is a fundamental Central Javanese customary inheritance law and marital property law jurisprudence precedent establishing vital rules concerning the inheritance rights of children from a first marriage over their father's share of property acquired during a third marriage (gono-gini of the third marriage).

Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established three primary legal principles:

  1. Cross-Marriage Inheritance Rights Over a Father's Gono-Gini Property: A child from a first marriage (Plaintiff: Ny. [Party C]) is legally entitled to inherit their father's share (the late H.R. Moh. Idris bin Alimarja) acquired during his third marriage, alongside the child from the third marriage (Defendant: Ny. Rr. Suratmi).
  2. Proportional Distribution of Third Marriage Joint Property (Gono-Gini): Property acquired during a third marriage constitutes joint marital property (gono-gini) between the father and the third wife, such that one-half (½) constitutes the deceased father's estate. This father's share is inherited equally by his two biological children (each receiving ¼ of the entire disputed estate / half of the half).
  3. Final Inheritance Distribution Ratio: Because the remaining ½ gono-gini share constitutes the absolute right of the third wife inherited entirely by her own biological child (Defendant), the final inheritance distribution ratio over the entire disputed object between the Plaintiff (child of the 1st wife) and the Defendant (child of the 3rd wife) is 1 to 3 (¼ share for the Plaintiff and ¾ share for the Defendant).

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Petitioner for Cassation (Ny. [Party C] alias Ny. M. Sadali), annulled Semarang High Court Decision No. 161/Pdt/1988/PT.Smg dated June 8, 1988, jo. Kebumen District Court Decision No. 42/Pdt.G/1986/PN.Kbm dated October 8, 1987, and independently adjudicated (mengadili sendiri) by declaring the disputed land and building as the estate of the late H.R. Moh. Idris subject to inheritance division at a 1 : 3 ratio, while ordering the Defendant to surrender ¼ of the disputed estate and pay damages of Rp 400,000 to the Plaintiff.

Legal / Procedural Issues

1. Legal Standing of Children from Preceding Marriages Over a Father's Estate Acquired in Subsequent Marriages (Central Javanese Customary Inheritance Law)

Is a biological child from a first marriage barred (excluded) or stripped of inheritance rights over their father's share originating from thegono-giniproperty of the father's third marriage?

Supreme Court Rule: Not Barred (Fully Entitled).

Pursuant to Central Javanese Customary Law, when a man enters into multiple marriages (polygamy/successive marriages), gono-gini property acquired within each respective marriage is separate. However, the one-half (½) share belonging to the late father from the third marriage's gono-gini constitutes estate property (karangwidda / inheritance) that must devolve upon ALL of his biological children across all marriages.

2. Cross-Marriage Inheritance Distribution Formula (Half of the Half / 1 : 3 Ratio)

What is the mathematical distribution formula over a single third-marriagegono-giniasset when a deceased father leaves 2 children (1 child from the 1st wife and 1 child from the 3rd wife)?

Supreme Court Rule:

  • The disputed gono-gini asset is split into two equal halves (½ father's share : ½ third wife's share).
  • The father's ½ share is divided equally between his 2 biological children: Plaintiff receives ½ × ½ = ¼ share (25%), and Defendant receives ½ × ½ = ¼ share (25%).
  • The third wife's ½ share is inherited entirely by her own biological child (Defendant) = ½ share (50%).
  • Plaintiff's total share = ¼ share (25%); Defendant's total share = ¼ + ½ = ¾ share (75%). The final distribution ratio is therefore 1 : 3.

3. Calculation of Rental Proceeds Compensation

A Plaintiff entitled to a ¼ share of the father's estate is entitled to claim compensation for rental/contract proceeds of the disputed building proportional to her share, amounting to ¼ of proven total rental receipts (i.e., ¼ × Rp 1,600,000 = Rp 400,000).

Question of Law vs Question of Fact

Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the Judex Facti (Kebumen District Court No. 42/Pdt.G/1986/PN.Kbm & Semarang High Court No. 161/Pdt/1988/PT.Smg) err in applying customary law (foutieve toepassing van het adatrecht) by ruling that a child from the first wife has no right whatsoever to the father's gono-gini share acquired from a third marriage?

Supreme Court Legal Answer: Yes. The Judex Facti misapplied the law. The Supreme Court granted the Plaintiff's cassation petition (Ny. [Party C]), annulled the Semarang High Court jo. Kebumen District Court decisions, and independently adjudicated by holding that the Plaintiff is entitled to a ¼ share of her father's estate under a 1 : 3 inheritance distribution ratio against the Defendant.

Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Kebumen District Court & Semarang High Court)

Examination of facts regarding marital genealogies of the late H.R. Moh. Idris bin Alimarja with Ny. [Party A] (1st wife), Ny. Roliyah (2nd wife), and Ny. [Party B] (3rd wife); births of Plaintiff (Ny. [Party C]) and Defendant (Ny. Rr. Suratmi); purchase of land & buildings on Parcel 41 Letter C 522 Panjer Kebumen in 1944 during the third marriage; leasing of the property to co-respondents (Exhibits TT.I/1 through TT.IV/1); and verification of rental proceeds valued at Rp 1,600,000.
