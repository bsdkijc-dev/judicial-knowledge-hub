By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 1947, 2026-09-28 · wiki source · Markdown

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 1900 K/Pid/2002 (deliberated on July 15, 2004 / pronounced on September 23, 2004) is a landmark Indonesian corruption and criminal procedure jurisprudence precedent establishing pivotal rules regarding the status of the Governor of Bank Indonesia as state cashier / holder of government accounts in executing the disbursement of the Government Guarantee Program (Bank Bali Scandal / PT Era Giat Prima Cessie Case) and the Inadmissibility of Cassation Petitions Against Pure Acquittals (vrijspraak ex Article 244 of KUHAP).

Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established three primary legal principles:

  1. Status of the Governor of Bank Indonesia as State Cashier: The action of the Governor of Bank Indonesia (Defendant Dr. [Defendant]) in processing and dispatching the disbursement of Government Guarantee Program funds to settle PT Bank Bali's claim regarding BDNI amounting to Rp 904,642,428,369 was executed within the scope of fulfilling duties as a state cashier or administrator of government accounts pursuant to instructions from IBRA (BPPN, specifically Pande N. Lubis as Vice Chairman of IBRA). The Governor of Bank Indonesia possessed no authority to determine claim eligibility and acted solely as a governmental cashier.
  2. Absence of Joint Intent (Bewustese Samenwerking / Medeplegen): Because the Defendant's actions were performed based on official IBRA instructions and standard banking administrative duties, the element of "acting jointly" (medeplegen) was unfulfilled, negating any abuse of authority or criminal intent (mens rea) to enrich oneself or others.
  3. Inadmissibility of Cassation Against Pure Acquittals (Article 244 of KUHAP): The Public Prosecutor's cassation petition against the Jakarta High Court decision acquitting the Defendant of all charges (vrijspraak) was declared Inadmissible (Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard / NO), because the Prosecutor's cassation reasons merely repeated evaluations of evidentiary findings (appreciations of fact) and failed to establish the existence of an impure acquittal.

The Supreme Court declared the cassation petition filed by the Petitioner for Cassation, the Prosecutor at the Central Jakarta District Attorney's Office, Inadmissible, and charged cassation court costs to the State.

Legal / Procedural Issues

1. Status and Limits of Legal Liability for the Governor of Bank Indonesia as State Cashier under the Government Guarantee Program (Presidential Decree No. 26/1998 jo. BI-IBRA Joint Decree)

Can the Governor of Bank Indonesia, who dispatches the execution of bank claim payments based on official request letters from IBRA, be penalized for joint corruption (medeplegen) alongside IBRA management or bank shareholders?

Supreme Court Rule: Cannot Be Penalized. The Governor of Bank Indonesia acted solely as a state cashier / administrator of government guarantee accounts upon the official request of IBRA (BPPN, specifically Pande N. Lubis as Vice Chairman). Verification authority and claim eligibility determinations rested entirely with IBRA (the Ministry of Finance / IBRA). The Governor of BI's policy forwarding payment technical dispositions to the executing unit in accordance with written IBRA instructions constituted neither an unlawful act nor an abuse of authority.

2. Limits of Public Prosecutor Cassation Admissibility Against Pure Acquittals (Vrijspraak ex Article 244 of KUHAP)

Does the Supreme Court possess authority to grant a Prosecutor's cassation petition against an acquittal when the Prosecutor's grounds relate exclusively to evidentiary evaluations (appreciations of fact)?

Supreme Court Rule: Lacks Authority / Must Be Declared Inadmissible (NO). Pursuant to Article 244 of KUHAP, cassation cannot be filed against an acquittal unless the Prosecutor proves that the acquittal constitutes an "impure acquittal" (niet-zuivere vrijspraak). Because the Public Prosecutor's cassation memorandum merely reiterated factual evidentiary evaluations (appreciations of fact), the Jakarta High Court's acquittal constituted a pure acquittal, requiring the Prosecutor's cassation petition to be declared Inadmissible (Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard).

Question of Law vs Question of Fact

Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the Public Prosecutor's Cassation Petition from the Central Jakarta District Attorney's Office against Jakarta High Court Decision No. 78/PID/2002/PT.DKI satisfy the formal admissibility requirements for cassation ex Article 244 of KUHAP, and did the Jakarta High Court Judex Facti misapply the law?

Supreme Court Legal Answer: The Cassation Petition Fails to Satisfy Admissibility Requirements. The Jakarta High Court judgment constitutes a pure acquittal (pure acquittal) because its legal considerations were based on the unproven elements of abuse of authority and joint participation (medeplegen). The Public Prosecutor's cassation memorandum, which merely contained re-evaluations of factual evidence, is inadmissible on cassation ex Article 244 of KUHAP.

Statutory Regulations

  • Law No. 8 of 1981 (Criminal Procedure Code / KUHAP): Article 244 (Prohibition of Cassation Against Pure Acquittals) & Article 197 paragraph (1) letter d.
  • Law No. 23 of 1999 on Bank Indonesia: Duties of the Governor of BI in Managing State Treasury Accounts.
  • Presidential Decree No. 26 of 1998 & Ministry of Finance-BI-IBRA Joint Decrees: Provisions on the Government Guarantee Program for Commercial Bank Obligations.

Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

  • Doctrine of Evidentiary Evaluation on Cassation: Evaluations of evidentiary results constituting appreciations of fact (bewijswaardering) fall within the exclusive domain of the judex facti and cannot be reviewed on cassation.
  • Doctrine of Pure Acquittals: When a Prosecutor's cassation grounds merely repeat facts without demonstrating legal misinterpretations of statutory elements by the judex facti, the cassation petition must be declared inadmissible.

Uncertainties and Gaps

Legal Protection for Monetary Authority Officials Executing State Cashier Functions: This landmark ruling establishes a monumental legal precedent in Indonesia: Monetary Authority Officials (Governors of Bank Indonesia) executing administrative state payment disbursement functions based on instructions from authorized governmental bodies (IBRA / Minister of Finance) cannot be criminalized for alleged corruption, provided such actions represent official duty performance devoid of criminal intent (mens rea) or personal conflicts of interest.

Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Can the Governor of Bank Indonesia be penalized for corruption for processing bank guarantee claim payments upon IBRA instructions, and can Prosecutors challenge pure acquittals on cassation?
The Governor of BI cannot be penalized, and Prosecutor cassations are inadmissible. Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence No. 1900 K/Pid/2002:

  • The Governor of Bank Indonesia, who dispatches disbursements of Government Guarantee Program funds upon IBRA instructions, acts solely as a state/government cashier, committing neither unlawful acts nor abuse of authority (medeplegen).
  • Pursuant to Article 244 of KUHAP, public prosecutor cassation petitions against pure acquittals (vrijspraak) that merely challenge factual evidentiary evaluations must be declared Inadmissible (Niet Ontvankelijk Verklaard / NO).
