Relationship

A relationship connects two knowledge entities through a defined semantic connection.

Basic Structure

SUBJECT → RELATIONSHIP → OBJECT

Example:

Court → decides → Case

Relationship Examples

Subject Relationship Object
Court decides Case
Decision interprets Law
Regulation governs Procedure
Case contains Evidence
Research evaluates Policy
AI System affects Right

These examples are structural models and do not establish factual relationships without evidence.

Relationship Metadata

A relationship may contain:

  • Relationship type
  • Source
  • Date
  • Confidence
  • Evidence level
  • Validity period

Temporal Relationships

Some relationships change over time.

For example:

Regulation A → amended-by → Regulation B

The Wiki should preserve the chronology.

AI

Explicit relationships improve machine reasoning and retrieval.

However, inferred relationships should be distinguished from verified relationships.
