JPW-REPORT-012

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 1147, 2026-09-16 · wiki source · Markdown

Final Report on the Implementation of the Court-wide Strategy on Gender Equality and Workplace Culture (2023–2025) is the official three-year comprehensive evaluation report issued by the International Criminal Court (ICC), reviewing the progress of its first court-wide strategy across 30 action components and 3 strategic pillars [1–5, 10–12, 24, 110–125].

Summary

This report evaluates the execution of the GEWC Strategy 2023–2025. Key outcomes include the strengthening of disciplinary frameworks, an increase in female representation among senior management (P-5 and D-1 levels), the formalization of flexible working arrangements, and the adoption of core organizational values [4, 6–9, 110–120].

Legal Basis

  • Rome Statute: Article 112(4) regarding independent oversight mechanisms.
  • Assembly of States Parties Resolutions: ICC-ASP/1/Res.10, ICC-ASP/8/Res.1, ICC-ASP/12/Res.6, ICC-ASP/19/Res.6, and ICC-ASP/22/Res.3.
  • ICC Administrative Instructions: ICC/AI/2022/001, ICC/AI/2022/002, ICC/AI/2022/003, ICC/AI/2022/004, ICC/AI/2023/001, ICC/AI/2024/002, and ICC/AI/2024/003.
  • Presidential Directives: ICC/PRESD/G/2024/001 (Protection Against Retaliation).

Background

The GEWC Strategy was launched at the 21st session of the ASP in December 2022 to address recommendations from the 2020 Independent Expert Review (IER) and staff engagement surveys from 2018 and 2021 [10, 16–19].

Objectives

  • Pillar I: Building a safe and inclusive workplace culture and processes [2, 25–70].
  • Pillar II: Fostering the integration of personal and professional life [2, 71–80].
  • Pillar III: Advancing gender equality and career opportunities [2, 81–108].

Key Provisions

  • **Disciplinary & Anti-Harassment Framework**: Implementation of zero-tolerance policies and reporting channels via the Ombuds and the Independent Oversight Mechanism (IOM) [6, 25–36].
  • **Work-Life Integration**: Introduction of Flexible Working Arrangements (ICC/AI/2022/004) and gender-neutral parental leave (ICC/AI/2024/003).
  • **Gender Parity Achievements**: Overall female staff representation reached 52% in 2024. P-5 female representation rose to 46%, and D-1 reached 44%.
  • **Core Values**: Establishment of core values—"Integrity, Fairness, Accountability, and Inclusion"—in January 2024.

Implementation

Monitoring was conducted jointly by the Focal Point for Gender Equality (FPGE) and the Staff Wellbeing and Engagement Committee (SWEC). Mandatory training modules ("United to Respect" and "Gender Equality") were completed by 1,090 and 991 personnel respectively as of 24 June 2025.

Policy Issues

  • Continued fear of retaliation (65% of misconduct victims remain reluctant to report incidents).
  • The need to bridge gender gaps at the P-4 professional level (37% female representation in 2024).
