Law No. 5 of 1986 on State Administrative Courts (Article 55); Law No. 14 of 1970 on Judicial Power; Law No. 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court

Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 330 K/TUN/2001 dated May 10, 2002, establishes fundamental State Administrative Court procedure jurisprudence regarding statute of limitations (*kedaluwarsa* / lawsuit time limits): Because the objects of the lawsuit (Building Use Rights / *HGB* and Freehold Title / *SHM* certificates) were issued on average around 1987, whereas the lawsuit was only filed at the Bandung State Administrative Court on January 26, 2000, the lawsuit has exceeded the 90-day time limit stipulated under Article 55 of Law No. 5 of 1986. Consequently, the appellate *Judex Facti* reasoning dismissing the lawsuit on the merits must be annulled and replaced with a ruling declaring the Plaintiffs' lawsuit inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard*).

Case Background

The Original Plaintiffs / Petitioners for Cassation (1. Adang Effendi, 2. Layim Adipraja, 3. Ny. Omi Tewi Adiwiria, 4. Ny. Nani Kalni Adipraja, 5. Hadis Surtana, 6. Achmad Memed, 7. Sapri, 8. Tata Setiawan, 9. Piping Dana Wiria, 10. Otong, 11. H. Soearli, 12. Odjh Huraedji, 13. Hj. Kona'ah, 14. Oting — substitute heirs of Nji Mas Siti Aminah / Nji Mas Entjeh / Osah) filed a lawsuit at the Bandung State Administrative Court against Defendant I (Head of the Land Office of Bandung Regency) and Intervention Defendant II (PT Adhi Dharma Bumi Indonesia Indah / certificate holders).

The object of the lawsuit sought the annulment of State Administrative Decrees in the form of Building Use Rights Certificate (HGB) No. 37/Cibeureum Village (covering 32,550 m² in the name of PT Adhi Dharma Bumi Indonesia Indah, later subdivided into 92 HGB certificates Nos. 41 to 132/Cibeureum Urban Village) as well as Freehold Title Certificates (SHM) Nos. 3365, 1862, 2060, etc., issued over land formerly under *Eigendom Verponding* Right No. 3323, parcel 50, covering 64,394 m² in Cibeureum Urban Village, South Cimahi Sub-District, Bandung Regency.

The Bandung State Administrative Court (Decision No. 04/G/2000/PTUN-BDG dated July 20, 2000) granted the Plaintiffs' lawsuit in part. However, the Jakarta State Administrative High Court (Decision No. 05/B/2001/PT.TUN.JKT dated February 19, 2001) annulled the Bandung State Administrative Court judgment and independently adjudicated by rejecting the Plaintiffs' lawsuit in its entirety. Dissatisfied with the Jakarta State Administrative High Court ruling, the Plaintiffs filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The cassation arguments are well-founded because the *Judex Facti* (Jakarta State Administrative High Court) committed an error in applying the law in its legal reasoning.
  2. The *Judex Facti* held that the Plaintiffs failed to substantiate their claims, requiring the lawsuit to be dismissed on the merits. Such reasoning cannot be sustained because the objects of the lawsuit (the certificates) were issued on average around 1987, whereas the lawsuit was only filed at the Bandung State Administrative Court on January 26, 2000.
  3. Consequently, the filing of the lawsuit exceeded the 90-day time limit prescribed under Article 55 of Law No. 5 of 1986 on State Administrative Courts.
  4. Therefore, the exception raised by Intervention Defendant II must be accepted, and the Plaintiffs' lawsuit must be declared inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard*).
  5. Legal Principle: "Because the objects of the lawsuit were issued on average around 1987, whereas the lawsuit was filed at the Bandung State Administrative Court on January 26, 2000, it has exceeded the 90-day time limit stipulated under Article 55 of Law No. 5 of 1986."
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioners for Cassation: 1. ADANG EFFENDI, 2. LAYIM ADIPRAJA, 3. NY. OMI TEWI ADIWIRIA, 4. NY. NANI KALNI ADIPRAJA, 5. HADIS SURTANA, 6. ACHMAD MEMED, 7. SAPRI, 8. TATA SETIAWAN, 9. PIPING DANA WIRIA, 10. OTONG, 11. H. SOEARLI, 12. ODJH HURAEDJI, 13. HJ. KONA'AH, 14. OTING; Annulling the Jakarta State Administrative High Court decision dated February 19, 2001, No. 05/B/2001/PT.TUN.JKT, which upheld the Bandung State Administrative Court decision dated July 20, 2000, No. 04/G/2000/PTUN-BDG; and Independently Adjudicating: IN EXCEPTION: Accepting the exception of Intervention Defendant II; IN THE PRINCIPAL CASE: Declaring the Plaintiffs' lawsuit inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard*); Ordering the Petitioners for Cassation to pay all court costs across all levels of jurisdiction, with cassation costs assessed at Rp 200,000 (two hundred thousand rupiahs). *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Friday, May 10, 2002, by Ny. Hj. Emin Aminah Achadiat, S.H. [Presiding Judge], Ny. Hj. Chairani A. Wani, S.H., and H. Benjamin Mangkoedilaga, S.H. [Associate Justices]).*
