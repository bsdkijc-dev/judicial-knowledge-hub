Research on Dismissal in Civil Procedure of the United States (Korean: 미국의 소각하에 관한 연구) is an official judicial policy research report published by the Judicial Policy Research Institute (JPRI) of the Supreme Court of Korea as JPRI Research Series 2017-12.1 It analyzes the norms, case law and procedural mechanisms for early dismissal of civil cases (motions to dismiss) at the US federal and state level—FRCP Rule 12, Rule 41, Rule 11, vexatious-litigant statutes and anti-SLAPP laws—to design an early case-screening scheme and improve judicial resource allocation in South Korea.

Legal basis

The research was conducted under the Supreme Court Annual Research Plan 2017. The principal references are:

  • Constitution, Article 27(3): the right to a speedy trial (신속한 재판을 받을 권리)
  • Civil Procedure Act, Articles 1 and 199: the principles of efficiency and good faith, and the five-month processing deadline
  • US Federal Rules of Civil Procedure: Rule 1, Rule 8, Rule 11, Rule 12(b), Rule 12(e)/(f), Rule 41(a)/(b), Rule 56

Background

In Korea, most civil cases must go through oral argument and full evidentiary examination before a court can rule, even when a claim is defective or frivolous. In 2016, average civil case processing time reached 187.5 days for single-judge panels and 322.3 days for collegiate panels, exceeding the five-month deadline mandated by Civil Procedure Act Art. 199.2

Objectives

  1. Compare motion-to-dismiss mechanisms at the US federal and state levels.
  2. Assess the effectiveness of early case screening in allocating judicial resources to cases that genuinely require substantive review.
  3. Recommend reforms to Korea's Civil Procedure Act, including codified grounds for early dismissal, stronger contempt-of-court sanctions, and legislation on vexatious litigation.

Policy problems

  • Burden of frivolous/vexatious litigation. Individuals filing hundreds or thousands of baseless suits (one plaintiff, "A", filed 2,327 suits against judges and public officials in 2016) drain judicial resources.
  • Limited scope of Korea's existing early-dismissal rules. Existing no-trial dismissal procedures — an order dismissing a complaint (소장각하명령) and a judgment of dismissal without oral argument (무변론 소각하판결) — have a very narrow scope and a vague standard ("clearly without grounds", 청구가 이유 없음이 명백한 때).

Legal and policy gaps

  • No authority to dismiss for non-compliance. Korean law has no explicit basis for a judge to dismiss a suit as a sanction for failing to comply with a court order or for abuse of the right to litigate.
  • No vexatious-litigant or anti-SLAPP rules. Korea lacks a specific mechanism to bar a serial litigant from filing new suits without court permission (a prefiling order) or to dismiss a public-participation-suppressing (SLAPP) suit at an early stage.

Comparative analysis

Jurisdiction System (stable ID) Key features
US federal courts FRCP Rule 12(b)(6) motion to dismiss (JPW-SYS-US-FRCP12) Allows dismissal for failure to state a claim; tightened by the plausibility standard of Twombly (2007) and Iqbal (2009). An IAALS study (2009) found motions to dismiss filed in 23.31% of cases, granted at a rate of 54.33%.
US federal courts FRCP Rule 41 involuntary dismissal (JPW-SYS-US-FRCP41) Permits dismissal for failure to prosecute or comply with a court order or the rules.
US federal courts FRCP Rule 11 sanctions (JPW-SYS-US-FRCP11) Requires that filings not be made for an improper purpose, be warranted by existing law, and have evidentiary support; violations may draw monetary sanctions or an award of the opposing party's attorney's fees.
New York CPLR § 3211 and the Single Motion Rule (JPW-SYS-US-NY-CPLR) Prevents repeated dismissal motions from delaying a case; CPLR § 3214(b) automatically stays discovery once a dismissal motion is filed.
California CCP Demurrer and Vexatious Litigant Act (JPW-SYS-US-CA-CCP) Uses the demurrer mechanism (CCP § 430.10) with a mandatory pre-filing "meet and confer" obligation (CCP § 430.41); 2013–2014 data show 78% of high-value (unlimited civil) cases resolved before trial. The Vexatious Litigant Act (CCP § 391) allows prefiling orders and security requirements for litigants who lost five cases in seven years or repeatedly file baseless motions. Over 29 states have enacted anti-SLAPP laws (cf. Pennsylvania Rule 233.1).

Reform proposals

  1. Codify specific grounds for early dismissal: add new grounds such as failure to comply with court orders, failure to prosecute, and abuse of the right to sue, as bases for sua sponte dismissal.
  2. Strengthen contempt-of-court sanctions: integrate a graduated order-to-show-cause process before ruling on dismissal.
  3. Anti-vexatious-litigant and anti-SLAPP legislation: draft a law regulating vexatious litigants (부당소송인규제법) and a specific dismissal procedure for public-participation-suppressing suits.
  4. Early case management and classification: actively use the judge's power to direct proceedings (소송지휘권) to sort cases from the outset.

Frequently asked questions

What are the report's title, series number and lead author?
Dismissal in Civil Procedure of the United States (Series 2017-12), by lead researcher Oh, Giu Sung, published by JPRI on 28 December 2017.

Why does Korea's civil justice system need a US-style early-dismissal mechanism?
Because most cases must go through lengthy oral proceedings even when frivolous, causing backlogs (an average of 322.3 days for collegiate panels) and wasting judicial resources.

How does "dismissal" in US law differ from 소각하 in Korean law?
In Korea, 소각하 applies only to procedural defects, while US dismissal covers failure to state a claim, sanctions for non-compliance, and dismissal with prejudice, which is equivalent to a rejection on the merits (청구기각).

What is the "plausibility" standard from Twombly and Iqbal?
A standard requiring a plaintiff to plead sufficient, plausible facts supporting the claim, rather than mere legal conclusions or speculative allegations.

How does FRCP Rule 11 prevent frivolous litigation?
It requires that filings not be made for an improper purpose, be warranted by law, and be supported by evidence; violations of this duty to investigate can draw monetary sanctions or fee-shifting.

What does California's "meet and confer" rule (CCP § 430.41) require?
Defendants must contact the plaintiff in person or by telephone to discuss defects in the complaint before filing a demurrer.

How does California's Vexatious Litigant Act work?
It lets courts impose a prefiling order on individuals who lost five cases in seven years or repeatedly file baseless motions, requiring them to obtain court permission before filing again.

What happened in Ceglia v. Zuckerberg (W.D.N.Y. 2014)?
The court used its inherent authority to dismiss Paul Ceglia's suit against Mark Zuckerberg with prejudice after discovery showed the contract document claiming 50% ownership of Facebook was fabricated.

How does Wright v. Fisher (S.D. Miss. 2016) illustrate FRCP Rule 41(b)?
The judge dismissed a pro se prisoner's suit sua sponte after he repeatedly ignored court orders to pay fees or file for in forma pauperis status, following several orders to show cause.

배경

우리나라에서는 청구가 부당하더라도 대부분의 민사사건이 구술변론과 전면적인 증거조사를 거쳐야만 판단을 받을 수 있다. 2016년 민사사건 평균 처리 기간은 단독 187.5일, 합의 322.3일로, 민사소송법 제199조가 정한 5개월의 처리 기한을 넘어섰다.3

연구 목적

  1. 미국 연방 및 주 차원의 소각하 제도를 비교 분석한다.
  2. 실질적 심리가 필요한 사건에 사법 자원을 배분하는 조기 심사의 효과를 평가한다.
  3. 조기 소각하 요건의 성문화, 법정모욕(감치) 제재 강화, 부당소송 규제 입법을 포함한 민사소송법 개정 방안을 마련한다.

정책 문제

  • 부당소송(vexatious litigation)의 부담: 합리적 근거 없이 수백~수천 건의 소를 제기하는 사람들(2016년 판사·공직자를 상대로 2,327건을 제기한 원고 "A" 등)이 사법 자원을 소모한다.
  • 현행 조기 소각하 제도의 협소함: 소장각하명령과 무변론 소각하판결 등 기존 무변론 절차는 적용 범위가 매우 좁고 "청구가 이유 없음이 명백한 때"라는 요건이 모호하다.

법제 공백

  • 불응에 대한 소각하 권한의 부재: 우리 법은 법원 명령 불이행이나 소권 남용에 대한 제재로서 소를 각하할 명시적 근거가 없다.
  • 부당소송인법·Anti-SLAPP 규정의 부재: 상습 제소자가 법원 허가 없이 새 소를 제기하지 못하도록 하는 사전제출명령(prefiling order)이나, 공적 참여를 억압하는 소(SLAPP)를 조기에 각하하는 제도가 없다.

개선 제안

  1. 조기 소각하 요건의 구체적 성문화: 법원 명령 불이행, 소송 수행 해태, 소권 남용을 직권 소각하의 새로운 근거로 추가한다.
  2. 법정모욕 제재 강화: 소각하를 결정하기 전에 단계적인 시정명령(order to show cause) 절차를 통합한다.
  3. 부당소송인규제법·Anti-SLAPP 입법: 부당소송인규제법과 공적 참여 억압 소송에 대한 별도 각하 절차를 마련한다.
  4. 조기 사건 관리와 분류: 소송지휘권을 적극 활용하여 사건을 초기 단계에서부터 선별한다.

부당한 소송도 긴 구술변론을 거쳐야 하여 사건 적체(합의부 평균 322.3일)를 낳고 사법 자원을 낭비하기 때문이다.

미국법상 "dismissal"과 우리 법상 "소각하"는 어떻게 다른가?
우리 소각하는 절차상 흠결에만 적용되나, 미국의 dismissal은 청구원인 불충분, 불응에 대한 제재, 본안 기각과 동등한 재소금지 각하(dismissal with prejudice)까지 포괄한다.

Twombly와 Iqbal의 "개연성" 기준이란?
원고가 단순한 법적 결론이나 추측이 아니라 청구를 뒷받침할 충분하고 개연성 있는 사실을 소장에 기재하도록 요구하는 기준이다.

FRCP 제11조는 부당소송을 어떻게 방지하는가?
서면 제출이 부당한 목적이 아니고, 법적 근거가 있으며, 증거로 뒷받침되어야 한다는 조사 의무를 부과하고, 위반 시 금전 제재나 상대방 변호사 비용 부담을 명할 수 있다.

캘리포니아주의 "협의 의무"(Meet and Confer, CCP §430.41)란?
데머러를 제기하기 전에 피고가 직접 또는 전화로 원고와 소장의 흠결을 협의하도록 요구하는 규정이다.

캘리포니아주 부당소송인법은 어떻게 작동하는가?
7년간 5회 패소했거나 근거 없는 신청을 반복하는 자에게 사전제출명령을 내려 새 소를 제기하려면 법원 허가를 받도록 한다.

Ceglia v. Zuckerberg(W.D.N.Y. 2014) 사건에서는 무슨 일이 있었는가?
증거개시 과정에서 페이스북 지분 50% 계약서가 위조되었음이 드러나자, 법원은 고유 권한으로 폴 세글리아의 마크 저커버그에 대한 소를 재소금지 각하하였다.

Wright v. Fisher(S.D. Miss. 2016)는 FRCP 제41조(b)를 어떻게 보여주는가?
나홀로 수용자 원고가 소송구조(in forma pauperis) 신청이나 비용 납부에 관한 법원 명령을 반복 무시하자, 수차례의 시정명령 후 법관이 직권으로 소를 각하하였다.
