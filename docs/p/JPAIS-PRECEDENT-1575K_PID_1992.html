By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 1760, 2026-09-23 · wiki source · Markdown

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 1575 K/Pid/1992 (dated January 7, 1993) is an Indonesian substantive and criminal procedure law precedent establishing a fundamental rule regarding the prohibition on cumulatively charging or sentencing a defendant as a principal perpetrator (dader ex Article 187 of the Criminal Code / KUHP) and under the offense of omitting to report another's criminal conspiracy (Article 164 of the Criminal Code).

Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established a primary legal principle:

  • Incompatibility of Cumulative Sentencing Between Principal Perpetrators (Article 187 KUHP) and Failure to Report Conspiracy (Article 164 KUHP): Article 187 of the Criminal Code (Arson causing general danger) should not be cumulatively charged or sentenced together with Article 164 of the Criminal Code (Criminal Conspiracy / Failure to Report a Crime). Article 187 KUHP is intended for persons charged as the perpetrators (dader / mededader), whereas Article 164 KUHP is intended for persons charged merely with knowing that others are conspiring to commit a crime but failing to notify authorized officials. If defendants are legally and convincingly proven to be the principal perpetrators of arson (Article 187 KUHP), they cannot simultaneously be sentenced under Article 164 KUHP, and must therefore be acquitted of the charge under Article 164 KUHP.

The Supreme Court amended the verdict of the Kupang High Court Decision No. 50/PID/1992/PT.K jo. Ruteng District Court Decision No. 4/Pid.B/1992/PN.Rut by declaring the Defendants guilty of Arson ex Article 187 paragraph (1) jo. Article 55 paragraph (1) 1st of the Criminal Code, while declaring the Defendants not legally and convincingly proven guilty of and acquitting them from the Second Charge (Article 164 jo. Article 55 of the Criminal Code).

Legal / Procedural Issues

1. Juridical Prohibition on Cumulative Charging Between Principal Perpetrators (Dader ex Article 187 KUHP) and Omission Offenses (Article 164 KUHP)

Is the Public Prosecutor justified in cumulatively charging and the Court sentencing a Defendant under Article 187 KUHP (as an arson perpetrator) and Article 164 KUHP (for knowing of an arson conspiracy without reporting to authorities)?

Supreme Court Rule: Unjustified / Cumulative Qualification Void by Operation of Law

The Supreme Court outlined that Article 187 KUHP and Article 164 KUHP are mutually exclusive regarding legal subject qualifications. A person acting as a perpetrator (dader / mededader) who directly sets fire or orders houses burned ex Article 187 KUHP cannot possibly be classified as an outsider who merely "knew of another's conspiracy and permitted it" ex Article 164 KUHP. The omission offense under Article 164 KUHP applies exclusively to third parties who are not perpetrators. Consequently, combining the sentencing of both provisions by the Judex Facti constitutes an error in law (error in judicando).

2. Limits of Cassation Review Authority Over Alibi Evidence Evaluations (Article 253 paragraph (1) of KUHAP)

Cassation grounds raised by Defendants I and VI claiming they were located elsewhere (Wontong Village) during the arson incident constitute factual evidentiary assessments falling under the exclusive domain of the Judex Facti and cannot be reviewed at the cassation level.

Question of Law vs Question of Fact

Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the Judex Facti (Kupang High Court Decision No. 50/PID/1992/PT.K jo. Ruteng District Court Decision No. 4/Pid.B/1992/PN.Rut) err in applying the law (foutieve toepassing van het recht) by cumulatively sentencing the Defendants under both Article 187 KUHP and Article 164 KUHP?

Supreme Court Legal Answer: Yes. The Judex Facti erred in applying the law. The Supreme Court granted the correction of the verdict, annulled the qualification of Article 164 KUHP, and independently adjudicated by declaring the Defendants guilty of violating Article 187 1st jo. Article 55 paragraph (1) 1st of the Criminal Code, while acquitting the Defendants from the Second Charge under Article 164 of the Criminal Code.

Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Ruteng District Court & Kupang High Court)

Examination of facts regarding the Rembong and Dimpong villagers' conspiracy meeting on September 28, 1991; the raid and arson attack on 22 residents' houses in Nggawut Village, Lawi Village, on Sunday, September 29, 1991, at 3:00 PM WITA; Defendant I ([Party B]) pouring gasoline/kerosene; Defendants II ([Party A]) & III (Nikolaus Kelau) retrieving fire from a kitchen; Defendants IV ([Party D]) & V (Romanus Harum) setting houses ablaze; Defendant VI ([Party C]) giving the command to "burn all houses"; seizure of physical evidence including zinc sheets, bamboo, beams, and burnt window glass; and evaluation of the alibis of Defendants I and VI.

  • Principle of Subjective Qualification Ne Bis In Idem: The principal perpetrator of a crime cannot simultaneously be sentenced as a party permitting that crime to occur.
