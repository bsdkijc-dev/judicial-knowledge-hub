Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 2356 K/Pdt/2008 (dated February 18, 2009) is a fundamental Indonesian contract law and Intellectual Property (Trademark) jurisprudence precedent establishing that trademark purchase and settlement agreements executed under duress while detained in a police holding facility constitute Abuse of Circumstances (Misbruik van Omstandigheden) under Article 1320 of the Civil Code.

Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established two primary legal principles:

  1. Abuse of Circumstances (Misbruik van Omstandigheden): Trademark sale and settlement agreements signed by a trademark holder while under police detention resulting from criminal charges filed by an opposing party constitute an Abuse of Circumstances (Misbruik van Omstandigheden).
  2. Defective Consent under Freedom of Contract (Article 1320 of the Civil Code): Coercion and duress inside a detention facility extinguish freedom of will (wilsvrijheid), rendering the agreement legally defective and void/voidable for failing to satisfy the legal requirements for valid agreements under Article 1320 of the Civil Code.

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Plaintiff ([Party A]), annulled Semarang High Court Decision No. 45/Pdt/2008/PT.Smg dated March 17, 2008, and independently adjudicated (mengadili sendiri) by partially granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit and declaring the Settlement Agreement and Trademark Purchase Agreement dated October 6, 2006, Null and Void (Batal Demi Hukum).

Legal / Procedural Issues

1. Construction of Defective Consent (Wilsgebreken) and Abuse of Circumstances (Misbruik van Omstandigheden) in Contract Law ex Article 1320 of the Civil Code

Do trademark purchase and settlement agreements executed while a party is detained by the police due to criminal charges brought by the opposing party possess legal validity?

Supreme Court Rule: Legally Defective / Null and Void.

Signing an agreement while detained by the police under criminal allegations from an adversary constitutes an Abuse of Circumstances (Misbruik van Omstandigheden). Such duress eliminates freedom of will (wilsvrijheid), meaning the agreement fails to satisfy the subjective validity requirements under Article 1320 of the Civil Code, rendering it null and void / voidable.

2. Qualification of Criminal Process Abuse to Usurp Intellectual Property / Trademarks (Abuse of Legal Process / Bad Faith Pressure)

Filing criminal complaints against registered trademark holders to engineer detention, then exploiting that detention to coerce victims into surrendering or selling their trademarks through settlement agreements, qualifies as an abuse of criminal reporting to unlawfully misappropriate an opponent's civil/trademark rights.

Question of Law vs Question of Fact

Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the Judex Facti Semarang High Court (Decision No. 45/Pdt/2008/PT.Smg) err in applying contract law (foutieve toepassing van het perikatenrecht) by upholding the Settlement Agreement and Trademark Purchase Agreement dated October 6, 2006, without accounting for the fact that the agreements were executed while the Plaintiff was in police detention?

Supreme Court Legal Answer: Yes. Semarang High Court misapplied contract law. The Supreme Court granted the Plaintiff's cassation petition ([Party A]), annulled Semarang High Court Decision No. 45/Pdt/2008/PT.Smg, and independently adjudicated by declaring the Settlement Agreement and Trademark Purchase Agreement dated October 6, 2006, Null and Void.

Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Semarang District Court & Semarang High Court)

Examination of facts regarding registration of Trademark Certificate "ARISE SHINE CES" Registration No. 477275 dated May 22, 2001, in the name of [Party A]; copyright infringement criminal complaint filed on August 8, 2006, by Yayasan Hwa Ing Fonds & Lo Iwan Setia Dharma at Semarang City Police; Plaintiff's detention on October 5, 2006; execution of the trademark purchase deed for Rp 150 Million and delivery of a Rp 400 Million Giro Note on October 6, 2006, inside the detention facility; police report withdrawal on October 6, 2006; release from detention on October 7, 2006; and issuance of Semarang City Police SP3 on October 12, 2006.

Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

  • Doctrine of Misbruik van Omstandigheden (Abuse of Circumstances): A modern evolution of defective consent alongside duress (dwang), fraud (bedrog), and mistake (dwaling). It occurs when one party exploits an emergency, distress, or detention position of another party to secure disproportionate advantages.
  • Jurisprudence on Agreements Invalidated by Criminal Duress: Agreements born from coercion involving threats or exploitation of criminal proceedings lack freedom of contract (wilsvrijheid) and are null and void.

What Evidence Establishes / Does Not Establish

Established by Evidence

  • The Plaintiff ([Party A]) is proven to be the lawful holder of Trademark Certificate "ARISE SHINE CES" Registration No. 477275 dated May 22, 2001.
  • The Settlement Agreement and Trademark Purchase Agreement dated October 6, 2006, were proven to have been executed while the Plaintiff was detained at the Semarang City Police facility.
  • The execution of said agreements involved Abuse of Circumstances (Misbruik van Omstandigheden) and duress, rendering them NULL AND VOID.
  • Semarang High Court Decision No. 45/Pdt/2008/PT.Smg is entirely annulled by the Supreme Court.
  • The Plaintiff's lawsuit is partially granted.

Annulled (Semarang High Court Verdict)

  • Semarang High Court considerations declaring the trademark purchase agreement valid and dismissing the Plaintiff's lawsuit were entirely annulled by the Supreme Court as erroneous applications of contract law.

Uncertainties and Gaps

Protecting Civil Rights Against Unfounded Criminal Extortion: This landmark ruling establishes an essential legal safeguard in Indonesian business and intellectual property practice: criminal complaints must not be weaponized as instruments of legal extortion (legal duress) to misappropriate intellectual property or civil assets. Any compromise agreement or asset transfer born under the pressure of criminal investigation detentions is legally defective as an abuse of circumstances (misbruik van omstandigheden).

Frequently Asked Questions (GEO)

Are trademark purchase or settlement agreements signed while a person is in police detention legally valid under the law?
Invalid and Null and Void. Pursuant to Supreme Court Jurisprudence No. 2356 K/Pdt/2008:

  • Purchase or settlement agreements executed under pressure and in a state of duress while detained by the police due to an opponent's criminal complaint constitute an Abuse of Circumstances (Misbruik van Omstandigheden).
  • Such detention deprives the detained party of freedom of will (wilsvrijheid), meaning the agreement fails to satisfy the validity requirements under Article 1320 of the Civil Code and is NULL AND VOID / VOIDABLE.
  • The Supreme Court annulled the Semarang High Court verdict, declaring the Settlement Agreement and Trademark Purchase Agreement dated October 6, 2006, null and void.
