Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 2678 K/Pdt/1992 (dated October 27, 1994 / deliberation on September 15, 1994) is a fundamental civil procedural law jurisprudence precedent establishing a vital rule concerning the legal standing (legitimasi persona standi in judicio) of banking branch offices in civil legal relations.

Executive Summary

The Supreme Court established two primary legal principles:

  1. Persona Standi in Judicio of Bank Branch Offices: A bank branch office is an extension (extension) of the head office. Therefore, a bank branch office possesses legal standing (persona standi in judicio) to be sued and to sue in civil courts.
  2. Legal Capacity of Operational Managers: A lawsuit or objection directed against a Bank Branch Operational Manager acting on behalf of the Board of Directors pursuant to a Power of Attorney and Credit Agreement Deed is lawful and legally binding.

Where the Banda Aceh High Court (Decision No. 41/Pdt/1992/PT.Aceh) erred in applying the law by holding that a bank branch lacked persona standi in judicio without examining the material merits of the case, the Supreme Court annulled the High Court's decision and ordered the Banda Aceh High Court to reopen the trial to examine the merits of the case (terugwijzing).

Legal / Procedural Issues

1. Legal Standing (Persona Standi in Judicio) of Bank Branch Offices in Civil Litigation

Does a bank branch office of a legal entity (such as Bank Duta Lhokseumawe Branch) possess legal standing (persona standi in judicio) to act as a party sued or suing in a civil court?

Supreme Court Rule: Possesses Legal Standing / Lawful. A bank branch office is an extension (organ / extension) of the head office. In credit obligation disputes executed at a branch office, the Branch Head / Operational Manager authorized by the Board of Directors possesses legal standing (persona standi in judicio) to represent the bank as either Plaintiff or Defendant/Opponent.

2. Legal Consequences of Remanding a Case (Terugwijzing) When the Judex Facti Has Not Examined the Merits (Bodem Zaak)

Where a High Court (Judex Facti) misapplies formal law (e.g., erroneously holding that a party lacks persona standi in judicio) without examining and deciding the material merits of the case (bodem zaak), the Supreme Court does not adjudicate the merits directly, but instead annuls the High Court's decision and orders the High Court to reopen the trial to examine the merits (terugwijzing) pursuant to the Supreme Court Law.

3. Validity of Mortgage/Fiduciary Execution Prior to Final Tenor Maturity Due to Installment Default (Articles 1243, 1244 Civil Code)

A clause in a debt acknowledgment deed stipulating that failure to pay installments/interest for 2 consecutive months renders the entire debt immediately due and payable is valid and binding. Banks hold the right to petition for execution auctions of collateral without waiting for the final maturity tenor if the debtor is proven in default.

Question of Law vs Question of Fact

Question of Law (Judex Juris / Supreme Court)

Did the Judex Facti Banda Aceh High Court (Decision No. 41/Pdt/1992/PT.Aceh) err in applying civil procedural law (foutieve toepassing van het procesrecht) by holding that Bank Duta Lhokseumawe Branch was merely a branch lacking persona standi in judicio to be sued or sue?

Supreme Court Legal Answer: Yes. Banda Aceh High Court misapplied the law. A bank branch is an extension of the head office and possesses persona standi in judicio. The Supreme Court granted [Party B]'s cassation petition, annulled Banda Aceh High Court's decision, and ordered Banda Aceh High Court to reopen the trial to examine the material merits of the case.

Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Lhokseumawe District Court & Banda Aceh High Court)

Verification of facts concerning the execution of Credit Agreement Deeds Nos. 183 (KIK Rp 24M) and 184 (KMKP Rp 6M) dated June 28, 1989, before Notary Ridwan Usman, S.H.; verification of installment default starting July 1990; verification of five formal collection minutes (Exhibits T.4 to T.8); and verification of the surrender of Freehold Title (SHM) No. 242 in the name of Muhammad Ali Nafiah as debt collateral.

  • Civil Code (KUHPerdata / BW): Articles 1243, 1244 (Default) & Article 1338 (Principle of Freedom of Contract / Pacta Sunt Servanda).

  • Law No. 14 of 1970 jo. Law No. 14 of 1985 on the Supreme Court: Cassation Review Authority & Order to Reopen Trials (Terugwijzing).

Established Supreme Court Jurisprudence

  • Doctrine of Branch Persona Standi in Judicio: Corporate/banking branch offices hold independent legal capacity to represent obligations created by such branch offices within their territorial jurisdiction.
  • Doctrine of Terugwijzing (Remand of Case Files to Judex Facti): Where appellate review stops at formal exceptions/standing without touching the merits, the Supreme Court, upon annulling the appellate ruling, must remand the case for rehearing on the merits by the High Court.

Unresolved Merits (Remanded)

  • The merits of the auction objection regarding SHM No. 242 WERE NOT FINALLY DECIDED BY THE SUPREME COURT as the case was remanded to Banda Aceh High Court for re-examination of its substance.

Uncertainties and Gaps

Outcome of Merits Re-Examination at Banda Aceh High Court: This landmark cassation ruling focuses on resolving civil procedural law concerning the persona standi in judicio of bank branch offices. The substantive objection regarding the validity of the execution auction of SHM No. 242 prior to the 48-month tenor maturity was remanded for re-examination and judgment by Banda Aceh High Court via the remand (terugwijzing) mechanism.

  • A bank branch office is an extension of the head office; therefore, a bank branch possesses legal standing (persona standi in judicio) to be sued and to sue in court.
