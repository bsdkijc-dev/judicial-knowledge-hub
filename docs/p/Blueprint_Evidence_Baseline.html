Critical Historical Rule

These figures are historical baselines.

They must not be presented as 2026 statistics without updated authoritative evidence.

Strategic Use

The baseline can support:

  • historical trend analysis;
  • reform evaluation;
  • Blueprint–Renstra gap analysis;
  • performance benchmarking;
  • 2035 transformation assessment.
