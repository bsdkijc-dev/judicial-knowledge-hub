AI Privacy

AI privacy concerns the protection of personal information throughout the lifecycle of AI systems.

Applicable legal requirements depend on jurisdiction and the specific processing activity.

[HUMAN LEGAL VERIFICATION REQUIRED]

AI Data Lifecycle

COLLECT → STORE → PROCESS → TRAIN/RETRIEVE → OUTPUT → RETAIN/DELETE

Privacy risks can arise at each stage.

Key Questions

  1. What personal data is processed?
  2. Why is it processed?
  3. Is the processing authorized?
  4. Who can access it?
  5. Where is it stored?
  6. How long is it retained?
  7. Is it transferred to third parties?
  8. Can the data be corrected or deleted where applicable?

Judicial Context

Particular caution may be required for:

  • Case records
  • Witness information
  • Victim information
  • Contact details
  • Confidential submissions
  • Sensitive evidence

AI Tools

Before entering judicial information into an external AI service, users should verify institutional policy and applicable legal requirements.
