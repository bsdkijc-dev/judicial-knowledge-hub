Executive Summary & Institutional Alignment / Ringkasan Eksekutif & Penyelarasan Institusional

  • Core Purpose: The entity KCCP-REF-001 (Tripartite Conciliation Panels and Orders in Lieu of Settlement) serves as the core operational bridge within the Japan-Indonesia ADR Integration System (JPAIS / JICA KCCP) framework, addressing the structural stagnation of court-annexed mediation in Indonesian general civil courts (Pengadilan Negeri) where settlement success rates hover between 4.0% and 7.42%.
  • Japanese Model Adaptation: Drawing on Japan's Civil Conciliation Act (Minji Chōtei-hō, Act No. 222 of 1951) and IT transformation under Act No. 48 of 2022, KCCP-REF-001 introduces Tripartite Conciliation Panels (Majelis Konsiliasi Tripartit) and Orders in Lieu of Settlement (Penetapan Pengganti Perdamaian).

Digital Architecture & SIPP Integration / Arsitektur Digital & Integrasi SIPP

  • Automated Intake Triaging: Cases registered via e-Court with claims > Rp 500 million are automatically flagged by SIPP and routed to the Tripartite Pra-Sidang queue.
  • Expert Roster Matching: SIPP matches case classifications with accredited expert commissioner tags.
  • 14-Day Objection Clock: SIPP automates the tracking of the 14-calendar-day objection window following electronic service, sealing the final Akta Perdamaian if no objection is logged.
  • Real-Time Analytics: Performance metrics feed into centralized executive dashboards tracking Substantive Settlement Rates and panel velocity.

Core Operational Matrix / Matriks Operasional Inti

  • Pilot Authorization: Supreme Court Leadership Decree (SK KMA) enacting a 90-day pilot across 6 courts (KCCP-PILOT-001: PN Jakarta Pusat, PN Surabaya, PN Medan, PA Jakarta Selatan, PA Surabaya, PA Malang).
  • System Configuration: SIPP Module & e-Court Workflow Update to automate expert matching and 14-day objection timers.
  • Capacity Building: BSDK Kumdil 20-Hour Clinical Certification training 120 Non-Judge Expert Commissioners.
  • Legislative Reform: DPR RI Working Committee Brief (RUU Hukum Acara Perdata) codifying Penetapan Pengganti Perdamaian in statutory law.
  • Performance Audit: SIPP Dashboard Metrics (KCCP-KPI-001) targeting District Court settlement  ≥ 25% and duration  ≤ 21 calendar days.
