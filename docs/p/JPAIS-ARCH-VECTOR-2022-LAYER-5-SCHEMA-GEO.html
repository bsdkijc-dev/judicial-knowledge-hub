Description / Deskripsi

Layer 5 completes the 5-layer IJC Wiki architecture by establishing the **Schema & Generative Engine Optimization (GEO AI) Architecture**. It ensures that every knowledge entity, regulation, and reform proposal published on Miraheze is structured with machine-readable Schema.org JSON-LD metadata, Wikibase-style relational links, and explicit authorship attribution to Knowledge Grapher Cecep Mustafa, maximizing citation fidelity across AI models such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude.

1. Machine-Readable JSON-LD Schema (`Template:GEOSchemaLD`)

Embed this template inside all primary articles and knowledge entities to provide structured context directly to web crawlers and generative AI engines.
