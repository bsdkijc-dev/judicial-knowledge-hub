Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 294 K/Pdt/1989 dated August 28, 1990 (pronounced on October 17, 1990), establishes two fundamental civil and commercial jurisprudence principles regarding trademark law:

  • **Procedure for Alleging Bad Faith in Trademark Registration:** The existence of bad faith on the part of the registrant must be declared in a separate lawsuit concerning unfair competition (*oneerlijke concurrentie*) or an unlawful act (*onrechtmatige daad*), and cannot be simultaneously declared within a decision granting a petition for trademark registration cancellation (ex Article 10 paragraph (2) of Law No. 21 of 1961).
  • **Scope of Trademark Protection Limited to Similar Goods:** Pursuant to Article 2 paragraph (1) of Law No. 21 of 1961, exclusive rights over a trademark apply and protect only the use of goods that are similar (*sejenis*) to the goods bearing such trademark. A registered cosmetics trademark cannot serve to cancel the registration of an identical word mark for dissimilar categories of goods (such as tobacco, cigarettes, food, and beverages).

Case Background

The Original Petitioner / Respondent for Cassation (Chanel, Societe Anonyme, Neuilly/Seine, France) is the owner of the famous trademark "Chanel" registered in Indonesia under No. 133.821 on January 27, 1979, protecting goods in Class 3 (cosmetics/perfumery). However, the Original Respondent I / Petitioner for Cassation (Perseroan N.V. Sumatra Tobacco Trading Company, Pematang Siantar) registered the word mark "CHANEL" under No. 197.890 on September 13, 1985, to protect tobacco products, cigarettes, cigars, pipes, beverages, biscuits, and other food substances.

Chanel, Societe Anonyme filed a petition for the cancellation of trademark registration No. 197.890 at the Central Jakarta District Court (Case No. 323/Pdt/G.D/1988/PN.Jkt.Pst.) on the grounds of bad faith aimed at exploiting the fame of the "Chanel" mark. The Central Jakarta District Court (Decision dated October 31, 1988, No. 323/Pdt/G.D/1988/PN.Jkt.Pst.) granted Chanel's petition in its entirety, recognized Chanel as the first user, annulled the trademark registration in the name of N.V. Sumatra Tobacco Trading Company, and declared the existence of bad faith. Dissatisfied with the Central Jakarta District Court ruling, N.V. Sumatra Tobacco Trading Company filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • **Ratio Decidendi:**
  1. The cassation objections raised by the Petitioner for Cassation (N.V. Sumatra Tobacco Trading Company) are well-founded because the Central Jakarta District Court erred in applying the law, specifically misinterpreting the provisions of Article 10 paragraph (2) of Law No. 21 of 1961 on Trademarks.
  2. The District Court erred in granting the trademark cancellation petition while simultaneously declaring that Respondent I acted in bad faith, whereas such bad faith must first be established in a separate lawsuit or judgment regarding unfair competition (*oneerlijke concurrentie*) or an unlawful act (*onrechtmatige daad*) committed by Respondent I (as formulated in Supreme Court Decisions No. 3146 K/Pdt/1983 and No. 2932 K/Sip/1982).
  3. Pursuant to Article 2 paragraph (1) of Law No. 21 of 1961, exclusive trademark rights do not extend to all categories of goods, but apply strictly to categories of goods similar (*sejenis*) to the goods bearing the mark. The Petitioner for Cassation's trademark registration (tobacco, cigarettes, beverages, biscuits) does not overlap with or infringe upon the Respondent for Cassation's goods (Class 3 cosmetics).
  4. National trademark legislation (Law No. 21 of 1961) takes precedence over the Paris Convention, and national law does not recognize discrimination while limiting protection strictly to similar goods. Consequently, the cancellation petition filed by Chanel, Societe Anonyme must be declared inadmissible (*niet ontvankelijke verklaard*).
  • **Ruling:** Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation: Perseroan N.V. SUMATRA TOBACCO TRADING COMPANY; Annulling the Central Jakarta District Court decision dated October 31, 1988, No. 323/Pdt/G.D./1988/PN.Jkt.Pst.; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Declaring the petition of the Petitioner (Chanel, Societe Anonyme) inadmissible; (2) Ordering the Respondent for Cassation / Original Petitioner to pay all court costs across first instance and cassation levels (cassation costs assessed at Rp 20,000). *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Tuesday, August 28, 1990, and pronounced in an open session on Wednesday, October 17, 1990, by Samsoeddin Aboebakar, S.H. [Presiding Judge], H. Roesli, S.H., and Gunawan, S.H. [Associate Justices]).*
