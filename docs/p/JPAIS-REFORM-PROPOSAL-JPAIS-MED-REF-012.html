  • Knowledge Grapher: Cecep Mustafa
  • Ingestion Status: Confirmed

Description / Deskripsi

Provides an exhaustive evaluation of peaceful conflict resolution in the Israeli-Palestinian conflict, analyzing historical structural failures (Oslo incrementalism, Camp David 2000, regional normalization), stakeholder dynamics, third-party monitoring precedents (MFO Sinai, ILMG), and proposing an Integrated Peaceful Conflict Resolution Model (IPCRM) structured across a four-tier settlement framework and a 30-60-90 day peacebuilding pilot[cite: 12].

Problem / Masalah

  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-001: The diplomatic architecture of the Israeli-Palestinian conflict has repeatedly suffered from systemic vulnerabilities, severe power asymmetries, deferral of final-status issues, settlement expansion, and domestic political instability[cite: 12].
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-002: International and bilateral mediation frameworks exhibit severe enforcement deficits and lack coercive mechanisms to enforce compliance with UN resolutions or interim treaty terms[cite: 12].
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-003: Severe territorial fragmentation in the West Bank (Areas A, B, and C) and the political/geographic schism between the Palestinian Authority and Hamas paralyze unified governance and negotiation mandates[cite: 12].

Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

  • JPAIS-MED-ID-001: While not a direct party to the conflict, Indonesia maintains consistent normative support for Palestinian statehood through international multilateral bodies (UN, NAM, OIC) and judicial proceedings (ICJ advisory opinions).

Japanese Practice / Praktik Jepang

  • JPAIS-MED-JP-001: Japan contributes via diplomatic mediation, humanitarian funding, and economic confidence-building initiatives (such as the Corridor for Peace and Prosperity initiative bridging Jericho and regional markets).

Gap / Kesenjangan

  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-001: Absence of binding final-status parameters in incremental peace frameworks, leaving processes vulnerable to irreversible facts on the ground and spoiler violence[cite: 12].
  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-002: Lack of robust, independent third-party monitoring and executive enforcement mechanisms equipped with independent civilian observer units and structured military-to-military liaison channels[cite: 12].

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Adopt the Integrated Peaceful Conflict Resolution Model (IPCRM) structured across four progressive tiers: Tier 1 (Immediate Ceasefire & Humanitarian Access), Tier 2 (Short-Term Confidence-Building Measures), Tier 3 (Medium-Term Negotiated Arrangements via hybrid international peacekeeping), and Tier 4 (Long-Term Settlement & Reconciliation featuring 1967 borders with 1:1 swaps, shared Jerusalem governance, and refugee compensation funds)[cite: 12].

Action / Aksi

  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-001: Deploy Phase 1 of the peacebuilding pilot (Days 1–30): implement simultaneous ceasefires, execute prisoner/hostage swaps under ICRC oversight, open border points for 500+ aid trucks daily, and stand up the Joint Ceasefire Coordination Cell (JCCC) in Cairo[cite: 12].
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-002: Execute Phase 2 (Days 31–60): deploy advance civilian observers, unblock PA clearance tax revenues via BIS escrow accounts, reactivate joint technical utility boards, and enforce settlement freezes[cite: 12].
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-003: Execute Phase 3 (Days 61–90): appoint a technocratic Palestinian municipal administrative council, convene regional steering committee talks, launch civil society dialogues, and formalize gender advisory boards under UNSCR 1325[cite: 12].

Pilot / Pilot Proyek

  • JPAIS-MED-PILOT-012: 90-day peacebuilding pilot framework structured across three sequential phases (Ceasefire Stabilization, Verification Deployment, Transitional Governance)[cite: 12].

KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

  • JPAIS-MED-KPI-012: 100% halt in verified cross-border strikes; 500+ daily aid trucks entering Gaza; 100% Phase-1 hostage/prisoner execution; 100% transfer of withheld clearance tax funds via BIS escrow; zero new settlement construction starts; technocratic council operational in all municipalities[cite: 12].

Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Establish executive mediation councils and international funding mechanisms to support regional stabilization, financial escrow accounts, and hybrid peacekeeping deployments[cite: 12].

Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Promulgate UN Security Council resolutions mandating ceasefire phases, hybrid peacekeeping mandates, and international financial oversight frameworks[cite: 12].

Evidence / Bukti Empiris

  • Historical analysis reveals structural failures in Oslo (1993/1995), Camp David (2000), and Wye River (1998), contrasted with successful third-party verification models such as the Sinai MFO (active since 1981) and the Israel-Lebanon Monitoring Group (ILMG)[cite: 12].
  • The 2024 UNSCR 2735 framework and the 2002 Arab Peace Initiative illustrate contemporary multilateral efforts and regional macro-settlement parameters[cite: 12].
