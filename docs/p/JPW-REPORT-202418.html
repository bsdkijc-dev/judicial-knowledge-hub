An official judicial policy research publication issued by the Judicial Policy Research Institute (JPRI) of the Supreme Court of Korea that conducts a comprehensive analysis of real estate auction procedures for properties undergoing urban redevelopment and reconstruction under the Act on the Improvement of Urban Areas and Residential Environments (Urban Improvement Act / 도시정비법); examines the conflict between general civil execution principles and urban re-allotment doctrines (환권법리); compares Korean practices with Japanese urban redevelopment registration rules; and proposes concrete statutory amendments to introduce a Management and Disposal Procedure Commencement Registration system (관리처분절차개시등기) and a Blanket Real Estate Clearance system (토지건물일괄말소).

4. Policy Analysis

Background
Land designated within urban improvement zones is both private property and part of the national spatial infrastructure. When land or buildings in redevelopment or reconstruction zones become subject to court auctions, three distinct legal frameworks collide: urban improvement procedures (정비사업), real estate auction under civil execution (경매), and real estate registration (등기).

Policy Problem
Incomplete Public Disclosure: The Urban Improvement Act regulates registration procedures after the public notice of transfer (이전고시), but leaves the lengthy multi-year period between building demolition and new building preservation registration without clear disclosure rules.

Doctrinal Collision (General Execution vs. Urban Re-Allotment): Under general civil law, building demolition results in absolute extinction of ownership (소유권의 절대적 소멸, Civil Act Art. 99). However, under urban re-allotment principles (환권법리), ownership and encumbrances do NOT extinguish upon physical demolition; they persist and transfer to the newly constructed property upon transfer notice (이전고시, Urban Improvement Act Art. 87). This creates severe confusion for execution courts on whether to cancel or suspend auctions on demolished structures.

Isolated Land Auctions & Rights Splitting: When execution courts cancel auctions on demolished buildings and auction the underlying land alone, a buyer acquires only land ownership. This creates severe litigation over whether the buyer acquires association membership (조합원자격), occupancy rights (조합원입주권), or becomes a co-owner with the former building owner, severely depressing auction bid values and impairing mortgagees' recovery.

Over-Indebted Cash Settlement Subjects (과다채무자): When a property owner is classified as a cash settlement subject (현금청산대상자), but total mortgages and attachments exceed the cash settlement value, re-construction associations cannot clear encumbrances to obtain clean title required for HUG sales guarantees (분양보증).

Legal/Policy Gap
Re-Allotment vs. Purchase-Distribution Models: For association members, the re-allotment doctrine (환권법리) preserves identity between old and new properties. For non-members/cash settlement subjects, the purchase-distribution doctrine (보류지·체비지 법리) treats new units as freshly created assets (원시취득), extinguishing old encumbrances upon transfer notice.

Deficit in Procedural Harmonization: Korea lacks statutory rules coordinating execution courts with urban improvement projects, whereas Japan's Urban Redevelopment Act (Art. 70) and Supreme Court Adjustment Rules (권리의 변환과 강제집행 등과의 조정에 관한 규칙) mandate procedure commencement registrations (권리변환절차개시등기) and permit court auction suspensions pending re-allotment.

Objectives
Harmonize urban improvement, civil execution, and registration procedures; protect mortgagees and auction buyers; eliminate incomplete disclosure during building demolition; and provide associations with legal tools to clear over-indebted titles.

Implementation & Proposed Statutory Reforms
Management and Disposal Procedure Commencement Registration (관리처분절차개시등기): Enact Article 78-2 in the Urban Improvement Act (도시정비법 제78조의2). Upon management and disposal plan approval (관리처분계획인가), associations must register commencement on property registers. This notifies the public that the property is subject to urban re-allotment, restricts individual piecemeal registrations, and empowers execution courts to suspend isolated auctions pending transfer notice.

Blanket Real Estate Clearance System (토지건물일괄말소): Amend Registration Rules on Urban Improvement to mandate that old building and land registrations be cancelled simultaneously with new building preservation registration upon transfer notice, preserving encumbrance continuity during construction.

Rights & Procedure Adjustment for Over-Indebted Properties: Enact Article 91-2 (도시정비법 제91조의2). Grants associations a Priority Purchase Right (우선매수권) in court auctions at or above appraised value. For over-indebted cash settlement subjects, allows associations to deposit the appraised cash settlement amount (현금청산금) in court and demand cancellation of mortgages, while court distribution procedures (채권배당절차) distribute the deposited funds among mortgagees.

Expansion of Land, etc. Owners Definition: Amend Article 2(9)(다) of the Urban Improvement Act (도시정비법 제2조 제9호 다목) to explicitly classify owners of underlying land after building demolition as "Land, etc. Owners" (토지등소유자), ensuring that occupancy rights (조합원입주권) naturally attach to the land lot during the demolition phase.

Evidence & Empirical Data
Judicial Statistics & Appraiser Feedback: Property appraiser surveys revealed that auctioning land alone while excluding demolished building values distorts market value, depresses bid prices, and creates post-auction ownership litigation between land buyers and original building owners.

Comparative Japanese Framework: In Japan, pre-demolition registration of right-conversion commencement (권리변환절차개시등기 under Urban Redevelopment Act Art. 70) and Supreme Court execution rules prevent isolated land sales, requiring auctions to be suspended or transferred to re-allotted shares.

6. MediaWiki Output

Understanding Redevelopment, Reconstruction, and Real Estate Auctions is an official judicial policy research report published by the Judicial Policy Research Institute (JPRI) of South Korea's Supreme Court.

Summary

This document is an official judicial policy research report published by the Judicial Policy Research Institute (JPRI) of South Korea's Supreme Court. It comparatively and practically analyzes the conflict between civil execution auction procedures (민사집행절차) and urban redevelopment procedures (정비사업절차) under the Urban Improvement Act (도시정비법). It formulates the distinction between the re-allotment doctrine (환권법리) and the purchase-distribution doctrine (보류지·체비지 법리), and proposes enacting a Management and Disposal Procedure Commencement Registration (관리처분절차개시등기) and a Blanket Real Estate Clearance System (토지건물일괄말소).

Legal Basis

The legal frameworks analyzed include:

  • Act on the Improvement of Urban Areas and Residential Environments (도시 및 주거환경정비법 / 도시정비법): Articles 2, 35, 39, 63, 64, 73, 74, 78, 81, 86, 87, 88, 91, 129, and proposed Articles 78-2 & 91-2.
  • Civil Execution Act of the Republic of Korea (민사집행법): Articles 81, 91, 96, 97, 101, 121, 127, 140, 141, 144, 223–252, 268.
  • Civil Act of the Republic of Korea (민법): Article 99 (Concept of Immovable Property), Articles 186, 187, 342, 364, 370 (Real Subrogation of Mortgages).
  • Act on Ownership and Management of Aggregate Buildings (집합건물법): Article 20 (Principle Prohibiting Separate Disposal) and Article 47.
  • Registration Rules on Urban Improvement (도시 및 주거환경정비 등기규칙): Articles 5, 6, 7, 16.

Background

When property in a redevelopment (재개발) or reconstruction (재건축) area is auctioned by the courts, a clash of legal doctrines occurs. General civil law treats demolition of the old building as an absolute extinguishment of ownership (소유권). By contrast, urban improvement law (환권법리) provides that ownership and encumbrances on the old building do not extinguish but instead automatically transfer to the new building upon the official transfer notice (이전고시). Auctioning the land alone (토지만의 경매) by the courts often creates legal uncertainty, depressed auction prices, and ownership disputes.

Objectives

  1. Harmonize civil execution auction procedures with the urban improvement schedule to prevent isolated land auctions.
  2. Address the incomplete public disclosure gap (불완전 공시) between demolition of the old building and registration of the new building.
  3. Grant a priority purchase right (우선매수권) to reconstruction associations to take over over-indebted properties (과다채무자).

Scope

  • Legal & Empirical Analysis: Supreme Court precedent 95Da10570, a survey by the Korea Association of Property Appraisers (한국감정평가사협회), and execution data provided by Chief Judicial Officer Min Dong-geun.
  • Comparative Japanese Legal Study: Article 70 of Japan's Urban Redevelopment Act (都市再開発法) on right-conversion procedure commencement registration (권리변환절차개시등기) and Japan's Supreme Court execution adjustment rules.

Key Provisions

  • Re-Allotment Doctrine (환권법리): For association members, rights in the old building are transformed into occupancy rights (조합원입주권), so auctioning land alone without the building is not permitted.
  • Purchase-Distribution Doctrine (보류지·체비지 법리): For non-members / cash settlement subjects (현금청산), rights in the new unit are an original acquisition (원시취득), and old encumbrances extinguish upon transfer notice.
  • Procedure Commencement Registration (관리처분절차개시등기 — Proposed Article 78-2): Must be registered upon approval of the management and disposal plan (관리처분계획인가) to lock individual registrations and empower courts to suspend isolated auctions.

Implementation

Execution courts suspend isolated land auctions until transfer notice, while associations may use the priority purchase right (우선매수권) or deposit the cash settlement amount (청산금) with the court to clear mortgages exceeding property value.

Policy Issues

  • Auctioning land alone (토지만 매각) results in very low bid values, as buyers bear the risk of ownership disputes (매수위험).
  • Failure to clear encumbrances on over-indebted properties prevents associations from obtaining HUG sales guarantees (주택도시보증공사 분양보증).

Reform / Amendments

  1. Enactment of Article 78-2 of the Urban Improvement Act (관리처분절차개시등기).
  2. Enactment of Article 91-2 of the Urban Improvement Act (association priority purchase right & cash settlement deposit).
  3. Amendment of Article 2(9)(다) of the Urban Improvement Act (expanding the definition of land owners).
  4. Adoption of the Blanket Clearance System (토지건물일괄말소) for old buildings and land.
