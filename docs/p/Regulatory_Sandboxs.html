Regulatory Sandbox

A regulatory sandbox provides a controlled environment for testing innovative judicial processes.

Boundaries

  • legal scope;
  • duration;
  • participants;
  • data;
  • safeguards;
  • evaluation;
  • exit criteria.

Principle

Experimentation must remain controlled.
