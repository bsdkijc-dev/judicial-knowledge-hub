JPW-REGULATION-EU-DATA-ACT

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 918, 2026-09-15 · wiki source · Markdown

EU Data Act is a European Union regulatory framework that establishes unified rules for accessing, using, and sharing data generated by connected products and related services across the EU Passage 1.

Executive Summary

The EU Data Act entered full applicability on September 12, 2025 Passage 1. The regulation is designed to maximize value from industrial and Internet of Things (IoT) data, accelerate technological innovation, and enforce competitive fairness across European data markets Passage 1, Passage 2.

Legal Basis

  • EU Data Act, fully applicable across all EU Member States as of September 12, 2025 Passage 1.
  • Eversheds Sutherland Executive Compliance Guide, released on September 22, 2025 Passage 4.

Background

The exponential increase in connected products and IoT ecosystems yields vast volumes of industrial data Passage 2. This legislative framework was enacted to remove barriers to data reuse and establish equitable rights for both personal and non-personal data processing within the EU single market Passage 1, Passage 2.

Objectives

  1. Expand access to and economic utilization of industrial and IoT data Passage 2.
  2. Foster digital innovation and preserve fair market competition Passage 1.
  3. Protect commercial entities from unfair standard B2B contractual terms Passage 3.
  4. Enable seamless switching between cloud service providers and enhance cross-system interoperability Passage 3.

Key Provisions

  • Data Scope: Encompasses both personal and non-personal data generated by connected devices and associated digital services Passage 2.
  • B2B Data Sharing: Enforces rules requiring data holders to share data under fair, reasonable, and non-discriminatory conditions Passage 3.
  • Cloud Provider Switching & Interoperability: Removes technical and legal barriers preventing users from migrating data and applications between competing cloud processing services Passage 3.

Implementation

  • Full Enforcement: Mandatory compliance binding all EU Member States effective September 12, 2025 Passage 1.
  • Compliance Strategy: Guidance published by Eversheds Sutherland on September 22, 2025, to assist enterprise governance and contract reviews Passage 4.

Policy & Compliance Issues

  • Unfair Contractual Clauses: Identifying contractual imbalance criteria and invalidating unilateral data access restrictions in B2B transactions Passage 3.
  • Technical Cloud Architecture: Upgrading cloud and IoT architecture standards to conform with statutory interoperability protocols Passage 3.
