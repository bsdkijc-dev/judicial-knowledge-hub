Comparative Research on the Diversity of Judges (Korean: 각국의 법관 다양화에 관한 연구) is an official judicial policy research report published by the Judicial Policy Research Institute (JPRI) of the Supreme Court of Korea as JPRI Research Series 2017-06.1 It analyzes systems of judicial diversification in the United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Japan and the Netherlands, and proposes structural reforms to reduce judge workloads, strengthen specialization and improve public trust in the Korean judiciary.

Legal basis

The research was conducted under the Supreme Court Annual Research Plan 2016. The constitutional and statutory references are:

  • Constitution, Article 27(1): the right to a trial (재판을 받을 권리)
  • Constitution, Article 101(1): vesting of judicial power in courts composed of judges (사법권의 귀속)
  • Court Organization Act (법원조직법)

Background

The Constitution guarantees citizens a trial by independent judges (Art. 27(1)) and vests judicial power in courts composed of judges (Art. 101(1)). Litigation has grown in volume, complexity and technical sophistication. To sustain public trust, courts must provide both thorough hearings and efficient dispute resolution, and major foreign jurisdictions have created varied judge categories to relieve professional judges and bring in specialized expertise.2

Objectives

  1. Analyze models of judge diversification in Anglo-American (US, UK) and Continental (Germany, France, Japan, Netherlands) legal systems.
  2. Classify models into two functions: a workload-reduction track (part-time or temporary judges for minor or pre-trial matters) and a specialized-expertise track (technical or administrative judges for complex disputes).
  3. Formulate statutory and constitutional reform strategies compatible with Korea's legal structure and judicial independence.

Scope

The study evaluates the US Magistrate Judge and Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) systems; the UK Recorder and Lay Magistrate systems; German Technical Judges at the Federal Patent Court and honorary judges; French temporary general judges and the abolished juridiction de proximité; Japanese part-time conciliation judges and Summary Court judges; and Dutch deputy judges. Korean comparison frameworks are the Senior Judge System (원로법관제도), Specialized Judge System (전담법관제도), Judicial Officer System (사법보좌관제도), Technical Examiner System (기술심리관제도), Municipal/County Court Judge System (시·군법원 판사제도) and the former Probationary Judge System (예비판사제도).

Policy problems

  • Overburdened professional judiciary. Full-time career judges handle every stage of litigation, causing case overload, delay and a risk to hearing quality.
  • Monolithic judicial cadre. Korean law distinguishes only the Chief Justice, Supreme Court Justices and Judges, with no non-career or part-time tracks tailored to particular dispute types.
  • Integrating specialized technical knowledge. Patent, administrative and tax cases demand domain expertise that career judges may lack without technical judicial officers.

Legal and policy gaps

  • Rigid constitutional term. Constitution Art. 105 fixes a 10-year term for judges other than Supreme Court Justices, which limits appointing short-term or part-time judges without amendment or statutory workarounds.
  • High bar for lateral entry. The Court Organization Act requires 10 years of legal experience for judge appointment under career-judge unification, against 3–5 years for part-time judges in Japan, the US and France.
  • Independence of part-time officers. Part-time judges who also practice, such as lawyers sitting temporarily, create conflict-of-interest risks unless strictly regulated.

Netherlands Deputy judges, rechter-plaatsvervanger (JPW-SYS-NL-DEPUTY) Part-time substitute judges (lawyers, professors) sitting in three-judge panels to supply specialized expertise.

Korean Technical Examiners (기술심리관) are seconded patent-office officials who advise judges without a vote in deliberations, whereas German Technical Judges are full professional judges who vote on decisions.

Reform proposals

  1. Constitutional and statutory amendment: amend Constitution Art. 105 to delegate judges' terms to statute, permitting flexible terms for specialized or part-time judges.
  2. Statutory amendment: amend the Court Organization Act to create explicit judge sub-categories with tailored qualifications, for example lower experience requirements for part-time or conciliation roles.
  3. Indirect path (우회적 다양화 방식): amend procedural statutes (Civil and Family Conciliation Acts) to appoint civil and family conciliation officers (민사/가사조정관) with judicial power without changing the formal definition of judge, following Japan.
  4. Ethics and independence safeguards: strict conflict-of-interest rules for part-time judges, restricting criminal defense practice, barring representation before the court where they sit, and preventing commercial exploitation of judicial status.
  5. Expand domestic analogues: strengthen the Judicial Officer System and Technical Examiner System to absorb routine non-dispositive judicial tasks.

Frequently asked questions

What is the report and what is its objective?
An official JPRI policy research report analyzing foreign judicial diversification and proposing mechanisms to ease judge workloads and bring technical expertise into Korean courts.

What constitutional foundations does it analyze?
Article 27(1), the right to a trial, and Article 101(1), vesting judicial power in courts composed of judges.

Who wrote it and when was it published?
Kim, Joo Kyung, published 19 September 2017 (Series 2017-06, ISBN 979-11-6168-015-6).

How does the US Magistrate Judge system work?
Federal judicial officers created in 1968, chosen by Merit Selection Panels for 8-year (full-time) or 4-year (part-time) terms, handling criminal pre-trial matters, petty offenses and consent civil trials.

What is the role of German Technical Judges?
Full-time lifetime judges at the Federal Patent Court with science or engineering degrees and 10–15 years of patent-examiner experience, voting on patent invalidation and appeal chambers.

How does Japan's part-time judge system work?
Lawyers with 5+ years of experience serve as civil or family conciliation officers one day a week while continuing private practice.

What happened to France's juridiction de proximité?
It was abolished on 1 July 2017; its small-claims jurisdiction moved to district and police courts and its judges were merged into temporary general judges.

How do Korean Technical Examiners differ from German Technical Judges?
Korean examiners advise without a vote; German technical judges are full judges who vote.

What reform pathways are proposed?
Constitutional and statutory amendment of Art. 105 and the Court Organization Act; direct statutory creation of judge sub-types; indirect creation of conciliation officers through procedural statutes.

배경

헌법은 국민에게 독립된 법관에 의한 재판을 받을 권리를 보장하고(제27조 제1항) 사법권을 법관으로 구성된 법원에 부여한다(제101조 제1항). 소송은 양적으로 늘고 질적으로 복잡·전문화되었다. 사법 신뢰를 높이려면 충실한 심리와 효율적인 분쟁 해결이 모두 필요하며, 주요 외국은 전문 법관의 부담을 덜고 전문 지식을 결합하기 위해 다양한 법관 유형을 두고 있다.3

연구 목적

  1. 영미법계(미국, 영국)와 대륙법계(독일, 프랑스, 일본, 네덜란드)의 법관 다양화 모델을 분석한다.
  2. 모델을 업무 부담 경감 유형(경미·사전 사건을 맡는 비상근·임시 법관)과 전문성 유형(복잡한 분쟁을 맡는 기술·행정 법관)으로 분류한다.
  3. 우리 법체계와 사법 독립에 부합하는 법률·헌법 개정 전략을 마련한다.

연구 범위

미국의 부판사(Magistrate Judge)와 행정법판사(ALJ), 영국의 기록판사(Recorder)와 치안판사, 독일 연방특허법원의 기술판사와 명예법관, 프랑스의 임시직 일반사법관과 폐지된 근린법원, 일본의 비상근재판관과 간이재판소 판사, 네덜란드의 부판사를 평가한다. 국내 비교 제도는 원로법관제도, 전담법관제도, 사법보좌관제도, 기술심리관제도, 시·군법원 판사제도, 구 예비판사제도이다.

정책 문제

  • 과중한 전문 법관 부담: 상근 직업법관이 소송의 모든 단계를 처리하여 사건 폭주, 재판 지연, 심리의 질 저하 위험이 있다.
  • 단일한 법관 구성: 우리 법은 대법원장, 대법관, 판사로만 구분하고 분쟁 유형에 맞는 비직업·비상근 법관 트랙이 없다.
  • 전문 기술 지식의 결합: 특허·행정·조세 등 복잡한 사건은 기술 담당 법관 없이는 직업법관이 갖추기 어려운 전문성을 요한다.

법제 공백

  • 경직된 헌법상 임기: 헌법 제105조는 대법관 외 법관의 임기를 10년으로 정하여, 헌법 개정이나 법률상 우회 없이는 단기·비상근 법관 임용이 어렵다.
  • 높은 법조경력 요건: 법원조직법은 법관 일원화에 따라 10년의 법조경력을 요구하나, 일본·미국·프랑스의 비상근 법관 제도는 3~5년 수준이다.
  • 비상근 법관의 독립성: 변호사가 임시 법관으로 활동하는 등 겸직은 엄격히 규율하지 않으면 이해충돌 우려가 있다.

개선 제안

  1. 헌법 및 법률 개정 방식: 헌법 제105조를 개정하여 법관의 임기를 법률에 위임함으로써 전문·비상근 법관의 임기를 유연하게 정한다.
  2. 법률 개정 방식: 법원조직법을 개정하여 법관의 하위 유형을 명시하고 자격 요건을 달리 정한다(예: 비상근·조정 법관의 경력 요건 완화).
  3. 우회적 다양화 방식: 일본처럼 법관의 형식적 정의를 바꾸지 않고 민사·가사 조정 관련 법률을 개정하여 재판권을 가진 민사·가사조정관을 임명한다.
  4. 윤리 및 독립성 보장: 형사변호 겸직 제한, 재직 법원에서의 대리 금지, 법관 지위의 상업적 이용 방지 등 비상근 법관의 이해충돌 방지 규정 제정.
  5. 기존 국내 제도 확충: 사법보좌관제도와 기술심리관제도를 강화하여 종국적이지 않은 일상 재판 업무를 흡수한다.
