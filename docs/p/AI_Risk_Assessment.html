AI Risk Assessment

AI risk assessment is a structured process for identifying, evaluating, mitigating, and monitoring risks associated with an AI system.

Risk Dimensions

Potential dimensions include:

  • Accuracy
  • Bias
  • Privacy
  • Security
  • Transparency
  • Explainability
  • Human oversight
  • Accountability
  • Accessibility
  • Reliability

Judicial Risk Model

A practical model may evaluate:

IMPACT × LIKELIHOOD × EXPOSURE

The exact scoring methodology should be established separately.

Risk Levels

A conceptual classification:

Level Example
Low Administrative drafting assistance
Moderate Research and classification assistance
High Systems influencing consequential decisions
Critical Systems capable of materially affecting rights without effective human control

This classification is illustrative and not a legal standard.

Mitigation

Possible controls include:

  • Human review
  • Source grounding
  • Access restrictions
  • Audit logging
  • Testing
  • Monitoring
  • Documentation
  • Appeals or correction mechanisms

Lifecycle

Risk assessment should not occur only before deployment.

DESIGN → TEST → DEPLOY → MONITOR → REASSESS
