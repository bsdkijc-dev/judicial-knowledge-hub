Judicial Foresight

Judicial Foresight is the systematic exploration of possible, probable, and desirable futures relevant to the judiciary.

Foresight is not prediction.

Core Questions

  • What could happen?
  • What is changing?
  • What risks may emerge?
  • What opportunities may emerge?
  • What capabilities will be required?
  • What should institutions prepare for?

Foresight Methods

Potential methods include:

  • Horizon scanning
  • Trend analysis
  • Scenario planning
  • Delphi studies
  • Expert consultation
  • Weak-signal analysis
  • Backcasting

Judicial Applications

Potential topics include:

  • AI
  • Digital courts
  • Cybersecurity
  • Access to justice
  • Demographic change
  • Legal complexity
  • Climate-related litigation
  • Emerging technologies

Scenario Structure

A scenario should distinguish:

FACTS

from

ASSUMPTIONS

from

UNCERTAINTIES

from

HYPOTHESES

AI Role

AI can assist in scanning large information environments and generating candidate scenarios.

Human strategic judgment remains essential.
