Policy:Main

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 7, 2026-06-06 · wiki source · Markdown

The Judicial Policy Portal serves as the primary gateway for Supreme Court regulations, guidelines, and benchbooks. Every active researcher and presiding judge begins here.

Supreme Court Benchbooks

Benchbooks represent authorized, standard operating criteria for procedural matters:

  • Civil Case Management Benchbook (Latest 2026 Edition): Authoritative directory managing pretrial timelines and remote deposition.
  • Criminal Trial Protocol Benchbook: Guidelines for electronic evidence chain-of-custody, SHA-256 hash validation, and remote detention rules.

SEMA Administrative Circulars

View and track administrative directives:

  • SEMA No. 2 of 2026: AI Speech-to-Text Transcriptions: Instructions for validating draft transcripts from automated transcription arrays.
  • SEMA No. 1 of 2026: Remote Signature Validation: Verification criteria for digital notary stamps on third-party cloud engines.
