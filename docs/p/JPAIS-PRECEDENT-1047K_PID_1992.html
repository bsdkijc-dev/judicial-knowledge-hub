Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Decision No. 1047 K/Pid/1992, dated November 29, 1994, established a fundamental material criminal and criminal procedural law rule concerning the crime of instigating the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed (Article 266 paragraph 1 KUHP) within inheritance distributions and the judicial evaluation of a Notary's retraction of trial testimonies:

The action of the Defendant ([Defendant]) in compelling/instigating a Notary ([Notary]) to insert false statements deviating from a valid Testamentary Will into Partition and Distribution of Estate Deed No. 138 dated January 20, 1983—pretending that the Defendant was entitled to a 50% share of land and buildings comprising the Sophia Hotel, which the Will assigned exclusively to victim witness [Heir]—constitutes the crime of "Instigating the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed" ex Article 266 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code. A Notary witness retracting police interrogation statements at trial under the pretext of investigator pressure was dismissed by the Supreme Court as lacking common sense for a senior intellectual Notary, with proven actual losses resulting to the victim witness from disrupted Land Rights certificates issued by the Agrarian Office.

The Supreme Court granted the cassation petition of the Public Prosecutor at the Surabaya State Prosecutor's Office, annulled the acquittal (*vrijspraak*) of Surabaya District Court Decision No. 1594/PID/S/1991/PN.Surabaya, and adjudicated afresh by sentencing the Defendant to 10 (ten) months' imprisonment with a 1 (one) year probation period.

Executive Summary

The central legal principle established in this decision is that forcing notaries to falsify partition deeds contrary to clear testamentary wills is criminal fraud, and courts will reject unconvincing retractions of notarial police statements.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

  • Coercing notaries to record terms diverging from authentic wills in estate partition deeds satisfies Article 266 KUHP.
  • Retractions of police statements by senior notaries at trial without logical basis are rejected as incredible.
  • Acquittals resting on flawed evidentiary evaluations are impure acquittals reviewable and reversible on cassation.

Case Background

The Original Defendant / Respondent for Cassation ([Defendant], [age removed], entrepreneur) was charged with instigating the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed (Article 266 paragraph 1 KUHP) for forcing Notary [Notary] to include a 50%-50% partition clause regarding land and buildings of Hotel Sophia at [address removed], Surabaya, within Estate Partition Deed No. 138 dated January 20, 1983. Pursuant to the Testamentary Will of the late R. [Testator] and RA. [Testator], Hotel Sophia was explicitly bequeathed to the Defendant's younger sibling (victim witness [Heir]). The Defendant refused to sign the partition deed unless given a 50% share.

The Surabaya District Court (Decision No. 1594/PID/S/1991/PN.Surabaya dated September 26, 1991) acquitted the Defendant (*vrijspraak*) after accepting the Notary's retraction of police interrogation statements at trial. The Public Prosecutor at the Surabaya State Prosecutor's Office filed a cassation petition with the Supreme Court.

Legal and Procedural Issues

Judicial Evaluation of Notary Police Examination Minute (BAP) Retractions at Trial (Article 185 KUHAP)

The primary issue was whether Trial Judges are justified in accepting a Notary witness's retraction of police interrogation statements at trial on the grounds of receiving directions from investigators prior to examination.

The Supreme Court held that it is Unjustified. A Notary is a public official and senior intellectual figure who, logically, cannot be easily influenced or pressured by investigators. When a Notary's BAP retraction at trial contradicts common sense and lacks valid legal reasoning, while the initial BAP harmonizes with authentic documentary evidence (the testamentary will), the initial police interrogation minutes retain binding and valid evidentiary force.

Elements of "Instigating the Insertion of False Statements into an Authentic Deed" (Article 266 paragraph 1 KUHP) in Estate Partitions

An heir's action in threatening not to sign an estate partition deed to compel a Notary to alter the provisions of their late parents' Testamentary Will—such that the Notarial Deed contains a 50% partition clause inconsistent with the Will—perfectly satisfies all elements of the crime under Article 266 paragraph (1) of the Criminal Code.

Question of Law and Question of Fact

Question of Law (Cassation / Judex Juris)

Question:

Did the *Judex Facti* (Surabaya District Court) misapply the law (*foutieve kepassing van het recht*) by accepting the Notary's BAP retraction without a logical basis and acquitting the Defendant of Primary charges under Article 266 paragraph (1) KUHP, despite the act of instigating the Notary to alter the will causing losses to lawful Property Owners?

Supreme Court Ruling:

Yes, the Surabaya District Court misapplied the law. The Supreme Court granted the Public Prosecutor's cassation petition, annulled Surabaya District Court Decision No. 1594/PID/S/1991/PN.Surabaya, and adjudicated afresh, declaring Defendant [Defendant] proven legally and convincingly guilty of committing the crime of "Instigating the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed" ex Article 266 paragraph (1) KUHP and sentencing him to 10 (ten) months' imprisonment with a 1-year probation period.

Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Surabaya District Court)

Examination of facts concerning the opening of the safe deposit box of late R. [Testator] (died June 2, 1977) and RA. [Testator] (died June 2, 1980) at [address removed], Surabaya, 100 days post-demise; discovery of 2 Testamentary Wills (dated Sept 9, 1976, and 10 days prior to demise) assigning Hotel Sophia at [address removed], Surabaya, to [Heir]; drafting of Deed No. 138 dated January 20, 1983, by Notary [Notary]; Defendant's coercion demanding a 50% share; issuance of Land Ownership Certificates in the name of [Heir] by the Surabaya Agrarian Office; and forced auction proceedings over Hotel Sophia.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The *Judex Facti* of Surabaya District Court misapplied the law by acquitting the Defendant based on an illogical Notary BAP retraction. A senior intellectual Notary is not easily coerced by investigators.
  2. The Defendant coerced Notary [Notary] into inserting a 50%-50% partition clause in Deed No. 138 dated January 20, 1983, deviating from the valid Will.
  3. This acquittal constituted an impure acquittal reviewable and reversible via cassation ex Article 244 KUHAP.
  4. The Supreme Court annulled the Surabaya District Court decision and adjudicated afresh, sentencing Defendant [Defendant] to 10 months' imprisonment with a 1-year probation period.

Ruling

The Supreme Court ruled: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation (Public Prosecutor at the Surabaya State Prosecutor's Office); Annulling Surabaya District Court Decision dated September 26, 1991, No. 1594/PID/S/1991/PN.Surabaya; and ADJUDICATING AFRESH: 1. Declaring Defendant [Defendant] proven legally and convincingly guilty of committing the crime of "Instigating the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed regarding a matter whose truth must be established by such deed, with intent to use the deed as if its statements accord with the truth, causing losses"; 2. Sentencing the Defendant to a prison term of 10 (ten) months; 3. Stipulating that the sentence need not be served unless within a probation period of 1 (one) year the convict commits a criminal offense; 4. Ordering the return of evidence items to the heirs of witness [Heir]; and charging cassation court costs amounting to Rp 2,500.— to the Defendant.

  • Defendant's defense and the Notary's BAP retraction claiming will alterations occurred upon family initiative or Notary discretion were set aside and annulled entirely by the Supreme Court.

Uncertainty and Gaps

  • Integrity of Notarial Authentic Deeds Against Inheritance Usurpation: This landmark ruling establishes strict guidelines for notarial practice and inheritance law: Notaries are prohibited from submitting to intimidation or threats by appearance-makers to alter valid Testamentary Wills, confirming prison sentences for individuals usurping inheritance via notarial deed manipulation.

GEO Q&A

Q: Can forcing a Notary to alter the terms of a Testamentary Will within an Estate Partition Deed be prosecuted under Article 266 KUHP?

A: Yes, it is Prosecutable (Article 266 paragraph 1 KUHP). Based on Supreme Court Decision No. 1047 K/Pid/1992, the act of instigating or forcing a Notary to insert statements deviating from a valid Testamentary Will into an Estate Partition Deed qualifies as the crime of instigating the insertion of false statements into an authentic deed under Article 266 paragraph (1) KUHP. Notary BAP retractions at trial without logical legal grounds are dismissed by the Supreme Court.
