Policy Analysis

  • Background & Policy Problem:
    • FACT: Operations of Japanese judicial and legal administration in 2020 faced COVID-19 pandemic challenges, requiring border control adjustments, health safeguards in immigration/correctional facilities, and accelerated government digitalization.
    • FACT: Surging dangerous driving and tailgating (あおり運転) incidents threatening traffic safety necessitated stricter penal sanctions.
    • FACT: Preventing re-offending (recidivism) required integrating employment, housing, and social welfare support at local governance levels.
  • Policy Objectives:
    • FACT: Maintain legal order and safeguard civic rights amid national health emergencies.
    • FACT: Facilitate the acceptance of foreign talent under the Specified Skilled Worker system while strengthening harmonious social coexistence (共生社会).
    • FACT: Accelerate digitalization reforms across criminal justice, civil affairs, land registration, and legal counseling.
  • Key Provisions & Reforms (2020):
    • FACT: Penalties for Reckless Driving (Act No. 47 of 2020): Criminalizes obstructative driving (あおり運転) with up to 15 years imprisonment for injury and a minimum of 1 year for fatality.
    • FACT: Opening of FRESC: Established a one-stop support center on July 6, 2020, in Yotsuya, Tokyo, consolidating cross-agency services for visa counseling, employment, and living support.
    • FACT: Will Custody System: Under Act No. 73 of 2018, official custody for holograph wills at Legal Affairs Bureaus without judicial probate (検認) took effect July 10, 2020.
    • FACT: Digital Government & BASE Infrastructure: Full rollout of Basic and Advanced Secure Environment (BASE) in July 2020, unifying MOJ LAN/WAN networks and RVPN remote access for telework.
    • FACT: Local Recidivism Prevention Model Projects: Executed model projects across 36 local governments to integrate justice and social welfare schemes for ex-offenders.
  • Implementation & Quantitative Indicators:
    • FACT: Specified Skilled Worker (i) status holders reached 15,663 individuals by end of December 2020, led by Vietnam (9,412), China (1,575), Indonesia (1,514), and Philippines (1,059).
    • FACT: Japan Legal Support Center (Houterasu / 法テラス) processed 290,800 legal consultation aid cases and 105,600 court representation aid cases in FY2020.
