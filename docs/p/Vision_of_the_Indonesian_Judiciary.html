Vision of the Indonesian Judiciary

The 2010–2035 Blueprint establishes the vision:

“The Realization of an Excellent Indonesian Judiciary.”

The vision was formulated by the leadership of the Supreme Court on 10 September 2009.

Strategic Meaning

The vision provides the long-term direction for judicial reform.
