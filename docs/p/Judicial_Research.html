Judicial Research

Judicial research concerns systematic research relating to courts, judicial institutions, legal processes, judicial performance, governance, reform, and justice outcomes.

Research Domains

Potential domains include:

  • Legal doctrine
  • Court administration
  • Judicial behavior
  • Access to justice
  • Judicial performance
  • Judicial education
  • Digital transformation
  • AI governance
  • Comparative judiciary
  • Public trust

Research Methods

Methods may include:

  • Doctrinal research
  • Empirical research
  • Quantitative analysis
  • Qualitative research
  • Comparative research
  • Mixed methods
  • Data science

Evidence Chain

A research claim should ideally be traceable:

CLAIM → DATA/SOURCE → METHOD → ANALYSIS → CONCLUSION

AI-Assisted Research

AI may support:

  • Literature discovery
  • Coding assistance
  • Thematic analysis
  • Summarization
  • Hypothesis generation
  • Data exploration

AI use should be documented where material to the research.

Reproducibility

Research knowledge should, where feasible, preserve:

  • Dataset
  • Method
  • Code
  • Sources
  • Research questions
  • Version
  • Limitations
