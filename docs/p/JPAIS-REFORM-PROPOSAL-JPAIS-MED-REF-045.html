Description / Deskripsi

This page records the official Supreme Court Judicial Policy Brief (*Naskah Policy Brief Mahkamah Agung RI - Edisi Agustus 2026*), drafted pursuant to the National Institute of Public Administration (LAN RI) Executive Template (June 2026 Edition) by the Expert Team and Working Group on Competition Law Reform (*Tim Ahli & Kelompok Kerja Pembaharuan Hukum Persaingan Usaha Mahkamah Agung RI*). Addressed directly to the Leadership of the Supreme Court of RI (Chief Justice, Deputy Chief Justice for Judicial Affairs, Chairman of the Civil Chamber, and Chamber Chairmen), the Policy Brief synthesizes empirical findings from the August 2026 Problem Inventory List (DIM 2026) across all 5 Commercial Courts (Central Jakarta, Medan, Semarang, Surabaya, and Makassar), ICLA, academia, and economic experts. To resolve 35 core operational and legal issues, the document formulates a strategic Dual-Track Policy: (1) amending PERMA No. 3/2021 for procedural reform; and (2) issuing a Supreme Court Decree (KMA) on Adjudication Guidelines for Commercial Judges, backed by deep comparative research into Germany (Civil Law / GWB), Australia (Common Law / ACT), and Singapore (Regional Neighbor / CAB). All structural entities, policy matrices, stakeholder roles, and implementation roadmaps have been ingested into the Knowledge Graph under the direction of Knowledge Grapher Cecep Mustafa.

Problem / Masalah

  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-054: Normative Conflicts & Regulatory Disharmony, characterized by conflicting definitions of days between PERMA No. 3/2021 (calendar days) and PP No. 44/2021 (working days), absolute competence ambiguity regarding MSME Partnership (*kemitraan*) rulings under KPPU Reg No. 2/2024, and a lack of explicit forum rules for foreign respondents.
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-055: E-Court Incompatibility & Evidentiary Complexity, where SIPP/e-Court architecture rejects secondary objection filings and lacks multi-petitioner consolidation features (triggering parallel trials and contradictory Cassation judgments), compounded by rigid evidence locks under Article 13(5) PERMA No. 3/2021 and a total absence of trade secret protections (*confidentiality rings*).

Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

  • JPAIS-MED-ID-045: Objections are processed without pre-trial mediation (Article 12 PERMA No. 3/2021) or formal *inzage* rules, forcing judges to conduct dossier-bound reviews without economic expert evaluation tools, while SIPP/e-Court architecture lacks multi-petitioner master case features and bank guarantees are demanded indiscriminately.

Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

  • JPAIS-MED-JP-045: Deep comparative analysis across Germany (Civil Law / GWB with specialized *Kartellsenat* and *Data Rooms*), Australia (Common Law / ACT with hybrid panels and *Hot Tubbing* concurrent expert evidence), and Singapore (Regional Neighbor with *Competition Appeal Board* and High Court point-of-law filters) demonstrates advanced procedural instruments absent in current Indonesian practice.

Gap / Kesenjangan

  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-045: Indonesia's PERMA No. 3/2021 operates as a hybrid administrative review without procedural tools found in Civil Law (Germany's *Data Room*), Common Law (Australia's *Hot Tubbing*), or Regional practice (Singapore's 2-month filing window and point-of-law filters).

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Adopt Alternative 3 (Strategic Dual-Track Policy Package): Track 1 (Promulgation of PERMA Amandemen PERMA No. 3 Tahun 2021 for procedural reform, redefining days as working days, bifurcated memory submission, proportional bank guarantees, e-Court master case consolidation, reciprocal evidence lock relaxation, and limited Judicial Review/PK); and Track 2 (Promulgation of KMA - Adjudication Guidelines for Commercial Judges introducing 4-stage economic analysis testing, hot-tubbing protocols, data room/confidentiality ring protocols, and indirect evidence guidelines).

Action / Aksi

  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-045: Promulgate PERMA Amandemen No. 3 Tahun 2021 and KMA Adjudication Guidelines for Commercial Judges under Supreme Court Leadership oversight.
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-046: Update SIPP/e-Court database schema for multi-petitioner registration and execute the Three Horizons Action Plan.

Pilot Proyek / Pilot Projections

  • JPAIS-MED-PILOT-045: National Commercial Court rollout across Central Jakarta, Medan, Semarang, Surabaya, and Makassar under the Three Horizons Action Plan (Horizon 1 Quick Wins, Horizon 2 Medium Term, Horizon 3 Long Term).

KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

  • JPAIS-MED-KPI-045: 100% implementation of dual-track reform instruments; successful execution of SIPP multi-petitioner database upgrades; adherence to strict trial timelines and specialized economic evidence protocols.

Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Ensuring full alignment between Supreme Court procedural regulations and statutory mandates under Law No. 11/2020, Law No. 6/2023, and Law No. 1/2023 (KUHP Baru), alongside establishing permanent institutional capacity building for commercial judges via BSDK Mahkamah Agung RI.

Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Issuing binding Supreme Court Regulations (PERMA) and Working Group Decrees (KMA) that systematically resolve the 35 core problem points identified in the DIM 2026 matrix.

Evidence / Bukti Empiris

  • Official Judicial Policy Brief (*POLICY BRIEF plus Data Supid.docx* / *POLICY BRIEF MAHKAMAH AGUNG REPUBLIK INDONESIA (1).docx*) drafted following the LAN RI Executive Template B June 2026 Edition, presented in August 2026 to Supreme Court Leadership.
