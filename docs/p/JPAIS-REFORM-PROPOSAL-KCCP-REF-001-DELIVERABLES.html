Core Institutional Deliverables / Deliverables Institusional Utama

  • Draft Supreme Court Leadership Decree (SK KMA): A formal decree draft authorizing the 90-day pilot across the six designated courts (PN Jakarta Pusat, PN Surabaya, PN Medan, PA Jakarta Selatan, PA Surabaya, and PA Malang), establishing the Steering Committee, and granting operational authorization for 3-member panels.
  • SIPP Technical & Workflow Specification: An operational blueprint for the IT development team to configure SIPP for panel assignment, expert commissioner roster management, automated tracking of the 14-day objection window, and real-time KPI dashboards.
  • Expert Commissioner Training & Roster Protocol: A specialized curriculum framework for BSDK Kumdil to train and accredit the initial cohort of 120 Non-Judge Expert Commissioners in evaluative co-mediation dynamics.
  • Policy Brief for Supreme Court Leadership: A high-level executive memorandum outlining the legal rationale, budget allocations (APBN/DIPA), and legislative amendments needed for the Draft Civil Procedure Code (RUU Hukum Acara Perdata).
