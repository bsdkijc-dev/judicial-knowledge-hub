JPW-BRIEFING-002

By Cecep Mustafa, Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) · revision 1157, 2026-09-16 · wiki source · Markdown

Statements for the Fifth Diplomatic Briefing of the International Criminal Court is an official publication of the International Criminal Court (ICC) detailing its headquarters contact details in The Hague, Netherlands, and announcing an extension of the nomination period for Assembly of States Parties (ASP) candidates beyond 6 November. Summary

This document contains the official ICC headquarters address, phone and fax numbers, and notices regarding the availability of candidate details and nomination period extensions for the Assembly of States Parties [1–74].
