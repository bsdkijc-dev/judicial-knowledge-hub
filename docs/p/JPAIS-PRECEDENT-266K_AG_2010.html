Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 266 K/AG/2010 dated July 12, 2010, establishes highly landmark and progressive Islamic civil jurisprudence regarding the division of joint property: A wife receives 3/4 (three-quarters) of the joint property and the husband receives only 1/4 (one-quarter), because the joint property was acquired entirely through the wife's own labor while the husband failed to provide maintenance for the wife and children for 11 years.

Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Respondent for Cassation (Ny. [Wife], Amd.Pd., Lecturer at Universitas Muhammadiyah Yogyakarta & Research Consultant) filed a divorce suit (*cerai gugat*) and a claim for the division of joint property against the Original Defendant / Petitioner for Cassation (Drs. [Husband], NGO worker in Solo) at the Bantul Religious Court. The Plaintiff and Defendant were married on April 8, 1995 (KUA Ketandan, Klaten) and blessed with 2 children. Since 1998, their household became discordant, marked by quarrels and physical/psychological abuse, culminating in the Defendant acting selfishly and harshly, and eventually expelling the Plaintiff on November 6, 2008. Furthermore, since 1997 (11 years / 132 months), the Defendant entirely failed to provide material or spiritual maintenance (*nafkah lahir maupun batin*) to his wife and children, forcing all family expenses and the acquisition of joint properties (immovable and movable assets) to be generated purely through the Plaintiff's own hard work.

The Bantul Religious Court (Decision No. 229/Pdt.G/2009/PA.Btl. dated August 20, 2009) granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part, pronounced a single *ba'in sughra* divorce, and ordered the division of joint property at 3/4 share for the Plaintiff (Wife) and 1/4 share for the Defendant (Husband). The Yogyakarta High Religious Court (Decision No. 34/Pdt.G/2009/PTA.Yk. dated November 19, 2009) annulled the Bantul Religious Court judgment and independently adjudicated by retaining the 3/4 share for the Plaintiff and 1/4 share for the Defendant. The Defendant filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court arguing that joint property must be divided equally in half (1/2 share each).

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The Petitioner for Cassation's cassation argument that joint property within a marriage must be divided equally in half (1/2 share each) cannot be sustained, as the Yogyakarta High Religious Court did not err in applying the law.
  2. Based on evidence and facts established at trial, it was proven that the husband (Petitioner for Cassation) failed to provide maintenance from his earnings for 11 years (132 months), and all joint properties were acquired by the wife (Respondent for Cassation) through her own labor.
  3. In the interest of justice (*ex aequo et bono*), it is proper and just for the Plaintiff (Wife) to receive 3/4 (three-quarters) of the joint property and the Defendant (Husband) to receive 1/4 (one-quarter) of the joint property.
  4. The Supreme Court also found it necessary to amend the dispositive order of the Yogyakarta High Religious Court to comply with Article 84 of Law No. 7 of 1989 (as amended by Law No. 3 of 2006 and Law No. 50 of 2009) by ordering the Registrar of the Bantul Religious Court to send a copy of the decision to the Marriage Registration Official (*PPN*).
  • Ruling: Rejecting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation: Drs. [Husband]; Amending the dispositive order of the Yogyakarta High Religious Court decision dated November 19, 2009, No. 34/Pdt.G/2009/PTA.Yk., which annulled the Bantul Religious Court decision dated August 20, 2009, No. 229/Pdt.G/2009/PA.Btl., so that the dispositive order in its entirety reads as follows: (1) Accepting the Appellant's appeal; (2) Annulling the Bantul Religious Court decision No. 229/Pdt.G/2009/PA.Btl.; INDEPENDENTLY ADJUDICATING: (a) Granting the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part; (b) Pronouncing a single *ba'in sughra* divorce of Defendant (Drs. [Husband]) against Plaintiff (Ny. [Wife], Amd.Pd.); (c) Stating that the child [Child] remains under the custody (*hadhanah*) of the Plaintiff; (d) Ordering the Defendant to pay child maintenance amounting to Rp 750,000 per month; (e) Declaring that assets consisting of agricultural land under SHM No. 1132 & SHM No. 1133 in Klaten, SHM No. 07435 in Bantul, SHM No. 01797 in Sleman, Kijang car AB 1781 Z, Legenda motorcycle AD 4802 EV, Supra Fit motorcycle, and household furniture constitute joint property; (f) Declaring that the Plaintiff / Appellee is entitled to own 3/4 (three-quarters) of the joint property and the Defendant / Appellant is entitled to own 1/4 (one-quarter) of the joint property; (g) Ordering the Defendant and Plaintiff to divide the joint property, and if partition in kind is unfeasible, to sell it via public auction; (h) Directing the Registrar of the Bantul Religious Court to send a copy of the decision to the Marriage Registration Official (*PPN*); (i) Ordering the Petitioner for Cassation / Defendant to pay cassation-level court costs amounting to Rp 500,000 (five hundred thousand rupiahs). *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Monday, July 12, 2010, by Drs. H. Andi Syamsu Alam, S.H., M.H. [Presiding Judge], Drs. H. Hamdan, S.H., M.H., and Drs. H. Mukhtar Zamzami, S.H., M.H. [Associate Justices]).*
