Timely Notification of Appellate, Cassation and Judicial Review Orders

Indicator 1.4 measures the percentage of notifications of the operative parts/orders of appellate, cassation, and judicial review judgments delivered to parties on time.

Formula

<math> \frac{\text{Notifications delivered on time}} {\text{Judgment orders received by referring courts}}\times100\ </math>

Strategic Function

Timely notification enables parties to proceed with subsequent legal processes in accordance with applicable rules.
