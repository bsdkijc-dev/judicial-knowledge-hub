Overview

Supreme Court Decision Reg. No. 280 K/AG/2004 dated November 10, 2004, establishes very important Islamic civil jurisprudence regarding the consequences of divorce and the protection of children's future: "That when a divorce has occurred, the consequences of the divorce must be determined in accordance with minimum living requirements based on compliance and fairness, and to ensure certainty and the child's future, it is necessary to establish the husband's obligation to finance his children's maintenance." The Supreme Court annulled the Jakarta High Religious Court decision which had rejected the talak petition, granted permission to pronounce talak, and established obligations for mut'ah, iddah maintenance/maskah/kiswah amounting to Rp 25,000,000, and child maintenance of at least Rp 1,000,000 per month per child until the children reach the age of 21 (majority).

Case Background

The Petitioner for Cassation / formerly Petitioner / Appellee (Abdullah [Father]) filed a petition for divorce talak against the Respondent for Cassation / formerly Respondent / Appellant ([Mother]) at the South Jakarta Religious Court. The Petitioner and Respondent were married on January 15, 1995 (recorded at PPN KUA Cilandak Sub-District) and blessed with 2 children: [Child A] (born [details removed]) and [Child B] (born [details removed]). Since October 2001, continuous disputes and quarrels arose triggered by differences of opinion and domestic incompatibility, leading them to separate beds and households for more than 1 year and 4 months.

The South Jakarta Religious Court (Decision No. 182/Pdt.G/2003/PA.JS dated June 11, 2003) granted the Petitioner's request, granted permission to pronounce *talak raj'i*, and ordered the Petitioner to pay iddah maintenance of Rp 20,000,000 and mut'ah in the form of a house at [address removed], South Jakarta, and a Caravelle car. However, the Jakarta High Religious Court (Decision No. 96/Pdt/2003/PTA.JK dated February 26, 2004) annulled the South Jakarta Religious Court judgment and rejected the Petitioner's request in its entirety. The Petitioner filed a cassation petition to the Supreme Court.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. The Petitioner for Cassation's cassation objections are well-founded because the Petitioner and Respondent have lived separately in terms of house and bed for more than 1 year and 4 months due to continuous disputes, and reconciliation efforts through family members failed, meaning the household has irretrievably broken down (*broken marriage*) and satisfies the divorce grounds under Article 19 letter (f) jo. Article 22 paragraph (2) of Government Regulation No. 9 of 1975 jo. Article 39 paragraph (2) of Law No. 1 of 1974. The Jakarta High Religious Court's decision rejecting the talak petition must be annulled.
  2. The total value of maintenance, maskah, and kiswah during the iddah period determined by the *Judex Facti* is deemed insufficient to meet minimum living requirements, compliance, and fairness, prompting the Supreme Court to increase the amount to Rp 25,000,000 (twenty-five million rupiahs).
  3. Because both children remain under the custody (*hadhanah*) of the Respondent, to ensure certainty and the children's future, it is necessary to establish the husband's obligation to finance the children's maintenance of at least Rp 1,000,000 per month for each child until they reach the age of 21 (majority).
  • Ruling: Granting the cassation petition from Petitioner for Cassation: ABDULLAH [Father]; Annulling the Jakarta High Religious Court decision dated February 26, 2004, No. 96/Pdt/2003/PTA.JK; and Independently Adjudicating: (1) Granting the Petitioner's request in its entirety; (2) Granting permission to the Petitioner (ABDULLAH [Father]) to pronounce a single *talak raj'i* against the Respondent ([Mother]) before the session of the South Jakarta Religious Court; (3) Ordering the Petitioner to pay to the Respondent: (a) Mut'ah in the form of a house located at [address removed], South Cipete Village, Cilandak Sub-District, South Jakarta; (b) Maintenance (*Nafkah, Kiswah, and Maskah*) during the iddah period amounting to Rp 25,000,000 (twenty-five million rupiahs); (c) Maintenance for 2 (two) children of at least Rp 1,000,000 (one million rupiahs) each per month from the time the Petitioner pronounces talak until the children reach the age of 21 (majority); (4) Directing the Registrar of the South Jakarta Religious Court to send a copy of the talak pronunciation determination to the Marriage Registration Official; (5) Rejecting the Petitioner's requests for anything further and otherwise; (6) Ordering the Petitioner to pay court costs across first instance, appellate, and cassation levels (cassation costs assessed at Rp 500,000). *(Decided in the deliberation meeting of the Supreme Court on Wednesday, November 10, 2004, by the Cassation Panel of Justices: Drs. H. Andi Syamsu Alam, S.H., M.H. [Presiding Judge], Drs. H. Hamdan, S.H., M.H., and Drs. H. Abdul Manan, S.H., S.I.P., M.Hum. [Associate Justices]).*
