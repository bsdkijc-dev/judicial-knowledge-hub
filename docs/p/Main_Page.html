     ⚖ JUDICIAL KNOWLEDGE
     

<Judicial Reasoning & Practice>

Doctrinal frameworks, comparative case law, and rigorous legal reasoning structures across civil, criminal, and commercial jurisdictions.

     🎓 ACADEMY
     

<Corporate University Academy>

Candidate judge training paths (PPC Terpadu), continuing professional education curricula, and certified benchbooks.

     🔬 RESEARCH
     

<Pustrajak Research & Policy>

Evidence-based judicial policy briefs, empirical micro-data repositories, and socio-legal reform impact analyses.

     📜 POLICY
     

<Regulatory Repository>

Authoritative collection of PERMA, SEMA, SK KMA, and binding administrative guidelines governing court management.

     🌏 COMPARATIVE
     

<Comparative Judiciary>

Cross-jurisdictional analyses comparing Indonesian court frameworks with Japan (Minji Chōtei, mints), Singapore, and Australia.

<AI & Digital Transformation>

Responsible AI governance, SIPP/EIS digital case management integration, and algorithmic transparency protocols.
