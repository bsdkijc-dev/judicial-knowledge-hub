Policy Analysis

  • Background & Purpose:
    • FACT: Document 法務資料 第468号 was published by the Judicial System Department of the Ministry of Justice of Japan in December 2022. It contains the official Japanese translation of Book 4 (Family Law, §§ 1297–1888) of the German Civil Code (Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch - BGB) as of January 1, 2023 (incorporating legislative changes known through August 2022).
    • FACT: The publication was commissioned to provide comparative legal research for ongoing family law reform discussions within Japan's Legislative Council (法制審議会).
  • Key Legal Provisions & Reforms Analyzed:
    • Same-Sex Marriage (同性婚 - § 1353(1)): Recognized since 2017 by defining marriage as being entered into for life by two persons of different or the same sex, phasing out the 2001 Registered Partnership scheme (Lebenspartnerschaft).
    • Marital Property Regime (婚姻財産制 - § 1363 et seq.): The statutory default regime is the Community of Equalized Gains (Zugewinngemeinschaft / 剰余共同制), where property remains separate during marriage, but net financial gains (剰余) are equalized upon dissolution.
    • Divorce System (離婚 - § 1564 & § 1565): Rejects administrative or consensual non-judicial divorce. Judicial divorce is mandatory based on the breakdown principle (破綻主義 / § 1565), determined by objective periods of physical separation (別居).
    • Parent-Child Relations (実親子関係 - § 1591 & § 1592): Section 1591 establishes that the mother of a child is the woman who gave birth to the child (出産者). Statutory reforms in 1969 and 1997 eliminated legal discrimination between marital and non-marital children.
    • Parental Care (親の配慮 / elterliche Sorge - § 1626): Replaced the traditional concept of "parental authority" (elterliche Gewalt) in 1979 to establish the child as a legal subject and emphasize parental duties. Joint care (共同配慮) serves as the primary principle.
    • Child Contact & Access Rights (子との交流 - § 1684): Contact with parents is defined as both a child's right and a parental obligation. Courts may appoint contact guardians (交流保護) to enforce access.
    • Guardianship & Adult Care (後見・法的世話 - § 1814 et seq.): Restructured guardianship for minors (Vormundschaft) and legal care for adults (rechtliche Betreuung) to safeguard individual autonomy.
  • Interpretations & Structural Inferences:
    • INTERPRETATION: Publishing this translation reflects a strategic initiative by the MOJ to examine dynamic legal models tied to constitutional fundamental rights (Grundgesetz Art. 6) and European Court of Human Rights (ECHR) jurisprudence to address stagnation in domestic civil code reform.
    • INFERENCE: Although both Japanese Civil Law and the German BGB share 19th-century Pandectist roots, Germany's frequent substantive updates (same-sex marriage, elimination of non-marital status distinctions, and transition from parental authority to parental care) make BGB Book 4 a benchmark for Japan's judicial policy formulation.
