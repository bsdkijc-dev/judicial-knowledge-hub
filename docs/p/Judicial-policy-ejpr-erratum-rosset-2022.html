Erratum: The electoral roots of unequal representation is an official erratum published in the European Journal of Political Research (Volume 61, Page 293, 2022) by the European Consortium for Political Research (ECPR).

Summary

This document is an official correction to the research article titled "The electoral roots of unequal representation. A spatial modelling approach to party systems and voting in Western Europe", authored by J. Rosset and A.-S. Kurella (originally published in 2021 in EJPR Volume 60, Issue 4, pages 785–806). The correction was issued to remove a second institutional affiliation from the first author (J. Rosset).

Legal Basis

  • Not found in the available sources: This document is an academic journal correction notice and is not based on any specific statutory or judicial legislative instrument.

Background

In 2021, J. Rosset and A.-S. Kurella published a research article on the electoral roots of unequal representation in Western Europe. Following publication, an error was identified in the recorded institutional affiliation of the first author.

Objectives

To correct the scholarly record by formally removing the affiliation "Mannheimer Zentrum für Europäische Sozialforschung, Universität Mannheim, Germany" from the first author's (J. Rosset's) profile.

Scope

This correction applies specifically to the Rosset & Kurella (2021) publication in the European Journal of Political Research, DOI `10.1111/1475-6765.12423`.

Key Provisions

  • Affiliation Removal: The second affiliation, "Mannheimer Zentrum für Europäische Sozialforschung, Universität Mannheim, Germany," was formally removed for the first author (J. Rosset).
  • Publication Details: The erratum was published in EJPR Volume 61, Page 293 (2022), DOI `10.1111/1475-6765.12488`.

Implementation

Published by John Wiley & Sons Ltd on behalf of the European Consortium for Political Research (ECPR).

Policy Issues

Correcting errors in recorded academic affiliations to preserve the integrity and accuracy of institutional attribution within scholarly citation databases.

Reform / Implications

Public issuance of a formal erratum notice within the official journal volume.
