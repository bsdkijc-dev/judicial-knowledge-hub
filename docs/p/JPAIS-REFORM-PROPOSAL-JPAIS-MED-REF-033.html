Description / Deskripsi

Provides a comprehensive evaluation of case statistics and the Issue Inventory (DIM) for KPPU business competition objection cases handled by the Makassar Commercial Court from 2021 to 2026. It highlights the resource allocation ratio of 6 certified judges against a single registered case footprint (No. 1/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2024/PN Niaga Mks), alongside the implications of a "Nil" (Nihil) DIM status on access to justice across Eastern Indonesia.

Problem / Masalah

  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-033: Under-utilization and geographical access inequality, where the expansive jurisdiction of Eastern Indonesia recorded only 1 case over 6 years due to high logistical costs and regional business disincentives.
  • JPAIS-MED-PROB-034: Imprecision in standard DIM reporting forms yielding a "Nil" status, effectively isolating the Makassar Commercial Court from e-Court infrastructure needs and regional economic evidence standardization.

Current Indonesian Practice / Praktik Indonesia Saat Ini

  • JPAIS-MED-ID-033: The Makassar Commercial Court consistently stations 6 certified competition judges annually and successfully resolved its single 2024 case by rejecting the objection without Supreme Court cassation escalation.

Japanese & Comparative Practice / Praktik Perbandingan

  • JPAIS-MED-JP-033: In Japan (Tokyo High Court / JFTC), centralized antitrust review is supported by Circuit Court mechanisms or full remote online hearings to accommodate enterprises from remote regions like Hokkaido or Okinawa.

Gap / Kesenjangan

  • JPAIS-MED-GAP-013: The imbalance between high certified judge capacity (6 judges) and extremely low case volume (1 case/6 years), compounded by under-optimized cross-island e-Court utilization.

Proposed Adaptation / Adaptasi Usulan

Establish the Makassar Commercial Court as an Eastern Indonesia e-Court Digital Hub for KPPU objection trials and reform qualitative DIM reporting instruments.

Action / Aksi

  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-042: Drafting e-Court accessibility technical guidelines specifically for cross-island competition law cases in Eastern Indonesia.
  • JPAIS-MED-ACT-043: Regular training and economic evidence "hot-tubbing" simulations for the 6 Makassar commercial judges in collaboration with BSDK Mahkamah Agung RI.

Pilot / Pilot Proyek

  • JPAIS-MED-PILOT-031: Implementation of the Makassar Commercial Court as a Digital Hub for Business Competition Dispute Services in Eastern Indonesia.

KPI / Indikator Kinerja Utama

  • JPAIS-MED-KPI-033: 100% electronic trial processing (*e-Court*) for KPPU objection cases; dispute resolution duration $\le 60$ working days in alignment with national reform standards.

Policy Implication / Implikasi Kebijakan

Optimization of mandatory full electronic trial provisions to reduce litigation costs for regional enterprises and enhance data synchronization between KPPU Regional Office VI Makassar and the court registry.

Regulatory Implication / Implikasi Regulasi

Refinement of Supreme Court DIM reporting mechanisms to map access-to-justice barriers objectively, even when real-time case data indicates a numerical zero.

Evidence / Bukti Empiris

  • Official DIM document dated July 10, 2026, signed by the Chief Judge of Makassar District Court (Dr. I Wayan Gede Rumega, S.H., M.H.) and Registrar (Handri Mamudi, S.H., M.H.), combined with registration records for Case No. 1/Pdt.Sus-KPPU/2024/PN Niaga Mks.
