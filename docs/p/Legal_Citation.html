Legal Citation

Legal citation is the structured identification of a legal authority so that the underlying source can be located and verified.

Citation Components

A citation may contain:

  • Authority
  • Title
  • Number
  • Article or provision
  • Date
  • Court
  • Case number
  • Decision date
  • Official publication
  • URL or repository identifier

The required format varies by jurisdiction and citation system.

Citation Principle

A citation should allow a researcher to answer:

What is the source?

Where exactly is the proposition?

Can the source be independently verified?

AI and Citation

AI systems can generate plausible-looking citations that are incorrect.

Therefore:

CITATION GENERATED BY AI ≠ VERIFIED CITATION

Verification Workflow

AI OUTPUT → LOCATE SOURCE → CHECK ORIGINAL → CHECK PROPOSITION → CHECK DATE/VERSION → APPROVE

Wiki Citation Standard

Every high-value legal claim should ideally contain:

  • Source
  • Specific provision or page
  • Date/version
  • Verification status
