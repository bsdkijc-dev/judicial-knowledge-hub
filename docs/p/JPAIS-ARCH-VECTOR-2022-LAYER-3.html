Description / Deskripsi

Layer 3 structures the core content domains of the Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) Wiki. It operationalizes the Mahkamah Agung Corporate University architecture by organizing judicial information across seven institutional portals: **Knowledge**, **Academy**, **Research**, **Policy**, **Repository**, **Comparative Judiciary**, and **Judicial AI**.

1. Knowledge Domain (Pusat Pengetahuan)

The Knowledge domain aggregates legal reasoning, doctrinal frameworks, and substantive civil/criminal jurisprudence.

  • **Topics & Doctrinal Taxonomies:** Systematic breakdown of contract law, torts, and constitutional adjudication.
  • **Judicial Decisions Directory:** Landmark rulings extracted from the Supreme Court *Direktori Putusan* with semantic tagging.
  • **Case Studies & Jurisprudential Analysis:** Detailed comparative notes on complex commercial and civil disputes.

2. Academy Domain (Akademi Korporasi)

The Academy domain serves as the educational backbone of the Mahkamah Agung Corporate University.

  • **Curricula & Learning Paths:** Structured training tracks for Candidate Judges (*Cakim* / PPC Terpadu) and continuing professional education.
  • **Teaching Materials & Benchbooks:** Standardized benchbooks, such as the *Yōken-jijitsu* evidence analysis guides.
  • **Faculty & Clinical Mentorship:** Resources supporting certified judicial mentors (SMYB) and unscripted trial simulation labs.

3. Research Domain (Pusat Riset Pustrajak)

Operated by the Agency for Policy Strategy, Legal Training, and Judicial Education (BSDK) / Pustrajak Kumdil.

  • **Policy Research & Working Papers:** Evidence-based studies evaluating trial speed, mediation success rates, and procedural bottlenecks.
  • **Empirical Datasets:** Micro-data repositories supporting judicial analytics and econometric impact assessments.
  • **Research Methodology:** Standardized HIR/RBg and comparative socio-legal research guidelines.

4. Policy Domain (Kebijakan & Regulasi)

The authoritative regulatory repository for binding judicial norms.

  • **PERMA (Peraturan Mahkamah Agung):** Supreme Court Regulations governing Vector-compliant procedures (e.g., PERMA No. 1 of 2016, PERMA No. 3 of 2022, PERMA No. 3 of 2023).
  • **SEMA & SK KMA:** Circular letters and leadership decrees directing court administration and SIPP metrics.
  • **Policy Briefs:** Strategic issue reports prepared for the leadership of BSDK and Mahkamah Agung.

5. Repository Domain (Repositori Dokumen)

The long-term institutional memory storage layer.

  • **Archival Documents:** Historical administrative records, conference proceedings, and bilateral cooperation agreements (e.g., JICA KCCP documentation).
  • **Multimedia & Documentation:** Video series (e.g., Japanese Mediation Series), training manuals, and multilingual guidebooks.

6. Comparative Judiciary Domain (Peradilan Perbandingan)

Enables international comparative legal analysis across advanced jurisdictions.

  • **Jurisdiction Profiles:** Structured comparative modules covering Japan (*Minji Chōtei*, *Chōtei Iinkai*, *mints* digital architecture), Singapore, Australia, Germany, and the United Kingdom.
  • **Cross-Border Reform Models:** Transferability assessments examining what mechanisms can (and cannot) be transplanted into the Indonesian judiciary.

7. Judicial AI & Digital Transformation Domain (AI Peradilan & Transformasi Digital)

Governs the intersection of emerging technologies and judicial administration.

  • **AI Governance & Responsible AI:** Ethical boundaries and compliance rules under the Code of Judicial Conduct (*KEPPH*), restricting AI from substantive decision-making while supporting administrative research.
  • **Digital Infrastructure:** Technical integration blueprints for SIPP, EIS 4.0, e-Court, e-Berpadu, and OASIS LegalDocML (Akoma Ntoso) data standards.
  • **Algorithmic Oversight:** Transparency protocols governing administrative tools like *Smart Majelis* and the *Simetris* Precedent Analytics Engine.
