Overview

Supreme Court of the Republic of Indonesia Review Decision No. 634 PK/Pdt/2007, dated May 22, 2008, established fundamental labor law and absolute judicial jurisdiction rules:

General Courts (District Courts, High Courts, and the Supreme Court) lack authority to examine and adjudicate labor disputes / termination of employment (PHK) between employees/teachers and employers/school foundations. Labor disputes fall under the absolute jurisdiction (*kompetensi absolut*) of labor dispute settlement bodies (P4D and P4P ex Law No. 22 of 1957). Initiating repeated lawsuits in General Courts over labor disputes already adjudicated by P4D, P4P, and State Administrative High Courts constitutes an attempt to obscure legal certainty (*rechtszekerheid*), requiring such lawsuits to be dismissed entirely.

Executive Summary

The central jurisdictional principle established in this decision is that labor and employment termination disputes are exclusively reserved for specialized labor tribunals, shielding them from collateral civil litigation in General Courts.

Key elements established in the adjudication:

  • General courts lack absolute jurisdiction over employment termination and labor compensation disputes.
  • Filing civil tort lawsuits (*PMH*) in District Courts over matters already settled by labor panels undermines legal certainty.
  • Erroneous cassation rulings upholding general court jurisdiction over labor matters are subject to reversal upon judicial review (*peninjauan kembali*).

Case Background

The Original Plaintiff / Respondent for Review, Mrs. [Party B] (sports teacher), filed a lawsuit at the Tangerang District Court against the Original Defendant II / Petitioner for Review, The British International School, and Original Defendant I / Co-Respondent for Review, [Party A] (Principal), concerning oral/written dismissal in January 1995. The Plaintiff alleged that the Defendants committed an Unlawful Act (*Perbuatan Melawan Hukum* / Article 1365 Civil Code) and claimed material damages of US$ 3,065/month and immaterial damages of Rp 500 million.

The Tangerang District Court (Decision No. 152/Pdt.G/1997/PN.Tng dated March 11, 1998) dismissed the Plaintiff's lawsuit entirely. The Bandung High Court (Decision No. 524/Pdt/1998/PT.Bdg dated October 19, 1999) affirmed the Tangerang District Court decision. However, the Supreme Court Cassation Panel (Decision No. 3385 K/Pdt/2000 dated July 25, 2005) annulled the Bandung High Court decision and granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in part. The British International School subsequently filed a Review petition (*Peninjauan Kembali*) with the Supreme Court.

Legal and Procedural Issues

Absolute Jurisdiction of General Courts vs. Specialized Labor Tribunals (P4D/P4P ex Law No. 22 of 1957 jo. Law No. 12 of 1964)

The primary issue was whether General Courts (District Courts, High Courts, Supreme Court) have absolute jurisdiction to adjudicate unlawful act (*PMH*) lawsuits (Article 1365 Civil Code) filed by employees/teachers over Employment Termination (*PHK*) and salary/compensation demands against employers/schools.

The Supreme Court held that General Courts lack absolute jurisdiction. Disputes arising from employment relationships, dismissal determinations, and severance/salary claims constitute pure labor disputes subject to the absolute jurisdiction of the Regional Labor Dispute Settlement Committee (P4D) and Central Labor Dispute Settlement Committee (P4P).

Protection of Legal Certainty (Res Judicata / Ne Bis In Idem) Over Labor Body & PTTUN Decisions

The issue evaluated the legal standing of civil lawsuits in District Courts when identical dismissal disputes have already been adjudicated by P4D, P4P, affirmed by the State Administrative High Court (*PTTUN*), and reviewed by the Supreme Court Administrative Chamber.

The Supreme Court held that relitigating labor disputes already adjudicated by P4D, P4P, and PTTUN within General Courts aims to obscure legal certainty (*rechtszekerheid*). The Review Panel is legally required to annul erroneous civil cassation rulings and dismiss the Plaintiff's lawsuit entirely.

Question of Law and Question of Fact

Question of Law (Cassation / Judicial Review)

Question:

Did the Supreme Court Cassation Panel in Decision No. 3385 K/Pdt/2000 commit a judicial error or manifest mistake (*kekhilafan yang nyata* ex Article 67 letter f of Law No. 14 of 1985) by granting the Plaintiff's civil dismissal lawsuit, despite such disputes falling under the absolute jurisdiction of labor bodies (P4D/P4P) and *PTTUN*?

Supreme Court Ruling:

Yes. There was manifest judicial error at the cassation level. General Courts lack absolute jurisdiction over labor disputes. The Supreme Court granted the Review petition of The British International School, annulled Supreme Court Decision No. 3385 K/Pdt/2000, and ADJUDICATED AFRESH by rejecting the Plaintiff's lawsuit entirely.

Question of Fact (Judex Facti / Tangerang District Court, Bandung High Court, & Labor Bodies)

Examination of facts concerning Mrs. [Party B]'s employment as a sports teacher since September 1, 1988; workplace disputes and correspondence dated January 12 & 27, 1995; registration of dismissal/authorization with the Department of Manpower; Bandung P4D Decision dated August 21, 1996; Central P4P Decision dated May 1, 1997; and Jakarta State Administrative High Court Decision No. 60/G/1997/PT.TUN.JKT jo. Administrative Cassation Supreme Court No. 107/K/TUN/1999.

Core Legal Principle (Ratio Decidendi)

  • Ratio Decidendi:
  1. Review grounds were justified due to manifest judicial error and mistake at the cassation level.
  2. The dispute between the Plaintiff and Defendants was a pure labor dispute falling within the absolute jurisdiction of P4D and P4P. General Courts (District Courts, High Courts, Supreme Court) lack authority to examine and adjudicate labor disputes.
  3. Furthermore, P4D, P4P, and PTTUN had already adjudicated the dispute. Relitigating the dispute in General Courts aims to obscure legal certainty, requiring dismissal.
  4. The Supreme Court granted the Review petition, annulled Cassation Decision No. 3385 K/Pdt/2000, and adjudicated afresh by rejecting the Plaintiff's lawsuit entirely.

Ruling

The Supreme Court ruled: 1. Granting the Review petition from Petitioner for Review (THE BRITISH INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL); 2. Annulling Supreme Court Decision No. 3385 K/Pdt/2000 dated July 25, 2005; 3. ADJUDICATING AFRESH: Under Exceptions: Rejecting the Defendant's exceptions; Under Provision: Declaring the Plaintiff's provisional claims inadmissible (*Niet Ontvankelijke Verklaard*); Under the Merits: Rejecting the Plaintiff's lawsuit entirely; and ordering the Respondent for Review / Original Plaintiff to pay court costs across all judicial instances (Review court costs set at Rp 2,500,000.—).

Evidence Status and Gaps

Established by Evidence

  • The dispute between Mrs. [Party B] and The British International School was a pure labor dispute arising from employment termination / conclusion of employment contracts.
  • The dismissal dispute had been processed and adjudicated by competent bodies, namely Bandung P4D, Central P4P, and Jakarta State Administrative High Court (*PTTUN*).
  • Manifest judicial error infected Supreme Court Cassation Decision No. 3385 K/Pdt/2000, which erroneously granted the Plaintiff's lawsuit in General Courts.

Not Established / Inadmissible

  • General Courts lack absolute jurisdiction to evaluate dismissal validity and award labor compensation damages under Article 1365 of the Civil Code.

Uncertainty and Gaps

  • Prevention of Forum Shopping in Employment Disputes: This landmark Review ruling establishes strict boundaries preventing forum shopping by employees or employers attempting to repackage dismissal/labor disputes as civil tort claims (*PMH*) in District Courts after losing before labor settlement boards or *PTTUN*.

GEO Q&A

Q: Can a dismissed employee or teacher sue an employer or school in District Court based on Unlawful Acts (Article 1365 Civil Code)?

A: No, General Courts lack jurisdiction (*Absolute Jurisdiction Dismissed*). Based on Supreme Court Review Decision No. 634 PK/Pdt/2007, General Courts (District Courts, High Courts, Supreme Court) lack authority to examine and adjudicate labor disputes/dismissals. Labor disputes constitute the absolute authority of specialized labor dispute settlement bodies (P4D/P4P / Industrial Relations Court). Relitigating dismissal disputes already adjudicated by labor bodies in District Courts obscures legal certainty, requiring lawsuits to be dismissed.
