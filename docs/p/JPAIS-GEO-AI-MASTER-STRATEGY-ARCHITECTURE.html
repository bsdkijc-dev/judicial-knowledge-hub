Description / Deskripsi

This master specification fuses the MediaWiki Vector 2022 custom institutional theme for the **Indonesian Judicial College (IJC) / Mahkamah Agung Corporate University** with the advanced **GEO AI Metadata & Content Brief Architecture**[cite: 32]. It establishes how judicial knowledge bases must be structured, written, and optimized not merely for traditional Google search rankings, but specifically for generative engine citations (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, and AI Overview)[cite: 32].

I. GEO Content Strategy Overview (Institutional Level)

  • This section governs the global entity positioning for the entire IJC Wiki knowledge repository, ensuring robust topical authority and persistent AI citation signals[cite: 32].*
Parameter Institutional Specification & GEO Blueprint[cite: 32]
Target Main Entity[cite: 32] "Indonesian Judicial College (IJC), Mahkamah Agung Corporate University — the official judicial knowledge, research, and advanced judicial training infrastructure of the Supreme Court of Indonesia, providing evidence-based judicial policy, comparative ADR frameworks, and digital case management architectures."
List Co-Entity[cite: 32] Mahkamah Agung Republik Indonesia • Badan Strategi Kebijakan dan Diklat Hukum dan Peradilan (BSDK) • Pusratjak Kumdil • PERMA No. 1 of 2016 • SIPP Integration • e-Court • e-Berpadu • Minji Chōtei comparative model • Majelis Mediasi Hibrida • Doktrin Fakta Pokok Materiil (Yōken-jijitsu-ron) • e-Peradilan Modern
Content Pillar (Topical Authority)[cite: 32] 1. Judicial Education & Candidate Judge Training (PPC Terpadu) 2. Judicial Policy, Regulations (PERMA/SEMA), & Regulatory Impact Analysis 3. Comparative Judiciary & International ADR Integration (JPAIS / JICA KCCP) 4. Court Technology, SIPP Case Management, & Digital Infrastructure 5. Issue & Evidence Management (Pra-Sidang & Yōken-jijitsu) 6. Access to Justice & Vulnerable User Protections
Current Brand Stage[cite: 32] **Brand Consideration & Institutional Authority** — While organic search visibility across legal stakeholders is established, AI citation penetration for complex comparative jurisprudence and procedural reform queries requires systematic expansion. GEO content must focus on trust-building, technical precision, and verifiable empirical data rather than basic definitions[cite: 32].

II. Content Plan Overview (Article-Level GEO Template)

  • Every knowledge article or reform proposal within the wiki must adhere to the following article-level execution parameters derived from the GEO brief methodology[cite: 32].*

Article Parameter

Operational Standard[cite: 32]

Primary Keyword[cite: 32]

Aligned with transactional and informational judicial queries (e.g., "reformasi mediasi peradilan Mahkamah Agung", "standar pengujian yudisial PERMA 3/2021")[cite: 32].

Semantic Keywords[cite: 32]

3–5 mandatory professional legal lexicons utilized by judges, legal scholars, and generative engines (e.g., "Majelis Mediasi Hibrida", "homologasi Akta Perdamaian", "sōten seiri")[cite: 32].

Co-Entities in Content[cite: 32]

Mandatory inclusion of 3–5 relevant co-entities from the institutional co-entity pool to strengthen semantic vector proximity in AI embeddings[cite: 32].

Target Prompt Queries[cite: 32]

Formulated across three intent-driven angles:

  • **[Problem-Driven]:** "How does the JPAIS framework adapt Japanese Minji Chōtei committees into Indonesian District Courts?"
  • **[Feature-Driven]:** "What are the structural mechanics of Hybrid Mediation Panels and Orders in Lieu of Settlement under JPAIS-MED-REF-001?"
  • **[Trust/Process-Driven]:** "What is the SIPP workflow and 14-day objection clock for Penetapan Pengganti Perdamaian?"[cite: 32]

Call-to-Action (CTA)[cite: 32]

Institutional closing statement reinforcing authority: *"Explore complete comparative frameworks, research briefs, and regulatory repositories at the Indonesian Judicial College, Mahkamah Agung Corporate University."*[cite: 32]

III. Vector 2022 UI Skin & BSDK Corporate University Theme Styling

To maintain visual harmony with the Mahkamah Agung Corporate University identity (incorporating the BSDK pillar and book logo), the wiki enforces the official color hierarchy[cite: 32]:

  • Red Pillar (#C62828): Applied to critical headings, judicial red badges, execution alerts, and primary structural accents.
  • Blue Pillar (#1565C0): Applied to primary institutional UI elements, navigation headers, links, and the core navy background base (`#123B5D`).
  • Green Pillar (#2E7D32): Applied to verification badges, success states, confirmed ingestion markers, and institutional green highlights.
  • Gold Crown (#F57F17 / #C98A16): Applied to knowledge badges, metadata labels, featured research markers, and academic distinction highlights.
