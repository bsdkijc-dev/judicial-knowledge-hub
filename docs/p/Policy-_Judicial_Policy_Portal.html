Supreme Court Benchbooks

Benchbooks represent authorized, standard operating criteria for procedural matters: [cite: 49, 51]

  • Civil Case Management Benchbook (Latest 2026 Edition): An authoritative directory managing pretrial timelines and remote deposition. [cite: 52]
  • Criminal Trial Protocol Benchbook: Guidelines governing electronic evidence chain of custody, SHA-256 hash validation, and remote detention rules. [cite: 53]

SEMA Administrative Circulars

View and track administrative directives: [cite: 54, 55]

  • SEMA No. 2 of 2026: AI Speech-to-Text Transcriptions: Strict instructions for evaluating and validating draft transcripts from automated transcription arrays. [cite: 56]
  • SEMA No. 1 of 2026: Remote Signature Validation: Verification criteria for digital notary stamps deployed on third-party cloud engines. [cite: 57]
