Knowledge-Based Judiciary

The Blueprint identifies research and development as a strategic function for building a knowledge-based Supreme Court organization.

Strategic Logic

Research

→ Evidence

→ Legal Knowledge

→ Policy

→ Institutional Learning

Institutional Significance

Knowledge should support:

  • adjudication;
  • legal development;
  • policy formulation;
  • organizational learning.
